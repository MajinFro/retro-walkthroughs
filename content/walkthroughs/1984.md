## Game Overview


### What this game is

StarTropics II: Zoda's Revenge is a Nintendo action-adventure for the NES/Famicom, released in 1994. The RetroAchievements set has **31 achievements worth 285 points**.

Mike Jones travels through time using the Oxford World Wonders book. At the end of each chapter he turns the page, says the magic words, and is sent to the next era. The words are "Paa Paa Paa Oom Maw Maw" 🟡. Each jump is a "slip to another time and space" 🟡.

The goal is to recover the Tetrads, which are the legacy of the Argonians. Mica tells Mike that her father Hirocon wanted him to find them 🟡. A Tetrad waits at the end of every chapter, and the game says to collect them all 🟡. Each one raises Mike's life level 🟡.

Zoda keeps interfering. In one chapter he grabs the Tetrad and challenges Mike again 🟡. Later he sends Mike to Translavania, where a vampire will hunt him 🟡. The finale is a rematch with the earlier bosses, which are the same as before but faster 🟡. The Zoda form that turns you into a boar is in that final stretch 🟡.

### The two screens

The game alternates between two modes.

**Travel screen.** This is used on world maps, in towns, and anywhere you can talk to someone. There are no enemies and you cannot attack 🟡.
- The D-pad moves Mike.
- A starts a conversation or inspects something 🟡.
- B cancels a conversation 🟡.
- Select shows which chapter you are in 🟡.

**Action screen.** This starts when Mike enters a dungeon 🟡.
- The D-pad moves Mike 🟡.
- B attacks 🟡 and A jumps 🟡.
- Select cycles through your weapons or the next inventory item 🟡.
- Start pauses 🟡.
- While paused, A uses the item you have selected 🟡.
- The two guides disagree on the pause menu: one says to press up to see Magic Items 🟡, the other says to press down and then use left and right 🟡. If one direction does nothing, try the other.

### Movement and jumping

- You can now jump diagonally, which the guides call a big improvement 🟡. You can also jump to the side without having to land on another platform 🟡.
- Mike takes about half a second to turn after you press the button 🟡.
- Diagonal jumps let you turn in midair, so it is easy to land in water or somewhere you did not intend 🟡.
- Mike can only jump one space at a time. Two-tile jumps need a special item 🟡.
- In some places, jumping straight forward is safer. In the spike-strip rooms of the Chapter 7 cellar, jump forward rather than straight up or to the side 🟡. Another guide says to keep to a straight line and not sway at all 🟡.
- Several traps are instant death. Rolling boulders kill you 🟡. Touching the Gold Cave miner kills you 🟡. The Mining Cyclops kills you if you touch him 🟡. The spikes in the Zoda-Y room also kill instantly 🟡.

### How dungeon rooms work

To get into the next room, you usually have to complete a task. That is normally defeating every enemy in the room or stepping on a switch tile 🟡. Switch tiles work in stages:
1. Walk over the tile. It lights up and makes a sound 🟡.
2. Jump on it. A little ball appears 🟡.
3. Collect the ball to activate the switch 🟡.

Rooms often have many tiles, and only the right one works 🟡. Other guides describe the same thing as a tile that reveals a question mark, which you then grab to open a door 🟡. If a tile has a switch, take it. Otherwise kill the enemies 🟡.

If a room seems to have no exit, walk on every tile. Some of them hold secrets 🟡.

### Weapons and items

**Weapons.**
- Your main weapons are permanent. Collected weapons are used up and disappear when you leave the dungeon 🟡.
- Magic weapons kill some enemies that normal weapons cannot, and they help destroy barriers 🟡.
- The Psychic Shockwave is needed to defeat certain enemies 🟡. Chapter 3 teaches it to you, and it also breaks the barrier in the Great Pyramid 🟡.
- The Super Psychic Shockwave needs 5 hearts to use 🟡. The Ultra version needs 10 hearts 🟡.
- Treat the pyramid guardians as shockwave-only enemies, because they are immune to the dagger 🟡. Chest monsters should also be hit with the shockwave to avoid heavy damage 🟡.

**Stored items and healing.**
- Stored items can be used whenever you want, but they vanish when you leave the dungeon 🟡.
- Castle medicines cannot be carried from place to place, so use them just before exiting a cave 🟡.
- Vitamin X restores energy up to 20. If your life meter is below 20, it slowly lowers the meter to its max 🟡.

### Hardcore Mode and the 100% plan

The set is compatible with Hardcore Mode. There is no difficulty setting or password, and everything is earned in story order in a single save file. In Hardcore you cannot load emulator snapshots, rewind, or use cheats, so learn the hazards above before pushing deep into a dungeon.

**One playthrough is enough.** The game moves forward chapter by chapter. Every chapter ends when Mike takes its Tetrad and the Oxford World Wonders sends him to the next period. Collect each chapter's items before taking its Tetrad.

**The one mutual exclusion is the Chapter 3 dagger.**
- Jack of All Trades offers Mike a bronze dagger trade 🟡.
- Once Mike has a dagger, Jack offers his axe instead 🟡.
- A commenter calls the Jack achievement "definitely a missable achievement" 🟡.
- Another commenter reports getting the dagger from Cleopatra instead and still unlocking it 🟡.
- Those reports disagree. The safe route is to talk to Jack, between the two palm trees north of the Egypt start, and take his dagger BEFORE Cleopatra trades hers 🟡.
- Cleopatra only offers her dagger trade if you do not already have the bronze dagger 🟡. On the safe route she simply sails you across, and Sharp Edge ("Obtain the Dagger") is expected to unlock with Jack's dagger instead (see Chapter 3).

**Other things to remember.**
- The gold nugget in Chapter 5 is lost at the start of the next chapter unless you sell it to Bob 🟡.
- In the Chapter 7 castle, open the Room 13 chest, which holds the spike balls, before you go down the hole 🟡.

Follow each chapter in order and use its closing checkpoint before taking the Tetrad. Each chapter also links the GameFAQs maps for its overworld and dungeons.

### Area Overview & Preparation

Chapter 1 is only an introduction to get you into the game 🟡. It contains no achievements, no items and no combat. All you do is answer Dr. Jones's prompts, get the Oxford book and let the reversed cipher carry Mike to Chapter 2. The route diagram above also shows bosses from later chapters. None of them appear in Seattle.

There is nothing to miss here and no lockout to worry about.

### Step-by-Step Walkthrough

#### Seattle

Unable to verify from collected sources whether a map exists for this area.

1. **Start the game and watch the opening.** The story sets Mike back in Seattle, and you can read Mica's telepathic contact, as the following steps describe.
2. **Answer the opening question.** Mike asks whether you remember his uncle. Either answer works.
   - If you answer **No**, Mike tells you his uncle is Dr. Jones, the tropical archaeologist 🟡.
   - If you answer **Yes**, the scene moves on without that explanation.
   - In both cases you learn that Dr. Jones is in Seattle, busy decoding the rest of the cipher from the tropics 🟡.
3. **Mica contacts Mike.** She first sounds as if she is calling by phone, then says she is using telepathy 🟡. Just keep advancing the text.
4. **Talk to Dr. Jones.** He is busy at his desk. Tell him about Mica's message. He will read it back to you and ask "Get it?"
5. **Answer "Get it?" with Yes.** This makes Dr. Jones realize that Hirocon means the cipher must be reversed 🟡.
   - If you answer **No**, he shows the sentence being broken up step by step, then asks again. Keep answering until you pick Yes. Unable to verify from collected sources whether a "No" has any lasting consequence.
6. **Fetch the book.** Dr. Jones asks Mike to get the Oxford Wonder World book 🟡. Pick it up where he points. It is the only thing you need to do, and it is not a stored inventory item.
7. **Let the cipher play out.** Dr. Jones reads the reversed cipher aloud. Do not try to stop it. The book flips its pages and Mike slips to another time and space, arriving in Chapter 2 🟡.

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Seattle:** There is nothing to collect, so you cannot miss anything. Just make sure you have answered the prompts and fetched the book so the story moves on.

### What Comes Next

Chapter 2 begins in the Stone Age, where Mike must reach a cave 🟡. Crossing the snow field and heading north leads to a cave in the mountains 🟡. The first weapon you find is the rocks, which are very weak and found in the Ice Age 🟡.

### Area Overview & Preparation

Chapter 2 opens after Mike says the magic words from the Oxford Wonder World book and "slips to another time and space" 🟡. In this chapter Mike has to cross a snowy field, reach a cave in the Stone Age, and take on Yum Yum's Cave, the first dungeon 🟡. Your tasks are: collect the Rocks in the random ice-cave action scenes, get the Axe from Tink, memorize the wall shapes, take the Medicine from the secret room, clear Yum Yum's Cave, and pick up the Tetrad (the Block) beyond Yum Yum.

Four achievements are available here: **Better Than Nothing** (Rocks), **A Caveman Weapon** (Axe), **Just Like Link** (Potion/Medicine), and **Your First Tetrad** (first dungeon).

> [!WARNING]

- Falling into a hole in the snow field sends you into a mini-dungeon 🟡. Unable to verify from collected sources: whether every crossing is guaranteed to trigger a Rocks cave, so keep crossing until you get Rocks.
- In Yum-Yum's Cave, Mike can move and jump diagonally 🟡.
- Hardcore: no emulator snapshots or rewinds. If you die in Yum Yum's Cave you respawn in the halfway room 🟡.

**Random Dungeon C**

### Yum Yum (Boss)

Difficulty: Laughable 🟡. Another guide calls it the easiest boss of its kind 🟡.

1. Yum Yum won't attack until he eats the Wild Boar on his plate 🟡. Let him finish eating 🟡. **Do not kill the boar**: he gets angry and attacks much faster and harder 🟡. (Another guide notes that killing it makes him engage immediately 🟡.)
2. Throwing axes immediately after entering should let you kill him before he gets a shot off 🟡.
3. In a longer fight, he runs back and forth shooting bubbles in a tri-spread 🟡. You can't hit him in the back row, so stay in the back and dodge bubbles 🟡. When he moves to the front, charge forward, jump his bubbles and fire axes constantly 🟡. Avoid the bubbles he burps at you 🟡.
4. About 30 axes bring him down 🟡. Near death he moves faster and stronger 🟡.
5. After he dies an opening appears to the right 🟡, and the next room has an exit to the north.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Point of No Return:** Taking the Tetrad behind Yum Yum ends Chapter 2 and the book sends Mike to Egypt. The Rocks only appear in Chapter 2, so pick them up before the Yum Yum fight.

- Stay as far away as possible and hit it repeatedly. It moves slowly and can be hit at any time 🟡.

#### Rooms 14-16: The falling sequence

You must keep moving while you fall through the next rooms.

1. **Room 14:** You cannot land safely here when falling from Room 13. You simply fall through to Room 15.

3. **Room 21:** A magician. Defeat him and the east door opens to Room 22 🟡.
4. **Room 22:** Five blue bats. Defeat them and head north to Room 23 🟡.

1. **Room 27:** Two skeletons and two bats. Defeat the bats and the west door opens to Room 28 🟡.

3. **Sleepwalking and bat-shaped rooms:** Another guide describes a room of sleepwalking enemies. Hit the switch in the top right set, and killing the enemies opens a doorway to medicine and weaponry 🟡. ### Boss: Zoda-Y

Difficulty is rated Hard.

**Opening:** As soon as you enter, move forward a little, because spikes rise from the floor right in front of the entrance 🟡.

**Form 1:**
1. Zoda appears, fires a spray of shots, then disappears. Shoot him when he first appears, then avoid his shots 🟡.
2. He releases bats. Kill the bats, then hit him a few times before he teleports. Repeat this pattern 🟡. This is slower but keeps the bats off you, and you need all the health you can get for the next form.

**Form 2 (bird creature):**
1. After enough hits he kneels, then changes into an owl-like bird form.
2. It releases two bats at a time, and they keep coming while he lives 🟡.
3. He flaps his wings and shoots wind that pushes you back toward the floor spikes. Hold up as hard as you can to resist it, because the spikes are instant death 🟡.
4. He also fires feathers that can damage you 🟡. You cannot really avoid them during the gust, since staying clear of the spikes comes first 🟡.
5. Hit him when he is exposed, using the Mace; use the shockwave when it runs out. Keep hitting the bird form and you will eventually defeat him, which opens the east door to Room 31 🟡.

Tip: you cannot really dodge the feathers during a gust, so keep pushing away from the spikes first and take the feather hits 🟡.

3. Take the **medicine** on the platform. You can also activate the tile in this room, but you must jump down to get the medicine it reveals and then go around and back 🟡.
4. Go up the left passageway with the pile of bones. Hit the bones so they fall down, then jump over them 🟡.
5. Take the **medicine** past them, then go back around the left side.
6. Go around and take **another medicine**.
7. Kill the Frankenstein while standing in the middle of the screen 🟡.

Other rooms in this area: Room 3 has no enemies, and you cannot reach the tile on the ledge, so go west 🟡. Room 4 has three skeleton heads; avoid them and take the northwest stairs. Room 5 has a tile in the middle that opens the wall east to Room 2 🟡. Room 6 has four robed skeletons and a **medicine** 🟡. Room 7 has a magician; defeating him drops you through the floor, and there is a **medicine** on the ledge if you fall in the middle against the north wall 🟡. Landing on the ledge in Room 8 lets you go straight north to Room 13.

Room 13 has two knights; kill them to open the west wall 🟡.

#### The conveyor rooms

Spikes form at the finish line of each fast conveyor belt, and small daggers appear at some point 🟡. Move quickly 🟡.

1. **First room:** take the shortcut halfway through 🟡.
2. **Second room:** take the up path, then the down path 🟡. Ride the belt up on the first loop and down on the second to avoid ground blades 🟡.
3. **Third room:** up and down again 🟡. Take the first loop down; on the second loop stay in the middle moving west 🟡.
4. **Last room:** down, then middle 🟡. Grab the four hearts after jumping the spikes 🟡.

### Knight (Boss)

You stand on a ring of conveyor belts that moves you quickly around the level. The Knight rides a flying horse and moves back and forth shooting bullets in two directions. In Room 17 you move in a perpetual counter-clockwise motion 🟡.

- Jump over his shots when they get near 🟡.
- Aim the shockwave diagonally at him and keep firing.
- He occasionally shoots at you; just avoid the shots 🟡.

### Merlin and the Ultra Psychic Shockwave

After the Knight, walk forward. An owl talks to you and reveals itself as Merlin 🟡. He explains that he was a friend of Hirocon from a faraway planet 🟡, that Hirocon spread the Tetrads across time and space so only a hero could find them 🟡, and that you must collect the last Tetrad and put them together 🟡. Then he gives you the last and most powerful magic 🟡.

> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/612769.png)
> *Obtain the Ultra Psychic Shock Wave*


You obtain the Ultra Psychic Shockwave 🟡. Remember it requires 10 hearts 🟡. Continue along the path to fight for the final Tetrad 🟡.

### Dragon's Cave Part II

🗺️ **Map:** [View Chapter 8 Level 2 Map on GameFAQs](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/nes/587654-zodas-revenge-star-tropics-ii/map/2189-chapter-8-level-2-map)

The music changes. Dragon Cave 2 room numbers below come from the room-by-room guide.

1. Room 2: grab the hearts and kill the bats to open a secret 🟡. Take the stuff and hit the switch in the bottom left, then the switch in the bottom right 🟡.
2. Grab the **medicine** and head back to the first bat room. Room 3 has a **medicine** and spike balls; its tile opens the north wall to Room 5 🟡, where a tile reveals another **medicine** 🟡.
3. Kill all enemies until the music changes 🟡. Room 7 has a Red Triceratops, which you can shoot from a safe distance with the new weapon 🟡.
4. Hearts sit in a tile, but you must kill all blobs to move on 🟡. Kill the big blobs for more hearts in the bottom left corner of the tile set 🟡.
5. Room 12: jump on the floating platform, then the moving platform, up to the stationary platform, then to the tile; jump back for the question mark that opens the door 🟡. Grab hearts and the orb 🟡.
6. Room 14, moving balls: shoot them once to stop and once to restart 🟡. Activate the tile, then grab the question mark 🟡.
7. Room 15, mines drop: hit tile switches 🟡. Find the tile and the question mark fast 🟡. One tile in the third-last row gives a **medicine** (fire around it); one in the third row opens the door.
8. Hearts in the bottom left and top right corners of the next room 🟡. Take the left path for **medicine**, then go right 🟡.
9. Kill the two enemies for a potion and weapons if needed 🟡, or get the bottom-left switch 🟡.

12. Room 22: two armored knights 🟡. Room 23: four rock guys, plus a tile for four hearts 🟡. Room 24: one armored and two regular knights 🟡. Room 25: two big green blobs and a **medicine**.
13. Kill all enemies until you reach the Dragon 🟡.

### Dragon (Boss)

Be heavily armed with lots of medicine 🟡. The Dragon is in Room 26 🟡.

- It slowly walks forward blasting fire in three directions while spikes grow 🟡. Beat it quickly or the spikes consume you 🟡.
- The only way to kill it is hitting its head 🟡. Jump and fire in midair; do not wait for it to breathe fire 🟡.
- Stay near the bottom right corner, jump, and use the Ultra Psychic Shockwave on its head 🟡.
- When it flies and shoots bullets, walk in a counter-clockwise circle 🟡.
- Spend as many medicines as needed to finish quickly.

Defeating it opens the east door to Room 27 🟡. Grab the Tetrad.

### Area Overview & Preparation

This is the final chapter. After you finish the Dragon's Cave, Mica calls Mike and asks him to return to C-Island 🟡. Mike turns to the last page of the book and says the magic words, and the book shows Chapter 9 🟡. He appears on C-Island and exclaims "I'm on C-Island!" 🟡. The game then tells him he must hurry to Coralcola to help Mica 🟡.

Mica reports that aliens came to C-Island and hypnotized Dr. J, who then revealed how to time slip 🟡.

What to expect in this chapter:

- A village visit in Coralcola, then the original StarTropics cave, which is the same cave as in the first game, sort of 🟡.
- C-Serpent as the first boss, as in the original game 🟡.
- A conveyor-belt section, where the bottom route is the one that gives medicines 🟡.
- Revamped versions of every earlier boss: the same, but faster 🟡.
- The final fight against Zoda-Z in three forms.
- Ending and credits, which unlock achievement 36912.

> [!WARNING]
> **Inventory warning:** Medicines are your lifeline in this chapter. Grab as many potions as you can before the final fight 🟡. Do not waste them on the rematch bosses. You will need all the help you can get against Zoda-Z 🟡. Unable to verify from collected sources: exact medicine counts per room.

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave each room:** The guide says to check every tile in the cave for secrets 🟡. Tiles frequently give hearts, weapons and medicine. There are no listed points of no return in this chapter, but there is also nothing to collect after the ending.

### C-Island and Coralcola

🗺️ **Map:** [View Chapter 9 Overworld Map on GameFAQs](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/nes/587654-zodas-revenge-star-tropics-ii/map/15892-chapter-9-overworld-map)

### Coracola Cave (The Original Cave)

🗺️ **Map:** [View Chapter 9 Level 1 Map on GameFAQs](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/nes/587654-zodas-revenge-star-tropics-ii/map/2191-chapter-9-level-1-map)

This is the original cave, remixed. Check every tile for secrets 🟡.

#### Opening Rooms (Rooms #1-#3)

Room #1: Take the passage north to Room #2 🟡.

Room #2: Defeat the three pink blobs to open the north passage. Collect the two sets of four hearts here 🟡. The other guide says to move up a screen and kill three snails to unlock a door 🟡.

Room #3: Six pink blobs are here, but you only need to defeat one specific random blob to open the north door to Room #4 🟡. Since you cannot tell which it is, simply keep killing blobs.

#### Rooms #4-#6

Room #4: Activate the tile to open the north door to Room #5 🟡. Step on the switch in the room to unlock the door 🟡. Unable to verify from collected sources: which tile specifically in this room.

Room #5: Defeat two purple bats and three blue rats to open the north door 🟡.

Room #6: Activate the tiles to get four hearts and open the north door to Room #7 🟡.

#### Rooms #7-#9

Room #7: Two purple asps and two pink blobs are here. Of the two tiles, one opens the north door to Room #9 and the other opens the east wall to Room #8 🟡.

Room #8: Take the medicine. A tile here opens the north wall to Room #9 🟡.

Room #9: An island in the middle holds two hearts, a medicine and a random life sign 🟡. Jump to it and back while taking as little damage as possible 🟡.

#### Chest Room and Side Rooms

A chest room features in the other guide. Jumping on the tiles reveals three switches that unlock two doors and a chest 🟡. Open the chest first and take the 25 torches inside 🟡. Go right through the skinny passageway to a room with a medicine 🟡.

> [!WARNING]
> The room beyond that unlocked side room kills you. Do not go in 🟡.

#### Room #10 and C-Serpent

Room #10: Defeat three bats and two asps quickly to open the north door to Room #11 🟡. Kill all enemies in the room to reach the first boss 🟡.

**Boss: C-Serpent (Room #11).** It is the old boss from the first game, now just bones 🟡. It moves side to side and breathes fire in three directions, like the mace weapon, which you should use 🟡. Avoid the shots and hit the head when the mouth is open 🟡. Defeating it makes a platform appear so you can go north to Room #12 🟡.

Unable to verify from collected sources: C-Serpent's HP.

### Conveyor Section (Rooms #12-#20)

Room #12: Nothing to do except take the path east to Room #13. Unable to verify from collected sources: further detail.

Room #13: Tiles form an arrow leading to one of three conveyor paths. Take any one of them to Room #14 🟡. However, the top route has very few potions and the bottom route has the most 🟡. Take the bottom route to get a bunch of medicines 🟡.

Rooms #14-#18 are connected by conveyor belts. Collect hearts and medicines and avoid spikes 🟡.

Room #19: You fall through the floor to Room #20 🟡.

Room #20: A greyscale room with seemingly no exit. Wait, and a tile in the middle will appear and open the north door to Room #21 🟡.

### Revamped Bosses (Rooms #21-#27)

All earlier bosses return, the same but faster 🟡. Save your medicines.

| Room | Boss | Tip | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|
| #21 | Yum Yum | No boar on his plate this time. Use the same technique as before; opens east door | 🟡 |
| #22 | Scorpion | Avoid the little scorpions; opens north door | 🟡 |
| #23 | Pharoah (mask) | Avoid his blasts; you then fall through the floor | 🟡 |

| #27 | Dragon | Hit his head by jumping in the air; opens east door | 🟡 |

Room #28: Two tiles eventually appear. One makes a pill appear; the other opens the north door to Room #29 🟡. Take the pill first, then use the other tile.

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Room #28:** Use up your preparations. Check you have as many medicines as possible 🟡; the next room is Zoda-Z.

### Zoda-Z (Room #29)

Zoda-Z is described as ten times the boss the original Zoda was, and a significant challenge 🟡. The guide says use any and all items you have 🟡.

**Form 1 – Larva.** He starts as a group of tiny creatures (four Zoda balls) and one is a different color 🟡. Avoid the matching ones and shoot the off-color one until it is defeated 🟡.

**Form 2 – Normal form.** The creatures come together and Zoda appears 🟡. He fires large energy balls that deal no damage but turn you into a wild boar on impact 🟡. While a boar you cannot attack, so run around dodging bullets until it wears off 🟡. Attack him with the shockwave 🟡.

**Form 3 – Minotaur.** After the second form falls, he turns into a minotaur, his final and hardest form 🟡. New tricks:

- **Spin:** When he spins he is invincible for a little while. Do not touch him until it wears off 🟡.
- **Fire walls:** He shoots fire from both sides of the wall; you can see it coming ahead of time 🟡.
- **Charge:** He comes out of nowhere and tries to slam into you head first 🟡.

Dodge his attacks and nail him with the Shockwave when you can, and be patient 🟡. Defeating the true form and then the final form opens the west door to Room #30 🟡.

Unable to verify from collected sources: Zoda-Z's HP and exact attack pattern order.

### Ending

Zoda is fought after the repeated bosses at the end of the game 🟡. Defeating him finishes the game 🟡. You beat the game.

> **C-Island is Normal Again! (25 Points)**

Room #30 is just an exit to the north 🟡. Mica tells you to check on the villagers; the village is back to normal. Mica will meet Mike at the chief's hut 🟡. Miss Coral greets Mike in the village 🟡, and a boy says he zapped the Zodas 🟡.

At the chief's hut the chief offers to help with the Tetrads 🟡. They are put together until they form a scepter, and Hirocon appears 🟡. He thanks Mike for saving him 🟡, explains he sent Mica away in the escape pod when trouble began on Argonia 🟡, and Mica says they can go back to Argonia and rebuild their society 🟡. Hirocon says Mike will always hold a place as the hero of Argonia 🟡. Hirocon flashes and everyone disappears 🟡. The credits start playing 🟡. Sit back and watch the ending.
