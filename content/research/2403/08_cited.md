## Game Overview

### Game Overview

Final Fantasy Adventure is a Game Boy action RPG. You fight in real time, you attack with the A button, and a single playthrough earns all 50 achievements (480 points). Nothing in the achievement set is mutually exclusive, and no chapter has to be replayed. The roadmap opens with the escape from Glaive Castle and ends at the Tower of Gemma and the Tree of Mana.

### Controls and Menus

- **D-pad:** moves the hero in four directions only, with no diagonals, and moves the menu cursor [[gf1-8-1]].
- **A button:** attacks with the equipped weapon in the world, and confirms commands in menus [[gf1-8-2]].
- **B button:** uses the equipped Magic or Item in the world, and cancels or exits in menus [[gf1-8-3]].
- **Start:** opens Item, Magic, Equip and Ask [[gf1-8-4]].
- **Select:** opens Save, Map and Status [[gf1-8-5]].

The screen shows HP, MP and Gold [[gf1-9-1]]. At zero HP you die, and at zero MP you cannot cast [[gf1-10-1, gf1-10-2]].

To use an item, select it with A twice, then press B outside the menus [[gf1-12-2, checklist-items-3-1]]. Keys work differently. Equip one and walk into the door [[checklist-items-3-2]]. Most story items trigger on their own [[checklist-items-3-3]]. Gear is changed in Equip by pressing A twice on it [[checklist-items-3-4]].

Take these inventory limits seriously:

- The item list holds 16 slots [[gf1-12-1]].
- Anything put in the Trash Can is gone for good [[gf1-12-3]].
- The game will not let you trash certain weapons needed to finish [[gf1-14-2]].
- If a chest will not open because you are full, trash a useless item and try again [[gf1-101-4]].

Do not sell or discard the Zeus Axe [[ra-comments-55-1]].

The game save works through Select. You can only load from the Continue option on the main menu [[gf1-18-2]]. Pressing A, B, Select and Start together resets the game without saving [[gf1-18-3]].

### Combat Basics

Touching a monster hurts you. A shield, a higher level than the monster, and the right shield type let you shove it aside instead [[gf1-22-1, checklist-items-6-10, gf2-13-1]].

Equipping a weapon starts the Will meter filling. At full Will, each weapon type does its own special attack [[gf1-22-2]]:

- **Swords:** hold A to spin while moving, or press A with a direction to charge [[gf1-23-1]].
- **Axes and spears:** thrown in the direction you face [[gf1-23-2, gf1-23-5]].
- **Sickle, chain and Morning Star:** their reach extends [[gf1-23-3, gf1-23-4, gf1-23-6]].

You start at level 1 with 19 HP, 6 MP and 50 GP [[gf2-25-1]].

### Magic and Status Ailments

There are eight spells: Cure, Fire, Slep, Heal, Ice, Mute, Lit and Nuke [[gf1-13-1]]. Press A on a spell to equip it and B to cast it. It fails if you lack the MP [[gf1-13-2]]. Costs are:

| Spell | MP cost | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| Cure | 2 | [[gf1-91-9]] |
| Heal | 1 | [[gf1-91-10]] |
| Sleep | 1 | [[gf1-91-11]] |
| Mute | 1 | [[gf1-91-12]] |
| Fire | 1 | [[gf1-91-13]] |
| Ice | 2 | [[gf1-91-14]] |
| Lit | 2 | [[gf1-91-15]] |
| Nuke | 3 | [[gf1-91-16]] |

Ice turns some enemies into snowmen, which you can push onto floor switches. You can steer the iceberg with the D-pad [[gf2-15-6]]. Puzzle rooms use this a lot.

Status ailments:

- **Poison:** cure with Heal or a Pure Potion [[gf1-26-2]].
- **Darkness:** cure with Heal or an Eye Drop [[gf1-26-3]].
- **Stone:** cure with Heal or a Soft Potion, and you can still attack in place [[gf1-26-4, gf2-22-3]].
- **Moogle:** your defense drops to 0 [[gf1-27-1]].

### Tools That Open the World

Different weapons cut different obstacles:

- Axes cut trees [[checklist-items-7-2]].
- The Sickle cuts bushes and vines [[checklist-items-7-11]].
- Mattocks break rocks and pots, and the Morning Star also smashes floating rocks [[gf1-20-1, gf1-20-4]].
- The Morning Star can replace Mattocks entirely [[gf2-48-4]].

Buy plenty of Keys and Mattocks before each dungeon so you never get stuck [[ra-comments-2-4, ra-comments-5-1]].

To raise levels and gold quickly, leave one monster alive on a screen, exit and re-enter. The monsters respawn, and you can repeat this as often as you like [[gf1-37-2]].

### Leveling and the Four "Way of" Achievements

Each level-up restores HP and MP and asks you to choose a stat. The stat you pick gains the most and sometimes boosts another a little [[gf1-24-1, gf1-24-2]]. Power, Stamina, Wisdom and Will each give +2 to the chosen stat [[gf1-25-1, gf1-25-2, gf1-25-3, gf1-26-1]].

The four stat achievements can be missed if you always pick the same stat [[ra-comments-2-1]]. Spread points evenly, or reaching 50 later will be very hard [[ra-comments-4-3]]. The plan is to alternate Power and Wisdom on level-ups [[gf1-104-9, gf3-1-11]]. The Power and Wisdom choices also raise Stamina and Will by 1 each [[gf1-25-1, gf1-25-3]].

The level-milestone achievements need total experience of 1,101 for Lv10, 16,254 for Lv25 and 128,969 for Lv50 [[gf3-10-1, gf3-10-2, gf3-10-3]].

### How Hardcore Mode Applies

Hardcore mode turns off save states, rewinds and cheats, so use the game's own save. You can save before any boss and retry [[ra-comments-4-2]]. Inventory swapping feels dated, but it is manageable once you adjust [[ra-comments-5-6]].

### The 100% Plan

One playthrough is enough: no achievement locks out another, and the only dangers are the timed missables listed here [[ra-comments-2-1, ra-comments-2-2]]. These items can be lost if you move on:

- **Fang and the Jadd clue:** the boy next to the Jadd item shop trades his clue "palm trees, and 8" for a Bag of Fang [[gf1-50-6]]. Walking a figure 8 around the palm trees opens Medusa's Cave [[gf1-51-1]]. It is unconfirmed whether the first Fang always pops the achievement, as some players needed another after Medusa [[ra-comments-18-3]]. Give a Fang before opening the cave and carry spares. If it does not pop, give another after beating Medusa.
- **Were Axe:** this is in Davias's Mansion [[gf1-53-6]]. Keep either the Were Axe or the Zeus Axe, because without one you must restart to get the axe achievement [[ra-comments-55-4]].
- **Blood Sword:** this is in the Dark Castle [[gf1-61-8]].
- **Zeus Axe:** you can get it in the Undersea Volcano [[gf1-72-4]]. One player killed Iflyte and then went back for it, and missed the Iflyte achievement [[ra-comments-3-2]]. It is unconfirmed whether leaving Iflyte's room before taking the Rusty Sword stops the achievement. Take the Zeus Axe first, then after Iflyte open the chest immediately without leaving the room [[gf5-22-4]].
- **Dragon gear:** the Dragon Sword and Dragon Shield are in the Cave of Ruins, and the Dragon Armor is in Dime Tower [[gf1-74-3, gf1-74-4, gf1-75-8]].
- **Wendel boy:** it only works after Glaive's attack, and only for the boy in the southwest corner [[ra-comments-1-1, ra-comments-54-3]]. It is unconfirmed whether Wendel can be revisited after Dime Tower. Commenters believe not [[ra-comments-54-5]]. Treat it as lost once you enter Dime Tower, and do it in chapter 4.
- **Samurai Armor, Samurai Helm and Aegis Shield:** they come from chest drops in the last dungeon. With your item inventory full but equipment space free, keep trying to open a dropped chest until armor appears [[gf1-104-12]].

Entering Dime Tower means you will not see the world map again, so rest at the Ish inn and buy Keys first [[gf2-63-2]]. Making a second save just before the sunken tower in the desert is advised [[ra-comments-2-2]].

Chapter 13 holds 15 of the 50 achievements. Farm the Samurai Armor and Helmet in the Tower of Gemma before the Mana sanctuary; the Elephants for the Aegis Shield, and the grinding for Level 50, the stats and 50,000 GP, can be done inside the sanctuary before you approach Julius [[gf3-8-7, gf3-8-8]].

## Playthrough Planning

**Playthroughs required:** 1

One playthrough earns all 50 achievements. No achievements are mutually exclusive and no chapter replay is needed; the lockouts are timed missables (Fang, Were Axe, Blood Sword, Zeus Axe before Iflyte, Dragon gear and the Wendel boy before Dime Tower, Samurai/Aegis farming and Level/stat/GP grinding before the Mana sanctuary). Commenters advise a second save just before Dime Tower as a safety net. [[ra-comments-2-2,ra-comments-4-2,ra-comments-5-1,ra-comments-2-4,ra-comments-5-3]]

**Settings:** Hardcore mode: no save states. The game's own save is available before every boss. Alternate Power and Wisdom on level-ups so all four stats reach 50.

This prologue chapter covers two fights against the Jackal inside the Glaive arena, a short walk through the castle, and the Dark Lord pushing the hero down the waterfall. That fall is what unlocks the only achievement here, Fall to Earth [[gf1-32-2, gf2-26-2, gf4-1-4]]. The most important thing to carry out of this chapter is a habit: from your very first level-up, alternate your stat choices [[gf1-104-9, gf3-1-11]].

### Preparation and Warnings

The hero begins at Level 1 with 19 HP, 6 MP and 50 GP [[gf2-25-1]]. Another guide adds that the base stats start at 2 [[gf3-2-1]]. Different guides name the hero differently (Sumo, Kain); you choose the name yourself, and no default is provided [[gf2-25-1, gf4-1-1]].

> [!WARNING]
There is no level-up in this chapter's two Jackal fights (they give 0 EXP), but the very first level-up you earn afterwards counts [[gf2-25-2, gf1-30-2]]. Alternate Power and Wisdom on every level-up [[gf1-104-9]]. Another guide confirms that reaching max stats requires alternating between the two warrior and mage style choices [[gf3-1-11]]. Players report that if you do not spread stats in a balanced manner, reaching 50 later is very hard [[ra-comments-4-3]].

#### Arena, Floor 1: the first Jackal

You start in a room next to the Battle Arena. Walk west into the Arena and the Jackal comes out of the north gate [[gf1-30-1]]. After naming screens you are taken straight into the arena [[gf2-25-1]].

**Jackal (first fight)**
- Rewards: 0 EXP, 0 GP, no items [[gf1-30-2, gf2-25-2]].
- Attacks: none [[gf1-30-2, gf2-25-2]].
- Weapon: the Sword; you have no choice [[gf2-25-2]].
- Pattern: it moves in a "W" pattern and will not move below the midpoint of the screen, so do not move your character up too much and you will not get hit [[gf1-30-2]]. From where you start you are safe, so just press Attack [[gf2-25-2]].
- Kill count: about 8 slashes [[gf2-26-1]], or 8 or 9 hits per another guide [[gf1-31-1]].

#### Cell and B-Floor 2

After the fight, a short narrative plays and you find yourself beside your friend Willy's bed [[gf1-31-2]]. Willy tells you the Gemma Knights must be informed and that you should see Bogard at the falls, then he passes away [[gf4-1-1, gf2-26-1]]. Willy is the one who tells the hero about the Mana Tree and the Knights of Gemma [[gf1-28-3]].

On B-Floor 2, once you can move, go north one screen and talk to Amanda, the woman walking around [[gf1-31-3]]. She wants to go home to see her little brother, and a man tells you the monsters' entrance leads to the outside [[gf4-1-2]]. The B-Floor 2 map shows an entrance at the bottom [[gf1-107-1]].

Then go east one screen and climb the stairs to Floor 1 [[gf1-31-4]].

#### Floor 1: the second Jackal

Go west one screen, where you must fight another monster, JACKAL2 [[gf1-31-4]]. It has the same stats as before: 0 EXP, 0 GP, no attacks, Sword [[gf1-32-2]]. Use the same strategy as the first fight: stay away from the upper half of the screen so it cannot touch you [[gf1-30-2]].

#### Out the gate and down the waterfall

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave:** There is nothing to collect in this chapter. Check that you understand the stat-alternating rule above before you take your first level-up [[gf1-104-9]].

After defeating JACKAL2, the gate to the north is still open. Go north through it, climb down the castle wall, and go west one screen [[gf1-32-2]]. There you overhear the Dark Lord and Julius talking about the Mana Tree [[gf1-32-2]]. Julius says the tree is in a shrine above the falls and that a girl has the key to it [[gf4-1-3]]. The Dark Lord is the emperor of Glaive and wants the Mana Tree to become more powerful [[gf1-29-2]].

Once the conversation ends, the Dark Lord spots you and chases you. He then pushes you down the waterfall, dropping you 5 screens, and the hero wakes up at the bottom [[gf1-32-2, gf4-1-4]]. You regain control on the World Map [[gf2-26-2]], in the Topple outskirts at F7 [[gf3-2-2]].


> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> **Fall to Earth (1 Points)**
> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/727967.png)
> *Escape from the Glaive Empire*


Fall to Earth unlocks here, for escaping the Glaive Empire [[gf1-32-2, gf2-26-2, gf3-2-2, gf4-1-4]].

### Jackal

The Jackal is the boss monster encountered on Floor 1 of the arena [[gf1-106-2]]. The full route for both fights is given above. Quick summary: both fights are harmless, 0 EXP and 0 GP, and are won with the Sword [[gf1-30-2, gf2-25-2]].

### Before You Move On

Where to head next is covered in the following chapter; for reference, the starting shop details in Topple are that the Broad Sword costs 60 GP [[gf1-33-1]] and the Iron Helmet costs 140 GP [[checklist-items-5-2]]. The Broad Sword is your starting weapon and deals 0 damage to undead monsters [[checklist-items-7-15]]. Keep alternating Power and Wisdom at each level-up [[gf1-104-9]].

This chapter takes you from the foot of the waterfall to Kett's. You rescue the Girl east of Topple, collect your first Mattock from Bogard, use it in the East Cave, and rest at Kett's to learn Cure. Three achievements are earned here: Damsel in Distress, Pearl of Wisdom and Rest and Heal Your Wounds [[gf1-33-10, gf1-34-1, gf1-36-4]].

> [!TIP]
> **Preparation:** Gold matters in this chapter. Buy the Iron Helmet in Topple (140 GP) and a set of Keys, and bring enough gold for the Battle Axe (150 GP) later [[gf1-33-1, gf1-33-2, gf1-33-7, gf1-35-2]]. Fight around the Topple area until you reach level 3 or 4 and have over 140 gold [[gf1-33-2]].

### Combat notes for this chapter

- When you level up, your HP and MP are fully restored [[gf3-2-3]].
- Goblins take 2 hits and drop Candy; Mushrooms take 1 hit. Both give only 1 experience point [[gf5-5-1]]. Candy restores 8 HP when used or sells for 2 G [[gf5-5-1]].
- Imps are dangerous: 3 hits to kill with only 1 level-up, 2 hits with 2 level-ups. Rabites take 1 hit [[gf5-5-3]].
- If you leave at least one enemy alive on a screen and move one area away, the enemies respawn. If you kill them all, you must move four areas away [[gf3-2-4]].
- The sword has a spin attack: press Attack with the d-pad neutral and keep holding to keep spinning for up to 8 seconds while moving freely [[gf3-2-6]].
- The Girl heals you when you ASK her [[gf1-33-11, gf2-17-2]].

### Topple

#### Outside Topple: rescuing the Girl

From the bottom of the falls, walk east two screens, south, east, and north to reach Topple [[gf1-33-1]]. Another route reads: east, east, south, south, then east to the screen with the Girl [[gf2-26-3, gf5-5-1]]. Before rescuing the Girl, you can farm: you visit this screen and the one east of it several times. Kill all but one enemy on the first and second visits, then clear them all. The aim is to be about 7 kills past level 2 before the rescue [[gf5-5-1]]. Keep alternating Power and Wisdom on these level-ups, as chapter 1 explains [[gf1-104-9]].

Go to the screen east of the farming area. A girl is yelling for help [[gf1-33-10]]. Defeat ALL monsters on this screen. The clue that they are all dead is that the music stops [[gf2-26-3]]. These are Mushrooms [[gf5-5-2]]. Then talk to the injured Hasim. He asks you to take the Girl to Wendel and see Bogard, and then dies [[gf1-33-10, gf4-2-4]]. The Girl joins your party [[gf1-33-10]].

### East Cave

This chapter takes you from Kett's to the lizardmen's nest for the Bronze Key, through Marsh Cave to the Hydra, and back to Kett's to expose the guard as a Werewolf, rescue the Girl and kill Mr. Lee. Four achievements are earned here: recruiting the man in Marsh Cave, beating the Hydra, killing the vampire, and reaching Level 10 [[gf1-36-2, gf1-37-1, gf1-38-1, gf1-40-4, gf1-40-11, gf1-109-1]].

### Preparation and Overview

Stock up on Keys and Mattocks before you start. The cave walkthrough calls for 4 Mattocks and 3 Keys, and Kett's Dungeon calls for 5 Mattocks and 2 Keys [[gf1-38-7, gf1-40-4]]. The Marshes shop sells Mattocks (x7) for 60 GP and Keys (x4) for 15 GP [[gf2-79-4]]. If you run out, Skeletons drop Keys and floating fish drop Mattocks [[gf5-6-4, gf5-6-1]].

> [!TIP]
> **Farming trick:** Fight every monster on a screen except one, then leave and re-enter. Everything respawns, which gives plenty of gold and EXP. It works especially well on the lizardmen screen [[gf1-37-2]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Level 10 needs 1101 total EXP [[gf3-10-1]]. Farm respawning monsters during this chapter and check your level before you leave Kett's. The Hydra is recommended at Level 13 and Mr. Lee at Level 15 [[gf1-39-2, gf1-41-1]].


> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> **Squire (5 Points)**
> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/728001.png)
> *Reach Level 10*


The Squire achievement is earned simply by reaching Level 10, so keep farming until it pops [[gf3-10-1]].

### Marshes

🗺️ **Map:** [View Cave Of Marsh(O9) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-CaveOfMarsh(O9).png)

Enemies here include Jellyfish (4 GP, 3 EXP), Flowers that drop Pure, and Marsh Ghosts that only magic can hurt. Poison Flowers inflict poison [[gf1-99-5, gf1-99-6, gf2-78-11, gf2-78-15]]. The tool shop in the Marshes sells the Battle Axe for 150 GP, and the general store also stocks Cure x2, Pure x3, Eydrp x3, Key x4 and Mattock x7 [[gf1-92-9, gf2-79-4]]. Axes cut down trees, though most leave a stump you cannot pass [[gf3-2-18]].

#### Get the Cure spell at Kett's

Inside Kett's, the guard says the eastern door is reserved for Mr. Lee [[gf1-36-1]]. In the north room, one person says the Marsh Cave key is in the lizardmen's nest to the south, and another says a mirror was thrown away in the Cave of Marsh [[gf1-36-2, gf1-36-3]]. If you did not already sleep here, go south, west, north and walk onto the unoccupied bed. The Girl gives you the Book of Cure and you learn Cure; by morning she is gone [[gf1-36-4, gf2-29-1]]. Ask the guard afterward and he denies knowing her [[gf1-36-4]].

#### The Bronze Key

From outside Kett's go east three screens, then south. Defeat every lizardman on that screen. A chest appears in the middle containing the Bronze Key [[gf1-37-1, gf2-30-1]]. Lizardmen take 2 or 3 hits and drop Cure potions [[gf5-5-8]]. Equip the Bronze Key from the item screen. Go north one screen, east, then north, and enter the door into Marsh Cave [[gf1-37-1]].

### Marsh Cave

Marsh Cave contains a Sickle, an Iron Shield, the Fire spell and the Mirror. Floating fish hit hard, so use Cure liberally [[gf5-6-1, gf1-107-3]].

#### Floor 1 entrance: recruit the man

As soon as you enter, a man wanders the screen. Talk to him and accept his offer to join [[gf1-38-1, gf4-3-2]]. He attacks with fireballs, and if you ask him he explains the Mattock [[gf1-27-3, gf2-18-1]]. One source calls recruiting him optional, but the achievement requires it [[gf3-2-29]].


> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> **Mysterious Marsh Man (5 Points)**
> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/727971.png)
> *Recruit the mysterious man dressed like a bard*


#### Route to the Sickle

Walk north, west, and step on the odd tile near the south wall to reveal stairs, then go down [[gf1-38-2]]. Another route describes the switch at the bottom of the screen revealing a ladder at the top left [[gf2-30-3]]. On the next floor, go east a screen and climb down the vine at the end of the log bridge. Go west a screen and re-enter the cave [[gf1-38-10]].

Go east one screen and use a Mattock on the middle of the south wall; striking it makes a cranking sound. Go south a screen and slide across the ice to the chest holding the **Sickle** [[gf1-38-4, gf2-30-4, checklist-items-7-10]]. Equip it to cut vines [[gf1-38-5]]. The floor pulls you back in, so equip the Sickle and hack away the herbs to leave [[gf2-30-4]]. Then return to the entrance room.

#### Iron Shield and healing spring

Go south; the man's fireballs kill the blobs. Use a Mattock on the west wall and go west [[gf1-38-6]]. For a full HP/MP restore: use a Key on the door to the north, push the chest onto the odd tile, descend, go south and search the bottom of the pond [[gf1-38-7, gf2-30-5]].

From that room go west twice, then north. Use a Key on the east door and defeat all monsters; a chest appears with the **Iron Shield** [[gf1-38-8, gf2-30-6, checklist-items-6-2]]. Equip it, since it blocks Hydra fireballs [[gf1-39-4]]. Go west through the door, cut the vines with the Sickle and go north. Walk east a screen and use a Mattock on the rock in the northeast corner to reveal stairs, then descend [[gf1-38-8, gf2-30-7]].

#### Down to the Hydra

Climb along the vines south, go west into the cave, then west and south, and use a Key on the door. Kill the monsters, slide east on the northernmost ice row, break the rock with a Mattock, then go south and break the southernmost rock. Step on the switch, save, and equip Cure; then take the stairs down [[gf1-38-10, gf2-30-7]]. Cure and Candy conserve MP before the boss [[gf5-6-8]].

### Hydra

Go east a screen to fight the Hydra [[gf1-39-1]]. Recommended level 13; it rewards 94 GP and 10 EXP [[gf1-39-2, gf1-39-3]].

**Strategy:**
- Equip the Sickle and Iron Shield: the Sickle destroys fireballs and the shield blocks them [[gf1-39-4]].
- Move north as the Hydra moves south, attack its heads as it comes up, retreat south as it rises, then repeat [[gf1-39-5]].
- Only hits to the head do damage; stay below it [[gf2-31-1]].
- If cornered, face the fireballs with the shield equipped [[gf2-31-2]].
- It deals up to 13 damage, so keep HP above that. Mash Attack after a hit to extend invincibility [[gf5-6-8]].

After the fight two chests appear. Open the first to learn **Fire**; attack the chest to destroy it and open the second for the **Mirror** [[gf1-40-1, gf1-40-2, gf5-6-9]].

### Kett's

🗺️ **Map:** [View Ketts(K10) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-Ketts(K10).png)

Return to Kett's and save outside. In the dungeon, Face Orbs and Pirates are hurt only by Fire [[gf1-40-4, gf1-40-5]]. Zombie Mice need magic [[gf2-78-21]].

Equip the Mirror and talk to the guard. He turns into a Werewolf. It is not a true boss [[gf1-40-4, gf2-31-5]]. It deals about 10 damage and 4 Axe hits kill it [[gf5-7-1]]. Afterward, rest at the beds to restore HP and MP [[gf1-40-4]].

#### Through the dungeon to the Girl

From the Werewolf room go east twice, north, west and up the stairs. On the next floor go south and Mattock the east wall, go through the hole, north, east, south twice, west. Break one of the pots with a Mattock and step on the odd tile; take the new stairs in the southeast corner [[gf1-40-4, gf1-40-6]].

Go east, north, unlock the door with a Key and open the chest for the **Chain Flail** [[gf1-40-7, checklist-items-7-6]]. Use it on the east pole to escape, and step on the odd tile to remove the blocks [[gf1-40-8, gf5-7-6]]. Go south twice, west, and up the stairs, then up the stairs to the north [[gf1-40-8]].

Go west and use the Chain across the chasm, then west and south down the stairs. Go west, then north, kill all creatures, and let the Attack Meter fill to MAX before crossing the gap with the Chain. If you fall, go north and up the stairs, then south through the door [[gf1-40-8, gf2-31-5]]. Then go east twice, north, west; push the chest onto the odd tile and descend [[gf1-40-6, gf5-7-8]]. Mattock the southeast pot and go down [[gf1-40-10]].

On B-Floor 2 go east, unlock the south door with a Key, and open the second coffin from the right on the bottom row to find the Girl [[gf1-40-11, gf2-31-6, gf5-7-10]]. Ask her to recover HP [[gf1-40-4]].

### Mr. Lee

Backtrack to the dungeon entrance, then save and heal before going west through the door [[gf1-40-4]]. Once you find the Girl, walking out triggers the fight at the entrance [[gf3-3-8]].

Recommended level 15; he drops 10 GP, 11 EXP and the Book of Sleep [[gf1-41-1, gf1-41-2]]. He moves among four spots, then opens his cape to launch bats [[gf1-41-3, gf2-33-2]].

**Strategy:**
- Block bats with your shield, dodge, and strike with the Chain [[gf1-41-3]].
- Target his body, not his wings [[gf2-33-3]].
- Fire works while he moves, and the Chain from below while he stands still [[gf5-7-11]].
- Heal with Cure or by asking the Girl [[gf1-41-3]].

> **RIP, Christopher Lee (5 Points)**
> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/727973.png)

You gain Sleep, then are warped outside Kett's [[gf1-41-2, gf4-3-7]].

> **Before you leave Kett's:** Confirm you have Fire and Sleep and have reached Level 10. Wendel is next: east three screens, south, west (cut the vines), south, west, north, cross the river with the Chain Flail, south, east, south twice, east, north twice [[gf1-42-1, gf3-10-1]].

### Refueling Lake

When you exit Gaia's Cave, Bogard joins you and tells you the Airship is refueling at the lake to the north [[gf1-46-2]]. Gaia only lets you through if you wear at least one piece of Silver equipment [[gf2-37-3, gf4-4-5]].

#### Werewolves (optional leveling)

One screen south of the cave exit is a group of four Werewolves. They are replenished even after you kill them all, so this is an easy place to gain levels. With a throwing-type weapon equipped, go to the screen south of them and wait for the attack bar to max out. Then go north and let the weapon fly to kill all of them in one shot, if your Power is high enough [[gf1-49-4, gf1-46-2]].

#### Item Shop near the lake

From the cave, go south, cut a path through the trees, then go west a screen. Next go north twice to see an Inn. Use the Battle Axe on the trees to your left, go west twice, then north into the Item Shop [[gf1-46-4, gf1-46-3]].

#### Reaching the Airship

From the shop, follow these steps [[gf1-46-4]]:

1. Go east twice, north, then east through the trees to the next screen.
2. Wait until your attack bar is at max, then use the Chain Flail to cross the stream. Only a maxed Chain reaches the pole [[gf2-37-7]].
3. Go north, east, use the Chain Flail, then go west.
4. Go down the vine and west.
5. Use the Chain Flail to cross the water, then go south.
6. Go west and north twice.

### Dark Castle

#### B-Floor 2: the bridges

Cross the north bridge, then the west bridge, and go north. Next go east, cross the bridge to the south, go east, and cross the bridge to the north. Go east one screen and up the stairs [[gf1-60-3]].

Climb the vine above you and go north one screen. On Floor 2 go north another screen. The last vine lifts you several floors to a set spot on Floor 5 [[gf1-60-4, gf1-119-4]]. Go down the stairs, then down another flight on Floor 4 [[gf1-60-4]]. If enemies block your path on the vine, re-enter the screen [[gf5-17-1]].

#### Floor 3: Chimera

Heal up and save on Floor 3, then go west to face Chimera [[gf1-60-5]]. Bring Cure.

**Chimera**
- Level 40 recommended; it awards 90 EXP and 21 GP in one source and 91 GP and 20 EXP in another [[gf1-61-1, gf1-61-2, gf2-51-1]].
- It flies above you, drops two flaming bombs from its wings, then flies down in a zigzag [[gf1-61-3]].
- One approach: stand near the top of the screen, attack as fast as you can, and cure occasionally [[gf1-61-3]].
- It is invulnerable to Lit, so cast Fire rapidly. Another guide lists the Morning Star as preferred, so use whichever you can land reliably [[gf5-17-2, gf2-51-1]].
- Each time it reaches the center of the screen it may dive for about 45 damage, so cast Cure if your HP is below 50 [[gf5-17-3]].
- Stay out of the range of the fireballs dropped from its wings [[gf5-17-2]].

### The Girl in Her Cell

After Chimera, descend the stairs in the southwest corner to Floor 2, which is the Girl's prison cell. She runs up and joins you and says the Dark Lord took her pendant [[gf1-61-4, gf3-5-3]]. She heals your HP when you ASK her, so do this often [[gf1-61-4]]. Go down the stairs next [[gf1-61-4]].

### Shop, Spikes and the Chain Puzzle

Go up the stairs north, then south down the steps to the next screen. On Floor 1 go west one screen and enter the building. You can rest on the bed and buy supplies at the Item Shop [[gf1-61-5]]. Exit, go east, and walk north up the steps [[gf1-61-5]].

Go west three times, south, and into the door [[gf1-61-5, gf1-61-6]]. Quickly walk across the spikes; they hurt a lot, so have the Girl heal you afterward [[gf1-61-6, gf3-5-4]]. Use a Key on the door and go north. Then go east, break the north wall with the Mattock or Morning Star, and go north through the hole. Go west, and use the Chain Flail to cross to the odd tile. Stand on it and a staircase appears. Go down [[gf1-61-6]].

The room's puzzle: Chain to the middle pole, then with a full Will bar chain to the left pole to hit the button, then with a full Will bar chain to the bottom pole [[gf5-17-1, gf2-51-2]]. Another route description says grapple the northern pole, then the left pole, step on the switch so a ladder appears in the lower right, then grapple the southern pole and use the ladder [[gf2-51-2]]. While flashing from damage you cannot press a button or enter stairs [[gf5-17-7]]. Wizards inflict Stone with magic or Moogle on touch, and can only be defeated with about 10 Morning Star hits [[gf5-17-5]]. Dark Knights die to one Fire cast if hit from the side or back [[gf5-17-6]].

### Werewolf Room

### Toward the Dark Lord

**Dark Lord**
- Level 41 recommended; 167 GP and 12 EXP in one source, 166 EXP and 11 GP in another [[gf1-62-1, gf1-62-2, gf2-52-2]].
- He runs around and swings his sword, follows your movements, and can use a MAX-type attack [[gf1-62-1]].

- If Lit glitches and he flies off the balcony, hit him with the Morning Star to finish him [[gf5-18-2]].
- Defeating him yields the Fake Pendant [[gf1-62-3, gf4-7-10]].

### Nuke spell (Lich)

- Recommended level is 48 [[gf1-69-1]]. Rewards are 220 GP and 198 EXP according to the first guide [[gf1-69-2]].

- Fill HP and MP first. The Flame Flail is suggested for its fast attack that hits him several times [[gf2-60-1]].
- Pattern: he flies a figure eight and sometimes leaves the screen and reappears on the other side [[gf1-69-4]]. Stand in the middle with the Morning Star; if you strike at the right time he passes over you without damage. Alternate attacks with Cure [[gf1-69-4]].

- If you play as a mage build, fully charged Lit spells are the advice; he hits for about 100 per hit [[gf5-23-1]].

This is the final chapter. You ride the reversed waterfall to the top of the mountain, climb the Tower of Gemma (called the Temple of Mana in one guide), beat three dragons, receive Excalibur, farm the best armor and shield, grind your levels, and finish with Julius and the Mana Tree [[gf4-10-8, gf1-77-1, gf1-80-3]]. Almost every achievement here must be done before the point of no return at the Mana sanctuary, so read the preparation section first.

### Overview and Preparation

The recommended levels for the bosses run from 57 (Dragon) to 60 (Dragon Zombie and Julius) [[gf1-77-6, gf1-78-7, gf1-80-1, gf1-81-2]]. One guide advises at least Level 50 for Julius [[gf2-85-4]].

Achievements to finish before leaving the tower area:

- **Gemma Knight:** Level 50 needs 128,969 total experience [[gf3-10-3]].
- **The Way of the Warrior / Wizard / Tank / Monk:** Power, Wisdom, Stamina and Willpower all need to reach 50. These are technically missable if you always pick the same stat when leveling up [[ra-comments-2-1]]. A commenter who over-invested in one stat found raising the others later was very slow, so spread points evenly [[ra-comments-4-3]]. One guide says to alternate between Senshi and Madoushi to reach max stats [[gf3-1-11]]. Another says to alternate Power and Wisdom [[gf1-104-9]].
- **Wow, You're Rich!:** You must hold 50,000 GP at one time. A commenter says reaching Level 50 naturally brings you close to this [[ra-comments-46-1, ra-comments-46-2]].
- **Samurai Style and Poacher:** both are missable and covered below.

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Samurai Style (Samurai Armor and Samurai Helmet) must be farmed in the Tower of Gemma, where the Ninjas and Demon Heads live, before you pass the first four screens of the Mana sanctuary; there is no way back afterwards [[gf1-104-12, gf2-67-1, ra-comments-52-2, gf3-7-15, gf3-7-16, gf3-8-8]]. Poacher (Aegis Shield) comes from the Elephants, which live inside the Mana sanctuary just before Julius, so farm it there before you walk up to the final fight [[gf3-8-7, ra-comments-53-1, gf2-67-3]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Timber!!! needs the Were Axe or Zeus Axe at the final battle. The Battle Axe cannot damage the final boss, and without one of the other two axes you must restart the game. Do not sell or discard the Zeus Axe [[ra-comments-55-1, ra-comments-55-2, ra-comments-55-3, ra-comments-55-4]].

### Mana Falls

After Garuda falls, Dime Tower collapses. Go west twice, and Marcie throws you across the gap but cannot jump himself [[gf1-76-4]]. Go west three times to the waterfall, which reverses its flow; walk into it to be carried to the top of the mountain [[gf1-76-5, gf4-10-8]]. At the top, go west, north twice, and enter the temple [[gf1-77-1]].

**Dragon** (recommended level 57, 257 to 262 GP depending on the source, 0 EXP) [[gf1-77-6, gf2-68-1, gf3-8-1]]:

- The Dragon travels in a circle through the arena; his fireballs can be blocked by your shield [[gf5-26-2, gf1-77-6]].
- Only the head can be damaged with a long-reach weapon (Flame Flail or Thunder Spear) [[gf2-68-2]].
- The mage route says the only vulnerable square is the front foot under his head, because his wing intercepts shots aimed higher. Use fully charged Nukes, and consider taking a weak fireball hit on purpose so the invincibility time lets you cast uninterrupted [[gf5-26-2, gf5-26-3]].

Another guide says to turn a Ninja into a snowman and put it on the floor switch second from the bottom in the left column, which opens the north door [[gf2-69-3]]. The mage guide agrees: the correct button is on the left side, second from the bottom [[gf5-26-6]]. Then go north three times and up the stairs [[gf1-79-9]].

Heal and save before the next fight [[gf1-79-9]].

### Armor and Shield Farming

#### Samurai Armor and Helmet drops: the drop-chest trick

This is the key to both missable equipment achievements. The contents of a chest are decided when you open it, not when it appears [[gf1-104-12]]. To use it:

1. Fill your item inventory completely, but keep room in your equipment inventory [[gf1-104-12]].
2. Kill the monster carrying the equipment you want until one drops a chest [[gf1-104-12]].
3. Try to open the chest. You get "Can't carry!" messages and the chest stays closed. Keep trying and eventually you get the armor instead of an item [[gf1-104-12, gf2-67-1]].

You only need one chest to drop rather than killing many monsters [[gf1-104-12]].

Respawning enemies: enemies return if you visit three other screens and come back, or if you leave one monster alive and return [[gf1-104-8]]. Commenters say that leaving one enemy alive, exiting and re-entering works well [[ra-comments-52-3, ra-comments-53-6]]. Another method: kill the Ninjas in one room, save, soft reset, load, and a new set spawns [[ra-comments-52-5]].

#### Samurai Armor (Ninjas)

The armor drops from Ninjas, which also drop Elixirs, and gives +45 defense [[gf1-99-72, checklist-items-4-13, gf3-8-23]]. Ninjas give 254 EXP and 10 GP [[gf2-78-13]]. Use the chest trick on a Ninja's chest [[gf2-67-1]].

#### Samurai Helmet (Demon Heads)

The helmet drops from Demon Heads, which also drop X-Ether, and gives +25 defense [[gf1-99-73, checklist-items-5-6]]. The Devil Head is vulnerable to Nuke magic and swords [[gf2-78-3]]. A commenter kills them with Nuke, saves, and uses Ethers and Elixirs to restore MP before repeating [[ra-comments-52-6]]. Use the chest trick on their chest [[gf2-76-6]].


> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> **Samurai Style (25 Points)**
> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/728014.png)
> *Equip the best armor and helm*


Equip the Samurai Armor and Samurai Helmet to earn Samurai Style [[gf2-85-2, gf1-103-2]].

#### Aegis Shield (Elephant drop)

Elephants drop the Aegis Shield (and Unicorns). They live in the Mana sanctuary, just before the final boss, so this farming happens after you cross the point of no return below, but before you approach Julius [[gf1-99-74, gf2-78-4, gf3-8-7, ra-comments-53-1]]. Excalibur is recommended against them [[gf2-78-4]]. Reload the screen until two Elephants are on it by leaving and returning without killing, and kill them on each screen where they appear. Their Unicorn drops fill inventory slots, then use the chest trick [[ra-comments-53-3, ra-comments-53-4, ra-comments-53-5]]. The Aegis Shield deflects any projectile, including the final boss's force balls [[gf2-67-3]].

> *Equip the best shield*


Equip the Aegis Shield to earn Poacher [[gf2-85-3, gf1-103-2]].
