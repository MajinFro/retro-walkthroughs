--------------
C. GAME BASICS
--------------

1. Moving About
---------------

Basically, you can move around with the directional pad. You cannot 
walkthrough walls, mountains, walk on water (you’ll be able to navigate the 
waters in the later parts of the game).

On the field or inside dungeons, you’ll see different kinds of enemies roaming 
around. If you touch one of these, you will receive damage at once. There are 
various ways of getting through enemies. First, just slash it will your weapon 
(although some enemies are not affected with certain types of weapons). 
Second, just go around it to prevent damage and save time. Lastly, just push 
them. You will be able to push an enemy, only if your level is higher than 
that monster (you can’t see the level of the enemy though).  

In the world map, you’ll also see some towns with people in it. You can talk 
to them for information regarding where to go next. Or just simple say hello 
to each other. 

Basically that’s all you have to do in the game besides attacking. Try to go 
with the flow of the story. It will excite you at the same time, you will be 
able to finish the game.

2. Saving and Loading your game
-------------------------------

The GamePak (Gameboy Cartridge) of Final Fantasy Adventure has 2 Slots. You 
can save 1 Game for every slot. Note that, both saves can be from the same 
game or from 2 distinct games. 

For loading games, you can only do that at the main menu by selecting the 
continue option. There is no Load command in the in-game menus. So, you’ll 
have to reset. Press the A, B, Select, and Start buttons at the same time. 
Note that doing this won’t save your game. 

3. Attacking and Battle System
------------------------------

Attacking in Final Fantasy Adventure is pretty basic. Go to an enemy within 
range of your weapon and then press the A button. Your character will attack 
using your weapon. If it hits the enemy, it’s HP will go down (the HP of the 
enemy cannot be seen). If the enemy’s HP reaches 0, it will die and will 
disappear from your sight. 

If ever your HP reaches 0, the Game will be over. And remember, once you’re in 
a new area, expect tougher enemies!

4. Magic System (Magic List)
----------------------------

There are only 8 Magic abilities for Final Fantasy Adventure. Here they are:

Cure  = Restores your character’s HP (2 MP)
Heal  = Remove any Status Ailment (except Moog) (1 MP)
Slep  = It lull’s enemies within the area into sleep (some are impervious
        to this magic spell (1 MP)
Mute  = This will disable the casting abilities of your enemy. Some cannot
        Be inflicted with this Magic Spell (1 MP)
Fire  = Throw a small fireball to the nearest enemy. This fireball will 
        Automatically chase your enemy even outside the screen area of the 
        Gameboy. (1 MP)
Ice   = Throws a small IceBerg on your enemies. Some enemies turn into 
        Snowmen after being hit by the IceBerg. You can then push them to
        Activate floor switches. IceBerg can be controlled by using the 
        Directional pad. If you accidentally hit a wall, the IceBerg will  
        Disappear. (2 MP)
Lit   = Throws a Lightning Bolt straight to the enemies in front of you.
        (2 MP)
Nuke  = Strongest Magic Attack. Hurls a ball of Nuclear Energy at your
        Enemies. It travels in a straight line only though. (3 MP)

Certain types of Magic do not affect some enemies. Example, fire-based enemies 
are not harmed by your Fire Spell. Some enemies also have a very high Magic 
Rating and cannot be inflicted with the Slep and Mute Spell. 

To acquire Magic spells, most of them can be read from Magic Books and then 
learned by your character. The Nuke Magic, can be obtained by beating Death.

To use a Magic spell, you MUST equip it first. Press the Start button and 
choose the Magic option. Now, select the magic you want to equip. You can now 
press the B button to execute the magic spell. Take note always of your 
available MP. If it reaches 0, you cannot use any magic anymore. 

The power of the Magic Spell also depends on your Will. If the Will Bar below 
the screen is full, you can unleash the strongest effect/damage of your magic 
spell. 

5. Items 
--------

Items are very important in the game. Especially the Keys. To be able to use 
an item, you MUST equip it first by selecting it in the Item Sub-Menu. Then 
use it by using the B button. Some items, like the key, are used 
automatically.

Each of your items has a maximum quantity. Example, Pure Item is MAXed out at 
3. You can have 16 different items in your inventory. You can have 3 Pure 
Items with 3 quantities each. If you use it up, it will disappear from your 
inventory (of course!). 

You can acquire items, from Item Shops, spoils from enemies, chest and others 
are acquired automatically. 

6. Equipments
-------------

Equipments play a very important role as to how your character will fare with 
the enemies in the game. Having weak weapons will give you a long time to 
defeat an enemy. The same goes for the armors, helmets and Shields. The weaker 
they are, the bigger the damage you get. So, make it a basic rule that, you 
acquire every new equipment from shops if you reach a new town.

Another note on Shields. If your character’s Level is high enough. Let’s say 
about 70. You can push the pathetic enemies out of your way. If you are weak, 
you’ll get damage. 

7. Non-Playable Characters (NPCs)
---------------------------------

In Final Fantasy Adventure, you also have other characters that can help you 
in your journey. Although, they are very helpful, you cannot control them. 
They have a quite stupid AI. They attack walls and trees, and even thin air 
for crying out loud. There is a concept here which I like most about the NPCs. 
It is your ability to ASK. If you ASK your NPCs, they will do their special 
abilities. Here are the infos about the NPCs in the game. Take note that if a 
NPC kills an enemy, its experience points will still be added to you. 

HEROINE
-------
Attack: None
You’ll meet her near Topple in the beginning of the game. She is a mysterious 
girl with mysterious powers. You’ll eventually know who she really is and what 
her destiny is. 

ASK: If you ASK her, she will slowly regenerate your HPs. 

MYSTERIOUS MAN
--------------
Attack: Fireball
You’ll meet him inside the Cave of Marsh. He will immediately offer his help 
in finding the Mirror that can help you find the Girl (Heroine).

ASK: If you ASK him, he will teach you how to use the item, Mattock

WATTS
-----
Attack: Axe
He’s a member of the Tribe of Dwarves. He will join you in finding the Silver 
to be able to enter Gaia. 

ASK: If you ASK him, he will let you shop for Items. 

BOGARD
------
Attack: Sword
The Old, but still powerful, Gemma Knight will help you save the girl at the 
Airship. 

ASK: If you ASK him, he will teach you how to use MAX attacks. 

CHOCOBO
-------
Attack: None
You can find its Egg near Menos. It will think that you are its parents and 
will join you.

ASK: If you ASK the chocobo, it will give you a ride on its back. During
     this time, any enemy cannot harm you. Later, Chocobo will be 
     able to navigate the waters.

AMANDA
------
Attack: Dagger
You’ll meet her at the beginning of the Game but she won’t join you yet. 
Later, you’ll meet her again at the Cave of Medusa to find the Tears of Medusa 
to free Lester from the spell of Davias. 

ASK: If you ASK her, he will heal you of Petrify (Ston) condition. 

LESTER
------
Attack: Arrows
He is the brother of amanda. You will see him in the form of a parrot inside 
Davias Mansion. He will join you upon breaking the spell which is upon him. 

ASK: If you ASK him, he’ll change the background music (pretty useless)

MARCIE
------
Attack: Laser Beams
Marcie is a robot built by Dr. Bowow. Marcie lived in the Dime Tower ever 
since. 

ASK: If you ASK Marcie, your MP will be restored to its full.

8. Leveling Up
--------------

The level up system here is quite different. You can create your own type of 
character with level ups. You can create a very powerful character in terms of 
physical attacks. You can also create a very powerful magician with nasty 
magic attacks. 

When your character levels up, after gaining a specific amount of Experience, 
you must choose from 4 types. 

Power - This will increase the Power rating of your character. This will
        make and battle with monsters easier.

Stamina - This level up concentrates on your defense. You can now have less
          damage from enemy attacks. And you’ll have a big HP boost.

Wisdom - This focuses on the Magic ratings of your characters. This level up
         Will give you additional MP plus additional damage caused by your
         Magic attacks to your enemies. 

Will - Leveling up your Will, lessens the time that your Will Bar fills up. 


9. MAX Attacks
--------------

When your Will Bar, at the bottom of the screen, fills up, you can use your 
weapons’ MAX Attack. Here’s what will happen to your weapons during a MAX 
Attack: (Note that MAX attacks increase the amount of damage you inflict on 
your enemies)

Sword - There are 2 types of MAX attack for the sword.
        1. Charge Attack - Press the A Button + Directional pad. Your 
                character will charge pointing to the direction depending
                on what direction you pushed in the Directional pad.
        2. Spin Attack - Press and hold the A button. This will make your
                character spin his sword. During the spinning of your
                character, you can move him around by pressing the 
                directional pad.

Axe - At MAX, your character will throw the Axe in front of him. Doing this,
      , the thrown Axe can still chop down trees. 

Chain - At MAX, the chain will be longer than usual. This will enable you to 
        reach poles which you cannot reach during normal chain attacks.

Spear - At MAX, the Spear will be thrown in the direction your pushed on the
        directional pad.

Sickle - At MAX, the area of the spin of the Sickle will become wider. 
         You can still cut off Herbs. 

Morning Star - At MAX, the length of the Morning Star will be much more 
               Longer than normal.

D. CHARACTER STATS
------------------

The Character Stat of your Character determines how your characters fare with 
the enemies around him. The higher his stat, the higher is his chance of 
beating stronger opponents. Here are the different characters stats and their 
explanations:

HP = Hit Points. This is the total amount of damage your character can 
     endure. If it reaches 0, your character will be dead. 
MP = Magic Points. This determines the total number of Magic you can use. 
     Take note also that not all magic abilities need 1 MP, some needs more.
E = Experience. The total experience your character gained from fighting 
    enemies. After reaching a certain amount of Experience, your character’s
    stats will go up. 
AP = The total attack power. This determines how strong your character is at
     beating opponents. The higher this stat is, the easier the enemies can
     be beaten.
DP = The total defense power. This stat determines how your character 
     endures magic and physical attacks. The higher the DP is, the lesser
     the HP loss.
Stamn = This is just like the DP. 
Power = This is just like the AP.
Wisdm = This will determine how strong your magic attacks will be. This also
        affects your total MP.
Will = this determines how fast your Will Power Bar fills up. 


--------------------
E. STATUS CONDITIONS
--------------------

The status conditions mainly reflect the status of your character in the 
battlefield. Some conditions affect how your character attacks or defends. 
Sometimes, conditions affect the stats of your character. Here are the 
different status conditions with their explanation and Cure.

POIS
----
Poisoned Status. The HP of your character will slowly deplete. To cure it, use 
the item “Pure”, or use the Magic “Heal”. 

MOOG
----
Moogle Condition. Your DP will drop to 0 therefore, even the weakest enemies 
will deal quite a big damage to you. You’ll turn into an image of the moogle 
in this condition. There is no known item that can cure it. Although there is 
a moogle item, you can’t use it during Moog. There 2 known ways to cure it. 
First, just let time pass by. Second, ASK your chocobo.

STON
----
Stone Condition. Your character will be petrified and cannot move. This makes 
you very vulnerable to enemy attacks. In the contrary, you can still attack in 
place. To cure it, use the item “Soft” or use the magic “Heal”. You can also 
ASK Amanda to cure it for you. 

DARK
----
Darkness Condition. During this time, your screen will be dark and you can 
barely see the field/room. You can also see the enemies but it’s really 
confusing. To cure it, use the item “Eyedrop”, or use the Magic “Heal”.

BEGINNING
=========

After the rolling of the Prologue, it’s time to name our Hero. There isn’t any 
default name given, so you can choose whatever name you like. For the sake of 
the walkthrough, let’s name it Sumo, just like what’s in the Manual. After 
that, name the Heroine of the game. For the sake of the walkthrough, let’s use

BOSS: 2-HEADED DRAGON
#############################################################################
EXP gained:         10
GP:                 95
Attacks:            Fireballs
Preferred Weapon:   Sickle

This battle will be hard, if you don’t know what to do. The only way to damage 
it is to hit it in the head. The best strategy here is to use the sickle and 
always stay below the hydra. Doing this, when the hydra comes forward, you can 
hit it with the sickle away from the path of the fireballs. After a few hits, 
it will go down. 

If you are cornered, and you have the Iron Shield, you can just stand in front 
of the fireballs (facing their direction) and the shield will negate it. In 
simpler terms, you can block the fireballs. Of course, you can’t block the 
head of the Dragon when it comes near you.
#############################################################################

After the fight, you’ll see 2 chests appear in the middle. The first chest 
contains your second magic, Fire. After getting the chest, hack it away and 
get the second chest for the Mirror. The man will tell you to show it to the 
guy at Kett’s. After that, the man will leave and you’ll be taken 
automatically to the world map. Head to Kett’s right away. Make sure to 
prepare your character again.

BOSS: MEGAPEDE
#############################################################################
EXP gained:         48
GP:                 161
Attacks:            Surprise!
Preferred Weapon:   Chain

This battle will be fairly easy. Just remember the pattern it does. It will 
move in a S formation. Always remember that it can only be damaged by hitting 
it’s head. Any attack to the body is null. He has only one attack (which I 
think is intentionally created by Square). After you beat him, as with most of 
the bosses, it’s body will explode. The explosion can hurt your character. The 
Magapede, due to its size, especially its length, will scatter the explosion 
in a very wide area so, right after you hear the explosion of the monster, 
quickly move away from it! Take note that the head will follow you after you 
kill it :)
#############################################################################

automatically taken outside the Mine. Here, Watts will take care of the

powerful Armor in the game, the Samurai Armor.

SUPER TIP #2:
The Devil Heads here that can only be destroyed by using the Nuke Magic, give 
off X-Ethers. If you do the same thing as you did to the Ninja, you’ll be able 
to get the most powerful Helmet in the game, the Samurai Helmet.

SUPER TIP #3:
The Elephants near the last boss will give you a Unicorn. If you do the same 
thing as you did with the Ninjas and the Devil Heads, you’ll get the most 
powerful shield in the game, the Aegis Shield. It can deflect ANY projectile 
attack at you. Even the strong force balls of the last boss.
\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\

Now, move on until you enter the Temple itself. Inside, just go south one 
screen and then go right two screens. Next, go north two screens. In here, 
equip a key and go north one screen and then use the ladders. In the next 
area, prepare your character for a boss fight. After the preparations, go 
north one screen and face...

BOSS: DRAGON
#############################################################################
EXP gained:         0
GP:                 257
Attacks:            Fire Ball
Preferred Weapon:   Flame Flail or Thunder Spear

This is a very easy fight. Just use your Flail or Spear to attack the Head 
ONLY. You can’t damage any other part of its body. 
#############################################################################

After the fight, use the teleporter that appears. In the next screen, just go 
right one screen (You’ll see the Devil Heads here so use Super Tip #2) and use 
the teleporter. 

In the next screen, go left one screen. Here, go north one screen and then go 
left two screens and use the teleporter at the end. In the next area, just go 
right to the next screen and use the teleporter. In the next area, go south 
one screen. Here, destroy the left wall then go left two screens. Next, go 
south one screen. Here, go left one screen and use the teleporter. Next, go 
left one screen and then go south one screen. Now, in this room, there’s a 
trapped monster in the bottom of the screen. Now, equip your Ice Magic and use 
to turn the monster into a snowman. Your Ice can pass through the circle 
thingy (whatever) so you can reach it. It’s easy to guide your icicle, just 
try again if you fail. If you successfully turned the monster into a snowman, 
the right door will open. Now, go right one screen and use the ladder. In the 
next screen, prepare your character again for another boss. If you’re prepared 
go north one screen.

BOSS: RED DRAGON
#############################################################################
EXP gained:         0
GP:                 257
Attacks:            Fire Ball
Preferred Weapon:   Flame Flail or Thunder Spear

It’s just the same as the previous dragon. Just use your Flail or Spear to 
attack the Head ONLY. You also can’t damage any other part of its body. 
#############################################################################

After you beat the Red Dragon, use the teleporter that will appear. In the 
next area, just go left one screen and use the teleporter. Now, go right one 
screen and then go north one screen and use the teleporter. In the next area, 
go right one screen and use the teleporter. 

Now, go south one screen and then go right one screen. Now, let’s call this 
screen Point BB. From here, go right one screen and use the teleporter. In the 
next screen, go left one screen using a key. Use the Recovery Spring and then 
go back to Point BB. From here, go south one screen and use the teleporter. 
Now, go left one screen and use the teleporter. When you reach the next area, 
go right five screens. Here, you’ll see a lot of floor switches formed in a 
rectangle. You have to change a Ninja into a snowman and put it in the correct 
Floor Switch. In the left COLUMN of switches, the correct one is the ones 
which is second from below. Put the snowman there and the door to the north 
will open. Now, go north three screens and use the ladder. In the next screen, 
just like a while ago, prepare your character again. If you’re ready, go north 
one screen.

BOSS: DRAGON ZOMBIE
#############################################################################
EXP gained:         0
GP:                 270
Attacks:            Fire Ball, Charge Attack
Preferred Weapon:   Flame Flail or Thunder Spear

The Dragon Zombie looks like the two-headed dragon you fought way back at the 
Cave of Marsh. All you have to do is to attack the head with your flail or 
spear. Beware though, if his fireballs hit you or you accidentally touched it, 
it will deal a big amount of damage compared to the previous 2 dragons.
#############################################################################

After the battle, use the teleporter that will appear. From here on, the path 
is pretty straight forward to the final boss! You can now use my Super Tip #3 
to get the Aegis shield for the best protection. Now, on the way, you’ll see 
Fuji’s Mom. She’ll tell you that you have been a great Gemma knight. She will 
now bestow you with the most powerful weapon in the game, the Excalibur! Equip 
it right away then move on. NOW READ CAREFULLY. When you reach the area where 
you can see 2 big trees to the left and right and a big ladder in the middle, 
prepare your character for the final battle! I recommend level 50+ when you’re 
here. You can level up your character by beating the elephants here until you 
reach level 50. If you’re ready go up the ladder and fight Julius!

NOTE: YOU WILL RECEIVE EXP AND GP!!! This very odd! The EXP is useful as you 
can level up while you fight Julius. But the GP? It’s useless.

FINAL BOSS: JULIUS (3 Forms)  162438 - 61535
#############################################################################
Note: You should use the Excalibur Sword. But if you want some challenge, try 
using the Broad Sword! :) And, in all three forms of Julius, the charging 
attack of the MAXed sword is the most efficient attack to use.

FIRST FORM:
-----------
Attacks:  Sword
EXP:      638
GP:       818

Julius will be in triplets. You just have to attack and beat all three until 
all of them disappears. After that, prepare for Julius’ second form.

SECOND FORM:
------------
Attacks:  Lightning Bolts, Charging Attack
EXP:      0
GP:       162

He will turn into a monster now and Julius will be harder to avoid this time. 
He’ll be flying on top while throwing Lightning bolts. From time to time, he 
will also charge downwards so try to be always by his side. He can charge from 
left to right or vice versa though. 

THIRD FORM:
-----------
Attacks:     Teleportation, Force Ball
EXP and GP:  No more

He will turn into a head with 2 arms. He will teleport from one place to 
another. There is a pattern but I won’t include it in the guide. Also, while 
teleporting from place to place, he will be throwing force balls at you. With 
you Aegis Shield, the force balls are NOTHING! He will be hard to beat, but 
with Fuji as your companion, you can ASK her to regenerate your HP while 
fighting. What I did was I also equipped the Nuke at the same time, attacking 
with the Excalibur. These made short work of him!
#############################################################################

After you beat all 3 forms of Julius, you’ll see a short scene. It’s somewhat 
sad but happy at the same time. I won’t spoil it, just finish the game. :) 
Well, that’s it for the best RPG for the Gameboy (My rechargeable batteries 
got a beating). So, thank you for reading the guide and congratulations! I 
hope you enjoyed the game! Until then. C’ya!

                          =-=-=-=-=--=-=-=-=-=-=-=-
                                GAMEPLAY TIPS
                          -=-=-=-=-==-=-=-=-=-=-=-=

1. The Moogle (Moog) condition cannot be cured even with the Moogle Item. If 
it is inflicted to your character, there are 2 ways to avoid contact with the 
enemy (these will also use up the time so that Moogle will be cured). First, 
ASK your Chocobo for a ride! Your enemies can’t hurt you at all. And then, ASK 
again and your Moog condition will be removed. Second, stay at the edge of the 
current area (in world map/field) or stay in front of the doorways (dungeons, 
towns, etc.) and go back and forth between this area and the other area. This 
will use the time and prevents enemy contact.

2. You are poisoned! (Pois Condition) You don’t want to use Pure or the Heal 
Magic. Stay at the edge of the current area (in world map/field) or stay in 
front of the doorways (dungeons, towns, etc.) and go back and forth between 
this area and the other area. This will use up the time but will definitely 
cure Poison after a while. Doing this also lessens the effect of the poison. 
Your HP will drop at most 2 times only. 

3. Another strategy for Pois. If the Heroine is with you, ASK her. This will 
counter the Pois effect. Every time Pois cuts down your HP, the heroine will 
heal that same amount of HP lost. Therefore, no change of HP will occur when 
in Pois Condition.

4. Yet another Pois Strategy. If you are alone and you don’t have items. 
You’re low on life, surrounded by enemies, and Poisoned at the same time, what 
do you do? Attack like crazy! Hitting the A button rapidly will make your 
character attack continuously. This will slow down the Pois condition. It will 
stop the depletion of your HP. Take note also that this does not mean that the 
Pois condition is cured.

5. In towns, YOU CAN KILL THE PEOPLE! Just be patient though. They have a high 
HP and won’t go down easily. When you use your strongest weapons, about 3-5 
hits will do. If you beat them, they will say their lines afterwards.

6. If you want to get a lot of experience in a certain place but you are 
afraid that the enemies will not respawn anymore? If you have 4 enemies in an 
area, if you beat them all and come back, there will be no more enemies, 
right? But, if you left one of them, alive, and then came back again, the 
enemies will respawn. This is good for leveling up and collecting GPs. 

7. When you strike a wall with a weapon and you hear a cranking sound, use 
your mattock or Morning Star to destroy that wall and reveal a new path. 

8. If an empty chest gets in your way, destroy it with your weapon and it will 
simply, disappear. 

9. If there are a lot of rocks, and they are beside each other, you can break 
them in one shot. With the Mattock, stand in between the rocks and then press 
the B button. The 2 rocks will disappear. With the Morning Star, just attack 
the rocks head on. 

10. Once you acquire the Morning Star, you won’t have any need for the 
Mattocks anymore. So, just sell ALL your mattocks to save space in the Items 
Inventory. This will enable you to get more important items such as Keys.

You should be at least Level 50. You won’t have any problems at all when
