[SpectralOrion] Got a pretty good solution that got me both pieces of armor rather quickly. Rather than going back and forth between rooms, you can camp in one room.

1. Go to a room with ninjas. Kill them. Save. Soft reset the game, load, and a new set of ninjas will spawn. You can farm EXP and Elixirs while you're looking for Samurai Armor.

2. Go to a room with the demon heads (likely the very first room of the Temple of Mana). Do the same exact thing to get the Samurai Helm, except kill the demons with the Nuke spell. Save and use up whatever Ethers and Elixirs on hand to get your MP back. And of course, leveling up will help too.

Got both in less than 30 minutes.
