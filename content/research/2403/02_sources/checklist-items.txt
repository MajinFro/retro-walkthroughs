3. - Using Items
==============================================================================

To use Items:

1. Press Start to open the menu, then select ITEM.
2. Place the cursor next to the item you wish to use.
3. Press the A Button to select the item. Press A once more to set the item.
4. Exit all menus and press the B Button when you wish to use said item.

Notes:
-With Keys, just equip it and walk into the door you wish to open.
-Most Special/Story items will be automatically used when the time is right.

To use Equipment:

1. Press Start to open the menu, then select EQUIP.
2. Place the cursor next to the weapon/armor you wish to use.
3. Press the A Button to select the item. Press A once more to equip it.

Notes:
-You can only change Armor/Weapons if you have Armor/Weapons to switch with
 (in other words, you always have to have some kind of Armor/Weapon
  equipped).

Item Labels:

Buff:     <-- Raises stats temporary.
Junk:     <-- Can be sold for money.
Healing:  <-- Restorative items.
Magic:    <-- Disposable magic spells (uses no MP).
Special:  <-- Objects required to push the story along.
Tool:     <-- Utilities, such as Keys.

Location Labels:

Boss:  <-- Obtained from a boss.
Buy:   <-- Purchasable items.
Chest: <-- Items found in treasure chests.
Drop:  <-- Items that monsters drop.
Story: <-- Items obtained as part of the story.

Some items can be obtained in multiple places/ways.

9. - Q&A
==============================================================================

Q: Where did these monster names come from?
A: I tried to use names that were most popular (Read: Ones that a majority of
   other FAQs used) to help with crosschecking. In some instances, I had to
   just make up a descriptive name. Thankfully most monsters are easy enough
   to figure out and are normally locked to areas that would make sense (IE:
   ice monster in Snow Fields).

Q: Hey, more than one monster drops [ITEM NAME HERE].
A: E-Mail it to me, I'll add it and credit you.

Q: Do elemental themed shields and helms not reduce corresponding elemental
   damage?
A: No. Helms increase defense and grant no further bonuses. Shields have no
   stats tied to them and are meant to block certain attacks while also
   providing the hero with the ability to push weaker monsters out of the way.
