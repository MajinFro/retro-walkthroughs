Storyline
---------

The world of Final Fantasy Adventure is infused with the power of the
Mana Tree, the source of all life in this world. The Tree of Mana is
guarded by the Gemma Knights, who defend it against the evils of the
Glaive Empire. Now the empire of Glaive, head by Dark Lord and his
servant the sorcerer Julius. Our Hero, a warrior of great renown,
sneaked into Glaive Castle in hopes of discovering their evil plans.
Captured by the sorcerer Julius, and to do this, he must fight every
day. One day after fighting the Hero comes the Hero was enslaved and
brought to the Arena. Here he must fight to survive back and finds his
dear friend Willy dying. Willy tells the Hero, "Mana is in Danger
now... We must let the knights of Gemma know about it." As Willy passes
away, the Hero swears vengeance and his quest begins.


Tree of Mana grows with the energy of
will from each and every thing of this world.

It grows high above the clouds in the air
on top of Mount Illusia.

Legend tells that it gives eternal power
to the one who touched it.

Dark Lord was trying to find the way to
the Tree of Mana and get the mighty
power to conquer the world.

Directional Pad
---------------
The directional pad allows you to move your character on the screen.
Your character can be moved in only four directions, up, down, left,
and right, but not diagonal. The directional pad can also move the
cursor on the menu screen.

A Button
--------
- On the World Screen, the A button can be used to make your character
attack with the weapon he has equipped.
- On the Menu Screen, the A button is used to execute the command that
you have selected. Attack with weapon and select a command on the menu
screen.

B Button
---------
- On the World Screen, the B button can be used to use Magic or an Item
that you are equipped with.
- On the Menu Screen, the B button can be used to cancel a command that
has been selected or to exit a menu screen.

Start
-----
The Start button opens the screen for Item/Magic/Equip/Ask commands,
the weapon and magic your character is currently equipped with, and
your character's condition.

Select
------
The Select button opens the screen for Save/Map/Status commands.

Using an Item:
You can move the cursor to different items my moving the directional
pad. To select the item of your choice, press the A button twice. To
use the item, press the B button once.

Changing an Item:
Move the cursor to the item that you want to move. Press the A button
once, and then a second cursor will appear. Move this cursor to the
spot where you wish to move you item. Then press the A button and the
item will move. You can also use this process to exchange different
items on the item screen.

Discarding an Item:
To discard an item, move the cursor to the item that you want to
discard. Press the A button once, and then a second cursor will appear.
Move this cursor to the Trash Can and press the A button. The item will
now disappear from the screen.
*NOTE* Once an item has been placed in the Trash Can, you will not be
able to get it back from the Trash Can.

to waster MP's accidentally by casting a spell. To remove the magic
spell that you are equipped with, you should exchange the magic with
any item that cannot be operated accidentally by pressing the B button.

----------------------------
Saving and Loading your Game
----------------------------

Saving:
The game Final Fantasy Adventure has 2 Slots. You can only save 1 Game
for every slot. This means that you can either have two different games
saved, or have save the same game saved in two different slots.

Loading:
You can only load a game by accessing the continue option during the
main menu.

Resting/Turning Off:
To reset the game you must press the A, B, Select, and Start buttons at
the same time. To access the games main menu you may also turn the
GameBoy off, and then back on. However, when you do either of these
methods, your game will not be saved.

Moving your character
---------------------

You move your character around with the directional pad. By pressing
left on the directional pad your character will move left. The same
applies with pressing the control pad in other directions, right=right,
up=up and down=down.

In the world screen, you'll also see some towns with people in it. You
can talk to these people, or simply avoid them. For the most part,
talking to people is not really important, but it does make the game-
play more interesting in my opinion.

Some terrain your character will be able to move over, but some he will
not. By using weapons, your character can cut or smash terrain that
blocks your path. Here is a list of terrain that allows travel, doesn't
allow travel, and terrain that can be cut or smashed.

Geography of the World of Final Fantasy Adventure:

   Terrain that your character can travel over
   -------------------------------------------
   Meadows - A region covered with low grass
   Marshes - An area with long damp grass
   Deserts - Stretches of golden sand
   Vines - Located on mountain walls
   Snowfields - A region covered with snow
   Desert of Crystal - A region with sharp crystals
   Field of Floating Rocks - A region with large floating rocks
   Bridges - Log bridges that cross over rivers

   Terrain that your character CANNOT travel over
   ----------------------------------------------
   *Forests - A region covered with trees
   Bushes - A region covered with large leafy plants
   Rock-Walls - The walls of mountains
   Oasis's - Fountains of water found in the desert
   Oceans - Large bodies of water around different continents
   Rivers - Small stretches of water crossing the land
   Lakes - Small bodies of water surrounded by land

   Terrain that can be cut or broken to allow travel
   -------------------------------------------------
   Forests - Trees that can be cut by an Axe.
   Marshes - Vines that can be cut by Sickles
   Floating Rocks - Rocks that can be smashed by the Morning Star


Geography of Dungeons:

   Stairs/Doors/Damage
   -------------------
   Ascending - Stairs that allow you to move up a floor
   Descending - Stairs that allow you to move down a floor
   Door - Can be opened by walking through, but some need Keys.
   Spikes/Lave/Barriers - When touched deal damage to Hero.

   Terrain that can be cut or broken to allow travel
   -------------------------------------------------
   Ferns - Can be cut with a Sickle
   Thorns - Can be cut with an Axe
   Rocks - Can be smashed with a Mattock or Morning Star
   Pots - Can be smashed with a Mattock or Morning Star

-------------
Battle System
-------------

Attacking
---------

Weapons:
The weapon attack is the most basic kind of attack. To attack enemies,
move your character so that the enemy is in range of your weapon. Then
press the A button. Every time you successfully hit the enemy he will
receive damage. Once his HP's reach 0, he will die.

Magic:
There are six types of magic that can be used for attacking. To use
magic on an enemy you must have the magic equipped. Then, press the B
button. Some types of magic only fly in straight lines, while others
will require you to manipulate the magic using the Directional Pad.

Items:
There are eight different types of Items that can be used for attacking
the enemy. Item attacks are used the same way as magic attacks and have
the same general effect as a magic spell would. However, the damage
dealt might be different, and unlike magic, Items have a limited number
of uses.

Defending
---------
When walking on the world screen or in dungeons, you will encounter
monsters. If you touch a monster, you will receive damage at once. The
only way to prevent damage is to either to avoid the monster, or touch
him with your Shield. Your Shield will only work if it is equipped, you
have the appropriate type of shield, and your level is higher than that
monster. If you have met all these requirements, you can push the
monster from your path using the shield.

------------------
Weapon Will Powers
------------------

When you are equipped with a weapon, your Will Meter (found at the
bottom of the screen), will increase until it is at its maximum. When
it reaches its max, your weapon will attack in a unique way. The same
weapon types will have the same unique attack. (i.e., the Broad Sword
and the dragon sword do the same thing) The different types of weapons
below are listed in the order that they are found in and with a
description of what the weapon will do.

Type-          DESCRIPTION (MAX)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Swords         Spin Attack - Press and hold the A button. This will
               make your character spin with his sword. While
               spinning you can move him with the directional pad.

               Charge Attack - Press the A Button and the Directional
               pad. Your character will charge in the direction you
               pushed the Directional pad in.

Axes           It will be thrown in the direction you're facing.

Sickle         The Sickle will extend longer than normal

Chains         The Chain will extend longer than normal

Spears         The Spear will be thrown in the direction you're facing.

Morning Star   The Morning Star will extend longer than normal.

-----
Items
-----

Items can be very useful in the game. Many items have properties that
magic will not be able to replicate. The different types of items in
the game are, items that recover your HP/MP, cure your status, sold for
money, open doors and create new routes, and items that can affect your
status. Items can only be used a certain number of times, so make sure
you are stocked up before you go exploring different caves.

-----------
Leveling Up
-----------

Every time you defeat a monster you will gain Experience and Gold.
After gaining a certain amount of Experience you will gain a level. A
message will appear on the screen telling you that your character
leveled up, and that your HP/MP has also been restored. Now a new
screen will appear. It asks you to choose what type of stat you want to
boost. This will allow you to custom make a character. You can create a
very powerful character in terms of physical attacks, a very powerful
magician with strong magic attacks, or anything in between.

The stat you choose will be the primary stat that will be boosted, some
stats will also boost a different stat, but to a lesser degree.

Types of Stat Growth
--------------------

Power
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
This will increase the Power rating of your character by 2 points. This
will raise the attack power of your character making it easier to
defeat monsters. This will also increase your Stamina and Will ratings
by 1 point.

Stamina
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
This will increase the Stamina rating of your character by 2 points.
This raises your HP and also concentrates on your character's defense.
You will now have less damage dealt to you by enemy attacks. This will
also increase your Power and Will ratings by 1 point.

Wisdom
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
This will increase the Wisdom rating of your character by 2 points.
This will give you additional MP and raise the damage caused by your
Magic attacks to your enemies. This will also increase your Stamina and
Will ratings by 1 point.

Will
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
This will increase the Will rating of your character by 2 points. This
will increase the speed of your gauge allowing your Will Bar to fill up
quicker. This will also increase your Stamina and Will ratings by 1
point.

-----------------
Status Conditions
-----------------

-> There are four different types of status changes that your character
can be afflicted with. These conditions are caused by attacks from
enemies.

1. Poison
---------
Effect- Character loses HP's rapidly
Remedy- Magic of Heal or Pure Potion

2. Darkness
-----------
Effect- Character is blind
Remedy- Magic of Heal or Eye Drop

3. Petrified
------------
Effect- Character is turned to stone and unable to move
Remedy- Magic of Heal or Soft Potion

4. Moogle
----------
Effect- Character is changed to a furry creature and Def drops to 0
Remedy- Magic of Heal or Moogle Potion

* This is a tip on how to gain some quick levels. If you defeat all the
monsters in once screen you will notice that if you exit a screen and
then go back in, that the monsters will not be there. However, there is
a way to get them back. You must go through three other different
screens and then go to the screen where you killed the monsters. The
monsters will be replenished. Monsters will also be replenished if
there is one or more monsters still alive on the screen. So what you
need to do is first, fight all the monsters in a screen, but leave one
alive. Then exit the screen and go back in. The monsters will have been
replenished. You can do this as many times as you want. This trick will
let you gain a huge amount of gold and experience. This trick works
especially well in the screen with the Lizard Men.

______________________________________________________________________
BOSS Name:          Hydra
Recommended Level:  13
GP:                 94
EXP gained:         10
Attacks:            Fireballs
Best Equipment:     Sickle
Items Gained:       Magic Book of Fire, Mirror

Strategy: To defeat this monster move north as the monster moves south.
Then attack the heads as the monster comes up. Once he is moving to the
top of the screen go south. Then once the Hydra goes down, move up and
attack the heads. This strategy sounds harder than it really is when
you are playing the game. The Iron Shield is able to block any
fireballs and your Sickle can destroy any of the fireballs. The only
real danger is if the Hydra hits you with his body. Keep on doing this
until the Hydra dies.
_______________________________________________________________________

After defeating the Hydra two treasure chests will appear. Open the
first one to learn the spell Fire. Destroy the chest with your weapon
and open the second for the Mirror. The Man tells you to show it to the
man at Kett's and then the man disappears. You are then warped back
outside the cave. Travel back to Kett's and save your game outside the
building.

BOSS Name:          Lich
Recommended Level:  48
GP:                 220
EXP gained:         198
Attacks:            Skulls
Best Equipment:     Morning Star, Cure Spell
Items Gained:       Nuke Spell

Strategy: The Lich moves in a figure eight pattern. He will
occasionally deviate from the pattern, flying off the screen and
reappearing on the other side of the screen. He will then start the
figure eight pattern again. The best strategy is to have your Cure
spell equipped and have the Morning Star equipped. Just stand in the
middle of the screen and strike the Lich. The most damaging weapon that
the Lich possess are his flying skulls. However, the Morning Star and
other weapons will destroy the skulls that the Lich throws. If you hit
the Lich at the right time it will pass right over you without dealing
damage. If you do this properly the Hero won't take damage. Watch your
HP and just hit the Lich alternating with the Cure Spell until the Lich
dies. This is one of the toughest bosses, so if you die, keep on trying
until you win.

Strategy: This fight is very easy, but remember that those spikes take

By about this time you should be around level 55-60. From outside the

Or if you need to re-supply, go back to Ish. From outside of Ish, the

As soon as you walk in, Dr. Bowow's robot Marcie will join you. If you
ASK him he recovers your MP. There are tablets scattered throughout the
tower. I recommend reading them if you want to read about the story
line of this game. The directions I give do not include directions to
the tablets. If you are interested in reading them, use the map of this
tower.

* Floor 1 * Go west, north twice, east, and use a Mattock/Morning Star

* Floor 5 * Go south and go into a bed to rest. Your HP and MP will be
restored. Go north and up the stairs.

* Floor 6 * There are two monsters surrounded by trees to your south.
You need to freeze both monsters, which can be difficult, since you
don't want to kill them when you chop down the trees. Position your
character in the bottom corners, facing the walls away from the trees.
Now swing your Axe and the trees will be chopped and the enemies
unharmed. Now freeze the monsters and go north. Use a Key on the door
and go east. Go south and up the stairs.

* Floor 7 * Heal-up and save your game. Go north and the fight will
begin.
____________________________________________________________________
BOSS Name:          Garuda
Recommended Level:  55
GP:                 265
EXP gained:         221
Attacks:            Feathers
Best Equipment:     Flame Chain , Cure Spell
Items Gained:       None

Strategy: Garuda will dive across the screen and throw feathers at you.
Garuda will also swoop down to the middle of the screen and drop more
feathers on you. The trick is to wait in the corner below, but at a
striking distance where the tip of your Flame Chain just reaches
Garuda. Use your Flame Chain to destroy the feathers Garuda drops and
to hit Garuda himself. When he swoops down he will miss you. Then go to
the other corner and attack Garuda again. Follow this pattern until the
monster is defeated.
_______________________________________________________________________

Once Garuda is killed go west twice. Just as you are ready to walk on
land, a part of the tower collapses. Marcie tells you that he will
throw you over and then jump himself. After he throws you across the
gap, the hero tells Marcie to jump. Sadly, Marcie is unable to... The
tower will then collapse carrying Marcie with it. The robot's last
words are for you to save the world. Once the tower collapses go west
three times to the waterfall. The waterfall fall will reverse its flow.
Walk into the waterfall and it will carry you up to the top of the
mountain.

XI. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ's)
=======================================================================

Last Updated: 11/01/2000

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Questions
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

-> This list is compiled from various emails that I have received.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. What does the Ask command do?

A: The Ask command allows you to use the special abilities of certain
NPC's. The abilities of NPC's are very important to your quest.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
2. I just got out of the town Jadd and I cannot find the cave of
Medusa. Could you help me out?

A: The Medusa Cave is hidden. To find the cave follow these directions.
From outside of Jadd, go south, east, south, south, east, south, south,
west, and north. Now dismount from your Chocobo. You should see two
separate palms trees to the north. Now walk a sideways figure 8 around
the palm trees. After walking a few complete sideways figure's 8 the
cave will open.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
3. I don't have any Keys or Mattocks left and the only way to go up a
floor is to go through a locked door. Do I have to start a new game, or
is there something I can do?

A: There are two monsters that will leave these items behind. A
Skeleton will leave behind Keys when it drops a treasure chest. A
FaceOrb will leave behind Mattocks when it drops a treasure chest.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
4. I am stuck behind a treasure chest and can't get by. Now what?

A: After opening a treasure chest, you can destroy it with your weapon.
If you can't open it, it's because you are full with other items. So,
throw a useless item into the Trash Can and open the treasure chest.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
5. I am near Jadd and I can't get past the poison gas. Help!?

A: To get past the poison gas you have to kill Davias. To do this, you
first need to go to Davias's mansion, free Lester, and then help him
avenge his sister.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
6. What does the Morning Star do?

A: The Morning Star can attack enemies and break walls like Mattocks.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
7. I'm in the Cave of Ruins, and I just beat the Mantis Ant. I went
right into this room with waterfalls on each side, and a switch. I
stepped on the switch and nothing happened. What do I do?

A: To activate the switch, you must have the Rusty Sword equipped
before you step on the floor switch.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
8. Which crystal do I nuke?

A: From outside of Ish go one screen west, north twice, and west. There
are two crystals sticking out in the middle of the screen. The right
one is the one you nuke.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
9. What are the most powerful weapons and armor in the game, and where
can I find them.

A: The most powerful sword is the Excalibur and the most powerful armor
you can find is the Aegis Shield, Samurai Helmet, and the Samurai Armor?
These pieces of equipment can be found by killing certain enemies in
the Tower of Gemma. These are the three different monsters that may
leave behind a special weapon or piece of armor.

Monster       Treasure Chest
---------------------------------------
Ninja         Elixir or Samurai Armor
Demon Head    X-Ether or Samurai Helmet
Elephant      Aegis Shield

XII. Tips, Tricks, and Strategies
=======================================================================

1. The Morning Star can also be used like a Mattock.

2. Ask Commands: You can take very good advantage of this command,
especially the ones that refuel your HP and MP.

3. Since NPC's attack in the direction that you move, you can help
guide their attacks.

4. Sound test: Press Down + B + Start at the title screen.

5. Easy Money/Exp: Enemies will return to a screen if...
      1) You visit three different screens and then go back or
      2) If you leave one monster in a screen and then come back. The
         monsters will have regenerated. This will let you gain money
         or exp very quickly.

6. Level Upgrades: When you increase in levels always alternate between
Power or Wisdom. This will lead to an even build up of status.

7. Selling Weapons: Never sell any of the main weapons unless you get
an upgraded version of it. Example: Only sell your Broad Sword after
you get the Silver Sword.

8. Killing People: It is possible to kill people in towns, but they
have a lot of HP. To defeat them, just keep on hitting them with your
weapon with the weapon at its max Will Power.

9. Treasure Chest Tip: This is a trick you can use throughout the game,
but is more helpful during the end of the game. What is in a treasure
chest is not determined when the chest appears, but instead when you
open it. This fact can be taken advantage of when trying to get the
Samurai Armor/Helmet and Aegis Shield. First, you must have your item
inventory full, but still have room in your Equipment inventory. Then
kill the monster carrying the equipment you want until one of them
drops a chest. Then try to open the chest. You will get a few "Can't
carry!" messages, but the chest will remain closed. Eventually you will
get armor instead of an item. This way you don't have to kill a ton of
those stupid elephants and only get those useless (as far as I can
tell) unicorn items, but instead need only get one to drop a chest.

10. If an empty treasure chest is blocking your path, you can destroy
it by attacking it with your weapon.

11. Warps-

Here are some fun tricks to try out!

Warp 2- In the cave where you get the Ice magic, go to the corner room
right before you get the Ice magic. Equip a sword, and put your will to
the max. While sliding on the two plates, do the max-move to the right.
Press left when you're flying back, and keep pressing left. You'll warp
to the cave where you get the star.

Warp 3- In the woods west of Wendel, there is one screen where a road
runs west to south and another road runs west to east on top of it. On
the bottom road, face west. Equip any ax and go to the western limit of
the screen. Make sure your limit gage is NOT full and attack to the
left, at the same time moving to the left screen. You'll warp to the
snowy fields.

Warp 4/5- In the last dungeon, there is a long zig-zag road. On the top
screen, stand on the farthest place you can go without entering the
forest-like place. Repeat the following until you go over the top of
the screen and warp to the fire cave. 1. press up 2. press "select"
while you're moving one step. (do this quickly) 3. save, reset Use this
method in the entrance to snowy fields and you don't have to get off
the Chocobo. (You can even enter the cave with the Chocobo)

Warp ?- In the bridge that leads to the town of Wendel, do this: 1.
step one up 2. While moving, and before landing onto the next step
press "select" 3. Save, reset And you'll warp to Jadd after reaching
the boundary of the screen.

=======================================================================
XIV. Game Genie Codes & Game Shark Codes
=======================================================================

I have tried most of these codes. However, some are untried...
REMEMBER, some of these codes may "mess up" the quest you are playing.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

FFE-6B9-E62 - Start with 255 in each stat category

40E-A49     - Level goes to 37
Use it at the start of a new game. It will raise your level, hit
points, and other stats. You should save your game as soon as possible
after using.

FFE-A69     - Level goes to 39
Will raise level to 39 at the start of a new game. It will raise your
level, hit points, and other stats. You should save your game as soon
as possible after using.

406-06A-E6E - Most Items in stores have no prices and are free

AA2-ECB-357 -
No enemies appear on the screen; glitched enemies replace the real
enemies. Bosses appear, but only take off 1 HP of damage. That means
this code is basically an invincibility code. This code will glitch if
there is a non-glitched enemy on a screen (not a boss) and you swing
your weapon. I played for a long time using this code and this only
happened on one screen in the game. Also, any other player than the
hero will become glitched so this means no other characters in the
game. (They will reappear when the story needs them.)
