Seiken Densetsu
Walkthrough (version 1.0)

Based on: Japanese version (DMG-FFJ)


- Aka. Final Fantasy Adventure / Mystic Quest / Adventures of Mana.
- The game doesn't track your play time.
- Physical damage is ATP - DFP. Many enemies have two different ATP
values: one for body contact and one for their actual attack (usually
a projectile). Many enemies are also immune to certain types of damage.
- You have 16 slots for usable items and 12 slots for equipment. The
weapons you can't get rid of will take up 5 slots.
- The PSV-version gives you a few hotkeys.
- The boss fights have been tweaked on the PSV.
- The PSV-version may occasionally crash or have some other issues (eg.
once I ended up inside the wall).
- Both the amount of Exp and Lucre you receive from enemies has a random
variable.
- The max amount of money you can carry is 65 535.
- Status ailments:
 - Doku: 9 hits at 5%.
 - Moumoku: Things get a little dark. Nothing to worry about.
 - Moogle: Unable to act, DFP drops to 0.
 - Kiseki: Unable to move.
- All status ailments last 8 seconds.
- Max HP is 19 + Vit^2 / 10. Max MP pretty much directly increases
with your Int.
- Cure: 20 + Int | 20 + Int*2 (full gauge)
- When you level up, you can choose one of four different ways to build
up your stats. To reach max stats, you need to alternate between Senshi
and Madoushi.

                                                \\\ Granz ||
- You start off at Level 1 with 19 HP, 6 MP, base stats at 2 and 50 Lucre.

Item: 7x Mattock                                \\\ Topple Region ||
- After your escape, you'll find yourself in Topple outskirts (F7).
- Spend some time leveling up and getting used to the mechanics. When
you level up, your HP and MP is fully restored.
- If you kill all the enemies in a given area, you generally have to
move four areas away to trigger a respawn. However, if you leave at
least one enemy alive, you'll get a respawn/reroll by simply moving
one area away.
- The sword has two different basic attacks: a swing and a thrust. You
trigger the latter by pressing in a direction as you attack.
- The sword also has two different charge attacks. One is a spin attack
where you press Attack while the d-pad is on neutral. Keep holding down
Attack to continue spinning (up to 8 seconds) during which you can move
freely. The other attack is triggered by pressing in a direction when
you attack. This causes you to fly forward and back.
- Pick up the Girl near Topple, then go see Bogard northwest. Use the
Mattocks you find at his place to get through the nearby cave.
- The Girl can heal you if you use the fourth option in the menu (Soudan).

- You can use axes to cut down trees. Most trees leave behind a stump

possible to end up stuck (with maybe one or two exceptions). Locked
doors and breakable walls will respawn if you simply refresh the area,

Special: Flare                                  \\\ Fuuin no Doukutsu ||
Boss: Lich
- This isn't even a dungeon. It's just a few screens long.

[Boss]
- 170 \ 170 ATP, 175 Exp, 210 Lucre
- A really tough boss, even if you don't come here early. Your weapon of
choice is the Fire Flail. He mostly sticks to a sideways figure 8 pattern.
Stay at the bottom and move left or right accordingly as he turns to
swoop towards you. He should narrowly pass you by, while the skull
projectiles fly at your previous position.
- He will periodically fly off-screen to the left or south and then appear
from the other side. When he goes south, you have to mix things up a bit,
as it's not safe at the bottom. You need to go "up" along with him, and
then quickly get back to the bottom after he passes you by.
- You can destroy the skull projectiles by attacking them, but it's difficult
to do consistently because of the angles.
