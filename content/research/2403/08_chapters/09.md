## Chapter 9: Return to Glaive

[MERMAID]

This chapter takes you back into the Glaive Empire. After the bridge collapses behind you, you come out next to the waterfall by the castle [[gf1-59-6]]. You then climb through the Dark Castle, beat Chimera, free the Girl, collect the Blood Sword, beat the Dark Lord, and are thrown down the waterfall again by Julius [[gf1-61-4, gf1-62-5]]. Four achievements fall in this chapter: Return of the Gemma, Drink the Blood of My Enemies, I Am (Not) Your Father, and Glaive Strikes Back.

### Preparation

- Bring at least 3 Keys; the castle needs them [[gf1-60-1]].
- The recommended level is 40 for Chimera and 41 for the Dark Lord [[gf1-61-1, gf1-62-1]].
- You will want the Morning Star from Mt. Rocks and the Lit spell from the Golem. The Chain Flail and the Ice spell are also used in the castle [[gf1-61-6, gf1-61-9]].
- An Item Shop and Inn sits on Floor 1 of the castle, so you can restock Keys there if you run short [[gf1-61-5, gf1-118-1]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** The Blood Sword (achievement 29087, Drink the Blood of My Enemies) sits in a chest in the Floor 2 knight room, and its deadline is before you defeat the Dark Lord [[gf1-61-8, gf2-51-4, ra-comments-48-2, gf1-62-5]]. After the Dark Lord you are thrown out of the castle [[gf1-62-5]], so open that chest and equip the sword first.

### Arrival at the Waterfall

The bridge collapses behind you when you go toward the cave, and the screen fades to the waterfall at the castle [[gf2-49-3, gf4-7-7]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29062]

From the waterfall, go east twice and into the castle area [[gf1-59-6]]. Face enemies outside can be killed in one hit with a Morning Star swing when your Will bar is full [[gf5-17-1]].

### Dark Castle

🗺️ **Map:** [View Glaive Castle Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-GlaiveCastle.png) (the Dark Castle is Glaive Castle; the same map covers the prologue). The source ASCII maps cover Floors 1 to 5 of the castle [[gf1-118-2]].

#### Floor 1: finding the way in

The main gate is locked. Walk west one screen, cut down the trees and go west again, then go north one screen and walk into the door [[gf1-60-2]].

#### B-Floor 2: the bridges

Cross the north bridge, then the west bridge, and go north. Next go east, cross the bridge to the south, go east, and cross the bridge to the north. Go east one screen and up the stairs [[gf1-60-3]].

#### Floors 1, 2, 5, 4: up the vine and down

Climb the vine above you and go north one screen. On Floor 2 go north another screen. The last vine lifts you several floors to a set spot on Floor 5 [[gf1-60-4, gf1-119-4]]. Go down the stairs, then down another flight on Floor 4 [[gf1-60-4]]. If enemies block your path on the vine, re-enter the screen [[gf5-17-1]].

#### Floor 3: Chimera

Heal up and save on Floor 3, then go west to face Chimera [[gf1-60-5]]. Bring Cure.

**Chimera**
- Level 40 recommended; it awards 90 EXP and 21 GP in one source and 91 GP and 20 EXP in another [[gf1-61-1, gf1-61-2, gf2-51-1]].
- It flies above you, drops two flaming bombs from its wings, then flies down in a zigzag [[gf1-61-3]].
- One approach: stand near the top of the screen, attack as fast as you can, and cure occasionally [[gf1-61-3]].
- It is invulnerable to Lit, so cast Fire rapidly. Another guide lists the Morning Star as preferred, so use whichever you can land reliably [[gf5-17-2, gf2-51-1]].
- Each time it reaches the center of the screen it may dive for about 45 damage, so cast Cure if your HP is below 50 [[gf5-17-3]].
- Stay out of the range of the fireballs dropped from its wings [[gf5-17-2]].

### The Girl in Her Cell

After Chimera, descend the stairs in the southwest corner to Floor 2, which is the Girl's prison cell. She runs up and joins you and says the Dark Lord took her pendant [[gf1-61-4, gf3-5-3]]. She heals your HP when you ASK her, so do this often [[gf1-61-4]]. Go down the stairs next [[gf1-61-4]].

### Shop, Spikes and the Chain Puzzle

Go up the stairs north, then south down the steps to the next screen. On Floor 1 go west one screen and enter the building. You can rest on the bed and buy supplies at the Item Shop [[gf1-61-5]]. Exit, go east, and walk north up the steps [[gf1-61-5]].

Go west three times, south, and into the door [[gf1-61-5, gf1-61-6]]. Quickly walk across the spikes; they hurt a lot, so have the Girl heal you afterward [[gf1-61-6, gf3-5-4]]. Use a Key on the door and go north. Then go east, break the north wall with the Mattock or Morning Star, and go north through the hole. Go west, and use the Chain Flail to cross to the odd tile. Stand on it and a staircase appears. Go down [[gf1-61-6]].

The room's puzzle: Chain to the middle pole, then with a full Will bar chain to the left pole to hit the button, then with a full Will bar chain to the bottom pole [[gf5-17-1, gf2-51-2]]. Another route description says grapple the northern pole, then the left pole, step on the switch so a ladder appears in the lower right, then grapple the southern pole and use the ladder [[gf2-51-2]]. While flashing from damage you cannot press a button or enter stairs [[gf5-17-7]]. Wizards inflict Stone with magic or Moogle on touch, and can only be defeated with about 10 Morning Star hits [[gf5-17-5]]. Dark Knights die to one Fire cast if hit from the side or back [[gf5-17-6]].

### Werewolf Room

On Floor 2 go north, east, south twice, east, use a Key on the door, and go east. Kill the enemies and rest on the bed to restore HP and MP [[gf1-61-6, gf1-118-3]]. Then go west three times. The room has four empty chests on odd tiles. Do not knock any of them off or the stairs vanish [[gf1-61-7, gf2-51-3, gf5-17-8]]. The Chain Flail works for killing the monsters here [[gf1-61-7]]. Then go down the stairs [[gf1-61-7]].

### Blood Sword (Knight Room)

On Floor 1 go east twice and use a Mattock on the east wall, then go east through the opening, then east, north, and up the stairs [[gf1-61-8]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Open the Blood Sword chest before moving on and equip it before the Dark Lord. Commenters ask for this achievement to be marked missable [[ra-comments-48-2, gf1-61-8]].

On Floor 2 go south and east and kill all the enemies in the room, then open the chest for the **Blood Sword** [[gf1-61-8, gf2-51-4]]. It gives +26 ATK, sells for 937 GP, and heals you for 25% of damage dealt [[checklist-items-7-18, gf1-83-8]].

Open the menu and equip it now.

### Toward the Dark Lord

Go west, north, and use a Mattock on the north wall. Go north through the opening, use a Key on the door, go east through the opened door, and go up the stairs [[gf1-61-8]].

On Floor 3 go south and west twice. Use the Ice spell to freeze two monsters into snowmen and push them onto the two odd tiles, then go down the stairs [[gf1-61-9]]. Only the Dark Knights can be frozen; the Black Mages cannot. If only one knight is present, leave and re-enter until two appear [[gf2-51-5, gf5-17-9]]. You cannot push a snowman while flashing from damage [[gf5-17-7]]. If the stairs do not appear even when solved, push both snowmen off the buttons and back on [[gf5-17-9]].

On Floor 2 go west, north, and up the stairs. On Floor 3 go north, west, heal up and save, then go up the stairs [[gf1-61-9]]. The throne room is next to the Dark Lord, so you can fight a few Dark Knights for EXP before the fight [[gf2-51-5, gf5-17-1]].

### Dark Lord

The Dark Lord is at the top of the castle. The Girl leaves and the fight begins [[gf1-61-9, gf4-7-10]]. Drink the Blood of My Enemies should already have unlocked when you equipped the Blood Sword; if it has not, equip it now, because after this fight you cannot go back to the knight room [[gf1-62-5]].

**Dark Lord**
- Level 41 recommended; 167 GP and 12 EXP in one source, 166 EXP and 11 GP in another [[gf1-62-1, gf1-62-2, gf2-52-2]].
- He runs around and swings his sword, follows your movements, and can use a MAX-type attack [[gf1-62-1]].
- One guide says the Morning Star passes through his shield and damages him [[gf2-52-3]]. Another says he can only be hurt by Lit and deals about 55 damage, so cast Cure if below 60 HP [[gf5-18-1]]. The sources disagree, so try the Morning Star and switch to Lit if it does no damage.
- Lit method: run to an upper corner with Cure equipped to get his AI stuck, wait for the Will meter to fill, equip Lit, walk into him so he turns away, then cast Lit at his back. Cure yourself and repeat [[gf5-18-2]].
- If Lit glitches and he flies off the balcony, hit him with the Morning Star to finish him [[gf5-18-2]].
- Defeating him yields the Fake Pendant [[gf1-62-3, gf4-7-10]].

### Mana Waterfall

Go south down the stairs. The Girl is gone and you are moved outside the castle. You see her walking by; follow her west to the waterfall [[gf1-62-5, gf4-7-10]]. The Pendant reverses the waterfall, but yours is a fake [[gf1-89-3, gf4-7-10]]. Julius appears and says the Girl is his. He has her use the real Pendant and cast the spell, the waterfall reverses, and Julius zaps you off the cliff [[gf1-62-5, gf4-7-11]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29064]

Your chocobo picks you up and carries you east into Ish [[gf4-7-11]]. This sets up the next chapter.

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave:** Make sure the Blood Sword is equipped and the Dark Lord is beaten before the cutscene. Afterward you wake in Ish, where the Inn costs 10 GP and the Weapon Shop sells the Flame Chain for 6300 GP [[gf1-63-1, checklist-items-7-8]].
