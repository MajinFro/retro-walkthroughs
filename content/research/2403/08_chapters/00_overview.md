### Game Overview

Final Fantasy Adventure is a Game Boy action RPG. You fight in real time, you attack with the A button, and a single playthrough earns all 50 achievements (480 points). Nothing in the achievement set is mutually exclusive, and no chapter has to be replayed. The roadmap opens with the escape from Glaive Castle and ends at the Tower of Gemma and the Tree of Mana.

### Controls and Menus

- **D-pad:** moves the hero in four directions only, with no diagonals, and moves the menu cursor [[gf1-8-1]].
- **A button:** attacks with the equipped weapon in the world, and confirms commands in menus [[gf1-8-2]].
- **B button:** uses the equipped Magic or Item in the world, and cancels or exits in menus [[gf1-8-3]].
- **Start:** opens Item, Magic, Equip and Ask [[gf1-8-4]].
- **Select:** opens Save, Map and Status [[gf1-8-5]].

The screen shows HP, MP and Gold [[gf1-9-1]]. At zero HP you die, and at zero MP you cannot cast [[gf1-10-1, gf1-10-2]].

To use an item, select it with A twice, then press B outside the menus [[gf1-12-2, checklist-items-3-1]]. Keys work differently. Equip one and walk into the door [[checklist-items-3-2]]. Most story items trigger on their own [[checklist-items-3-3]]. Gear is changed in Equip by pressing A twice on it [[checklist-items-3-4]].

Take these inventory limits seriously:

- The item list holds 16 slots [[gf1-12-1]].
- Anything put in the Trash Can is gone for good [[gf1-12-3]].
- The game will not let you trash certain weapons needed to finish [[gf1-14-2]].
- If a chest will not open because you are full, trash a useless item and try again [[gf1-101-4]].

Do not sell or discard the Zeus Axe [[ra-comments-55-1]].

The game save works through Select. You can only load from the Continue option on the main menu [[gf1-18-2]]. Pressing A, B, Select and Start together resets the game without saving [[gf1-18-3]].

### Combat Basics

Touching a monster hurts you. A shield, a higher level than the monster, and the right shield type let you shove it aside instead [[gf1-22-1, checklist-items-6-10, gf2-13-1]].

Equipping a weapon starts the Will meter filling. At full Will, each weapon type does its own special attack [[gf1-22-2]]:

- **Swords:** hold A to spin while moving, or press A with a direction to charge [[gf1-23-1]].
- **Axes and spears:** thrown in the direction you face [[gf1-23-2, gf1-23-5]].
- **Sickle, chain and Morning Star:** their reach extends [[gf1-23-3, gf1-23-4, gf1-23-6]].

You start at level 1 with 19 HP, 6 MP and 50 GP [[gf2-25-1]].

### Magic and Status Ailments

There are eight spells: Cure, Fire, Slep, Heal, Ice, Mute, Lit and Nuke [[gf1-13-1]]. Press A on a spell to equip it and B to cast it. It fails if you lack the MP [[gf1-13-2]]. Costs are:

| Spell | MP cost | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| Cure | 2 | [[gf1-91-9]] |
| Heal | 1 | [[gf1-91-10]] |
| Sleep | 1 | [[gf1-91-11]] |
| Mute | 1 | [[gf1-91-12]] |
| Fire | 1 | [[gf1-91-13]] |
| Ice | 2 | [[gf1-91-14]] |
| Lit | 2 | [[gf1-91-15]] |
| Nuke | 3 | [[gf1-91-16]] |

Ice turns some enemies into snowmen, which you can push onto floor switches. You can steer the iceberg with the D-pad [[gf2-15-6]]. Puzzle rooms use this a lot.

Status ailments:

- **Poison:** cure with Heal or a Pure Potion [[gf1-26-2]].
- **Darkness:** cure with Heal or an Eye Drop [[gf1-26-3]].
- **Stone:** cure with Heal or a Soft Potion, and you can still attack in place [[gf1-26-4, gf2-22-3]].
- **Moogle:** your defense drops to 0 [[gf1-27-1]].

### Tools That Open the World

Different weapons cut different obstacles:

- Axes cut trees [[checklist-items-7-2]].
- The Sickle cuts bushes and vines [[checklist-items-7-11]].
- Mattocks break rocks and pots, and the Morning Star also smashes floating rocks [[gf1-20-1, gf1-20-4]].
- The Morning Star can replace Mattocks entirely [[gf2-48-4]].

Buy plenty of Keys and Mattocks before each dungeon so you never get stuck [[ra-comments-2-4, ra-comments-5-1]].

To raise levels and gold quickly, leave one monster alive on a screen, exit and re-enter. The monsters respawn, and you can repeat this as often as you like [[gf1-37-2]].

### Leveling and the Four "Way of" Achievements

Each level-up restores HP and MP and asks you to choose a stat. The stat you pick gains the most and sometimes boosts another a little [[gf1-24-1, gf1-24-2]]. Power, Stamina, Wisdom and Will each give +2 to the chosen stat [[gf1-25-1, gf1-25-2, gf1-25-3, gf1-26-1]].

The four stat achievements can be missed if you always pick the same stat [[ra-comments-2-1]]. Spread points evenly, or reaching 50 later will be very hard [[ra-comments-4-3]]. The plan is to alternate Power and Wisdom on level-ups [[gf1-104-9, gf3-1-11]]. The Power and Wisdom choices also raise Stamina and Will by 1 each [[gf1-25-1, gf1-25-3]].

The level-milestone achievements need total experience of 1,101 for Lv10, 16,254 for Lv25 and 128,969 for Lv50 [[gf3-10-1, gf3-10-2, gf3-10-3]].

### How Hardcore Mode Applies

Hardcore mode turns off save states, rewinds and cheats, so use the game's own save. You can save before any boss and retry [[ra-comments-4-2]]. Inventory swapping feels dated, but it is manageable once you adjust [[ra-comments-5-6]].

### The 100% Plan

One playthrough is enough: no achievement locks out another, and the only dangers are the timed missables listed here [[ra-comments-2-1, ra-comments-2-2]]. These items can be lost if you move on:

- **Fang and the Jadd clue:** the boy next to the Jadd item shop trades his clue "palm trees, and 8" for a Bag of Fang [[gf1-50-6]]. Walking a figure 8 around the palm trees opens Medusa's Cave [[gf1-51-1]]. It is unconfirmed whether the first Fang always pops the achievement, as some players needed another after Medusa [[ra-comments-18-3]]. Give a Fang before opening the cave and carry spares. If it does not pop, give another after beating Medusa.
- **Were Axe:** this is in Davias's Mansion [[gf1-53-6]]. Keep either the Were Axe or the Zeus Axe, because without one you must restart to get the axe achievement [[ra-comments-55-4]].
- **Blood Sword:** this is in the Dark Castle [[gf1-61-8]].
- **Zeus Axe:** you can get it in the Undersea Volcano [[gf1-72-4]]. One player killed Iflyte and then went back for it, and missed the Iflyte achievement [[ra-comments-3-2]]. It is unconfirmed whether leaving Iflyte's room before taking the Rusty Sword stops the achievement. Take the Zeus Axe first, then after Iflyte open the chest immediately without leaving the room [[gf5-22-4]].
- **Dragon gear:** the Dragon Sword and Dragon Shield are in the Cave of Ruins, and the Dragon Armor is in Dime Tower [[gf1-74-3, gf1-74-4, gf1-75-8]].
- **Wendel boy:** it only works after Glaive's attack, and only for the boy in the southwest corner [[ra-comments-1-1, ra-comments-54-3]]. It is unconfirmed whether Wendel can be revisited after Dime Tower. Commenters believe not [[ra-comments-54-5]]. Treat it as lost once you enter Dime Tower, and do it in chapter 4.
- **Samurai Armor, Samurai Helm and Aegis Shield:** they come from chest drops in the last dungeon. With your item inventory full but equipment space free, keep trying to open a dropped chest until armor appears [[gf1-104-12]].

Entering Dime Tower means you will not see the world map again, so rest at the Ish inn and buy Keys first [[gf2-63-2]]. Making a second save just before the sunken tower in the desert is advised [[ra-comments-2-2]].

Chapter 13 holds 15 of the 50 achievements. Farm the Samurai Armor and Helmet in the Tower of Gemma before the Mana sanctuary; the Elephants for the Aegis Shield, and the grinding for Level 50, the stats and 50,000 GP, can be done inside the sanctuary before you approach Julius [[gf3-8-7, gf3-8-8]].
