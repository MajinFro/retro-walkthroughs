## Chapter 13: The Tower of Gemma and the Tree of Mana

[MERMAID]

This is the final chapter. You ride the reversed waterfall to the top of the mountain, climb the Tower of Gemma (called the Temple of Mana in one guide), beat three dragons, receive Excalibur, farm the best armor and shield, grind your levels, and finish with Julius and the Mana Tree [[gf4-10-8, gf1-77-1, gf1-80-3]]. Almost every achievement here must be done before the point of no return at the Mana sanctuary, so read the preparation section first.

### Overview and Preparation

The recommended levels for the bosses run from 57 (Dragon) to 60 (Dragon Zombie and Julius) [[gf1-77-6, gf1-78-7, gf1-80-1, gf1-81-2]]. One guide advises at least Level 50 for Julius [[gf2-85-4]].

Achievements to finish before leaving the tower area:

- **Gemma Knight:** Level 50 needs 128,969 total experience [[gf3-10-3]].
- **The Way of the Warrior / Wizard / Tank / Monk:** Power, Wisdom, Stamina and Willpower all need to reach 50. These are technically missable if you always pick the same stat when leveling up [[ra-comments-2-1]]. A commenter who over-invested in one stat found raising the others later was very slow, so spread points evenly [[ra-comments-4-3]]. One guide says to alternate between Senshi and Madoushi to reach max stats [[gf3-1-11]]. Another says to alternate Power and Wisdom [[gf1-104-9]].
- **Wow, You're Rich!:** You must hold 50,000 GP at one time. A commenter says reaching Level 50 naturally brings you close to this [[ra-comments-46-1, ra-comments-46-2]].
- **Samurai Style and Poacher:** both are missable and covered below.

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Samurai Style (Samurai Armor and Samurai Helmet) must be farmed in the Tower of Gemma, where the Ninjas and Demon Heads live, before you pass the first four screens of the Mana sanctuary; there is no way back afterwards [[gf1-104-12, gf2-67-1, ra-comments-52-2, gf3-7-15, gf3-7-16, gf3-8-8]]. Poacher (Aegis Shield) comes from the Elephants, which live inside the Mana sanctuary just before Julius, so farm it there before you walk up to the final fight [[gf3-8-7, ra-comments-53-1, gf2-67-3]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Timber!!! needs the Were Axe or Zeus Axe at the final battle. The Battle Axe cannot damage the final boss, and without one of the other two axes you must restart the game. Do not sell or discard the Zeus Axe [[ra-comments-55-1, ra-comments-55-2, ra-comments-55-3, ra-comments-55-4]].

### Mana Falls

After Garuda falls, Dime Tower collapses. Go west twice, and Marcie throws you across the gap but cannot jump himself [[gf1-76-4]]. Go west three times to the waterfall, which reverses its flow; walk into it to be carried to the top of the mountain [[gf1-76-5, gf4-10-8]]. At the top, go west, north twice, and enter the temple [[gf1-77-1]].

### Tower of Gemma

🗺️ **Map:** [View Temple Of Mana 1 Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-TempleOfMana-1.png)

🗺️ **Map:** [View Temple Of Mana 2 Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-TempleOfMana-2.png)

The guide provides floor maps for this tower [[gf1-127-6, map-temple-of-mana-1-1-1]]. You will need two Keys [[gf1-77-5, gf1-78-3]]. Mage walkthrough advice: avoid defeating ordinary enemies unless you are about to level; Ninjas' projectiles deal around 90 damage, and Ninjas and Fiend Heads can teleport directly on top of you [[gf5-26-1]].

#### Floor 1

Go south, east twice, north twice, unlock the north door with a Key, then go north and up the stairs [[gf1-77-5]].

#### Floor 2 and the Dragon

Heal and save, then go north to trigger the Dragon [[gf1-77-6]].

**Dragon** (recommended level 57, 257 to 262 GP depending on the source, 0 EXP) [[gf1-77-6, gf2-68-1, gf3-8-1]]:

- The Dragon travels in a circle through the arena; his fireballs can be blocked by your shield [[gf5-26-2, gf1-77-6]].
- Only the head can be damaged with a long-reach weapon (Flame Flail or Thunder Spear) [[gf2-68-2]].
- The mage route says the only vulnerable square is the front foot under his head, because his wing intercepts shots aimed higher. Use fully charged Nukes, and consider taking a weak fireball hit on purpose so the invincibility time lets you cast uninterrupted [[gf5-26-2, gf5-26-3]].

When he is defeated, enter the portal in the northeast corner [[gf1-78-1]].

#### Floors 3 to 7

- **Floor 3:** Go east and enter the portal [[gf1-78-2]].
- **Floor 4:** Go north, west, north, use a Key on the door, go west and enter the portal [[gf1-78-3]].
- **Floor 5:** Go east and enter the portal [[gf1-78-4]].
- **Floor 6:** Go south and break the west wall. Go west twice, south, west, and enter the portal [[gf1-78-5]].
- **Floor 7:** Go west and south twice. Cast Ice and steer the icicle with the control pad to hit the monster on the odd tile; the door opens. Go east and up the stairs [[gf1-78-6]]. In the mage route the icicle goes through the labyrinth, across the orb, to the Minotaur on the button, and it may take two tries because an extra Minotaur is down there [[gf5-26-4]].

Samurai Warriors/Ninjas here drop Elixirs or Samurai Armor, Demon Heads drop X-Ether or the Samurai Helmet, and Elephants drop the Aegis Shield [[gf1-77-2, gf1-99-73, gf1-99-74]]. The farming method is in the "Armor and Shield Farming" section below.

#### Floor 8 and the Red Dragon

Heal and save, then go north [[gf1-78-7]].

**Red Dragon** (recommended level 58, 257 GP, 0 EXP): it moves in the opposite direction and has more HP than the first Dragon, but the strategy is the same [[gf1-78-7, gf1-79-1, gf2-69-1, gf5-26-5]]. Attack only the head with a Flame Flail or Thunder Spear [[gf2-69-2]]. Enter the portal when it is defeated [[gf1-79-2]].

#### Floors 9 to 12

- **Floor 9:** Go west and enter the portal [[gf1-79-3]].
- **Floor 10:** Go north, east, north and enter the portal [[gf1-79-5]].
- **Floor 11:** Go east into the portal, then head south, east twice, and enter the next portal [[gf1-79-5]].
- **Floor 12 (healing room):** Use a Key on the door and go west. Go to the bottom of the pond to restore HP and MP, then leave and re-enter the portal [[gf1-79-6, gf1-130-3]].
- **Floor 11 again:** Go west, south, into the portal, then go west into the next portal [[gf1-79-5]].
- **Floor 12 (snowman puzzle):** Go east and use a Key on the door, go east through it and twice more. Freeze a monster into a snowman and place it on the odd tile directly above the odd tile in the bottom-left corner [[gf1-79-8]]. Another guide says to turn a Ninja into a snowman and put it on the floor switch second from the bottom in the left column, which opens the north door [[gf2-69-3]]. The mage guide agrees: the correct button is on the left side, second from the bottom [[gf5-26-6]]. Then go north three times and up the stairs [[gf1-79-9]].

Heal and save before the next fight [[gf1-79-9]].

#### Dragon Zombie

**Dragon Zombie** (recommended level 60, 270 GP, 0 EXP) [[gf1-80-1, gf2-70-1]]:

- Use a long-reach weapon on its head. Your weapon can destroy the fireballs and your shield can block them [[gf1-80-2]].
- Touching the body costs about 75 HP [[gf1-80-2]].
- Fireballs and contact hurt much more than with the previous dragons [[gf2-70-2]].
- The mage route says fire works well; Nuke is workable if you have many X-Ethers, and you should attack from below so you do not miss the erratic heads [[gf1-80-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29072]

Defeating Dragon, Red Dragon and Dragon Zombie completes Dragon Slayer [[gf4-10-9, gf4-10-10, gf4-10-11]]. Enter the portal once the monster is dead [[gf1-80-1, gf1-80-3]].

#### Floor 13: Excalibur restored

Go north four times and talk to the Lady, who transforms your Rusty Sword into Excalibur [[gf1-80-3]]. The script has her say you have been a great Knight of Gemma before giving you the sword [[gf4-10-12]]. Excalibur gives +85 attack and the Rusty Sword is removed permanently [[gf1-83-16, checklist-items-7-21]]. Equip it right away [[gf2-70-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29073]

Then go north twice, west, south, and west, and heal and save [[gf1-80-4]]. The Mana sanctuary is still ahead, and it is where the point of no return begins [[gf3-8-8]].

### Armor and Shield Farming

#### Samurai Armor and Helmet drops: the drop-chest trick

This is the key to both missable equipment achievements. The contents of a chest are decided when you open it, not when it appears [[gf1-104-12]]. To use it:

1. Fill your item inventory completely, but keep room in your equipment inventory [[gf1-104-12]].
2. Kill the monster carrying the equipment you want until one drops a chest [[gf1-104-12]].
3. Try to open the chest. You get "Can't carry!" messages and the chest stays closed. Keep trying and eventually you get the armor instead of an item [[gf1-104-12, gf2-67-1]].

You only need one chest to drop rather than killing many monsters [[gf1-104-12]].

Respawning enemies: enemies return if you visit three other screens and come back, or if you leave one monster alive and return [[gf1-104-8]]. Commenters say that leaving one enemy alive, exiting and re-entering works well [[ra-comments-52-3, ra-comments-53-6]]. Another method: kill the Ninjas in one room, save, soft reset, load, and a new set spawns [[ra-comments-52-5]].

#### Samurai Armor (Ninjas)

The armor drops from Ninjas, which also drop Elixirs, and gives +45 defense [[gf1-99-72, checklist-items-4-13, gf3-8-23]]. Ninjas give 254 EXP and 10 GP [[gf2-78-13]]. Use the chest trick on a Ninja's chest [[gf2-67-1]].

#### Samurai Helmet (Demon Heads)

The helmet drops from Demon Heads, which also drop X-Ether, and gives +25 defense [[gf1-99-73, checklist-items-5-6]]. The Devil Head is vulnerable to Nuke magic and swords [[gf2-78-3]]. A commenter kills them with Nuke, saves, and uses Ethers and Elixirs to restore MP before repeating [[ra-comments-52-6]]. Use the chest trick on their chest [[gf2-76-6]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29091]

Equip the Samurai Armor and Samurai Helmet to earn Samurai Style [[gf2-85-2, gf1-103-2]].

#### Aegis Shield (Elephant drop)

Elephants drop the Aegis Shield (and Unicorns). They live in the Mana sanctuary, just before the final boss, so this farming happens after you cross the point of no return below, but before you approach Julius [[gf1-99-74, gf2-78-4, gf3-8-7, ra-comments-53-1]]. Excalibur is recommended against them [[gf2-78-4]]. Reload the screen until two Elephants are on it by leaving and returning without killing, and kill them on each screen where they appear. Their Unicorn drops fill inventory slots, then use the chest trick [[ra-comments-53-3, ra-comments-53-4, ra-comments-53-5]]. The Aegis Shield deflects any projectile, including the final boss's force balls [[gf2-67-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29092]

Equip the Aegis Shield to earn Poacher [[gf2-85-3, gf1-103-2]].

### Grinding: Level 50, Stats and GP

Elephants near the final boss are a good place to grind to Level 50 [[gf2-70-4]]. Another source says one room in the dungeon is a good leveling spot and warns that going beyond Lv99 on the original Game Boy version reportedly freezes the game [[gf3-8-4]]. Keep alternating stat choices at each level-up so that Power, Wisdom, Stamina and Willpower can all reach 50 [[ra-comments-4-3, gf3-1-11]]. Power raises attack, Stamina raises HP and defense, and Wisdom raises MP and magic damage [[gf1-25-1, gf1-25-2, gf1-25-3]].

### Tree of Mana

Go north to find Julius and the Girl, and prepare to fight [[gf1-81-1]]. The path to the tree is one-way [[gf5-27-1]]. Excalibur provides 85 attack here [[gf3-8-9]]. The Girl can heal you if you ASK her [[gf1-82-4, gf2-72-1]]. Elixirs restore all HP and MP, so use them freely [[gf1-82-5]]. A Cure spell is recommended [[gf1-81-3]].

#### Julius (first form)

Recommended level 60; 638 EXP and 818 GP [[gf1-81-2, gf2-71-2]]. Julius splits into three duplicates; attack and defeat all three [[gf2-71-3]]. Use Cure as needed [[gf1-81-3]]. The charging attack of the maxed sword is the most efficient attack in all three forms [[gf2-71-1]].

Mage tip: his Nukes deal about 20 damage and touching him about 100, so stay above 100 HP. Fully charged Nukes kill the clones in one hit. You must kill the final clone while standing on the left or bottom side of the screen [[gf5-27-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29074]

#### Julius (second form)

He becomes a monster that flies above, throwing lightning bolts and swooping down [[gf1-82-1, gf2-71-5]]. Dodge the swoop and hit him with Excalibur [[gf1-82-2]]. Try to stay by his side, since he can charge from either direction [[gf2-71-5]]. He gives 162 GP [[gf2-71-4]]. Do not attack him from below when he is on the left or right side, or you cannot avoid the diagonal dive [[gf5-27-4]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29075]

#### Julius (final form / Mana Tree)

He becomes a head with two arms that teleports around the screen and fires Nukes or force balls [[gf1-82-3, gf2-72-1]]. Stay near the center of the screen and attack when he appears [[gf1-82-4]]. The Aegis Shield makes the force balls harmless; ask Fuji/the Girl to regenerate HP, and a Nuke equipped alongside Excalibur helps [[gf2-72-1]]. If he warps on top of you, you can take multiple body hits, so focus on healing [[gf3-8-11]]. The mage guide says his hands are invincible and he fires two Nukes from each of his positions, and that his Nukes can be intercepted by timed shots from the opposite side [[gf5-27-5]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Timber!!! requires the final blow on the Mana Tree to be struck with an axe. Switch to the Were Axe or Zeus Axe for the last hit. The Battle Axe cannot damage it [[ra-comments-55-1, ra-comments-55-2, ra-comments-55-3]]. One player used the Zeus Axe and it still took many hits, even after 10 hits with Excalibur, so bring healing [[ra-comments-55-5]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29076]

[ACHIEVEMENT:29094]

Defeat the corrupted Mana Tree, finishing with the axe, to earn The End of Everything and Timber!!! [[gf1-82-3, ra-comments-55-5]].
