## Chapter 6: Jadd, the Oasis and Medusa's Cave

[MERMAID]

This chapter takes you through Jadd, out into the desert for a Fang, around the two palm trees to open Medusa's Cave, and through the cave itself. You recruit Amanda, take the Ice spell, beat Medusa and then put Amanda down to collect her Tears. Three achievements are in play: To Infinity, and Beyond! (29023), Mercy (29058) and Knight (29079). The cave route uses Mattocks and Keys, so stock up in Jadd before you go [[gf1-50-6, gf1-51-1, gf1-51-3, gf1-115-3, gf3-10-2]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** To Infinity, and Beyond! (29023) must be earned while you are in the Jadd region, before the Mt. Rocks bridge collapses in chapter 8 [[gf1-50-6, ra-comments-18-1, ra-comments-18-3, gf2-49-3]]. Get a Fang from the lizards in the desert and give it to the boy next to Jadd's Item Shop now. Carry spare Fangs, because reports on whether the first Fang pops the achievement disagree. Whether the first Fang always triggers it is unconfirmed in the collected sources [[ra-comments-18-3, ra-comments-18-4, ra-comments-18-6, ra-comments-18-10]].

### Before You Start

- You need a Chocobo for the trip. From outside Menos, the route to Jadd is south, south, west, west, north, north, west, north [[gf1-49-5]].
- Bring Keys and Mattocks. The Medusa's Cave route uses a Mattock on the south wall and the north wall, and Keys on several doors [[gf1-51-3, gf1-51-4, gf1-51-6]].
- Have the Chain Flail equipped for the ice-patch puzzle [[gf1-51-4]].
- Knight (29079) asks for Level 25, which requires 16,254 total experience [[gf3-10-2]].

#### Gear and Shops

- The Inn costs 10 GP [[gf1-94-6]].
- The Weapon Shop sells Gold Armor for 1300 GP, Gold Helmet for 680 GP and Gold Shield for 920 GP [[gf2-80-5]]. Gold Armor gives +16 DP and Gold Helmet +14 DP [[gf1-85-4, gf1-86-4]]. If you are short on money, skip the Gold Shield [[gf5-13-1]]. You can also find a Gold Armor inside Medusa's Cave, so you may not need to buy it [[gf4-6-4]].
- The Item Shop sells X-Cure (2) for 160 GP, Pure (3) for 30 GP, EyeDrop (3) for 60 GP, Soft (3) for 90 GP, Moogle (3) for 120 GP, Key (4) for 15 GP and Mattock (7) for 60 GP [[gf1-50-11, gf1-50-12, gf1-50-13, gf1-50-14, gf1-50-15, gf1-50-16, gf1-50-17]].
- Davias says a Medusa is in the cave Amanda went to, and the bird beside him is Amanda's brother [[gf1-50-4]]. Do not go into the mansion yet. Medusa's Cave comes first.
- Do not walk one screen south, one east, one north and one east from outside Jadd. That leads to fatal poison gas [[gf4-5-9]]. The gas is cleared later in the story [[gf2-44-4]].

#### The Boy Next to the Item Shop

The boy next to the Item Shop trades his clue for a Bag of Fang [[gf1-50-6]]. Fangs are dropped rarely by the lizards living in the desert [[gf1-50-5, gf2-39-1, gf1-99-32]].

### Fang for the boy in Jadd (desert farming)

Leave Jadd and fight in the desert until a lizard drops a Fang [[gf1-50-5]].

Desert enemies:
- Basilisk drops a Ruby (51 GP, 40 EXP) [[gf1-99-30]].
- Scorpion drops nothing (10 GP, 54 EXP) [[gf1-99-31]].
- Lizard drops nothing or a Fang (41 GP, 48 EXP) [[gf1-99-32]].

Basilisks die to a single Fire shot [[gf5-12-1]]. A Fang sells for 14 GP and can be exchanged for info in Jadd [[checklist-items-9-1]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** This is the To Infinity, and Beyond! step. Hand a Fang to the boy before you leave the Jadd region for good [[gf1-50-6, gf2-49-3]]. Reports disagree on whether the first Fang is enough. Some players popped it with the first Fang before the cave was open, while others needed a Fang after beating Medusa. This is unconfirmed [[ra-comments-18-6, ra-comments-18-7, ra-comments-18-4]].

Return to Jadd and give the Fang:

1. Go to the boy next to the Item Shop and accept his offer of the clue for a Bag of Fang [[gf4-5-10]].
2. He says "Palm trees.. and 8." [[gf4-5-10]]

[ACHIEVEMENT:29023]

If the achievement does not pop, do not worry yet. Players report it popping after giving another Fang after entering the cave [[ra-comments-18-3, ra-comments-18-5]]. One player needed a third Fang [[ra-comments-18-4]]. Keep spare Fangs and try again after Medusa.

### Desert

- A magic shop sits south, east, south, south, west, west, north of Jadd [[gf1-50-7]]. It sells X-Ether for 640 GP, Flame (2) for 60 GP, Blaze (2) for 240 GP, Silence for 100 GP and Pillow for 100 GP [[gf1-95-1]].
- Medusa's Cave is south, east, south, south, east, south, south, west, north from outside Jadd [[gf1-50-9]]. An Inn (10 GP) lies south, east, east of the cave [[gf1-50-8, gf1-95-2]].

#### Opening the Cave

1. Stand at the two separate palm trees and dismount from your Chocobo [[gf1-51-1, gf1-100-2]].
2. Walk a sideways figure 8 around the two palm trees. You may need to do it two or three times before the cave opens [[gf1-51-1]].
3. Another description: the two trees make three lanes (between them, to the right and to the left). Walk up the center, down the right, up the center, then down the left [[gf5-12-2]].
4. When the cave opens, go inside [[gf2-39-2]].

### Medusa's Cave

🗺️ **Map:** [View Cave Of Palmy Desert(D15) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-CaveOfPalmyDesert(D15).png) (a different desert cave, the Lich Cave, which you enter in chapter 11; not needed now) [[gf1-68-3]]

🗺️ **Map:** [View Cave Of Medusa(C13) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-CaveOfMedusa(C13).png)

Items here: Gold Armor, the Ice spell, and Amanda's Tears [[gf1-114-6, gf1-115-3]]. Enemies include Mummies (drop Flame, use magic or swords), Biting Lizards (immune to Fire) and Shadow Zeroes, which deal about 50 damage and drop Ethers and Wisdom Crystals [[gf1-99-33, gf2-78-12, gf5-12-4, gf5-12-6]]. Keep HP full against Shadow Zeroes [[gf5-12-6]].

#### Entrance: Recruit Amanda

Inside, Amanda explains she took the Pendant to exchange for her brother, but Davias turned him into a parrot, and she seeks the Tears of Medusa. She joins your party [[gf1-51-2]]. She attacks with a Dagger and can cure petrification when you ASK [[gf1-27-4]].

#### Floor 1

Floor 1 has the entrance, locked doors, breakable walls, a healing spring, the Spell Book of Ice and the Gold Armor [[gf1-114-6]].

**Gold Armor route:**

1. From where you found Amanda, go south and use a Mattock on the south wall [[gf1-51-3]].
2. Go south through the new entrance [[gf1-51-3]].
3. Go west twice, south once, west twice, then north three times [[gf1-51-3]].
4. Go west, use a Key on the door, go south, and kill all the enemies in the room [[gf1-51-3]].
5. Open the chest for the **Gold Armor** [[gf1-51-3, gf4-6-4]].

**Ice spell route:**

1. Go north, east, and use a Key on the north door, then go north through it [[gf1-51-4]].
2. Two odd tiles are here. The one closest to the bottom opens the door and the other closes it [[gf1-51-4]].
3. Equip the Chain Flail. Go west and step onto the bottom ice patch, which brings you onto the first odd tile [[gf1-51-4]].
4. Use the Chain Flail on the post to the east so you do not slide onto the second button, then go east one screen through the opened door [[gf1-51-4, gf2-40-2, gf5-12-7]].
5. Open the chest for the **Ice spell** (Magic Book of Ice). It costs 2 MP and shoots controllable ice [[gf1-51-4, gf2-40-2, checklist-items-13-5]].

**Healing spring (optional):**

Use a Key on the west door, go through it and walk into the bottom of the pond to restore your HP and MP [[gf1-51-5]].

**Snowman puzzles:**

1. From the room next to the recovery fountain, go south, east, and use a Mattock on the north wall. Go through the wall [[gf1-51-6]].
2. Move the frozen snowman onto the floor tile to open the door [[gf1-51-6]].
3. Go north through the door. Cast Ice on one of the Spiders to freeze it into a snowman. You can control the ice as it travels [[gf2-40-3, gf1-51-6]].
4. Push the frozen enemy onto the odd tile, which opens the north door [[gf5-12-8, gf2-40-3, gf1-51-6]].
5. Go north through the door and down the stairs [[gf1-51-6]].

#### Floor 2

Go north and down the stairs [[gf1-51-7]].

#### Floor 3: Medusa

Climb down the vine. Just before you go west, heal up [[gf1-51-8]].

**Medusa** (recommended level 28):
- Rewards: 88 GP and 90 EXP in one source [[gf1-51-8, gf1-52-1]].
- Her attack is snakes, which can inflict Ston status. Ask Amanda to cure it [[gf2-41-2]].
- Stay below her, dodge the snakes, and keep attacking her head with the Wind Spear or Chain Flail [[gf1-52-2]]. The suggested gear also includes the Cure spell [[gf1-52-2]].
- Alternatively, Fire destroys any snakes it passes through, and her head takes much more damage from Fire than her skirt [[gf5-12-9]].

#### After the Fight: Mercy

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Amanda is bitten and will turn into a Medusa. You must attack and kill her, then walk into her body for the Tears. Your inventory needs room for Amanda's Tears [[gf1-52-3, gf2-42-1, gf5-12-10]].

1. Medusa dies but leaves no tears [[gf1-52-3]].
2. As you are ready to leave, Amanda says she was injured and will turn into a Medusa. She asks you to kill her and take her tears to her brother [[gf1-52-3, gf4-6-6]].
3. Attack Amanda once she transforms. A player who thought she had to be left alone found that hitting her killed her [[ra-comments-19-1, gf4-6-6]].
4. Walk into her corpse to collect **Amanda's Tear** [[gf5-12-10, gf1-115-3]]. You are then transported out of the cave [[gf1-52-3]].

### Knight (Level 25)

Reaching Level 25 requires 16,254 total EXP [[gf3-10-2]]. Knight normally pops around here, during or just after Medusa's Cave. If you are short, fight the desert enemies near Jadd: Basilisks, Scorpions and Lizards all give EXP [[gf1-99-30, gf1-99-31, gf1-99-32]].

### Back in Jadd: Retry the Fang

If To Infinity, and Beyond! still has not unlocked, go back to Jadd after Medusa and give the boy another Fang. This is the retry players report working [[ra-comments-18-3, ra-comments-18-4]]. Do this before the Mt. Rocks bridge in chapter 8 collapses, because you cannot go back after that [[gf2-49-3]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Jadd:** Confirm To Infinity, and Beyond! has popped, buy any Gold gear you want, and stock Keys and Mattocks. The next chapter goes into Davias's Mansion, where you equip Amanda's Tear and examine the parrot [[gf2-43-1, gf1-94-7, gf1-94-8, gf1-94-9]].
