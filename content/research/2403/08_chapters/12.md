## Chapter 12: The Cave of Ruins and Dime Tower

[MERMAID]

This chapter takes you from the Desert of Crystal, where you cast Nuke on the right-hand crystal, through the Cave of Ruins (Dragon Shield, Dragon Sword, the Mantis Ant boss and the Rusty Sword tile) and up Dime Tower, where Marcie joins you, the Dragon Armor waits on Floor 4 and Garuda guards the top [[gf2-59-5, gf2-61-4, gf1-74-3, gf1-74-4, gf1-75-1, gf1-75-8, gf2-64-1, gf1-76-4]]. Four achievements are placed here: Sierra's Blade, Larc's Leathers, Rise of the Robots and Robots Can't Jump.

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Sierra's Blade (Dragon Sword) and Larc's Leathers (Dragon Shield plus Dragon Armor) are both missable. Take the Dragon Sword and the Dragon Shield in the Cave of Ruins before you enter Dime Tower [[gf1-74-3, gf1-74-4, gf1-75-8, gf2-63-2]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** War Is Hell (the boy in Wendel) is also lost once you enter Dime Tower. Whether Wendel can be revisited afterwards is unconfirmed by any source, but commenters believe it cannot. Treat it as lost and do it before this chapter [[ra-comments-54-5]].

### Preparation

- You need the Nuke spell from the Lich. Dr. Bowow in Ish says Nuke can blow up a crystal in the Desert of Crystal right outside Ish [[gf1-70-1, gf1-73-7]].
- You need the Rusty Sword from Iflyte in your inventory for the tile in the Cave of Ruins [[gf5-22-4, gf1-75-1]].
- Bring Cure and Fire equipped as magic options, an Axe (for trees on Floor 6), a Flail, Ice, and a Mattock or Morning Star. Dime Tower uses Keys and breakable walls [[gf1-75-5, gf1-75-8, gf1-75-11, gf2-64-2]].
- The Desert of Crystal magic shop sells Thunder (x2) for 400 GP if you want it [[gf1-96-4]].
- A commenter advises making a second save just before the sunk tower in the desert [[ra-comments-2-2]].

### Desert of Crystal

From outside Ish go one screen west, north twice, and west. Two crystals stick out of the middle of the screen [[gf1-103-1]]. Cast Nuke on the right-hand crystal [[gf1-103-1, gf2-61-4]]. A cave opening appears; enter it [[gf2-61-4, gf5-24-2]].

> [!TIP]
> Mirrors and Snails around here each die to a single Nuke cast, but Mirrors fire projectiles and Snails do not. Robots are not affected by Nuke; Lit works on them [[gf5-24-3, gf5-24-4]].

### Cave of Ruins

🗺️ **Map:** [View Cave Of Ruins(H1) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-CaveOfRuins(H1).png)

#### Floor 1

Go west twice and descend the stairs [[gf1-74-2]]. Snails, Mirrors, Earth Elementals and Moths each give roughly 100-150 experience, so kill a few if you are close to a level [[gf5-24-5]].

#### B-Floor 2: Dragon Shield

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** The Dragon Shield is needed for Larc's Leathers. Grab it now; once you enter Dime Tower you cannot return [[gf1-74-3, gf2-63-2]].

From the stairs, go north, then use the slippery floors to go east one screen [[gf1-74-3, gf2-62-1]]. Open the chest for the **Dragon Shield** [[gf1-74-3, gf1-125-4, checklist-items-6-8]]. It sells for 4100 GP, but do not sell it [[checklist-items-6-8]].

#### B-Floor 2: Dragon Sword

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** The Dragon Sword is needed for Sierra's Blade. Take it before entering Dime Tower [[gf1-74-4, gf2-63-2]].

From the shield chest go west, north twice, then west twice [[gf1-74-4, gf2-62-2]]. Open the chest for the **Dragon Sword** (+56 ATK) [[gf1-74-4, checklist-items-7-20]].

Equip the Dragon Sword from your equipment menu to unlock the achievement.

[ACHIEVEMENT:29089]

#### Path to Mantis Ant

From the sword chest go east four times, north three times, and west [[gf1-74-5]]. To go north through the tile rooms, use the left half of the center tiles until you are kicked to the right side, then take the upper right tile. For the eastward room, use the left half until you are kicked to the upper side, then take the upper right tile [[gf5-24-6]]. Heal and save before the boss [[gf1-74-5]].

### Mantis Ant

Recommended level is 53, and the suggested equipment is the Dragon Sword and the Cure spell [[gf1-74-6, gf1-74-7]]. Spikes around the arena edges deal damage equal to three times your level, so do not get knocked into them [[gf3-7-5]].

**Easy method:** Equip Fire before entering, do not move once on the screen, and cast Fire at the boss whenever your Will meter fills. One player won in about 20 casts [[gf1-74-8, gf5-24-8]]. You can also stay at the doorway and use ranged attacks [[gf3-7-5]].

**Melee method:** The source says to cross the lightning barrier into the middle, cure at once, and attack with a fast weapon. Mantis Ant stays mostly at the top and rarely uses its charge [[gf2-62-3]]. Winning pays 214 EXP and 229 GP in one source [[gf2-62-3]].

After the fight, equip Cure and survive the 100 HP damage tiles as you cross the arena to the right exit [[gf5-24-9]].

### Raising Dime Tower

The east door opens after Mantis Ant dies [[gf1-75-1]]. Heal to full, go through, and equip the **Rusty Sword** [[gf2-63-1, gf1-75-1]]. You must have it equipped before stepping on the floor switch, or nothing happens [[gf1-102-3]]. Step on the odd tile; the sword's power is released and Dime Tower is raised [[gf1-75-1, gf5-24-10]].

> [!CAUTION]
> **Point of No Return:** Entering Dime Tower closes off the world map. Afterwards the only route is the waterfall to the Tower of Gemma; Wendel and the Cave of Ruins are gone [[gf2-63-2, gf3-7-7, ra-comments-54-5, ra-comments-54-6]].

Before you enter, confirm that War Is Hell is done and that you hold both the Dragon Sword and the Dragon Shield. Rest at the Ish inn and buy Keys until you hold 8 sets [[gf2-63-2]]. Ish is south and west twice from outside Ish [[gf1-75-3]]. A magic shop is also nearby [[gf2-63-2]].

#### Desert of Crystal: Finding Dime Tower

From outside the Cave of Ruins go south twice, east, south, and west, then try to walk across the screen. Dime Tower rises from the sand [[gf1-75-2]]. Enter the door [[gf5-24-10, gf1-75-2]].

### Dime Tower

🗺️ **Map:** [View Dime Tower(H4) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-DimeTower(H4).png)

Tablets are scattered through the tower. Reading them is optional and the route below skips them [[gf1-75-5, gf2-64-1]].

#### Marcie joins

As soon as you walk in, Marcie joins you. Use ASK to restore your MP to full [[gf2-64-1, gf1-28-2, gf5-25-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29070]

Marcie attacks with a Laser Beam [[gf1-28-2]]. Enemies here include Swords, Gauntlets, Birdmen (can poison) and beret fuzzballs (can inflict Moogle). Angry Ducks drop X-Cure [[gf5-25-4, gf1-99-69]].

#### Floor 1

Go west, north twice, east, and use a Mattock or Morning Star on the east wall. Go east, north, west, and up the stairs [[gf1-75-5]].

#### Floor 2

Go west, south, east, south twice, east, and up the stairs [[gf1-75-6]].

#### Floor 3

Climb the vine north one screen and go through the door [[gf1-75-7]]. The four-floor-switch room lies on the way; use the Flail to reach the middle and step on all four switches to open the north door [[gf2-64-2]].

#### Floor 4: Dragon Armor

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Equip the Dragon Armor with the Dragon Shield for Larc's Leathers. You cannot return for either after the tower [[gf1-75-8, gf2-65-1, gf3-7-7]].

Go west and use a Key on the north door. Go north, then east, and open the chest in the middle for the **Dragon Armor** (+35 DEF) [[gf1-75-8, gf2-65-1, gf4-10-6]]. Equip the Dragon Armor and Dragon Shield together.

[ACHIEVEMENT:29090]

Then go west and use a Mattock or Morning Star on the west wall, go west, and climb the stairs [[gf1-75-9]].

#### Floor 5

Go south and rest in a bed to restore HP and MP, then go north and up the stairs [[gf1-75-10, gf1-127-3]].

#### Floor 6

Two monsters are surrounded by trees to the south. Stand in the bottom corners facing the walls away from the trees and swing the Axe so the trees fall without hurting the monsters [[gf1-75-11]]. Then freeze both with Ice, which opens the north door [[gf2-65-2]]. Ice can be steered diagonally to avoid cutting trees that let Marcie destroy your snowmen [[gf5-25-5]].

Go north, use a Key on the door, go east, then south and up the stairs [[gf1-75-12]].

#### Floor 7: Garuda

Heal and save, then go north to start the fight [[gf1-75-12]].

Recommended level is 55 and the suggested equipment is the Flame Chain and Cure [[gf1-76-1, gf1-76-2]]. Garuda dives across the screen and drops feathers. When he is upper left, stand in the lower left corner; when he is upper right, stand in the lower right. Follow him when he charges. Your weapon can destroy his feathers [[gf2-66-3, gf5-25-6, gf1-76-1]]. Stay near the center until he is about to dive so feathers are not aimed at the corner [[gf5-25-6]]. Fully charged Nuke blasts also work [[gf5-25-7]]. He gives 221 EXP and 266 GP in one source [[gf2-66-1]].

#### After Garuda

Go west one screen. Before moving on, cast Cure until your HP is full and ASK Marcie to restore all MP, since this is the last time to use him [[gf2-66-4]]. Then go west twice [[gf1-76-4]].

Part of the tower collapses. Marcie throws you across the gap and cannot jump himself; the tower falls with him [[gf1-76-4]]. The achievement pops before the cutscene, as commenters note [[ra-comments-4-1, ra-comments-32-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29071]

Go west three times to the waterfall, which reverses flow and carries you to the mountain top [[gf1-76-5]].

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave: confirm you have the Dragon Sword, Dragon Shield and Dragon Armor. From here the only route is the waterfall and the Tower of Gemma; you cannot return to the world map [[gf3-7-7, ra-comments-54-6]].
