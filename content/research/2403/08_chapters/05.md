## Chapter 5: The Airship and Menos

[MERMAID]

This chapter takes you from the refueling lake to Glaive's Airship, where you speak to the Girl through her window and receive the Pendant. Julius then blasts the hero off the ship, and you land in Menos. After shopping, you go to the forest north of Menos and hatch a chocobo egg [[gf1-46-2, gf1-47-2, gf1-47-3, gf1-48-2, gf1-49-2]]. Two achievements fall here: I Believe I Can Fly... and Familiar Feathered Friend.

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave:** Stock up on Keys and Mattocks before boarding the Airship. The route needs a Key for the south door on B-Floor 4 and a Mattock for a pot on B-Floor 2 [[gf1-47-1, gf1-46-3]].

### Refueling Lake

When you exit Gaia's Cave, Bogard joins you and tells you the Airship is refueling at the lake to the north [[gf1-46-2]]. Gaia only lets you through if you wear at least one piece of Silver equipment [[gf2-37-3, gf4-4-5]].

#### Werewolves (optional leveling)

One screen south of the cave exit is a group of four Werewolves. They are replenished even after you kill them all, so this is an easy place to gain levels. With a throwing-type weapon equipped, go to the screen south of them and wait for the attack bar to max out. Then go north and let the weapon fly to kill all of them in one shot, if your Power is high enough [[gf1-49-4, gf1-46-2]].

#### Item Shop near the lake

From the cave, go south, cut a path through the trees, then go west a screen. Next go north twice to see an Inn. Use the Battle Axe on the trees to your left, go west twice, then north into the Item Shop [[gf1-46-4, gf1-46-3]].

The Item Shop sells [[gf2-80-2, gf4-4-7]]:

| Item | Price | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| Silver Helmet | 240 GP | [[gf2-80-2]] |
| Cure x2 | 40 GP | [[gf2-80-2]] |
| Ether | 320 GP | [[gf2-80-2]] |
| Key x4 | 15 GP | [[gf2-80-2]] |
| Mattock x7 | 60 GP | [[gf2-80-2]] |

Buy the Silver Helmet, a set of Keys and a set of Mattocks [[gf1-46-3]]. The Silver Helmet gives +8 DP and sells for 120 GP [[gf1-86-3]].

#### Reaching the Airship

From the shop, follow these steps [[gf1-46-4]]:

1. Go east twice, north, then east through the trees to the next screen.
2. Wait until your attack bar is at max, then use the Chain Flail to cross the stream. Only a maxed Chain reaches the pole [[gf2-37-7]].
3. Go north, east, use the Chain Flail, then go west.
4. Go down the vine and west.
5. Use the Chain Flail to cross the water, then go south.
6. Go west and north twice.
7. Climb the chain up into the Airship.

### Airship

🗺️ **Map:** [View Glaive's Airship(G10) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-Glaive'sAirship(G10).png)

You need 1 Key and 1 Mattock here [[gf1-47-1]]. The Pendant is only yours temporarily [[gf1-47-2, gf4-5-3]].

Enemies here include Carrot (44 GP, 38 EXP) and Porcupine (37 GP, 42 EXP) in the Airship Area [[gf1-99-25, gf1-99-26]]. Clouds (28 EXP, 18 G) and Eye (21 EXP, 25 G) can only be hurt by Magic or swords [[gf2-78-2, gf2-78-6]]. Kill the Owls with the chain, since they may drop a Crystal [[gf5-11-1]]. Eye Spys often die in one shot if you attack with Fire while the Will bar is partially full [[gf5-11-2]]. Be careful with Eye Spies, because once you are Moogled you can die from a single hit [[gf3-3-27]].

Bogard is a Gemma Knight who fights with a Sword. If you ASK him, he teaches you MAX attacks [[gf1-27-7]].

#### Floor 1 (Deck) and B-Floor 2: going in

1. Starting from the deck, go north a screen along the west side of the mast. Go west, south and through the door [[gf1-47-1]].
2. On B-Floor 2, go west three times, north, east and then down the stairs [[gf1-47-1]].

#### B-Floor 3: the odd tiles

1. Go west a screen, then south a screen [[gf1-47-1]].
2. Step onto the odd tile in the northeast corner, then stand on the odd tile in the northwest corner. Stairs appear in the southeast corner. Go down them [[gf1-47-1]].

#### B-Floor 4: finding the Girl

1. Go west twice, use a Key on the south door, and go south. The Girl is in a jail cell [[gf1-47-1, gf1-114-1]].
2. Walk south to the jail door. The Airship begins to move. The Girl cannot open the gate but thinks she can escape through the window, so she needs your help from outside [[gf1-47-1, gf4-4-8]].
3. Bogard stays behind to guard her once you try to leave [[gf1-47-1, gf5-11-4]].

#### Climbing to the outside window

1. From the Girl's room, go north a screen, east twice and up the stairs [[gf1-47-1]].
2. On B-Floor 3, go north, west, then up the stairs [[gf1-47-1]].
3. On B-Floor 2, go south, west three times, then north. Use a Mattock on the north pot and go up the stairs [[gf1-47-1]].
4. On Floor 1, go east once and then north a screen, staying on the left side of the mast. Go west twice, south, then east [[gf1-47-1]].
5. Go south down the ladder to the next screen, then east a screen [[gf1-47-1]].

If a fence blocks you on the deck, press the right-hand switch to reach the left-hand one [[gf5-11-3]]. A second route, where you kill the Werewolves on the left side before stepping on the upper right switch to open the fence and the upper left switch to reveal a ladder, is also described [[gf2-38-1]].

#### The Pendant and the fall

Talk to the Girl leaning out of the second window. She gives you the Pendant [[gf1-47-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29006]

Julius then blasts the hero with a fireball and you fall off the ship. You cannot avoid it. You fall several screens and land in a house in Menos [[gf1-47-3, gf4-5-2]].

### The Chocobo Egg

The chocobo is in the forest north-east of Menos, at G8 on the Hikuutei map notes and H7 on the world map legend [[gf3-3-29, gf1-106-1]].

1. From outside Menos, go south twice and cross the bridge [[gf1-49-1]].
2. Go north, east, north twice, west, north twice, east, south, east [[gf1-49-1]].
3. Equip the Chain and use it to cross the gap [[gf1-49-1, gf2-38-3]].
4. Examine the egg. It hatches into a Chocobo that follows you [[gf1-49-2, gf2-38-3, gf4-6-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29022]

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** One player reported that the Familiar Feathered Friend achievement did not pop when they fell from Glaive Castle a second time, and they were already at the Temple of Mana. Whether it can be triggered later is unconfirmed [[ra-comments-17-1]]. Hatch the egg now, as described above, to be safe.

Once back across the chasm, ASK the Chocobo to ride it [[gf1-49-2]]. While riding, enemies cannot harm you, but you cannot kill monsters either [[gf1-49-3, gf2-18-4]].

#### Heading to Jadd

From outside Menos, Jadd is south, south, west, west, north, north, west, north. If you go directly after getting the Chocobo, from the first bridge go west, west, north, north, west, north [[gf1-49-5]]. The next chapter continues from there. Jadd's Weapon Shop sells Gold Helmet (680 GP) and Gold Shield (920 GP) [[gf1-50-1, gf1-50-2]].
