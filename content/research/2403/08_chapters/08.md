## Chapter 8: Mt. Rocks and the Golem Cave

[MERMAID]

This chapter takes you from Jadd, where Lester clears the poison gas and teaches you Mute, through the Metal Crab cave and up Mt. Rocks into the Golem Cave. There you beat Cyclops for the Morning Star, equip it, and beat Golem for the Lit spell [[gf4-7-1, gf1-58-6, gf1-59-5]]. Finish everything in the Jadd and Mt. Rocks region before you walk past Golem, because a bridge collapses behind you afterwards.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Point of No Return:** Entering the cave past Golem collapses the bridge behind you and you cannot return [[gf2-49-3, gf1-59-6]]. Once you proceed, you will not be able to leave Granz Castle until you have completed the events there [[gf3-4-39]]. Finish the Fang and the Were Axe business first, and buy any armor you want at the Mt. Rocks Weapon Shop before you go.

### Preparation

- Golem Cave needs 1 Mattock and 2 Keys [[gf1-57-1]].
- Recommended levels: Metal Crab 33, Cyclops 37, Golem 39 [[gf1-56-1, gf1-58-1, gf1-59-1]].
- Keep a Cure spell handy for all three bosses [[gf1-56-1, gf1-58-3, gf1-59-3]].
- Do not try to fight Golem until you have the Morning Star [[gf3-4-35]].

### Jadd: Lester and Mute

After Davias falls you are taken back to Jadd, where Lester plays his harp to clear the poison gas to the north [[gf4-7-1]]. Before you leave, Lester hands you the Magic Book of Mute (1 MP, blocks monster magic) and you learn it immediately [[gf4-7-1, checklist-items-13-6]].

Heal at the Inn and stock up on anything you need before leaving town; take your Chocobo with you [[gf4-6-9, gf1-55-1]].

Jadd's Weapon Shop sells Gold Helmet for 680 GP and Gold Shield for 920 GP [[gf1-50-1, gf1-50-2]]. You can skip these if you plan to buy Opal gear at Mt. Rocks.

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave the Jadd area, make sure you have finished the Fang (Bag of Fang, which the boy in Jadd wants) and the Were Axe from Davias's Mansion. Those belong to the region you cannot return to [[gf1-50-3, gf3-4-39]].

### Cave North of Jadd

🗺️ **Map:** [View Cave Of Mt Rocks(B3 E4) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-CaveOfMtRocks(B3-E4).png)

#### Overworld approach

From outside Jadd travel south, east, north, east, north twice, and east (chop the trees with your Axe). Then go north twice, east, north, and finally west into the cave [[gf1-55-3]]. Along the way, going down the second vine to the east and south twice reaches a Magic Shop [[gf1-55-4]].

While walking, Molebears, Werewolves and Gorillas each die to a single Fire cast, and Iffish are invulnerable to Fire [[gf5-14-1]].

#### Inside the cave

Go west, north, west (use the Axe), west, then south. Save your game before going left to the Metal Crab [[gf1-55-5]]. The room with the Face enemies takes 2 hits each and they drop X-Ethers [[gf5-14-2]].

#### Boss: Metal Crab

- Recommended level 33; it yields 108 GP and 104 EXP in one guide, and 107 EXP and 101 GP in another [[gf1-56-1, gf1-56-2, gf2-45-1]].
- Stand in the upper left corner of the screen, where the Metal Crab cannot hit you. Equip the Were Axe, wait for your Will Meter to max, then step down and throw the axe at its head [[gf1-56-3]].
- Only its face is vulnerable, and that is not reachable when it is at the bottom of the screen [[gf5-14-3]]. A Wind Spear is also suited to hitting its mouth [[gf2-45-1]].

After the fight, take the stairs down to Basement Floor 2, go west three times, and climb the stairs up to leave the cave [[gf1-56-4]].

### Mt. Rocks

🗺️ **Map:** [View Mt Rocks(F2) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-MtRocks(F2).png)

🗺️ **Map:** [View Shop(D4) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-Shop(D4).png)

#### Outside the cave

From the cave exit, one route goes west, south, east, south; an Item Shop is a screen east then north [[gf1-56-5]]. Otherwise go south three times and east to a pond. Walking to the bottom of the pond restores HP and MP [[gf1-56-6]].

Beware of the robot-like enemy on the way: touching it can inflict Moogle, dropping your defense to 0 [[gf2-46-1]]. Springs are invincible, fast, kill in 2 hits, and inflict Moogle too [[gf5-15-1]].

#### Weapon Shop

From the pond head west, north (stay right), east, north, east, then take the rightmost vine north and go north into the Weapon Shop [[gf1-56-7]].

| Item | Price | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| Opal Armor | 3000 GP (+19 DP) | [[gf1-95-3, gf2-75-5]] |
| Opal Helmet | 3500 GP | [[gf1-95-4, gf2-76-5]] |
| Opal Shield | 3900 GP | [[gf1-95-5, gf2-77-5]] |

Opal gear is far stronger than Gold [[gf2-46-2]]. If you lack money, fight nearby enemies until you can afford it [[gf2-46-2]].

The Mt. Rocks Magic Shop sells Ether 320, Flame x2 60, Blaze x2 240, Frost x2 320, Silence 100, and Pillow 100 GP [[gf2-81-1]].

#### To Golem Cave

Climb south to the base of the cliff, take the left vine north three times, then go west and chop the trees. Walk to the north side, then east, north twice, east, south twice (use the Axe), east, and north into Golem Cave [[gf1-56-8]].

### Golem Cave

#### Floor 1

Many screens warp you back to the first screen [[gf1-57-2, gf1-117-3]]. If you go the wrong way you return to the start [[gf5-16-1]].

1. Go north, east twice, and use a Mattock on the north wall. Go north, then west [[gf1-57-4]].
2. In the four-chest room, place a chest on a tile, then knock it off (attack it) to open the door. Per another guide, place all four chests on the four tiles and attack one [[gf1-57-4, gf2-47-1]]. If you destroy a chest, leave and re-enter [[gf2-47-1]]. The Werewolf puzzle room is solved by pushing a chest onto a button and destroying it [[gf5-16-2]].
3. Go north, east, open the door with a Key, then east, south three times, east twice, and up the stairs [[gf1-57-5]].

#### Floor 2 and Boss: Cyclops

Save and heal before going east [[gf1-57-6]].

- Level 37; yields 63 GP and 71 EXP [[gf1-58-1, gf1-58-2]].
- Stay below him as he charges horizontally and hit him with the Were Axe; when he reaches the bottom go above and hit him as he climbs [[gf1-58-4]].
- His weapon can kill in one hit; his eye takes more damage than his feet [[gf5-16-3]]. He only attacks horizontally, sometimes twice in a row [[gf2-48-2]].

### Morning Star (Cyclops chest)

Open the chest that appears after the fight for the Morning Star [[gf2-48-3, gf1-58-6]]. Equip it right away [[gf2-48-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29061]

The Morning Star gives +30 ATK and does everything a Mattock does, so you can discard Mattocks [[checklist-items-7-12, gf2-48-4]].

### Golem

Go back west three times to the entrance, north, heal and save, then unlock the north door with a Key [[gf1-58-7]].

- Level 39; 96 GP and 172 EXP [[gf1-59-1, gf1-59-2]].
- Use only the Morning Star. Stay at the bottom, sidestep downward charges and strike [[gf1-59-3, gf1-59-4]]. Stay close and mash the attack button to score 2 hits per swing [[gf5-16-5]].
- Reward: Spell Book of Lit (2 MP, thunderbolt) [[gf1-59-5, checklist-items-13-7]].

Then go north, smash the rocks with the Morning Star, and climb the stairs. On Floor 2 climb the vine and go east; the bridge collapses and you come out beside the waterfall [[gf1-59-6]].
