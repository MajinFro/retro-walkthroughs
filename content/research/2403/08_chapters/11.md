## Chapter 11: The Lich, the Kraken and the Undersea Volcano

[MERMAID]

This chapter takes you from Lorim to the Lich Cave for the Nuke spell, then to the Bone Key cave in the Plain of Floating Rocks for the Kraken, then through the Undersea Volcano to Iflyte and the Rusty Sword. The guide recommends getting Nuke before the Legendary Sword, even though the story points you to the sword first [[gf1-68-2]]. Four achievements are available here: Meltdown, Who's Your Octo-Daddy Now?, Duck Hunter and Through the Fire and Flames. The Zeus Axe has to be picked up and equipped before Iflyte, so read the warnings first.

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning (Duck Hunter, 29088):** The Zeus Axe sits in a chest on Undersea Volcano B-Floor 3 [[gf1-72-4, gf1-123-3]]. Take it and equip it BEFORE you fight Iflyte [[gf1-72-4, ra-comments-3-2]]. Never sell or discard it; the Timber!!! achievement needs it later [[ra-comments-55-1]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning (Through the Fire and Flames, 29068):** One player killed Iflyte and then went back for the Zeus Axe, and lost the Iflyte achievement [[ra-comments-3-2]]. Another player asked whether leaving the room after the kill was the cause [[ra-comments-29-1]]. This is unconfirmed: no source answers whether leaving the room before taking the Rusty Sword stops the achievement. Take the Zeus Axe first, then open Iflyte's chest immediately without leaving the room.

### Preparation

- You need Keys, a Mattock or the Morning Star, the Sickle and the Ice spell for the dungeons ahead [[gf1-71-1, gf1-72-2, gf1-72-7]].
- The Item Shop in the Plain of Floating Rocks sells Keys (x4) for 15 GP and Mattocks (x7) for 60 GP [[gf1-97-4]]. The guide notes you need 1 Key for the Kraken's Cave and 2 Keys for the Undersea Volcano [[gf1-71-1, gf1-72-4, gf1-72-7]].
- Cure and the Morning Star are the guide's picks for the Lich [[gf1-69-4]].

### Lorim: the Bone Key (if not already collected)

From Wendel, you reach Lorim by going south twice to the pier, then (s,w,w,s,w,w,s,e,s,s,s,s) onto the pier, then (n,e,s,s,e,n) into the town [[gf1-64-4]]. After finishing the Ice Cave, walk back to Lorim, go north into the throne room and talk to the king [[gf1-68-2]]. If you already collected the Bone Key at the end of chapter 10, skip to the Lich Cave.

1. Make sure you have a free Items slot [[gf2-56-4]].
2. From the town entrance go east one screen, then north one screen, and talk to Cibba [[gf4-9-12, gf2-56-4]].
3. Cibba gives you the Bone Key and tells you to use it at the cave in the Floatrocks, where the guardian holds the Legendary Sword [[gf2-56-4, gf1-68-2]].

### Lich Cave

The guide's advice is to take Nuke before the sword [[gf1-68-2]]. From outside Lorim go (s,w,n,n,w,s), step into the water, go (n,n,n,n,w,n,n,w,w,w,n,n) to dry land, then (w,s,w,s,w,n) into the cave [[gf1-68-3]].

#### Floors 1 to B-Floor 3

Take the stairs down on Floor 1, then take the stairs down again on B-Floor 2 to reach B-Floor 3 [[gf1-68-4]]. On B-Floor 3, heal up and save; the Lich waits to the north [[gf1-68-4]].

🗺️ **Map:** [View Cave Of Palmy Desert(D15) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-CaveOfPalmyDesert(D15).png) (the Lich Cave sits at world-map square D15) [[gf1-68-3]]

### Nuke spell (Lich)

- Recommended level is 48 [[gf1-69-1]]. Rewards are 220 GP and 198 EXP according to the first guide [[gf1-69-2]].
- Attacks: flying skulls. Your weapon can destroy them [[gf1-69-3, gf3-6-22]].
- Never touch him; contact deals very heavy damage, and you can hit him on any part of his body [[gf2-61-1]].
- Fill HP and MP first. The Flame Flail is suggested for its fast attack that hits him several times [[gf2-60-1]].
- Pattern: he flies a figure eight and sometimes leaves the screen and reappears on the other side [[gf1-69-4]]. Stand in the middle with the Morning Star; if you strike at the right time he passes over you without damage. Alternate attacks with Cure [[gf1-69-4]].
- Another guide advises staying at the bottom and moving left or right as he turns; when he goes south, go up with him and then return to the bottom after he passes [[gf3-6-20, gf3-6-22]].
- If you play as a mage build, fully charged Lit spells are the advice; he hits for about 100 per hit [[gf5-23-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29069]

Defeating the Lich gives you the final spell, Nuke [[gf1-70-1, gf4-8-6]]. It costs 3 MP [[checklist-items-13-8]]. You now have all 8 spells for this chapter's checkpoint [[gf1-70-1]]. Note: one guide says the Nuke Magic comes from "Death" [[gf2-15-9]]; the boss fight above is the one other sources confirm as the Lich.

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave the Lich Cave:** Nuke is needed later to blast the crystal in the Desert of Crystal [[gf1-73-7]].

### Plain of Floating Rocks

🗺️ **Map:** [View Shop(N16) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-Shop(N16).png)

From outside the Lich Cave go (s,e,n,e,n,e,s,s,w,w,w,w,w,n,n,w,w,n,n,n,n,n,n,w,n,n,w,s) onto dry land [[gf1-70-2]]. Other starting points: from outside Lorim use the long route [[gf1-70-3]], or from the pier south of Wendel use (s,s,s,s,e,e,s,s,s,s,s,w,n,n,w,s) [[gf1-70-4]].

1. On dry land go north, then east a screen, use the chain to cross the stream, go south twice, east, south three times and west into the Plain of Floating Rocks [[gf1-70-5]].
2. Break rocks with the Morning Star and continue south [[gf1-70-5, gf5-21-5]].
3. An Item Shop is one screen west of that path [[gf1-70-6]]. Stock: X-Cure (x2) 160 GP, Pure (x3) 30 GP, EyeDrop (x3) 60 GP, Soft (x3) 90 GP, Moogle (x3) 120 GP, Key (x4) 15 GP, Mattock (x7) 60 GP [[gf1-97-4]].
4. Go south, east, south twice, west, north, and across the bridge. Then south twice into the Weapon Shop [[gf1-70-5]].
5. Buy the **Thunder Spear** for 11250 GP (sells for 5625) [[gf1-70-7, gf4-9-11]]. It is the recommended weapon for the Kraken and Iflyte [[gf1-71-3, gf1-73-3]]. It can paralyze and does double damage to water monsters [[checklist-items-7-14]].
6. Go south, east, south, east twice and north [[gf1-70-5]]. The Magic Shop is here [[gf1-70-8]]. It sells X-Ether 640 GP, Flame 60 GP, Blaze 240 GP, Frost 320 GP, LitBolt 100 GP and Thunder 400 GP [[gf1-97-6]].
7. Go west and use the Bone Key on the door (equip it first) [[gf1-70-5, gf5-21-6]].

Tortoises here drop X-Cure [[gf1-99-62]]; one guide says to use the Morning Star only on them [[gf2-78-18]].

### Kraken's Cave

🗺️ **Map:** [View Cave Of Floatrocks(A15&P1) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-CaveOfFloatrocks(A15&P1).png)

#### Floor 1

Go north, use a Key, go north again and break the east wall with a Mattock or Morning Star. Go east twice, step on the top tile and then the bottom tile, and pass through the east door. Go north twice, break the north wall, then go north to the stairs down [[gf1-71-1]].

Another guide describes a room with two switches: destroy the rocks to press the upper switch and then the lower one [[gf2-57-2]]. A mage guide says the first button opens the river and the second opens the door [[gf5-21-7]].

#### B-Floor 2: Kraken

Heal and save, then cross the bridge; the Kraken rises from the water [[gf1-71-2]].

- Recommended level 47 [[gf1-71-2]]. Rewards: 199 EXP and 150+ GP in one guide [[gf2-58-1]].
- Attacks: tentacles. Use the Thunder Spear and Cure [[gf1-71-3]].
- Stand directly above it and stab its head quickly; your shield blocks about half the blows. Its explosion may damage you [[gf1-71-4]].
- Hit its head, then move out of the way of its tentacles [[gf2-58-2]]. You will likely take heavy damage regardless [[gf3-6-10]].
- Magic option: the tentacles are invulnerable; cast Fire at its head from the second bridge tile from the right with a full Will meter [[gf5-21-7]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29067]

A new exit appears after the Kraken falls [[gf1-71-4, gf4-9-12]].

#### Side trip: Elixir Island (optional)

Defeating all the fire-breathing sea horses with Lit on Elixir Island makes an Elixir appear [[gf5-21-3]]. No achievement needs it, so skip it if you are short on time.

### Undersea Volcano

Exit the cave onto an island, go east, south, west, north and enter the cave [[gf1-72-1]]. Enemies are strong here, so watch HP [[gf1-72-4]]. Earth Elementals die to one Lit cast and drop X-Ethers [[gf5-22-1]].

#### Floor 1

Go south, west twice, north and break the west wall. Go west, clear the vines with the Sickle, freeze two enemies with Ice and put them on the odd tiles, then take the stairs down [[gf1-72-2]]. Another guide says to leave the knights alive, turn two into snowmen on the switches, and re-enter if only one is left [[gf2-58-1]].

#### B-Floor 2 and B-Floor 3: Zeus Axe (past the lava pools)

Go west and through the door [[gf1-72-3]]. On B-Floor 3 the marsh costs about 100 HP, so cross it quickly [[gf1-72-4]]. Lava also hurts: walking across does damage equal to three times your level [[gf3-6-17]].

Route: go west, north a screen, then north, west, south, west, south. Unlock the door with a Key and go south [[gf1-72-4]]. Kill every enemy and a chest appears in the middle [[gf2-58-4]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29088]

Open the chest for the **Zeus Axe** (+48 ATK, sells for 4900 GP but do not sell it) [[checklist-items-7-5, gf1-72-4, ra-comments-55-1]]. It is past the lava pools [[ra-comments-49-1]]. Equip it now to unlock Duck Hunter before moving on [[gf1-72-4]].

Then go north twice, break the east wall, go east, north, east, north, break the east wall and descend the stairs [[gf1-72-5]]. On B-Floor 4 go into the new cave opening [[gf1-72-5]].

#### B-Floor 5

Go north, east twice, south twice, and walk to the bottom of the middle pond to restore HP and MP [[gf1-72-6]]. Go north, use a Key on the door, go east, break the bottom-right boulder and descend [[gf1-72-7]]. The door is locked going west [[gf1-124-2]].

#### B-Floor 6 and 7

Choose the right door of three [[gf1-72-8]]. On B-Floor 7 go south, west, north, freeze two monsters onto the odd tiles, knock away a snowman and take the stairs down [[gf1-72-9]].

#### B-Floor 8: Iflyte

Heal up and save, then go west [[gf1-73-1]]. Confirm the Zeus Axe is already equipped or at least collected.

- Recommended level 50 [[gf1-73-2]]. Rewards about 264 to 271 GP and 213 to 223 EXP [[gf2-59-1]].
- Best gear: Thunder Spear and Cure [[gf1-73-3]].
- He rolls into a ball across the screen, and stops at the top to show his body. Hit his head when revealed, or hit his center while rolling [[gf1-73-4]]. Dodge the roll [[gf2-59-2]].
- Lit works too, even while he bounces [[gf5-22-3]].

Once he dies a chest appears in the middle [[gf2-59-3]]. Open it right away, without leaving the room [[gf4-9-14]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29068]

You receive the **Rusty Sword** (+20 ATK) [[gf5-22-4, checklist-items-7-17]]. Do not worry about it vanishing later; it becomes XCalibur as part of the story [[checklist-items-7-17]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave the Undersea Volcano:** You are taken outside after the chest [[gf2-59-3]]. If your Chocobot is stuck in the upper left corner, leave the screen and return [[gf2-59-3]]. Next, return to Cibba in Lorim and then head to the Cave of Ruins [[gf1-73-7]].
