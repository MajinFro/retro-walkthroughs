## Chapter 7: Davias's Mansion

[MERMAID]

Davias's Mansion is the big house in the northwest of Jadd. Three achievements are tied to it: equipping the Were Axe, recruiting Lester, and defeating Davias [[gf1-50-1, gf1-53-6, gf2-43-1, gf1-116-4]]. Davias's death sends you straight back to Jadd, so finish everything in the mansion before you fight him [[gf1-55-1]].

### Preparation

🗺️ **Map:** [View City Of Jadt(B9) Davias Mansion Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-CityOfJadt(B9)-DaviasMansion.png)

- You need Amanda's Tears, the Ice Spell, at least two Mattocks and two Keys [[gf1-53-4, gf1-53-6, gf1-53-7, gf2-43-1]]. The Jadd Item Shop sells Keys (15 GP) and Mattocks (60 GP) [[gf1-50-1]].
- Amanda's Tears come from Amanda's body after Medusa's Cave [[gf1-115-3, gf2-42-1]].
- The Jadd Weapon Shop sells the Gold Helmet (680 GP) and Gold Shield (920 GP) [[gf1-50-1, gf1-50-2]]. The Gold Shield can sometimes block Black Mage fireballs [[gf5-13-3]].
- Some mansion monsters are invulnerable to the Wind Spear, so the Silver Sword is recommended [[gf1-53-2]].
- Black Mages in the mansion are invincible and deal huge damage, so avoid them [[gf5-13-3]]. Flames and Vampires need Magic or Swords (a Morning Star also works on Vampires) [[gf2-78-8, gf2-78-19]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** The Were Axe (Kevin's Worst Nightmare) is missable. Take it from the chest on Floor 1 and equip it before you defeat Davias, whose death warps you to Jadd [[gf1-53-6, gf2-43-3, gf1-55-1]]. Do not sell it: an axe is needed for Timber!!! later, and the Battle Axe cannot damage the final boss [[ra-comments-55-4, gf1-104-10]].

### Davias's Mansion

#### Floor 1 to Floor 2: Lester freed from the curse

1. Enter the mansion from Jadd, go up the stairs on Floor 1, then go east twice on Floor 2 into the room where Davias and the bird were [[gf1-53-4, gf2-43-1]].
2. Equip Amanda's Tears and talk to the parrot. He turns into Lester and joins you [[gf1-53-4, gf2-43-1, gf1-27-5]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29059]

3. A staircase appears in the lower right corner after Lester plays his mystic tune. Take it down to Floor 1 [[gf4-6-7, gf1-53-4]].

#### Floor 1: Mattock wall and the tile room

4. Go east twice and use a Mattock on the north wall. Go north twice, use a Key on the west door, go west and south one screen. Walk counter-clockwise around the tiles and climb the stairs [[gf1-53-4]].

#### Floor 2: Panel puzzle

5. Go west four times. Step onto the odd tile one space north of the iron fence and walk counter-clockwise until stairs appear, then go down [[gf1-53-5]].
6. Another guide describes the stair trigger as pressing right, up, left, then down in order [[gf5-13-4]]. A third describes stepping on the floor panels in numbered order to make a ladder appear [[gf2-43-2]]. All three describe the same stair trigger; the numbered-panel diagram from that guide is:

```
 43       Legend: the numbers are the order of the panels you should follow. 
5xx2
6xx1
 7 
```

#### Floor 1: Were Axe (snowman switch room)

7. Back on Floor 1, go north, west, and south four times [[gf1-53-6]].
8. Cast Ice on one monster to freeze it into a snowman. Push it onto the odd tile (the switch) and go east through the opened door [[gf1-53-6, gf2-43-3]].
9. The door locks behind you. Defeat every enemy in the room and a chest appears [[gf2-43-3, gf1-53-6]].
10. Open the chest for the **Were Axe** (+20 AP, chops trees) [[gf1-53-6, gf1-83-7]]. Equip it now.

[ACHIEVEMENT:29086]

#### Return to the upper floors

11. Go west, north twice, and use a Mattock on the east wall. Go east and south, then up the stairs [[gf1-53-7]].
12. On Floor 2, unlock the north door with a Key and go north, then take the stairs up to Floor 3 [[gf1-53-7]].

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave the mansion, heal up and make sure nothing remains to collect, since Davias's defeat sends you to Jadd [[gf1-54-1, gf1-55-1]].

### Davias

Heal up, then go west on Floor 3 [[gf1-54-1]]. The recommended level is 30; he gives 98 GP and 95 EXP in one guide [[gf1-54-1, gf1-54-2]].

- He is guarded by four circling fireballs and throws flame balls, moving east and west across the top of the screen [[gf1-54-3]].
- Quick approach: stay inside the fireball ring and keep slashing with the Were Axe. Safer approach: stay at the bottom, dodge, and fire your powered-up weapon [[gf1-54-3]].
- Nectar adds +15 attack (Davias normally takes 5 damage). Walk into his face and mash attack to extend invincibility. Cast Cure when HP is under 45, since his body hits for about 40 [[gf5-13-6]].
- Do not touch the ring fireballs; you can attack the other fireballs with your weapon [[gf1-54-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29060]

After he falls, he reveals Garuda flew the Pendant over Mt. Rocks toward Glaive, and you are warped to Jadd [[gf4-6-9, gf1-55-1]]. Lester plays his harp to clear the poison gas and gives you the Magic Book of Mute [[gf4-7-1]].
