## Chapter 10: Ish, Lorim and the Ice Cave

[MERMAID]

This chapter takes you from Ish, where Dr. Bowow turns your chocobo into the Chocobot, across the sea to Wendel and Lorim, through the Ice Cave to Kary, and back to Cibba for the Bone Key [[gf1-63-14, gf1-64-3, gf1-64-5, gf1-68-1, gf1-68-2]]. Two achievements are here: Chocobo 2.0 and Defroster.

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Ish:** spend your money here first. The Ish weapon shop sells the Flame Chain (6300 GP), Flame Armor (5625 GP) and Flame Shield (4400 GP) [[gf1-63-2, gf1-63-3, gf1-63-4]]. One guide says to buy the Flame Flail but not the armor or shield [[gf3-5-10]]. Another says to buy the Flame Armor [[gf5-19-2]]. The Flame Chain is the one purchase the Ice Cave requires [[gf1-66-4]]; the armor and shield are optional.

> [!WARNING]
> **Wendel revisit unconfirmed:** Whether Wendel can be revisited after Dime Tower is unconfirmed; commenters believe not. If you skipped War Is Hell in chapter 4, you can still kill the boy in south-west Wendel now, on this visit. Treat it as lost once you enter Dime Tower.

### Ish

🗺️ **Map:** [View City Of Isch(J3) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-CityOfIsch(J3).png)

Your chocobo carries you into Ish, and you wake in a house beside Bogard, who survived the fall from the airship thanks to Sarah but has a broken back [[gf4-8-1]].

1. Leave the house and talk to Sarah. She says the chocobo is being fixed at Doctor Bowow's house [[gf1-63-13]].
2. Shop before leaving. Ish contains an Inn (10 GP), a Weapon Shop and an Item Shop [[gf1-63-1]].
   - Weapon Shop: Flame Chain 6300 GP, Flame Armor 5625 GP, Flame Shield 4400 GP [[gf1-63-2, gf1-63-3, gf1-63-4]]. You need the Flame Chain for the Ice Cave [[gf1-66-4]].
   - Item Shop: X-Cure 160, Pure 30, EyeDrop 60, Soft 90, Moogle 120, Key 15, Mattock 60 [[gf1-63-5, gf1-63-6, gf1-63-7, gf1-63-8, gf1-63-9, gf1-63-10, gf1-63-11]]. The Ice Cave needs 2 Keys [[gf1-66-4]]. Buy extra Keys and a Mattock too.
   - A Magic Shop west of Ish sells X-Ether (640 GP) [[gf1-64-1, gf2-81-4]].
3. Rest at the Inn for 10 GP [[gf2-53-2]].

### Dr. Bowow builds the Chocobot

Go west twice and enter Dr. Bowow's house [[gf1-63-14]]. He has mechanized the chocobo's legs so it can cross water, and renamed it Chocobot [[gf4-8-2, gf2-53-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29065]

Visit his house on the west side of town; you can only enter or leave the water at a dock [[gf3-5-11]].

1. Talk to Bogard again. He says you need the sword Excalibur to beat Julius and tells you to ask Cibba in Wendel [[gf1-63-15, gf4-8-3]].
2. Talk to Dr. Bowow again. He says to sail east from the northern pier and go north to Wendel; south leads to the Ammonites [[gf4-8-4]].
3. Leave town to the south and talk to the Chocobot outside to recruit it [[gf5-19-2]]. If it is stuck behind rocks in the upper left corner, leave the screen and return [[gf2-59-4]].

#### Journey to Wendel

From outside Ish go west, west, north, north, west to the pier, step into the water, go east seven times, north twice, onto the pier, then north twice into Wendel [[gf1-64-2]]. In Wendel go north once and talk to the girl near the top. She says Cibba went to Lorim, near the snowfields [[gf1-64-3, gf4-8-7]].

Wendel has an Inn, a Weapon Shop, an Item Shop and a temple [[gf1-42-2]]. If War Is Hell was missed in chapter 4, kill the boy now: he stands one screen west of the entrance and says "Dwarf Cave is to the west!" [[gf4-3-1, ra-comments-54-8]]. Commenters say it can be done on a later Wendel visit before Dime Tower [[ra-comments-54-7]].

### Lorim

🗺️ **Map:** [View Lorim(M6) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-Lorim(M6).png)

From Wendel go south twice to the pier, then (s,w,w,s,w,w,s,e,s,s,s,s) onto the pier, then (n,e,s,s,e,n) into Lorim [[gf1-64-4]]. Go north into the throne room. The king says Kary froze his subjects and Cibba [[gf1-64-5]]. Your task is to find Kary [[gf1-64-5]].

Optional: Ice Armor (7350 GP) and Ice Shield (6200 GP) are sold at a weapon shop [[gf1-65-1, gf4-9-1, gf4-9-2]]. Guides name its location differently (Snow Field, NW Snow Fields or Ammonite Coast); it is the lone shop at map square N2, linked as the Shop(N2) map below [[gf1-85-7, checklist-items-4-10, gf2-75-7, gf3-5-15]]. Directions from outside Lorim: (s,w,n,n,w,s,n,n,n,n,w,n,e,e,e,e,e,s,s,s,e,s,s,s,s,w,n,n,w,s,n,e,s,e,n,n,n,w,n,w,n) [[gf1-65-2]]. Ice Armor makes Kary's hits weak [[gf1-67-5]].

### Snowfields

🗺️ **Map:** [View Shop(N2) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-Shop(N2).png)

From outside Lorim go south, west, north twice, west, chop the trees with the Axe, and go north four screens [[gf1-66-1]]. There is an item shop one screen west with the usual stock [[gf1-66-1, gf1-97-5]]. The Snowfields are home to Snowman, Walrus and Wolfhead [[gf1-99-53, gf1-99-54, gf1-99-55]]. Wolves and Snowmen die to one Fire cast; Walruses die if your Will meter is mostly full [[gf5-19-3]]. Then go east twice, south twice, west, south, east twice and enter the cave [[gf1-66-1]].

### Ice Cave

🗺️ **Map:** [View Cave Of Snowfields(N4) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-CaveOfSnowfields(N4).png)

You need 2 Keys, the Flame Chain and a Mattock or Morning Star [[gf1-66-4, gf1-66-5]]. Ice acts as a one-way conveyor belt [[gf5-20-4]]. Eyes here are invincible and inflict Stone [[gf5-20-1]]. Potos die to one Fire cast and drop X-Ethers; Duck Knights die to two [[gf5-20-2, gf5-20-3]].

#### Floor 1

1. Go north, walk counter-clockwise around the ice and go north [[gf1-66-3]].
2. Step on the odd tile, go south to the wall and wait for the snowman, then push him north onto the odd tile [[gf1-66-3, gf2-55-1]].
3. Go east, use the Flame Chain to go north, cross the ice with the Chain going west [[gf1-66-4]].
4. Use a Key on the north door, go east twice, use a Key on the next door and climb the stairs [[gf1-66-4]].

#### Floor 2

Go north, west and onto the ice. Walk around the rocks and back onto the ice. In the room with the down stairs, take the bottom-left ice path south, walk around the rocks and back on the ice, then take the single middle ice path west. Break the north wall with a Mattock or Morning Star, go north and up the stairs [[gf1-66-5]].

#### Floor 3

1. Break the north wall and go north to the pond that heals HP and MP [[gf1-66-6, gf5-20-5]].
2. Go south a screen and west a screen, then south to a room of enemies. Kill all of them and open the chest for an **Elixir** [[gf1-66-7, gf2-55-2]].
3. Go north, east twice, use a Key on the door and climb to Floor 4 [[gf1-66-8]].
4. Go east and back into the cave to Floor 3 [[gf1-66-9]].
5. Cross the ice with the Chain, go west twice, south, and use the Chain to reach the middle. Step on the tile and take the northwest ice patch, then step on the odd tile in the southeast corner. Go south and east twice, cutting the bramble with the Axe, then take the east ice track and the south ice [[gf1-66-11]].
6. Use the Chain across the ice west, then a Key on the north door [[gf1-66-11]]. Walk to the bottom of the lake to restore HP and MP [[gf1-66-10]].
7. Go onto the ice west, break the west wall with a Mattock or Morning Star, go through, cross the streams with the Chain and climb the stairs [[gf1-66-11]].
8. On Floor 4 go east and back into the cave, then on Floor 3 go east, north and up the stairs [[gf1-66-12]].

One room has a switch that opens the south door by hitting middle poles with a flail and stepping on the switch [[gf2-55-3]].

### Kary

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave for the boss:** heal up. A guide recommends level 46 [[gf1-67-1]]; another says you need 35 Will [[gf5-3-5]].

Go east to fight Kary [[gf1-67-1]]. She attacks with Icicle Throw and Tail Lash and only her head is vulnerable [[gf1-67-3]]. The Flame Chain destroys icicles [[gf1-67-3]]; the Flame Shield blocks all of them [[gf2-56-2]]. She moves across the screen at top, middle or bottom: stay above or below her, hit her head, and after she crosses the middle ice move opposite to her direction [[gf1-67-4]]. Magic users: she is immune to Nuke; hit her head with Fire, then Lit, then Lit [[gf5-20-6, gf5-20-7]]. Victory gives 129 GP and about 181 EXP (another guide says 183) [[gf1-67-2, gf2-56-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29066]

A chest appears; open it for the **Ice Sword** and a transport out of the cave [[gf1-68-1, gf2-56-3]]. It is +40 ATK, double damage to fire monsters and zero to ice monsters [[checklist-items-7-19]].

### Ammonite Coast

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** keep one free item slot before speaking to Cibba, or you may not receive the Bone Key [[gf2-56-4]].

Return to Lorim, talk to the king, then go (s,e,n) to Cibba, who gives you the **Bone Key** [[gf1-68-2, gf4-9-1]]. He sends you via the Ammonite Coast to the cave in the Floatrocks [[gf4-9-1]].

Ammonite Coast hazards: Manta Rays inflict Poison and Griffin Hands inflict Moogle, which hopping on the Chocobo cures [[gf5-21-4]]. Octopuses give 132 EXP and 152 G [[gf2-78-14]]. The Morning Star destroys Floating Rocks [[gf2-56-5]]. The Kraken's cave opens by equipping the Bone Key [[gf5-21-6]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Lorim:** confirm the Bone Key is in your inventory and you hold the Ice Sword.
