## Chapter 2: Topple, Bogard and Kett's

[MERMAID]

This chapter takes you from the foot of the waterfall to Kett's. You rescue the Girl east of Topple, collect your first Mattock from Bogard, use it in the East Cave, and rest at Kett's to learn Cure. Three achievements are earned here: Damsel in Distress, Pearl of Wisdom and Rest and Heal Your Wounds [[gf1-33-10, gf1-34-1, gf1-36-4]].

> [!TIP]
> **Preparation:** Gold matters in this chapter. Buy the Iron Helmet in Topple (140 GP) and a set of Keys, and bring enough gold for the Battle Axe (150 GP) later [[gf1-33-1, gf1-33-2, gf1-33-7, gf1-35-2]]. Fight around the Topple area until you reach level 3 or 4 and have over 140 gold [[gf1-33-2]].

### Combat notes for this chapter

- When you level up, your HP and MP are fully restored [[gf3-2-3]].
- Goblins take 2 hits and drop Candy; Mushrooms take 1 hit. Both give only 1 experience point [[gf5-5-1]]. Candy restores 8 HP when used or sells for 2 G [[gf5-5-1]].
- Imps are dangerous: 3 hits to kill with only 1 level-up, 2 hits with 2 level-ups. Rabites take 1 hit [[gf5-5-3]].
- If you leave at least one enemy alive on a screen and move one area away, the enemies respawn. If you kill them all, you must move four areas away [[gf3-2-4]].
- The sword has a spin attack: press Attack with the d-pad neutral and keep holding to keep spinning for up to 8 seconds while moving freely [[gf3-2-6]].
- The Girl heals you when you ASK her [[gf1-33-11, gf2-17-2]].

### Topple

🗺️ **Map:** [View City Of Toppel(I8) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-CityOfToppel(I8).png)

#### Outside Topple: rescuing the Girl

From the bottom of the falls, walk east two screens, south, east, and north to reach Topple [[gf1-33-1]]. Another route reads: east, east, south, south, then east to the screen with the Girl [[gf2-26-3, gf5-5-1]]. Before rescuing the Girl, you can farm: you visit this screen and the one east of it several times. Kill all but one enemy on the first and second visits, then clear them all. The aim is to be about 7 kills past level 2 before the rescue [[gf5-5-1]]. Keep alternating Power and Wisdom on these level-ups, as chapter 1 explains [[gf1-104-9]].

Go to the screen east of the farming area. A girl is yelling for help [[gf1-33-10]]. Defeat ALL monsters on this screen. The clue that they are all dead is that the music stops [[gf2-26-3]]. These are Mushrooms [[gf5-5-2]]. Then talk to the injured Hasim. He asks you to take the Girl to Wendel and see Bogard, and then dies [[gf1-33-10, gf4-2-4]]. The Girl joins your party [[gf1-33-10]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:28943]

Ask the Girl whenever you need healing; she regenerates your HP gradually [[gf2-26-3]]. Try to get your second level-up and 165 G as soon as possible [[gf5-5-2]].

#### Town of Topple

Go back left one screen, then north one screen to find the entrance to Topple [[gf2-26-3]]. The Inn is right at the entrance and costs 10 GP [[gf2-26-4, gf1-33-3]]. Go to the next screen left and then north for the Item and Weapon shops [[gf2-26-4]].

Buy the Iron Helmet, equip it, and sell your Bronze helmet [[gf1-33-2, gf2-26-5]]. Buy Keys at the Item Shop: a set of 4 costs 15 GP [[gf1-33-7]], and one guide recommends 2 sets (30 GP) [[gf2-26-6]]. Consider buying a couple of Cure potions if you had trouble with the monsters [[gf1-33-1]]. Then exit the town.

### Bogard

Exit Topple and go east two screens. From here, go south, east, north, west, north, west three times, and north two screens. Follow the vines around and go south one screen. Then go west one screen and climb the vine north. Enter Bogard's house [[gf1-33-13]].

### Bogard's House

🗺️ **Map:** [View Bogard's House(G5) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-Bogard'sHouse(G5).png)

Talk to the old man three times. The first times he walks away from you, and on the third he notices the Girl's pendant and reveals himself as Bogard [[gf1-34-1, gf2-27-1]]. He tells you to see Cibba in Wendel and opens his back room [[gf1-34-2, gf2-27-1]]. Go north into the room and open the chest for a **Mattock** [[gf1-34-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:28944]

Bogard tells you to use the Mattock to break rocks in the cave on the east side of the mountains [[gf4-2-5]]. To use it, equip it from the item screen and press B; unequip it afterward so you do not use it by accident [[gf1-34-2]].

### East Cave

🗺️ **Map:** [View Cave(East Of Bogard's House)(J6 J7) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-Cave(EastOfBogard'sHouse)(J6-J7).png)

From Bogard's house, go south, east, north, south on the other side of the mountains, and east twice. Climb down the vine into the East Cave [[gf1-34-4]]. Equip the Mattock, stand in front of the rocks blocking the way south, and press B to clear them [[gf2-28-1, gf1-35-1]]. Go south two screens and up the stairs [[gf1-35-1]]. Use the ladder at the bottom right to leave to the world map [[gf2-28-1]].

#### The Battle Axe house

Once outside, head east twice, south, and east, and enter the little house in the middle of the screen [[gf1-35-2]]. The man sells a **Battle Axe** for 150 gold (+8 ATK) [[gf1-35-2, checklist-items-7-1]]. Buy it and also a Pure potion, then equip the Axe [[gf1-35-2]]. If you lack gold, kill enemies and come back [[gf2-28-2]]. In the marsh, Flowers die in one hit (ignore their treasure) and Marmablues take 2 or 3 hits, so avoid them [[gf5-5-6]]. You may notice you cannot damage the Swamp Man; some monsters are protected against some weapons or magic [[gf1-35-3]].

### Kett's

🗺️ **Map:** [View Ketts(K10) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-Ketts(K10).png)

From the shop, go east, south twice, and west three screens to the building [[gf1-35-2]]. Enter Kett's. The man at the door says the east room is only for Mr. Lee [[gf1-36-1]]. In the north room, two people tell you the key to the Marsh Cave is in the Lizardmen's nest to the south, and that a man threw a mirror away in the Cave of Marsh [[gf1-36-2, gf1-36-3]].

Go south from that room, west into another room, then north through the door. Walk onto the unoccupied bed [[gf1-36-4, gf2-29-1]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Sleeping makes the Girl disappear by morning [[gf1-36-4, gf5-5-7]]. You cannot use her healing afterward in this chapter, so heal and stock up first.

Just before you sleep, the Girl finds the Book of Cure and gives it to you. You learn **Cure** (2 MP, recovers HP) [[gf1-36-4, checklist-items-13-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:28945]

In the morning the Girl is gone. Ask the man guarding Mr. Lee's room and he says he has not seen her [[gf1-36-4]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Kett's:** The Lizardmen's nest and Kett's dungeon are handled in the next chapter. Make sure you have the Battle Axe, Keys, Mattocks and Cure equipped, and gold in hand [[gf1-37-1, gf1-35-2]].
