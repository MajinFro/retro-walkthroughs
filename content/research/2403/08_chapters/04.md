## Chapter 4: Wendel, the Silver Mine and Gaia

[MERMAID]

This chapter takes you from the Vampire fight's aftermath into Wendel, where the man who helped you in Marsh Cave is unmasked as Julius [[gf4-3-10]]. You then buy Oil, ride the mine cart, recruit Watts, beat Megapede for Silver, equip Silver gear and cross Gaia, where Bogard joins you [[gf1-44-2, gf1-45-2, gf2-37-3, gf1-46-2]]. The one thing you must not skip is the boy in south-west Wendel (War Is Hell), which is missable [[ra-comments-1-1, ra-comments-54-5]].

### Area Overview & Preparation

- Wendel has an Inn (10 GP), a weapon shop, an item shop and the temple where Cibba lives [[gf1-42-2, gf1-92-10]].
- Heal is a 1 MP spell that cures status ailments, and you receive it from Cibba as a story gift [[checklist-items-13-4]].
- The Oil you need costs 500 GP, so carry enough gold before you leave Wendel. If you are short, kill enemies outside until you reach 500 GP [[gf1-93-4, gf2-34-3]].
- Silver gear costs 700 GP (armor), 505 GP (shield) and 562 GP (sword) from Watts, so keep gold for at least one piece [[gf1-43-3, gf4-4-4]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** War Is Hell (the boy in south-west Wendel) can only be done after Glaive's attack, because the boy is invincible before it [[ra-comments-1-1, ra-comments-54-1]]. Commenters believe you cannot return to Wendel after Dime Tower, so treat it as lost once you enter Dime Tower and do it in this chapter [[ra-comments-54-5]]. Whether Wendel can be revisited after Dime Tower is unconfirmed by any source. It can also be done later, when the story brings you back to Wendel, but before Dime Tower [[ra-comments-54-7, ra-comments-1-1]].

### Wendel

🗺️ **Map:** [View City Of Wendel(N13) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-CityOfWendel(N13).png)

#### Arrival and shopping

From outside Kett's, go east three times, south, west (cut the vines), south, west, north, cross the river with the Chain Flail, then south, east, south twice, east and north twice to reach Wendel [[gf1-42-1]]. The man who helped you in Marsh Cave greets you east of the entrance [[gf4-3-10, gf1-42-4]].

The weapon shop sells Broad Sword 60 GP, Iron Helmet 140 GP, Iron Shield 185 GP and Iron Armor 315 GP (+8 DEF) [[gf4-3-9, checklist-items-4-2]]. The item shop sells Cure x2 for 40 GP, Pure for 30 GP, Eyedrop x3 for 60 GP, Key x4 for 15 GP and Mattock x7 for 60 GP [[gf4-3-8, gf1-42-6]]. Buy Mattocks and Keys here, since the Silver Mine needs Mattocks [[gf5-8-7, gf1-44-1]]. The Inn costs 10 GP [[gf1-92-10]].

#### The temple scene

Go east and north to the temple, then walk north to Cibba [[gf1-42-2, gf4-3-10]]. Cibba has Jena stand on the circle and her mother appears, telling her to guard the Mana Tree with the Gemma Knights. Then a man announces that Glaive is attacking and takes Jena away [[gf4-3-10]].

Exit the temple and walk south to the town entrance. You may avoid or kill monsters on the way [[gf1-42-4]]. At the entrance the man takes off his disguise and reveals himself as Julius, and Glaive now has Jena [[gf4-3-10]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29003]

Julius defeats you, and you wake in the temple, where Cibba tells you Julius took the Girl away in his airship and gives you Heal [[gf1-42-4, gf4-3-11]].

> [!TIP]
> After Heal, use the Inn to recover MP before leaving, as Heal costs 1 MP and your MP may be low [[gf5-8-4, gf3-3-10]].

### Boy in SW Wendel (War Is Hell)

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Do this now. War Is Hell only works after Julius takes the Girl, and only on the boy in the south-west corner of Wendel [[ra-comments-54-1, ra-comments-54-3]].

#### Killing the boy in south-west Wendel

Find the boy one screen west of the entrance. He says "Dwarf Cave is to the west!" [[gf4-3-1, ra-comments-54-8]]. He is the only child in town who counts, and the little girl does not work [[ra-comments-54-4, ra-comments-54-3]].

People in towns can be killed, but they have a lot of HP. Keep hitting him with a weapon at max Will Power [[gf1-104-11]]. One player killed him quickly with the Blood Sword at Power 42 [[ra-comments-54-9]]. One commenter reported him as immortal, which is unconfirmed for the correct timing [[ra-comments-54-10]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29093]

If you miss it, it can still be done when the story returns you to Wendel, before Dime Tower [[ra-comments-54-7]].

### Wendel Area

#### From Wendel to the Oil shop

Leave Wendel going south, then west, south, west. Cut the tree to the west directly above the sand with the Battle Axe, go west twice and enter the cave [[gf1-42-5]]. Enemies here include Pig Warriors (Candy or Gold Bag) and Bees [[gf1-99-17, gf1-99-18]]. Bees are dangerous: kill them with Fire before walking past [[gf5-8-2]]. Crabs here drop Nectar, so get at least one if you can [[gf5-8-5]].

Optional: a Wendel Area warp trick exists in the woods west of Wendel, but nothing needs it [[gf1-104-15]].

### Dwarf Cave

🗺️ **Map:** [View Dwarf Cave(J15) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-DwarfCave(J15).png)

🗺️ **Map:** [View Shop(F12) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-Shop(F12).png)

Talk to the dwarves. One says you need Oil for the trolley, and that Gaia likes Silver, which can be found in the mine to the north [[gf1-43-1, gf4-4-1]].

#### Buying Oil

Leave the cave and go north one screen, cross the stream with the Chain, head south twice, east, and enter the item shop. Buy Oil for 500 GP [[gf1-43-1]]. Also buy Keys and Mattocks here if needed [[gf5-8-7]].

Then go north, cross the stream with the Chain, go north twice, equip the Battle Axe and chop the trees across two screens, then go west into the Silver Mine [[gf1-43-2]].

### Silver Mine

🗺️ **Map:** [View Silver Mine(J12) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-SilverMine(J12).png)

#### Floor 1: the mine cart

Equip the Oil and the Sickle, then walk into the cart [[gf2-35-1, gf1-44-1]]. Switches must be hit with the Sickle [[gf1-111-2]]. Do not hit the first switch. Hit the second and third [[gf1-44-1, gf5-9-1]]. Hold Up + Right and mash Attack on entering the screen for the second switch, then hold Down + Right and mash Attack for the third [[gf2-35-2]]. Attack Spiders as you pass through them to avoid damage [[gf5-9-1]]. If you miss, the cart loops back to the start, so just try again [[gf5-9-2, gf1-44-1]]. The cart then hits a rock pile and you fall into the mineshaft [[gf1-44-1]].

#### B-Floor 2: Watts

At the bottom, Watts joins you [[gf1-44-2, gf4-4-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29004]

ASK Watts to shop for items [[gf2-35-4]]. Go north and break the northern boulder with a Mattock, then go east through the door [[gf1-44-3]]. A path south has rocks to break with a Mattock, leading to a recovery spring [[gf2-35-5, gf1-112-1]]. Back north, break the east wall with a Mattock and go through. In the slippery-floor room, break the pot (the right jar), step on the odd tile or switch and slide east on the ice to the stairs down [[gf1-44-3, gf2-35-6, gf5-9-5]].

Then go west twice, west, north and up the stairs on B-Floor 3, go west and down the stairs on B-Floor 2, and arrive at the boss floor [[gf1-44-3, gf2-35-6]].

### Megapede

Recommended level is 19. Equip the Chain and have a Cure spell ready [[gf1-45-1, gf1-45-2]]. Fire also works on the head [[gf5-9-6]].

- Megapede comes out of one of four holes and moves in a figure 8. Wait at a hole and hit it in the head. Body hits do nothing [[gf1-45-3, gf2-36-2]].
- It follows a set path: it enters from the right, loops, exits top, then enters from the bottom, loops, and exits left [[gf5-9-6]].
- It explodes on defeat, so keep at least 20 HP and move away right after the explosion [[gf1-45-3]].

Reward: 161 GP, 48 EXP, and a chest with Silver [[gf1-45-2, gf4-4-3]]. Opening the chest transports you outside [[gf5-9-7, gf4-4-2]].

### Gaia

🗺️ **Map:** [View Gaia(I12 I14) Map by RyuMaster](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/GB-GBC/FinalFantasyAdventure-Gaia(I12-I14).png)

#### Buying Silver gear

Watts forges Silver gear and tells you to meet him at the Dwarf Cave [[gf4-4-2]]. Go to the Dwarf Cave, go south one room and talk to Watts: Armor 700 GP, Shield 505 GP, Sword 562 GP [[gf4-4-4, gf2-37-2]]. Equip at least one piece; one is enough [[gf2-37-3]]. Without Silver, Gaia refuses to let you in [[gf4-4-5]]. The Silver Sword deals double damage to undead [[checklist-items-7-16]].

Exit the Dwarf Cave, go north twice, west, and north one screen to the cave mouth [[gf2-37-3, gf1-45-2]].

#### Crossing Gaia

Go west and south. Walking south between the two statues closes the door. Step back north slowly until it opens, then go south through it and up the stairs in the southeast corner [[gf1-46-1, gf2-37-4, gf5-10-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:29005]

Outside, Bogard joins you and says the Airship is refueling at the lake to the north [[gf1-46-2, gf4-4-6]]. Ask him to learn MAX attacks [[gf2-18-3]].

#### South of Gaia

Cut a path through trees south, then west. Crabs here die to 2 Silver Sword hits and drop Nectar [[gf1-46-3, gf5-10-4]]. Go north twice to an Inn (10 GP) [[gf1-93-6, gf2-37-3]]. Then chop trees west and enter the item shop: Silver Helmet 240 GP, Cure, Ether 320 GP, Keys, Mattocks [[gf1-94-1, gf1-46-3]]. Clouds drop Ether [[gf1-99-27]]. The Chain gap needs a full Will meter [[gf5-10-6]]. The route to the Airship continues in the next chapter [[gf1-46-4]].

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave, confirm you have equipped Silver gear, bought Keys and Mattocks, and killed the Wendel boy. XP and cash can be boosted very quickly around this point [[ra-comments-1-4, ra-comments-54-5]].
