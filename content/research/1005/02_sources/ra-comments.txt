[1ofAkind] You can get him to stage 5 by feeding him a secret fruit at stage 4.

Notice the monsters want a specific item in the feeding menu, giving them that item fills 3 bars instead of 1, this can save a lot of money and help you evolve them sooner.

All the monsters share the same pool of wanted items per stage, most of the stage 1 items can be bought in Sundletan shop, most of the stage 2 items can be bought in the Tanbel and Clamento shops (apple cider/taplows are bought at the bar in Sundletan), the stage 3 items are more spread out between the shops from Bound to Treadool (Plated Cloth is in Gruberik shop).

if you get an item you have no access to or is a grind (like the rocks in stage 3) you can reset the request either by having a battle in the overworld or feeding them something else from the pool (preferably the cheapest thing).

if you plan to do the ancient cave subset it can be more efficient to just feed them all the blue chest multiples you get.

[ShianKiri] I watched a speedrun guide on how to do this. Also found a lot of documentation on Ancient Cave. Some resources:
https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1sz45yBONOMBdfs1yYovWCa438y_IlKJQfOcwv5_GAn0/edit?usp=sharing
https://www.speedrun.com/lufia2/guides/68bvm

General notes:
I recommend using Guy, Dekar and Artea. 

By the time you get to Floor 43, you want to be at least level 22. You can easily achieve this in the early runs. 

Deadly Sword, Deadly Rod and Fatal Pick are great if you can get them early so you can blast through mobs. However, you will be spending a lot of time trying to find Curselifters so you can set up for Master Jelly. 

Dekar Blade starts showing up Floor 15+ (1/2 Damage)
Uncursed Deadly Rod does 1/8 Damage.
Old Sword does 1/6 Damage. 

You also want to open Blue Chests to find some starter gear so if you're struggling, just spam Floor 1-30 until you get a decent set of starter gear. 

If you can get Gades Sword and Dekar Blade, that's basically 6000-8000 Damage.

Floor 71-80 is when you start trying to finalize your equipment. 
Floor 81-98 get to the stairs as fast as you can.

Learn about some really devastating monsters and run from them at all costs. 

Make sure Artea is the one with a Deadly Rod since his AGI is so high, he goes first and can kill something right away. 

Don't spend too much time fighting once you're level 30. If you can wreck mobs, do so, otherwise avoid fights.
