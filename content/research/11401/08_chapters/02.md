## Chapter 2: The Original Eight: Base Roster Campaigns & Sub-Boss Unlocks

[MERMAID]

In this chapter, we take each of the eight original starting fighters through Arcade Mode on Medium difficulty without changing characters [[gf1-18-3, ra-comments-56-1]]. Each base combatant faces a designated sub-boss rival in Stage 8 and the Mishima Zaibatsu patriarch Heihachi Mishima in Stage 9 [[gf1-18-4, gf1-18-5, gf4-11-1]]. Clearing Arcade mode with each fighter unlocks their defeated rival as a playable character, and executing each fighter's signature 6-hit combo earns their respective Iron Fist achievement [[gf1-18-3, ra-comments-22-1]].

### Kazuya Mishima Arcade Run

Kazuya Mishima is the legitimate heir to the Mishima financial empire and a deadly karate combatant possessing exceptional mid-range launchers and punishing strikes [[gf1-6-1, gf4-12-1]]. His signature moves include the Dragon Punch (`F, D, DF + LP`), the Lunging Uppercut (`F, D, DF + RP`), and the Twin Pistons (`DF + LP, RP`) [[gf1-6-2, gf3-3-1, gf4-5-1]].

> **Kazuya Mishima Campaign Profile:**
> - Stage 8 Rival: Lee Chaolan (Defeat to unlock Lee Chaolan) [[gf1-18-3, gf1-18-5]].
> - Stage 9 Final Boss: Heihachi Mishima [[gf1-18-4, gf3-1-1]].
> - Signature 6-Hit String: `F, F(Hold)+RP, LP, RP, RP, LK, RK` [[gf1-6-1, ra-comments-22-1]].

Navigate through Stages 1 through 7 using Kazuya's powerful Lunging Uppercut to launch advancing opponents into the air, following up with a grounded Axe Kick for massive damage [[gf1-6-2, gf3-6-1]]. In Stage 8, you face Kazuya's adoptive brother and rival, Lee Chaolan [[gf1-18-5, gf1-26-1]]. Lee favors quick sliding kicks; block low by holding `Down-Back` and punish his recovery with Twin Pistons [[gf1-6-3, gf3-4-1]]. In Stage 9, confront Heihachi Mishima at the Mishima estate [[gf1-18-4, gf3-9-1]]. Bait Heihachi's Wind God Fist, duck beneath his high punches, and counter with heavy throws (`LP + LK`) [[gf1-5-1, gf3-8-1]]. Defeating Heihachi without changing characters completes the campaign, unlocking Lee Chaolan [[gf1-18-3, ra-comments-56-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90395]

To earn Kazuya's combo achievement, perform the first 6 hits of his 10-attack string: input `Forward, Forward(Hold) + Right Punch, Left Punch, Right Punch, Right Punch, Left Kick, Right Kick` (`F, F(Hold) + RP, LP, RP, RP, LK, RK`) [[gf1-6-1, ra-comments-22-1]]. Once the 6th hit registers on the combo counter, the achievement pops [[ra-comments-22-1]]. This can be completed during your Arcade run or against an idle Player 2 dummy in Versus Mode [[ra-comments-38-2]].

### Paul Phoenix Arcade Run

Paul Phoenix is an American martial artist who travels the globe seeking a worthy adversary [[gf1-7-1, gf1-22-1]]. Paul's arsenal revolves around pure physical devastation, headlined by his legendary Power Punch (`Down, Down-Forward, Forward + RP` / `D,DF,F + RP` / PonKen) which inflicts 50 damage on clean hit, and the Hammer Fist (`Down + LP`) [[gf1-7-2, gf2-3-2]].

> **Paul Phoenix Campaign Profile:**
> - Stage 8 Rival: Kuma (Defeat to unlock Kuma) [[gf1-18-3, gf1-18-5, gf4-11-1]].
> - Stage 9 Final Boss: Heihachi Mishima [[gf1-18-4, gf3-1-1]].
> - Signature 6-Hit String: `LP, RP, LK, RP, LP, RP` [[gf1-7-1, gf2-3-1, ra-comments-24-1]].

Paul's campaign is straightforward due to the absurd stopping power of PonKen [[gf2-3-2]]. Backdash away from enemy attacks, wait for the AI to whiff, and unleash `D,DF,F + RP` to shred their life bar [[gf1-7-2, gf2-3-2, gf4-3-1]]. In Stage 8, Paul encounters Kuma, Heihachi's loyal guard bear [[gf1-18-5, gf4-11-1]]. Kuma's massive hitbox makes him especially vulnerable to Paul's Power Punch; time your strikes outside Kuma's bear hug range to down the beast quickly [[gf1-7-2, gf4-11-1]]. Proceed to Stage 9 and defeat Heihachi with well-timed counter punches to finish the run and unlock Kuma [[gf1-18-3, ra-comments-59-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90408]

For Paul's combo achievement, input the opening 6 hits of his universal 10-attack sequence: `Left Punch, Right Punch, Left Kick, Right Punch, Left Punch, Right Punch` (`LP, RP, LK, RP, LP, RP`) [[gf1-7-1, gf2-3-1, ra-comments-24-1]]. The sixth punch registers cleanly to unlock the award [[ra-comments-24-1]].

### Marshall Law Arcade Run

Marshall Law is an expert martial artist and chef inspired by classic Jeet Kune Do [[gf1-8-1, gf1-23-1]]. His offensive style features rapid multi-hit kick strings and backflips, including the Somersault Kick (`Up-Forward + RK`), the Dragon Slash, and low spinning sweeps [[gf1-8-2, gf2-4-1]].

> **Marshall Law Campaign Profile:**
> - Stage 8 Rival: Wang Jinrei (Defeat to unlock Wang Jinrei) [[gf1-18-3, gf1-18-5, gf4-11-3]].
> - Stage 9 Final Boss: Heihachi Mishima [[gf1-18-4, gf3-1-1]].
> - Signature 6-Hit String: `DF(tap) + LP, RP, RP, LP, LK, LK` [[gf1-8-1, gf2-4-1, ra-comments-26-1]].

Use Law's swift standing jabs and Somersault Kick to counter aggressive opponents throughout the tournament ladder [[gf1-8-2]]. In Stage 8, Law faces his rival, the martial arts master Wang Jinrei [[gf1-18-5, gf4-11-3]]. Wang utilizes tricky mid-range pokes and parries; bypass his guard by utilizing low sliding kicks and overhead axe kicks [[gf1-8-2, gf3-6-1]]. Overcome Heihachi in Stage 9 without swapping characters to clear the campaign and unlock Wang Jinrei [[gf1-18-3, ra-comments-60-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90409]

To earn Law's combo achievement, execute the first 6 strikes of his 10-attack combination: tap `Down-Forward + Left Punch, Right Punch, Right Punch, Left Punch, Left Kick, Left Kick` (`DF(tap) + LP, RP, RP, LP, LK, LK`) [[gf1-8-1, gf2-4-1, ra-comments-26-1]]. The rapid string connects for 6 consecutive hits [[ra-comments-26-1]].

### Nina Williams Arcade Run

Nina Williams is an Irish assassin trained in deadly Bone Martial Arts and Aikido joint locks [[gf1-9-1, gf4-13-1]]. Nina's toolset emphasizes brutal combo strings, the Hunting Kick combo, and devastating multi-part throws such as the Arm Break and Crab Hold [[gf1-9-2, gf2-2-1]].

> **Nina Williams Campaign Profile:**
> - Stage 8 Rival: Anna Williams (Defeat to unlock Anna Williams) [[gf1-18-3, gf1-18-5, gf4-11-4]].
> - Stage 9 Final Boss: Heihachi Mishima [[gf1-18-4, gf3-1-1]].
> - Signature 6-Hit String: `LP, RP, LP, RP, LK, LK` [[gf1-10-1, ra-comments-34-1]].

Control space with Nina's forward-advancing kicks and punish missed attacks with her swift three-punch combos [[gf1-9-2]]. In Stage 8, Nina confronts her bitter sister and rival assassin, Anna Williams [[gf1-18-5, gf4-11-4]]. Anna shares much of Nina's offensive cadence; anticipate Anna's high punches by ducking and countering with Nina's low sweeps or Bone Cutter (`F,F,F + RK`) [[gf1-9-2, gf2-2-1]]. Finish Stage 9 against Heihachi to unlock Anna Williams as a playable fighter [[gf1-18-3, ra-comments-58-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90407]

For Nina's combo achievement, execute the first 6 strikes of her 10-hit sequence: input `Left Punch, Right Punch, Left Punch, Right Punch, Left Kick, Left Kick` (`LP, RP, LP, RP, LK, LK`) [[gf1-10-1, ra-comments-34-1]]. The final kick cements the 6-hit threshold [[ra-comments-34-1]].

### Jack Arcade Run

Jack is a Russian military combat android engineered purely for overwhelming physical destruction [[gf1-10-1, gf1-28-1]]. Jack's enormous reach and heavyweight moves include the Overhead Hammer (`Down-Forward + LP + RP`), Machine Gun Punches, and the high-damage Iron Fist [[gf1-10-2, gf4-4-1]].

> **Jack Campaign Profile:**
> - Stage 8 Rival: Prototype Jack (Defeat to unlock Prototype Jack) [[gf1-18-3, gf1-18-5, gf1-28-1]].
> - Stage 9 Final Boss: Heihachi Mishima [[gf1-18-4, gf3-1-1]].
> - Signature 6-Hit String: `D + RP, LP, RP, LP, D + LP, D + RP` [[gf1-11-1, ra-comments-28-1]].

Capitalize on Jack's exceptional attack range by keeping opponents at bay with crouching jabs and the Overhead Hammer (`DF + LP + RP`) [[gf1-10-2, gf4-4-1]]. In Stage 8, Jack squares off against his robotic predecessor, Prototype Jack [[gf1-18-5, gf1-28-1]]. Both androids possess massive damage output, so maintain a defensive high guard against P.Jack's wind-up punches and punish his slow recovery frames [[gf1-10-2, gf3-4-1]]. Defeat Heihachi in Stage 9 to unlock Prototype Jack [[gf1-18-3, ra-comments-61-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90410]

To unlock Jack's combo award, perform the opening 6 strikes of his 10-attack string: input `Down + Right Punch, Left Punch, Right Punch, Left Punch, Down + Left Punch, Down + Right Punch` (`D + RP, LP, RP, LP, D + LP, D + RP`) [[gf1-11-1, ra-comments-28-1]]. The heavy blows connect sequentially to trigger the achievement [[ra-comments-28-1]].

### King Arcade Run

King is a Mexican luchador who wears a jaguar mask, dedicated to winning the tournament's prize money to fund his orphanage [[gf1-12-1, gf1-29-1]]. King is the premier grappler of the roster, wielding devastating throws like the Coconut Crush (`1+3`), Brain Buster (`2+4`), and Frankensteiner, alongside the lethal Hop Kick [[gf1-12-2, gf2-5-1]].

> **King Campaign Profile:**
> - Stage 8 Rival: Armor King (Defeat to unlock Armor King) [[gf1-18-3, gf1-18-5, gf1-29-1]].
> - Stage 9 Final Boss: Heihachi Mishima [[gf1-18-4, gf3-1-1]].
> - Signature 6-Hit String: `D + RP, LP, LP, LP, RP, LK` [[gf1-13-1, gf2-5-1, ra-comments-30-1]].

Use King's crouching jabs and hop kicks to control incoming rushdowns, transitioning smoothly into high-damage command throws whenever the AI hesitates [[gf1-12-2, gf2-5-1]]. In Stage 8, King faces his former mentor and bitter rival, Armor King [[gf1-18-5, gf1-29-1]]. Armor King possesses similar wrestling throws but adds a lunging uppercut; keep Armor King grounded with King's low sweeps and diving elbow drops [[gf1-12-2, gf4-9-1]]. Conquer Stage 9 against Heihachi to unlock Armor King [[gf1-18-3, ra-comments-62-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90411]

To earn King's combo achievement, execute the first 6 strikes of his 10-attack string: input `Down + Right Punch, Left Punch, Left Punch, Left Punch, Right Punch, Left Kick` (`D + RP, LP, LP, LP, RP, LK`) [[gf1-13-1, gf2-5-1, ra-comments-30-1]]. Connecting all six strikes awards the achievement [[ra-comments-30-1]].

### Yoshimitsu Arcade Run

Yoshimitsu is the cybernetic ninja leader of the honorable Manji Clan, wielding a mystical sword and eccentric ninjutsu evasions [[gf1-8-1, gf1-24-1]]. His distinctive abilities include sword slashes, the multi-spin evasive maneuver (`hold Back + LP`), spinning sweeps, and helicopter pounces [[gf1-8-2, gf4-7-1]].

> **Yoshimitsu Campaign Profile:**
> - Stage 8 Rival: Ganryu (Defeat to unlock Ganryu) [[gf1-18-3, gf1-18-5, gf2-8-1]].
> - Stage 9 Final Boss: Heihachi Mishima [[gf1-18-4, gf3-1-1]].
> - Signature 6-Hit String: `LP, RP, LP, RK, RK, RK` [[gf1-9-1, gf2-4-2, ra-comments-32-1]].

Exploit Yoshimitsu's sword range and spinning sweeps to trip advancing enemies [[gf1-8-2, gf4-7-1]]. In Stage 8, Yoshimitsu confronts the corrupt sumo rikishi Ganryu [[gf1-18-5, gf2-8-1]]. Ganryu is slow and heavily vulnerable to low attacks; utilize Yoshimitsu's spinning leg sweeps (`tap DB + LK`) to repeatedly knock Ganryu flat on his back [[gf1-8-2, gf4-7-1]]. Proceed to Stage 9 and defeat Heihachi to unlock Ganryu [[gf1-18-3, ra-comments-57-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90396]

For Yoshimitsu's combo achievement, input the opening 6 hits of his 10-attack sequence: `Left Punch, Right Punch, Left Punch, Right Kick, Right Kick, Right Kick` (`LP, RP, LP, RK, RK, RK`) [[gf1-9-1, gf2-4-2, ra-comments-32-1]]. Once the sixth strike connects cleanly, the achievement unlocks [[ra-comments-32-1]].

### Michelle Chang Arcade Run

Michelle Chang is a brave young woman fighting to reclaim her ancestral homeland and avenge her father's demise at the hands of Heihachi [[gf1-11-1, gf1-25-1]]. Michelle's fighting style blends rapid Chinese kenpo strikes, high-impact pounces (`UF + LK + RP`), and swift punch flurries [[gf1-11-2, gf4-6-1, gf4-9-1]].

> **Michelle Chang Campaign Profile:**
> - Stage 8 Rival: Kunimitsu (Defeat to unlock Kunimitsu) [[gf1-18-3, gf1-18-5, gf2-8-2]].
> - Stage 9 Final Boss: Heihachi Mishima [[gf1-18-4, gf3-1-1]].
> - Signature 6-Hit String: `RP, LP, LP, RP, LK, LK` [[gf1-11-2, gf4-11-1, ra-comments-36-1]].

Michelle is one of the most effective fighters in Tekken due to her lightning-quick 5-punch flurry (`RP, LP, LP, LP, RP`) and powerful hopback stomp [[gf4-6-1, gf4-9-1]]. In Stage 8, Michelle encounters the masked rogue ninja Kunimitsu [[gf1-18-5, gf2-8-2]]. Kunimitsu attacks with rapid dash strikes; block high and retaliate with Michelle's 5-punch sequence [[gf3-4-1, gf4-6-1]]. After defeating Kunimitsu, topple Heihachi in Stage 9 to complete your base roster campaigns and unlock Kunimitsu [[gf1-18-3, ra-comments-63-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90412]

To complete Michelle's combo trial, execute the first 6 strikes of her 10-attack string: input `Right Punch, Left Punch, Left Punch, Right Punch, Left Kick, Left Kick` (`RP, LP, LP, RP, LK, LK`) [[gf1-11-2, gf4-11-1, ra-comments-36-1]]. Connecting all six hits triggers the achievement [[ra-comments-36-1]].
