### Heihachi Mishima Final Boss Analysis & Arsenal Breakdown

Throughout your previous 16 arcade runs, Heihachi Mishima stood as the final boss in Stage 9 [[gf1-18-4, gf3-1-1]]. Now that he is selectable, his overwhelming power is completely at your disposal [[gf3-9-1]]. Heihachi shares many fundamental mechanics with his son Kazuya, but possesses enhanced attack properties, superior priority on his strikes, and devastating special commands [[gf3-7-1, gf3-9-1]].

> **Heihachi Mishima Core Arsenal:**
> - Wind God Fist (FuuJinken): `F, D, DF + RP` (Inflicts 30 damage, massive launcher) [[gf2-7-1, gf3-9-1]].
> - Dragon Punch (Thunder God Fist): `F, D, DF + LP` [[gf1-6-2, gf3-9-1]].
> - Twin Pistons: `DF + LP, RP` (Fastest mid-level counter-strike) [[gf3-3-1, gf3-9-1]].
> - Stonehead Throw: `F, F + LP + RP` [[gf1-5-1, gf3-8-1]].
> - Signature 6-Hit Sequence: `F, F(Hold)+RP, LP, RP, RP, LK, RK` [[gf1-18-1, ra-comments-38-1]].

Heihachi's primary neutral weapon is the Wind God Fist (`F, D, DF + RP`), which knocks opponents high into the sky and allows for devastating juggle follow-ups [[gf2-7-1, gf3-9-1]]. His Twin Pistons (`DF + LP, RP`) serve as an unbeatable close-range interruption against aggressive AI strings, while his Stonehead throw (`F, F + LP + RP`) delivers heavy damage when opponents attempt to turtle in a standing guard [[gf1-5-1, gf3-3-1, gf3-8-1]].

### Heihachi Mishima Arcade Run

Start an Arcade Mode campaign and select Heihachi Mishima, confirming that the game is set to Medium difficulty or higher [[gf1-20-1, ra-comments-65-1]]. Because you are playing as the tournament host, your arcade ladder features a unique progression structure where you fight through eight challenging contenders before facing Kazuya Mishima as your climatic final confrontation [[gf1-18-5, gf3-9-1]].

Dominating this campaign is remarkably straightforward: rely heavily on Heihachi's Wind God Fist (`F, D, DF + RP`) to launch approaching adversaries, followed by a running shoulder tackle or a diving grounded hip-press down strike (`U,2`) [[gf2-7-1, gf3-5-1, gf3-9-1]]. Against Kazuya in the final match, watch for Kazuya's Rising Uppercut; maintain high guard, block the strike, and punish with Heihachi's Twin Pistons or Stonehead throw [[gf1-6-2, gf3-4-1, gf3-8-1]]. Defeating Kazuya without changing characters completes the 17th and final character story campaign [[ra-comments-65-1]].

To complete Heihachi's Iron Fist combo trial, execute the first 6 strikes of his 10-attack combination [[gf1-18-1, ra-comments-38-1]]. Input `Forward, Forward(Hold) + Right Punch, Left Punch, Right Punch, Right Punch, Left Kick, Right Kick` (`F, F(Hold) + RP, LP, RP, RP, LK, RK`) [[gf1-18-1, ra-comments-38-1]]. Advanced players can also practice Heihachi's lethal 100% combo string in Versus Mode: `F,F+RP---LP---LP---LP---D,DF,F+RP` [[gf5-1-1, ra-comments-38-2]]. Once the 6th consecutive hit registers on the combo counter, the achievement unlocks [[ra-comments-38-1]].
