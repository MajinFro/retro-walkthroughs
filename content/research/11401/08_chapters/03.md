## Chapter 3: The Sub-Boss Uprising & Full Roster Completion

[MERMAID]

With the eight original tournament contenders victorious, their respective mid-boss rivals are now fully unlocked and selectable on the character screen [[gf1-18-3]]. In this chapter, we conquer Arcade Mode on Medium difficulty with all eight unlocked sub-bosses, master their unique 6-hit combo requirements, and unlock the final boss Heihachi Mishima by completing Arcade Mode without using continues [[gf1-18-4, gf3-1-1]]. Doing so completes the entire 17-character roster, securing the major milestone achievement All Fighters Ready for Combat! [[gf1-18-3, ra-comments-15-1]].

### Wang Jinrei Arcade Run

Wang Jinrei is a venerable Chinese martial artist and former companion to Kazuya's grandfather [[gf1-13-1, gf1-27-1]]. Wang shares several baseline techniques with Michelle Chang, but brings devastating unique strikes such as the Standing Dragon Punch (`F, D, DF + RP`), the Diving Elbow Drop, and swift homing silver punch down strikes (`U,2`) [[gf1-13-1, gf2-10-1]].

> **Wang Jinrei Campaign Profile:**
> - Stage 8 Rival: Marshall Law [[gf1-18-5, gf4-11-3]].
> - Stage 9 Final Boss: Heihachi Mishima [[gf1-18-4, gf3-1-1]].
> - Signature 6-Hit String: `DF(tap) + RP, LP, RP, LP, LK, LK` [[gf1-15-1, ra-comments-55-1]].

Advance through the tournament stages by utilizing Wang's quick low pokes and punish whiffed attacks with his powerful Standing Dragon Punch [[gf1-13-1, gf4-5-1]]. In Stage 8, Wang battles his designated rival Marshall Law [[gf1-18-5, gf4-11-3]]. Guard Law's somersault kick strings high, crouch under his flying kicks, and retaliate with heavy throws (`LP + LK`) [[gf1-5-1, gf1-8-2, gf3-4-1]]. Defeat Heihachi in Stage 9 without changing characters to complete Wang's campaign [[ra-comments-64-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90413]

For Wang's combo trial, execute the first 6 strikes of his 10-attack sequence: tap `Down-Forward + Right Punch, Left Punch, Right Punch, Left Punch, Left Kick, Left Kick` (`DF(tap) + RP, LP, RP, LP, LK, LK`) [[gf1-15-1, ra-comments-55-1]]. The rapid flurry registers 6 consecutive hits to unlock the award [[ra-comments-55-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90483]

### Lee Chaolan Arcade Run

Lee Chaolan, known as "The Silver-haired Demon," is Heihachi's adopted son and Kazuya's lifelong rival [[gf1-12-1, gf1-26-1]]. Lee combines martial arts techniques adapted from Marshall Law with lightning-fast kicks, notably the Lazer's Edge Kick Combo (`Down + RK, RK, RK, RK` / `D,4,4,4,4`), rapid sliding kicks, and running tackles [[gf1-12-1, gf2-9-1]].

> **Lee Chaolan Campaign Profile:**
> - Stage 8 Rival: Kazuya Mishima [[gf1-18-5]].
> - Stage 9 Final Boss: Heihachi Mishima [[gf1-18-4, gf3-1-1]].
> - Signature 6-Hit String: `D,4,4,4,4` or 10-String opening [[gf2-9-1, ra-comments-43-1]].

Lee's Lazer's Edge Kick combo (`D,4,4,4,4`) is one of the most effective AI-punishing moves in the entire game [[gf2-9-1, ra-comments-80-1]]. The AI frequently fails to crouch-block the rapid sequence of low kicks, allowing you to breeze through early opponents [[ra-comments-80-1]]. In Stage 8, face Kazuya Mishima [[gf1-18-5]]. Anticipate Kazuya's Rising Uppercut, block high, and counter with Lee's slide kick or rapid jab strings [[gf1-6-2, gf3-4-1]]. Overcome Heihachi in Stage 9 to finish Lee's run [[ra-comments-66-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90415]

To complete Lee's combo achievement, input his 10-attack string opening or chain his Lazer's Edge Kicks into multi-hit strings (`D,4,4,4,4`) [[gf2-9-1, ra-comments-43-1]]. Connecting 6 consecutive hits triggers the achievement [[ra-comments-43-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90477]

### Prototype Jack Arcade Run

Prototype Jack (P.Jack) is Russia's early experimental combat robot, designed without exterior skin purely to maximize brute mechanical power [[gf1-15-1, gf1-28-1]]. P.Jack shares Jack's destructive reach but boasts devastating unique juggles like `DF+RP---DF+LP---D+LP---F+RP` and spinning wind-up punches [[gf1-15-1, gf5-2-2]].

> **Prototype Jack Campaign Profile:**
> - Stage 8 Rival: Jack [[gf1-18-5]].
> - Stage 9 Final Boss: Heihachi Mishima [[gf1-18-4, gf3-1-1]].
> - Signature 6-Hit String: `D + RP, LP, RP, LP, D + LP, D + RP` [[gf1-17-1, ra-comments-45-1]].

Maintain distance against incoming enemies by utilizing P.Jack's crouching jabs and Overhead Hammer (`DF + LP + RP`) [[gf1-15-1, gf4-4-1]]. In Stage 8, P.Jack faces the mass-production model Jack [[gf1-18-5]]. Wait for Jack's slow hammer punches to miss, then execute P.Jack's heavy juggle string `DF+RP---DF+LP---D+LP---F+RP` [[gf5-2-2]]. Defeat Heihachi in Stage 9 to complete the robotic campaign [[ra-comments-67-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90416]

For P.Jack's combo trial, execute the first 6 strikes of his 10-attack combination: input `Down + Right Punch, Left Punch, Right Punch, Left Punch, Down + Left Punch, Down + Right Punch` (`D + RP, LP, RP, LP, D + LP, D + RP`) [[gf1-17-1, ra-comments-45-1]]. The six consecutive heavy blows trigger the achievement [[ra-comments-45-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90478]

### Armor King Arcade Run

Armor King is a former Mexican pro wrestling champion and masked rival to King, sporting an eye patch and a menacing black jaguar mask [[gf1-16-1, gf1-29-1]]. Armor King mirrors King's devastating wrestling arsenal but incorporates a deadly Lunging Uppercut and bone-crushing suplex throws [[gf1-16-1, gf1-29-1]].

> **Armor King Campaign Profile:**
> - Stage 8 Rival: King [[gf1-18-5, gf1-29-1]].
> - Stage 9 Final Boss: Heihachi Mishima [[gf1-18-4, gf3-1-1]].
> - Signature 6-Hit String: `D + RP, LP, LP, LP, RP, LK` [[gf1-17-2, ra-comments-47-1]].

Utilize Armor King's lunging uppercuts to launch incoming fighters airborne, following up with hop kicks or driving elbow drops as they hit the mat [[gf1-16-1, gf4-9-1]]. In Stage 8, Armor King encounters his former pupil King [[gf1-18-5, gf1-29-1]]. Avoid King's grab range by ducking under grab attempts, countering with crouching jabs and high-damage Brain Busters (`2+4`) [[gf2-5-1, gf3-8-1]]. Defeat Heihachi in Stage 9 to complete Armor King's arcade trial [[ra-comments-68-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90417]

For Armor King's combo trial, execute the first 6 strikes of his 10-attack string: input `Down + Right Punch, Left Punch, Left Punch, Left Punch, Right Punch, Left Kick` (`D + RP, LP, LP, LP, RP, LK`) [[gf1-17-2, ra-comments-47-1]]. All six strikes connect to unlock the achievement [[ra-comments-47-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90479]

### Ganryu Arcade Run

Ganryu is an excommunicated sumo rikishi possessing enormous physical bulk and devastating palm thrusts [[gf1-17-1, gf2-8-1]]. Ganryu's offensive centerpiece is the Hundred Hand Slap (Enmatsuppari: `Forward + 1,2,1,2,1,2,1,2`), a rapid-fire barrage of high palm strikes that deals tremendous damage [[gf2-8-1]].

> **Ganryu Campaign Profile:**
> - Stage 8 Rival: Yoshimitsu [[gf1-18-5]].
> - Stage 9 Final Boss: Heihachi Mishima [[gf1-18-4, gf3-1-1]].
> - Signature 6-Hit String: `F + LP, RP, LP, RP, LP, RP` (Enmatsuppari) [[gf2-8-1, ra-comments-51-1]].

Ganryu controls close-quarters encounters with ease by spamming his Enmatsuppari palm strikes (`F,1,2,1,2,1,2,1,2`) [[gf2-8-1]]. In Stage 8, Ganryu battles his ninja rival Yoshimitsu [[gf1-18-5]]. Guard Yoshimitsu's spinning slashes high, watch for his low sweep attempts, and retaliate with Ganryu's heavy sumo slaps [[gf2-8-1, gf3-4-1]]. Pummel Heihachi in Stage 9 to finish the campaign [[ra-comments-69-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90418]

Ganryu's combo trial is one of the most satisfying in the game [[ra-comments-51-1]]. Execute his Enmatsuppari by inputting `Forward + Left Punch, Right Punch, Left Punch, Right Punch, Left Punch, Right Punch` (`F + LP, RP, LP, RP, LP, RP`) [[gf2-8-1, ra-comments-51-1]]. The flurry of rapid palm strikes connects for 6 consecutive hits, granting the achievement [[gf2-8-1, ra-comments-51-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90481]

### Anna Williams Arcade Run

Anna Williams is Nina's glamorous and lethal younger sister, sharing Nina's bone-breaking assassinations and multi-part joint throws [[gf1-14-1, gf2-8-3]]. Anna's toolkit includes the Kakaekomi Hijuuchi throw (`Down-Forward, Down-Forward + LP` / `df,df,1`), Hunting Kicks, and swift sliding kick knockdowns [[gf1-14-1, gf2-8-3]].

> **Anna Williams Campaign Profile:**
> - Stage 8 Rival: Nina Williams [[gf1-18-5, gf4-11-4]].
> - Stage 9 Final Boss: Heihachi Mishima [[gf1-18-4, gf3-1-1]].
> - Signature 6-Hit String: `LP, RP, LP, RP, LK, LK` [[gf1-14-1, ra-comments-49-1]].

Utilize Anna's forward dashing jabs and Kakaekomi Hijuuchi throw (`df,df,1`) to keep pressure on the computer AI [[gf1-14-1, gf2-8-3]]. In Stage 8, Anna battles her rival sister Nina Williams [[gf1-18-5, gf4-11-4]]. Duck beneath Nina's high punch combos and punish with Anna's rapid kick combos [[gf1-14-1, gf3-4-1]]. Conclude Stage 9 against Heihachi to complete Anna's arcade run [[ra-comments-70-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90419]

To earn Anna's combo achievement, input the first 6 strikes of her 10-attack combination: `Left Punch, Right Punch, Left Punch, Right Punch, Left Kick, Left Kick` (`LP, RP, LP, RP, LK, LK`) [[gf1-14-1, ra-comments-49-1]]. Connecting all six hits unlocks the achievement [[ra-comments-49-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90480]

### Kuma Arcade Run

Kuma is Heihachi Mishima's massive pet brown bear, trained as a fearsome bodyguard capable of ferocious mauling attacks [[gf1-16-1, gf4-11-1]]. Kuma's attacks feature enormous reach, lethal bear hugs (`1+3`), overhead double-paw smashes, and heavy homing down strikes [[gf1-16-1, gf4-11-1]].

> **Kuma Campaign Profile:**
> - Stage 8 Rival: Paul Phoenix [[gf1-18-5, gf4-11-1]].
> - Stage 9 Final Boss: Heihachi Mishima [[gf1-18-4, gf3-1-1]].
> - Signature 6-Hit String: `D + RP, LP, LP, LP, RP, LK` [[gf1-16-1, ra-comments-53-1]].

Keep your distance and let aggressive AI opponents walk into Kuma's massive standing right paw swipe [[gf1-16-1]]. In Stage 8, Kuma encounters Paul Phoenix [[gf1-18-5, gf4-11-1]]. Beware of Paul's Power Punch; block high and punish immediately with Kuma's bone-crushing bear hug [[gf2-3-2, gf3-4-1, gf4-11-1]]. Defeat master Heihachi in Stage 9 to finish Kuma's run [[ra-comments-71-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90420]

For Kuma's combo trial, execute the first 6 strikes of his 10-attack string: input `Down + Right Punch, Left Punch, Left Punch, Left Punch, Right Punch, Left Kick` (`D + RP, LP, LP, LP, RP, LK`) [[gf1-16-1, ra-comments-53-1]]. Connecting all six strikes unlocks the achievement [[ra-comments-53-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90482]

### Kunimitsu Arcade Run

Kunimitsu is a rogue female ninja and former member of the Manji Clan who stole ancient ninja techniques from Yoshimitsu [[gf1-17-1, gf2-8-2]]. Kunimitsu possesses blistering agility, spinning slashes, and her signature 6-hit ninjutsu combination: Ninpoh ManjiKazura (`Back + 1,1,1,1,1,1` / `B,1,1,1,1,1,1`) [[gf2-8-2]].

> **Kunimitsu Campaign Profile:**
> - Stage 8 Rival: Michelle Chang [[gf1-18-5]].
> - Stage 9 Final Boss: Heihachi Mishima [[gf1-18-4, gf3-1-1]].
> - Signature 6-Hit String: `B, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1` (Ninpoh ManjiKazura) [[gf2-8-2, ra-comments-41-1]].

Use Kunimitsu's fast dash sliding kicks to slip beneath enemy defenses, followed by rapid jumping slash kicks [[gf1-17-1, gf2-8-2]]. In Stage 8, Kunimitsu faces Michelle Chang [[gf1-18-5]]. Michelle's 5-punch string is dangerous at close range; keep Michelle pinned with Kunimitsu's Ninpoh ManjiKazura combination [[gf2-8-2, gf4-6-1]]. Overcome Heihachi in Stage 9 to conclude Kunimitsu's arcade campaign [[ra-comments-72-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:90421]

Kunimitsu's combo achievement is exceptionally easy to execute [[ra-comments-41-1]]. Input her Ninpoh ManjiKazura: hold `Back` and tap `Left Punch` six consecutive times (`Back + LP, LP, LP, LP, LP, LP` / `B,1,1,1,1,1,1`) [[gf2-8-2, ra-comments-41-1]]. All six slashes strike the opponent rapidly in a single continuous combo, granting the achievement [[gf2-8-2, ra-comments-41-1]].

### All Fighters Ready Milestone

Unlocking all eight sub-bosses through their respective Arcade runs, combined with unlocking Devil Kazuya via the Galaga challenge and unlocking final boss Heihachi Mishima by beating Arcade mode without continues, completes the entire 17-character roster [[gf1-18-2, gf1-18-3, gf1-18-4, gf3-1-1]].

Return to the character select screen to verify that all fighter portraits are active: the top row contains the eight original fighters, the middle row houses all eight sub-bosses, and the center reveals Heihachi Mishima and Devil Kazuya [[gf1-18-3, gf1-18-4, gf3-1-3]]. With every warrior assembled, the grand roster milestone achievement unlocks [[ra-comments-15-1]].
