[MrRubens] Mastered!

This was my first time playing the original Tekken, as I played Tekken 3, 4, and 5 when I was a kid.

Regarding the achievements, many are easy to get, but I recommend getting the Galaga minigame first, even though it's not related to Tekken, because it's probably the hardest, or at least the most tedious. I spent two hours memorizing each stage, trying hundreds of times, but I finally got it. The first stage is the most difficult, and you can unlock the second ship by finishing the first stage in 18.5 seconds or less. The other stages with two ships are much easier.

Getting the 10-hit combo achievement was quite easy with Wang, but it's also easy with King, Law, or Michelle. The 6-hit combos with each character are also very easy. Simply pressing the same RP or LP button (square or triangle) six times in a row will get you the achievement but with Jack, P-Jack, and Puma, I was forced to use a combo because their attacks are very slow.  Another tip for quickly unlocking the arcade mode achievements for each character is to reduce the number of rounds to 1, and even change the difficulty to hard since the opponent won't block as many attacks.

Next step, Tekken 2!
