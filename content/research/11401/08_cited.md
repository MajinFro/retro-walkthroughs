## Game Overview

Tekken on the Sony PlayStation is Namco's seminal 1995 3D fighting masterpiece, introducing players to the King of Iron Fist Tournament with a revolutionary 4-button limb-based combat system, intricate juggles, and an expansive roster of 17 distinct combatants [[gf1-3-1, gf1-18-3]]. This guide provides an authoritative, step-by-step roadmap to achieve 100% completion in RetroAchievements Hardcore Mode, covering every character unlock, combo trial, difficulty hurdle, and the legendary Galaga boot mini-game challenge [[gf1-18-2, ra-comments-9-1]].

## Core Battle System & Controller Layout

Tekken assigns each of the PlayStation face buttons to a specific limb rather than abstract punch or kick intensities [[gf1-3-1]]. Mastering this layout is the fundamental prerequisite for executing all basic attacks, throws, and devastating combinations:
- **Left Punch (LP / 1):** Square button (`[Square]`) delivers high-speed jabs and initiates string openers [[gf1-3-1, gf2-3-1]].
- **Right Punch (RP / 2):** Triangle button (`[Triangle]`) provides heavier straight punches and launcher uppercuts [[gf1-3-1, gf4-5-1]].
- **Left Kick (LK / 3):** Cross button (`[Cross]`) controls low sweeps, side kicks, and knee strikes [[gf1-3-1, gf2-3-1]].
- **Right Kick (RK / 4):** Circle button (`[Circle]`) executes roundhouse kicks and high-damage knockdown boots [[gf1-3-1, gf1-4-2]].

Universal throws are executed by pressing two buttons simultaneously at close range: press `LP + LK` (`[Square] + [Cross]`) for standard Left Throws, or `RP + RK` (`[Triangle] + [Circle]`) for heavier Right Throws [[gf1-5-1, gf4-7-1]]. To avoid being thrown, players must duck underneath grab attempts by crouching, as throws in Tekken cannot connect against crouching opponents [[gf3-8-1]].

## Defensive Fundamentals & Movement Mechanics

Defensive mastery in Tekken requires an understanding of the two-state blocking architecture [[gf3-4-1]]:
- **High / Mid Guard:** Hold `Back` (`[B]`) on the directional pad to automatically defend against standing punches, jumping attacks, and mid-level strikes [[gf3-4-1]].
- **Low Guard:** Hold `Down-Back` (`[DB]`) to crouch and block low sweeps and sliding kicks [[gf3-4-1]]. Note that crouching leaves your fighter completely vulnerable to mid-level launcher uppercuts and axe kicks [[gf3-6-1]].
- **Dashing & Spacing:** Tap `Forward, Forward` (`[F], [F]`) to execute a rapid forward dash, or tap `Back, Back` (`[B], [B]`) to perform a defensive backdash out of strike range [[gf4-3-1]].
- **Running Attacks:** Double tap and hold forward from at least two screen distances away to initiate a sprint [[gf3-5-1, gf4-3-1]]. Running attacks deal tremendous kinetic damage, knock opponents clean across the arena, and allow offensive juggles against escaping adversaries [[gf3-5-1]].
- **Grounded Punishments (Pounces):** When an opponent is knocked flat on the floor, press `Up + Right Punch` (`[U] + [RP]`) to execute a diving pounce attack that inflicts reliable ground damage before they can tech roll [[gf4-8-1]].

## Combination Systems & Execution Hierarchy

Tekken separates offensive sequences into three distinct execution categories [[gf1-4-1, gf4-10-1]]:
1. **Natural Combos & 3-Hit Juggles:** True combos where the initial strike stuns or launches the opponent airborne, guaranteeing that subsequent hits connect before recovery [[gf1-4-1, gf5-2-1]]. Landing any clean 3-hit juggle unlocks the *Beginner Combo* achievement [[ra-comments-17-1]].
2. **Ten-Attack Strings:** Extensive pre-programmed sequences unique to each combatant [[gf1-6-1, gf4-10-1]]. While not all hits within a 10-string combo naturally trap the opponent if blocked, executing all ten inputs cleanly in sequence completes the *Ultimate Combo* achievement [[gf4-10-1, ra-comments-19-1]].
3. **Character Signature 6-Hit Combos:** Each of the 17 playable fighters possesses an achievement requiring a 6-hit combo [[ra-comments-22-1, ra-comments-38-1]]. While these can be executed during regular Arcade mode play, they are most comfortably practiced and triggered in 2-Player Versus Mode against an idle second controller [[ra-comments-38-2]].

## Tournament Roster & Progression Architecture

The Iron Fist Tournament features a structured 17-character unlock tree [[gf1-18-3, ra-comments-15-1]]:
- **The Starting Eight:** Kazuya Mishima, Paul Phoenix, Marshall Law, Nina Williams, Jack, King, Yoshimitsu, and Michelle Chang are available by default [[gf1-6-1, gf1-11-1]].
- **The Eight Sub-Boss Rivals:** Each base character faces a designated sub-boss rival in Stage 8 of Arcade Mode [[gf1-18-5, gf4-11-1]]. Defeating that rival and finishing the run unlocks them as a fully playable fighter: Kazuya unlocks Lee Chaolan; Paul unlocks Kuma; Law unlocks Wang Jinrei; Nina unlocks Anna Williams; Jack unlocks Prototype Jack; King unlocks Armor King; Yoshimitsu unlocks Ganryu; and Michelle unlocks Kunimitsu [[gf1-18-3, gf4-11-1]].
- **Heihachi Mishima:** The final boss of the tournament can be unlocked by conquering Arcade Mode with any character without using a single continue [[gf1-18-4, gf3-1-1]].
- **Devil Kazuya:** The sinister alternate costume for Kazuya is unlocked by mastering the Galaga mini-game during the initial game boot screen [[gf1-18-2, gf3-1-2]].
- **Roster Milestone:** Unlocking all eight sub-bosses, Heihachi Mishima, and Devil Kazuya triggers the *All Fighters Ready for Combat!* achievement [[gf1-18-3, ra-comments-15-1]].

Welcome to the King of Iron Fist Tournament. Before stepping into the arena, our journey begins directly on the game's initial loading sequence, where a classic Namco arcade mini-game hides one of the game's most iconic secret characters [[gf1-18-2, ra-comments-9-1]]. From there, we configure optimal tournament options, enter Arcade Mode to earn our first defensive accolade, and transition into Versus Mode (Combo Practice) to master universal combination mechanics against an idle sparring partner [[gf1-3-1, ra-comments-38-2]].

### PlayStation Boot Screen & Galaga Boot Mini-Game

When you boot Tekken on PlayStation, Namco presents a fully playable rendition of the legendary arcade shoot-'em-up Galaga while the game disc loads assets into memory [[gf1-18-2]]. Conquering this mini-game is your very first objective in the 100% walkthrough [[ra-comments-9-1]].

> **Galaga Mini-Game Targets:**
> - Stage 1 Clear Time: 18.5 seconds or under grants an automatic Dual-Ship configuration [[gf1-18-2]].
> - Stages 1 through 8 Clear: Finish all 8 stages using no more than one continue to unlock Devil Kazuya [[gf1-18-2, gf3-1-2, ra-comments-11-1]].

To earn both the achievement and the character unlock, you must clear all 8 stages of Galaga using no more than one single continue [[gf1-18-2, gf3-1-2, ra-comments-11-1]]. If you wipe out all enemy waves in Stage 1 in 18.5 seconds or less, you are awarded an immediate dual-ship configuration [[gf1-18-2]]. Having double firepower makes wiping out the challenging formations in Stages 2 through 8 significantly easier [[gf1-18-2]].

> [!WARNING]
> Do NOT use the player 2 controller dual-ship cheat code [[ra-comments-11-1]]. RetroAchievements detects the memory flags altered by the external input cheat, permanently invalidating the achievement for that session [[ra-comments-11-1]]. You must earn your dual ship legitimately by shooting down the Stage 1 enemies quickly or proceed with a single fighter [[gf1-18-2, ra-comments-11-1]].

Focus your fire on the diving alien tractor-beam commanders, keep your ship centered during initial entry patterns, and rapidly tap fire [[gf1-18-2]]. Once you successfully destroy the boss flagship on Stage 8 while using one continue or fewer, the game announces your victory and returns to the title screen [[gf3-1-2, ra-comments-11-1]].

> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> **Namco Classic Challenge (50 Points)**
> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/99159.png)
> *Beat all stages in Galaga with a perfect while using no more than one continue in order to unlock Devil Kazuya*

Clearing this challenge permanently unlocks Devil Kazuya on your save file [[gf1-18-2, gf3-1-2]]. To play as Devil Kazuya, highlight Kazuya Mishima on the character select screen and press `Start` or `Triangle` [[gf3-1-3]].

### Main Menu & Game Options Configuration

From the title screen, enter the **Game Options** menu to confirm and verify standard tournament regulations [[gf1-3-1, gf1-20-1]]. Ensuring these baseline settings are active is mandatory for subsequent Arcade campaign achievements to register properly [[ra-comments-77-1]].

Verify that your settings match the following parameters [[gf1-20-1]]:
- **Difficulty:** Medium (Default) [[gf1-20-1, ra-comments-56-1]]
- **Fight Count (Rounds):** 3 Rounds to Win (Default) [[gf1-20-1, ra-comments-79-1]]
- **Round Time:** 40 Seconds (Default) [[gf1-20-1, ra-comments-77-1]]
- **Violence / Sparks:** Default setting displaying standard hit sparks [[gf4-14-1]].

Unless an achievement explicitly dictates otherwise (such as Ultra Hard for achievement 90422 or 5 Rounds for achievement 90429), keep these default settings untouched throughout your playthroughs [[gf1-20-1, ra-comments-76-1, ra-comments-79-1]].

### Arcade Mode Ladder: The Flawless Round

With your settings configured, select **Arcade Mode** from the main menu and choose any character you are comfortable with—Paul Phoenix or Kazuya Mishima are excellent choices due to their heavy single-hit damage [[gf1-7-1, gf3-7-1]].

In Stage 1 of Arcade Mode, the computer AI operates at a very passive level [[gf1-3-1]]. Take advantage of this immediately to score a Perfect round victory without sustaining a single point of damage [[ra-comments-8-1]]. Hold `Back` to guard any incoming high punches, or crouch by holding `Down-Back` if the opponent attempts a low sweep [[gf3-4-1]]. As soon as the opponent whiffs an attack, punish immediately with a heavy attack or a throw by pressing `LP + LK` [[gf1-5-1, gf3-8-1]]. Winning any single round with your life bar completely full unlocks your next milestone [[ra-comments-8-1]].

> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> **Master of Evasion (5 Points)**
> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/98999.png)
> *Win any round with a perfect*

### Versus Mode (Combo Practice): Universal Combo Mastery

Exit back to the main menu and select Versus Mode (Combo Practice) [[gf1-3-1, ra-comments-38-2]]. Using a second controller allows you to practice combinations against a completely stationary sparring dummy that never blocks or counter-attacks [[ra-comments-38-2]].

First, we will satisfy the 3-hit combo requirement [[ra-comments-17-1]]. Select Kazuya Mishima on Player 1 [[gf1-6-1]]. Approach Player 2 and execute Kazuya's Double Walking Uppercut followed by a standing jab: input `Down-Forward + LP, RP`, immediately followed by a neutral `LP` (`(DF + LP, RP), LP`) [[gf1-6-3]]. All three strikes connect consecutively as a natural airborne juggle, granting the achievement [[gf1-6-3, ra-comments-17-1]].

> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> **Beginner Combo (5 Points)**
> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/99084.png)
> *Perform a 3 hit combo*

Next, we tackle the formidable 10-hit combination [[gf1-7-1, ra-comments-19-1]]. In Tekken, ten-attack strings are intricate pre-programmed attack sequences that unleash a barrage of high, mid, and low hits [[gf4-10-1]]. Paul Phoenix possesses one of the most reliable and rhythmic 10-hit combos in the game [[gf1-7-1, gf2-3-1]].

Select Paul Phoenix and position yourself directly in front of the idle Player 2 dummy [[gf1-7-1, ra-comments-38-2]]. Enter the following 10-strike sequence rhythmically: press `LP`, then `RP`, then `LK`, then `RP`, then `LP`, then `RP`, then `LP`, then `RK`, then `RP`, and finally `LP` (`Square -> Triangle -> Cross -> Triangle -> Square -> Triangle -> Square -> Circle -> Triangle -> Square`) [[gf1-7-1, gf2-3-1]]. Input each button as the prior strike begins its animation [[gf1-7-1, gf2-3-1]]. Once the tenth strike connects and the hit counter reaches 10, the Ultimate Combo achievement unlocks [[ra-comments-19-1]].

> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> **Ultimate Combo (25 Points)**
> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/125570.png)
> *Perform a 10 hit combo*

### Kazuya Mishima Arcade Run

Kazuya Mishima is the legitimate heir to the Mishima financial empire and a deadly karate combatant possessing exceptional mid-range launchers and punishing strikes [[gf1-6-1, gf4-12-1]]. His signature moves include the Dragon Punch (`F, D, DF + LP`), the Lunging Uppercut (`F, D, DF + RP`), and the Twin Pistons (`DF + LP, RP`) [[gf1-6-2, gf3-3-1, gf4-5-1]].

> **Kazuya Mishima Campaign Profile:**
> - Stage 8 Rival: Lee Chaolan (Defeat to unlock Lee Chaolan) [[gf1-18-3, gf1-18-5]].
> - Stage 9 Final Boss: Heihachi Mishima [[gf1-18-4, gf3-1-1]].
> - Signature 6-Hit String: `F, F(Hold)+RP, LP, RP, RP, LK, RK` [[gf1-6-1, ra-comments-22-1]].

Navigate through Stages 1 through 7 using Kazuya's powerful Lunging Uppercut to launch advancing opponents into the air, following up with a grounded Axe Kick for massive damage [[gf1-6-2, gf3-6-1]]. In Stage 8, you face Kazuya's adoptive brother and rival, Lee Chaolan [[gf1-18-5, gf1-26-1]]. Lee favors quick sliding kicks; block low by holding `Down-Back` and punish his recovery with Twin Pistons [[gf1-6-3, gf3-4-1]]. In Stage 9, confront Heihachi Mishima at the Mishima estate [[gf1-18-4, gf3-9-1]]. Bait Heihachi's Wind God Fist, duck beneath his high punches, and counter with heavy throws (`LP + LK`) [[gf1-5-1, gf3-8-1]]. Defeating Heihachi without changing characters completes the campaign, unlocking Lee Chaolan [[gf1-18-3, ra-comments-56-1]].

> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/98787.png)

> **Paul Phoenix Campaign Profile:**
> - Stage 8 Rival: Kuma (Defeat to unlock Kuma) [[gf1-18-3, gf1-18-5, gf4-11-1]].

Paul's campaign is straightforward due to the absurd stopping power of PonKen [[gf2-3-2]]. In Stage 8, Paul encounters Kuma, Heihachi's loyal guard bear [[gf1-18-5, gf4-11-1]]. Kuma's massive hitbox makes him especially vulnerable to Paul's Power Punch; time your strikes outside Kuma's bear hug range to down the beast quickly [[gf1-7-2, gf4-11-1]]. ### Five Minute Speedrun Trial: The Death Punch Blitz

For the speedrun trial, return to the **Game Options** menu and reset the difficulty to **Medium**, ensuring Fight Count is 3 rounds and Round Time is 40 seconds [[gf1-20-1, ra-comments-77-1]]. Your objective is to complete all nine stages of Arcade Mode in under 5 minutes total [[ra-comments-77-1]].

> **Speedrun Requirements & Strategy:**
> - Time Limit: Under 5 Minutes Total Elapsed Time [[ra-comments-77-1]].
> - Difficulty Setting: Medium or higher with default round settings [[ra-comments-77-1]].
> - Trigger Window: The achievement triggers immediately AFTER the post-game results screen [[ra-comments-77-1]].

The undisputed strategy for this achievement is Michelle Chang's ten-attack string cheese [[ra-comments-78-1]]. For reasons rooted in the early PS1 AI programming, the CPU simply does not know how to defend against Michelle's 10-hitter combo: `2, 1, 1, 2, 3, 3, 3, 4, 4, 1` (`Right Punch, Left Punch, Left Punch, Right Punch, Left Kick, Left Kick, Left Kick, Right Kick, Right Kick, Left Punch`) [[gf1-11-2, ra-comments-78-1]].

Select Michelle Chang and immediately initiate her 10-attack string at the start of every single round [[gf1-11-2, ra-comments-78-1]]. The sequence continually traps and chips through the computer's guard, defeating almost every opponent in 5 to 7 seconds per round [[ra-comments-78-1]]. By looping this sequence from Stage 1 through Stage 9, you will comfortably finish the entire arcade ladder with an overall time between 4:20 and 4:40, well beneath the 5:00 deadline [[ra-comments-78-1]].

> [!NOTE]
> *Five Minute Death Punch* does not trigger immediately upon striking Heihachi in Stage 9 [[ra-comments-77-1]]. You must let the ending cutscene play and wait until the final post-game results screen finishes calculating before the achievement pops [[ra-comments-77-1]].

> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> **Five Minute Death Punch (10 Points)**
> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/99059.png)
> *Finish Arcade mode within 5 minutes on Medium difficulty or higher with default settings - Will trigger after the results screen*

### Long Journey 5-Round Marathon

Our final trial is the test of sheer endurance: the **Long Journey** marathon [[ra-comments-79-1]]. Enter the **Game Options** menu and adjust the round count [[gf1-20-1, ra-comments-79-1]]:
- **Fight Count (Rounds):** Set to **5 Rounds to Win** [[ra-comments-79-1]]
- **Difficulty:** Medium (Default) [[gf1-20-1, ra-comments-79-1]]
- **Round Time:** 40 Seconds (Default) [[gf1-20-1, ra-comments-79-1]]

> **Long Journey Marathon Rules:**
> - Round Count: Exactly 5 Rounds per match (Requires winning 5 individual rounds per stage) [[ra-comments-79-1]].
> - Mandatory Restrictions: No Continues allowed, and No Character Changes allowed [[ra-comments-79-1, ra-comments-79-2]].
> - Marathon Duration: Up to 45 individual rounds across 9 consecutive opponents [[ra-comments-79-1]].
> - Recommended Core: Use Beetle PSX or SwanStation if DuckStation triggers experience issues [[ra-comments-79-1]].

Because you must win five individual rounds across all nine stages, this grueling marathon requires winning a staggering 45 rounds without dropping a single match or using a continue [[ra-comments-79-1, ra-comments-79-2]]. The achievement specifically enforces a strict "no character change" condition to prevent continuing or swapping fighters [[ra-comments-79-2]].

The safest and most reliable fighter for this marathon is Lee Chaolan [[ra-comments-80-1]]. Lee's Lazer's Edge Kick combo (`Down + RK, RK, RK` / `D+4,4,4`) trips and sweeps the computer AI with near-flawless consistency throughout long sessions [[gf2-9-1, ra-comments-80-1]]. Crouch and spam `D+4,4,4` repeatedly; the CPU repeatedly attempts standing punches, eating the rapid low kicks and collapsing to the mat [[gf2-9-1, ra-comments-80-1]].

> [!TIP]
> Pay close attention to who you fight in the opening three stages [[ra-comments-80-1]]. King's AI can be aggressive and unpredictable with sudden throws and hop kicks later in a run [[gf2-5-1, ra-comments-80-1]]. If you draw King in Stages 1 through 3, his AI is much more passive, making it the ideal seed for a successful deathless marathon [[ra-comments-80-1]].

Maintain your concentration through Stage 8 against Kazuya and Stage 9 against Heihachi, continuing to abuse low kicks and mid-range slides [[gf1-12-1, gf2-9-1, ra-comments-80-1]]. When Heihachi falls for the fifth and final round, the 43rd and final achievement unlocks, crowning you as the undisputed Master of the Iron Fist Tournament [[ra-comments-79-1]].

> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> **Long Journey (50 Points)**
> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/99061.png)
> *Finish Arcade mode on Medium difficulty or higher without changing characters or using continues, and with default settings except for the round count set to 5*
