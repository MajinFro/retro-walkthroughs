Welcome to the guide for **Dragon Warrior II (NES).**

This guide is a **100% Walkthrough** , covering the game from beginning to end, including all key objectives, important items, and progression steps needed to fully complete the adventure. Whether you're playing for the first time or revisiting this classic, this guide is designed to provide clear and efficient direction every step of the way.

Press the Start Button and select BEGIN A NEW QUEST. Choose an empty ADVENTURE LOG, then enter your character’s name. Throughout this guide, the main character will be referred to as “Hero” or “Hero II.” Select your preferred Message Speed.

* * *

PROLOGUE

Many years ago a young warrior who was of the line of the great Erdrick came to Alefgard and defeated the dreaded Dragonlord, restoring peace to the land.

For many generations the descendants of that warrior ruled Alefgard and the surrounding lands, including the Kingdom of Moonbrooke across the eastern sea from Alefgard.

* * *

One day in the Kingdom of Moonbrooke, the King and his daughter are talking in the castle courtyard when the long years of peace suddenly come to an end. The screen shakes and turns green. The King cries out, asking what in the world is happening. One of the King’s guards approaches and reports that the castle is under siege by the forces of Hargon the wizard. Hargon is in the castle. The King resolves to stand firm and fight. A monster appears near the guard and kills him, but the King defeats the monster. The King and the Princess move to the staircase in the southeast, where the King tells her to hide. He says that his cousin, the King of Midenhall, must be warned. Another monster appears in front of the Princess, but the King draws its attention and defeats it. Two more monsters then ambush the King and kill him. Monsters flood the castle, slaughtering everyone in their path. One of the King’s guards manages to escape. He must reach the King of Midenhall and tell him of Moonbrooke’s defeat. He leaves the castle and watches it burn to the ground. The guard makes it to Midenhall Castle.

The Guard from Moonbrooke Castle will walk forward a bit before falling to the ground. His wounds are serious. The other Guards nearby will help him to the King. The Guard will tell the King about the army of Hargon the Sorcerer, and then he will die. We are the young prince standing by the King’s side. Since the King is old, his task falls to us. The King tells us to come with him.

You gain control over Hero.

Hero comes equipped with a **Leather Armor**. Speak to the Old Man and Guard. The Old Man will miss Hero. Our path will lead us to different places the Guard cannot see. Move to the King then down the staircase.

The King tells you to open the treasure chest and to seek out worthy companions along the road. In Cannock and Moonbrooke, there are those who are also of Erdrick’s line. They will help us to defeat Hargon. Open the treasure chest to obtain **COPPER SWORD** and **50 GOLD**. Equip the Copper Sword on Hero.

The guard nearby will remind you to equip any weapons and armor you find. The young man nearby will tell you that there is a town called Leftwyne to the west of this castle. The young lady nearby says she will miss the Hero. Go south, then west, and speak to the old man to learn that the whirlpool across the bridge is called a Travel Door. Do not step on it, as it only leads to a small island with no path.

From the Travel Door, go south and speak to the young man to learn that there is a young Prince in Cannock and that Moonbrooke had a princess. Go north from the Travel Door to find two locked rooms. Farther north and to the left, there is a man behind a counter who says that when in need, you should visit the Houses of Healing.

From the Travel Door, go east, then south, to reach the outdoor section of the castle. There is a Tools Shop to your left and an Inn to your right. There is also a dog wandering around nearby.

That is everything we can do in this castle for now, as everywhere else is locked. When you purchase items in this game, there is a chance of receiving **Lottery Tickets**. We will learn what they are used for later. Buy three Medical Herbs, then head out to the Overworld.

Follow my directions carefully so you do not get lost.

From the body of water, go through the opening south to find a chest. Open it for a **MEDICAL HERB**. Return to the body of water.

He went to Midenhall Castle. Speak to him again and answer his question with a No. he will anoint you with water from the spring. The Token of Erdrick is kept by a brave hero.

Leftwyne  
---  
  
Go into the Weapons and Armor Shop. Unlock the Silver Door. The Merchant here runs the Lottery. This is where you use the Lottery Tickets.

Lottery Mini-Game Rules and Prizes  
---  
As the Lottery wheels spin, push thy controller button to stop them.Three (Sun Symbol) marks win the first prize — a Golden Card.Three (Star Symbol) marks win the second prize — a Wizard’s Ring.There may be further prizes, too.  
-  
**Position**| **Mark**| **Prize**  
1st Place| Sun Symbol| Golden Card  
2nd Place| Star Symbol| Wizard’s Ring  
3rd Place| Moon Symbol| Wizard’s Wand  
4th Place| Water Symbol| Dragon’s Bane  
5th Place| Life Symbol| Medical Herb  
-  
If you get only two matching symbols, you will receive a consolation prize: a free **Lottery Ticket**.  
  
The **Golden Card** is a special item. If you have it, you will automatically receive a discount on items at stores.

Travel to Cannock Castle.
