[Torbix] For this achievement, there are only 5 chests in the game that you have to open to progress through the story: 

1. Crown: Found in a chest in the 3rd level of the Marsh Cave, needed to fight Astos
2. TNT: Found in a chest behind a locked door in Coneria Castle, needed to open a canal to the west for your ship
3. Ruby: Found in a chest on the 3rd level of Earth Cave after defeating the Vampire, needed to progress through the Titan's Tunnel and reach Sarda's Cave
4. Floater: Found in a chest on the 3rd level of the Ice Cave, needed to obtain the Airship
5. Slab: Found in a chest at the top level of the Sea Shrine (note that you enter the Shrine on the 3rd level), needed to talk to the people in Lefein

Bonus Tip: You'll see the icon for this achievement on your screen if you're still meeting the requirement for it. The icon will temporarily disappear if you're facing a chest with an item that's not needed for progression, to indicate that you shouldn't open it. If you open one of these unnecessary chests, the icon will stay gone, indicating that you no longer meet the requirement.
