[3] CLASSES                                                             [0200]

==============================================================================

=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-
 WARRIOR/KNIGHT
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-

The Warrior is your most well-rounded character, with good stats and a little 
below average magic. He's got great HP, Defense, and Attack. You don't learn 
much magic at all, hence the low Magic Attack and Defense. As a Knight, you 
can only learn some basic White Magic spells, and no more. Frankly, I think 
you should always have one in your party, unless you're a real veteran at 
this game and want a challenge.


=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-
 MONK/MASTER
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-

The Monk is the strongest character in the game, topping the Warrior's 
attack. He has lower HP and Defense though, but his strength makes up for it. 

He's best bare handed, which means that you shouldn't equip him with any 
weapon at any time. It will just make him incredibly weak, making him 
virtually useless. Monks also don't have any MP, which means that they can't 
use any type of magic. A Master isn't a big step up from a Monk, just better 
attributes. I recommend a Monk for new players. 


=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-
 THIEF/NINJA
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-

Thieves are most well known for their agility, and that's what they're really 
only good attribute. Agility is their best stat, and most is either average 
or poor. He has low MP and Intelligence. His Attack, Defense, and HP is 
pretty average. Once upgraded, a Ninja can use some low level Black Magic, 
but I would use them for their speed, not magic. I think newbies should have 
one in their party, but more experience players should replace them with 
let's say, a mage of some sort.


=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-
 BLACK MAGE/BLACK WIZARD
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-

In my opinion, I think Black Mages are pretty useless. They have abysmal HP; 
the lowest in the game, and every other stat except MP and Intelligence are 
pretty low. They have superb magic and Intelligence and some spells that only 
they can get, but it doesn't help. They'll end up getting killed easily. 
Black Mages get better attributes once upgraded, but not enough to make them 
exra useful. I think you should just use a Red Mage instead.

                                                                           
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-
 WHITE MAGE/WHITE WIZARD
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-
                                                                           
White Mages are your healing party members, which is the most important 
aspect of a team. Of course they have average or poor stats, but their MP and 
incredibly useful and important White magic spells make up for it.

Their HP is still higher than the Black Mages, and their stats are somewhat
bad. White Wizards gain better spells and get stronger. Despite their average
stats, White Mages are needed nearly every time in a battle and should always
be in your party.


=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-
 RED MAGE/RED WIZARD
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-
                                                                           
What makes the Red Mage useful is that they are the only party member that
can learn both White and Black magic. Sure, they can't learn every spell that
White and Black Mages can, but they learn enough to make them a very
important character.

They can still use basic healing spells, like Life, Protect, and Curaga. They
can learn spells such as Blizzaga, Thundaga, and Firaga. They have a bit worse
MP and Intelligence than the mages, but outflank them in terms of HP, Attack,
and Defense. Red Wizards get stronger and can learn more spells as well. I
think Red Mages are an incredibly important character to have in your party.

==============================================================================
 RESCUING THE PRINCESS                                                  [0401]
==============================================================================

The game starts off with you in the World Map; right in front of a city. You 
can walk around and fight monsters, but head inside the city first by walking 
over it.

Once in the city, talk to the guy behind you. He will bring you to the King's
Castle where he asks for your party's help. He belives you are the prophesied
Warriors of Light, and he wants you to rescue his daugher, the princess. He
says that he will rebuild the northern bridge if you can rescue her from the
traitor Garland. Leave the castle once the conversation ends and explore.

Get a feel for the vicinity and try to level up by fighting monsters out

that level, go north past a cave on the mountainside. Don't worry about that 
cave; you won't have to go there for a while. Keep heading north towards the 
peninsula and enter the Chaos Shrine.

Potion inside the chests. Go back to the entrance of the shrine and head 
north into the center of the room. Talk to the man surrounded by bats to 
engage in your first boss battle!

 THE EARTH CRYSTAL                                                      [0402]
==============================================================================
                                                                           
Stay near Cornelia until you're about Level 5 or so, then go across the 
bridge to new land. Go north past the mountains and hang a left around the 
bend, and keep going north. You will find a small path of rocks with a cave 
entrance. Go inside and enter Matoya's Cave.

                                                                           
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-
 MATOYA'S CAVE
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-

You can't do anything now here, so just pick up the Potion and Antidote from 
the chests and talk to the brooms. They will tell you to access the map; 
press Circle and Select at the same time to bring up the world map. It's very 
useful.

                                     ---

time. You'll be travelling quite a long way until you reach Pravoka. There 
are some even deadlier monsters here, so keep your guard up. If you are 
seriously injured, either make a risky dash for Pravoka or turn back and make 
a stop at Cornelia. You will reach Pravoka past a small forest.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The keep is abandoned except for one person. Head through the entrance and go 
left, then up into the main quarters. Talk to the man to find out that he's 
the king. He will tell you that a dark elf, Astos, decieved him and stole his 
crown. The king asks you to return it to him by going to Marsh Cave so he can 
restore glory to the castle. You can't access the locked door with the items 
in it, so ignore them for now.

                                     ---
                                                                           
Return to Elfheim to prepare for your journey. Level up around the town and
gain more Gil to purchase more useful spells and items. Make sure to pick up
Fira and Diara, which prove to be quite useful in Marsh Cave. Stock up on many
Antidotes and Tents, then head out to marsh Cave.

Go west past the mountains. Head up past the forest and before the first 
river, go west through the mountains. Go shouth past the rocks and at the 
very tip of the land is Marsh Cave.

 MARSH CAVE
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-

 Monsters: Anaconda, Black Widow, Bloodbones, Crawler, Gargoyle, Ghoul, Gray

    Items: Gil, Antidote, Broadsword, Copper Armlet, Cottage, Crown, Dagger,

The Marsh Cave is your first real big dungeon. I recommend that you should be 
at least level 11 before you hang out in this place, so level up outside if 
you're not that level. Watch out for the slimes. Most of the slimes you 
encounter here are immune to physical attacks, so whip out the magic spells. 
Green Slimes and Scorpions can poison you; that's why I told you to buy lots 
of Antidotes. First off, go east and north from the exit. Walk down the 
narrow, winding path. You will reach an exit at the end of the pathway. 

2F
--
On the next floor, go north and east towards the door. Go inside and pick up 
the Dagger from the chest. Exit the room and go west past the rock pillars 
and into the next room. Pick up the Gil from the chest, exit the room, and go 
south around the stone pillars into the next room. You will find a Broadsword 
and more Gil. Equip the Broadsword if it gives an attack boost to any of your 
party members, then leave the floor through the entrance. 

1F
--
Back in the first floor, go back to the entrance. This time, head south, and 
keep going south past the fork in the road. Go past the room and go through 
the door on the eastern side. You will find another ladder leading to the 
basement. Go down the ladder to the last floor. 

3F
--
The basement is a giant floor filled with small rooms which all hold chests. 
The four bottom rooms are locked and cannot be opened yet, so ignore them. 
From the ladder, go douth and into the first room to find a Phoenix Down. Go 
east to the last room and pick up the Gil inside. Head south to the next row 
of rooms and go west into the third room. Get the Cottage inside, then on the 
next room is a Copper Armlet. It's very useful, so have someone equip it. 
Proceed south from that room past the pillars. The first room is empty, but 
the room to the right of it is where the crown is. Go inside, but when you 
try to open the chest, you are ambushed. Boss time!

Return to Elfheim and heal up, then go back to Western Keep.

 WESTERN KEEP
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-

 Monsters: None
    Items: Falchion, Power Staff, Steel Gloves

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                                                           
You still can't open the locked door, but you will soon. Head into the main 
quarters again and talk to the king. You give him the crown, and he starts 
laughing maniacally. That doesn't sound like a king, does it? Nope; it's a 
trap! (had to do it) The king reveals himself as the king of the dark Elves, 
Astos himself. It was all a trick. You fell for his plan, and now since Astos 
has the Crown, he can rule over the elves with an iron fist! Not if we have 
anything to say about it.

=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-

 Monsters: Ankheg, Fire Elemental, Fire Gigas, Fire Hydra, Fire Lizard, Gray

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This area is basically a giant volcano, filled to the brim with lava and
all nasty stuff like that. The fact that you cannot encounter enemies on lava
tiles doesn't mean you should tread all over them. The tiles covered in lava
will damage you each time you step on them, so avoid 'em as much as possible.

1F
--
The first floor is pretty small, but the center is filled with lava. You'll 
have to step through numerous lava tiles, but try to shorten your trip across 
to minimize damage taken. Go north from the stairs and around the bend. 
Proceed west, across the lava, then into the staircase north of your 
location. 

2F
--
The second floor is the first area in Mt. Gulg where a large option is in 
your path. Several areas in this dungeon give you two options: take the easy 
road to the next floor and miss out on many valuable items and equipment, or 
take the long road to get all of the goodies. Of coruse, I will be guiding 
you through the longer route. If you don't want any of the items, then simply 
go west across the lava to the next staircase.

If you want the items, then go through the doorway next to the floor 
entrance. Go north until you reach the first intersection, then move west 
towards the first chest. Open it to get a Mythril Helm. Continue south 
through the tunnels until you reach another chest containing 1520 Gil, 
situated at a dead end.

Return to the treasure chest where you found the Mythril Helm. Go back to the 
intersection and walk north this time. Once you reach another fork in the 
road that splits to the right and left, take the left path. Follow the 
passage until you reach a dead end with a chest at the end. Open it for a 
Great Sword and return to the fork.

Go right this time as you make your way down the winding passage. Along the
way is a chest containing 4150 Gil. Head north, then west. When you reach
another intersection, pass it and go south at the next intersection
immediately past the first. You will reach two chests at the end of the
passage, both containing 750 Gil. 

Continue south and pass by the first intersection. Once you reach the wall, 
proceed east to a large chamber with a plethora of chests. Open them to 
obtain *takes deep breath* 1520 Gil, 1760 Gil, Mythril Gloves, Mythril Helm, 
1455 Gil, Hi-Potion, Tent, 1975 Gil, Antidote, Phoenix Down, Mythril Axe, and 
a Mythril Shield. Oh my! Equip all of that new equipment to your party 
members and return to the entrance to the floor. Now you can continue east 
across the lava to the staircase. Go through to access the next floor.

3F
--
There are no treasure chests on this floor. Instead of having multiple spaces 
filled with lava, many of the tiles on the floor are checkered with lava. 
Navigate east towards the staircase, stepping on the least amount of lava 
tiles as you can. Once you reach the stairs, descend them to the next floor 
of the volcano.

4F
--
Nearly every tile on this floor is covered in lava. Luckily, there are a few 
safe spots here, found in the form of giant rock structures. From the 
entrance, run south towards dry land. Walk around the wall, then go south to 
the next rock structure directly below. From there, move east towards another 
rock. The staircase is to the right of the rock; run over to it to head to 
the next floor.

5F
--
Head south from the entrance of the floor across the lava. You will reach a 
large rock. From the rock, go west onto dry land. Walk over to the next rock 
to the left, then proceed south over the lava tiles. Walk across more dry 
land, then once you step on the lava, head west across the lava. Keep moving 
west and you will come to another staircase.

6F
--
This next floor has a lot of treasures to get. Walk through the doorway from 
the start and open both chests inside: they contain a Gold Needle and 2750 
Gil. Exit and head west, then south. At the intersection, stick to the 
western side and enter the first doorway that you come to. Go inside and open 
the chest for 1750 Gil. 

Leave the room and follow the path north, then west. Enter the doorway once 
you reach the dead end. Open all of the treasure chests inside for 880 Gil, 
Antidote, 7340 Gil, Flame Shield, and an Ice Brand. Once you have all of 
that, return to the intersection. From there, proceed south. Move east at the 
wall and go through the doorway right in front of the fork. The two chests 
contain 10 Gil and 155 Gil. Exit to the fork and move north. You will come to 
another doorway at the end of the path. The chests contain a Cottage and 2000 
Gil.

Exit the room and return to the previous fork. Continue south down the path 
this time, moving east at the wall. Enter the doorway leading to the final 
room. You must fight an enemy to obtain the item in these two chests. The 
battle is easy, and you will get a Staff and 1250 Gil after the battle. 
Pretty useless stuff, but it won't hurt to get them. Exit the room and go 
southwest towards the staircase.

7F
--
If you got all of the items during the trip through Mt. Gulg, then you only 
need to make one stop on this floor before the boss. Go west across the lava 
until you reach a room. Go on inside and open the chest for Flame Mail. 
Return to the entrance to the floor and move southwest. Follow the path until 
you reach the final room of the dungeon. Heal and prepare for the worst, 
because you will encounter the guardian of the Fire crystal in the room 
beyond, Marilith. Are you ready?

    Items: Gil, Adamantite, Black Robe, Clothes, Cottage, Diamond Gloves,

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Flying Fortress is your toughest dungeon yet, so heal and prepare yourself
now. You'll also meet your good friend, Evil Eye, here. Yay for good times.

1F
--
You will find yourself in a small room in the center of a large floor. Exit 
and go south, around the walled room. Enter it to find a Razer in the chest. 
Equip it to any party members and return to the center of the floor. Head 
west this time and pillage the room to find 7900 Gil, 9900 Gil, 4150 Gil, and 
a Potion.

Go to the right this time to find 180 Gil, 5000 Gil, 6720 Gil, Healing Helm, 
and a Protect Ring in the room. Finally, go north from the center of the 
floor and step on the warp tile.

2F
--
The last room was a four-way intersection, but this one is an eight-way 
intersection. Yay for complexity! Walk southward to the center of the room. 
Your first priority is to go southwest. Walk down the path and enter the 
room. Collect the Adamantite from the chest. This is very important, so keep 
it safe in your item bag. Return to the center and go west for 880 Gil and 
13000 Gil in the room. Now go northwest from the center of the room. You will 
find a Cottage and a Mythril Helm in the room.

The left side of the floor is finished, so go northeast this time. You'll 
find a lone chest with Diamond Gloves inside. From the middle of the room, go 
east. You will obtain a Diamond Shield and a Ribbon in the chests. Return to 
the center of the room. Now go southeast and enter the room. Collect the 
Black Robe and White Robe, and equip them to your Wizards; they're deathly 
useful. Phew! Simply go south from the center of the room to make your exit.

3F
--
You will be transported to the middle of yet another huge floor. There is a 
robot near the warp tile. Talk to him and he will tell you to look out of the 
window. Here, you can get a view of the whole world. It will teach you how 
the four elements meet at one point back on the earth. Use that as a clue as 
to where your next destination may be. And plus, the view is nice. :)

Go north and follow the path to a doorway. Enter the room and open the chests 
for a Potion, Protect Ring, 9000 Gil, and 5450 Gil. Continue south again and 
once you reach the entrance, go east. Enter the room and open the treasure 
chests to obtain a Gold Needle, 6400 Gil, 9500 Gil, 8135 Gil, a Protect 
Cloak, and Clothes. 

The southern end of the room is barren, so the only other way to go is west. 
Make your way west from the starting point and go inside the room. Get the 
Gold Needle, 4150 Gil, 3400 Gil, and Sasuke's Blade. Equip your new weapon 
and return to the path. The teleporter is right next to the room, so step 
through to teleport to the next floor.

4F
--
This floor is a never-ending cycle. There are numerous holes situated on the 
floor. Here's the direction to take to reach the warp tile: north two holes 
and west two holes. You should reach the warp tile; step on it once you do. 

5F
--
This long corridor is home to not only the final guardian, but an imensely 
powerful enemy named Death Machine. You can only find him on this floor, and 
you have a 1 in 64 chance of encountering him. He is very hard, so I will 
provide you with a strategy if you happen to encounter him.

 THE FINAL BATTLE                                                       [0406]

To prepare for the final dungeon, it would be wise to obtain one of the most

 MT. DUERGAR

tackling some of, if not all, the bosses in the Soul of Chaos. They really 
help give you loads of EXP and present you with nice items and equipment. 
Once you feel that you're ready, fly over to the Chaos Shrine.

 |  ATK: 220       AGI: 87       M DEF: 220         Drop: Ragnarock        |
 |  ACC: 200       INT: 70         GIL: 0                                  |
 |                                                                         |
 |   Weakness: None                                                        |
 | Resistance: Quake, Stone, Death, Time, Poison                           |
 |-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
 | Shinryu was tough, but I beat him on my second try. This menacing foe   |
 | has very high attack, but his weak point is in his defense: it's only   |
 | are mere 60. Magic attacks don't hurt him that much; he has high magic  |
 | defense. Use pumped-up physical attacks by using Giant's Gloves, Haste, |
 | and Temper. Have your Mage cast Temper and Haste on your strong         |
 | characters, and then let him use black magic to take out Shinryu. If    |
 | you have weapons like Excalibur and Masamune, you will deal loads of    |
 | damage to Shinryu, so hack away. I had my Knight and Master attack him, |
 | my Ninja healed everyone and used Rune Staff nearly every turn, and I   |
 | had my Mage use stat increasing spells and black magic.                 |
 |                                                                         |
 | The battle might take a while, but He isn't that bad. Shinryu's         |
 | physical attacks hurt your fighters a lot, but his spells like Tidal    |
 | Wave will probably deal 400 damage to your party. Use forms of Heal if  |
 | you have a White Wizard, but if not, have your weakest character use    |
 | Rune Staff constantly. He tends to use powerful spells when his health  |
 | is low, so it should give you some type of clue when he'll die or not.  |
 | Once Shinryu is dead, you'll recieve Ragnarock, a sword even more       |
 | powerful than Excalibur! Just because he was so annoying, open the      |
 | treasure chest after the battle, and you'll recieve the Hero's Shield,  |
 | a powerful shield that nicely boosts your defense.                      |
 '-------------------------------------------------------------------------'

 .-------------------------------------------------------------------------.
 | BOSS: Typhon                                                            |
 |-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
 |   HP: 10000     DEF: 100        EVA: 0            EXP: 0                |
 |  ATK: 70        AGI: 10       M DEF: 190         Drop: Genji Helm       |
 |  ACC: 70        INT: 40         GIL: 0                                  |
 |                                                                         |
 |   Weakness: Ice                                                         |
 | Resistance: Quake, Time, Poison, Fire, Stone, Death                     |
 |-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
 | Typhon has moderate amount of health, and well, his stats are pretty    |
 | moderate too. He doesn't have any Evasion, but he does have high        |
 | efense, so you might have trouble dealing a lot of damage to him. I     |
 | thought Typhon was going to be easy, but surprisingly, he was           |
 | incredibly annoying.                                                    |
 |                                                                         |
 | Make sure you have a lot of Phoenix Downs, because he has a one-hit KO  |
 | spell called Snort that seems to rarely miss. He uses Snort more than   |
 | he does attack you! Use Phoenix Downs and Life if your members die, and |
 | use Blizzaga to take him out. Typhon is weak against Ice, so Blizzaga   |
 | may be your best bet against this boss. If you want, you can use        |
 | Temper, Saber, and Haste to pump up a strong character and use physical |
 | attacks, but either method is fine. You will obtain the Genji Helm, a   |
 | nice piece of armor, after the battle with Typhon.                      |
 '-------------------------------------------------------------------------'

 .-------------------------------------------------------------------------.
 | BOSS: Orthros                                                           |
 |-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
 |   HP: 17000     DEF: 40         EVA: 0            EXP: 0                |
 |  ATK: 60        AGI: 30       M DEF: 180         Drop: Rune Staff       |
 |  ACC: 80        INT: 20         GIL: 0                                  |
 |                                                                         |
 |   Weakness: Fire, Lightning                                             |
 | Resistance: Quake, Time, Poison, Ice, Stone, Death                      |
 |-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
 | Orthros? More like Ultros!! Ha ha. In terms of the battle, I was        |
 | actually disappointed by Orthros. His stats are pretty low, and even    |
 | though his health is somewhat high, this guy can be taken out rather    |
 | quickly. He's weak to Fire and Lightning, so use those two spells       |
 | against Orthros to ensure maximum damage dealt to the octopus.          |
 |                                                                         |
 | If you're high enough level, Ink probably won't even hit you. If you    |
 | are hit by Ink, simply cure it with Eye Drops. Orthros does pack a      |
 | punch, but as always, use Protera to negate that. use your strongest    |
 | attacks and spells that are strong against Orthros, and he won't be     |
 | much of a problem to your party.                                        |
 '-------------------------------------------------------------------------'

 .-------------------------------------------------------------------------.
 | BOSS: Phantom Train                                                     |
 |-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
 |   HP: 9999      DEF: 80         EVA: 0            EXP: 0                |
 |  ATK: 200       AGI: 30       M DEF: 180         Drop: Megalixer        |
 |  ACC: 50        INT: 40         GIL: 0                                  |
 |                                                                         |
 |   Weakness: Fire, Dia                                                   |
 | Resistance: Quake, Stone, Death, Time, Poison                           |
 |-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
 | Holy moly, look at his attack! Phantom Train does have an obnoxiously   |
 | high attack, so keep your guard up. Cating Protera should be your first |
 | priority. Have a Mage use Protera, and if you have him, have a Ninja    |
 | use Temper on himself or a Knight. Phantom Train uses Acid Rain most of |
 | the time, which deals 300+ damage to your party. Not good.              |
 |                                                                         |
 | Here is my strategy: I had Ninja and Mage use Temper on my Knight. My   |
 | Mage used Haste on my Knight next turn, and my Knight was dealing at    |
 | least 1500 damage to Phantom Train (thank you Excalibur). You can also  |
 | use Fire and Dia spells if you don't use my strategy, since those two   |
 | spells are Phantom Train's main weaknesses. You'll need a lot of        |
 | Phoenix Downs and Ethers for this fight, but since Phantom Train has    |
 | pretty low HP and very high Attack, you'll either have a hard time      |
 | with him or breeze through him. You'll get a nice Megalixer afterwads.  |
 '-------------------------------------------------------------------------'

 .-------------------------------------------------------------------------.
 | BOSS: Death Gaze                                                        |
 |-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
 |   HP: 30000     DEF: 150        EVA: 30           EXP: 0                |
 |  ATK: 90        AGI: 95       M DEF: 220         Drop: Lightbringer     |
 |  ACC: 200       INT: 50         GIL: 0                                  |
 |                                                                         |
 |   Weakness: Fire, Dia                                                   |
 | Resistance: Quake, Time, Poison, Ice, Stone, Death                      |
 |-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
 | Death Gaze was really tough, since he's the last boss of Whisperwind    |
 | Cove. Traveling through 40 levels must have been exhausting, so heal    |
 | before you fight him. He is a challenging fight, but easier than Omega  |
 | and Shinryu. Death Gaze uses Dispel a lot, so a character that you've   |
 | been powering up is hit by it, well, all your stat-raising spells are   |
 | negated. Death Gaze is powerful and uses a wide variety of spells to    |
 | put the pain on you.                                                    |
 |                                                                         |
 | Use Protera on your first turn, and have your weakest character use the |
 | Judgement Staff, or if you don't have a White Wizard, use a Light Axe   |
 | on him. He has high defense, so I wouldn't reccomend physical attacks.  |
 | It takes a while for you to power up someone enough to deal a good      |
 | amount of damage, so just stick to spells. Use Firaga and Diaga on      |
 | Death Gaze, and if you want, you can use powered up physical attacks if |
 | you have a Master or Knight in your party. Once Death Gaze is defeated, |
 | you will be rewarded with the Lightbringer.                             |
 '-------------------------------------------------------------------------'
