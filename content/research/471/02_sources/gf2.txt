 =-   2)   STORY                                                          -=
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
Super Mario RPG's story, from the instruction manual...

  One day, while visiting Mario, Princess Toadstool was picking flowers in his
garden...

  Suddenly, out if nowhere, Bowser appeared and grabbed the Princess again!
(Like you'd expect anyone else to?)

  Mario hearing Toadstool's scream for help, sped off alone towards Bowser's
Keep.

  After Mario trounced Bowser for the millionth time (this time they fought on
one of Bowser's chandeliers), everyone felt Bowser's Keep beginning to shake!

  A HUGE sword crashed into Bowser's Keep! What's gonna happen now?

 =-   3)   CONTROLS                                                       -=
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
This section lists the game's controls.

D-Pad   ->  Move Mario

A       ->  Talk
            Confirm selection
            Select Attack in battle

B       ->  Jump
            Cancel selection
            Select Etc. in battle

X       ->  Open menu
            Select Item in battle

Y       ->  (Hold) Run
            Select Special in battle

L       ->  Switch between Mono/Stereo in file selection screen

R       ->  Same as L

Start   ->  Start game

Select  ->  Same as L

 =-   4)   CHARACTERS                                                     -=
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
This section contains info on the characters and their abilities.

<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   MARIO                                                                >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  The main character and hero of the game. You must always have him in your
party throughout the entire game. Mario is a fairly good fighter, armed with
his Lazy Shell, he can cause some major damage! Also his Jump specials are
very effective if you can time it right. So, he's basically balanced in attack
and specials.

-----------------------
Weapons with Timed Hits
-----------------------
Punch - Press A before you hit the enemy for another punch.
Hammer - Before he swings the hammer, hit A for another hit.
Shell - Just before the shell hits his foot, press A.

--------
Specials
--------
Jump
Cost: 3 FP
Description: Jump on an enemy. Hit Y before hitting for more damage.

Fire Orb
Cost: 5 FP
Description: Throw fireballs at an enemy. Press Y rapidly to throw more.

Super Jump
Cost: 7 FP
Description: Jump multiple times on an enemy. Press Y before landing to
             execute another jump. If you can keep timing this correctly, you
             can practically jump forever!

Super Flame
Cost: 9 FP
Description: Throw larger fireballs at an enemy. Press Y rapidly to throw more
             of them.

Ultra Jump
Cost: 11 FP
Description: Just like Super Jump, but it jumps on a random enemy.

Ultra Flame
Cost: 14 FP
Description: Just like Super Flame, but it has larger fireballs and randomly
             hits an enemy.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   MALLOW                                                               >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  Supposedly a "frog," Mallow was raised by Frogfucius. Later on, he learns a
terrifying secret about his past. His weather-related Specials are very useful
throughout most of the game. And he can read an enemy's current HP, which can
help you decide which attack you should use next. Mallow isn't really much of
a fighter, but he really shines with his special attacks!

-----------------------
Weapons with Timed Hits
-----------------------
Punch - Like Mario, hit A before hitting for another punch.
Stick - Press A before swinging the stick for another hit.
Cymbal - Just before they come together for some more 'music'!

--------
Specials
--------
Thunderbolt 
Cost: 2 FP
Description: Hit all enemies with thunder. Hit Y before ending to cause more
             damage.

HP Rain
Cost: 2 FP
Description: Restore HP of an ally. Press Y before ending to recover more Hit
             Points.

Psychopath
Cost: 1 FP
Description: Read an enemy's current HP and what they are thinking. Press Y
             when the cloud appears to read their thoughts.

Shocker
Cost: 8 FP
Description: Big bolt of lightning hits an enemy. Press Y when it hits to
             cause more damage.

Snowy
Cost: 12 FP
Description: A big snowman lands on all of the enemies. Rotate the Control Pad
             to create more damage.

Star Rain
Cost: 14 FP
Description: A giant star hits all enemies. Press Y before it hits so the star
             bounces on the enemy again.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   GENO                                                                 >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  Coming from above, Geno takes control of Gaz's doll. He is helping Mario
collect the Star Pieces so he can repair the Star Road. His base strength is
pretty high, and a few of his specials are quite useful.

-----------------------
Weapons with Timed Hits
-----------------------
Punch - Like the others, hit A before punching.
Gun - Press A before firing for more damage.

--------
Specials
--------
Geno Beam
Cost: 3 FP
Description: Fire a beam of energy at an enemy. Hold Y until 3 stars appear 
             for more damage.

Geno Boost
Cost: 4 FP
Description: Very useful! Boosts attack power of an ally, hit Y after the
             arrows disappear to boost defense.

Geno Whirl
Cost: 8 FP
Description: Time it right and normal enemies take 9,999 HP damage! Of course,
             it doesn't work against bosses.

Geno Blast
Cost: 12 FP
Description: This attack works well against undead baddies. Hold Y until 3
             stars appear to cause more damage.

Geno Flash
Cost: 16 FP
Description: Another cool attack with a light show! Do the 3 stars thing again
             for more damage.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   BOWSER                                                               >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  Can it be!? Bowser is fighting with Mario! Bowser lets the team "join" his
Koopa Troop so he can get his keep back from Smithy. Bowser is a strong
fighter, but he has the lowest magic power, so you don't use his Specials very
much. Equipping him with the Jinx Belt makes him an even better because it
boosts attack power.

-----------------------
Weapons with Timed Hits
-----------------------
Claw - Hit A before swiping at an enemy for another hit.
Chomp - Just before throwing it, hit A.
Hurly Glove - Press A before picking up the doll.

--------
Specials
--------
Terrorize
Cost: 6 FP
Description: Big Boo comes and can cause Fear to some enemies. Rotate the pad
             for higher chance of success.

Poison Gas
Cost: 10 FP
Description: Toxic gas appears on the enemies and can cause Poison. Rotate the
             Control Pad for a higher chance.

Crusher
Cost: 12 FP
Description: A sharp piece of rock appears under an enemy. Press Y before it 
             appears for more damage.

Bowser Crush
Cost: 16 FP
Description: A giant Mecha Koopa stomps on all enemies. Hit Y rapidly for more
             damage.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   TOADSTOOL                                                            >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  The princess from the Mushroom Kingdom decides to join Mario on his quest. 
She is very good to have in battle because of her healing specials. Her attack
power is pretty low, so I would recommend giving her the Lazy Shell armor to
boost her defense.

-----------------------
Weapons with Timed Hits
-----------------------
Glove - Press A before slapping for another hit.
War Fan/Parasol - Press A before swinging for another hit.
Pan - Before swinging, press A for ANOTHER hit! (What a surprise... =P)

--------
Specials
--------
Therapy
Cost: 2 FP
Description: Restores HP and Status of an ally. Press Y before it ends for
             more HP.

Group Hug
Cost: 4 FP
Description: Same as Therapy but on all allies! The healing amount is slightly
             lower than Therapy though.

Sleepy Time
Cost: 4 FP
Description: Puts some enemies to sleep. Rotate the pad for a higher chance.

Come Back
Cost: 2 FP
Description: Revives and restores 1/2 HP of fallen ally. Press Y when the star
             hits the character for full HP.

Mute
Cost: 3 FP
Description: Can Mute enemies. Rotate the Control Pad for a longer effect.

Pysch Bomb
Cost: 15 FP
Description: Press Y rapidly to drops tons of bombs on all enemies!


=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
 =-   5)   WALKTHROUGH                                                    -=
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
This section guides you through the game.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   BOWSER'S KEEP                                                        >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  Make your way through the keep until you reach the room filled with
Terrapins. Jump and dodge you way through them and at the door you fight four
of them. One punch is enough to take one out, so you shouldn't have much of a
problem. In the room with the chandeliers, you can find Bowser and the
Princess tied up. When you fight him, aim for the chain called Kinklink. Hit
it a few times, and it will lose grip of the chandelier. Just when Mario is
about to get the Princess, a giant sword appears and lands in the keep! This
sends Bowser and Toadstool in different directions. Mario ends up back at his
house...


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   MARIO'S PAD                                                          >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  Talk to Toad and save your game before revisiting Bowser's Keep. The giant
sword that landed in the keep destroys the bridge! Now what will Mario do? Go
back to his house and talk to Toad. He mentions that you should visit the
Chancellor at Mushroom Kingdom. Go to the exit and Mario bumps into Toad. He
teaches you about Items and Timed Hits and he gives you 4 Mushrooms. Exit the
area and head to Mushroom Way.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   MUSHROOM WAY                                                         >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  This is a good area to practice your skills in battle. Along the way, you
can find Toad trapped by an enemy. Free him by fighting the enemy and doing 
so gets you a reward. If you need to rest, visit Mario's house. At the end of 
the area, Toad is trapped by the Hammer Bros. Before fighting them, build your
levels to 2 or 3 and then take them on!


=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
BOSS: Hammer Bros.                                                     HP: 50

  You face two of them. Two Jump specials can take one out, so use it on one
of 'em. The remaining Brother will use "Valor Up" which increases his defense.
Use any FP leftover on the Jump attack, then bash him with normal ones. If
your HP gets low, use a Mushroom.

EXP: 6                       Coins: 20                      Items: Flower Jar
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=


  Once you defeat them, a Hammer is left on the ground. Equip it to boost
Mario's attack by 10, then head into the kingdom.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   MUSHROOM KINGDOM                                                     >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  When you enter here, go to the Item Shop for some things and talk to the
people. Then go to the castle where Toad is waiting for you and follow him to
the Chancellor. The Chancellor gives you a Map and tells you to go to the
vault. Talk to the guard and he teaches you about Coins, Flower Points, and
Mushrooms found in treasure boxes. 

  Exit the castle, and a crocodile steals a coin from someone. Talk to the 
person he stole it from to find out that it's Mallow from Tadpole Pond. Mario
joins him to get the coin back from Croco. Go to the shop and get the armor
for Mallow. Then talk to the guard who keeps jumping. He says he saw the
person who stole the coin, and then Croco runs out of the kingdom. Go and
follow him to Bandit's Way.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   BANDIT'S WAY                                                         >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  You chase Croco throughout this area. This is the first area where you use
this level up trick! Go where there is a star in a treasure box but don't use 
it. Save before using it, then you can open the box and get the star. Once 
you are done using the star, die in battle. Then you appear back where you 
saved but with all your levels and EXP! Also the star will still be in the
box. So you can do this as much as you like. Use the trick here until your
levels are at least 4. In the last area, Croco goes into hiding. Sneak up
behind him three times and you fight him.


=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
BOSS: Croco                                                           HP: 320

  He has a weakness to fire, so use Mario's Fire Orb to cause some damage.
When you hit him with fire, he doesn't attack for a turn or two. Have Mallow
stay in Defend and have him heal, and use items when needed. His bomb attacks
cause 10+ damage, so watch out! When his HP is low, he uses "Weird Mushroom"
which recovers 60 HP. Eventually, Mallow punches Croco and gets the coin back.

EXP: 8                        Coins: 10                     Items: Flower Tab
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=


  Mallow now has his Frog Coins back! You also find a Wallet left behind by
Croco. Hop on the Warp Trampoline and go to Mushroom Kingdom.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   MUSHROOM KINGDOM                                                     >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  When you get back, a dark cloud has appeared in the area and Shysters are
scaring everybody! Defeat the Shyster chasing the man near where you came in
and give him his Wallet. He gives a Flower Tab in return. For another Tab,
enter the house to the far left from where you came in and beat the Shysters
inside. The family gives you the item. 

  After eliminating the Shysters, go into the castle. Save Toad and he goes 
into the next room. Beat the enemies there and next room after that. Then you
get another Flower Tab and he tells you that the Chancellor is still in the
throne room! Go back to the Vault and take the items there. Go into the nearby
room and talk to the person there. He gives you a Wake Up Pin. Now, build your
levels to about 5 or 6 and then head to the throne room. There you meet Mack
which is the person causing the havoc around here. Since he doesn't want you
to stop him, you have to deal with him!


=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
BOSS: Mack / Bodyguard                                           HP: 480 / 30

  This guy isn't that hard. Use Mallow's Thunderbolt to get rid of his minions
for a little while, then have Mario use Super Jump on him once if you have it.
Have Mario do regular attacks while Mallow heals/uses items. His Bodyguards
come back eventually, but now as soon you get rid of them, they come back so
just ignore them.

EXP: 12                       Coins: 20                           Items: None
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=


  Once you beat him, you get a Star Piece, which you learn more about later.
Mallow suggests going to his Grandpa in Tadpole Pond, but first head back to
the Item Shop to get the item Mallow wanted earlier with his Frog Coin. Then
leave and head to Kero Sewers.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   KERO SEWERS                                                          >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  This area introduces swimming and using pipes. Enter the pipe and save your
game. This area is confusing, so listen carefully. From the Save Block, go
into the nearby pipe in that room. From that one, go down and enter the pipe
there. From there, go right and jump over the Rat Funk that's on the ledge and
enter that pipe. In that room, hit the "!" Switch to drain the water. Go back
through the pipe you came and enter the pipe that was underwater. Now you
should be in the area that was above the "!" Switch. Enter that pipe and you
land right in front of Belome. Before fighting him, build your levels to
about 7. 

  If you search the pipe near the underwater one, you can find a treasure box 
with an enemy in it called Pandorite. Beating him will get you a Trueform Pin, 
which will help against Belome. Equip the pin with Mario, and go fight Belome.


=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
BOSS: Belome                                                  HP: 500

  Beat him like the other bosses. He's weak to Thunder, so use as much as you
can. At one point in battle, he uses the "S'crow Funk" attack which turns
Mario into a scarecrow if you don't have the Trueform Pin equipped! Then he
swallows Mallow, turning the battle into a one-on-one fight. Use Mario's
normal and special attacks and use a Mushroom if his HP is low. Eventually,
he spits Mallow back out, ending the battle.

EXP: 15                   Coins: 40                       Items: None
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=


  When you beat him, water comes gushing out...sending the team into the Midas
River mini-game. Two caves on the waterfall contain a Flower in them. After
completing the waterfall, you go the barrel jumping event. You don't need to
collect a lot of coins for now, but you can if you want. For more info on this
mini-game, check out the "Mini-Games" section. After beating that, the guy
there gives you a Noknok Shell, so equip it now and head to Tadpole Pond.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   TADPOLE POND                                                         >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  Go straight ahead and step on the stone block to talk to the tadpole. Then
Frogfucius comes. Go across the pond to his sanctuary and talk to him. He
explains about what happened at the keep earlier and about someone named
Smithy who causing the trouble around the world. Then he says that Mallow
isn't a tadpole and now he must search for his real parents. Afterwards, talk
to him again and hand over the Cricket Pie from the Mushroom Kingdom. He gives
you Mallow's first weapon, the Froggie Stick. Before you go, head over to
right to visit Melody Bay. Toadofsky needs your help with his music, so play
this tune:


                 ------------------------------------------
                          (3)
                 ----------------(4)---------(6)-------(8)-
                                      (5)       (7)
                 ------(2)---------------------------------

                 -(1)--------------------------------------

                 ------------------------------------------


  Playing this melody will get an Alto Card, which can get you more items in
the Juice Bar in this area. Now leave and go to Rose Way!


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   ROSE WAY                                                             >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  In the first area you use the yellow tiles to move around. Going to the
upper-left area, you can get a Frog Coin in a Treasure Box. Use the tiles to
go to the upper-right area. After passing through there, use these tiles to
get to the lower-right section. Here you can find Shyguys on top of Treasure
Boxes. Four of them contain 5 coins while the other one has a mushroom in it.
If you exit and come back to this section, the chests are refilled! In the
next area, you find Bowser and his Koopa Troop. Bowser can't get back into his
castle, but he's trying to find a way. Continue on and enter Rose Town.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   ROSE TOWN                                                            >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  Looks like someone is busy shooting arrows at the townspeople. But these 
arrows freeze the person they hit! Enter the Inn to find a kid playing with
his toy Mario, Bowser and Toadstool dolls. Mario decides to play with him. Gaz
ends up shooting Mario with the gun from the Geno doll, causing Mario to
collapse and waking up in bed. Meanwhile, a spirit takes control of the Geno
doll and leaves the town. Gaz says he saw Geno go into Forest Maze!

  Before you go, visit the shops and buy some supplies. In the corner of the
town, a man is left out of his house. Jump on his head and go in the house 
and hit the "!" Switch. If you want, you can take the 2 Flowers in the boxes.
The man tells to go Left, Left, Straight, Right in the "maze" area of the
forest. Now exit and go to Forest Maze.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   FOREST MAZE                                                          >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  Throughout this area are Mushrooms, you can pick up some to use in upcoming
battles. But some come to life and attack you! Here's another neat trick. When
you enter here, go to the first Wiggler. If you can jump on him 10 times (not
in battle), you get one Frog Coin along with 10 coins! Keep going through the
forest until you reach an area with a bunch of tree trunks. Enter the one at
the far end and jump on the sleeping Wiggler. Now you have access to another
area. Keep going until you reach a Save Block. Now save your game and enter
the maze area. If you follow the directions you were told at Rose Town, you
can find a hidden entrance to a treasure room. BUT follow the directions from
Mario's perspective! For more info on it, check the "Secrets" section. Follow
Geno through this area. 

  After following him, you should be where the guy shooting the arrows is.
Before you go in there, build your levels to least 8. When you enter, one of
Bowyer's Flunkies finds a Star and Geno comes in. They fight and Mario and
Mallow decide to help Geno out.


=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
BOSS: Bowyer                                                          HP: 720

  Use Geno's special attack for now while Mario should use normal attacks and
Mallow does the usual. Then Bowyer has trick up his sleeve...when an arrow
hits Y, X or A, you can't use that command! Geno is pretty weak at this point,
so watch his HP level.

EXP: 20                        Coins: 50                     Item: Flower Box
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=


  Now, Geno explains about the Star Pieces and Star Road. He joins you to get
all the stars back so he can repair Star Road. Before spending 50 coins on a
weapon for Geno, go to Rose Town and talk to Gaz. He gives the Finger Shot for
him. Now head to Pipe Vault.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   PIPE VAULT & YO'STER ISLAND                                          >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  Going through this area allows you to reach Yo'ster Isle, home of the
Yoshis! Here, you can play a Goomba Thumping game. You can play if you want a
Flower Tab or Jar. Beware of Chompweeds in the area, you lose 2 coins each
time you are hit! Once get through here, you get to the island. Mario meets
his old pal, Yoshi. Talk to him and hop on. Now, you need to race Boshi. Talk
to the pink Yoshi near where you came in and he gives you some Yoshi Cookies.
Talk to Boshi so you can race him. To gain speed in the Mushroom Derby, you
need to alternate A and B to the beat of the music. 

  After beating him, all the Yoshis are able to race together, and Yoshi gives
you 3 Yoshi Cookies. Time to move onto Moleville.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   MOLEVILLE                                                            >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  As usual, hit the shops and buy supplies. Go to the mountain to see Bowser
and his very small Koopa Troop. Then talk to the moles at the mountain. They
say that a star crashed into the mountain and trapped two kids! Go inside and
enter the coal mines. Hop on the trampoline inside and Mario faints. Croco
then steals all your coins! Chase him through the area. Before fighting him,
fight his minions and after each battle you get a Flower Tab. When you are
ready, keep chasing him and run into him.


=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=

it lowers his attack by 10 if you have the Lazy Shell equipped. Make your way
through the area until you reach a boss called Countdown. You can build your
levels to about 25 or higher if you want, then fight him.


=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
BOSS: Countdown / Ding-A-Ling                                 HP: 2400 / 1200

  Focus your attacks on Countdown so you won't have to put up with his magic.
Do the usual, have the two characters attack and Toadstool heals. The attack
you need to worry about is the Fear Roulette from Ding-A-Ling, which can
instantly kill an ally! Once the clock is done, go after the Ding-A-Lings.

EXP: 20                          Coins: 0                         Items: None
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=


  As you continue through the factory, you can find the "Machine-Made"
versions of some bosses. They sometimes drop a good item after beating them in
battle, and they are a great way to earn EXP! Once you reach the room with the
machine-made Yaridovich, fight him. After you win, the block he was on
disappears. When you jump down the hole you face another boss.


=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
BOSS: Cloaker / Domino                                         HP: 1200 / 900

  Each of them is strong in an area. Cloaker is strong with regular attacks 
while Domino is strong in magic. For now, keep attacking Domino and then
Cloaker runs to Earth Link. Focus your attacks on the snake instead of
Cloaker. Do the same thing for the past bosses. Both of their attacks can
cause 50+ to someone, so watch what you do. You should really worry about the
snake's Carni-Kiss, which causes 150+ to an ally! Once you defeat Earth Link,
Cloaker goes down with him.

EXP: 40                         Coins: 0                          Items: None
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=


  Now you can head into the Factory. Now is a good time to build your levels
to 30 because the Yaridoviches give you 60 EXP and 50 Coins. I'll let you do
the rest of the game because it's just a series of bosses. In the Factory,
you'll fight the Clerk, Manager, Director, Factory Director and Gunyolk. Then
to the final battle with Smithy. Use everything you know against the bosses
and good luck!

 =-   9)   MINI-GAMES                                                     -=
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
This section contains information on the mini-games.

<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   MIDAS WATERFALL & BARREL JUMPING                                     >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
Location: Midas River

  Your objective is to collect as many coins as you can on the waterfall and
the barrel jumping event. The coins you grab are used to get Frog Coins. The
more you get, the lower the price becomes. If you can get 100 coins, the 
price becomes 50 and you get 5 Frog Coins as a bonus. The downside is...the 
coins you get, you don't get to keep! BUT you can keep the coins by doing 
this: Go to Moleville and buy Fireworks. Trade Fireworks for a Shiny Stone, 
then talk to one little mole in the house to trade for a Carbo Cookie. Give 
the cookie to the mole on the bucket. Go down the bucket and into Midas River!


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   GOOMBA THUMPING                                                      >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
Location: Pipe Vault

  For 10 Coins, you must jump on as many Goombas as possible. Here is how the
scoring works:

Goomba - 1 Point
Gold Goomba - 3 Points
Spikey - Lose 1 Point

  The strategy I use would is, stand in the middle and jump on the Goombas
that appear on the pipes to the left and right of Mario. If you get a certain
number of points, the mole gives you a prize.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   DERBY                                                                >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
Location: Yo'ster Isle

  You can play this game whenever you want once you help Yoshi. When you want
to race, talk to Boshi and make sure you have some Yoshi Cookies. You wager
some cookies and you get some more if you win. At the race, alternate A and B
to the beat of the music playing. If you have any cookies left over from
wagering, press Y and Yoshi gets a short burst of speed. The more you win, the
harder the race becomes.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   TROLLEY RIDE                                                         >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
Location: Moleville

  In this mini-game, you can earn some extra coins if you break your record
time. You can bet 10 to 30 coins, and you get more coins if you win. On the
ride, use the brakes to get past sharp turns so you won't go off the track.
You can find Mushrooms on the tracks which are used as a speed boost. To make
this much easier, go very slow on the first time so breaking the record won't
be difficult.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   BOOSTER HILL RUN & BEETLE RUN                                        >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
Location: Booster Hill

  On the 1st time, you can get lots of FP. Use the barrels and Snifits to move
ahead to touch the Princess, doing so gets you one Flower Point. Make sure you
do your best because this the only time when you can do this! Once you get the
Beetle Box from "Beetles Are Us" in Seaside Town, you can revisit Booster Hill
to earn some extra cash. Again, use the barrels and Snifits to move ahead and
collect the Beetles. 

  Here is what you can get:

Female Beetle -> 1 Coin
Male Beetle   -> 50 Coins
Gold Beetle   -> 1 Frog Coin


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   MUSHROOM BOY'S SHOP                                                  >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
Location: Seaside Town

  If you can bring him some Mushrooms that grow in the Forest Maze, you can
collect some goodies. Go to the forest and collect as many as you can. Give
them to Mushroom Boy and you can get some of the following:

Regular Mushroom -> Nothing
Berry Mushroom   -> Maple Syrup
Rippin' Mushroom -> Rock Candy
Flower Mushroom  -> Flower Tab


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   DONUT BRIDGE                                                         >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
Location: Land's End

  You can also earn some more coins and Frog Coins here. You cross the Donut
Bridge while dodging the Bullet Bills. The higher the difficulty, you face
more challenges, but the bigger the payoff. If you select "Special", you can
earn a Frog Coin but it's much harder than the other difficulties. For Normal
and Expert, you can earn coins.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   TROOPA SHELL CLIFF SCALING                                           >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
Location: Land's End

  Here, you jump shell to shell to scale the cliff. This the easiest way to
earn Frog Coins and it's free! The first time you get under about 11.50,
Sargent Flutter gives you a Troopa Pin, but each time afterwards you get one
Frog Coin. If you get under 11 seconds, you get 5 Frog Coins! You can get tons
of Frog Coins if you are willing to take the time to do so.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   GRATE GUY'S CASINO                                                   >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
Location: Entrance found in Bean Valley

Here you can play 3 different games!

---------
Blackjack
---------
  You wager 1 Frog Coin for this game. The objective is to get closest to 21
without going over. The dealer will give you and himself 2 cards. You can get
more cards, but try not to go over 21! If the dealer wins, you lose the Frog
Coin, you win...you get 2 Frog Coins.

------------
Slot Machine
------------
  Not a hard game to learn. You use 10 coins, and if you get 3 of the same
symbols, you win a Frog Coin. If you lose, you get nothing.

------------------
Look The Other Way
------------------
  Keep talking to Grate Guy and he'll play this game with you. Press <- or ->
on the pad, and if you look the other way where his finger is, you get an
item. If you lose, you don't gain or lose anything. Sometimes he gives you
some good items, but you usually get junk like Moldy Mush and Wilt Shrooms.
Play this game 100 times to get the best item in the game, the Star Egg!


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   BEETLE MANIA                                                         >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
Location: Boy from Mushroom Kingdom

  You can purchase this Game Boy game from the kid at the Inn if you are
willing to spend a mere 500 coins. You can buy this once you defeat
Yaridovich. You control a little brown beetle that can shoot stars at the
incoming Troopa Shells. When you hit a shell with a star, the star explodes
and little stars fly to a certain distance. This usually hits your beetle, 
and when you get hit, press A rapidly to revive him. If you can grab a Heart,
your beetle's life is restored. 

To get a high score, try the following technique:

  Let a bunch of shells come on screen, then fire some stars. The explosions
will hit other shells and do the same thing. You can wipe out mostly all the
shells with a few stars! You can usually earn 500+ points after doing this.
You don't anything for beating the high score, but it's a fun game to try.

 =-   10)   SECRETS                                                       -=
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
This section contains secrets and other things about the game.

<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   BUILD LEVELS EASILY                                                  >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  I mentioned this trick in the beginning of the walkthrough. First, go to an
area with a star in a treasure box. Make sure you save somewhere close to the
area. Now, get the star and hit as many enemies as you can. Then, die in
battle and you return to where you saved with all your EXP! You can repeat the
trick until you get your levels to where you want them.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   DIZZY MARIO                                                          >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  At Mushroom Kingdom, you can find a girl running in a circle. If you can
manage to jump on her head, Mario will jump off and faint after a while. Not
much, but it's sorta funny.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   FOREST BOOTY                                                         >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  Remember those directions, Left, Left, Straight, Right from the guy in Rose
Town? If you are lost on what he is saying, listen up. At the "maze" area of
Forest Maze, follow those direction from Mario's perspective, not from where
you are. If you do so, you can find a tree trunk in the room. Go down to find
the following in Treasure Boxes: 1 Flower, 1 Mushroom, 1 Frog Coin, and two
other boxes containing Coins.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   RARE ITEM SHOP                                                       >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  After you save Dyna & Mite, the man from the mine opens an item shop. At
different points in the game, he finds new items. He can sell you a Lucky
Jewel, Mystery Egg, and Frying Pan. The Frying Pan may sound useless, but it's
actually Toadstool's best weapon, Metal Plate.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   STAR WISHES                                                          >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  On Star Hill, you can find some stars with a face on them. If you talk to
them, you read someone's wish. Most of them come from characters in the game
like Mallow, Croco, and Belome. There is also one with Mario's brother, Luigi!


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   PIPE PEEPING                                                         >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  Once you beat Jonathan Jones, return to the Sunken Ship. Look into the pipe
in the room with Jonathan Jones in it. Now you can look into other areas of
the ship.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   LAZY SHELL WEAPON & ARMOR                                            >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  The gardener in Rose Town is looking for the legendary Seed and Fertilizer.
You can get the Fertilizer once you beat Valentina. Go to the far right of the
town, and you can walk off the ground to a hidden place. Talk to the Shy Away
and he gives you the item. You can get the Seed once you beat Megasmilax. Read
the piece of paper that floated down and take the Seed. Now go to Rose Town,
and go past the Item Shop to the gardener's house. Give him the items and he
grows a beanstalk. Climb up to get the Lazy Shell weapon for Mario and the
Lazy Shell armor for anybody!


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   LINK CATCHING ZOME Z'S                                               >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  Sleep at the Inn at Rose Town. When Mario wakes up, Link is there sleeping!
He doesn't talk to you, but if you try, the game plays a familiar Zelda sound.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   THE THREE MUSTY FEARS                                                >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  When you rest in the bed at Monstro Town, they play a game with you called
"Find the Flag". Each of them hides a flag somewhere. And here are the
locations...

#1: Find it under Mario's bed at Mario's Pad.
#2: This one is behind the wooden flower at Rose Town.
#3: Find it between the O and A in the word GOAL at Yo'ster Isle.

  Rest again at the bed and the Three Musty Fears give you a Ghost Medal.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   RAZ & RAINI'S YOSHI HONEYMOON                                        >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  When you first reach Yo'ster Isle, look in the mailbox to find a honeymoon
reservation at the island. Return there later to find Raz and Raini on their
honeymoon!


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   THE BRIGHT CARD                                                      >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  To get into Grate Guy's Casino, you need a Bright Card. To get it, return to
Booster Tower to find Knife Guy inside. Talk to him to play a game. You guess
which hand the yellow ball is. Win this game about 16 times, each loss
decreases your win by 1, to get the Bright Card. Don't sell it because you can
NEVER get another one!


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   WIGGLER'S TREASURE                                                   >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  In Forest Maze, if you can jump on a Wiggler 10 times (Not in battle,
though), you get one Frog Coin. You can keep on doing this for more Coins and
Frog Coins.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   FIREWORKS FINALE                                                     >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  When you get the ending, the mole from Moleville shoots some fireworks. The
more you bought from, a different shape appears. Here is what you can get.

0: Mushroom
1: Mushroom
2: Mushroom
3: Flower
4: Flower
5: Star


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   BOOSTER'S BRIDE SEARCH                                               >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  When you go to Booster Tower after beating Valentina, you can find her at
the balcony with Booster! Will history repeat itself?


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   CULEX, THE DARK NIGHT OF VANDA                                       >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  Remember that sealed door in Monstro Town? Well, this is when you can open 
it. To open it, you must get a Shiny Stone. Go to Moleville and purchase a
Fireworks. Now trade the Fireworks at the little mole's Pur-Tend Store for 
the Shiny Stone. Use the stone to open the door. Before you go in, make sure 
your levels are 25+ and bring plenty of Max Mushrooms, Royal Syrup, Pick Me 
Ups, a Crystalline, a Power Blast, Rock Candy, Kerokero Colas and a Star Egg 
if you got it. Also, put Toadstool in your party equipped with the Lazy Shell 
Armor and a Safety Ring to protect her from most magic attacks. Then enter 
the door and fight Culex...


=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
BOSS : Culex / Fire Crystal / Earth Crystal     HP: 4096 / 2500 /
Water Crystal / Wind Crystal                        3200 / 1800 / 800

  Final Fantasy fans should recognize the battle music here! Anyway, as you
can see from HP levels, these guys are very hard. Use the Crystalline and
Power Blast to make the battle somewhat easier. If you have the Star Egg, keep
using that and heal when needed. With that item, you can cause 100+ damage to
ALL of them! If you don't have the Star Egg, use Rock Candy or any bombs you
might have. You have some choices to complete this battle: You can use all of
your attacks on Culex and the Crystals disappear when you beat him, but you
have to put up the attacks from the Crystals. Or you can get rid of the
Crystals and then attack Culex, but this way you may use a lot of time and
items. Some of the attacks can inflict 100+ to all characters, so be very
careful! Each of the Crystals have their own "element" to them. For example,
the Fire Crystal attacks with fire.

EXP: 600               Coins: 0                   Items: Quartz Charm
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=


  When you win, he gives you a Quartz Charm. Exit the door and it vanishes
forever!


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   DREAM CUSHION GIFT                                                   >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  When you rest at Nimbus Land a few times, use the Dream Cushion. Sometime or
later, Mario dreams that Toad turned into a monster! When he wakes up, he sees
Toad and gets scared. Toad gives him a Red Essence to make him feel better.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   8-BIT MARIO                                                          >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  Remember what Mario looked like in Mario Bros. for the NES? Now you can be
him! Go to Booster Tower and go to the room with the curtains in front of a
corner. Go behind the curtains and when you come out, he becomes the 8-Bit
Mario! The game also plays some slightly changed music from Mario Bros. But
when you leave the room, he goes back to normal and you can't do this again...


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   JUMPING CONTEST                                                      >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  Talk to the Chow at Monstro Town and he says he will give you a prize for
jumping 30 times using the Super Jump. The key to getting those jumps is the
timing. When you jump a certain number of times, the timing changes. If you
jump 30 times, he gives you an Attack Scarf. Jump 100 times and he gives you
a Super Jacket.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   LOOK THE OTHER WAY PRIZE                                             >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  At Grate Guy's Casino, keep talking to Grate Guy to play this game. Play it
100 times, just playing, NOT winning, to get the best item in the game, the
Star Egg! It causes damage to all enemies and it can never be used up!


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   JINX & JAGGER                                                        >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  At Monstro Town, go through the door that has a "J" sign beside it. Inside
is an old friend of Bowser's, Jagger. Fight him and Jinx appears. If you can
beat him in the three rounds, he gives you a Jinx Belt. Make sure your
character's levels are at least 25. Equip Toadstool with the Lazy Shell and
the Safety Ring to protect her. After each round, Jinx is stronger, and he has
more attacks. A couple of his attacks can cause Instant Death, so use a Pick
Me Up or Come Back.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   BABY YOSHI                                                           >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  Return to Yo'ster Island later in the game to find one of the eggs hatched.
Talk to the Yoshi nearby if you don't have any Cookies and he gives you three
of them. Find them to the baby and keep repeating. The baby gives you an item
such as Yoshi-Ade for feeding him!


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   UNLIMITED COINS                                                      >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  Once you beat Magikoopa, he creates a Gold Treasure Box. Now you can keep
hitting it to get 999 coins and it never runs out!


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   SUITE SWEETS                                                         >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  Resting at the Suite at the Marrymore Hotel can get you some valuable items.
But each stay costs 200 coins. Here is what you can get.

1 Stay  -> Flower Tab
3 Stays -> Flower Jar
5 Stays -> 1 Frog Coin


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   MARRIAGE MANIA                                                       >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  When you collect the wedding gear for Toadstool at Marrymore, the reward
changes. If you collect them and talk to Booster when two pairs of candles 
are lighted, Toadstool kisses Mario. If get them with more candles lighted,
Booster and Bowser kiss Mario.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   DAMAGE DEDUCTION                                                     >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  Did you know you can decrease damage by 3/4? First, have the character
defend. Then when he/she is attacked, do the timed defend (press B before 
hit). The damage is decreased by 3/4 instead of 1/2! This doesn't work all of
the time, though.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   THE ULTIMATE TEAM                                                    >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  If you are not sure what team to use on the last few bosses of the game,
here is a party that I would recommend:

-----
Mario
-----
Weapon    -> Lazy Shell
Armor     -> Hero Shirt
Accessory -> Safety Badge

---------
Toadstool
---------
Weapon    -> Frying Pan
Armor     -> Lazy Shell
Accessory -> Safety Ring

------
Bowser
------
Weapon    -> Drill Claw
Armor     -> Heal Shell
Accessory -> Jinx Belt

  If you want, Geno is another great pick instead of Bowser!


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   TREASURE BOX HUNT                                                    >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  In Monstro Town, a Pandorite tells you about hidden treasure boxes. To find
one, you have to jump in a certain area. If you want to get them, equipping
the Signal Ring can really help. The downside to this is that the Pandorite
doesn't give you anything for finding them.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   THE MYSTERY OF THE B'TUB RING                                        >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  Ever wondered what that accessory was for? Here is what is does...

  First, get the ring and the Mystery Egg from the Rare Item Shop. Now, equip
Toadstool with the ring. Go into battle and have the princess use the egg 10
times. Then it changes into Lamb's Lure. In the next battle, have anyone use
the item about 48 times. Now it changes into Sheep Attack. When you use this
weird item in battle, it can change most enemies into sheep and they run away.
It can never be used up, but if all enemies change, you get no EXP and Coins.


<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
<<<   UNLIMITED YOSHI COOKIES                                              >>>
<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  Later in the game at Yo'ster Isle when the baby appears, you can get all the
cookies you want! Talk to the Yoshi near the baby for three cookies. When you
run out, talk to him and you get three more. Now you can you get some free
items!

secret! With some of these cookies, you can transform enemies into great
items...for free! This list doesn't have every enemy, but it has a good bit of

NOTE: When you use a Yoshi Cookie and Yoshi fails to transform, he drops a

      transform the enemy.
