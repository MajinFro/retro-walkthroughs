River section: this is easier than it seems, you don't have to memorize everything, it's a simple pattern.
You start on the upper lane, you immidiately switch to the lower lane, than you stay in that lane for two set of coins and switch to the upper, and then you repeat this pattern until the end. only when you are in the upper lane you have to take the coin right before switching lanes. So the pattern goes:
-Lower (5)
-Lower (4)
-Lower (4)
-Upper (4)
with the number in brackets representing the number of coins you have to take.
Be careful when switching lanes because you will have to immidiately jump at the right time to take the coin, it will take a bit of practice to learn the timing but it shouldn't be too difficult.

[Cassievania] Took me 6 tries. I didn't get it until I understood the mechanics of the minecart.

Imagine the minecart has 5 speeds.
- Your default speed is 3.
- Tapping or holding the brakes reduces your speed by 1.
- Surviving a hairpin turn requires speed 2.
- A mushroom sets your speed to 5.
- To survive a hairpin turn after using a mushroom, you need to TAP the brake button 3 times.
- As soon as your turning, you've survived the turn and can use a mushroom again.

OVERHEAD 1
1. Jump to get the mushroom and immediately use it.
2. Hold Right to get the next mushroom.
3. Brake at the tight turns, then use a mushroom as you're coming out of the final turn. Start braking just before the end of the straightaway.
4. Hold right to go onto the next mushroom path, then as you're approaching the turn before the mushrooms, use a mushroom and immediately jump twice out of the turn to get the mushrooms. Start braking at the end of the straightaway.
5. As you enter the stretch of track with a side-mushroom, use a mushroom (skipping the side-mushroom). Start braking once you see the track touching your track.
6. Jump to get a mushroom and use it once you land. Do it again, then brake once at the turn at the end. You should have 1 mushroom remaining and be at no more than 0:50:00.

SIDESCROLLER 1
1. You only need to press X and B here, so with one hand, mash X to constantly spend all your mushrooms to go as fast as possible.
2. Jump as soon as you get the first mushroom.
3. Jump as the bottom track disappears to get another mushroom.
4. Wait through a set of 5 coins. At the end of a set of 4 coins, wait half a second and then jump up a track, and then jump as soon as you land.
5. Fall to the bottom track and wait until you pick up 2 mushrooms.
6. Look at the top of the screen. Jump up a track once you see a mushroom at the top. Jump again when you collect a mushroom. Stop mashing X when you have 1 mushroom left. You should finish below 1:05:00.

[Cassievania] OVERHEAD 2
1. Immediately use a mushroom, jump, brake, jump, use mushroom, then when you're nearing the hairpin turn, brake twice. (X, B, Y, B, X... Y, Y.)
2. Wait a second to get to the track with a side-mushroom. Boost past it, then start braking near the end.
3. Brake for tiny track, then boost. Brake at the end.
4. Hold right to get onto the right track. Wait for the track with the mushroom and then boost. Brake at BOTH of the next 2 turns.
5. Hold left and then jump to take the shortcut. Don't boost, just brake once at the tight left+right turn. Don't boost here either.
6. Boost on the next turn, then start braking when you see the turn.
7. Don't do anything except jump to grab the mushroom. Then jump and boost once you reach the pothole. At the end of the track, jump to grab the mushroom and DON'T brake.
8. At the last of the 3 turns, boost, then brake twice well before the end of the track. If you're below 1:50:00, you've got it even if you miss a jump in the next section. If you're over 1:56:00, start over.

SIDESCROLLER 2
1. Just like Sidescroller 1, mash X to constantly spend all your mushrooms. This section is a series of hills and valleys.
2. Jump when you're 1 support beam before the bottom of the first 3 valleys to get 2 mushrooms. (You got the right spot of you get all 3 coins in the first valley.)
3. Jump at the coins near the top of the 3rd hill to grab the another mushroom.
4. Mash X to the end.

[SteelCat] The trick I found is that before 14 jumps, the "extra frames" on the timing window are from the START of the window, not the end. So while on jumps 1-13 it is way more forgiving of early hits, it is still just as unforgiving of late hits as 14+, so to find the window for 14+ you need to try and be as late as possible on jumps 1-13 and find out exactly the point where it registers as an obvious late input (easier to see during 1-13 because the early window is more forgiving). On earlier jumps, you will FEEL when you are late, try to hone in on exactly when that happens.

Then make a note of where Mario's sprite is exactly just before that point, and time your hits to that sprite placement. Online guides that show visuals for this timing are not useful, as they're either using original hardware or an emulation setup that is different than yours, you gotta find your own timing AND a cue to make it consistent.
