## Hidden Treasure Chests

There are 39 hidden treasure chests scattered throughout the world of _Super Mario RPG_. A talking treasure chest in Monstro Town will inform you of how many are left to find. There is no reward for finding them all other than the items you obtain from the chests themselves. Chests without items will have "(empty)" in the Item section.

### Mushroom Kingdom/Bandit's Way

Chest Number| Map Location| Item| Where/How To Find  
---|---|---|---  
1| Mushroom Kingdom| Frog Coin| When you enter the palace at the start of the game, Toad will greet you and then run up the hall to tell the Chancellor. Quickly follow him and jump on his head before he exits through the door. Jump on top of the door to an invisible ledge, then jump until you get the treasure chest.  
2| Mushroom Kingdom| Flower| In the basement of the Item Shop. Follow the purple guy's instructions to find it.  
3| Mushroom Kingdom| Flower| Same as #2. Stand on purple guy's head and wait for him to go to the lower-right corner (diagonal from the boxes) and jump.  
4| Bandit's Way| KeroKeroCola| In the second screen of the area, use the spinning red flower and jump towards the exit.

## Treasure Rooms

There are two rooms in the game, accessible through special means, that are loaded with various treasures for Mario.

### Belome Temple Treasure Room

Start by going to Monstro Town and entering the second building from the left. There will be a Chow and a Thwomp sitting next to each other. Talk to the Thwomp multiple times; each time you talk to him, he will stomp the ground, bringing the key on the ledge outside closer and closer to the edge. After many "chats" with the Thwomp, go back outside. The key should have fallen down and you can run over it to pick it up.

Now go back to Belome Temple. The elevator that takes you to Belome requires you to read a fortune first. There are two fortunes you can receive here. If your fortune reads, "Mmm, I'm starving! Wish I had something to eat." then exit and re-enter the room. You want the second fortune, which reads: "Sorry, I'm not accepting visitors past my bedtime." When you get this fortune, ride the elevator down and enter the next room. Belome will be guarding the room. Give him the key you obtained in Monstro Town to gain access to the room. The room contains big Flowers, Frog Coins, and assorted items.

### Forest Maze Treasure Room

In Rose Town, when you press the button in the house on the cliff to let the guy back into his house, he will give you a tip: "go left, left, straight, right." These instructions refer to Mario's orientation on the screen. So go to the Forest Maze and when you get to the maze section where you followed Geno around:

  1. **Left** : go to the door in the upper-left
  2. **Left** : go to the door in the bottom-left
  3. **Straight** : go to the door in the bottom left again
  4. **Right** : go to the door in the upper-left



You'll find a stump that takes you to the treasure room. Inside the room is a bunch of treasure chests containing Coins, a Frog Coin, a Flower, and a Healing Mushroom.

## Get Rich Quick!

This section contains numerous ways to quickly accumulate coins and Frog Coins. Information in this section was contributed by gibcircle[at]yahoo[dot]com, Lord_rawnblade[at]hotmail[dot]com, and andyhuang155[at]yahoo[dot]com. Thanks again, everybody!

### Ten Frog Coins

To quickly obtain ten Frog Coins, go to Land's End and enter the cave beneath the Donut Block bridge. [(It's the same cave you enter to obtain Hidden Treasure Chest #27.)](hidden-treasure-chests#lands-end-and-surrounding-areas) Follow the path to where three barrels are stacked. There should be a treasure chest floating nearby. Get on top of the chest, then jump down onto the trampoline. You will emerge in Kero Sewers. Jump onto the platform southwest of the pipe. and hit the treasure chest to obtain an item called "Cricket Jam." Take this time to Frogfucius in Tadpole Pond and he will give you the Frog Coins as thanks.

### Frog Coin Grinding

There are two methods for grinding Frog Coins in Land's End. The first is in the areas with the whirlpools of sand. With good timing, you can jump on the ant that appears inside the whirlpools, netting 20 Coins and 1 Frog Coin for a complete cycle.

The second method is simply by replaying the Paratroopa Cliff Scaling game anytime after you scale the cliff for the first time. Scoring a time of 12 seconds or lower will net you a Frog Coin.

Another method similar to the Land's End whirlpool method is available in Forest Maze. In rooms with stumps, there are usually Wigglers popping in and out of the stumps. If you can hop onto one without triggering a fight and stay on him for ten jumps, you'll get a Frog Coin. This is easily repeatable and much safer than the ants in the whirlpools.

### A Ghost Of A Chance

A quick way to obtain lots of Coins is to fight ghost-type monsters (like the Boos in Kero Sewers, for example). Each time you defeat ghost enemies, you receive Pure Water, an item that can insta-kill any ghost-type enemies. (Nice of them to give you their kryptonite.) Pure Waters are easily grindable and sell for 75 coins apiece, making for an quick cash grab if you need to buy new armor or are saving up for the Beetlemania game.

## Special Weapons & Armor

This section will break down the unique and powerful weapons and armor obtainable in the game.

### The Super Jump Items

In Monstro Town, enter the second door on the left. The Chow in this building will keep track of how many Super Jumps you land on enemies. Super Jump is learned by Mario at level 6. The timing shifts at around jump #18, but otherwise it's fairly consistent to keep landing hits once you get going. You will automatically hop off an enemy if you hit 100 jumps.

After you achieve 30 Super Jumps, talk to the Chow to obtain the Attack Scarf, an accessory which greatly boosts Attack Power and can be worn by anyone. After 100 Super Jumps, talk to the Chow again and he'll give you the Super Suit, one of Mario's best armors.

### Ultimate Weapons

#### Lazy Shells

The Lazy Shell items are two very powerful tools to have when facing the last few bosses of the game. To gain access to them, you need two key items: the Seed and the Fertilizer.

The Seed is fairly hard to miss. After you defeat Smilax in Bean Valley, the Shy-Away drops what appears to be a piece of paper. If you "read" this paper, you'll find that it is actually the Seed. Take it.

Later, after you defeat Valentina, return to Nimbus Land. On the eastern (right-hand) side of the town, hug the wall until you walk right out into thin air. You'll find the Shy Away again, who will gladly give you the Fertilizer in exchange for letting him live.

Once you have both the Seed and the Fertilizer, go to Rose Town. There will be an exit to the northeast, which will take you to a section of town with just a single house. Enter the house and speak to the man inside. He's a farmer who has spent his life looking for the rare Seed and Fertilizer. Hand both of them over to him. After a manic speech or two, he'll plant the Seed in the giant pot in his house and fertilize it with the Fertilizer. This will cause a large vine to sprout up through his chimney and into the sky. Climb this vine to the top. You'll be in the clouds, where two chests containing the Lazy Shell weapon and armor await you.

The Lazy Shell weapon is Mario's most powerful weapon, with the same Critical Hit timing as the other shell weapons in the game. The Lazy Shell armor can be worn by anyone; it drastically decreases attack power but boosts defense to near-impenetrable levels.

## Cameos & Secret Bosses

This section of the guide details the location of both secret bosses as well as some special Nintendo character cameos.

### Jinx

In Monstro Town, climb the stairs near the Save Block to a door with a sign hanging outside painted with a "J". Enter the door. After a scene with Bowser and the Koopa in here (his name is Jagger), talk to him and fight. After you win the fight (Jagger isn't too difficult, just keep pressure on him and you'll have no trouble), Jinx will reveal himself as the master of this dojo. You can now talk to Jinx to challenge him to a fight.

Jinx has an average amount of health compared to most bosses, but the real danger is his attacks, which either deal high damage or are essentially one-hit KOs that can decimate a party in just a few turns. Having the Lazy Shell armor on a healer character (Peach is a good choice) helps a lot if you tackle him right when you get to Monstro Town. Geno's Geno Boost can also help punch through his high physical defense stat. NT220 of the GameFAQs message boards also notes that Jinx is susceptible to Jump attacks.

Once you defeat Jinx three times (he gains more HP and defense each time), he'll acknowledge Mario as the true dojo master. Your reward for beating him is the Jinx Belt, which in addition to boosting a character's stats also prevents one-hit KOs. The sign outside the dojo will also change from "J" to "M" in acknowledgment of Mario's fighting prowess.

### Culex

In Monstro Town, notice that the third door from the left (the one right before the Item Shop) is sealed tight. To open it, go to Moleville. Buy some Fireworks from the guy in the house near where the little girl is sitting next to five crates. After, go talk to that girl and trade the Fireworks for a Shiny Stone. Go back to Monstro Town and examine the door again. The Shiny Stone will react to the door and allow you to enter. Before you do, make sure you save your game and prepare for the fight ahead.

Culex is a throwback to classic Final Fantasy bosses (though he is not, as was rumored for years, from any particular Final Fantasy game). Culex himself only uses physical attacks and a special move called Shredder, which negates any boosts to attack and defense, such as Geno's Geno Boost ability. He also has ridiculously high HP, so be ready for a lengthy fight. Following are a couple of strategies for tackling this guy.

Use Peach as a healer and have Mario and Bowser attack normally. You can use Bowser's full-screen attacks to hit the crystals as well, but his specials aren't all that powerful.

Only heal her when she gets in the low 50s health-wise. Don't worry about reviving Mario or Bowser once they fall.

Also, once you've defeated Culex, the door behind which he was waiting will disappear from Monstro Town forever.

### Link (The Legend of Zelda

After defeating Bowyer, return to Rose Town and spend a night at the Inn. When you awaken, Link will be sleeping in the bed next to yours. If you try to talk to him, you'll hear the signature "found a secret" chime from the Zelda games!

Samus is cool enough to have two separate cameos in this game. First, after you clear Seaside Town and obtain the fifth Star Piece for good, go back to the Mushroom Kingdom, enter the Palace, and find the guest bedroom (it's across the way from the vault, which you should have visited earlier in the game). ## Sidequests, Minigames, & Miscellany

This section covers a few of the sidequests and mini-games available for playing in the game, as well as some miscellaneous things that don't really fit in anywhere else in the guide.

### Beetlemania

Anytime after you've rescued Toadstool from Booster, head to the first floor of the Inn in the Mushroom Kingdom. There will be a toad playing a handheld game in the corner. Interrupt him enough times and he'll say he beat his high score, then offer to sell you the game for 500 Coins.

The game is BeetleMania. It can be accessed from the X menu at any time in the overworld. You control a beetle moving along the bottom of the screen. Your objective is to shoot down the turtle shells by firing stars with any of the four buttons (A, B, X, Y) on the controller. If you get hit, rapidly mash the buttons to recover. The way to rack up high scores in this game is to use the shrapnel of the shells to your advantage. Each time you destroy a turtle shell, it creates a ring of damage around it that can start a chain reaction, wrecking multiple turtle shells in one go.

### The Three Musty Fears

The door to the right of the Item Shop in Monstro Town serves as a kind of Inn for resting and restoring HP and FP. There's a catch: in the middle of the night, you'll be visited by three ghosts who call themselves the Three Musty Fears. They want to play a game with you: seek out the three flags the ghosts plant in various locations throughout the world.

  1. Greaper's Flag: In Rose Town. Press **A** behind the flower-shaped Welcome sign.
  2. Dry Bones's Flag: In Mario's Pad. Press **A** in front of the bed.
  3. Big Boo: In Yo'ster Isle. Press **A** between the letters O and A in the word **GOAL**.



Once you have all three flags, return to Monstro Town and sleep again. The Fears will reappear, congratulate you on finding their flags, and equip the Ghost Medal onto Mario. The Ghost Medal raises your defense while attacking; more importantly, it's worth 800 coins, which will buy you just about anything you need prior to the end of the game.

### Grate Guy's Casino/Star Egg

The Star Egg is one of the most useful items in _Super Mario RPG_ so naturally it can only be found in one place: Grate Guy's Casino. Getting to the Casino is a two-step process.

First, go to Booster Tower after you've cleared Marrymore. Climb the tower until you encounter former boss Knife Guy juggling some multi-colored balls on a staircase. Talk to him and he'll ask you to play a game. The game is to guess which hand is holding the yellow ball. Every time you win, you'll obtain an item and your win counter goes up; every time you lose, the win counter goes down by one. You have to win the game 13 times in order to receive the Bright Card, which you'll use to get into the casino. A simple method of correctly guessing which hand the yellow ball is in is to watch its location just before the juggling is over. If the yellow ball was in the front, it will be in Knife Guy's left hand. If the ball was in the back, it will be in his right hand.

With Bright Card in hand, go to Bean Valley and find the screen with five pipes. Enter the pipe at the top, closest to the Save Block. (As usual with this screen, you'll have to wait for the Shy Guy to water the Smilax, then fight the Smilax, in order to gain access.) This area has three lizard enemies and a Gold Chomp. Clear out the Chomp, then go to the far left corner and jump three times. You'll know you've found the right spot because each time you jump, you will hear a chime. On the third jump, a platform will appear, leading you to an exit. Leave via this exit and you will be back on the overworld, with the path to Grate Guy's Casino unlocked.

Feel free to play any of the games in the Casino, but the one you want to be playing is offered by Grate Guy himself: Look the Other Way. Talk to Grate Guy a couple of times and he'll ask you if you want to play the game. The goal is to look in the opposite direction of where Grate Guy looks; so if he looks left, you'll want to be looking right, and vice versa. There's no real trick to this game, but fortunately there's no win/loss counter like with Knife Guy; you simply have to win 100 times to receive the Star Egg.

As an aside, once you're done with the Casino, you can sell the Bright Card to a guy outside the Marrymore chapel. You can refuse his first offer of 100 Coins; he'll then offer 5 Frog Coins. Refuse again and the price goes up to 10 Frog Coins. You can buy the Card back for 15 Frog Coins, as well.

### The Bellhop Gig

In Marrymore, you can buy the deluxe suite at the Hotel for a higher price than the normal room (both rooms will heal you when you sleep in the bed). The cost only covers a one- night stay, but you are quite free to stay as many nights as you like, as long as you don't go downstairs to the lobby. When you finally do go down to the lobby, having overstayed your welcome, the owner will subtract the total cost of your stay from your current funds. If he winds up bankrupting you, you'll have to work as a bellhop in the Hotel until you've paid off your debt.

The process of being a bellhop is mostly automatic: when a customer pays for the deluxe suite, you take them upstairs and explain everything, just like the original bellhop did when you stayed there. Afterwards, talk to the customers and see if you get a tip off of them. Leading one customer up to the suite will pay for one of the extra nights you overstayed in the suite. When the debt is fully repaid, you're finally released into the world again.

### Melody Bay

Off to the right side of the main screen of Tadpole Pond is an area called Melody Bay. It features a large pond set up almost like a piece of musical notation, with a Toad named Toadofsky standing on the other side. Toadofsky needs help composing a piece; you can help him out by using the pond to play out the songs.

From left to right, the notes are: Mi Re Do Ti La So Fa. You will encounter the songs Toadofsky is thinking of throughout the game.

Song #| Song| Where/When To Find| Notes To Play  
---|---|---|---  
1| Frogfucius' Suite #18| At Tadpole Pond, on a piece of paper behind Frogfucius. Available as soon as you unlock Rose Town.| So La Mi Re Do Re Do Re  
2| Moleville Blues| In Moleville Mountain after you've obtained the third Star Piece. Talk to the miners near where you blew up the wall.| Mi Do So Do Re La Ti Do  
3| Star Song| In Monstro Town, first house on the left. Talk to the star upstairs to hear the song. Available as soon as you reach Monstro Town.| La Ti Do Re So Do Re Mi  
  
After the Star Song, Toadofsky will ask you to write a conclusion to the piece. You can input any melody you like.  
  
After you successfully play a song for Toadofsky, he'll give you a card. These cards will allow you to buy items at the Juice Bar in Tadpole Pond (left of the tadpole bridge to Frogfucius on the mainland).

Card| Song| Juice Bar Items  
---|---|---  
Alto| Frogfucius' Suit #18| Froggie Drink (14 Coins), Elixir (38 Coins)  
Tenor| Moleville Blues| Froggie Drink (12 Coins), Elixir (36 Coins), Megalixir (90 Coins)  
Soprano| Star Song| Froggie Drink (8 Coins), Elixir (24 Coins), Megalixir (60 Coins), KeroKeroCola (200 Coins  
  
### Skip Mack Glitch

This is one of the most well-known, highly useful glitches in Super Mario RPG. To accomplish it, walk into the throne room where Mack and his cronies are bouncing around. Approach the Shysters on the left-hand side by walking up the part of the stairs on that side not covered by carpet. Jump onto their heads when you get the opportunity. You can now walk along their heads. Jump off their heads onto the platform where Mack and the throne are. If you do it correctly, you won't trigger the cutscene that occurs right before fighting Mack. Now you can walk right up to the Chancellor and talk to him. When you do, the game will proceed as if you have already defeated Mack and obtained the first Star Piece!

The graphics will be a little confused at first (the cutscene will play while Mack and his cronies' graphics are still on-screen) but will resettle very quickly. The only problem this causes in terms of normal gameplay is that you won't be able to view the Star Piece menu. As that menu is largely useless anyway, it's not a huge loss.

### Vine Climbing

On the second screen in the clouds (counting from when you first enter the clouds via Bean Valley's beanstalk), there are two vines to climb. Take the left vine to its peak and jump to reveal a treasure chest. Jump down and climb the other vine up until you can jump onto the top of the treasure chest. Use the chest to jump onto the vine previously out of reach and climb it to the next area. Climb up until you jump onto a yellow vine, then jump to reveal a hidden platform. Make your way around and up until you can get on the yellow platform, then make a running jump to the green vine. Climb up to find some treasure chests with items inside.

If he winds up bankrupting you, you'll have to work as a bellhop in the Hotel until you've paid off your debt.

The process of being a bellhop is mostly automatic: when a customer pays for the deluxe suite, you take them upstairs and explain everything, just like the original bellhop did when you stayed there. Afterwards, talk to the customers and see if you get a tip off of them. Leading one customer up to the suite will pay for one of the extra nights you overstayed in the suite. When the debt is fully repaid, you're finally released into the world again.

## Sidequests, Minigames, & Miscellany

### Melody Bay

Off to the right side of the main screen of Tadpole Pond is an area called Melody Bay. It features a large pond set up almost like a piece of musical notation, with a Toad named Toadofsky standing on the other side. Toadofsky needs help composing a piece; you can help him out by using the pond to play out the songs.

From left to right, the notes are: Mi Re Do Ti La So Fa. You will encounter the songs Toadofsky is thinking of throughout the game.

## Sidequests, Minigames, & Miscellany

### Skip Mack Glitch

This is one of the most well-known, highly useful glitches in Super Mario RPG. To accomplish it, walk into the throne room where Mack and his cronies are bouncing around. Approach the Shysters on the left-hand side by walking up the part of the stairs on that side not covered by carpet. Jump onto their heads when you get the opportunity. You can now walk along their heads. Jump off their heads onto the platform where Mack and the throne are. If you do it correctly, you won't trigger the cutscene that occurs right before fighting Mack. Now you can walk right up to the Chancellor and talk to him. When you do, the game will proceed as if you have already defeated Mack and obtained the first Star Piece!

The graphics will be a little confused at first (the cutscene will play while Mack and his cronies' graphics are still on-screen) but will resettle very quickly. The only problem this causes in terms of normal gameplay is that you won't be able to view the Star Piece menu. As that menu is largely useless anyway, it's not a huge loss.

## Sidequests, Minigames, & Miscellany

### Vine Climbing

On the second screen in the clouds (counting from when you first enter the clouds via Bean Valley's beanstalk), there are two vines to climb. Take the left vine to its peak and jump to reveal a treasure chest. Jump down and climb the other vine up until you can jump onto the top of the treasure chest. Use the chest to jump onto the vine previously out of reach and climb it to the next area. Climb up until you jump onto a yellow vine, then jump to reveal a hidden platform. Make your way around and up until you can get on the yellow platform, then make a running jump to the green vine. Climb up to find some treasure chests with items inside.
