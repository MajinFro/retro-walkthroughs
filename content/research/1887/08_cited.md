## Game Overview

Princess Tomato in the Salad Kingdom is an adventure game developed and published by Hudson Soft for the Nintendo Entertainment System [[sw1-1-1]]. Players control Sir Cucumber on an epic journey to rescue Princess Tomato from Minister Pumpkin and restore the Salad Kingdom [[sw1-2-1]].

## Two-Playthrough Strategy & Mutual Exclusion

For your first playthrough do not buy any juices in chapter 2; buying them locks you out of future achievements [[ra-comments-1-1]].

- **Playthrough 1 (Primary Hardcore Run — No Passwords):** Complete Chapters 1 through 9 from start to finish without using any passwords to earn One-Course Veggie Tale (98092) [[ra-comments-107-1]]. During this run in Chapter 2, you **MUST NOT** buy Grape Juice or Clover Juice [[ra-comments-1-1]]. Skipping these juices allows you to receive Grape Juice from Mr. Leek in Chapter 4 and trade it for Clover Juice in Chapter 5 [[ra-comments-54-1, ra-comments-58-1]]. Be extremely cautious during the Minister Pumpkin boss battle in Chapter 8, as defeat causes an immediate game over that invalidates the no-passwords run [[ra-comments-1-2]].
- **Playthrough 2 (Post-Game Password Cleanup):** After defeating the final boss and earning the no-passwords achievement, enter the Chapter 2 password to reload Saladoria [[ra-comments-23-1]]. On this replay, purchase both Grape Juice and Clover Juice at the store to earn the Chapter 2 item collection achievement [[ra-comments-23-1]].

## Core Controls & Finger Wars Combat

Commands line both sides of the screen: Move to a destination, Look for details, and Check for hidden objects [[sw1-3-1, cgp1-1-1]].

Finger Wars combat directions are standardized [[ra-comments-100-1]]:

- **Rock-Paper-Scissors Throw:** Press **UP** for Rock, **LEFT** for Scissors, and **RIGHT** for Paper [[ra-comments-100-1]].
- **Look-Away Phase:** Press up to pick rock, then press down to look down matching the enemy direction [[ra-comments-42-1]].

## Playthrough Planning

**Playthroughs required:** 2

Two playthroughs / chapter replay approach: Playthrough 1 is a clean no-passwords run covering 51 achievements without purchasing grape or clover juice in Chapter 2, culminating in One-Course Veggie Tale (98092). Playthrough 2 is a post-game Chapter 2 replay using password to purchase the juices and collect all items for Saladoria's Cornucopia (98049), safely avoiding the mutual exclusion / lockout conflict.

**Settings:** Play on standard NES emulator settings with standard controls. Rock-Paper-Scissors direction controls: Up = Rock, Left = Scissors, Right = Paper. Look-Away controls: Up = Up, Down = Down, Left = Left, Right = Right.

### Step-by-Step Walkthrough

> [!WARNING] Missable Achievement Warning
> Before speaking to or interacting with Percy in Celery Forest, you must execute the Percy menu command on the Starting Road [[ra-comments-7-1]].

1. **MOVE** → Starting Road at the very beginning of the adventure [[gf1-2-1]].
2. **LOOK** → Check your immediate surroundings on the Starting Road [[gf1-2-1]].
3. **PERCY** → Call out to Percy before ever meeting him to trigger the secret interaction [[ra-comments-7-1]].

> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> **Diospyros Who? (1 Points)**
> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/107562.png)
> *Use a peculiarly-named command before meeting the character it belongs to*

4. **TAKE** → Pick up the Red Flower resting on the ground [[gf1-2-1, sw1-9-1]].
5. **MOVE** → Advance north along the path into Celery Forest [[gf1-2-2, sw1-9-2]].
6. **LOOK** → Notice a parched baby persimmon named Percy lying on the road [[gf1-2-2, sw1-9-2]].
7. **TALK** → Speak with Percy to learn that he is desperately thirsty [[gf1-2-2, sw1-9-2]].
8. **MOVE** → Travel east to the shores of Lake Quench [[gf1-2-3, sw1-9-3]].
9. **LOOK** → Search the trees around Lake Quench to discover a fern bird nest [[gf1-2-7, sw1-9-4]].
10. **CHECK** → Search the nest in the tree to locate a shiny gold coin [[gf1-2-7, sw1-9-4]].
11. **TAKE** → Take the Lake Gold Coin from the nest [[gf1-2-7, sw1-9-4]].
12. **TAKE** → Collect water from Lake Quench into your canteen to store three drinks [[gf1-2-3, sw1-9-3]].
13. **MOVE** → Return west to Celery Forest to aid the suffering persimmon [[gf1-2-4, sw1-9-5]].
14. **GIVE** → Offer water from your canteen to Percy to quench his thirst [[gf1-2-4, sw1-9-5]].
15. **TALK** → Speak to Percy twice to learn about his captured companions [[gf1-2-4, sw1-9-5]].
16. **LOOK** → Examine Percy, and he gratefully decides to join your party as a loyal companion [[gf1-2-4, sw1-9-5]].

> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> **Drink of Water, Boss? (2 Points)**
> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/106583.png)
> *Meet Percy, the baby persimmon*

17. **MOVE** → Head north to the Parsley Forest Bush crossroads [[gf1-2-5, sw1-9-5]].
18. **LOOK** → Check the bush near the fence [[gf1-2-5, sw1-9-5]].
19. **CHECK** → Search the bush to find Prince Lettuce hiding underneath [[gf1-2-5, sw1-9-5]].
20. **GIVE** → Give water to Prince Lettuce to restore his strength [[gf1-2-5, sw1-9-5]].
21. **TALK** → Speak with Prince Lettuce, who shows you a secret passage leading to the Melon Patch [[gf1-2-5, sw1-9-5]].
22. **MOVE** → Step through the secret passage to enter the Melon Patch [[gf1-2-6, sw1-9-6]].
23. **LOOK** → Observe the four withered melons lying in the patch [[gf1-2-6, sw1-9-6]].
24. **GIVE** → Pour water onto all four melons to revive them completely [[gf1-2-6, sw1-9-6]].
25. **TALK** → Speak to Cantaloup, who gives you the Town Gate Pass as a reward [[gf1-2-6, sw1-9-6]].
26. **TAKE** → Pick up the Town Gate Pass from the grateful melons [[gf1-2-6, sw1-9-6]].
27. **MOVE** → Return to Lake Quench to refill your Water Canteen before departing [[gf1-2-3, sw1-9-3]].
28. **TAKE** → Refill your water supply completely at Lake Quench [[gf1-2-3, sw1-9-3]].
29. **MOVE** → Travel to the Saladoria Town Gate to enter the capital city [[gf1-2-8, sw1-9-7]].

> [!WARNING] Missable Achievement Warning
> Ensure you have acquired all 5 items (Red Flower, Water Canteen, Gold Coin, Gate Pass, and Spinach) before entering Saladoria [[ra-comments-11-1]].

> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> **Harvesting Spinach Heights (5 Points)**
> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/106582.png)
> *Collect every item in Chapter 1*

30. **GIVE** → Show the Town Gate Pass to the guard at the Saladoria Town Gate [[gf1-2-8, sw1-9-7]].
31. **PERCY** → Consult Percy, who explains that the guard frequently dozes off on duty [[gf1-2-8, sw1-9-7]].
32. **MOVE** → Move away and return repeatedly until the guard falls sound asleep [[gf1-2-8, sw1-9-7]].

> [!CAUTION] Point of No Return: Saladoria Town Gate
> Sneak past the sleeping guard at the gate to enter the town [[gf1-2-8, sw1-9-7]].

33. **MOVE** → Slip quietly past the sleeping guard and proceed through the Saladoria Town Gate into town [[gf1-2-8, sw1-9-7]].
