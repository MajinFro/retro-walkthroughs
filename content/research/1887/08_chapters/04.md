### Step-by-Step Walkthrough

1. **MOVE** → Spinach Heights Crossroads after safely fleeing the police station [[gf1-4-8, sw1-17-1]].
2. **CHECK** → Search the bush twice at Lake Quench to discover a Blue Umbrella [[gf1-4-8, sw1-17-1]].
3. **TAKE** → Pick up the Blue Umbrella [[gf1-4-8, sw1-17-1]].
4. **MOVE** → Head to the Melon Patch Mole Hole [[gf1-4-8, sw1-17-2]].
5. **CHECK** → Examine the third hole twice to discover that a mole lives inside [[gf1-4-8, sw1-17-2]].
6. **USE** → Open the Blue Umbrella over the hole to shade the mole from sunlight [[gf1-4-8, sw1-17-2]].
7. **GIVE** → Give fresh water to the mole to restore his energy [[sw1-17-2]].
8. **TALK** → Speak to the mole in the Melon Patch [[sw1-17-2]].

9. **MOVE** → Visit Mr. Leek at his woodland shelter [[gf1-4-9]].
10. **GIVE** → Return the Blue Umbrella to Mr. Leek [[gf1-4-9]].
11. **TAKE** → Accept the Water Wings from Mr. Leek in return [[gf1-4-9]].
12. **MOVE** → Advance toward the swift Parsley Forest Stream [[gf1-4-10, sw1-17-3]].
13. **USE** → Equip the Water Wings on Percy to safely swim across the rushing current [[gf1-4-10, sw1-17-3]].
