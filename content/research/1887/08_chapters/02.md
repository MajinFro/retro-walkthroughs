### Step-by-Step Walkthrough

1. **MOVE** → Orange Park where garlic wanderers rest among the trees [[gf1-3-1, sw1-11-1]].
2. **LOOK** → Look around Orange Park to spot the elderly garlic wanderer [[gf1-3-1, sw1-11-1]].
3. **TALK** → Speak to the Garlic Wanderer to learn about the resistance movement [[gf1-3-1, sw1-11-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98046]

4. **HIT** → Hit the fountain until you get a coin to take [[gf1-3-2]].
5. **TAKE** → Pick up the coin from the ground [[gf1-3-2]].
6. **MOVE** → Travel to Main Street in the heart of the city [[gf1-3-3, sw1-11-4]].

> [!WARNING] Missable Achievement Warning
> Octoberry has a random chance of appearing when you enter Main Street. Alternate entering and exiting Main Street until Octoberry appears, then check the street immediately [[gf1-3-3, ra-comments-15-1]].

7. **MOVE** → Enter and exit Main Street repeatedly until Octoberry walks into view [[gf1-3-3, ra-comments-15-1]].
8. **CHECK** → Check the street right after Octoberry walks past to spot a dropped coin [[gf1-3-3, ra-comments-15-1]].
9. **TAKE** → Pick up the Octoberry Gold Coin from the cobblestones [[gf1-3-3, ra-comments-15-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98045]

10. **MOVE** → Enter the Saladoria Pharmacy on the corner [[gf1-3-7, sw1-11-7]].
11. **TALK** → Speak with Mr. Fig twice about medicine for the resistance [[gf1-3-7, sw1-11-7]].
12. **TAKE** → Receive the bottle of Aspirin from Mr. Fig [[gf1-3-7, sw1-11-7]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98047]

13. **MOVE** → Visit the public Restroom nearby [[gf1-3-6, sw1-11-6]].
14. **CHECK** → Inspect the trash can twice to find a box of matches styled as a donut [[gf1-3-6, sw1-11-6]].
15. **TAKE** → Take the Donut (Matches) out of the trash can [[gf1-3-6, sw1-11-6]].
16. **MOVE** → Head to the Coffee Shop on Main Street [[gf1-3-4, sw1-11-5]].
17. **BUY** → Purchase a cup of warm coffee from Mrs. Plum [[gf1-3-4, sw1-11-5]].
18. **MOVE** → Enter the General Store (Ch 2) to purchase provisions [[gf1-3-4, sw1-11-3]].
19. **BUY** → Buy a sandwich from the deli counter [[sw1-11-3]].

> [!WARNING] Playthrough Routing & Lockout Notice
> On your primary playthrough, **DO NOT BUY** Grape Juice or Clover Juice here [[ra-comments-1-1, ra-comments-23-1]]. Buying them locks you out of With No Intent to Ferment (98064) and Quit Your Wining... (98066) [[ra-comments-54-1, ra-comments-58-1]]. On Playthrough 2 (Password Replay), buy both juices now to complete the full collection [[ra-comments-23-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98049]

20. **MOVE** → Walk over to the Saladoria Bookshop [[gf1-3-8, sw1-11-8]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98048]

21. **CHECK** → Check behind the counter in the bookshop to discover a hidden key [[gf1-3-9, sw1-11-8]].
22. **TAKE** → Pick up the Bookshop Key from beneath the counter [[gf1-3-9, sw1-11-8]].

> [!CAUTION] Point of No Return: Saladoria Bookshop
> Exiting the Saladoria Bookshop triggers an ambush by Minister Pumpkin's farmies and immediately ends Chapter 2 [[gf1-3-10, sw1-11-8]].

23. **MOVE** → Step outside the bookshop entrance, where Farmie guards surround you and haul you off to prison [[gf1-3-10, sw1-11-8]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98050]
