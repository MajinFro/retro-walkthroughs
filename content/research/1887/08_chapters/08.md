### Step-by-Step Walkthrough

1. **MOVE** → Castle Basement Maze through the tunnel entrance [[gf1-9-1, sw1-29-1]].
2. **MOVE** → Follow the winding subterranean labyrinth to locate the Baby Monster [[gf1-9-1, sw1-29-1]].
3. **TALK** → Speak kindly to the Baby Monster to make friends [[gf1-9-1, sw1-29-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98085]

> [!WARNING] Missable Achievement Warning
> Explore the dead ends of the basement maze to locate the Mama Monster. In Finger Wars, she plays Rock-Rock-Paper (counter with Paper-Paper-Scissors) and always looks Left on Look-Away [[gf1-9-2, ra-comments-96-1]].

4. **MOVE** → Step into the dead-end chamber where the Mama Monster lurks [[gf1-9-2, ra-comments-96-1]].
5. **FIGHT** → Defeat the Mama Monster by throwing Paper (**RIGHT**) and pointing Left (**LEFT**) [[gf1-9-2, ra-comments-96-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98086]

6. **MOVE** → Seek out the Papa Monster guarding the main stairs [[gf1-9-3, sw1-29-3]].
7. **FIGHT** → Challenge and defeat the Papa Monster in Finger Wars, always pointing Left on Look-Away [[gf1-9-3, sw1-29-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98087]

8. **MOVE** → Climb the staircase into the Castle Storage Room [[gf1-7-4, sw1-28-1]].
9. **TAKE** → Take the Oil Pot and Chain, along with the pine tar and mirror from the shelves [[gf1-7-4, ra-comments-102-1]].
10. **MOVE** → Enter the Castle Dark Room study [[ra-comments-102-1, gf1-10-4]].
11. **CHECK** → Search the third drawer of the desk to locate the secret letter and gold coin [[ra-comments-102-1]].
12. **TAKE** → Collect the Letter and Dark Room Coin from the desk drawer [[ra-comments-102-1]].
13. **MOVE** → Step outside into the tranquil Castle Garden and Pool [[gf1-8-1, sw1-28-2]].
14. **TAKE** → Collect Garden Water from the fountain and pick up the ornament [[ra-comments-102-1]].
15. **LOOK** → Notice Percy drowning in the pond [[gf1-8-1, sw1-28-2]].
16. **USE** → Rush forward and dive into the water to rescue young Percy from drowning [[gf1-8-1, sw1-28-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98081]

17. **MOVE** → Advance toward the Castle Hallway Guard on patrol [[gf1-8-2, sw1-28-3]].
18. **USE** → Use oil pot so guard slips and use chain to tie up guard [[gf1-8-2, sw1-28-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98082]

19. **MOVE** → Go to jail cell to find caterpillar on Lettuce [[gf1-8-3, ra-comments-102-1]].
20. **MOVE** → Unlock and enter Princess Tomato's Cell in the tower [[gf1-8-4, sw1-28-5]].
21. **LOOK** → Behold the radiant, true Princess Tomato alive and unharmed [[gf1-8-4, sw1-28-5]].
22. **TALK** → Speak to Princess Tomato, who embraces you and prepares for rescue [[gf1-8-4, sw1-28-5]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98083]

23. **MOVE** → Approach the decorated royal door of the throne room [[gf1-8-5, sw1-28-6]].
24. **FIGHT** → Defeat the elite eggplant guard in Finger Wars to gain entry [[gf1-8-5, sw1-28-6]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98084]

25. **TAKE** → Grab the formal Clothes from the hallway rack outside the bedroom [[ra-comments-102-1]].

> [!WARNING] Missable Achievement Warning
> Confirm all 12 Chapter 8 items (Oil Pot, Chain, Pine Tar, Mirror, Letter, Coin, Water, Ornament, Caterpillar, Clothes, Books, Tea Cups) have been collected before confronting the Minister [[ra-comments-102-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98089]

26. **MOVE** → Enter the chamber to battle Minister Pumpkin [[gf1-9-4, sw1-29-4]].
27. **TAKE** → Collect the Maze Books and porcelain Tea Cups from the nightstand [[ra-comments-102-1]].

> [!CAUTION] Point of No Return: Confronting Minister Pumpkin in his Chambers
> Entering combat with Minister Pumpkin triggers the endgame castle escape sequence [[gf1-9-4, sw1-29-4]]. Defeat in this battle results in a game over that invalidates the no-passwords run [[ra-comments-1-2]].

28. **FIGHT** → Face Minister Pumpkin in a high-stakes duel of Finger Wars [[gf1-9-4, ra-comments-100-1]].
29. **FIGHT** → Execute his exact repeating 8-move throw sequence: **Paper (RIGHT)**, **Paper (RIGHT)**, **Rock (UP)**, **Scissors (LEFT)**, **Rock (UP)**, **Scissors (LEFT)**, **Scissors (LEFT)**, and **Paper (RIGHT)** [[ra-comments-100-1]].
30. **FIGHT** → Win against Minister Pumpkin using the repeating pattern [[ra-comments-100-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98088]

31. **MOVE** → Escape with Princess Tomato through the secret passage out into the courtyard [[gf1-9-5, sw1-29-5]].
