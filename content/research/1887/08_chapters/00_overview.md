# Princess Tomato in the Salad Kingdom — Walkthrough Overview

Princess Tomato in the Salad Kingdom is an adventure game developed and published by Hudson Soft for the Nintendo Entertainment System [[sw1-1-1]]. Players control Sir Cucumber on an epic journey to rescue Princess Tomato from Minister Pumpkin and restore the Salad Kingdom [[sw1-2-1]].

## Two-Playthrough Strategy & Mutual Exclusion

For your first playthrough do not buy any juices in chapter 2; buying them locks you out of future achievements [[ra-comments-1-1]].

- **Playthrough 1 (Primary Hardcore Run — No Passwords):** Complete Chapters 1 through 9 from start to finish without using any passwords to earn One-Course Veggie Tale (98092) [[ra-comments-107-1]]. During this run in Chapter 2, you **MUST NOT** buy Grape Juice or Clover Juice [[ra-comments-1-1]]. Skipping these juices allows you to receive Grape Juice from Mr. Leek in Chapter 4 and trade it for Clover Juice in Chapter 5 [[ra-comments-54-1, ra-comments-58-1]]. Be extremely cautious during the Minister Pumpkin boss battle in Chapter 8, as defeat causes an immediate game over that invalidates the no-passwords run [[ra-comments-1-2]].
- **Playthrough 2 (Post-Game Password Cleanup):** After defeating the final boss and earning the no-passwords achievement, enter the Chapter 2 password to reload Saladoria [[ra-comments-23-1]]. On this replay, purchase both Grape Juice and Clover Juice at the store to earn the Chapter 2 item collection achievement [[ra-comments-23-1]].

## Core Controls & Finger Wars Combat

Commands line both sides of the screen: Move to a destination, Look for details, and Check for hidden objects [[sw1-3-1, cgp1-1-1]].

Finger Wars combat directions are standardized [[ra-comments-100-1]]:

- **Rock-Paper-Scissors Throw:** Press **UP** for Rock, **LEFT** for Scissors, and **RIGHT** for Paper [[ra-comments-100-1]].
- **Look-Away Phase:** Press up to pick rock, then press down to look down matching the enemy direction [[ra-comments-42-1]].
