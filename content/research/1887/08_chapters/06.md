### Step-by-Step Walkthrough

1. **MOVE** → Peanut Village Plateau to survey the settlement [[gf1-6-2, sw1-22-2]].
2. **MOVE** → Enter the Village Chief's House to talk to the chief [[gf1-6-4, sw1-22-3]].
3. **TALK** → Speak to the chief to learn that his child Nutty has been kidnapped [[gf1-6-4, sw1-22-3]].
4. **TAKE** → Take the Oil Lamp from the shelf [[gf1-6-2]].
5. **MOVE** → Travel up the mountainside to Bananda's Cave [[gf1-6-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98069]

6. **USE** → Light the Oil Lamp to illuminate Bananda's cave [[gf1-6-2]].

> [!WARNING] Missable Achievement Warning
> Search every branching path of Bananda's Cave to collect all 5 coin pouches before rescuing Nutty and leaving the cave [[gf1-6-5, ra-comments-68-1]].

7. **MOVE** → Navigate the cavern corridors, picking up all 5 coin pouches along the floor [[gf1-6-5, sw1-23-1]].
8. **TAKE** → Take all 5 coin pouches from the cavern floor [[gf1-6-5, sw1-23-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98071]

9. **MOVE** → Step into the cave to encounter Bananda [[gf1-6-3, sw1-22-2]].
10. **HIT** → Attack the villainous Bananda to peel away his defenses [[gf1-6-3, sw1-22-2]].
11. **TAKE** → Strip off the Banana Skin from Bananda until he is completely defeated [[gf1-6-3, ra-comments-78-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98070]

12. **TALK** → Talk to rescued Nutty to save him [[gf1-6-4, sw1-22-3]].
13. **MOVE** → Return to the Village Chief's House in the village [[gf1-6-4, sw1-22-3]].
14. **TALK** → Speak with the grateful Village Chief, who presents you with the Robot Battery [[gf1-6-4, sw1-22-3]].
15. **TAKE** → Take the Robot Battery from the chief [[gf1-6-4, sw1-22-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98072]

16. **MOVE** → Visit the village convenience store on the plateau [[ra-comments-72-1, ra-comments-78-1]].
17. **BUY** → Purchase the Wordbook, squid, peanuts, and broom using your gold coins [[ra-comments-72-1, ra-comments-78-1]].
18. **MOVE** → Move to Peanut Park to decipher the statue [[gf1-6-1, sw1-22-1]].
19. **LOOK** → Inspect the mysterious Hudson Bee monument standing in Peanut Park [[gf1-6-1, sw1-22-1]].

> [!WARNING] Missable Achievement Warning
> Examine the Hudson Bee statue with the Wordbook in your inventory to decipher the inscription [[gf1-6-1, ra-comments-72-1]].

20. **CHECK** → Examine the Hudson Bee statue and read its ancient cipher using the Wordbook [[gf1-6-1, ra-comments-72-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98073]

21. **MOVE** → Journey up the winding path to the Hermit's Hut [[ra-comments-78-1, gf1-10-2]].
22. **GIVE** → Hand the dried squid to the hungry hermit [[ra-comments-78-1]].
23. **TAKE** → Receive the restorative Hermit Medicine from the hermit [[ra-comments-78-1, gf1-10-2]].
24. **MOVE** → Walk to the base of the Giant Tree (Ch 6) [[gf1-6-8]].
25. **TAKE** → Harvest the rare Chameleon Grass growing on the Giant Tree [[gf1-6-8]].
26. **TAKE** → Pick up the Right Instructions blueprint scrap from the soil [[ra-comments-78-1]].

> [!WARNING] Missable Achievement Warning
> Ensure all 12 Chapter 6 items (Carrot, Oil Lamp, Battery, 5 Pouches, Skin, Wordbook, Squid, Peanuts, Broom, Medicine, Grass, Instructions) have been gathered before operating the robot [[ra-comments-78-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98076]

27. **MOVE** → Reach the Dice-O-Matic in the cave [[gf1-6-6, sw1-23-2]].
28. **USE** → Insert the Robot Battery into the control console of the Dice-O-Matic to unlock the cockpit door [[gf1-6-6, sw1-23-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98074]

> [!CAUTION] Point of No Return: Boarding the Dice-O-Matic Robot
> Enter the cockpit of the Dice-O-Matic to end Chapter 6 [[gf1-6-6, sw1-23-2]].

29. **MOVE** → Climb into the Dice-O-Matic cockpit to confront the War Melon Army pilots [[gf1-6-7, sw1-23-3]].
30. **FIGHT** → Execute the winning combat sequence against the War Melon Army: press **DOWN**, then **RIGHT**, then **RIGHT**, then **LEFT** [[ra-comments-76-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98075]

31. **MOVE** → Pilot the hijacked robot through the mountains toward Sopville [[gf1-6-7, sw1-23-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98077]
