### Step-by-Step Walkthrough

1. **MOVE** → Police Holding Cell where other captured vegetable prisoners sit [[gf1-4-1, sw1-14-1]].
2. **LOOK** → Check the jail cell where Potato is sitting [[gf1-4-1, sw1-14-1]].
3. **HIT** → Strike Potato to make him take you seriously [[gf1-4-1, sw1-14-1]].
4. **TALK** → Speak to Potato, who hands over a hidden Metal File [[gf1-4-1, sw1-14-1]].
5. **TAKE** → Take the Metal File from Potato [[gf1-4-1, sw1-14-1]].
6. **USE** → Use the Metal File on the cell bars to attempt an escape [[gf1-4-2, sw1-14-2]].
7. **MOVE** → Get caught during the escape attempt and escorted to the Interrogation Room [[gf1-4-2, sw1-14-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98051]

8. **TALK** → Speak with Sgt. Pepper three times in the Interrogation Room [[gf1-4-2, sw1-14-2]].
9. **GIVE** → Hand over the Metal File when Sgt. Pepper notices it [[gf1-4-3, sw1-14-2]].
10. **MOVE** → Get locked inside the water-filled Torture Room as punishment [[gf1-4-3, sw1-14-2]].
11. **CHECK** → Inspect the door twice to find a hidden keyhole [[gf1-4-3, sw1-14-3]].
12. **HIT** → Hit the heavy door three times until Sgt. Pepper assumes you are trapped and walks away [[gf1-4-3, sw1-14-3]].
13. **USE** → Hit the keyhole and use the key to escape [[gf1-4-3, sw1-14-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98052]

14. **MOVE** → Step out into the Police Station Hallway [[gf1-4-4, sw1-14-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98053]

17. **HIT** → Hit the door again to scare off the guard [[gf1-4-3, sw1-14-3]].
18. **USE** → Use the key to unlock the Torture Room door once more [[gf1-4-3, sw1-14-3]].
19. **MOVE** → Re-enter the Police Station Hallway and check the cells [[gf1-4-6, sw1-14-4]].

21. **GIVE** → Give the Donut (Matches) to the Garlic Wanderer [[sw1-14-4]].

23. **MOVE** → Head down the corridor to the Police Station Closet [[gf1-4-5, sw1-14-5]].
24. **TAKE** → Take the Rope, paper, and bucket from the storage shelves [[gf1-4-5, ra-comments-33-1]].
25. **MOVE** → Approach Sgt. Pepper's private office door [[sw1-14-4]].
26. **MOVE** → Enter the office to confront Sgt. Pepper directly [[sw1-14-4]].
27. **USE** → Threaten Sgt. Pepper with the Fake Soap Grenade to make him surrender [[gf1-4-6, sw1-14-4]].
28. **USE** → Enter the storage room to find rope and tie up Sgt. Pepper [[gf1-4-5, sw1-14-5]].
