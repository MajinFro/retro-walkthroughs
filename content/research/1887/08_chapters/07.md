### Step-by-Step Walkthrough

1. **MOVE** → Sopville Town main thoroughfare where hungry villagers gather [[gf1-7-1, sw1-25-1]].
2. **LOOK** → Look at wanderers Yam and Aspara [[gf1-7-1, sw1-25-1]].
3. **MOVE** → Enter the Sopville General Store on the corner [[gf1-7-1, sw1-25-2]].
4. **BUY** → Purchase Fresh Bread and Sweet Juice for your coins [[ra-comments-82-1, ra-comments-85-1]].
5. **BUY** → Purchase the remaining goods: Socks, Shampoo, and Spices [[ra-comments-82-1]].
6. **MOVE** → Return to street to talk to wanderers [[gf1-7-1, sw1-25-1]].

> [!WARNING] Missable Achievement Warning
> Ensure you feed both Yam and Asparagus before advancing the main plot, using the bread and juice bought from the store [[ra-comments-85-1]].

7. **GIVE** → Give the Fresh Bread to hungry Yam [[ra-comments-85-1]].
8. **GIVE** → Give the Sweet Juice to thirsty Asparagus [[ra-comments-85-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98080]

9. **MOVE** → Enter the dimly lit Sopville Bar across the street [[gf1-7-2, sw1-25-2]].
10. **LOOK** → Spot Radish Ron sitting in the corner booth [[gf1-7-2, sw1-25-2]].
11. **GIVE** → Show the Resistance Crest to Radish Ron [[gf1-7-2, sw1-25-2]].
12. **TALK** → Converse with Radish Ron repeatedly until he hands over Ron's Underground Key [[gf1-7-2, sw1-25-2]].
13. **TAKE** → Take Ron's Underground Key from the table [[gf1-7-2, sw1-25-2]].
14. **MOVE** → Travel to the Old Lady's House on the edge of town [[ra-comments-82-1, gf1-10-3]].
15. **CHECK** → Check ground to grab coin [[gf1-10-3, ra-comments-82-1]].
16. **TAKE** → Pick up the Old Lady Coin from the ground [[ra-comments-82-1]].
17. **MOVE** → Walk down narrow Pea Street [[gf1-7-3, sw1-25-3]].
18. **LOOK** → Check Pea Street with the shovel tool [[gf1-7-3, sw1-25-3]].
19. **GIVE** → Hand over all remaining gold coins to E.P. until your purse is empty [[ra-comments-82-1]].
20. **TAKE** → Take the shovel on Pea Street [[gf1-7-3, sw1-25-3]].

> [!WARNING] Missable Achievement Warning
> Confirm Chapter 7 items include Key, Coin, Shovel, Bread, Socks, Juice, Spices, and Shampoo [[gf1-10-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98078]

21. **USE** → Use the shovel to dig away the wall and reveal exit [[gf1-7-3, sw1-25-3]].
22. **USE** → Insert Ron's Underground Key into the subterranean hatch [[gf1-7-3, sw1-25-3]].

> [!CAUTION] Point of No Return: Entering the Secret Underground Passage
> Navigate the passage forward, right, left, forward and dig with shovel to exit [[gf1-7-3, sw1-25-3]].

23. **MOVE** → Descend into the darkness of the Secret Underground Passage [[gf1-7-3, sw1-25-3]].
