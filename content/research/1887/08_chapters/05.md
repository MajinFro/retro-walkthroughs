# Chapter 5: The Resistance Headquarters

Meet the leader of the resistance, General Cantaloupe, and receive the resistance crest [[gf1-5-6, sw1-20-1]].

[MERMAID]

### Step-by-Step Walkthrough

1. **MOVE** → Resistance Base main hallway where resistance fighters gather [[gf1-5-6, sw1-20-1]].
2. **MOVE** → Meeting room to speak with General Cantaloupe [[gf1-5-6, sw1-20-1]].
3. **LOOK** → Look upon General Cantaloupe, the noble leader of the rebellion [[gf1-5-6, sw1-20-1]].
4. **GIVE** → Present the Yam Medallion and Aspirin to General Cantaloupe [[gf1-5-6, sw1-20-1]].
5. **TALK** → Speak with General Cantaloupe, who commissions you as an official resistance operative [[gf1-5-6, sw1-20-1]].
6. **TAKE** → Receive the Resistance Crest from General Cantaloupe as proof of authority [[gf1-5-6, sw1-20-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98065]

7. **MOVE** → Return to the corridor outside the meeting room [[gf1-5-7, sw1-20-2]].
8. **LOOK** → Notice Grapy the guard standing watch before the supply armory [[gf1-5-7, sw1-20-2]].

> [!WARNING] Missable Achievement Warning
> Trade the Grape Juice you received from Mr. Leek to Grapy. If you purchased Clover Juice earlier in Chapter 2, you will be locked out of this reward [[gf1-5-7, ra-comments-58-1]].

9. **GIVE** → Give your Grape Juice to Grapy the thirsty guard [[gf1-5-7, ra-comments-58-1]].
10. **TALK** → Speak to Grapy, who gives you a refreshing bottle of Clover Juice in appreciation [[gf1-5-7, ra-comments-58-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98066]

11. **MOVE** → Enter the Resistance Arms Room now left unguarded by Grapy [[gf1-5-8, sw1-20-3]].
12. **LOOK** → Look inside the arms room at the shelves [[gf1-5-8, sw1-20-3]].

> [!WARNING] Missable Achievement Warning
> You must equip BOTH the Nut Bomb and the Black Bean in the Arms Room. Even though the Black Bean has no practical combat use, taking both is strictly required for the achievement [[gf1-10-1, ra-comments-60-1]].

13. **TAKE** → Take the powerful Nut Bomb from the munitions bench [[gf1-5-8, sw1-20-3]].
14. **TAKE** → Take the Black Bean resting beside the explosive ordnance [[gf1-10-1, ra-comments-60-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98067]

15. **MOVE** → Return to the meeting room to report mission readiness to General Cantaloupe [[gf1-5-8]].
16. **TALK** → Speak to prepare to leave the base [[gf1-5-9, sw1-20-3]].

> [!CAUTION] Point of No Return: Exiting Resistance Base for Peanut Village
> Leaving the base to Peanut Village permanently closes Chapter 5 and locks off the headquarters [[gf1-5-9, sw1-20-3]].

17. **MOVE** → Leave the underground Resistance Base and march onward toward Peanut Village [[gf1-5-9, sw1-20-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:98068]
