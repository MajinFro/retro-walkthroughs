**Princess Tomato in the Salad Kingdom** (サラダの国のトマト姫, __Sarada no Kuni no Tomato Hime__[?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Japanese "wikipedia:Help:Japanese")) is an adventure game by [Hudson Soft](/wiki/Hudson_Soft "Hudson Soft") originally released in [1984](/wiki/1984 "1984") for the [NEC PC-8801](/wiki/NEC_PC-8801 "NEC PC-8801"), [NEC PC-6001](/wiki/NEC_PC-6001 "NEC PC-6001"), [Fujitsu FM-7](/wiki/Fujitsu_FM-7 "Fujitsu FM-7"), and [MSX](/wiki/MSX "MSX") Japanese home computers. On May 27, 1988, it was ported to the [Famicom](/wiki/Famicom "Famicom"). It was then translated and published on February 8, 1991 for the [NES](/wiki/NES "NES") in North America. It was also released on the [Wii](/wiki/Wii "Wii")'s [Virtual Console](/wiki/Virtual_Console "Virtual Console") in Japan on January 19, 2010 and in North America on February 8, 2010.

## Story

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Princess_Tomato_in_the_Salad_Kingdom&veaction=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Story") | [edit source](/w/index.php?title=Princess_Tomato_in_the_Salad_Kingdom&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section's source code: Story")]

"Many growing seasons ago, there was a place where vegetables lived happily, and in perfect harmony. One day, Minister Pumpkin betrayed King Broccoli. He kidnapped Princess Tomato and stole the Turnip Emblem. He took them to his castle in the Zucchini Mountains. He sent his cruel Farmies out to terrorize all the vegetables in the Salad Kingdom. Shortly thereafter, the poor King died from the loss of his beautiful daughter. But he promised you, brave Sir Cucumber, the Princess' hand and the kingdom if you bring them back safely. God speed Sir Cucumber! Hurry! Saladoria is down this path. The Zucchini Mountains are over yonder." 

The characters are primarily cartoon-like anthropomorphic fruit and vegetables. Taking the role of Sir Cucumber, a knight, the player is assigned by King Broccoli (now deceased) to defeat the evil Minister Pumpkin who has kidnapped Princess Tomato. Early on, Sir Cucumber gains a sidekick, Percy the baby persimmon, who offers advice and helps throughout the quest (and always calls Sir Cucumber "Boss"). 

## Gameplay

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Princess_Tomato_in_the_Salad_Kingdom&veaction=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Gameplay") | [edit source](/w/index.php?title=Princess_Tomato_in_the_Salad_Kingdom&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section's source code: Gameplay")]

_Princess Tomato in the Salad Kingdom_ plays similarly to a text adventure, though due to the NES's lack of a keyboard accessory, the possible commands are represented by buttons which line both sides of the screen. The commands are fixed and do not change during gameplay. Primarily, the game consists of still screens with the exception of the "finger wars", mazes and occasional animated character, such as the octoberry and fern birds. 

## Remix version

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Princess_Tomato_in_the_Salad_Kingdom&veaction=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Remix version") | [edit source](/w/index.php?title=Princess_Tomato_in_the_Salad_Kingdom&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section's source code: Remix version")]

Giovani Akira Faganello in association with Pixeltemple has created a PHP/HTML fan version of Princess Tomato in the Salad Kingdom called "Princess Tomato in the Salad Kingdom - REMIX". This version, which is currently offline, has updated graphics for the first level, and are made to match the original game graphics. Graphics for all other levels are the same as the original NES version and it mostly retains the same dialog from the original game.

[](/wiki/File:PTitSK_Ch6_map.png) | Aside from the fight with Bananda (located at **B**), there isn't a lot to discover in this maze.

Carrot Plateau |  | Advance ×3 | You will see the same scene for a while before something happens.   
War Melon army | Advance | Once you see the enemies, you must still advance to confront them.   
Enemy | Fight | Once again, you will be locked in a battle of Finger Wars. The enemy usually alternates between looking right and left, so if you beat them at Rock-Paper-Scissors, be sure to choose left or right, and never up or down.   
Minister Pumpkin | Fight | After defeating the regular enemies, you must face Minister Pumpkin in a Finger Wars fight. Like the previous enemies, the minister only ever looks left or right, although with less predictability than the previous enemy. Use this knowledge to your advantage and cut the fight as short as possible. If you succeed, your Dice-O-Matic will break down, and the minister will get away.

Percy will realize that the grass transforms you into whatever you were thinking about. He suggests that you transform into Farmies the next time you use the grass. By doing so, you will advance to the next chapter.

Princess Tomato | Talk ×2 | Her true identity revealed, the princess won't seem too troubled that you've discovered who she is. She will tell you that you need to regain the Turnip Emblem from Minister Pumpkin.   
Minister's door | Eggplant soldier | Fight ×2 | Defeat the Eggplant soldier in Finger Wars to advance to the room beyond the door. The Eggplant soldier's weakness is that he always look up.

Move to the door | There's little else of interest in this room.   
Minister's Bedroom |  | Look | You will see a large bed, a bookshelf, a trophy, and a table.   
Check the Bookshelf | You will see many books on it.   
Take a book | You will grab one of the books.   
Check the book | The book contains a particular clue about how to proceed through a maze when you stumble upon a baby monster.   
Check the Bed | The bed will move and a flight of stairs will be revealed below it.   
Check the Stairs | Before you're ready to move through them, you will be pushed down by someone.   
Dungeon  
[](/wiki/File:PTitSK_Ch8_map.png) | Your goal in this dungeon is to locate the papa monster and defeat him in battle. In order to do this, you will first have to locate the baby monster, and then proceed through the maze in a particular fashion in order to encounter the father. Throughout the dungeon, you are likely to encounter the mama several times at various dead-ends. You begin at the square labeled **S**. 

  * **M** : These are the squares where you will encounter the mama monster. The mother monster starts with three "points," and always looks left if defeated in the rock-paper-scissors portion of Finger Wars. Whenever you win, the mother runs off to another location.
  * **B** : This is the location where you will find the baby monster. Being so young, it does not pose a threat, but you must find him first, and the traverse the dungeon in a particular way to reach the father.
  * **P** : This is where the papa monster will appear and fight you. However, in order to find him there, you must start from the baby's location, turn around, and proceed through the dungeon travelling as straight as possible, turning only when you are forced to. Along the way, you will encounter three holes in the ground who each provide a message whenever you pass them. If you arrive at the **P** in any other manner, all you will find is a breeze coming through the wall.

  
Dungeon | Papa Monster | Fight | Like the Mama Monster, the Papa Monster will only ever look left. Other than having to defeat it more times to win, it is the same. When you win, you will return to the Minister's chambers.   
Minister's Bedroom | Minister Pumpkin | Look | You will finally come face to face with Minister Pumpkin   
Fight | You must defeat the Minister in Finger Wars. You have one advantage: the clue that the Green Pepper gave you in the prison. It happens to be the strategy that the Minister uses to fight. When he reaches the end of the sequence, he starts it right back over. To counter this sequence, all you must do is use paper, paper, rock, scissors, rock, scissors, scissors, paper, and so on until you defeat him.   
Talk | After defeating the minister, he will tell you that he does not have the Turnip Emblem, and that he gave it to his son.   
Princess' Chambers | Princess Tomato | Talk ×2 | She will ask about the Turnip Emblem, but ultimately, she will agree that it's time to escape.   
Garden |  | Move to exit | You will escape from the minister's compound and return to safety with the princess.
