## Chapter 3: Spirals

[MERMAID]

### Area Overview & Preparation

Spirals is an Aerial event with a 2:30 time limit [[gf1-2-8]]. A GUTS Show is made up of 4 events (the other mode being a Single Event) [[gf1-1-3]]; the sources do not say whether Spirals is always one of them. The event plays exactly like Attack, with no more and no less [[gf1-2-9]]. It also borrows the Slam Dunk method: press Down to the point you want on the power meter, then press B at the height of your jump [[gf2-6-2]].

The objective is to score the most points. Each spiral that goes through the "hoop" is worth 5 points [[gf2-6-1]]. Two achievements are tied to this chapter:

- **The Spiraly Thing Will Make You Go Insane:** finish the Spirals challenge and score, inside a GUTS Show [[gf2-6-1, gf1-1-3]].
- **Wowing the Crowd:** get 30+ points in Spirals using only specials [[gf1-2-9, gf1-2-10]].

> [!TIP]
> A one-player game is not an official game; it counts as training [[gf2-2-2]]. Because the first achievement is flagged as a Guts Show event, play the event inside a GUTS Show and not as a Single Event [[gf1-1-3, gf2-6-1]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** For Wowing the Crowd, the 30+ points must come from specials only. Do not take regular B shots during the run you are trying to use for this achievement [[gf1-2-9, gf1-2-10]]. If you slip, you can simply try again; this is not missable.

### Spirals

#### Basic shot method

1. Remember the Slam Dunk instructions: press Down to the desired point on the power meter, then go along with the ride and press B at the height of your jump [[gf2-6-2]].
2. For a reliable setup, go to the far right as far as you can, then push the power meter to the 5th notch, not much above or below it [[gf2-6-3]].
3. When you get close to the basket, release the shot [[gf2-6-3]].
4. Press Down on your way back so you do not fall off the padding [[gf2-6-4]].
5. Repeat this for every shot. Each spiral through the hoop is worth 5 points [[gf2-6-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:598950]

To earn this one, play the event inside a GUTS Show and finish the 2:30 run having scored at least once [[gf2-6-1, gf1-1-3, gf1-2-8]]. Using the far-right, 5th-notch setup above is the recommended way to score [[gf2-6-3]].

### Spirals specials (X then Y)

A special is harder in this event than in Attack, but it can be pulled off with practice [[gf1-2-10]]. To do a special you must press X and Y together: not at the same instant, but close to it [[gf1-2-10]]. If Spirals copies Attack's scoring, a special is worth 2 regular shots, or 10 points [[gf1-2-9]]. Unable to verify from collected sources: the guide states the 10-point value only for Attack, so watch your score after your first special.

#### Wowing the Crowd

1. Set up as for a normal shot (Slam Dunk method with Down on the meter), and keep to the same positioning on the padding [[gf2-6-2, gf2-6-3]].
2. Instead of pressing B, press X and Y close together, but not at the exact same moment [[gf1-2-10]].
3. Keep doing specials only. Do not use B. You need 30+ points from specials alone [[gf1-2-9, gf1-2-10]].
4. Press Down on the way back so you stay on the padding and do not waste time [[gf2-6-4]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:598961]

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Spirals:** Make sure both achievements have popped. The scoring run (The Spiraly Thing) must be done in a GUTS Show, and the specials-only run (Wowing the Crowd) needs 30+ points [[gf2-6-1, gf1-2-9, gf1-2-10]].
