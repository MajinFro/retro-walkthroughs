## Chapter 7: The Aggro Crag

[MERMAID]

### Area Overview & Preparation

The Aggro Crag is the final event of a GUTS Show. It is a Mountain event with a 7:00 time limit [[gf1-2-18]]. The guide calls it the hardest event in the entire game [[gf1-2-19]]. Another guide says it plays like Basic Training and Tornado Run, only harder than both [[gf2-8-6]]. This chapter covers the two achievements tied to it: **Piece of the Rock** and **Yodelayheehoo!**.

The objective is to jump on all 6 actuators in the fastest time. Each second under the 7-minute limit is worth 1 point [[gf2-8-4]]. You must jump on the actuators. Walking over them does not count [[gf2-8-5]]. Every actuator must be hit before the last one goes down [[gf1-2-20]].

A show has 4 events. The other mode is a Single Event [[gf1-1-3]]. To reach the Aggro Crag you need a GUTS Show, not a Single Event [[gf1-1-3, gf1-1-4]].

One guide calls a one-player game training rather than an official game [[gf2-2-2]], but in Guts Training you are still told you need 700 points to compete on the Aggro Crag [[gf2-2-3]]. No source says a one-player game blocks the Crag or its achievements.

Hazards on the climb:
- Water spouts are the vertical water things that drain some of your energy [[gf1-2-19]].
- Green rocks take a huge chunk out of your energy [[gf1-2-19]].
- Falling to the bottom of the course means climbing all the way back up [[gf1-2-19]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Yodelayheehoo! (reach the top of the Aggro Crag in 1:40 or less) can be missed if you do not qualify for the Crag. You need at least 700 points in a show. Falling even 1 point short means you miss the Aggro Crag [[gf1-1-4, gf2-2-3, gf2-8-4]]. Count your points through the earlier events of the show before the Crag begins.

### 700 points to reach the Aggro Crag

The qualifying rule is the same everywhere: you must have 700 points in a show to take on the Aggro Crag [[gf1-1-4]]. The Event Setups option shows scoring for events [[gf1-1-1]]. The scoring in the earlier events was covered in earlier chapters, so use it to track your total.

For this chapter, the sourced scoring rules are:
- Gym events give 1 point per second left on the clock, so 30 seconds left scores 30 points [[gf1-2-21]].
- On the Aggro Crag itself, each second left also gives 1 point [[gf1-2-21]].

Your score from the first events of the show must already reach 700 before the Crag starts. Starting with a GUTS Show, not a Single Event, is required [[gf1-1-4]].

> [!TIP]
> Before you start the Crag, check your points. If you are below 700, you will not get the Crag at all [[gf1-1-4]]. Restart the show and try again rather than hoping for a pass.

No source gives a step-by-step route or a split for the 1:40 climb. Unable to verify from collected sources. Only the following tips are sourced:

- Keep a steady head once the music starts. The music is adventurous and dramatic, but do not let the pressure get to you [[gf1-2-21]].
- The guide says not to worry about the clock, since 7:00 is a lot of time [[gf1-2-21]].
- On Advanced, take your time. A few spots are classic NES/SNES platforming where you cannot see what is ahead of you off the screen [[ra-comments-1-4]].

> [!NOTE]
> The 1:40 target for Yodelayheehoo! is far tighter than the 7:00 limit. The "take your time" advice suits Piece of the Rock. For Yodelayheehoo!, expect to need a clean run with no fall to the bottom [[gf1-2-19, ra-comments-1-5]]. No source provides a faster route.

### Aggro Crag: all 6 actuators

Actuators are GUTS's term for the lights, which in this event look like sewer covers [[gf1-2-21]]. Rules:

1. **JUMP** onto each of the 6 actuators. Walking over them does not register [[gf2-8-4, gf2-8-5]].
2. Hit every actuator before the last one goes down [[gf1-2-20]].
3. Do not skip one. The guide singles out missing one actuator as a problem, though its sentence is cut off [[gf1-3-3]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Missing even one actuator changes what you see at the top. Count the lit flags when you arrive, as described below [[gf1-2-22]].

### Aggro Crag summit flags

At the top you see a number of lit-up flags. This number is the number of actuators you hit [[gf1-2-21]]. If 5 of the 6 flags are lit, stomp on the last one and you beat the game [[gf1-2-22]].

Steps at the summit:
1. Reach the top of the Crag.
2. Count the lit flags [[gf1-2-22]].
3. If 5 of 6 are lit, stomp on the unlit flag [[gf1-2-22]].
4. You beat the game and take home a piece of the rock [[gf1-3-1]].

Reaching the top within 1:40 earns Yodelayheehoo!. The route to the top should be a clean climb with all actuators hit [[gf1-2-20, gf2-8-4]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:598967]

The timing requirement is reaching the top in 1:40 or less. Because the clock starts at 7:00 and each second left is worth a point, a fast climb also raises your score [[gf1-2-21]]. Unable to verify from collected sources: how many points a 1:40 climb scores.

### Piece of the Rock

Piece of the Rock requires completing the Aggro Crag Challenge and the GUTS Challenge [[gf1-2-23, gf1-3-1, gf2-8-4, gf2-8-5]]. Beating the game gives you a piece of the rock [[gf1-3-1]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** One guide says you still take home a glowing piece of the rock even if you do not reach the top [[gf1-2-23]]. Do not count on a failed climb for the achievement. It requires completing the Aggro Crag [[gf1-3-1]]. If you fail, retry. There is always a second chance [[gf1-3-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:598954]

After you finish, you get to see your piece of the Aggro Crag [[gf2-8-7]]. In a 2-player game, whoever has the most points at the end wins [[gf2-8-7]]. Two-player Aggro Crag scoring is 725 for 1st place and 325 for 2nd place [[gf2-8-8]]. No source says either achievement in this chapter needs a two-player game.

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave the Crag, confirm that both achievements have popped. If Yodelayheehoo! did not unlock, you will need a new show with 700+ points and a faster climb [[gf1-1-4, gf2-8-4]].
