## Chapter 6: Tornado Run

[MERMAID]

### Area Overview & Preparation

Tornado Run is a Gym event with a 7:00 time limit [[gf1-2-1]]. It is essentially Basic Training made much harder [[gf2-7-2]]. The goal is to reach the finish line in the fastest time, and every second under the 7:00 limit is worth 1 point [[gf2-7-1]]. Two achievements are tied to this event:

- **Tengu Man Would Be Proud:** finish the Tornado Run challenge by reaching the finish line, inside a GUTS Show [[gf2-7-1]].
- **Most Extreme Elimination Challenge:** reach the end in 2:25 or less [[gf1-2-4, gf1-2-5, gf2-7-2, gf2-7-4]].

A GUTS Show is the 4-event mode; the other mode is Single Event [[gf1-1-3]]. Choose the Show for the finish-line achievement. One guide calls a one-player game training rather than an official game [[gf2-2-2]]; no source says this affects achievements.

> [!TIP]
> **Practice:** The guide says you need to practice this event often to be great at it [[gf1-2-7]]. Unless you are a true beginner, you should not run out of time [[gf2-7-3]]. The 2:25 target is far tighter than the 7:00 limit, so expect to repeat the course.

### Controls

Tornado Run uses Left, Right, Down, Up, X and Y [[gf1-2-2]].

- **Left / Right:** move [[gf1-2-3]].
- **Up:** grab onto poles [[gf1-2-3]].
- **Down:** slide and crouch. To slide, hold the diagonal after you run [[gf1-2-3]].
- **X:** a Super Jump, bigger and more wacky than a basic jump [[gf1-2-4]].
- **Y:** a basic jump, smaller than the Super Jump but usually giving better foot placement [[gf1-2-5]].

A second guide mentions only X for jumping. It also says Down crouches, Left/Right crawl while crouching or run, and Up/Down climb a pole or rope [[gf2-7-4]]. If Y does not behave as the first guide describes, rely on X.

#### Running the course

1. Move with Left and Right, and use Up to grab poles as you meet them [[gf1-2-3]].
2. Jump with Y when you want precise footing, and with X when you need the bigger Super Jump [[gf1-2-4, gf1-2-5]].
3. Never get hit with no stamina left. That automatically fails the event [[gf1-2-6]]. If your stamina is low, stand still and let your character breathe to refill it, as in Basic Training [[gf1-2-6, gf2-5-7]].
4. Keep moving toward the finish line. Reaching it inside a GUTS Show is what unlocks the first achievement [[gf2-7-1]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Being hit while out of stamina fails the event automatically [[gf1-2-6]]. You can retry if you fail.

Reach the finish line during a GUTS Show to earn the first achievement [[gf2-7-1]].

#### The 2:25 time

To get under 2:25, the sourced advice is limited to the following. Use the Y jump for precise landings and the X Super Jump for bigger gaps, while avoiding hits [[gf1-2-4, gf1-2-5]]. Slide by holding the diagonal after you run [[gf1-2-3]]. Use Up and Down to climb poles and ropes [[gf2-7-4]]. Because Tornado Run is much harder than Basic Training, expect many attempts [[gf2-7-2, gf1-2-7]]. No source gives a split time or a target route for 2:25. Unable to verify from collected sources.

Finish the course in 2:25 or less to earn the second achievement [[gf1-2-4, gf1-2-5, gf2-7-2, gf2-7-4]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:598965]

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Tornado Run:** Confirm both achievements popped. If you cleared the course but were slower than 2:25, retry the event and practice until you can finish under 2:25 [[gf1-2-7, gf2-7-1]].

> [!NOTE]
> It's an Obstacle Course, Not a Scavenger Hunt and Artful Dodger accept any Track Challenge, so a Tornado Run attempt also counts. Chapter 5 does them in Basic Training because it is the slightly easier, shorter course [[gf1-2-13]].
