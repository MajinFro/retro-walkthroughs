## Chapter 5: Basic Training and the Bonus Round

[MERMAID]

### Area Overview & Preparation

Basic Training is a Gym event with a 6:00 time limit [[gf1-2-11]]. The goal is to reach the finish line in the fastest time, and every second left under the limit is worth 1 point [[gf2-4-1, gf1-2-12]]. It is a slightly easier, shorter version of Tornado Run, but you have less time, so your reflexes matter more [[gf1-2-13]]. Because of that, this is the best place to earn the "any Track Challenge" achievements: Move Out, Private!, Call Me Barry Allen, It's an Obstacle Course, Not a Scavenger Hunt, and Artful Dodger [[gf1-2-13]].

After Basic Training comes the Bonus Round, an Aerial event; one guide lists a 1:00 limit, though see the clock note under Strategy below [[gf1-2-24, ra-comments-16-8]]. It holds the two missable ball achievements.

This is a GUTS Show chapter. A GUTS Show is 4 events; the other mode is Single Event [[gf1-1-3]]. Move Out, Private! must be earned by reaching the finish line during a GUTS Show [[gf2-4-1]].

> [!TIP]
> **Hardcore advice:** Practice Basic Training before you commit to a full Show. The 2-player Basic Training differs from the 1-player version, so do your runs in a 1-player game [[gf2-5-8]]. A one-player game is training rather than an official game [[gf2-2-2]].

The five Track/Bonus challenges are not documented as combining in a single run. Plan separate attempts: one for finishing quickly, one for a clean run, and Bonus Round attempts afterward. Unable to verify from collected sources.

### Controls for Basic Training

- **Left/Right** move you. On a pole or rope, press the direction toward the side you are climbing to switch sides (press Right to switch to the left side). You jump in the direction you are facing when you leave a rope or pole [[gf2-4-2]].
- **X** jumps [[gf2-4-3]].
- **Up** looks up and climbs poles and ropes [[gf2-4-4]].
- You need an absolutely perfect position to grab a pole or rope, so keep trying if you cannot climb right away [[gf2-4-5]].
- **Down** crouches. Press Down with Left or Right in one fluid motion to slide under obstacles, which works better than pressing Down and then moving [[gf2-4-6]].

### The Energy Meter

The little orange character's meter is an energy meter. When it is full you run a little faster, and not by much [[gf2-5-5]].

The meter goes down from [[gf2-5-6]]:
1. Landing too hard on a jump
2. Sliding off a pole (more likely than on a rope)
3. Getting hit by a swinging GUTS bag
4. Falling down a gap
5. Running into the course

The meter refills when you stand still and let your character breathe, or when you take the jumping jack man's item [[gf2-5-7]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning (not missable):** Artful Dodger needs a finish in 3:20 or less with no bump or hit. The energy-drain list above (hard landings, sliding off poles, GUTS bag hits, gaps, running into the course) is your checklist of things to avoid [[gf2-5-6]]. In the related Tornado Run event, being hit without stamina automatically fails the event [[gf1-2-6]]. Retry any run that goes wrong.

The only route guidance in the sources is to follow the arrow signs; they will not mislead you [[gf2-5-1]]. Room-by-room obstacle positions: Unable to verify from collected sources.

1. Start the course and run in the direction the arrow signs point [[gf2-5-1]].
2. Climb poles and ropes with Up, line yourself up precisely, and switch sides by pressing toward the side you are climbing [[gf2-4-2, gf2-4-4, gf2-4-5]].
3. Slide under low obstacles by pressing Down plus Left or Right in one fluid motion [[gf2-4-6]].
4. Jump with X, and avoid landing too hard [[gf2-4-3, gf2-5-6]].
5. Grab every stopwatch you pass; they give you more time to finish, which can mean more points [[gf2-5-3]].
6. Avoid the swinging GUTS bags and gaps [[gf2-5-6]].
7. If the meter drops, stand still briefly so your character breathes and refills it [[gf2-5-7]].

#### Stopwatch pickups

🗺️ **Map:** Use the Basic Training variant maps above [[map-basic-training-variant1-1-1]].

Stopwatches are time bonuses that give you more time to complete the course [[gf2-5-3]]. Take every one for the pickup achievement.

#### Jumping jack man (meter refill)

The jumping jack man refills the meter, but grabbing him only buys about 5 seconds of faster running before hard jumping drains life again [[gf2-5-4]]. For It's an Obstacle Course, Not a Scavenger Hunt you need every pickup, so grab him on a dedicated pickup run [[gf2-5-4]]. On a speed run you can skip him [[gf2-5-4]].

### Bonus Round

🗺️ **Map:** [View Bonus Round1 Map by zagato blackfist](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/SuperNES/NickelodeonGUTS-BonusRound1.png) [[map-bonus-round1-1-1]]

🗺️ **Map:** [View Bonus Round2 Map by zagato blackfist](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/SuperNES/NickelodeonGUTS-BonusRound2.png) [[map-bonus-round2-1-1]]

The Bonus Event is an Aerial event (listed in one guide with a 1:00 limit). You knock "baseballs" off the wall, much like "Off The Wall" from the show. Hold Up to jump, Left and Right to move, and let your out-of-control character knock them off [[gf1-2-24]]. Each ball is worth 1 bonus point [[gf1-2-25]]. Knocking off every ball is extremely hard and gives 1 point per second left on the clock; otherwise you only get 1 point per ball [[gf1-2-26, gf1-2-27]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Similar Quality to Ballz (598963) and Gotta Collect 'Em All (598962) must be earned in the Bonus Round before its clock runs out. A failed attempt means running Basic Training again to get another try [[gf1-2-24, gf1-2-26, gf1-2-27, ra-comments-16-8]]. For Gotta Collect 'Em All, every ball must be off the wall with at least 35 seconds left on the clock [[ra-comments-1-1, ra-comments-16-1]].

#### Strategy

- Gotta Collect 'Em All needs 0:35 or more on the Bonus Round clock when the last ball falls. Go by the on-screen clock: one guide lists a 1:00 limit for the Bonus Event [[gf1-2-24]], but a player who mastered the set describes the target as clearing every ball in 55 seconds with 35 or more left, which implies a longer clock [[ra-comments-1-1]]. Unable to verify from collected sources: the Bonus Round's starting time. You need to be efficient from start to finish [[ra-comments-1-2]].
- The arm hitbox can surprise you when collecting balls, but the achievement is doable [[ra-comments-1-3]].
- Bounce so you clear each vertical section of balls as you go, constantly moving forward to the next [[ra-comments-16-2]].
- Leave only a couple of balls in a line on each wall, then jump up into them as you move forward to the next wall [[ra-comments-16-3]].
- You may not always bounce forward and can end up stopped in a vertical hang, desperately recovering bounce momentum [[ra-comments-16-4]].
- The smallest board takes 2 swipes, the tall narrow board can take 2 if it is not the first board, and the big boards take at least 3, often 4 [[ra-comments-16-9]].
- Board order is random, which matters at the start because you begin with 0 momentum [[ra-comments-16-7]]. A new draw means running Basic Training again [[ra-comments-16-8]].
- Character choice made no noticeable difference here [[ra-comments-16-11]].
- The requirement was raised from 25 to 35 seconds because 25 was too close to the wire. One player cleared it with 39.4 seconds left, so there is some room for error [[ra-comments-16-1, ra-comments-16-5]]. A logic problem meant the achievement would not have triggered earlier; it was fixed [[ra-comments-16-13]].
- Practice teaches ball positions, spacing between ball boards and bounce behavior [[ra-comments-16-6]].
