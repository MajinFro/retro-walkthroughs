## Chapter 1: Game Setup and Options

[MERMAID]

### Area Overview & Preparation

This chapter covers everything you do before your first event: the options screen, the choice between a GUTS Show and a Single Event, picking a color, and picking a character. Nothing here is a combat or exploration area, so there are no chests, enemies, or bosses. The one thing to take away is the 700-point rule for the Aggro Crag, described below. [[gf1-1-4]]

The game supports 2-player play, but only 2 players can play at once. [[gf2-1-1, gf1-1-1]]

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning (Aggro Crag access):** You must have at least 700 points in a show to take on the Aggro Crag. Falling even 1 point short means you miss it. Treat every event in a show as points you cannot afford to waste. [[gf1-1-4]]

> [!TIP]
> The Aggro Crag's name means "Aggressive Rock." [[gf1-1-5]]

### Options Screen

On the opening screen, move up and down to pick an option and press A to change it.

Unable to verify from collected sources: the exact button list for changing options beyond A (one guide says A and Start are the only ones the author knows). [[gf1-1-1]]

The options, in order:

1. **Player (1 Player Game / 2 Player Game):** This sets the number of players, and only 2 can play at once. [[gf1-1-1]] A one-player game is not an official game; you are in training. [[gf2-2-2]]
2. **Level (Beginner / Advanced):** The screen does not label this "Level." Beginner is better if you are new, and Advanced is the harder setting. [[gf1-1-2]] If you are going for the Advanced-related achievement, note that the Aggro Crag on Advanced has spots where you cannot see what is ahead off-screen, so take your time there. [[ra-comments-1-4]]
3. **Music (On/Off):** Toggles the menu, in-game, and description music. [[gf1-1-1]] (Unable to verify from collected sources: any achievement effect of this setting.)
4. **Event Setups (On/Off):** With it on, you are shown the event's scoring. Turning it off hides the event's name, object, and scoring. Leave it on while you learn the game. [[gf1-1-1, gf2-2-1]]
5. **Start Game:** Confirms the options and moves on. [[gf1-1-1]]

### GUTS Show vs. Single Event

After Start Game you choose between two modes: a GUTS Show, which is 4 events including the Aggro Crag at the end, or a Single Event, where you practice one event. [[gf1-1-3]]

The sources do not say whether Single Event attempts count toward the individual event challenges that are not tied to a full show. Unable to verify from collected sources.

In a show, you need 700 points to reach the Aggro Crag. [[gf1-1-4, gf2-2-3]]

### Color Selection

Once you have chosen GUTS Show or Single Event, the game asks you to pick a color. [[gf2-2-5]]

1. Move to any color you like. There is no advantage to any color; it is just a style choice. [[gf2-2-5]]
2. Confirm it. The game then moves on to the character selection screen. [[gf1-1-3, gf2-2-5]]

No achievement depends on the color, so you can keep the same one for every show, including the Advanced show in Chapter 8. [[gf2-2-5]]

### GUTS Show: The Events

A show runs 4 events. Here is what the collected sources say about each one.

- **Attack** (Aerial, 2:30): Score the most points, 5 per point made. It uses the same controls as Slam Dunk and Spirals: charge the meter, press B to shoot, press Down to return to the padding. [[gf1-1-16, gf2-8-1, gf2-8-2]]
- **Tornado Run** (Gym, 7:00): Reach the finish in the fastest time; each second under 7:00 is worth 1 point. It is like Basic Training but much more challenging. [[gf1-2-1, gf2-7-1, gf2-7-2]]
- **Spirals** (Aerial, 2:30): The same as Attack, no more and no less. Each spiral in the hoop is worth 5 points. [[gf1-2-8, gf1-2-9, gf2-6-1]]
- **Basic Training** (Gym, 6:00): Reach the finish quickly; each second under 6:00 is worth 1 point. Follow the arrow signs, which will not mislead you. [[gf1-2-11, gf2-4-1, gf2-5-1]]
- **Slam Dunk** (Aerial, 2:30): Each made shot is worth 5 points. It is the easiest event in the game. [[gf1-2-14, gf2-3-1, gf1-2-17]]

Detailed strategy for each event appears in the chapters that cover them.
