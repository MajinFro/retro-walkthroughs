## Game Overview


### Premise

Nickelodeon Guts is the SNES game based on the kids' sports game show. Kids compete in off-the-wall athletic events, and the big finale is a huge mountain called the Aggro Crag, where the winner receives a piece of that "radical rock" [[gf2-1-1]]. The game supports two-player play [[gf2-1-1]]. This set has 20 achievements worth 185 points.

The events fall into two families:

- **Aerial events:** Slam Dunk, Spirals and Attack. You charge a power meter, jump and shoot at a target. The Bonus Event is also classed as Aerial [[gf1-2-24, gf2-8-2]].
- **Running events:** Basic Training, Tornado Run and the Aggro Crag. You race a course against the clock and score 1 point for each second you have left [[gf2-4-2, gf2-7-2, gf2-8-4]].

### Setting up a game

On the opening screen, the options you can set are:

- **Players:** Only 2 can play at once [[gf1-1-1]]. A one-player game is not an official game; it is training [[gf2-2-2]].
- **Level:** Beginner or Advanced, with Advanced being harder [[gf1-1-2]]. Only the Do You Have It? achievement (598955) needs Advanced [[gf1-1-2]].
- **Event Setups:** Leave this on. Turning it off hides each event's name, object and scoring [[gf2-2-1]].
- **Game type:** Choose a GUTS Show (4 events) or a Single Event [[gf1-1-3]].
- **Color:** Gives no gameplay advantage [[gf2-2-5]].
- **Character:** Each profile has a favorite sport that may help in events featuring that sport, but this is unproven [[gf2-2-4]]. Basketball characters are ideal for It's All about That Rebound [[ra-comments-12-4]]. Character choice made no noticeable difference in the Bonus Round [[ra-comments-16-11]].
- **Name:** Up to 8 letters [[gf2-2-6]].

### Core mechanics

**Aerial events (Slam Dunk, Spirals, Attack).**
- Hold Down to fill the power meter, release, then press B near the target to shoot [[gf2-3-2, gf2-8-2]].
- Always hold Down on the way back so you land safely on the padding. If you don't hold it enough, you can stagger or stumble off the padding, which costs time [[gf2-3-3, gf2-3-4]].
- In Slam Dunk, B is a regular shot. X and Y together make a special shot, which is actually easier than a regular shot [[gf1-2-15]].
- In Attack, regular shots are worth 5 points and specials 10. Specials are harder to hit and need good timing [[gf1-1-17, gf1-1-18]].
- Spirals plays the same way as Attack. To do a special there, press X and Y close together but not simultaneously [[gf1-2-9, gf1-2-10]].

**Running events (Basic Training, Tornado Run, Aggro Crag).**
- Basic Training scores 1 point for every second left, so 30 seconds left is 30 points [[gf1-2-12]].
- Tornado Run is like Basic Training but much more challenging [[gf2-7-2]].
- The Aggro Crag is even harder than both [[gf2-8-6]].
- You must be in a perfect position to grab a pole or rope, so keep trying if you cannot climb right away [[gf2-4-5]].
- Press the d-pad toward the side you are climbing to switch sides [[gf2-4-2]].
- Down crouches. Down with Left or Right in one fluid motion slides under obstacles, which works better than pressing Down and then moving [[gf2-4-6]].
- The energy meter only makes you run a little faster when full [[gf2-5-5]]. Hard landings, sliding off poles, GUTS bag hits, gaps and running into the course lower it [[gf2-5-6]]. Standing still or taking the jumping jack man's item refills it [[gf2-5-7]].
- In Tornado Run, being hit without stamina automatically fails the event [[gf1-2-6]].
- In Basic Training, follow the arrow signs; they won't mislead you [[gf2-5-1]].
- In the Aggro Crag, you must jump on every one of the 6 actuators. Walking over them does not count [[gf2-8-4, gf2-8-5]].

### Hardcore Mode

Play with RetroAchievements Hardcore Mode on. That means no save states, no cheats and no rewinds. Nothing in the collected sources describes a way to save mid-event, so expect to repeat an event from the start if you fail. Retrying the Bonus Round means running through Basic Training again [[ra-comments-16-8]]. Per the community comments, the Bonus Round board order is random [[ra-comments-16-7]].

### The 700-point rule

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** You need at least 700 points in a show to reach the Aggro Crag. Falling even 1 point short means you miss it [[gf1-1-4, gf2-2-3]]. Aggro Crag achievements are not available if a show falls short, so score well in the earlier events. If you fall short, start another show.

### The 100% plan in plain language

1. **Chapter 1 – Game Setup and Options:** Get the options right (Beginner for most of the set, Event Setups on) [[gf1-1-2, gf2-2-1]].
2. **Chapters 2–5 – The earlier events:** Slam Dunk, Spirals, Attack, and Basic Training with the Bonus Round. The sources do not say which events a show contains or in what order, so keep playing shows until every event achievement has unlocked. Unable to verify from collected sources.
3. **Chapter 6 – Tornado Run.**
4. **Chapter 7 – The Aggro Crag,** reached only with 700+ points in a show [[gf1-1-4]].
5. **Chapter 8 – Advanced GUTS Challenge:** Do You Have It? requires the Advanced level [[gf1-1-2]].

Plan on at least two playthroughs. Run Beginner shows (as many as you need) for the event, Bonus Round and Aggro Crag achievements. Then run one show on Advanced for Do You Have It? No source reports achievements that lock each other out.

### Toughest challenge

The hardest achievement is widely regarded to be Gotta Collect 'Em All: knock off every ball in the Bonus Round, finishing with 35 or more seconds left on the clock [[ra-comments-1-1, ra-comments-16-1]]. One guide lists a 1:00 time limit for the Bonus Event [[gf1-2-24]], but the mastery comment implies a longer clock (clearing in 55 seconds with 35 left), so go by the on-screen clock [[ra-comments-1-1]]. Knocking off every ball is extremely hard and pays 1 point per second left [[gf1-2-26]]. If you miss any ball, you only get 1 point per ball knocked off [[gf1-2-27]].

Basic Training, Tornado Run and the Aggro Crag can be fun to optimize despite the jank movement [[ra-comments-1-6]].

### Area Overview & Preparation

This chapter covers everything you do before your first event: the options screen, the choice between a GUTS Show and a Single Event, picking a color, and picking a character. Nothing here is a combat or exploration area, so there are no chests, enemies, or bosses. The one thing to take away is the 700-point rule for the Aggro Crag, described below. [[gf1-1-4]]

The game supports 2-player play, but only 2 players can play at once. [[gf2-1-1, gf1-1-1]]

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning (Aggro Crag access):** You must have at least 700 points in a show to take on the Aggro Crag. Falling even 1 point short means you miss it. Treat every event in a show as points you cannot afford to waste. [[gf1-1-4]]

> [!TIP]
> The Aggro Crag's name means "Aggressive Rock." [[gf1-1-5]]

### Options Screen

On the opening screen, move up and down to pick an option and press A to change it.

Unable to verify from collected sources: the exact button list for changing options beyond A (one guide says A and Start are the only ones the author knows). [[gf1-1-1]]

The options, in order:

1. **Player (1 Player Game / 2 Player Game):** This sets the number of players, and only 2 can play at once. [[gf1-1-1]] A one-player game is not an official game; you are in training. [[gf2-2-2]]
2. **Level (Beginner / Advanced):** The screen does not label this "Level." Beginner is better if you are new, and Advanced is the harder setting. [[gf1-1-2]] If you are going for the Advanced-related achievement, note that the Aggro Crag on Advanced has spots where you cannot see what is ahead off-screen, so take your time there. [[ra-comments-1-4]]
3. **Music (On/Off):** Toggles the menu, in-game, and description music. [[gf1-1-1]] (Unable to verify from collected sources: any achievement effect of this setting.)
4. **Event Setups (On/Off):** With it on, you are shown the event's scoring. Turning it off hides the event's name, object, and scoring. Leave it on while you learn the game. [[gf1-1-1, gf2-2-1]]
5. **Start Game:** Confirms the options and moves on. [[gf1-1-1]]

### GUTS Show vs. Single Event

After Start Game you choose between two modes: a GUTS Show, which is 4 events including the Aggro Crag at the end, or a Single Event, where you practice one event. [[gf1-1-3]]

The sources do not say whether Single Event attempts count toward the individual event challenges that are not tied to a full show. Unable to verify from collected sources.

In a show, you need 700 points to reach the Aggro Crag. [[gf1-1-4, gf2-2-3]]

### Color Selection

Once you have chosen GUTS Show or Single Event, the game asks you to pick a color. [[gf2-2-5]]

1. Move to any color you like. There is no advantage to any color; it is just a style choice. [[gf2-2-5]]
2. Confirm it. The game then moves on to the character selection screen. [[gf1-1-3, gf2-2-5]]

No achievement depends on the color, so you can keep the same one for every show, including the Advanced show in Chapter 8. [[gf2-2-5]]

### GUTS Show: The Events

A show runs 4 events. Here is what the collected sources say about each one.

- **Attack** (Aerial, 2:30): Score the most points, 5 per point made. It uses the same controls as Slam Dunk and Spirals: charge the meter, press B to shoot, press Down to return to the padding. [[gf1-1-16, gf2-8-1, gf2-8-2]]
- **Tornado Run** (Gym, 7:00): Reach the finish in the fastest time; each second under 7:00 is worth 1 point. It is like Basic Training but much more challenging. [[gf1-2-1, gf2-7-1, gf2-7-2]]
- **Spirals** (Aerial, 2:30): The same as Attack, no more and no less. Each spiral in the hoop is worth 5 points. [[gf1-2-8, gf1-2-9, gf2-6-1]]
- **Basic Training** (Gym, 6:00): Reach the finish quickly; each second under 6:00 is worth 1 point. Follow the arrow signs, which will not mislead you. [[gf1-2-11, gf2-4-1, gf2-5-1]]
- **Slam Dunk** (Aerial, 2:30): Each made shot is worth 5 points. It is the easiest event in the game. [[gf1-2-14, gf2-3-1, gf1-2-17]]

Detailed strategy for each event appears in the chapters that cover them.

### Area Overview & Preparation

Slam Dunk is an Aerial event with a 2:30 time limit [[gf1-2-14]]. The goal is to score the most points, and every shot you make is worth 5 points [[gf2-3-1]]. The guide calls it the easiest event in the game, even though it can feel frustrating at first [[gf1-2-17]]. That makes it the best place to pick up the "any Aerial Challenge" achievements as well as the Slam Dunk ones.

This chapter covers four achievements:

- **Come On and Slam** (598949): finish the Slam Dunk Challenge and score, inside a GUTS Show [[gf2-3-1]].
- **We Wanna Be Like Mike** (598959): get 30+ points in Slam Dunk using only special shots [[gf1-2-15]].
- **Like Quickdraw McGraw, Only, Uh, for Sports?** (598957): land 5 specials in a row on any Aerial Challenge [[gf1-1-18, gf1-2-15]].
- **It's All about That Rebound** (598958): get 20+ successful shots in any Aerial Challenge without tripping off the platform [[ra-comments-12-1]].

A GUTS Show is made up of 4 events; the other mode is a Single Event [[gf1-1-3]]. Come On and Slam is a Guts Show achievement, so Slam Dunk must be played as part of a GUTS Show and you must score. A one-player game is not official: it counts as training [[gf2-2-2]]. Unable to verify from collected sources whether training affects achievement unlocking.

Character choice matters slightly for It's All about That Rebound. Two characters have basketball as their favorite sport, which is ideal because they get ready slightly faster after climbing back onto the platform [[ra-comments-12-4]]. Per the character lists, these are character 2 (male, 98 lbs, 5'1") and character 3 or 8 depending on the guide's numbering (male, 110 lbs, 5'4") [[gf1-1-9, gf1-1-10]]. Pick either of the two male basketball players on the selection screen.

> [!TIP]
> There is no advantage to any particular GUTS color; it is only style [[gf2-2-5]].

### Slam Dunk

🗺️ **Map:** [View Slam Dunk Map by zagato blackfist](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/SuperNES/NickelodeonGUTS-SlamDunk.png) [[map-slam-dunk-1-1]]

#### Controls

1. While on the big padding, move left or right across it [[gf2-3-1]].
2. Press and hold Down until the power meter (on the left side of the screen) is filled to your liking, then release Down [[gf2-3-2]].
3. Wait until you are near the basket before pressing B to shoot [[gf2-3-2]].
4. **Hold Down on the way back.** The power meter will move up a little, and if done correctly you land back on the padding safely [[gf2-3-3]]. If you skip it or don't hold enough, you may stagger onto the padding or even stumble off, costing valuable time [[gf2-3-4]].

#### Shot types

- **B** makes a regular shot, which is a bit harder than a special [[gf1-2-15]].
- **X and Y together** make a special shot, which is actually easier than the regular shot in this event [[gf1-2-15]].

#### Recommended setup

Move to the extreme left of the platform, then hold Down to about the 4th or 5th notch of the meter, let go of Down, shoot, and let the ball go in [[gf1-2-16]].

### Slam Dunk special shots (X+Y)

Because specials are easier than regular shots here, use X+Y for everything in this chapter [[gf1-2-15]]. Elsewhere, specials are harder to hit and need good timing [[gf1-1-18]], so Slam Dunk is the friendliest place to chain them.

#### Plan for the special-focused achievements

1. Start a **GUTS Show** (not a Single Event) so the run also counts for Come On and Slam [[gf1-1-3, gf2-3-1]].
2. Pick one of the two basketball characters [[ra-comments-12-4]].
3. Move to the far left and set the 4th/5th notch every time [[gf1-2-16]].
4. Shoot using X+Y only, and always hold Down on the return trip [[gf1-2-15, gf2-3-3]].


> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> **Like Quickdraw McGraw, Only, Uh, for Sports? (10 Points)**
> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/681039.png)
> *On any Aerial Challenge, land 5 specials in a row*

Land 5 specials in a row. Because X+Y is easier than B here, simply never use B and repeat the same setup until five consecutive specials go in [[gf1-2-15, gf1-1-18]].


> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> **We Wanna Be Like Mike (10 Points)**
> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/681041.png)
> *In Slam Dunk, get 30+ points using only special shots*

For We Wanna Be Like Mike you need 30+ points using only special shots. ### Area Overview & Preparation

Attack is an Aerial event with a 2:30 time limit [[gf1-1-16]]. You shoot balls into a square frame with a net in it, using a lacrosse stick (the guide's author is not certain it is lacrosse) [[gf2-8-1]]. The goal is to score the most points inside the time limit [[gf2-8-1]].

This chapter has two achievements. **What Is This, an Action RPG?** requires you to finish the Attack Challenge and score, and it must be done inside a GUTS Show [[gf2-8-1]]. **Sky Dancer** requires 30+ points in Attack using only special shots [[gf1-1-17, gf1-1-18]].

The Show is the mode with 4 events, the other being a Single Event [[gf1-1-3]]. Sky Dancer can be retried in any run of Attack, since it only needs the special-only point total [[gf1-1-17]].

### Scoring and Controls

| Shot type | Button | Points | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Regular shot | B | 5 | [[gf1-1-17]] |
| Special shot | X and Y, pressed close together (see below) | 10 (worth two regular shots) | [[gf1-1-17, gf1-2-9, gf1-2-10]] |

- Attack uses the same controls as Slam Dunk and Spirals: hold Down to charge the meter, press B to shoot, and press Down to get back up on the padding [[gf2-8-2]].

### Attack

🗺️ **Map:** [View Attack Map by zagato blackfist](https://www.vgmaps.com/Atlas/SuperNES/NickelodeonGUTS-Attack.png) [[map-attack-1-1]]

Attack is a single arena. You stay on the padding, charge the meter, and shoot at the net. There are no chests or items in this event. Two guides offer different shooting positions; try one, and switch to the other if it is not working for you.

#### Option A: Far left, 4th-5th notch

1. Move to the far left of the padding, until your character can't move any farther [[gf1-1-19]].
2. Hold Down until the power meter sits roughly between the 4th and 5th notch. The notches are the long stripes on the meter, which looks like a ruler [[gf1-1-19]].
3. Shoot with B. The guide says you will hit more shots from this spot [[gf1-1-19]].

#### Option B: Far right, full meter at the top of the jump

1. Move to the far right side of the padding [[gf2-8-3]].
2. Hold Down to fill the meter to its maximum [[gf2-8-3]].
3. Shoot at the very top of your jump [[gf2-8-3]].

This was the approach that worked for that guide's author, who notes that you may find something else that works for you [[gf2-8-3]].

#### Getting Back Up

After each shot, press Down to get back up on the padding [[gf2-8-2]]. The related Slam Dunk event warns that if you skip this or don't do it enough, you may stagger onto the padding or stumble off, costing valuable time [[gf2-3-4]].

### Attack Specials (10 points)

Specials are the key to Sky Dancer [[gf1-1-17]].

1. Use whichever position above gives you the most consistent hits, because specials are the hardest shots to land and depend on timing [[gf1-1-18]].
2. Your character performs a special move when you throw one [[gf1-1-17]]. A successful special is worth 10 points [[gf1-1-17]].
3. Count only the special points. Reach 30 or more from specials alone, which means at least three landed specials [[gf1-1-17]].
4. **Input:** press X and Y close together, but not at the exact same instant. The guide says Spirals is done exactly the same way as Attack, and gives this X-then-Y input for the Spirals special [[gf1-2-9, gf1-2-10]]. Do not press B during a Sky Dancer attempt.

You have 2:30 for the whole event, so do not spend too long on a single attempt [[gf1-1-16]].

The five Track/Bonus challenges are not documented as combining in a single run. Plan separate attempts: one for finishing quickly, one for a clean run, and Bonus Round attempts afterward. Unable to verify from collected sources.

### Controls for Basic Training

- **Left/Right** move you. On a pole or rope, press the direction toward the side you are climbing to switch sides (press Right to switch to the left side). You jump in the direction you are facing when you leave a rope or pole [[gf2-4-2]].
- **X** jumps [[gf2-4-3]].
- **Up** looks up and climbs poles and ropes [[gf2-4-4]].
- You need an absolutely perfect position to grab a pole or rope, so keep trying if you cannot climb right away [[gf2-4-5]].
- **Down** crouches. Press Down with Left or Right in one fluid motion to slide under obstacles, which works better than pressing Down and then moving [[gf2-4-6]].

### The Energy Meter

The little orange character's meter is an energy meter. When it is full you run a little faster, and not by much [[gf2-5-5]].

The meter goes down from [[gf2-5-6]]:
1. Landing too hard on a jump
2. Sliding off a pole (more likely than on a rope)
3. Getting hit by a swinging GUTS bag
4. Falling down a gap
5. Running into the course

The meter refills when you stand still and let your character breathe, or when you take the jumping jack man's item [[gf2-5-7]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning (not missable):** Artful Dodger needs a finish in 3:20 or less with no bump or hit. The energy-drain list above (hard landings, sliding off poles, GUTS bag hits, gaps, running into the course) is your checklist of things to avoid [[gf2-5-6]]. In the related Tornado Run event, being hit without stamina automatically fails the event [[gf1-2-6]]. Retry any run that goes wrong.

#### Strategy

- Gotta Collect 'Em All needs 0:35 or more on the Bonus Round clock when the last ball falls. Go by the on-screen clock: one guide lists a 1:00 limit for the Bonus Event [[gf1-2-24]], but a player who mastered the set describes the target as clearing every ball in 55 seconds with 35 or more left, which implies a longer clock [[ra-comments-1-1]]. Unable to verify from collected sources: the Bonus Round's starting time. You need to be efficient from start to finish [[ra-comments-1-2]].
- The arm hitbox can surprise you when collecting balls, but the achievement is doable [[ra-comments-1-3]].
- Bounce so you clear each vertical section of balls as you go, constantly moving forward to the next [[ra-comments-16-2]].
- Leave only a couple of balls in a line on each wall, then jump up into them as you move forward to the next wall [[ra-comments-16-3]].
- You may not always bounce forward and can end up stopped in a vertical hang, desperately recovering bounce momentum [[ra-comments-16-4]].
- The smallest board takes 2 swipes, the tall narrow board can take 2 if it is not the first board, and the big boards take at least 3, often 4 [[ra-comments-16-9]].
- Board order is random, which matters at the start because you begin with 0 momentum [[ra-comments-16-7]]. A new draw means running Basic Training again [[ra-comments-16-8]].
- Character choice made no noticeable difference here [[ra-comments-16-11]].
- The requirement was raised from 25 to 35 seconds because 25 was too close to the wire. One player cleared it with 39.4 seconds left, so there is some room for error [[ra-comments-16-1, ra-comments-16-5]]. A logic problem meant the achievement would not have triggered earlier; it was fixed [[ra-comments-16-13]].
- Practice teaches ball positions, spacing between ball boards and bounce behavior [[ra-comments-16-6]].

## Chapter 6: Tornado Run

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  Tornado_Run["Tornado Run"]
  classDef ponr fill:#7f1d1d,stroke:#f87171,color:#fff;
  classDef boss fill:#4c1d95,stroke:#a78bfa,color:#fff;
```

### Area Overview & Preparation

Tornado Run is a Gym event with a 7:00 time limit [[gf1-2-1]]. It is essentially Basic Training made much harder [[gf2-7-2]]. The goal is to reach the finish line in the fastest time, and every second under the 7:00 limit is worth 1 point [[gf2-7-1]]. Two achievements are tied to this event:

- **Tengu Man Would Be Proud:** finish the Tornado Run challenge by reaching the finish line, inside a GUTS Show [[gf2-7-1]].
- **Most Extreme Elimination Challenge:** reach the end in 2:25 or less [[gf1-2-4, gf1-2-5, gf2-7-2, gf2-7-4]].

A GUTS Show is the 4-event mode; the other mode is Single Event [[gf1-1-3]]. Choose the Show for the finish-line achievement. One guide calls a one-player game training rather than an official game [[gf2-2-2]]; no source says this affects achievements.

> [!TIP]
> **Practice:** The guide says you need to practice this event often to be great at it [[gf1-2-7]]. Unless you are a true beginner, you should not run out of time [[gf2-7-3]]. The 2:25 target is far tighter than the 7:00 limit, so expect to repeat the course.

### Controls

Tornado Run uses Left, Right, Down, Up, X and Y [[gf1-2-2]].

- **Left / Right:** move [[gf1-2-3]].
- **Up:** grab onto poles [[gf1-2-3]].
- **Down:** slide and crouch. To slide, hold the diagonal after you run [[gf1-2-3]].
- **X:** a Super Jump, bigger and more wacky than a basic jump [[gf1-2-4]].
- **Y:** a basic jump, smaller than the Super Jump but usually giving better foot placement [[gf1-2-5]].

A second guide mentions only X for jumping. It also says Down crouches, Left/Right crawl while crouching or run, and Up/Down climb a pole or rope [[gf2-7-4]]. If Y does not behave as the first guide describes, rely on X.

### Tornado Run

Study them before your attempt. No source describes which variant appears when, or an obstacle-by-obstacle route. Unable to verify from collected sources.

#### Running the course

1. Move with Left and Right, and use Up to grab poles as you meet them [[gf1-2-3]].
2. Jump with Y when you want precise footing, and with X when you need the bigger Super Jump [[gf1-2-4, gf1-2-5]].
3. Never get hit with no stamina left. That automatically fails the event [[gf1-2-6]]. If your stamina is low, stand still and let your character breathe to refill it, as in Basic Training [[gf1-2-6, gf2-5-7]].
4. Keep moving toward the finish line. Reaching it inside a GUTS Show is what unlocks the first achievement [[gf2-7-1]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Being hit while out of stamina fails the event automatically [[gf1-2-6]]. You can retry if you fail.

Reach the finish line during a GUTS Show to earn the first achievement [[gf2-7-1]].


> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> **Tengu Man Would Be Proud (5 Points)**
> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/681035.png)
> *Finish the Tornado Run Challenge (must reach finish line) [Guts Show]*


#### The 2:25 time

To get under 2:25, the sourced advice is limited to the following. Use the Y jump for precise landings and the X Super Jump for bigger gaps, while avoiding hits [[gf1-2-4, gf1-2-5]]. Slide by holding the diagonal after you run [[gf1-2-3]]. Use Up and Down to climb poles and ropes [[gf2-7-4]]. Because Tornado Run is much harder than Basic Training, expect many attempts [[gf2-7-2, gf1-2-7]]. No source gives a split time or a target route for 2:25. Unable to verify from collected sources.

Finish the course in 2:25 or less to earn the second achievement [[gf1-2-4, gf1-2-5, gf2-7-2, gf2-7-4]].


> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> **Most Extreme Elimination Challenge (10 Points)**
> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/681047.png)
> *In Tornado Run, get to the end in 2:25 or less*


> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Tornado Run:** Confirm both achievements popped. If you cleared the course but were slower than 2:25, retry the event and practice until you can finish under 2:25 [[gf1-2-7, gf2-7-1]].

> [!NOTE]
> It's an Obstacle Course, Not a Scavenger Hunt and Artful Dodger accept any Track Challenge, so a Tornado Run attempt also counts. Chapter 5 does them in Basic Training because it is the slightly easier, shorter course [[gf1-2-13]].

## Chapter 7: The Aggro Crag

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  Aggro_Crag["Aggro Crag"]
  classDef ponr fill:#7f1d1d,stroke:#f87171,color:#fff;
  classDef boss fill:#4c1d95,stroke:#a78bfa,color:#fff;
```

### Area Overview & Preparation

The Aggro Crag is the final event of a GUTS Show. It is a Mountain event with a 7:00 time limit [[gf1-2-18]]. The guide calls it the hardest event in the entire game [[gf1-2-19]]. Another guide says it plays like Basic Training and Tornado Run, only harder than both [[gf2-8-6]]. This chapter covers the two achievements tied to it: **Piece of the Rock** and **Yodelayheehoo!**.

The objective is to jump on all 6 actuators in the fastest time. Each second under the 7-minute limit is worth 1 point [[gf2-8-4]]. You must jump on the actuators. Walking over them does not count [[gf2-8-5]]. Every actuator must be hit before the last one goes down [[gf1-2-20]].

A show has 4 events. The other mode is a Single Event [[gf1-1-3]]. To reach the Aggro Crag you need a GUTS Show, not a Single Event [[gf1-1-3, gf1-1-4]].

One guide calls a one-player game training rather than an official game [[gf2-2-2]], but in Guts Training you are still told you need 700 points to compete on the Aggro Crag [[gf2-2-3]]. No source says a one-player game blocks the Crag or its achievements.

Hazards on the climb:
- Water spouts are the vertical water things that drain some of your energy [[gf1-2-19]].
- Green rocks take a huge chunk out of your energy [[gf1-2-19]].
- Falling to the bottom of the course means climbing all the way back up [[gf1-2-19]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Yodelayheehoo! (reach the top of the Aggro Crag in 1:40 or less) can be missed if you do not qualify for the Crag. You need at least 700 points in a show. Falling even 1 point short means you miss the Aggro Crag [[gf1-1-4, gf2-2-3, gf2-8-4]]. Count your points through the earlier events of the show before the Crag begins.

### 700 points to reach the Aggro Crag

The qualifying rule is the same everywhere: you must have 700 points in a show to take on the Aggro Crag [[gf1-1-4]]. The Event Setups option shows scoring for events [[gf1-1-1]]. The scoring in the earlier events was covered in earlier chapters, so use it to track your total.

For this chapter, the sourced scoring rules are:
- Gym events give 1 point per second left on the clock, so 30 seconds left scores 30 points [[gf1-2-21]].
- On the Aggro Crag itself, each second left also gives 1 point [[gf1-2-21]].

Your score from the first events of the show must already reach 700 before the Crag starts. Starting with a GUTS Show, not a Single Event, is required [[gf1-1-4]].

> [!TIP]
> Before you start the Crag, check your points. If you are below 700, you will not get the Crag at all [[gf1-1-4]]. Restart the show and try again rather than hoping for a pass.

No source gives a step-by-step route or a split for the 1:40 climb. Unable to verify from collected sources. Only the following tips are sourced:

- Keep a steady head once the music starts. The music is adventurous and dramatic, but do not let the pressure get to you [[gf1-2-21]].
- The guide says not to worry about the clock, since 7:00 is a lot of time [[gf1-2-21]].
- On Advanced, take your time. A few spots are classic NES/SNES platforming where you cannot see what is ahead of you off the screen [[ra-comments-1-4]].
- Falling to the bottom when you are halfway up is very painful. The commenter says the timer is pretty generous, so even if you fall a couple of times you should be fine [[ra-comments-1-5]].

> [!NOTE]
> The 1:40 target for Yodelayheehoo! is far tighter than the 7:00 limit. The "take your time" advice suits Piece of the Rock. For Yodelayheehoo!, expect to need a clean run with no fall to the bottom [[gf1-2-19, ra-comments-1-5]]. No source provides a faster route.

### Aggro Crag: all 6 actuators

Actuators are GUTS's term for the lights, which in this event look like sewer covers [[gf1-2-21]]. Rules:

1. **JUMP** onto each of the 6 actuators. Walking over them does not register [[gf2-8-4, gf2-8-5]].
2. Hit every actuator before the last one goes down [[gf1-2-20]].
3. Do not skip one. The guide singles out missing one actuator as a problem, though its sentence is cut off [[gf1-3-3]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Missing even one actuator changes what you see at the top. Count the lit flags when you arrive, as described below [[gf1-2-22]].

### Aggro Crag summit flags

At the top you see a number of lit-up flags. This number is the number of actuators you hit [[gf1-2-21]]. If 5 of the 6 flags are lit, stomp on the last one and you beat the game [[gf1-2-22]].

Steps at the summit:
1. Reach the top of the Crag.
2. Count the lit flags [[gf1-2-22]].
3. If 5 of 6 are lit, stomp on the unlit flag [[gf1-2-22]].
4. You beat the game and take home a piece of the rock [[gf1-3-1]].

Reaching the top within 1:40 earns Yodelayheehoo!. The route to the top should be a clean climb with all actuators hit [[gf1-2-20, gf2-8-4]].


> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> **Yodelayheehoo! (10 Points)**
> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/681049.png)
> *Reach the top of the Aggro Crag in 1:40 or less*


The timing requirement is reaching the top in 1:40 or less. Because the clock starts at 7:00 and each second left is worth a point, a fast climb also raises your score [[gf1-2-21]]. Unable to verify from collected sources: how many points a 1:40 climb scores.

### Piece of the Rock

Piece of the Rock requires completing the Aggro Crag Challenge and the GUTS Challenge [[gf1-2-23, gf1-3-1, gf2-8-4, gf2-8-5]]. Beating the game gives you a piece of the rock [[gf1-3-1]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** One guide says you still take home a glowing piece of the rock even if you do not reach the top [[gf1-2-23]]. Do not count on a failed climb for the achievement. It requires completing the Aggro Crag [[gf1-3-1]]. If you fail, retry. There is always a second chance [[gf1-3-2]].


> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> **Piece of the Rock (25 Points)**
> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/681036.png)
> *Complete the Aggro Crag Challenge and complete the GUTS Challenge*


After you finish, you get to see your piece of the Aggro Crag [[gf2-8-7]]. In a 2-player game, whoever has the most points at the end wins [[gf2-8-7]]. Two-player Aggro Crag scoring is 725 for 1st place and 325 for 2nd place [[gf2-8-8]]. No source says either achievement in this chapter needs a two-player game.

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave the Crag, confirm that both achievements have popped. If Yodelayheehoo! did not unlock, you will need a new show with 700+ points and a faster climb [[gf1-1-4, gf2-8-4]].

## Chapter 8: Advanced GUTS Challenge



### Area Overview & Preparation

This final chapter asks you to replay the full game on the harder setting. The Level option offers Beginner or Advanced, and Advanced is the harder of the two [[gf1-1-2]]. Set Level to Advanced, choose a full GUTS Show instead of a Single Event, and win it, including the Aggro Crag, to earn **Do You Have It?** [[gf1-1-2, gf2-2-1]]. A GUTS Show is 4 events, and the other mode is a Single Event [[gf1-1-3]].

**Setup checklist on the opening screen:**

- **Player:** This sets the number of players, and only 2 can play at once [[gf1-1-1]]. Play solo for this chapter. Note that a one-player game is treated as training rather than an official game, but the 700-point requirement still applies [[gf1-1-4]].
- **Level:** Set it to **Advanced** [[gf1-1-2]].
- **Event Setups:** Turning this off hides the event's name, object, and scoring [[gf2-2-1]]. Keep it on if you want that information shown.
- **Player name:** You can enter up to 8 letters [[gf2-2-6]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** You must have at least 700 points in a show to reach the Aggro Crag. Falling even 1 point short means you miss the Crag, and this achievement needs the Crag [[gf1-1-4, gf1-1-2]]. Do not coast through the earlier events.

### Advanced_Level: Advanced Difficulty

#### Step 1: Configure the game

1. On the opening screen, move up and down to pick options and press A to change them [[gf2-2-1]].
2. Change Level to **Advanced** [[gf1-1-2]].
3. Select Start Game, then choose the full GUTS Show rather than a Single Event [[gf1-1-3]].
4. Pick a color, then your character, then enter a name of up to 8 letters [[gf2-2-6]].

#### Step 2: Win the show and bank 700 points

Play all the events in the show and make sure your total reaches 700 or more before the finale [[gf1-1-4]]. Unable to verify from collected sources: the exact per-event point targets on Advanced.

#### Step 3: The Aggro Crag on Advanced


> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> **Do You Have It? (25 Points)**
> ![Badge](https://retroachievements.org/Badge/681037.png)
> *Complete the GUTS Challenge on Advanced difficulty*


Beat the Aggro Crag in the full Advanced show to unlock this achievement [[gf1-1-2, ra-comments-1-4]].

- **Take your time.** On Advanced, a few spots are classic NES/SNES-style platforming where you cannot see what is ahead because it is off-screen [[ra-comments-1-4]].
- **Falls are costly.** Falling to the bottom when you are halfway up is extremely painful [[ra-comments-1-5]].
- **The timer is generous.** The commenter says that even if you fall a couple of times you should be fine, so play carefully rather than rushing [[ra-comments-1-4, ra-comments-1-5]].

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave: confirm the show was played on Advanced and that you finished the Aggro Crag. This is the final achievement of the game [[gf1-1-2, ra-comments-1-4]].
