## Chapter 11: Floor 86: Forgotten Tower

[MERMAID]

Floor 86 runs from the Valley Where Soldiers Walked outside town, through the Forward Base of the Living Dead (1F to 3F), the Ancient Battlefield of Bleeding Fierce Combat and the Fortress of the Living Dead, to the labyrinth entrance and the Forgotten Tower [[sk1-33-1, sk1-33-2, sk1-33-3, sk1-33-4, xneo1-1-24]]. The Skeleton King is the boss at the top [[sk1-33-8, sk1-33-9]]. After the boss, the character events for Asuna, Lisbeth and Sinon open up [[sk1-91-902, sk1-92-6, sk1-93-901]].

Whether the character-episode tiers open on reaching a floor or on clearing it is unconfirmed in the collected sources. Do each tier's events after defeating the Floor 86 boss. If one has not triggered yet, continue and retry after the next boss.

> [!TIP]
> **Preparation:** Bring Asuna as your partner if you want the wedding event, because the NM Knight of Oath fight in Forward Base 1 is done with her [[sk1-91-4]]. Quests are accepted from NPC Kirby in front of the Arc Sophia transfer gate on Floor 76 [[wiki2-5-1]]. The three Floor 86 quests are quests 32, 33 and 34 on the quest list [[sk1-95-32, sk1-95-33, sk1-95-34]].

### Arc Sophia

Before you leave town for Floor 86, take the three quests from Kirby at the Arc Sophia transfer gate [[wiki2-5-1]].

- **The Undead Return (quest 32):** defeat 13 Lost Soldiers in the Valley Where Soldiers Walked on Floor 86 [[sk1-95-32]].
- **Collect in Field (quest 33):** collect 5 Red Crystals in the Ancient Battlefield of Bleeding Fierce Combat on Floor 86 [[sk1-95-33]].
- **The Treasure Blade (quest 34):** collect 3 Bloodstained Holy Swords, a drop from Rune Knights in the Valley Where Soldiers Walked and similar places on Floor 86 [[sk1-95-34]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** For The Undead Return, only the Lost Soldiers in the Valley Where Soldiers Walked, right outside the town, are counted. There are many Lost Soldiers inside the dungeon, but those are not counted [[xneo1-1-24, wiki1-12-7]]. Do not waste time hunting them elsewhere.

### Floor 86

#### Valley Where Soldiers Walked

Leave town and fight the Lost Soldiers here until you have 13 kills. Finishing this quest also satisfies Floor 86 Check 1 [[sk1-95-32, sk1-102-25]].

#### Ancient Battlefield of Bleeding Fierce Combat

Red Crystals are picked up at random here [[sk1-34-16]]. On Floor 86 they can be gathered at 3 or 4 craters in this battlefield [[wiki1-12-5]]. Decorative Stones are also random pickups here [[sk1-34-17]]. Gather 5 Red Crystals for Collect in Field [[sk1-95-33]].

#### Fortress of the Living Dead

Beyond the battlefield lies the Fortress of the Living Dead. A branch on the right leads to the Treasure Vault of the Living Dead, which holds many chests [[sk1-33-5]]. The side of the Fortress marked with the red arrow is a dead end, so it is efficient to go around from that side [[wiki1-12-6]].

#### NM Necromancer

In the deepest room of the Fortress, beyond the Ancient Battlefield, you fight NM Necromancer [[sk1-33-3, xneo1-1-25]]. Defeating it satisfies Floor 86 Check 2 and opens the door to the labyrinth entrance, the Supply Base of Unrewarded Warriors [[sk1-102-26, sk1-33-4]]. ### Forgotten Tower

Go through the door to the Supply Base of Unrewarded Warriors, the entrance of the Floor 86 labyrinth [[sk1-33-4]].

> [!TIP]
> Every room of the Forgotten Tower holds many Lost Soldiers and Cursed Medics, and fighting them one room at a time takes a lot of time. It is best to skip most of them with the Hiding or Invisible battle skills or with a HIDE potion [[sk1-33-6]]. The power devices on 1F and 3F open doors, but you can proceed to the next floor without opening them [[sk1-33-7]].

#### 1F

Chests: Weapon Chest H [[sk1-34-30]], Weapon Chest I [[sk1-34-31]] and Earth Crust Ore x6 [[sk1-34-32]].

Interrupt mission: defeat 10 Lost Soldiers within the time limit to receive the Physical Charm [[sk1-34-29]].

#### 2F

Chests: Weapon Chest J [[sk1-34-34]], Recovery Crystal x3 [[sk1-34-35]], Wind Flower Ore x8 [[sk1-34-36]], Weapon Chest K [[sk1-34-37]] and Weapon Chest L [[sk1-34-38]].

Interrupt mission: defeat 5 Cursed Medics within the time limit to receive the Meteor Chain [[sk1-34-33]].

#### 3F

Chests: Grand Potion x6 [[sk1-34-40]], Weapon Chest N [[sk1-34-41]], Ash-White Ore x8 [[sk1-34-42]], Weapon Chest M [[sk1-34-43]] and Recovery Crystal x3 [[sk1-34-44]].

Interrupt mission: defeat 3 Rune Knights within the time limit to receive the Noble Boots [[sk1-34-39]].

### Skeleton King

The Skeleton King is weak to blunt (strike) damage [[sk1-33-9]]. It mainly uses very wide-range attacks on the whole party that add poison, paralysis and darkness [[sk1-33-8]].

- Go on the offensive early and cut its HP quickly [[sk1-33-8]].
- When its HP gets low, its area attacks become stronger. In the late phase, use support skills while looking after the party members' HP [[sk1-33-8]].

Unable to verify from collected sources: the boss's exact HP and which healing items work best.

Beating the Skeleton King completes the Forgotten Tower.

### After Floor 86: Character Episodes

Do these after the boss, per the open question above. If one does not trigger, continue and retry after the next boss.

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** The Asuna wedding episode requires Asuna to be with you in Forward Base of the Living Dead 1 [[sk1-91-4]].

#### Asuna: Ritual of Blessing

1. After clearing Floor 86, talk to Asuna in Arc Sophia to advance the quest Ritual of Blessing [[sk1-91-902]]. Asuna is invited to the party at Agil's shop in the central plaza [[wiki1-6-1]].
2. Take Asuna to Forward Base of the Living Dead 1 on Floor 86 and defeat NM Knight of Oath [[sk1-91-4]].
3. At the end of Ritual of Blessing the wedding event occurs and you obtain the Marriage Ring [[sk1-91-4]].
