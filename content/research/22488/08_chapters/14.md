## Chapter 14: Floor 89: Temple Turbid

[MERMAID]

Floor 89 runs from the Spring of Twin Streams to the Floating Cloud Corridor Inviting to the Sky, and from the corridor into the 89th-floor labyrinth Temple Turbid (Mausoleum of Muddy Torrents) [[sk1-41-901]]. The floor boss, Murderer Fang, waits at the end of the labyrinth [[sk1-41-4, sk1-41-6]]. This chapter covers the two tower quests, the NM Blaze Armor fight, every chest on the floor and the boss.

Both quests are accepted from NPC Kirby in front of the Arc Sophia transfer gate on floor 76 [[wiki2-5-1]]. Accept them before you start the towers so the kills count as you go [[sk1-95-40, sk1-95-41]].

### Arc Sophia

Before you leave town, handle these things:

- Pick up the two quests from Kirby at the transfer gate: "The Tower of Lizards" (defeat 10 Elder Lizardmen in the Single Tower Controlling Torrents) and "Spell of the Tree Man" (defeat 10 Wisdom Trents in the Single Tower Controlling Rapids) [[wiki2-5-1, sk1-95-40, sk1-95-41]].
- Optional: talk to Agil in his shop at night to trigger the shop-keeping event [[wiki1-10-6]].
- Optional: Sachi's message can be used in Kirito's room [[wiki7-13-1]]. Using a Recording Crystal while alone in Kirito's room triggers a flashback scene of Sachi [[wiki8-9-1]].
- Optional: in Arc Sophia date conversations, starting the talk while seated increases the number of times you can speak. Press the X button to sit on benches or fountains [[wiki7-13-6]].
- Strea: once the 82nd floor boss is dead, she can be talked to at Agil's inn [[ra-comments-57-4]]. Repeated conversations eventually make her agree to accompany you when asked [[ra-comments-57-1]].

#### Spring of Twin Streams and the two towers

The route leads from the Spring of Twin Streams to the Floating Cloud Corridor [[sk1-41-901]]. Two towers sit on the way: the Single Tower Controlling Torrents and the Single Tower Controlling Rapids. Each has a waterway power device that is started by defeating specific enemies [[sk1-41-1, sk1-41-2]]. The corridor is reached after clearing the twin towers [[xneo1-2-6]].

**Tower Controlling Torrents:** On the map, go to the lower-left room and defeat the Lizardmen there to start the waterway power device [[sk1-41-1]]. Kill Elder Lizardmen here for the quest. Only Elder Lizardmen in this tower count toward it [[sk1-95-40, xneo1-2-5]]. Open its chests for Wind Flower Ore x10 and Sharp Blade Ore x10 [[sk1-42-1, sk1-42-2]]. The 500000 Col and Crimson Flame Ore x10 chests are listed under a truncated tower name, so they most likely belong to the towers too [[sk1-42-3, sk1-42-4]].

Once 10 Elder Lizardmen are down, the quest is complete [[sk1-95-40]]. It is also Floor 89 Check 1 [[sk1-102-31]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365811]

**Tower Controlling Rapids:** On the map, defeat the Treefolk in the central (deepest) room to start its power device [[sk1-41-2]]. Defeat 10 Wisdom Trents in this tower for the quest [[sk1-95-41]].

#### Floating Cloud Corridor

After the twin towers, the route continues to the Floating Cloud Corridor Inviting to the Sky [[sk1-41-901]]. Arriving here earns the "Reach on Cloud Corridor" achievement.

[ACHIEVEMENT:365809]

NM Blaze Armor is in the deepest part of the corridor, and you fight it there [[xneo1-2-6, sk1-41-3]]. Defeating it is Floor 89 Check 2 [[sk1-102-32]]. Open the corridor's chests for Earth Crust Ore x10, Weapon Chest A and Grand Potion x6 [[sk1-42-5, sk1-42-6, sk1-42-7]]. From the corridor the route continues to the Floor 89 labyrinth, Temple Turbid [[sk1-41-901]].

#### Temple Turbid (Mausoleum of Muddy Torrents)

**1F:** Defeat 2 Green Slimes within the time limit to receive the Tower Shield [[sk1-42-8]]. The chests hold Weapon Chest B, Ash-White Ore x10 and Teleport Crystal x3 [[sk1-42-9, sk1-42-10, sk1-42-11]].

**2F:** Defeat 2 Deep Crimson within the time limit to receive the Heart Clover [[sk1-42-12]]. The chests hold Diamond Ore x10, Grand Potion x5, Weapon Chest C and Weapon Chest D [[sk1-42-13, sk1-42-14, sk1-42-15, sk1-42-16]].

**3F:** Defeat 2 Fat Slimes within the time limit to receive the Mirage Belt [[sk1-42-17]]. The chests hold Weapon Chest E and Weapon Chest F [[sk1-42-18, sk1-42-19]].

The exact room position of each chest and the time limits: Unable to verify from collected sources.

When the last chest on the floor is open, the sweep is complete.

### Boss: Murderer Fang

Weakness: blunt (strike) attribute [[sk1-41-6]].

- Its wide-range paralysis breath should be answered with the Battle Skill Airy Shield, activated at the right moment [[sk1-41-4]].
- Use the Battle Skill Exact Onslaught while it is preparing a skill. This stops the skill fairly easily [[sk1-41-5]].

Boss HP and recommended level: Unable to verify from collected sources.

Defeat it to complete the Temple Turbid.
