## Chapter 9: Floor 84: Dwell Devour

[MERMAID]

Floor 84 is the Ant Queen floor. You leave the town and clear the Gouged Cavern 2 area, where defeating the Ant Soldier and Ant Commander opens the Devoured Sandstone Wasteland. You then reach the Road Connecting to the Ruler, defeat NM Ant Princess, and enter the Dwell Devour labyrinth (暴食の寝座) [[sk1-25-2, sk1-25-3]]. On the labyrinth's 2F, Strea joins the party until the boss room, and you cannot go back [[wiki1-12-901]]. This chapter covers the 84F quests, the 84F chest sweep, the optional NM Mabethck, and the character episodes that follow the floor.

> [!WARNING]
> **Save Warning (unconfirmed report):** One commenter reports that the English/Portuguese translation patch freezes after the Floor 84 boss. This is unconfirmed. Keep a separate save from before the Ant Queen fight. If the game freezes, continue from that save without the patch. Never overwrite your only save before this boss.

### Arc Sophia

Quests are accepted from NPC Kirby in front of the Arc Sophia transfer gate on Floor 76 [[wiki2-5-1]]. The Floor 84 quests in the quest list are "Run Through the Hot Spring Area!" (温泉地帯を突っ走れ！), "Scatter the Ants" (アリを蹴散らせ), and "The Cave Is an Ant's Nest!" (洞窟は蟻の巣だ！) [[sk1-95-26, sk1-95-27, sk1-95-28]].

Floor 84 Check 1 is completing the quest "The Ant's Nest" [[sk1-102-1]]. Check 2 is defeating NM Ant Princess on the path leading to the ruler [[sk1-102-22]].

Preparation for the Ant Queen: the guide recommends the battle skill "Airy Breath" (エアリーブレス) against breath-attack ailments, and the battle skill "Purifying Flame" (浄化の炎) plus antidote crystals and paralysis-cure crystals against poison and paralysis [[sk1-25-6, sk1-25-7]].

> [!TIP]
> **Strea bond (separate achievement):** After the Floor 82 boss, Strea can be talked to at Agil's inn, and her bond rises through repeated conversation [[ra-comments-57-4, ra-comments-57-1]]. This is tracked by a different achievement from this chapter's.

### Floor 84

#### Army Gathering Dry Valley (outside town)

The Hot Spring Water (温泉水) is picked up in this valley [[sk1-26-1]]. It is on a rock beside the water spot right outside town [[wiki1-12-3]]. The quest "Run Through the Hot Spring Area!" asks for 6 Hot Spring Water from the valley just outside the Floor 84 town [[sk1-95-26]]. A **Sulfur Fragment** can also be picked up here [[sk1-26-2]].

Complete the 6-piece collection quest for [ACHIEVEMENT:365780] [[sk1-95-26, sk1-26-1]].

#### Gouged Cavern 1 (optional for progress)

You can skip this cave without affecting dungeon progress [[sk1-25-1]]. However, it holds two chests, **Weapon Chest A** and **Weapon Chest B**, which count toward the floor's chest sweep [[sk1-26-4, sk1-26-5, sk1-26-1]].

The NM Mabethck at the back of the cave has a high level setting. Avoid challenging it unless you have plenty of spare strength [[sk1-25-1]]. If you beat it within the time limit, it drops the **Guardian Greaves** (leg armor) [[sk1-26-3]].

Safe method from a wiki player: when Mabethck targets you, run back to the cave entrance. Also pull an antlion along with you. Mabethck then gets caught on the rocks near the entrance. With a slight distance adjustment, even normal attacks hit it safely, and you can use sword skills freely [[wiki7-13-2]].

Kill Mabethck for [ACHIEVEMENT:365871] [[wiki7-13-2, sk1-26-3]].

#### Gouged Cavern 2

This cave is reached from the town exit toward the right side [[wiki1-12-4]]. Defeat the Ant Soldier and Ant Commander here to open the way to the Devoured Sandstone Wasteland [[sk1-25-2]]. The chests contain **Ash-White Stone x10** (灰白石), **Diamond Stone x10** (金剛石), and **Teleport Crystal x2** [[sk1-26-6, sk1-26-7, sk1-26-8]].

The Holy Flame (聖なる業火) is in the far right of this cave [[wiki1-12-4]]. See the Lisbeth episode below.

#### Road Connecting to the Ruler

From Gouged Cavern 2, go straight on to the Road Connecting to the Ruler. NM Ant Princess waits at its deepest part [[xneo1-1-20]]. Defeating it opens the way to the Dwell Devour labyrinth [[sk1-25-3]]. The chest here holds **Weapon Chest C** [[sk1-26-9]].

Princess Guards on this road drop the Ant Honey Pot (アリの蜜壺). Collect 3 for the quest "The Cave Is an Ant's Nest!" [[sk1-95-28]].

Complete that quest for [ACHIEVEMENT:365779] [[sk1-95-28, sk1-102-1]].

#### Dwell Devour 1F

> [!CAUTION]
> **Point of No Return:** Going up to the 2F forces Strea into your party until the boss room, and you cannot go back [[wiki1-12-901]]. Finish everything on 1F before climbing.

The Strea event triggers when you approach the north staircase on 1F. Whichever partner you brought is replaced, and you are forced into a party with Strea [[wiki1-9-7, sk1-25-4]]. A conversation event between your former partner, Kirito and Strea occurs along the way [[wiki1-9-9]].

1F chests:
- **Recovery Crystal x3** [[sk1-26-11]]
- **Crimson Flame Stone x10** [[sk1-26-12]]
- **Weapon Chest D** [[sk1-26-13]]
- **Sharp Blade Stone x10** [[sk1-26-14]]

Time-limit mission: defeat 9 Honey Mites within the time limit for the **Alliance Arm** (hand armor) [[sk1-26-10]].

Quest "Scatter the Ants": collect 8 Ant Mandibles (アリの大顎), dropped by Ant Commanders in the Dwell Devour and other places [[sk1-95-27]]. Finishing it gives [ACHIEVEMENT:365778] [[sk1-95-27]].

#### Dwell Devour 2F

There are no chests on 2F, and you are in a party with Strea [[xneo1-1-20]]. Mission: defeat 6 Ant Commanders within the time limit for the **Blood Ankh** (charm) [[sk1-26-15]].

#### Dwell Devour 3F

Queen Guards and Ant Commanders are very hard to tell apart on 3F, so be careful [[sk1-25-5]]. You are still in a party with Strea here [[xneo1-1-20]].

3F chests:
- **Weapon Chest E** [[sk1-26-17]]
- **Wind Flower Stone x10** [[sk1-26-18]]
- **Weapon Chest F** [[sk1-26-19]]
- **Flowing Water Stone x10** [[sk1-26-20]]
- **Grand Potion x4** [[sk1-26-21]]

Time-limit mission: defeat 4 Queen Guards within the time limit for the **Skill Holder** (back slot) [[sk1-26-16]].

Approaching the south staircase on 3F separates you from Strea, and your original partner returns [[wiki1-9-8]]. Open the 3F chests before taking that staircase.

Once every chest on the floor is opened, [ACHIEVEMENT:365783] unlocks. The chests are Weapon Chests A and B (Cavern 1), Ash-White Stone, Diamond Stone and Teleport Crystal (Cavern 2), Weapon Chest C (Road), the four 1F chests, and the five 3F chests [[sk1-26-4, sk1-26-5, sk1-26-6, sk1-26-7, sk1-26-8, sk1-26-9, sk1-26-11, sk1-26-12, sk1-26-13, sk1-26-14, sk1-26-17, sk1-26-18, sk1-26-19, sk1-26-20, sk1-26-21]].

### Ant Queen (Floor 84 boss, Dwell Devour)

The boss is the Ant Queen (BOSS "The Queen Of Ant"), and its effective attribute is blunt (打) [[sk1-25-8, wiki1-9-9]]. Unlike earlier bosses, it is not alone. Queen Guards appear around it [[sk1-25-6]].

- Breath attacks inflict status ailments such as paralysis. The battle skill "Airy Breath" prevents these [[sk1-25-6]].
- At low HP, it uses a powerful all-target attack and a poison attack. Prepare "Purifying Flame", antidote crystals and paralysis-cure crystals [[sk1-25-7]].

Defeat the Ant Queen for [ACHIEVEMENT:365781] [[sk1-25-6, sk1-25-7, sk1-25-8, wiki1-9-9]].

Back up your save as noted in the warning above before this fight.
