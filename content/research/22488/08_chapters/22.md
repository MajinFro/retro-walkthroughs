## Chapter 22: Floor 97: Death King

[MERMAID]

Floor 97 is a hub chapter. You arrive from the Floor 96 boss, clear the quests that unlock the floor's checks, sweep the chests in the Death King labyrinth, and fight the Emperor of Death. The chapter also carries the level 130 and level 140 Kirito achievements, which the Inferno Dragon quest farm handles [[wiki7-12-4, wiki7-13-4, wiki7-13-5]]. All three Floor 97 quests are accepted from NPC Kirby in front of the Arc Sophia transfer gate [[wiki2-5-1, sk1-95-58, sk1-95-59, sk1-95-60]].

> [!NOTE]
> **Unconfirmed:** No source says when Kirito reaches levels 100, 110 and 120 in normal play. They unlock automatically and the chapters follow the set order. If you have not reached them by this chapter, finish them with the Inferno Dragon farming method described below.

### Preparation: Quests, Checks and Levels

Quests of every floor are accepted from NPC Kirby at the Arc Sophia transfer gate on Floor 76 [[wiki2-5-1]]. Floor 97 has three quests on the list:

| Quest | Requirement | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| Defeat That Knight! | Defeat 5 Rune Knights on the Path of Labyrinth Eaters | [[sk1-95-58]] |
| The Target Crystal | Collect 6 Demon Crystals dropped by demons from Death King 1F onward | [[sk1-95-59]] |
| Saw The Dragon's Figure (high difficulty) | Defeat the Inferno Dragon in Death King 1F, which appears after you accept the quest | [[sk1-95-60]] |

Floor 97's Check 1 is completing Defeat That Knight!, and Check 2 is defeating NM Apollyon Knight in the Upper Corridor of Coffins and Lamentation [[sk1-102-4, sk1-102-45]].

> [!TIP]
> Weapons sold in the 97th floor shop have an upgrade limit of 30 but can only be upgraded to +15. Their stat bonuses cannot be expected and forging them takes a lot of effort [[wiki7-6-1]].

### Arc Sophia

Go to the transfer gate and speak to NPC Kirby [[wiki2-5-1]]. Accept Defeat That Knight!, The Target Crystal and Saw The Dragon's Figure. The dragon quest is the one that makes the Inferno Dragon appear [[sk1-95-60]].

Optional extras in town:
- Sachi's message can be used in Kirito's room [[wiki7-13-1]].
- Using a Recording Crystal while alone in Kirito's room triggers a flashback scene of Sachi [[wiki8-9-1]].
- Date conversations in Arc Sophia allow more conversation turns if you begin them seated on a bench or fountain with the X button [[wiki7-13-6]].
- Go to Agil's shop in the central plaza to invite Asuna to the party [[wiki1-6-1]]. Strea can be talked to at Agil's inn after the Floor 82 boss [[ra-comments-57-4]]. Repeated conversations raise her bond until she agrees to accompany you [[ra-comments-57-1]].

### Floor 97

On entering the map, a sub-event battle with Lv200 Alberich starts on the Path of Labyrinth Eaters, just outside the town [[xneo1-2-15, sk1-75-1]]. Use debuff-type battle skills such as Defense Nullify to chip away his HP efficiently [[sk1-75-2]]. After he is driven off, an event plays and you return to the Path of Labyrinth Eaters [[sk1-75-1]].

#### Path of Labyrinth Eaters: Defeat That Knight!

Rune Knights spawn only one at a time here, so after defeating one, wait for the next to respawn [[xneo1-2-15]]. Defeat 5 of them to complete the quest [[sk1-95-58]]. Turn it in at Kirby.

Completing it also clears Check 1 for the floor [[sk1-102-4]].

### Death King Labyrinth

From the Path of Labyrinth Eaters, use the transfer device to reach Death King 1F [[sk1-75-1]]. Each time you go up a floor of the labyrinth you must pass through the Corridor of Coffins and Lamentation, and all progress uses transfer devices [[sk1-75-1]]. The order is:

1. Death King 1F, then the Lower Corridor of Coffins and Lamentation
2. Death King 2F, then the Middle Corridor
3. Death King 3F, then the Upper Corridor
4. Death King 4F [[sk1-75-1]]

Weapon chests hold weapons whose names, stats and upgrade counts are randomly determined, and some may match the weapon type Kirito or his partner has equipped [[sk1-76-13]]. Exact room positions for the chests are not given in the collected sources. Unable to verify from collected sources. Sweep every room of each floor.

#### Death King 1F

- Defeat a Shadow Lord within the timed mission's limit to receive the body armor **Paladin Blazer** [[sk1-76-2]].
- A chest holds **Diamond Ore x10** [[sk1-76-3]].
- A chest holds **Crimson Flame Crystal x6** [[sk1-76-4]].
- Three weapon chests (A, B, C) are also listed for this floor, with contents determined randomly [[sk1-76-13]].

**Inferno Dragon (Saw The Dragon's Figure):** With the quest accepted, the Inferno Dragon appears on this floor [[sk1-95-60]]. Many active enemies are nearby, so Sneaking and Invisible are essential to avoid pulling extras when you drag the dragon into the passage [[wiki7-12-7]]. Lure it into the passage with Kirito against the passage wall, then fire your arts from out of its reach [[wiki7-12-4, wiki7-12-7]]. Other enemies touching the dragon will sometimes warp Kirito, so stay alert [[wiki7-12-7]].

Other tips for this fight:
- When the dragon starts a skill, unlock the camera, move to a spot it cannot hit, and cast your strongest skill without locking on [[ra-comments-148-1]].
- Unlocking and targeting your partner buys time to cast your strongest skill on the dragon [[ra-comments-148-2]].
- If you attack the dragon and then leave its room, it gets stuck because it is too big for the exit. You can then keep spamming attacks while it barely reacts [[ra-comments-148-3, ra-comments-148-4]].

#### Death King 3F

- A chest holds **Sharp Blade Crystal x6** [[sk1-76-10]].

### Kirito Levels 130 and 140: Inferno Dragon Farming

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Do not turn in Saw The Dragon's Figure until Kirito is past level 140. Use cancel and re-accept at Kirby to repeat the fight meanwhile [[wiki7-13-5]].

High-difficulty quest NMs can be trapped in specific terrain, granting around 46,000 to 50,000 EXP until Kirito reaches about level 170 [[wiki7-12-4]]. For the Inferno Dragon, around level 130 the fight gave 38,400 EXP almost every time [[wiki7-13-5]]. To repeat:

1. Accept Saw The Dragon's Figure from Kirby and trap the Inferno Dragon in Death King 1F as described above [[wiki7-13-5, wiki7-12-7]].
2. Kill it with your strongest arts [[wiki7-13-5]].
3. Go to NPC Kirby and cancel the quest, then accept it again to fight it repeatedly [[wiki7-13-5]].

A similar procedure works with the Floor 96 Crimson Spinner, where cancelling Thrill of the Giant Spider at Kirby allows repeats [[wiki7-13-4]]. Keep farming until the level goals are reached.

### Saw The Dragon's Figure: Turn-in

Only after Kirito has passed level 140 do you finish the quest. The steps are:

1. Make sure the quest is currently accepted at Kirby, since the Inferno Dragon appears only after acceptance [[sk1-95-60]].
2. Go to Death King 1F and bring the dragon into the passage with Sneaking and Invisible active, as in the farm [[wiki7-12-7]].
3. Use your strongest arts from out of its reach, using the camera-unlock and partner-target tips if needed [[ra-comments-148-1, ra-comments-148-2]].
4. Defeat the Inferno Dragon, then return to NPC Kirby and report the quest without cancelling it [[sk1-95-60, wiki7-13-5]].

### Emperor of Death (Floor 97 Boss, Death King)

The floor's checks must be done before the boss fight: Defeat That Knight! and NM Apollyon Knight [[sk1-102-4, sk1-102-45]].

**Boss strategy:**
- Weakness: the effective attribute is blunt (打) [[sk1-76-14]].
- Its attack power is far higher than that of lower-floor bosses, and it uses high-damage wide-area attacks from the start. Right after the battle begins, use a skill such as Misdirection to draw hate onto Kirito, then use support skills [[sk1-76-1]].
- It always keeps two Lost Soldiers with it; leave them to your party members [[sk1-76-1]].
- Its attacks have long wind-ups, so Parrying makes it easy to cause a Risk Break [[sk1-76-1]].
- As its HP drops it frequently uses party-wide attacks that inflict poison, blindness and paralysis [[sk1-76-1]].
- Landing the finishing blow with Kirito grants the Last Attack Bonus [[sk1-76-1]].
