## Chapter 17: Floor 92: Guardian Maze

[MERMAID]

Floor 92 is a field route followed by a dungeon. You leave Arc Sophia and cross the Spiral Path to the Abyss Green and the Agile Wyvern Nest (Middle, Upper and Lower layers). You defeat a Red Blast in the Upper Layer, which opens the way to the Windless Abyss Green. A second Red Blast there opens the Floor 92 labyrinth, the Guardian Maze, where Chaos Dragon waits [[sk1-56-1, sk1-56-2, xneo1-2-10]]. Two quests, Make! Wing of Armour and Guardian Dragon and the Boy, are also completed on this floor [[sk1-95-46, sk1-95-47]]. Treasure Guardian asks you to open every chest on the floor [[sk1-57-3]].

> [!TIP]
> This chapter has no point of no return. Work through the chest table below as you route, then finish the quests before you move on.

### Arc Sophia

Quests are accepted from the NPC Kirby in front of the transfer gate on Floor 76 [[wiki2-5-1]]. The two quests for this floor are collection quests, and both feed achievements:

- **Make! Wing of Armour:** Collect 6 Wyvern Great Wings (飛竜の大翼). Wyverns on the Spiral Path to the Abyss Green and similar places on Floor 92 drop them [[sk1-95-46]].
- **Guardian Dragon and the Boy:** Collect 3 Dragon Scales (竜鱗). Dragons in the Guardian Maze and similar places on Floor 92 drop them [[sk1-95-47]].

Kill wyverns and dragons as they appear along your route.

> [!TIP]
> Guardian Dragon and the Boy is listed as Floor 92 Check 1 [[sk1-102-35]].

#### Town events on Floor 92

These optional scenes are available in town this floor:

- Talk to Agil in his shop to trigger the "Let's have fun together again" event [[wiki1-11-13]].
- Try to leave town, including through the transfer gate, to trigger an event. After an earthquake, talk to Agil again for another event [[wiki1-11-14]].
- Visit the shopping district alone during the day for the "Shopping together" event [[wiki1-11-15]].
- Sachi's message can be used in Kirito's room [[wiki7-13-1]]. Using a Recording Crystal alone in Kirito's room triggers a flashback scene of Sachi [[wiki8-9-1]].

#### Strea

After the Floor 82 boss is killed, Strea can be talked to at Agil's inn [[ra-comments-57-4]]. Talk to her repeatedly until she agrees to accompany you when asked [[ra-comments-57-1]]. In date conversations in Arc Sophia, starting the conversation while seated (X button on benches or fountains) increases the number of times you can speak [[wiki7-13-6]].

#### Shopping

The Duel series armor is sold on Floor 92. It has high parameter corrections and defense approaching heavy armor. Pieces other than the feet can be obtained earlier through last-attack drops [[wiki7-6-4]].

### Floor 92

#### Spiral Path to the Abyss Green

Leave town and cross the Spiral Path to the Abyss Green, then continue into the Agile Wyvern Nest [[sk1-56-1, xneo1-2-10]]. Wyverns here drop Wyvern Great Wings for the armour quest [[sk1-95-46]]. You will come back to this path later, because the Windless Abyss Green is reached from it [[xneo1-2-10]].

#### Agile Wyvern Nest, Middle Layer

The Upper Layer lies down-left from the Middle Layer [[xneo1-2-10]]. Open the seven Middle Layer chests:

1. Earth Crust Ore x10 [[sk1-57-3]]
2. Weapon Chest A [[sk1-57-4]]
3. Wind Flower Ore x6 [[sk1-57-5]]

Unable to verify from collected sources: the exact positions of these chests inside the Middle Layer.

#### Agile Wyvern Nest, Upper Layer

Go down-left from the Middle Layer into the Upper Layer [[xneo1-2-10]]. Open its four chests:

1. Weapon Chest D [[sk1-57-10]]
2. Grand Potion x4 [[sk1-57-11]]
3. Sharp Blade Ore x10 [[sk1-57-12]]
4. Weapon Chest E [[sk1-57-13]]

The NM Lord of Wind is in this layer [[xneo1-2-10]]. Defeating it is Floor 92 Check 2 [[sk1-102-36]]. Then go to the bottom-most room of the map and defeat the Red Blast there. This opens the way from the Spiral Path to the Abyss Green into the Windless Abyss Green [[sk1-56-1]].

#### Windless Abyss Green

Return to the first Spiral Path to the Abyss Green and take the area on the right, the Windless Abyss Green [[xneo1-2-10]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365827]

Reaching this area for the first time unlocks Blink Like I Blink [[sk1-56-1, xneo1-2-10]]. A Red Blast fights you again here. Defeat it to gain access to the labyrinth [[xneo1-2-10, sk1-56-2]].

### Guardian Maze

The Floor 92 labyrinth is the Guardian Maze (剛健なる守護者の迷宮) [[sk1-56-2]].

#### 1F

- Defeating a Volcano Dragon within the time limit gives the Ex Charm [[sk1-57-20]].
- Open three chests: Weapon Chest I [[sk1-57-21]], Flowing Water Stone x10 [[sk1-57-22]] and Ash-White Crystal x4 [[sk1-57-23]].

#### 2F

- Defeating 3 dragons within the time limit gives the Protection Ankh [[sk1-57-24]]. Dragons here can drop the Dragon Scales for Guardian Dragon and the Boy [[sk1-95-47]].
- Open five chests: Weapon Chest J [[sk1-57-25]], Grand Potion x3 [[sk1-57-26]], Teleport Crystal x3 [[sk1-57-27]], Weapon Chest K [[sk1-57-28]] and Weapon Chest L [[sk1-57-29]].
- An SNM Overeat appears among the normal dragons here. Its strength is about that of a floor boss, so it is fine if you understand the battle system [[wiki7-12-3]].

#### 3F

- Defeating a Tri-Head Dragon within the time limit gives the Chain Arm [[sk1-57-30]].

### Chaos Dragon (Floor 92 Boss)

- **Effective element:** slash [[sk1-57-1]].
- **Low HP:** it uses Dragon Flare Breath, which causes bleed-stop, VIT down and paralysis. Block it with the battle skill Airy Shield, or bring Purifying Light or a Purifying Crystal to recover. Taking consecutive normal attacks in this state drains HP quickly [[sk1-57-2]].
- **Last attack:** the Sacred Cross (D1250, interval 180) is the Floor 92 last-attack item [[wiki6-6-2]].

Defeating the boss completes the Guardian Maze [[sk1-56-2, sk1-57-2]].
