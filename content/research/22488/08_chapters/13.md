## Chapter 13: Floor 88: Tenants' Work

[MERMAID]

This chapter covers the Floor 88 quests, the Dragon Seal Ruins, the Necro Dragon fight, the labyrinth Tenants' Work and the boss Rebellious Eyes. The route in the sources runs Paradise of Man-Eating Flowers, Strange Waterlily Lakeside, the Dragon Seal Ruins (Shadow Seal, Sun Seal, Center Room), Cold Shaded Rain Rifts and then the labyrinth [[sk1-37-1, sk1-37-2, sk1-37-5, sk1-37-6]]. The Treasure Tenants achievement asks for every chest on Floor 88 [[sk1-38-3]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Floor 88:** the three quests are Walking Flower of Pollen Doom, Little Wings and Glowing Eyes in the Darkness [[sk1-95-37, sk1-95-38, sk1-95-39]]. Chests sit in Paradise of Man-Eating Flowers, both seals and all three labyrinth floors [[sk1-38-3, sk1-38-9, sk1-38-14, sk1-38-20, sk1-38-24, sk1-38-29]]. Check the checklist at the end before you enter the boss room.

### Preparation and Arc Sophia

🗺️ No map was collected for this chapter.

#### Arc Sophia

Quests are accepted from the NPC Kirby in front of the Arc Sophia transfer gate on Floor 76 [[wiki2-5-1]]. Accept the three Floor 88 quests there before heading out: Walking Flower of Pollen Doom, Little Wings and Glowing Eyes in the Darkness [[sk1-95-37, sk1-95-38, sk1-95-39]].

Optional extras in town:

- Strea can be talked to at Agil's inn once the Floor 82 boss is dead; repeated conversations raise the bond until she agrees to accompany you when asked [[ra-comments-57-4, ra-comments-57-1]].
- Date conversations in Arc Sophia allow more lines if you start the conversation while seated (via the X button) on a bench or fountain [[wiki7-13-6]].
- Sachi's message can be used in Kirito's room [[wiki7-13-1]]. Using a Recording Crystal while alone in Kirito's room triggers a flashback scene of Sachi [[wiki8-9-1]].
- At night in your room, an event about a suspected peeping and an odor can occur, and it also occurs when you are not alone [[wiki1-10-5]].
- The 88th floor shop sells the Dragon Cloak: STR +6, VIT +12, attack +15, defense +50, poison +5%, paralysis +5%, bleed +5% [[wiki7-6-11]].

### Floor 88

#### Dim Plant Marshland

The Walking Flower of Pollen Doom quest is completed by defeating 7 Venom Flowers in the Floor 88 Dim Plant Marshland [[sk1-95-37]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365805]

Kill 7 Venom Flowers here, then report back to Kirby in Arc Sophia [[sk1-95-37, wiki2-5-1]].

#### Paradise of Man-Eating Flowers

The NM Blue Rogue here has abnormally high defense and uses SP-slip attacks. Use a defense-lowering battle skill such as "Defense Nullify", use skill chains, and stock SP-leak-stopping crystals [[sk1-37-1]].

Chests here:

- Water-flow ore x8 [[sk1-38-3]]
- Crimson Flame Ore x8 [[sk1-38-4]]
- Weapon chest A [[sk1-38-7]]
- Weapon chest B [[sk1-38-8]]

Pickups:

- Sky-blue Feathers can be picked up randomly here [[sk1-38-5]]. The Little Wings quest is completed by gathering 4 of them in this area [[sk1-95-38]].
- Old Forest Weeds can also be picked up randomly [[sk1-38-6]].
- Defeat 4 Blue Rogue within the time limit for a Strength Charm [[sk1-38-2]].
- The westernmost passage of this area holds four NM Blue Rogue Brothers placed close together [[wiki7-12-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365806]

After collecting 4 Sky-blue Feathers, turn the quest in. Little Wings is also Floor 88 Check 1 [[sk1-95-38, sk1-102-29]].

#### Strange Waterlily Lakeside

A leaf branch leads to a hilltop with the NM Discard Guardian, which appears only from 19:00 to 05:59; it has nothing to do with dungeon progression [[sk1-37-2]].

The Glowing Eyes in the Darkness quest is completed by collecting 8 Hard Shell drops from Poison Crabs on this part of the floor [[sk1-95-39]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365807]

Collect 8 Hard Shells and hand the quest in [[sk1-95-39]].

#### Dragon Seal Ruins: Shadow Seal, Sun Seal and Center Room

Both seal crystals must be released before the middle door of the Center Room opens [[sk1-37-5]]. Both seals follow the same flow: press the switch, defeat the inner NM, open the door and destroy the crystal [[xneo1-2-3]].

##### Shadow Seal

Activate the device in the back-right room after entering. This opens the door of the central room; defeat the NM Sealguard Golem inside to open the innermost door and release the seal crystal [[sk1-37-3]].

Chests:

- Teleport Crystal x3 [[sk1-38-9]]
- Earth Crust Ore x8 [[sk1-38-10]]
- Sharp Blade Ore x8 [[sk1-38-11]]
- Grand Potion x5 [[sk1-38-12]]
- Weapon chest C [[sk1-38-13]]

##### Sun Seal

Activate the device in the back-left room after entering, then defeat the NM Sealguard Knight in the central room to open the innermost door and release the crystal [[sk1-37-4]].

Chests:

- Diamond Ore x8 [[sk1-38-14]]
- Weapon chest D [[sk1-38-15]]
- Weapon chest E [[sk1-38-16]]
- Gray-white stone x8 [[sk1-38-17]]
- Wind Flower Ore x8 [[sk1-38-18]]

##### Center Room: Necro Dragon

After releasing both crystals, go through the middle door of the Center Room and fight the NM Necro Dragon in the back room [[sk1-37-5]]. Tip: if it targets you, retreat; it gets stuck, and with slight distance adjustment you can keep landing normal attacks [[wiki7-13-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365804]

Defeating the Necro Dragon lets you proceed to Cold Shaded Rain Rifts [[sk1-37-5]].

#### Cold Shaded Rain Rifts

The NM Gluttony Rafflesia is fought here [[sk1-37-6, xneo1-2-4]]. Defeating it is Floor 88 Check 2 [[sk1-102-30]].

### Boss: Rebellious Eyes

Rebellious Eyes is weak to slash attacks [[sk1-38-35]].

- Its stun breath can be avoided with the battle skill Airy Shield [[sk1-38-1]].
- Its sword attacks hit fairly hard, but lowering its accuracy, ideally with two or more skills, makes them almost never land [[sk1-38-1]].

Before the fight, the Floor 88 Checks include Check 1 (Little Wings) and Check 2 (Gluttony Rafflesia) [[sk1-102-29, sk1-102-30]]. Unable to verify from collected sources: the condition of Check 3.

[ACHIEVEMENT:365808]

Defeating Rebellious Eyes completes the Tenants' Work [[sk1-38-1, sk1-38-35]].
