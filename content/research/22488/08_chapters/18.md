## Chapter 18: Floor 93: Magic Building

[MERMAID]

Floor 93 is a short floor. Clear the Top Grade Meat quest and the NM Scarlet Beak check, solve the Magic Building's broken-teleport puzzle, open all 9 chests in the Magic Building (鋼弦の魔楼) 1F–3F, and defeat Lava Creeper [[sk1-102-37, sk1-102-38, sk1-61-2, sk1-62-1, sk1-61-5]]. This chapter also carries a deadline: the Strea bond achievement must be done before Floor 94 is finished [[ra-comments-57-2]].

### Area Overview & Preparation

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** The **Strea** achievement is missable. Finishing Floor 94 is the cutoff, so max her bond before you clear it. Do it in this chapter at the latest [[ra-comments-57-2, ra-comments-57-4]].

Objectives on this floor:

- Max the Strea bond in town [[ra-comments-57-2]].
- Complete the quest The Top Grade Meat (上質肉入手作戦), which is Floor 93's Check 1 [[sk1-95-48, sk1-102-37]].
- Defeat NM Scarlet Beak in the Pierced Cleaved Wilderness (穿たれた裂断の原野), which is Check 2 [[sk1-102-38, sk1-61-1]].
- Fix the broken teleport stone on Magic Building 1F, open every chest, and beat Lava Creeper [[sk1-61-2, sk1-62-1, sk1-61-4]].

### Arc Sophia

Do your town business first. The Strea bond is raised here, and it has to be done before Floor 94 is finished [[ra-comments-57-2]].

#### Strea bond (Achievement 365762)

After you kill the Floor 82 boss, Strea can be talked to in the city at Agil's inn [[ra-comments-57-4]].

1. Go to Agil's inn and talk to Strea. Raising the bond is tiresome at first. The best method found was to talk to her again and again [[ra-comments-57-1]].
2. Keep going until she agrees to accompany you when asked [[ra-comments-57-1]].
3. Then invite her to take a stroll, buy her food and talk to her to raise the friendship [[ra-comments-57-5]].
4. For fast gains, buy a large number of snacks, as with the other characters [[ra-comments-57-3]].

Bond ranks run on a friendship value. RANK5 is 0–14, RANK4 is 15–99 (Acquaintance), RANK3 is 100–299 (Good Friends), RANK2 is 300–499 (Intimate) and RANK1 is 500 (Peerless) [[xneo1-3-3]]. At RANK 3 the "Talk" and "Walk the city together" options are added. At RANK 2 "Form party" and "Gift weapon" are added. At friendship 400 or more, "Gift armor" is added [[xneo1-3-4]]. The achievement asks for the max level, which is RANK1 at 500 [[xneo1-3-3]].

Strea is a guest character with no signature equipment, and she handles a two-handed sword with one hand [[wiki5-14-3]].

In Arc Sophia date conversations, start the conversation while seated (press the X button at a bench, a fountain or any seat). This increases the number of times you can speak [[wiki7-13-6]].

### Floor 93

#### Meadow Linked to the Frayed Edge (綻びに連なる草原): The Top Grade Meat

The quest is completed by collecting 3 Top Grade Boar Meat (上質なボア肉), a drop from the Rage Boar or Master Boar in this meadow [[sk1-95-48]]. Completing it satisfies Floor 93's Check 1 [[sk1-102-37]].

1. Fight the Rage Boars and Master Boars in the meadow until you have 3 Top Grade Boar Meat [[sk1-95-48]].
2. Quest 48 is complete once you hold all three [[sk1-95-48]]. Unable to verify from collected sources: where or how the quest is turned in.

### Magic Building (鋼弦の魔楼)

#### 1F: the broken teleport stone

Defeat NM Earth Disaster and move on past it. The Mysterious Teleport Stone (謎の転移石) lies on the ground beyond [[sk1-61-2]]. Another guide says the normal teleport stone is at the right end of the 1F map [[xneo1-2-11]].

1. Pick up the **Mysterious Teleport Stone**, a key item [[sk1-62-3]].
2. Carry it to the broken teleport stone at the left end of the floor [[sk1-61-2, xneo1-2-11]].
3. Exchange it there to teleport to 2F [[xneo1-2-11, sk1-61-2]].

### Lava Creeper (Floor 93 Boss)

Weak element: blunt (打) [[sk1-61-5]]. Unable to verify from collected sources: Lava Creeper's HP and the recommended level.

Strategy:

- Poison Dance (ポイズンダンス) is a very wide-range poison attack with a short skill activation time. Cancel it with the battle skill Exact Onslaught (エグザクトオンスロート) [[sk1-61-4]].
- If the whole party is poisoned, HP drops quickly. Use Purifying Light (浄化の光) repeatedly to cure the poison [[sk1-61-4]].
