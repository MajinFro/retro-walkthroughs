## Chapter 20: Floor 95: Beast Master

[MERMAID]

This chapter covers the town of Arc Sophia, the Floor 95 fields, the twin lair dungeons, the Beast King's Throne labyrinth and the boss Genocide Eyes. It awards three quest achievements, the Floor 95 chest sweep and the boss achievement [[sk1-95-52, sk1-95-53, sk1-95-54, sk1-69-15]]. Entering the Beast King's Throne requires both the Lizard Key and the Goblin Key, which come from the left and right dungeons [[sk1-68-2]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Arc Sophia:** Quests are accepted from the NPC Kirby in front of the Arc Sophia transfer gate on Floor 76 [[wiki2-5-1]]. Pick up the three Floor 95 quests (Goblin Farm, Horror of the Lizard, The Flower of Life) before heading out [[sk1-95-52, sk1-95-53, sk1-95-54]].

### Arc Sophia

The wiki tells you to take the quest "The Flower of Life" from the quest board [[wiki1-11-1]]. When you enter Floor 95, an event involving Alberich plays [[wiki1-11-16]].

If you still need Strea's bond-related achievement, she can be talked to at Agil's inn once the Floor 82 boss is dead. Raising her bond takes repeated conversations until she agrees to accompany you [[ra-comments-57-4, ra-comments-57-1]]. This is not part of this chapter's achievements.

Optional town extras:
- Sachi's message can be used in Kirito's room [[wiki7-13-1]].
- Using a Recording Crystal while alone in Kirito's room triggers a flashback of Sachi [[wiki8-9-1]].
- In Arc Sophia date conversations, starting the conversation while seated (X button on benches or fountains) increases the number of times you can talk [[wiki7-13-6]].

### Floor 95

#### Wasteland Ravine Away from War

Goblin Swordsmen and Elder Lizardmen roam here, so avoid them as you go [[wiki1-11-2]]. Keep going straight to reach the next field [[wiki1-11-3]].

#### Rock Cave Lined with Battlefields

The monsters here are Goblin Shamans, Goblin Swordsmen, Sackmites and Elder Lizardmen [[wiki1-11-3]]. A chest here holds Weapon Chest A, which the wiki lists as the rapier Justice Lake +5 [[sk1-69-1, wiki1-11-3]]. This is chest 1 of the sweep.

#### Forest Filled with Deep Sleep

The only main monsters are Orc Warriors and NM Gaia Breaker [[wiki1-11-9]]. NM Gaia Breaker is fought in the central section of the forest [[sk1-68-1]]. Defeating it satisfies Floor 95 Check 2 [[sk1-102-42]]. It drops Beastman Fang, SP Hi-Potion, Diamond Stone and Orc Fang [[wiki1-11-9]].

Two chests are here:
- **Teleport Crystal x3** (chest 2) [[sk1-69-2, wiki1-11-9]]
- **Wind Flower Ore x10** (chest 3) [[sk1-69-19, wiki1-11-9]]

After the fight, return to the cave and head to the upper right to reach the Battlefield of Intersecting Twin Walls [[xneo1-2-13]].

#### Battlefield of Intersecting Twin Walls

All three quests are completed here [[sk1-95-52, sk1-95-53, sk1-95-54]]. The Flower of Life can be picked up from glowing points near the entrance and near the road leading to the Lizardman-side door [[wiki1-11-4, sk1-69-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365844]
Gather 4 Flowers of Life to complete "The Flower of Life" [[sk1-95-54]]. This quest is also Floor 95 Check 1 [[sk1-102-41]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365842]
Defeat 10 Goblin Archers in this field to complete "Goblin Farm" [[sk1-95-52]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365843]
Defeat 10 Paladin Lizardmen in this field to complete "Horror of the Lizard" [[sk1-95-53]].

#### Dragon God's Lair (left)

Three chests are here: Weapon Chest B, Weapon Chest C and Weapon Chest D [[sk1-69-4, sk1-69-5, sk1-69-6]]. The wiki lists the contents as the katana Danzai-to Ruten +9, the rapier Kaiser Recht +8 and the two-handed sword Kaiser Dios +9 [[wiki1-11-8]].

In the deepest central room, the Lizardman Monarch always traps you in. Defeat it to obtain the **Lizard Key** [[sk1-68-3, wiki1-11-7]]. It drops Beastman Tail, SP Hi-Potion, Diamond Stone and Lizard Tongue [[wiki1-11-7]].

#### Wise Goblins' Lair (right)

Five chests are here [[wiki1-11-6]]:
- **Crimson Flame Ore x10** [[sk1-69-20]]
- **Weapon Chest E** [[sk1-69-7]]
- **Flowing Water Ore x10** [[sk1-69-21]]
- **Weapon Chest F** [[sk1-69-8]]
- **Weapon Chest G** [[sk1-69-9]]

The wiki lists the weapons as the spear Waldgear +10, the scimitar Kaiser Tarantula +9 and the dagger Venom Intercept +6 [[wiki1-11-6]].

In the deepest central room, defeat the Goblin King to obtain the **Goblin Key** [[sk1-68-4, wiki1-11-5]]. It drops Junk Crown, SP Hi-Potion and Diamond Stone [[wiki1-11-5]].

### Beast King's Throne (labyrinth)

With both keys in hand you can enter the labyrinth [[sk1-68-2]].

#### 1F

On the right route of 1F, the interrupt mission "Defeat 5 Paladin Lizardmen" starts (10-minute limit). The reward is **Heal Boots** [[sk1-69-10, wiki1-11-10]]. Activate the power devices in the left and right rooms to open the two center doors and advance to 2F [[sk1-68-5]].

#### 2F

An interrupt mission to defeat 3 Goblin Gladiators (10-minute limit) appears here. The reward is **Heal Gloves** [[sk1-69-11, wiki1-11-17]]. One chest holds **Weapon Chest H** [[sk1-69-12]].

#### 3F

Here the interrupt mission is to defeat the Lizardman Lord and Goblin Garland within the time limit. The reward is **Heal Cloth** [[sk1-69-13, wiki1-11-11]].

Two chests are here:
- **Weapon Chest I** [[sk1-69-14]]
- **Full Recovery Crystal x3**. After opening it, go to another floor and come back and the contents return, so Full Recovery Crystals can be farmed [[sk1-68-6, wiki1-11-11]]. The wiki also lists the two-handed axe Insane Interact +9 among the floor's chests [[wiki1-11-11]].

### Boss: Genocide Eyes

Genocide Eyes is weak to slash [[sk1-69-15]].

- **Sword attacks:** It uses the same sword skills as a two-handed sword. They hit hard but have a long wind-up, so Parrying makes a Risk Break easy to aim for [[sk1-69-16]].
- **Status attacks:** They inflict Blind, Paralysis and Stun, so equip a Light Crystal or Paralysis Cure Crystal [[sk1-69-17]].
- **Low HP:** When its HP drops it uses a powerful party-wide attack. Use skills that raise defense and healing and lower the enemy's attack to reduce casualties [[sk1-69-18]].

HP, recommended level and the Last Attack Bonus: Unable to verify from collected sources.
