## Chapter 21: Floor 96: Hall Paladin

[MERMAID]

Floor 96 is the Hall Paladin floor. You leave Arc Sophia, fight the Named Monster (NM) Bloody Wasp (NMブラッディワプス) outside town, work through a chain of trap-filled corridors including a teleport-stone puzzle, climb the three-floor labyrinth (聖騎兵の剣堂), and finish with Slaughter Fang (スローターファング). The chapter has five achievements: three quests (Remains of Demon's Dwelling, The Suspicious Client, Thrill of the Giant Spider), the boss (The Slaughter Fang), and the floor chest sweep (Treasure Paladin) [[sk1-95-55, sk1-95-56, sk1-95-57, sk1-72-23, sk1-72-2]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Arc Sophia:** Quests are accepted from NPC Kirby in front of the Arc Sophia transfer gate on floor 76 [[wiki2-5-1]]. Take all three Floor 96 quests before heading out: 悪魔の住む遺跡, 怪しい依頼人 and 巨大グモの戦慄 [[sk1-95-55, sk1-95-56, sk1-95-57]].

### Floor 96

#### Road of Wisdom (叡地に連なる街道)

Immediately outside town you are forced into a fight with NM Bloody Wasp [[sk1-71-1]]. Defeating it satisfies Floor 96 Check 2 [[sk1-102-44]].

The Crimson Spinner is on this same road once you have accepted the quest [[sk1-95-57]].

**Crimson Spinner strategy.** Two methods are documented:

- **Pillar trap.** Hook it between the large and small pillars near the entrance of the Corridor of Sturdy Lock [[wiki7-13-4]]. Lure it between the pillars while you cannot see behind you, and if it fires Destiny Net (ディスティニーネット), crush it with Exact Onslaught (エグザクトオンスロート) [[wiki7-12-6]]. Then chain your secret skills; one report at Lv129 was 32000 EXP for fights 1-2 and 38400 EXP for fights 3-4 [[wiki7-13-4]].
- **Open fight (player report).** When it uses either of its two skills, unlock your aim and move out of the skill range [[ra-comments-143-1]]. Stand just outside the big area skill's range and use your best skill while it is still performing it [[ra-comments-143-2]]. For its other skill, keep unlocking aim until it fires, then use your best skill [[ra-comments-143-3]]. Use your AI partner as a shield so you can keep spamming your best skill [[ra-comments-143-4]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365849]

Defeat the Crimson Spinner after accepting the quest to complete Thrill of the Giant Spider [[sk1-95-57]].

#### Corridor of Sturdy Lock (堅剛なる錠の回廊)

1. Enter and turn right immediately. The chest there holds the **[Key of the Sturdy Corridor]** (堅剛なる回廊の鍵) [[sk1-71-3]]. This chest counts toward Treasure Paladin [[sk1-71-3]].
2. Use the key to open the left door. The power device (動力装置) is beyond it; activating it opens the central door [[sk1-71-4]].
3. Just past the central door, a room on the right holds another power device. Activate it to open the second central door [[sk1-71-4]].
4. Defeat NM Shadow Fang (NMシャドウファング) at the far end to open the door [[sk1-71-5]]. It has high attack power and uses paralysis and bleed attacks [[sk1-71-5]].
5. Take the transfer device in the room beyond the opened door to the Corridor of the Multitude [[sk1-71-5]].
6. Open the chest in this corridor for **700000 Col** [[sk1-72-2]].

Arch Demons for Remains of Demon's Dwelling can be hunted in this corridor or in the next one [[sk1-95-55]].

#### Corridor of the Multitude (多勢なる戦の回廊)

This corridor has many trap rooms that you cannot leave until the enemies are defeated [[sk1-71-2]]. Phantoms in the Corridor of the Multitude and similar areas drop the Cloak of Darkness for The Suspicious Client, and the Arch Demons count for Remains of Demon's Dwelling [[sk1-95-56, sk1-95-55]].

Chests in this corridor:

- **Weapon Chest A** [[sk1-72-3]]
- **Weapon Chest B** [[sk1-72-4]]
- **Wind Flower Stone x10** (風花石) [[sk1-72-26]]
- **Earth Crust Stone x10** (地殻石) [[sk1-72-27]]

Defeat NM Shadow Fang at the far end. This opens the central door to the Floor 96 labyrinth, Hall Paladin 1F [[sk1-71-2]].

Eight Arch Demons defeated in either corridor complete the first quest [[sk1-95-55]].

#### Corridor of the Cunning Bell (狡猾なる鐘の回廊)

Trap magic circles lock you in a room until the enemies are dead, so expect trap rooms here [[sk1-71-6]]. Defeat NM Shadow Fang at the far end and open the door; the transfer device in the central room leads to 策謀なる転の回廊 [[sk1-71-6]]. Unable to verify from collected sources how you first enter this corridor.

Chests:

- **Grand Potion x4** [[sk1-72-5]]
- **Weapon Chest C** [[sk1-72-6]]
- **Crimson Flame Stone x10** (紅炎石) [[sk1-72-28]]

#### Corridor of Scheming Turns (策謀なる転の回廊)

This is a teleport-stone puzzle. Several rooms contain multiple teleport stones (転移石). The right stone advances you; a wrong stone breaks and returns you to the entrance of that route [[sk1-72-20]].

**Solution (first source):** 1st from the left, then 3rd from the left, then 6th from the left, then 10th from the left, which leads to the labyrinth's 1F (Hall Paladin 1F) [[sk1-72-20]].

**Solution (second source):** from the start, left 1, left 3, right 4, right 4, which leads into the labyrinth [[xneo1-2-14]].

The two sources are worded differently, so Unable to verify from collected sources whether they describe the same stones. Try the first and use the second if a stone breaks.

Chests:

- **Weapon Chest D** [[sk1-72-7]]
- **Weapon Chest E** [[sk1-72-8]]
- **Full Recovery Crystal x3** (全快結晶) [[sk1-72-9]]

### Slaughter Fang (Floor 96 boss, Hall Paladin)

Floor 96 Check 1 is completing The Suspicious Client, and Check 2 is defeating NM Bloody Wasp [[sk1-102-43, sk1-102-44]]. Unable to verify from collected sources what Check 3 requires on Floor 96.

**Boss facts:**

- **Weakness:** blunt (打) [[sk1-72-23]].
- **Breath attack:** causes paralysis. Equip the battle skill "Airy Shield" (エアリーシールド) or a paralysis-cure crystal (消痺結晶) [[sk1-72-24]].
- **Charge attack:** inflicts bleed, SP slip and stun, so watch for it [[sk1-72-24]].
- **Low HP:** it becomes faster and uses a strong area attack with paralysis and bleed [[sk1-72-25]].
- The Floor 96 Last Attack item is Northern Licht (ノーザンリヒト), D1280, interval 220 [[wiki6-6-3]].

**Strategy:** Equip Airy Shield or a paralysis-cure crystal, favor blunt damage, and watch for the charge and the stronger area attack once its HP drops [[sk1-72-23, sk1-72-24, sk1-72-25]].
