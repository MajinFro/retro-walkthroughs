## Chapter 7: Floor 82: Solid Grotto

[MERMAID]

Floor 82 is split into two routes that both lead to the same crystal seat. The upper route starts at Deep Cliff Corridor 1F and the lower route starts at Deep Cliff Corridor 3F. Both routes connect back to a transfer device, and the Solid Grotto labyrinth and the boss Grand Legacy lie beyond [[sk1-18-1, sk1-18-3, sk1-19-2]]. This chapter also covers the four Goblin Collector's Cry quests, The Legendary Gem, the NM Greater Bandit and a full chest sweep for Treasure Grotto [[sk1-95-20, sk1-95-23, sk1-95-19, sk1-19-1]].

Quests on every floor are accepted from the NPC Kirby in front of the Arc Sophia transfer gate on Floor 76 [[wiki2-5-1]]. Pick up the Floor 82 quests (numbers 19 to 23 in the quest list) before you head into the dungeon [[sk1-95-19, sk1-95-20, sk1-95-21, sk1-95-22, sk1-95-23]].

### Arc Sophia

Before leaving town, take care of the following.

1. Talk to Kirby at the Floor 76 transfer gate and accept the Floor 82 quests [[wiki2-5-1, sk1-95-19]].
2. After you beat the Floor 82 boss, return here: Strea becomes available to talk to at Agil's inn [[ra-comments-57-4]].

> Before you leave town, remember that the Floor 82 check list requires the quest The Legendary Gem and the defeat of NM Greater Bandit [[sk1-102-18, sk1-102-19]]. Whether a third check exists or is already done: Unable to verify from collected sources.

### Floor 82

The area outside the town has a fork: the upper path leads to Deep Cliff Corridor 1F and the lower path to Deep Cliff Corridor 3F. Start with the upper path [[sk1-18-1]].

#### Deep Cliff Corridor 1F (upper path)

- In the room at the bottom right, open the chest for **Cliff Corridor Key 1**, then use it to open the central door [[sk1-18-1]].
- Other chests on this floor contain **Ash-White Stone x10** [[sk1-19-4]], **Flowing Water Stone x10** [[sk1-19-5]] and **Diamond x10** [[sk1-19-6]].
- Goblin Swordsmen, Archers and Shamans on this floor and elsewhere on Floor 82 drop the items for the Goblin Collector's Cry quests listed in the table above [[sk1-95-20, sk1-95-21, sk1-95-22]]. Fight them as you go.

[ACHIEVEMENT:365758]
Collect 4 Rusty Straight Swords from Goblin Swordsmen to complete Goblin Collector's Cry [[sk1-95-20]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365759]
Collect 3 Holed Quivers from Goblin Archers to complete Goblin Collector's Cry II [[sk1-95-21]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365760]
Collect 5 Broken Spears from Goblin Shamans to complete Goblin Collector's Cry III [[sk1-95-22]].

#### Deep Cliff Corridor 2F

- In the third room, defeating the Goblin King opens the door [[sk1-18-2]].
- Press the switch in the depths of Cliff Corridor 2 from above the cliff [[xneo1-1-17]].
- Using the power device to open the door leads out to Twin Walls Intersecting Crystals [[sk1-18-1]].
- The transfer device there lets you travel back to the earlier area [[sk1-18-1]].

#### Twin Walls Intersecting Crystals

- Rainbow Ultimate Stone can be gathered at 3 spots here. Collect 5 in total for the quest The Legendary Gem [[xneo1-1-16, sk1-95-19]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365766]
Complete The Legendary Gem after collecting the 5 stones. It also satisfies Floor 82 Check 1 [[sk1-95-19, sk1-102-18]].

- NM Greater Bandit is below the cliff of the crystal seat. Defeating it satisfies Floor 82 Check 2 [[xneo1-1-16, sk1-102-19]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365764]
Defeat Greater Bandit here [[sk1-19-1, sk1-102-19]].

> [!TIP]
> Exactly where to descend the cliff to reach the Greater Bandit: Unable to verify from collected sources.

#### Deep Cliff Corridor 3F (lower path)

- Take the lower path; the way onward is reached through it [[sk1-18-3]].
- Defeat the fake door at the far right back. The room it opens holds a chest with **Cliff Corridor Key 2**. Use it to open the central door [[sk1-18-3]].
- Other chests here: **Crimson Flame Ore x6** [[sk1-19-7]], **Earth Crust Stone x6** [[sk1-19-8]] and **Sharp Blade Ore x6** [[sk1-19-9]].

#### Deep Cliff Corridor 4F

- Defeat the NM Fat Slime in the back-right room to open the door leading to the back left [[sk1-18-4]].
- Press the switch in the depths of Cliff Corridor 4 from below the cliff [[xneo1-1-17]].
- Chests: **Sharp Blade Stone x10** [[sk1-19-10]] and **Flowing Water Crystal x5** [[sk1-19-11]].
- From the crystal seat, continue to the labyrinth Solid Grotto [[sk1-19-1]].

#### Goblin King farming (Goblin Collector's Cry IV)

The Goblin King drops the Junk Crown needed for the final quest [[sk1-95-23]]. One RetroAchievements note says to go to the Floor 82 dungeon arena, climb to the top floor and kill the Goblin King [[ra-comments-60-1]]. Sleep at the inn, then repeat until the item drops [[ra-comments-60-2, ra-comments-60-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365765]
Obtain 1 Junk Crown and complete Goblin Collector's Cry IV [[sk1-95-23, ra-comments-60-3]].

### Grand Legacy (Floor 82 boss, Solid Grotto)

- **Weakness:** blunt attacks [[sk1-19-2, wiki4-15-1]].
- **Recommended combat power:** 835 Pts. Last Attack bonus: Virtus [[wiki4-15-1]].
- **Threat:** It uses wide-area attacks, and laser attacks once its HP gets low. Avoid having your skill activations cancelled [[sk1-19-3]]. Its attacks inflict paralysis, so bring Hemostatic Crystals or battle skills against status ailments [[sk1-19-3]].
- **Breaking the core** seals its invincibility move [[wiki4-15-1]].
- **Punch attacks** can be dodged by retreating [[wiki4-15-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365767]
Defeat Grand Legacy to complete Solid Grotto [[sk1-19-2, wiki4-15-1]].
