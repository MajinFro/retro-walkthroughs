## Chapter 10: Floor 85: Stream Grotto

[MERMAID]

Floor 85 starts with a forked forest path. The right branch (Woodland Path of Rustling Fertility) must be cleared before the left branch (Bewildering Tree of Wandering) will open, and the labyrinth at the end (Stream Grotto) splits into a "weak path" and a "conqueror path" on each of its first three floors [[sk1-30-1, sk1-30-4, xneo1-1-21]]. This chapter routes every listed chest, three quests, the Emerald Dragon check, three Sylvern achievements and the Tri-Maelstrom boss. Many chest items are listed by area and floor only, so the exact positions are marked as unverified where that applies.

> [!TIP]
> **Before you start:** Quests are accepted from NPC Kirby in front of the Arc Sophia transfer gate on Floor 76 [[wiki2-5-1]]. The Bewildering Forest (No. 29), Insomnia (No. 30) and Drop of Flower (No. 31) are all Floor 85 quests [[sk1-95-29, sk1-95-30, sk1-95-31]]. Accept all three in town before you head out.

### Floor 85

Floor 85 is made of two forest branches outside town, a lake, a cave west of the lake, a corridor to the labyrinth, and the labyrinth itself [[sk1-30-1, sk1-30-2, sk1-30-3]].

#### Forest fork outside town

Right after leaving, the right-hand east forest is Woodland Path of Rustling Fertility and the west forest is Bewildering Tree of Wandering [[xneo1-1-21]]. The west forest cannot be advanced until the map event in the east forest is cleared, so go east first [[sk1-30-1, xneo1-1-21]].

#### Woodland Path of Rustling Fertility (east forest)

Head to the far right (east) side. A map event starts; defeat the Orc General and the NPC gives you the Map of the Lost Forest [[sk1-30-1, xneo1-1-21]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365784]

This area has three chests, containing Weapon Chest A, Weapon Chest B and Weapon Chest C [[sk1-31-2, sk1-31-3, sk1-31-4]]. Unable to verify from collected sources: the exact chest positions.

While here, defeat 9 Orc Warriors for The Bewildering Forest; only Orc Warriors in this area count [[sk1-95-29, xneo1-1-22]]. Then report to Kirby.

[ACHIEVEMENT:365789]

With Leafa as your partner, NM Crescent Wing is fought in this area to find the Moon Pendant; see the episode list at the end of the chapter [[sk1-93-2]].

#### Bewildering Tree of Wandering (west forest)

This area only progresses after the Orc General map event [[sk1-30-1]]. It has two chests: 6 Flowing Water Ore (流水鉱) and 6 Crimson Flame Ore (紅炎鉱) [[sk1-31-5, sk1-31-6]].

#### Lakeside of Wavering Illusions

The cave Dwelling of the Hunted Hermit lies to the west of this lake [[xneo1-1-23]]. Unable to verify from collected sources: the full lake layout and the exact route to the corridor.

Herb pickups here are random: Silent Herb (静寂の香草) and Old Forest Weed (古森の雑草) [[sk1-31-7, sk1-31-8]]. Collect 4 Silent Herbs for Insomnia and report to Kirby [[sk1-95-30]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365790]

Mandrakes here and in similar areas drop Walking Flower Drop; collect 7 for Drop of Flower [[sk1-95-31]]. Silent Herbs also count toward the Drop of Flower pickups list for this lake [[sk1-31-7]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365791]

#### Dwelling of the Hunted Hermit (cave)

Go to the deepest part of the cave, where NM Emerald Dragon appears; defeating it also fulfils Floor 85 boss check 2 [[sk1-30-2, xneo1-1-23, sk1-102-24]]. It drops the Seeker Plate (body armor) [[sk1-31-13]]. Chests in the cave: 6 Diamond Ore (金剛鉱), 3 Teleport Crystals, Weapon Chest D and Weapon Chest E [[sk1-31-9, sk1-31-10, sk1-31-11, sk1-31-12]].

For Lisbeth's episode, with her as partner, come back here for the Wayland Hammer and fight NM Great Tusk [[sk1-92-4]].

#### Corridor of Brimming Waters

Start the power device so the water in the channels drains, and advance; this brings you out at the Floor 85 labyrinth, Stream Grotto [[sk1-30-3]]. Two chests: Weapon Chest F and Weapon Chest G [[sk1-31-14, sk1-31-15]].

### Stream Grotto (labyrinth)

On 1F, 2F and 3F the route divides into a "weak path" and a "conqueror path". The weak path lets you reach the next floor without meeting monsters; the conqueror path forces you to defeat the tough NM Sylvern first. On 3F the conqueror path means four Sylvern at once [[sk1-30-4]]. The source recommends the weak path only for players who do not need the chests; since Treasure Stream and the Difficult Path achievements need the chests and the Sylvern, take the conqueror path [[sk1-30-4, sk1-31-2]].

Unable to verify from collected sources: which branch holds each labyrinth chest, and the exact route on each floor.

#### 1F

Defeat both Sylvern on the conqueror path [[sk1-30-4]]. The 1F chest holds 6 Sharp Blade Ore (鋭刃鉱) [[sk1-31-16]].

### Tri-Maelstrom (Floor 85 boss, Stream Grotto)

The boss's effective element is slash [[sk1-31-19]].

- **Area attacks:** Its chained area attacks are very strong and can kill party members if you get careless. Use battle skills such as Misdirection to gather hate on Kirito and avoid casualties [[sk1-31-1]].
- **Status effects:** It frequently uses attacks that cause poison and paralysis, so buy as many antidote and paralysis-cure crystals as you can [[sk1-31-1]].
