## Chapter 24: Floors 99 and 100: The Final Battles and Asuna Ending

[MERMAID]

This chapter covers the last two floors. Floor 99 has no dungeon. You win a run of five consecutive boss fights to reach Floor 100 [[sk1-84-1]]. Floor 100 also has no dungeon, and it ends with four consecutive battles. After them you receive an individual ending with the girl you chose as your partner [[sk1-88-1, xneo1-2-17]]. Take Asuna as your partner here to earn the Asuna ending.

### Preparation

> [!WARNING]
> **Save Warning:** Before you start Floor 99, make a save and keep it. Chapter 25 reloads it. That save must already hold every non-ending achievement: all bonds, all chest sweeps, all quests and Kirito at Lv.140. Do not overwrite it.

- The game has multiple endings: one for each of the 5 partner heroines, plus Yui. There is no ending for Klein [[wiki8-7-1]].
- **Open question:** How the Yui ending is reached, and whether one pre-Floor-99 save can be reloaded for every ending, is unconfirmed in the collected sources. Max Yui's bond before you make the save. If the Yui ending does not come from any partner choice, a further playthrough may be needed.
- Weapons sold in the Floor 99 shop are already enhanced up to +15 with bonus effects, so they leave no room for further upgrades [[wiki7-6-2]].
- The last Floor 100 fight is against Heathcliff, and only Kirito and his chosen partner fight it [[wiki4-17-1]].
- The third Floor 100 battle is the hardest. Equip every party-targeted recovery and clone skill you can before it [[wiki4-16-1]].
- A comment on the wiki's boss page says that if Klein dies in the third battle, the result is an immediate Game Over [[wiki4-18-4]].

### Arc Sophia

Arc Sophia is the town where you do your last shopping and bond-building before the boss rush.

- To recruit Asuna, go to Agil's shop on the central plaza and invite her to your party [[wiki1-6-1]].
- In Arc Sophia date conversations, start talking while seated with your partner to get more conversation turns. Press the X button to sit on a bench, a fountain, or any spot you can sit [[wiki7-13-6]].
- Use Sachi's message in Kirito's room [[wiki7-13-1]]. Use a Recording Crystal while alone in Kirito's room to trigger the Sachi flashback scene [[wiki8-9-1]].
- Quests are accepted from the NPC Kirby in front of the Arc Sophia transfer gate [[wiki2-5-1]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Arc Sophia:** Make sure your kept save, with all non-ending achievements, exists. Floor 99 has no dungeon, and the five boss fights begin right away [[sk1-84-1]].

### Floor 99

Floor 99 has no dungeon [[sk1-84-1]]. The Floor 99 boss rush (Reverse bosses) is five bosses in a row, and winning takes you to Floor 100 [[sk1-84-1]].

The Last Attack Bonus occurs only on the fifth boss, Deity Ruler [[sk1-85-4]].

#### Battle 1: Radiance Reverse

- Its effective attribute is blunt (strike) [[sk1-84-5]].
- Acid Squall is a status attack that causes VIT down and SP slip [[sk1-84-2]].
- Sandstorm is a party-wide area attack with a blindness effect [[sk1-84-2]].
- When its HP drops, it fires Bugs Impact, which causes bleeding and paralysis [[sk1-84-2]].

#### Battle 2: Blazing Reverse

- Its effective attribute is blunt (strike) [[sk1-84-6]].
- It uses two-handed sword skills, so many of its attacks are high-power area moves [[sk1-84-3]].
- When its HP drops, it activates a self-buff skill [[sk1-84-3]].

#### Battle 3: Death Reverse

- Its effective attribute is blunt (strike) [[sk1-84-7]].
- It uses the two-handed sword skill Fight Blade, a self-buff that raises its attack according to its SP amount. Be careful when it is active [[sk1-84-4]].
- When its HP drops, it frequently uses Poison Cloud, a party-wide area attack that causes poison, blindness and paralysis [[sk1-84-4]].

#### Battle 4: Chaos Reverse

- Its effective attribute is slash [[sk1-85-3]].
- Its Gnaw (bite) attack causes missing-limb and bleeding effects [[sk1-85-1]].
- Heavy Eater causes stun and increased Risk [[sk1-85-1]].
- When its HP drops, it uses the Dragon Flare breath, which causes VIT down, bleeding and paralysis [[sk1-85-1]].

#### Battle 5: Deity Ruler

- Its effective attribute: Unable to verify from collected sources.
- Supernova is a party-wide area attack with blindness and paralysis effects [[sk1-85-2]].
- Sword Impact causes bleeding [[sk1-85-2]].
- When its HP drops, it uses Dancing Burst, which causes paralysis, stun and increased Risk [[sk1-85-2]].
- This is the only one of the five bosses that gives the Last Attack Bonus [[sk1-85-4]].

When the fifth fight ends, you advance to Floor 100 [[sk1-84-1]].

### Floor 100

Floor 100, the Ruby Palace, has no dungeon, just like Floor 99. Moving from the Ruby Palace to the Corridor to the Throne puts you straight into the passage in front of the boss room [[sk1-88-1]].

### Floor 100 Final Battles (Ruby Palace)

There are no fields on this floor. Four consecutive battles follow. After them, you receive an individual ending with the girl you chose as your partner [[xneo1-2-17]]. Choose Asuna for the Asuna ending.

#### Battle 1: Hollow Avatar

- This is the hollow GM avatar that Kayaba used in the death game announcement [[wiki4-15-2]].
- Its attacks are Death Baptism (bleeding), Release of Despair (a very wide area attack with stun) and, when its HP drops, End of Light (blindness, paralysis and stun) [[sk1-88-5]].
- Landing the final blow gives the Last Attack Bonus Dark Repulser [[sk1-88-2]]. Unlike on Floor 98, this drops the genuine Dark Repulser [[wiki4-15-2, wiki8-16-2]].
- A hidden Last Attack on this fight also yields Boots of Midnight [[sk1-88-3]]. How to trigger the hidden Last Attack: Unable to verify from collected sources.

#### Battle 2: MHCP Prototype Units

- Seven units attack at once and use rapier sword skills [[sk1-88-4]].
- Their attack power is not very high, but they take time to kill. Take them down one at a time [[sk1-88-4]]. If you have room, use area sword skills of a two-handed sword or spear to kill them all at once [[sk1-88-4]].
- They equip daggers or one-handed swords, and units 8 and 9 use stronger skills than the others [[wiki4-16-1]].
- Because there are seven, an ally targeted by several of them is slowly worn down [[wiki4-16-1]].
- The third battle is the biggest hurdle, especially if you are aiming for zero deaths or Klein is taking part. Equip every party-targeted recovery and clone skill you can [[wiki4-16-1]].

#### Battle 3: Hollow Strea

- Hollow Strea is fixed to the wall and fights only with powerful area attacks. Even her normal attacks are area attacks, and her biggest threat is that she cannot be moved away from the wall [[wiki4-16-2]].
- Cancel her skill activations with Exact Onslaught or Weapon Bash [[sk1-89-1]]. Cancel her normal attacks with Parrying [[sk1-89-2]].
- Some attacks, such as Dominion Hollow, can be nullified with Protection Seal [[wiki4-16-2]].
- Another commenter says to interrupt her party-wide attacks with Throat and similar skills, and to parry her hand attacks the moment she raises her hand. Klein dying here is a Game Over [[wiki4-18-4]].
- In the second half she chains wide-area attacks. Once her HP is red, fire your secret skills, take the cursor off her, dash away, and repeat [[wiki7-13-7]].
- She sometimes uses small area attacks that reach even the corner of the room in front of the door, so heal in advance [[wiki7-13-7]].
- A costly alternative is to gift Blood Necklaces to every raid member and fire secret skills from far away. This stabilizes the fight because the healing happens during the cutscene [[wiki4-16-2]].

#### Battle 4: Heathcliff

This is the final fight, and only Kirito and your chosen partner take part [[wiki4-17-1]]. Take Asuna here.

- He is Lv.140 with very high defense [[sk1-89-3]].
- He is the only enemy that actually uses shield defense, which often halves your damage. Choose skills with many hits rather than high single-hit power [[wiki4-17-2]].
- Parrying is especially useful for causing Risk Break. His frequent Unicorn Charge is easy to time: parry after his backstep [[wiki4-17-3]].
- Divine Cross is used from the start, a downward slash into a horizontal slash with a very short startup [[wiki4-17-6]].
- At about half HP he begins using Gospel Square, a four-hit vertical diamond slash, and Perfection Style. Kirito builds focused on evasion need to be wary of Perfection Style [[wiki4-17-7, wiki4-17-9]].
- When he uses Parrying, you can avoid his counterattack by using any skill right after [[wiki4-17-8]].
- At very low HP (around 20% or less) he uses the ultimate skill Akashic Armageddon. It cannot be parried and is very powerful, so interrupt it with Throat or Bash [[wiki4-17-4]].
- Defense Call and Danger Avoidance orders raise your defense, because their effects do not expire during the time limit whatever you do [[wiki4-17-5]].
- One player reports beating him easily by staying outside his effective range and spamming The Eclipse [[wiki4-18-5]].

When he falls, the ending for your chosen partner plays [[xneo1-2-17]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365861]

Winning the whole sequence completes the 100th Floor.

### Asuna Ending

With Asuna as your partner, the Floor 100 battles end in her individual ending [[xneo1-2-17, wiki8-7-1]].
