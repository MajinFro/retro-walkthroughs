## Chapter 8: Floor 83: Kingdom Bandits

[MERMAID]

Floor 83 has you clear the Guardian Trees Forest Path (NM Fear Tree), then the two Wise Demon Settlements, and finally the labyrinth "Kingdom of Greedy Thieves" with its boss Furious Horn [[sk1-21-1, sk1-22-3]]. Eight achievements are tied to this floor: two quests, the Fear Tree, the Settlement 2 room trap, the Interrupt Mission, the boss, the chest sweep and the Kirito level 120 goal [[sk1-95-24, sk1-95-25, sk1-21-1, sk1-22-17, sk1-22-3]].

### Preparation and Warnings

> [!NOTE]
> **Open question (unconfirmed):** The "Survivor" trap in Agile Monsters 2 (Wise Demon Settlement 2) is not described by name in any collected guide. The guides only describe the keys and the Goblin King room, so clear every room of Settlement 2 and defeat everything that locks you in.

> [!NOTE]
> **Open question (unconfirmed):** No source says when Kirito reaches level 100, 110 or 120 in normal play. Unable to verify from collected sources. The level 120 achievement unlocks automatically as you progress; keep following the chapter order.

> [!NOTE]
> **Open question (unconfirmed):** It is not confirmed whether the character-episode tiers ("cleared up to Floor N") open on reaching or on clearing Floor N. Do the events listed at the end of this chapter after defeating Furious Horn; if one has not triggered, continue and retry after the next boss.

> [!TIP]
> **Quest and clear requirements:** Floor 83 has two checks. Check 1 is the quest The Walking Mushroom and Check 2 is defeating NM Fear Tree in Forest-Path Woods [[sk1-102-20, sk1-102-21]]. Quests are accepted from NPC Kirby in front of the Arc Sophia transfer gate on floor 76 [[wiki2-5-1]].

> [!TIP]
> **Chest weapons:** The lists below call weapon chests Weapon Chest A and so on, as the chest list does [[sk1-22-13]].

### Arc Sophia (before leaving)

The Walking Mushroom quest is completed by collecting 4 Poison Spores, the drop from Poison Mushrooms in the Colored Hyphae Sea on Floor 83 [[sk1-95-25]]. The Return My Memory quest is completed by finding the Memorial Pendant in the Colored Hyphae Sea forest on Floor 83 [[sk1-95-24]]. Pick both up from Kirby before you go [[wiki2-5-1]]. The sources do not name a required partner for Floor 83 itself. Unable to verify from collected sources.

### Floor 83

#### Colored Hyphae Sea

On Floor 83 Kirito pays attention to certain mushrooms, and following them takes the shortest route [[wiki1-12-2]].

Chests here (all count toward Treasure Kingdom):
- **400000 Col** [[sk1-22-1]]
- **Sharp Blade Stone x5** [[sk1-22-5]]
- **Teleport Crystal x4** [[sk1-22-6]]
- **Recovery Crystal x5** [[sk1-22-7]]
- **Flowing Water Crystal x5** [[sk1-22-8]]

The Memorial Pendant lies beside the bottom-right chest [[wiki1-12-10, sk1-22-2]]. Picking it up completes the Return My Memory quest [[sk1-95-24]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365773]

Kill the Poison Mushrooms here until you hold 4 Poison Spores, the drop that completes The Walking Mushroom [[sk1-95-25]]. Completing the quest also clears Check 1 [[sk1-102-20]].

#### Crossroads of Fierce Travelers and Guardian Trees Forest Path

NM Fear Tree is in the Guardian Trees Forest Path, which lies west of the Crossroads of Fierce Travelers [[xneo1-1-18]]. Fight it there; it is listed in the check list [[sk1-21-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365770]

Chests here:
- **Ash-White Crystal x5** [[sk1-22-9]]
- **Wind Flower Crystal x5** [[sk1-22-10]]
- **Earth Crust Crystal x5** [[sk1-22-11]]
- **Teleport Crystal x2** [[sk1-22-12]]
- **Weapon Chest A** [[sk1-22-13]]

#### Wise Demon Settlement 1

Get the Armory Key and the Commercial District Key from treasure chests [[sk1-21-2]]. The key to advance is the Commercial District Key at the far upper right [[xneo1-1-19]]. The Armory Key opens the door from the central room to the room with many weapon chests [[sk1-21-2]]. The Commercial District Key opens the door from the central room into Settlement 2 [[sk1-21-3]].

Chest: **450000 Col** [[sk1-22-14]].

#### Wise Demon Settlement 2 (Agile Monsters 2)

Open the chests holding the Hall Key and the Bedroom Key to open the central room's door [[sk1-21-4]]. The Hall Key is at the far left and the King's Key at the far upper right [[xneo1-1-19]]. Go into the room on the right; its chests give the Treasure Vault Key and the King's Key [[sk1-21-5]].

Use the King's Key door and defeat the Goblin Kings beyond to free the captured companions [[sk1-21-5]]. The "Survivor" trap itself is unconfirmed; clear the room fully and defeat everything that locks you in [[sk1-21-4, sk1-21-5]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365771]

Chest: **400000 Col** [[sk1-22-15]].

### Kingdom Bandits (Kingdom of Greedy Thieves)

#### 1F

Beat 3 Funny Caps within the time limit to receive the Ignite Boots [[sk1-22-16]].

#### 2F

Reach the exit within the time limit for the Rabbit Cape; this is the Interrupt Mission [[sk1-22-17]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365772]

Chests:
- **Wind Flower Stone x10** [[sk1-22-18]]
- **Recovery Crystal x2** [[sk1-22-19]]
- **Crimson Flame Crystal x5** [[sk1-22-20]]
- **Diamond Ore x5** [[sk1-22-21]]
- **Teleport Crystal x2** [[sk1-22-22]]

#### 3F

Beat 2 Orc Generals within the time limit for the Sylvan Greaves [[sk1-22-23]]. Chests: **Healing Crystal x3** [[sk1-22-24]] and **Teleport Crystal x2** [[sk1-22-25]].

With the last chest opened, Treasure Kingdom is done [[sk1-22-25]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365775]

### Boss: Furious Horn

Furious Horn is weak to Slash attacks [[sk1-22-3]]. It uses self-buff skills, so prohibit skill activation with a battle skill such as Phantom Break [[sk1-22-4]]. It wields an axe: hits hurt but accuracy is low, so raise party evasion or lower enemy accuracy [[sk1-22-4]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365774]

### Kirito Level 120

High-difficulty quest NMs can be trapped in terrain for roughly 46000-50000 EXP until Kirito is around level 170 [[wiki7-12-4]]. No timing for level 120 is sourced. Unable to verify from collected sources. If you are not level 120 by chapter 22, finish it with the Inferno Dragon farming method there [[wiki7-13-4]].
