## Chapter 23: Floor 98: Trial of Ruby / Ruby Site

[MERMAID]

Floor 98 is the last stretch before the boss rush. It has two side towers, the labyrinth "Trial of Ruby" and the Floor 98 boss. The floor carries the achievements for the Hell Walls, the Trial of Ruby, the Ruby Site and every chest on the floor [[sk1-79-4, sk1-80-1, sk1-80-4, sk1-81-1]]. This is also the last chance to finish every partner bond and the Klein rank before Floors 99 and 100. Floors 98, 99 and 100 have no "check" requirements, so you can go straight to the boss fights [[sk1-102-46]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Floor 98:** Open all 17 chests listed below and make sure every bond and Klein rank is maxed. Floor 99 has no dungeon and is a five-fight boss gauntlet, so nothing on Floor 99 gives you a chance to catch up [[sk1-81-1, sk1-84-1]].

### Arc Sophia (bond cleanup)

Use the town to finish every bond achievement before the boss rush.

The Yui ending is unconfirmed. No collected source says how it is reached, or whether one save from before Floor 99 can be reloaded for every ending. Max Yui's bond before you leave Floor 98. If the Yui ending does not come from any partner choice, a further playthrough may be needed.

#### How bonds work

Friendship has five ranks [[xneo1-3-3]]:

| Rank | Friendship | Title | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|
| RANK5 | 0-14 | (none) | [[xneo1-3-3]] |
| RANK4 | 15-99 | Acquaintance | [[xneo1-3-3]] |
| RANK3 | 100-299 | Good Friends | [[xneo1-3-3]] |
| RANK2 | 300-499 | Intimate | [[xneo1-3-3]] |
| RANK1 | 500 | Peerless | [[xneo1-3-3]] |

The top rank is RANK1 at 500 friendship, and that is what each bond achievement asks for [[xneo1-3-3]]. Menu options unlock with rank [[xneo1-3-4]]:
- RANK3 adds "Talk" and "Walk the city together".
- RANK2 adds "Form party" and "Gift weapon".
- Friendship of 400 or more adds "Gift armor".

**How to raise friendship.** In Arc Sophia, talk to the character, invite them to walk the city with you, and buy them food; each of these raises friendship [[ra-comments-57-5]]. Buying a large number of snacks is the fastest way, as one RetroAchievements commenter reports for Strea [[ra-comments-57-3]]. "Form party" and "Gift weapon" appear at RANK2, and "Gift armor" at 400 friendship [[xneo1-3-4, sk1-100-1]]. Repeat until the rank reads RANK1 (500) [[xneo1-3-3]].

In Arc Sophia date conversations, starting the conversation while seated with your partner increases the number of times you can speak. Press the X button to sit on benches, fountains or any seat [[wiki7-13-6]].

#### Asuna

Go to Agil's shop in the central plaza of Arc Sophia and invite Asuna to the party [[wiki1-6-1]]. Keep raising her bond to RANK1 [[xneo1-3-3]]. Training her also makes her use the star-attribute sword skill Delta Attack [[wiki5-11-3]].

#### Silica

Silica's default armor is Silver Thread Armor [[wiki5-12-1]]. Once she levels up she gets a passive that regenerates SP constantly while equipped, and her pet Pina also supports her [[wiki5-12-2]]. Raise her bond to RANK1 [[xneo1-3-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365746]

#### Leafa

If you raise Switch and Chain, Leafa uses Vertical Square, a star-attribute skill that costs 100 [[wiki5-13-1]]. Her initial equipment and the automatic upgrade at Lv.114 (Euryale's Treasure Sword Omega) are exclusive to her [[wiki5-13-2]]. Raise her bond to RANK1 [[xneo1-3-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365747]

#### Lisbeth

Lisbeth is a blunt-weapon specialist, so she works best against bosses weak to blunt damage [[wiki5-12-3]]. The attack hitbox of her Trinity Arts comes out very fast, at least for a mace skill [[wiki5-12-4]]. Raise her bond to RANK1 [[xneo1-3-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365748]

#### Sinon

Sinon's default armor is Scout Breast. Her ATK armor is Minerva Suit, her DEF armor is Hunter Suit and her BUF armor is Bloom Dress [[wiki5-13-3]]. She fights in a long-range sniping mode and a close-combat mode, and she switches from a dagger to a bow as the story advances [[wiki5-13-4]]. Once her friendship has risen, give her the Cross Necklace to ease her SP problems [[wiki5-13-5]]. Raise her bond to RANK1 [[xneo1-3-3]].

#### Klein rank

Klein's katana skills have a star attribute only at Lv1, so he tends to use diamond-attribute sword skills [[wiki5-14-1]]. After the game is cleared, he joins the last battle in a slot separate from your partner under certain conditions [[wiki5-14-2]]. His achievement asks for the maximum Klein Rank, which uses the same rank scale [[xneo1-3-3]].

#### Extras in town

- Sachi's message can be used in Kirito's room [[wiki7-13-1]]. Using a Recording Crystal while alone in Kirito's room triggers a flashback of Sachi [[wiki8-9-1]].
- The Dragon Scale Belt (STR +10, VIT +20, attack +10, defense +40) is sold in the Floor 98 shop [[wiki7-6-15]].

### Floor 98

Some chests hold weapons of the same type that Kirito or his partner has equipped when you open them [[sk1-81-19]]. The floor has a Shield Tower and a Sword Tower, each with three floors, then the labyrinth "Trial of Ruby" [[sk1-78-1, sk1-79-2, sk1-80-1]].

#### Shield Tower 1F

After you start the generator, go to the central room. NM Chaos Lord appears and traps you inside [[sk1-78-1]]. Open the chests on this floor:
- **Weapon Chest A** [[sk1-81-1]]
- **Grayish-White Stone x10** [[sk1-81-2]]

#### Shield Tower 2F

The bottom-left room on the left side of the map has an obviously suspicious large chest. Opening it spawns NM Surpriser, a giant Mimic, so be ready to fight [[sk1-78-2]]. Chests on this floor:
- **Weapon Chest B** [[sk1-81-3]]
- **Crimson Flame Stone x10** [[sk1-81-4]]
- **Weapon Chest C** [[sk1-81-5]]

#### Shield Tower 3F

The room right inside the entrance is a repeating trap. Beating one group of monsters brings out the next. NM Grim Reaper appears on the third wave, and defeating it reveals a huge generator at the back [[sk1-79-1]].

#### Sword Tower 1F

Start both generators, then go to the central room. NM Blade of Nemesis appears [[sk1-79-2]]. Chests on this floor:
- **Earth Crust Stone x10** [[sk1-81-6]]
- **Diamond x10** [[sk1-81-7]]

#### Sword Tower 2F (Hell Walls)

Several rooms are closed, and you open them by starting generators in this order [[sk1-79-3]]:
1. Start the generator in the second room from the top on the right side of the map. This opens the top room on the right [[sk1-79-3]].
2. Start the generator in the top room on the right. This opens the second room from the top on the left [[sk1-79-3]].
3. Start the generator in the second room from the top on the left. This opens the third room from the top on the left and the room at the back center [[sk1-79-3]].

Another source gives the same order as the switches right-middle, then upper-right, then left-middle [[xneo1-2-16]].

The back-center room holds NM Hell Walls, a giant version of the fake door. The guide says it is extremely strong, so stay back and kill it with a Mystic Art [[sk1-79-4]]. One player's approach was to stay far away with aim lock, spam the best skill available and hope Asuna survives tanking it [[ra-comments-151-1]].

Chests on this floor:
- **Weapon Chest C** [[sk1-81-8]]
- **Weapon Chest D** [[sk1-81-9]]

#### Sword Tower 3F

This floor has another repeating trap in the first room. NM Demonic Eye appears on the third wave. Defeat it to reach the huge generator at the back [[sk1-79-5]].

### Trial of Ruby (Labyrinth)

#### Trial of Ruby 1F

You fight NM Ancient Relic in the first room you enter [[sk1-80-1]]. Chests on this floor:
- **Wind Flower Stone x10** [[sk1-81-11]]
- **Weapon Chest E** [[sk1-81-12]]
- **Weapon Chest F** [[sk1-81-13]]

#### Trial of Ruby 2F

Start the generator in the bottom-center room. This opens the center door and starts the fight with NM Scorbius. Scorbius has two kinds of poison attack, and catching both poisons drains your HP very quickly [[sk1-80-2]]. Chests on this floor:
- **Weapon Chest G** [[sk1-81-14]]
- **Weapon Chest H** [[sk1-81-15]]
- **Weapon Chest I** [[sk1-81-16]]

#### Trial of Ruby 3F

Start the generator in the center room. The door to Trial of Ruby 4F opens and you fight NM Trinity Ferlin [[sk1-80-3]]. The chest on this floor:
- **Weapon Chest J** [[sk1-81-17]]

Clearing the Trial of Ruby floors completes the Trial of Ruby.

### Boss: Hollow Avatar (Floor 98)

The boss strategy source describes a Kaiser Dragon fight on Floor 98, and also names Hollow Avatar as the Floor 98 boss. How the two relate is unconfirmed [[sk1-80-4, sk1-80-5]].

**Kaiser Dragon.**
- It has two breath attacks, Dragon Flare and Crimson Flare. Their status effects include bleed, paralysis, VIT down and stun, and they can knock you back [[sk1-80-4]].
- Bleed is applied in stacks, so equip a hemostatic crystal [[sk1-80-4]].
- It breathes at very short intervals. Quick Skill into Airy Breath is effective [[sk1-80-4]].
- Its "Bite" attack causes limb loss [[sk1-80-4]].

**Hollow Avatar.** Defeating it gives the one-handed sword **Elucidator** [[sk1-80-5, sk1-81-18]]. Elucidator is the Last Attack reward for the Floor 98 boss [[wiki8-16-4]]. Unable to verify from collected sources: Hollow Avatar's weakness and attack patterns.
