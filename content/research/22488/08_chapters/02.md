## Chapter 2: Floor 77: Treasure Maze

[MERMAID]

Floor 77 is the Treasure Maze. Lisbeth can fight beside you as a partner from this floor on [[sk1-99-2]]. This chapter covers the field route, the three Floor 77 quests, the Fat Slime interrupt missions in the labyrinth, every chest on the floor, and the floor boss, Crystallize Claw. The chest sweep is worth five points, each Fat Slime achievement five, and the boss ten [[sk1-4-4, sk1-4-28, sk1-3-5, wiki1-7-9]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave:** finish the leftover chests, the quests and the Goblin Leader before you start the boss fight. The boss fight begins when you talk to the PC in front of the transfer gate after reaching Labyrinth 4F [[wiki1-7-9]]. The Goblin Leader defeat is one of the floor's boss checks [[sk1-102-9]]. The Lisbeth episode tier is handled at the end of this chapter.

### Preparation

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** The quest items are marked as vendor-sale goods. Do not sell them before turning the quests in [[sk1-95-6, sk1-95-7]]. Quests are accepted from NPC Kirby in front of the Arc Sophia transfer gate on Floor 76 [[wiki2-5-1]]. Pick up the three Floor 77 quests there before you start: 恐怖の溶解液 (The Terrifying Slime), 森の贈り物 (The Gift of the Forest) and 未知なる甘みへの挑戦 (The Sweetness Challe) [[sk1-95-5, sk1-95-6, sk1-95-7]].

### Arc Sophia

- Quests are accepted from NPC Kirby in front of the transfer gate [[wiki2-5-1]].
- The Guardian Ring (VIT +5, defense +40) is sold in the Floor 77 shop [[wiki7-6-14]].
- One Floor 77 boss check requires clearing the quest The Sweetness Challe [[sk1-102-8]]. The other requires defeating NM Goblin Leader in the Sneering Demons Cave [[sk1-102-9]].
- The boss fight is started from the PC in front of the town gate once you have reached Labyrinth 4F [[wiki1-7-9]].

### Floor 77

#### Sky-Facing Serpent Tail

1. Move to Sky-Facing Serpent Tail. An event triggers and you fight NM Overlay Slime, so prepare first [[wiki1-7-8, xneo1-1-4]].
2. Overlay Slime resists blunt attacks, so fight it with slash attacks. Its Mucus counts as breath, so dodge it or negate it with the Airy Shield battle skill [[wiki4-11-2]].
3. Green Slimes here are also the target of the quest The Terrifying Slime: defeat 8 Green Slimes in Sky-Facing Serpent Tail [[sk1-95-5, wiki2-6-21]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365716]

Turn the quest in for 12,000 Col and 7,500 EXP [[wiki2-6-7]].

#### Corridor of the Crawling Fool

Teleport here after the Serpent Tail. The device in front of you will not activate yet, so go inward and beat the NM Fat Slimes to activate the switches [[xneo1-1-5]].

1. Open the chests here: Diamond Stone x4, Weapon Chest A, Weapon Chest B and Running Water Stone x4 [[sk1-4-4, sk1-4-5, sk1-4-6, sk1-4-7]]. Another source also lists a Fate Etancel +2 one-handed sword among them [[wiki1-7-1]].
2. Defeat three Fat Slimes [[sk1-3-1, xneo1-1-5]].
3. Defeating all 3 activates the central teleporter to Silent Fresh Green Forest Path [[sk1-3-1, xneo1-1-5]].

**Fat Slime strategy:** The small room with 2 Fat Slimes always links them, so the fight is hard. Remove your shield so evasion can occur, and favour evasion gear and battle skills [[wiki4-11-5]].

#### Silent Fresh Green Forest Path

Use the central teleporter [[xneo1-1-5]].

1. Open the chests: Weapon Chest C, Crimson Flame Stone x4, Weapon Chest D and Weapon Chest E [[sk1-4-8, sk1-4-9, sk1-4-13, sk1-4-14]]. Another source lists Insane Volcano +2, Waldstream +2 and Dead Slugger +4 along with the stone [[wiki1-7-2]].
2. High-Grade Mushrooms, Deadly Poison Mushrooms and Edible Mushrooms can be picked up at random here [[sk1-4-10, sk1-4-11, sk1-4-12]]. The quest The Gift of the Forest needs 5 High-Grade Mushrooms [[sk1-95-6, wiki2-6-22]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365717]

Turn in for 15,000 Col and 4,500 EXP [[wiki2-6-8]].

3. Wisdom Treants here drop Sweet Sap for The Sweetness Challe: collect 3 [[sk1-95-7, wiki2-6-23]]. Camilla's mail request shares the same target, so 6 in total are needed to clear both [[wiki2-6-24]]. Camilla's request is a collect-and-hand-in, not a joint fight [[wiki8-15-3]]. The Sweet Sap comes from Treants, not goblins [[wiki8-15-2]]. If you collect in the preceding forest first, the request completes immediately when it activates [[wiki8-15-4]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365718]

Turn in for 8 SP High Potions, 13,340 Col and 5,600 EXP [[wiki2-6-23, wiki2-6-9]].

#### Raging War Demon's Clearing

- After clearing Floor 76, Camilla can be found here at night [[wiki10-5-1]].
- On the hill is the Treant Dominator, ringed by Wisdom Treants. It is not required for progress [[sk1-3-3]]. It is very dangerous for assault-team members [[wiki8-13-3]]. If you fight it, pull its two adds with throwing knives from the edge of detection range [[wiki4-11-4, wiki8-15-8]], or defeat the surrounding Wisdom Treants one at a time with Long Cast rather than Step-In [[sk1-3-3]].
- Goblin Dojo: six goblins here allow continuous proficiency training up to 600 [[wiki7-11-2]].

#### Cave of Sneering Demons

The entrance is at the back-left of the Clearing and is easy to miss [[sk1-3-2]]. Follow the left wall from the entrance and drop down into the cave [[wiki8-15-1]]. The NM Goblin Leader is in the deepest part [[xneo1-1-6]]. Defeating it is required for the boss check [[sk1-102-9]].

1. Chests: Healing Crystal x5, Weapon Chest F, Crimson Flame Ore x4, Teleport Crystal x3, Wind Flower Stone x4 and Weapon Chest G [[sk1-4-15, sk1-4-16, sk1-4-17, sk1-4-18, sk1-4-19, sk1-4-20]].
2. Goblin Leader is accompanied by 2 bow goblins; kill them first [[wiki4-11-3]].
3. The interrupt mission reward for defeating it is Journey Glove [[sk1-4-21, wiki1-7-3]].

- Chests: Earth Crust Stone x10, Wind Flower Ore x4, Ash-White Stone x4, Healing Crystal x3 and Sharp Edge Stone x4 [[sk1-4-23, sk1-4-24, sk1-4-25, sk1-4-26, sk1-4-27]].

#### Labyrinth 4F and the Boss Door

After reaching Labyrinth 4F, talk to the PC in front of the transfer gate to start the floor boss battle [[wiki1-7-9]].

### Crystallize Claw (Floor 77 Boss)

- Weak to blunt attacks; recommended combat power 760 Pts; Last Attack drop is Duel Glove [[wiki4-11-1, sk1-4-1]].
- It inflicts paralysis and poison, so bring status-cure items or battle skills [[sk1-4-2]].
- An all-party attack halts everyone's movement. Parry to cause a Risk Break, then chain attacks [[sk1-4-2]].
- Deal the finishing blow with Kirito to get the Last Attack Bonus [[sk1-4-3]].
