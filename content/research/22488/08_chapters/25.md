## Chapter 25: The Other Five Endings

[MERMAID]

### Overview and Preparation

This chapter covers the remaining endings. The game has multiple endings for the five partner heroines and Yui, and none for Klein [[wiki8-7-1]]. The Asuna ending is handled in the previous chapter, so what remains is the Silica, Lisbeth, Leafa, Sinon and Yui endings. Floor 100 has no field. It is four consecutive battles, and after them you receive an individual ending with the girl you chose as your partner [[xneo1-2-17]].

The route is to keep a save from before Floor 99 and repeat the Floor 99-100 battles once per remaining heroine, choosing a different heroine as your partner each time [[xneo1-2-17]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Unconfirmed (Yui ending):** No collected source says how the Yui ending (Long Time No See, Papa, Mama...) is reached, or whether one pre-Floor-99 save can be reloaded for every ending. The Yui ending is not confirmed to come from any partner choice. Max Yui's bond before you make the save. If no partner choice gives the Yui ending, a further playthrough may be needed. This is unconfirmed.

Every run ends with the same four-battle sequence, so the strategy is written once below, and each ending achievement is placed at the end of the run it belongs to [[xneo1-2-17]].

### Arc Sophia

Arc Sophia is the town on floor 76 where you accept quests and meet your partners. Quests are accepted from the NPC Kirby in front of the transfer gate [[wiki2-5-1]]. The Egil shop in the central plaza is where Asuna is invited to the party [[wiki1-6-1]].

> [!NOTE]
> Unable to verify from collected sources: the exact steps for selecting each heroine as your partner before Floor 99.

Useful notes for preparing in town:

- Your partner's bond rises through talking and dates. Starting a date conversation while seated (X button on a bench or fountain) lets you speak more times [[wiki7-13-6]].
- Strea becomes available to talk to at Agil's inn after the Floor 82 boss. Raising her bond takes repeated conversations until she agrees to join you [[ra-comments-57-4, ra-comments-57-1]].
- Sachi's message can be used in Kirito's room [[wiki7-13-1]]. With nobody else present, using a Recording Crystal in the room triggers a flashback scene of Sachi [[wiki8-9-1]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Arc Sophia:** Confirm that you keep a save from before Floor 99 and that your chosen partner's bond is as high as you can make it. The game has endings for five heroines and Yui [[wiki8-7-1]]. The Yui requirement is unconfirmed, so max Yui's bond first.

### Floor 100

The Floor 100 Ruby Palace has no dungeon. Going from the Ruby Palace to the corridor to the throne drops you straight into the corridor before the boss room [[sk1-88-1]]. The last stretch has no field, just four consecutive battles [[xneo1-2-17]].

### Floor 100 Final Battles (Ruby Palace)

The sequence is four battles [[xneo1-2-17]]. In the preceding Floor 99 boss rush, the known weaknesses are: Radiance Reverse, Blazing Reverse and Death Reverse are weak to blunt (打) attacks [[sk1-84-5, sk1-84-6, sk1-84-7]], and Chaos Reverse is weak to slashing (斬) attacks [[sk1-85-3]].

#### Battle 1: Hollow Avatar

Hollow Avatar uses Baptism of Death (bleed), Release of Despair (very wide, stun) and, at low HP, End of Light (blind, paralysis, stun) [[sk1-88-5]]. The Last Attack drops the genuine Dark Repulser, unlike the Floor 98 fight [[wiki4-15-2, sk1-88-2]]. A hidden Last Attack also gives Boots of Midnight [[sk1-88-3]]. The genuine Dark Repulser is obtained only as the Last Attack reward from this Floor 100 boss, so deliver the final blow yourself [[wiki8-16-2]].

#### Battle 2: MHCP units

Seven MHCP units attack at once, using rapier sword skills. Kill them one at a time, or use two-handed sword or spear area skills to take several at once [[sk1-88-4]]. The units wield daggers or one-handed swords, and units 8 and 9 use stronger skills. The danger is the number: allies targeted by several units lose HP steadily [[wiki4-16-1]].

> [!TIP]
> **Prepare for Battle 3 here:** Equip every party-wide heal and clone skill you can. Klein's death is an immediate Game Over [[wiki4-16-1, wiki4-18-4]].

#### Battle 3: Hollow Strea

Hollow Strea is fixed to the wall and uses powerful area attacks, one after another [[wiki4-16-2]].

- Her skill activations can be cancelled with Exact Onslaught or Weapon Bash, and her normal attacks with Parrying [[sk1-89-1, sk1-89-2]].
- Interrupt party-wide Hollow attacks with Throat, and parry hand attacks the moment the hand is raised [[wiki4-18-4]].
- Protection Seal can nullify some attacks such as Dominion Hollow [[wiki4-16-2]].
- Once her HP turns red, fire off your secret skill, then move the cursor off her and dash away. Heal in advance because small area attacks reach even the room corners in front of the door [[wiki7-13-7]].
- Alternatively, give Blood Necklaces to every raid member and spam secret skills from afar [[wiki4-16-2]].

#### Battle 4: Heathcliff

Only Kirito and the chosen partner fight [[wiki4-17-1]]. Heathcliff is level 140 with high defense [[sk1-89-3]].

- He is the only enemy that uses shield defense, which often halves damage, so use skills with many hits rather than one big hit [[wiki4-17-2]].
- Parrying works well for inflicting Risk Break, especially against Unicorn Charge after his backstep [[wiki4-17-3]].
- Divine Cross is used from the start and its first hit comes very fast [[wiki4-17-6]]. Gospel Square is a four-hit diamond slash used after half HP [[wiki4-17-7]].
- When he uses Parrying, executing any skill right after avoids his counter [[wiki4-17-8]].
- At half HP he uses Perfection Style, a warning for evasion-focused builds [[wiki4-17-9]].
- At about 20% HP or less he uses the ultimate Akashic Armageddon, which cannot be parried. Interrupt it with Throat or Bash [[wiki4-17-4]].
- Defense Call and Danger Avoidance orders boost defense because their effects do not expire during the time limit [[wiki4-17-5]].
- One player won easily by staying outside his effective range and spamming The Eclipse [[wiki4-18-5]].

### The Endings

After the four battles you receive an individual ending with the girl chosen as your partner [[xneo1-2-17]]. Reload the pre-Floor-99 save for each run.

**Silica run:** Choose Silica as partner and win all four battles [[xneo1-2-17, wiki8-7-1]].
