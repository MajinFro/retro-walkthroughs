### Game Overview

*Sword Art Online: Infinity Moment* is a PSP action RPG by Aquria, published by Bandai Namco and released on 2013-03-14. Its RetroAchievements set has 174 achievements worth 1281 points. This guide covers one playthrough that climbs Floors 76 to 100 in order, one chapter per floor. The last two chapters cover the endings. The set uses fan-translation names, and this guide gives the Japanese originals where they help.

The story is split into six endings. The game has endings for the five partner heroines and for Yui, but none for Klein [[wiki8-7-1]]. After four consecutive battles on Floor 100, you get an individual ending with the girl you chose as your partner [[xneo1-2-17]]. Chapter 24 covers the Asuna ending and Chapter 25 covers the other five.

### How the 100% Plan Works

The plan is one playthrough with a held save. Chapter 24 keeps a save from before Floor 99, and Chapter 25 reloads it for each remaining heroine. Whether a second playthrough is needed for any ending is not settled by the sources.

> [!NOTE]
> **Unconfirmed:** How the Yui ending (Long Time No See, Papa, Mama...) is reached, and whether one pre-Floor-99 save can be reloaded for every ending, are not answered by any collected source. Max Yui's bond before you make that save. If the Yui ending does not come from any partner choice, a further playthrough may be needed.

Hard Mode is added by a patch and is selectable only when starting a new game from the second playthrough onward, with higher enemy levels [[wiki9-5-1]]. The patch was released on 2013/06/06 [[wiki8-7-2]]. Nothing in this set requires Hard Mode.

### Hardcore Mode Rules

Hardcore Mode turns off the emulator's save states, rewinds and cheats, so you rely entirely on the game's own saves. Do not plan around any emulator save feature.

- Save often. The game makes an auto-save when you change area, and you can quit and reload from it [[wiki2-5-6]].
- If you reset before the map-transition auto-save happens, you return slightly earlier. START OF LAYER on the title screen is another way to resume [[wiki8-15-9]].
- Keep a separate save from before the Ant Queen fight on Floor 84. One commenter reports that the game freezes after the Floor 84 boss is defeated and blames the translation patch [[ra-comments-1-1]]. If it freezes, continue from that separate save without the patch. Never overwrite your only save before this boss.
- Keep the pre-Floor-99 save described above for the endings.

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** The Strea bond achievement has a point of no return. Finishing Floor 94 closes it, so raise her bond before then [[ra-comments-57-2]].

> [!CAUTION]
> **Point of No Return:** Entering the Floor 84 labyrinth 2F forces a party with Strea up to the boss room, and you cannot go back [[wiki1-12-901]]. Finish everything you want on Floor 84 first.

### Basic Controls and Interface

- **Map and menus:** Press L and R together, then Triangle, in a dungeon to show the map. This is faster than opening it from the menu. Circle opens the item menu [[wiki7-7-1]].
- **Item sorting:** Triangle auto-sorts the inventory, although no button help appears on screen [[wiki7-7-3]].
- **Skill lists:** Sword Skills can be edited with Triangle and rearranged with Square, just like Battle Skills [[wiki7-7-4]]. To remove one from the list, open the edit screen with Triangle, press Triangle again to set no skill, and confirm [[wiki7-7-5]].
- **Cutscenes:** Hold L to fast-forward dialogue. START skips movies, and R hides the text window during event conversations [[wiki7-7-6]].
- **Partner growth screen:** On field areas outside town, Triangle opens the partner growth screen [[wiki7-7-2]].

### Combat Fundamentals

**Target lock and movement.** Pressing X once releases the target lock. The cursor turns from purple to yellow, and you can move at field speed with your weapon drawn [[wiki7-8-1]]. Unlocking also lets you dodge enemy skills without step skills. You can then pick another target with D-pad left and right to inflict stun or seal [[wiki7-8-2]]. Once the cursor is yellow, D-pad left or right selects a target and Circle returns the cursor to purple [[wiki4-6-4]]. Pressing X twice stops attacking and sheathes your weapon, which lets you escape combat [[wiki7-8-4]].

**Early versus late combat.** With few skills, early combat is mostly watching auto-attacks and using a skill now and then. Later it gets busy with giving instructions and chaining skills [[wiki8-8-4]].

**Skills in combat.**
- Sword Skills are interrupted when you are attacked during standby or activation. Consecutive hits risk a Risk Break, so reliably kill the easiest enemy first, one at a time [[wiki4-6-2]].
- Sword Skills come in diamond and star types. Diamond skills are easily cancelled by normal attacks and waste SP, so prefer star skills [[wiki8-12-1]]. Secret skills are diamond but can be triggered at any distance [[wiki8-12-2]].
- Most buff and debuff skills apply their effect as a percentage of the base value, so they grow stronger in the late game and in long boss fights, where they increase total damage or reduce hits taken [[wiki4-6-1]].
- Powerful enemy skills are basically crushed with stun techniques such as Weapon Bash (SP 50, very fast to start) and Exact Onslaught (SP cost 0) [[wiki4-8-1]].
- Hyper Armor consumes SP 100 and has a long recast, but it blocks all normal attacks and skills [[wiki4-8-2]].
- Robe of Mercy consumes SP 50 and blocks physical attacks three times, but it cannot block breath or consecutive attacks [[wiki4-8-3]].
- Using a battle skill with the Bunshin (clone) effect dodges all incoming attacks several times [[wiki4-9-3]].

**Level difference.** The game adds positive or negative modifiers to damage, hit rate and evasion based on level difference [[wiki7-8-5]].

**Field mid-bosses.** Field mid-bosses and NMs often bring escorts. If you first attack only the escorts with the Triangle knife throw, the boss may not come at you [[wiki4-6-3]].

**Gathering and respawns.**
- Gathering points respawn when in-game time passes 0:00. They also respawn if you let the area-change auto-save happen, quit, and reload [[wiki2-5-6]].
- Some mid-bosses and named monsters respawn after you return to town, after 10 to 12 hours, or via the quit-and-reload method [[wiki7-9-3]].

### Weapons, Skills and Equipment

- Using only one weapon type blocks useful derived battle skills, so use a variety of weapons [[wiki4-5-1]]. Unlocking battle skills requires raising various weapons. Some enemies have extreme resistances (thrust against skeletons, blunt against slimes), so develop weapons evenly [[wiki8-10-5]].
- Weapon skill rises easily up to about 200, slows past 500, and becomes a grind beyond 700 [[wiki8-11-1]]. Learning every sword skill and battle skill needs 1079 spt in total [[wiki8-11-2]].
- Each weapon has 9 to 10 sword skills but there are only 8 skill slots, so decide in advance which to drop [[wiki8-11-3]].
- Dual Wield is available from the start by equipping two One-Handed Swords [[wiki8-10-4]]. Its speed and attack both come from adding the two hands together and dividing by two [[wiki6-6-5]].
- A katana can be equipped, but its skills do not grow unless Curved Sword skill is raised and the prerequisite skill is learned [[wiki8-10-1]]. Most katana skills are diamond type [[wiki8-10-2]].
- A bow's normal attack is treated as a skill and cannot be shortened, so choose a bow for attack power or for status ailments such as poison [[wiki6-6-6]].
- Enhancing a weapon improves its performance only up to +15. You can keep enhancing after that, but nothing changes [[wiki6-5-1]].
- Added effects such as STR rise by 2 per enhancement, up to 30 [[wiki6-5-2]].
- A weapon with two added stats has a lower enhanceable stat relative to its enhancement value, so it keeps working up to +16 [[wiki6-5-3]].
- HP and SP recovery battle skills often hit the whole party. They matter most if you do not want casualties in floor boss fights [[wiki8-11-4]]. If SP runs short, SP Charge (Tactics, from Spear) helps [[wiki8-11-6]]. Lightning Attack (Lightning, from Rapier) helps as well [[wiki8-11-7]].
- Hidden passive skills on equipment can be viewed by pressing SELECT on the Active Skill screen. For example, the Rage Ring grants permanent Counter [[wiki8-16-1]].
- Weapons in dungeon chests have randomized names, stats, effects and enhancement counts. Some chests give the same weapon type that Kirito or his partner is using [[sk1-2-41]]. The name is a random prefix plus suffix [[wiki6-6-4]].
- From Floor 81, treasure chests can be mimics. They are annoying but pay a large amount of Col [[sk1-14-1]].

### Partners and Bonds

Partners are fixed per floor from Floor 76 to Floor 80 and become selectable from Floor 81 [[wiki8-12-3]]. Partners other than Asuna join in the late 80s in level and need training to survive [[wiki8-12-4]]. Weapons can be gifted to partners, and armor can be gifted once friendship passes a threshold [[sk1-100-1]].

**Friendship ranks.** The ranks run RANK5 (0 to 14), RANK4 (15 to 99, Acquaintance), RANK3 (100 to 299, Good Friends), RANK2 (300 to 499, Intimate) and RANK1 (500, Peerless) [[xneo1-3-3]]. At RANK3 the Talk and Walk the city together options appear. At RANK2 Form party and Gift weapon appear. At friendship 400 or more, Gift armor appears [[xneo1-3-4]]. Another guide puts equipment gifts at weapons for RANK2 and armor, body and neck items for RANK1 [[wiki5-5-3]]. A partner refuses a gift whose sell price is lower than their current gear [[wiki5-5-4]]. Partners switch equipment automatically at levels 102, 108 and 114, with Klein's levels differing [[wiki5-5-1]].

**Date conversations.** In Arc Sophia, starting a conversation while seated on a bench or fountain with X increases how many times you can talk [[wiki7-13-6]].

**Strea.** After the Floor 82 boss is killed, Strea can be talked to at Agil's inn [[ra-comments-57-4]]. Inviting her on a stroll, buying her food and talking to her raise the friendship [[ra-comments-57-5]]. Buying many snacks raises her bond quickly [[ra-comments-57-3]].

**Partner AI.**
- Praising a partner's action with good in battle grows their AI and changes their tendencies [[sk1-100-2]]. Actions fall into 12 items on the diamond graph, and each GOOD! raises the matching one [[wiki5-6-1]].
- GOOD! on a specific skill makes the partner use it more [[wiki5-6-2]]. Raising some categories shrinks growth in others, so not every item can be raised [[wiki5-6-4]].
- Praise text is color-coded: red for attack actions, blue for defense, green for buffs and recovery [[wiki7-7-7]].
- Partners use skills at 300 SP by default [[wiki5-8-1]].
- Instructions given with R, such as "fall back", stay in effect if you leave them alone. Revise them as the situation changes, or the battle can collapse [[wiki4-7-5]].
- If cornered, use a switch to swap places with your partner [[wiki4-7-3]].
- Partner items are refilled automatically when you return to Arc Sophia [[wiki7-9-1]].

**Character-specific notes.**
- Asuna can be grown into using the star-type Delta Attack [[wiki5-11-3]].
- Silica gains a passive that regenerates SP at higher levels, and she also gets support from her pet Pina [[wiki5-12-2]].
- To use Pina, talk to her, decide a growth policy, and feed her ore. She then supports you in various ways in battle [[sk1-94-1]].
- Lisbeth specializes in blunt damage, which suits bosses weak to it [[wiki5-12-3]].
- Lisbeth's Trinity Arts has a very fast attack hitbox [[wiki5-12-4]].
- Advancing Lisbeth's character episodes up to the Wayland Hammer raises the weapon enhancement success rate by the maximum +50% [[sk1-92-5]].
- Leafa uses the star-type Vertical Square if you raise Switch and Chain [[wiki5-13-1]].
- Sinon starts with a dagger and uses a bow as the story advances [[wiki5-13-4]].
- Klein tends to use diamond skills because his katana has only a Lv1 star skill [[wiki5-14-1]]. If you raise Furinkazan to the top guild, Klein joins boss battles even when not your partner [[wiki4-18-1]].

### Floor Bosses and Checks

Each floor has "checks". Clearing them unlocks boss traits and weaknesses in the GUILDS menu, and you cannot challenge the boss until every check on that floor is cleared [[sk1-102-5]]. Floors 98, 99 and 100 have no checks and go straight to the bosses [[sk1-102-46]].

- Boss evaluation uses damage dealt, damage taken, action disruption and casualties. Zero casualties is an absolute condition, and clear time does not matter [[wiki4-10-1]]. Interrupting a boss skill with bash adds points [[wiki8-14-4]].
- Floor bosses have parts with their own HP. Reducing a part to 0 destroys it and temporarily disables the matching skill, which recovers over time. Destroying a part also gives experience [[wiki4-8-4]].
- Striking the final blow earns a Last Attack bonus, a special equipment piece. It must be Kirito himself. If a partner or assault team takes it, you can basically never get it again [[wiki4-9-1]]. Secret arts darken the screen so no attacks occur, making the last hit much easier. They need 600 or 800 weapon skill [[wiki4-9-4]].
- Assault Team parties gain experience as in-game time passes. Right after a new floor opens their levels are low, so help them for about 2 levels to avoid deaths [[wiki7-8-6]].

### Skill Training

The tutorial combat training can be used for idle skill training if you do not switch when told to, up to about 500 skill. The weapon must have no poison or bleed [[wiki7-10-3]]. Hitting the other 2 bees as well lets all 3 attack you, which trains evasion or shield skill [[wiki7-10-4]]. Higher-level monsters raise skills more easily, and auto-attacks give more chances than sword skills [[wiki7-10-1]].

These points are unconfirmed; the chapters follow the guidance below.

I'm Sorry For Causing You Trouble." (description "Save Shinon"). The chapters list them with their description only, and give no steps. The trigger for "Hehe..." is also unconfirmed; see Chapter 1.

- **Protect the Farm Crop and The Poisonous Spider.** The guides disagree between 8 and 10 kills. Kill 10 of each target.
