## Chapter 5: Floor 80: Monster Maze

[MERMAID]

Floor 80 is the Monster Maze floor, and Sinon becomes a partner here, initially equipped with only a dagger [[sk1-99-6]]. The route runs through the Sacrifice Level prison floors (a key opens the inner door on each), the three Cultist Research Lab floors (a switch opens each door), the Hidden Research Prison, the Road of the Dead, and the Monster Maze labyrinth with its boss, Guilty Scythe [[sk1-12-1, sk1-12-3, wiki1-9-3, wiki1-9-4, wiki1-9-5, sk1-12-2]]. This chapter covers every chest on the floor, three quests (The Lady's Secret, Bone Collector and Treasure Fight), NM Darkness, Yui's "Papa!" event and the boss.

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Floor 80:** This chapter has no point of no return, but Treasure Maze needs every chest on the floor. Sweep every area below before you fight Guilty Scythe so you do not have to backtrack [[sk1-13-1, sk1-13-2]].

### Arc Sophia

Quests are accepted from NPC Kirby in front of the Arc Sophia transfer gate [[wiki2-5-1]]. Pick up the three Floor 80 quests there before you head out:

- **The Lady's Secret:** collect 9 Sticky Slime Mucus (ねばねば粘液), dropped by Red, Green and Yellow Slimes in the Cultist Research Lab and similar places [[sk1-95-15]]. It is also Floor 80 Check 1, so it must be done before you can fight the boss [[sk1-102-14]].
- **Bone Collector:** collect 9 Sturdy Femurs (丈夫な大腿骨), dropped by Skeleton Knights or Skeleton Pawns in the Sacrifice Level [[sk1-95-16]].
- **Treasure Fight:** pick up 7 Mole Iron (土竜鉄) on the Road of the Dead [[sk1-95-14]].

For the boss, prepare paralysis countermeasures: the Scale Mantle has paralysis resistance, and Flame of Purification is essential [[wiki4-13-4]].

Optional town extras, if you are in Arc Sophia anyway:

- In date conversations, start talking while seated on a bench or fountain (press the X button to sit) to get more conversation turns [[wiki7-13-6]].
- Sachi's message can be used in Kirito's room [[wiki7-13-1]].
- Using a Recording Crystal in Kirito's room while alone triggers a flashback scene of Sachi [[wiki8-9-1]].

### Floor 80

#### Sacrifice Level 1F (嘆く犠牲の留置所1階)

Open every chest on this floor. The contents are:

- Weapon Chest A [[sk1-13-1]]
- Weapon Chest B [[sk1-13-2]]
- 10 Flowing Water Stones (流水石) [[sk1-13-31]]
- 10 Earth Crust Stones (地殻石) [[sk1-13-32]]
- 10 Crimson Flame Stones (紅炎石) [[sk1-13-33]]

The key chest is in the middle cell on the right side [[sk1-12-3]]. Another guide says it is the left-center or right-center chest and that it may vary, so check both [[wiki1-9-1]]. Take the **Detention 1F Key** and use it on the inner door to reach 2F [[sk1-12-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365740]

Skeleton Knights and Skeleton Pawns in this prison drop Sturdy Femurs for Bone Collector, so fight them as you go [[sk1-95-16]].

#### Sacrifice Level 2F (嘆く犠牲の留置所2階)

Chests on this floor:

- Weapon Chest C [[sk1-13-3]]
- Weapon Chest D [[sk1-13-4]]
- 5 SP Grand Potions [[sk1-13-5]]
- 10 Diamond Stones (金剛石) [[sk1-13-34]]
- 6 Ash-White Ore (灰白鉱) [[sk1-13-35]]

The **Detention 2F Key** is in the cell chest of the left-hand cell on the left side, which is the upper-right room on the map [[sk1-12-1]]. A second guide agrees it is the upper-right room's chest [[wiki1-9-2]]. Use the key on the inner door to enter Cultist Research Lab 1F [[sk1-12-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365741]

Once you hold 9 Sturdy Femurs, turn in Bone Collector at Kirby in Arc Sophia [[sk1-95-16, wiki2-5-1]].

#### Cultist Research Lab 1F (狂信者達の研究所1階)

The switch at the lower right of the map opens the door onward [[wiki1-9-3]]. Chests:

- 300000 Col [[sk1-13-6]]
- 3 Grand Potions [[sk1-13-7]]

Red, Green and Yellow Slimes in the lab drop Sticky Slime Mucus for The Lady's Secret [[sk1-95-15]].

#### Cultist Research Lab 2F (狂信者達の研究所2階)

The switch at the upper left of the map opens the door [[wiki1-9-4]]. Chests:

- 3 Grand Potions [[sk1-13-8]]
- Weapon Chest E [[sk1-13-9]]

#### Cultist Research Lab 3F (狂信者達の研究所3階)

The switch at the left of the map opens the door [[wiki1-9-5]]. Chests:

- Weapon Chest F [[sk1-13-10]]
- Weapon Chest G [[sk1-13-11]]
- 2 Recovery Crystals [[sk1-13-12]]

Beyond the door that was previously closed on the right side of the map, you are locked into a room and fight the Dragon Zombie, the "Decayed Dragon King". Prepare before you go in [[wiki1-9-6]].

Chests:

Leave the prison for the Road of the Dead. NM Darkness is here, before the labyrinth. Defeat it before entering the Monster Maze [[sk1-12-2, xneo1-1-12]]. It is also Floor 80 Check 2 [[sk1-102-15]].

#### Monster Maze 1F (魂求めし怪物達の迷宮1階)

- Defeat 3 Mandrakes within the time limit for the Nacht Buckler [[sk1-13-18]].
- Chests hold 3 SP Hi-Potions [[sk1-13-19]], 10 Wind Flower Stones (風花石) [[sk1-13-36]] and 10 Sharp Blade Stones (鋭刃石) [[sk1-13-37]].

#### Monster Maze 2F (魂求めし怪物達の迷宮2階)

NM Fat Slimes sit one per room in six rooms. They are tough but weak attackers, which makes them good targets for training partner AI [[wiki7-12-1]]. Defeating 6 of them within the time limit gives the Blood Charm [[sk1-13-21]].

Chests:

- 4 SP Hi-Potions [[sk1-13-22]]
- Weapon Chest H [[sk1-13-23]]
- Weapon Chest I [[sk1-13-24]]
- Weapon Chest J [[sk1-13-25]]
- 6 Earth Crust Ore (地殻鉱) [[sk1-13-38]]
- 6 Diamond Stones (金剛石) [[sk1-13-39]]

Yui's event triggers on this floor and gives you a Diamond [[sk1-13-20]].

### Guilty Scythe (Floor 80 boss, Monster Maze)

Guilty Scythe awards 2200 EXP and 790000 Col, and its Last Attack bonus is the Hyper Ring [[wiki4-13-2]].

- **Weakness:** blunt attacks [[sk1-13-30, wiki4-13-2]].
- **Paralysis:** bring the Scale Mantle and Flame of Purification [[wiki4-13-4]].
- **Area attacks:** to avoid them, release your lock-on and dash away. A backstep or shuffle will not get you clear in time [[wiki4-13-4]].
- **Last Attack:** the Last Attack bonus is the Hyper Ring [[wiki4-13-2]].
