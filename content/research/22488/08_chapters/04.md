## Chapter 4: Floor 79: Dragon Palace

[MERMAID]

### Overview and Preparation

Floor 79 is a waterway-type labyrinth [[wiki8-5-1]]. Leafa can fight beside you as a partner from this floor on [[sk1-99-4]].

The route on this floor goes through the Flowing Water Corridor, where activating the device at the far end opens the door beside the entrance and brings out the Huge Poison Crab. Defeating it lets you go on to the Dragon Palace of Rushing Waves labyrinth [[sk1-9-901]]. The Waterfall Stream (束ねし瀑布の湖道) holds the NM Zain Lizardman among many lizardmen [[sk1-9-1]].

This chapter has eight achievements: Zain Lizardman, Huge Poison Crab, Unforgivable Song, Arrival of Chef, The Endless Crab Roe, The Tempest of Trihead, Treasure Palace and "Wow L-Liz!". "Wow L-Liz!" has no source for its trigger; it is listed at the end as unverified.

Quests are accepted from the NPC Kirby in front of the Arc Sophia transfer gate on Floor 76 [[wiki2-5-1]]. Pick up the three Floor 79 quests below before heading out.

Two Floor 79 "checks" are listed in the boss-strategy table. Check 1 is completing the quest Unforgivable Song, and check 2 is defeating the NM Zain Lizardman in Waterfall Stream [[sk1-102-12, sk1-102-13]]. No collected source describes any other check for this floor. Unable to verify from collected sources.

### Floor 79

#### Slope of the Kneeling Sunset

Step out of town. This is the first field, and only Sirens here count for the quest [[xneo1-1-9]]. Defeat 7 of them to complete the quest Unforgivable Song [[sk1-95-11]]. This is also check 1 for the floor [[sk1-102-12]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365733]

#### Water Corridor (たゆたう水の回廊)

Move on to the Water Corridor and open its chest for **Weapon Chest A** [[sk1-10-1]].

The Water Corridor is named as a place where Flesh Crabs or Father Crabs drop the Large Crab Legs (大カニの歩脚) for Arrival of Chef [[sk1-95-12]]. The same crabs drop the Crab Roe (カニミソ) for The Endless Crab Roe [[sk1-95-13]]. Start collecting these drops here.

#### Waterfall Stream (束ねし瀑布の湖道)

Many lizardmen appear here. Among them is the NM Zain Lizardman, listed in the boss-strategy checks [[sk1-9-1]]. It is in the immediate vicinity of the Lake Path of Bundled Cascades [[xneo1-1-10]]. Defeat it for check 2 [[sk1-102-13]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365731]

> [!TIP]
> This is the third area of Floor 79: an open plaza with about 14 lizardmen and 1 NM. Lizard Tongues from them sell for 3000 Col, a good money source [[wiki7-11-3]].

#### Flowing Water Corridor (流れる水の回廊)

Chests here:
- **3 Teleport Crystals** [[sk1-10-2]]
- **Weapon Chest B** [[sk1-10-3]]
- **10 Crimson Flame Stones (紅炎石×10)** [[sk1-10-18]]

Activate the device at the far end. The door beside the entrance opens and the Huge Poison Crab appears [[sk1-9-901]]. Defeat it to be able to go on to the Dragon Palace of Rushing Waves labyrinth [[sk1-9-901]].

#### Dragon Palace of Rushing Waves (奔波の竜宮)

High-level Father Crabs keep 1 HP left even after continuous damage, so be careful when finishing them [[sk1-9-2]].

##### 1F
- Defeat 10 Flesh Crabs within the time limit for the **Silver Chain** [[sk1-10-6]].
- Chests: **Weapon Chest D** [[sk1-10-7]], **Weapon Chest E** [[sk1-10-8]], **Weapon Chest F** [[sk1-10-9]].

##### 2F
- Defeat 3 Father Crabs within the time limit for the **Might Ring** [[sk1-10-10]]. It gives STR +40 and attack +10 [[wiki7-6-13]].
- Chests: **4 SP Grand Potions** [[sk1-10-11]], **Weapon Chest G** [[sk1-10-12]], **10 Sharp Blade Stones (鋭刃石×10)** [[sk1-10-21]].

##### 3F
- Defeat Strong Scissors within the time limit for **Yamato Leggings** [[sk1-10-13]].
- Chests: **4 Grand Potions** [[sk1-10-14]], **Weapon Chest H** [[sk1-10-15]], **10 Earth Crust Stones (地殻石×10)** [[sk1-10-22]], **10 Wind Flower Stones (風花石×10)** [[sk1-10-23]].

Keep defeating Flesh Crabs and Father Crabs along the way. Collect 5 Large Crab Legs for Arrival of Chef [[sk1-95-12]] and 7 Crab Roe for The Endless Crab Roe [[sk1-95-13]]. Where to hand these quests in is not stated in the collected sources. Unable to verify from collected sources.

### Tri-Tempest (Floor 79 Boss, Dragon Palace)

- **Weakness:** Slash [[sk1-10-16]].
- **Breath:** Block its breath attacks with the battle skill Airy Shield [[sk1-10-17]].
- **Status:** It inflicts paralysis and stun. Destroying its right neck seals its all-target attack [[wiki4-13-1]].
- **Low HP:** As its HP drops, it spams a wide-angle, short-range fan attack that carries a paralysis debuff. Flame of Purification and Paralysis Removal Crystals are essential items [[wiki4-13-3]].
- **Last Attack:** The finishing blow earns the Duel Vest [[wiki4-13-1]].

Defeat Tri-Tempest to clear the Dragon Palace.

### After the Boss: Character Episodes

It is unconfirmed whether these tiers open when a floor is reached or when it is cleared. Do each one now after the boss. If one has not triggered, continue and retry after the next boss.

The roadmap lists these episodes for this point: Silica (a growth item for Pina), Lisbeth ("a place with a nice view"), Sinon ("combat training", daytime, alone, in Kirito's room) and Yui ("wants to help", daytime). The collected sources give no further detail on any of them. Unable to verify from collected sources.

If Asuna is your partner, there is a ring event tied to Floor 79:
- Fight the NM Ring Guarder (NMリングガーダー) in Waterfall Stream to find the material for the paired ring. A key-item cup (貴重品杯) also drops [[sk1-91-1]].
- Go back to the Floor 79 church in the daytime to receive the Ritual Ring (colorless) [[sk1-91-2]].

> [!TIP]
> In Arc Sophia date conversations, begin the conversation while seated on a bench or fountain with the X button. This increases how many times you can speak [[wiki7-13-6]].
