## Chapter 6: Floor 81: Halls Strong

[MERMAID]

Floor 81 changes the dungeon format: from here treasure chests can be mimics, and beating a mimic pays out a large amount of Col [[sk1-14-1]]. The labyrinth entrance is visible from Sealed Mirage Sands but is blocked until the Shadow Demons in the four ruins are defeated [[sk1-14-2, xneo1-1-14]]. This chapter covers the Deadly Desert quest, the Reaping Scythe quest, the four trials (Evil Wipeout, Blocking, Maze, Mimicry), the Halls Strong labyrinth and the boss, The Knight of Darkness.

Quests are accepted from NPC Kirby in front of the Arc Sophia transfer gate on Floor 76 [[wiki2-5-1]]. From Floor 81 the partner is free to choose.

> [!NOTE]
> Whether character-episode events open when a floor is reached or when it is cleared is unconfirmed in the collected sources. Do the post-boss events in the final section after beating the Floor 81 boss. If one has not triggered, continue and retry after the next boss.

### Arc Sophia

Before heading out, take care of the quest pickup and the town events listed below.

#### Arc Sophia errands

- Quests (The Deadly Desert, the Reaping Scythe quest) are taken from Kirby at the transfer gate [[wiki2-5-1]].
- Date conversations in Arc Sophia allow more lines if you start talking while seated; press the X button to sit on benches, fountains or any seat [[wiki7-13-6]].
- Using a Recording Crystal alone in Kirito's room triggers a flashback scene of Sachi [[wiki8-9-1]].
- Sachi's message can be used in Kirito's room [[wiki7-13-1]].
- Entering Agil's shop with Leafa in the party triggers the "everyone is a little sister" event [[wiki1-9-10]].
- The Silica Evolution Ore lead is available after the Floor 81 boss; see the final section [[sk1-92-901]].

### Floor 81

#### Desert of Sun-Drenched Heat Waves (quest: The Deadly Desert)

Accept The Deadly Desert from Kirby, then kill 10 Scorpions in the Desert of Sun-Drenched Heat Waves, the area right outside the Floor 81 entrance [[sk1-95-17]]. Only Scorpions in that desert count [[xneo1-1-13]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365756]

This quest is also Check 1 for Floor 81 [[sk1-102-16]].

#### Sealed Mirage Sands

From here you can see the entrance to the Floor 81 labyrinth, but you cannot go in yet. Four ruins branch off in four directions, each with one Shadow Demon [[sk1-14-2]]. Defeating the Shadow Demon at the deepest point of each ruin removes the roadblock [[xneo1-1-14]].

The four ruins are the Evil Wipeout Trial (邪気漂う殲滅の試練), the Blocking Trial (実直なる封鎖の試練), the Maze Trial (果てある迷路の試練) and the Mimicry Trial (嘲笑せし擬態の試練) [[sk1-15-1, sk1-15-3, sk1-15-5, sk1-15-8]]. The Evil Wipeout Trial is in the front-left ruin [[wiki4-18-3]], and NM Blazer Demon is in the Evil Wipeout Trial on the west front [[xneo1-1-15]]. Unable to verify from collected sources: the exact positions of the other three ruins.

Once all four Shadow Demons are defeated, the Halls Strong labyrinth ("至りし強者達の回廊") becomes reachable from Sealed Mirage Sands [[sk1-14-3]].

#### Evil Wipeout Trial

🗺️ No map was collected for this area.

Defeating the Dullahan opens the doors that lead onward [[sk1-14-4]]. Deeper inside, defeat NM Blazer Demon [[sk1-14-5]], which is in the front-left ruin [[wiki4-18-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365755]

Defeating Blazer Demon is Check 2 for Floor 81 [[sk1-102-17]]. Go further in and defeat the Shadow Demon [[sk1-14-6]].

Chests here: Weapon Chest A [[sk1-15-1]], Weapon Chest B [[sk1-15-2]] and 10 Sharp Blade Stone [[sk1-15-21]].

Phantoms in this trial and elsewhere on Floor 81 drop Death's Scythe; hand-in requires collecting 9 of them for the Reaping Scythe quest [[sk1-95-18]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365750]

#### Blocking Trial

Activate the power device to open the door that leads onward [[sk1-14-7]]. Defeat the Shadow Demon in the deepest room [[sk1-14-8]].

Chests: 4 SP Grand Potions [[sk1-15-3]], the Blocking Trial Key [[sk1-15-4]] and 5 Earth Crust Stone [[sk1-15-22]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365754]

#### Maze Trial

Follow the path to the deepest room and defeat the Shadow Demon there [[sk1-14-9]].

Chests: Weapon Chest C [[sk1-15-5]], 300000 Col [[sk1-15-6]], 3 Teleport Crystals [[sk1-15-7]] and 10 Wind Flower Stone [[sk1-15-23]].

#### Mimicry Trial

Apart from the key chest, the other chests are mimics and weapon chests [[xneo1-1-15]]. The key is in the middle chest of the small room at the upper right [[sk1-14-10, xneo1-1-15]]. Take the Mimicry Trial Key [[sk1-15-11]].

Use the key to open the door of the central room, go through, and defeat the Shadow Demon [[sk1-14-11]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365753]

Other chests: Weapon Chests D, E and F [[sk1-15-8, sk1-15-9, sk1-15-10]]. Mimics beaten here pay high Col [[sk1-14-1]].

#### Halls Strong (labyrinth)

Enter after all four Shadow Demons have fallen [[sk1-14-3]].

##### 1F

Chests: Weapon Chest G [[sk1-15-13]], Weapon Chest H [[sk1-15-14]] and 10 Crimson Flame Stone [[sk1-15-24]].

Timed mission: defeat 10 Skeleton Pawns within the time limit to receive the Bone Belt [[sk1-15-12]].

##### 2F

Chests: 3 Grand Potions [[sk1-15-16]] and 3 Recovery Crystals [[sk1-15-17]].

Timed mission: defeat 10 Elder Lizardmen within the time limit to receive the Scale Belt [[sk1-15-15]].

##### 3F

Chests: Weapon Chest I [[sk1-15-18]] and Weapon Chest J [[sk1-15-19]].

> [!TIP]
> **Timed mission (3F):** Defeat 10 Phantoms within the time limit to get the Paralyze Charm (charm slot) [[sk1-15-25]]. Do it while you are on 3F. No achievement depends on it.

With all 21 chests on Floor 81 opened, the sweep is complete.

### The Knight of Darkness (Boss)

Floor 81 boss: Dark Knight. It is weak to blunt (strike) attacks [[sk1-15-20, wiki4-14-1]]. Recommended combat power is 820 Pts, and the Last Attack bonus is the Blood Necklace [[wiki4-14-1]].

Strategy:

- Lightning: if one full set hits a raid member directly, they will almost certainly die. Stop it even after it has been activated [[wiki4-14-2]].
- Infinity Guard (used when nearly dead): every attack becomes "miss", so Bash and Onslaught are ineffective [[wiki4-14-2]].
- Landing the final blow earns the Blood Necklace [[wiki4-14-1]].
