## Chapter 1: Floor 76: Cauldron Dungeon

[MERMAID]

This chapter covers the opening of the game. You fight the tutorial battle against Heathcliff [[wiki1-5-1]], accept the four Floor 76 quests in Arc Sophia [[sk1-95-1]], remove the sealed gem in Greed Hole 1 [[sk1-1-1]], and work through the Cauldron of Death. You then defeat the floor boss Ghastly Gaze [[sk1-1-7]], and finish with the Klein rumour and the Leafa and Sinon events in the Poison Weave Forest [[wiki1-6-901, wiki1-6-17]]. You also sweep every chest on the floor.

Quests for every floor are accepted from NPCs in Arc Sophia [[sk1-95-1]]. The NPC Kirby in front of the Arc Sophia transfer gate gives them out [[wiki2-5-1]]. Asuna can fight alongside you as a partner from Floor 76 [[sk1-98-2]].

> [!TIP]
> **Bond tip:** Start raising every heroine's bond now by talking, walking together, giving gifts and sharing food. The bond achievements are planned for a later chapter, but there is no reason to wait. In Arc Sophia, starting a date conversation while seated (press X on a bench or fountain) increases how many times you can speak [[wiki7-13-6]].

### Arc Sophia

After the opening, a tutorial fight against Heathcliff takes place [[wiki1-5-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365724]

Finishing that fight completes the Tutorial [[wiki1-5-1]].

1. Go to the central plaza and visit Agil's shop, then invite Asuna into your party [[wiki1-6-1]].
2. Move to the front of the transfer gate, where the quest NPC Kirby stands, and accept the Floor 76 quests [[wiki2-5-1]].

### Floor 76

🗺️ No map was collected for this floor.

Leave through the transfer gate. The route runs from the Windy Highway to the Cobweb-Drifting Windy Hills and then to Greed Hole [[sk1-1-1]]. Black spiders are strong. If you are not used to the controls, skip them and head in the direction with no black spider [[wiki1-6-1]].

- Frenzy Boars for Protect the Farm Crop are in the Windy Hills [[sk1-95-1]].
- Death Hornets for Poisonous Needles are found outside town [[sk1-95-3]].

#### Greed Hole 1 and 2

1. Enter Greed Hole 1 and ignore the magic circle on the right for now. Go straight on to Greed Hole 2 [[xneo1-1-1]].
2. Keep heading south in Greed Hole 2. Take the branch heading east. The chest at the end holds **180000 Col** and a weapon (Weapon Chest A) [[wiki1-6-2, sk1-2-3, sk1-2-4]].
3. The mid-boss is Bishop Lizardman with 2 Paladin Lizardmen and 2 Elder Lizardmen [[wiki1-6-3]]. Prepare before you go in.
4. Kill the spear lizardmen first, because their area skill cannot be parried [[wiki4-10-2]].
5. Winning releases the barrier object in Greed Hole 1 and opens the way to Losers' Canyon [[sk1-1-1, wiki1-6-3]].
6. Return to the earlier area and examine the device next to the magic circle to proceed [[xneo1-1-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365708]

The Greed Hole 1 sealed gem achievement comes from releasing that object by beating the Bishop Lizardman [[sk1-1-1, wiki1-6-3, xneo1-1-1]].

Open the Greed Hole 1 chests now that the barrier is down:

- Northwest, just past the barrier: **Wind Flower Stone x10** [[wiki1-6-4, sk1-2-5]].
- West: **Earth Crust Ore x5** and **Ash-White Ore x5** [[wiki1-6-5, sk1-2-6, sk1-2-7]].
- Southwest: **Diamond Stone** (x10 per the item list) [[wiki1-6-6, sk1-2-8]].

#### Losers' Canyon

1. Enter Losers' Canyon. An event shows the PCs being attacked by spiders led by a Giant Spider [[wiki1-6-7]].
2. Open the chest for **Grand Potion x2** [[wiki1-6-7, sk1-2-9]].
3. Gather the Valley Blooming Herbs for The Gift for the Girl. There are 3 spots: near where the Giant Spider was, near the second turn of the cliff climb, and near the rock on the left just before the exit [[xneo1-1-2, wiki2-6-18, wiki2-6-19]].
4. A Valley Herb and a Hard Nut can also be picked up here [[sk1-2-1, sk1-2-2]].
5. Defeat Mad Tarantulas along the way for The Poisonous Spider [[sk1-95-4]].

A spider dojo sits in the lowland just past the cave, with 5 small spiders and 1 medium spider. It is useful for weapon proficiency up to 500, and you can order partners to fall back so they do not kill the spiders too fast [[wiki7-10-5]].

#### Predator's Pilgrimage Road

1. Moving here starts an event, then a fight with the large spider Crimson Spinner [[wiki1-6-8, sk1-1-2]].
2. It has 2 HP bars, so expect a long fight [[wiki4-10-4]].
3. Defeating it is Check 2 for the boss information [[sk1-102-7]].
4. It drops 150,000 Col, 6,200 EXP and Insect Thread x5 [[wiki4-10-4, wiki7-9-4]].
5. Changing area without defeating it restores its HP. Warping to the Corridor of Battlefield with Asuna's Teleport Crystal after the kill respawns it immediately [[wiki7-11-1]].

#### Corridor of Battlefield

1. Small slimes can be killed in one hit with Step-In alone [[sk1-1-3]].
2. At the first fork, go north to a dead end with chests. The wiki lists a curved sword, a one-handed sword, a spear, a mace, **Diamond Crystal x3** and **Healing Crystal x4** [[wiki1-6-9, sk1-2-14, sk1-2-15]].
3. The four weapon chests here are B, C, D and E [[sk1-2-10, sk1-2-11, sk1-2-12, sk1-2-13]].
4. The big blue slime Blue Impact traps you in a fight if you approach it. Stay away from it if you plan to turn back [[wiki1-6-10]].

#### Cauldron of Death

This is the Floor 76 labyrinth. Only 1F has a branch, and going right to 2F connects to the 3F route [[sk1-1-4]].

##### 1F

Chests here hold **Running Water Stone x4**, **Sharp Edge Stone x10**, **Grand Potion x3** and **SP Hi-Potion x4** [[wiki1-6-11, sk1-2-16, sk1-2-17, sk1-2-18, sk1-2-19]].

##### 2F

- East side: **Wind Flower Stone x10** and **Ash-White Stone x10** [[wiki1-6-12, sk1-2-22, sk1-2-23]].
- West side: a two-handed axe (Weapon Chest F) and **Teleport Crystal x3** [[wiki1-6-13, sk1-2-20, sk1-2-21]].

##### 3F

1. An interrupt mission (15:00 limit) tells you to defeat the Lizardman Monarch. You can ignore it and take the south staircase to 4F [[wiki1-6-14]].
2. Beating it within the time limit gives **Journey Boots** [[wiki1-6-14, sk1-2-24]].
3. Chests on 3F: **HIT Potion x3**, **Grand Potion x2**, **Crimson Flame Stone x10** and Weapon Chests G and H [[sk1-2-25, sk1-2-26, sk1-2-27, sk1-2-28, sk1-2-29]].

##### 4F

4F is only the passage to the boss room. At the boss room door an event asks whether to return to town, and you are sent back to prepare [[sk1-1-5, wiki1-6-15]]. The wiki places Grand Potion x2, HIT Potion x3, a katana, Crimson Flame Stone x10 and a one-handed sword on this floor [[wiki1-6-15]]. The item list places the potions and stone on 3F [[sk1-2-25, sk1-2-26, sk1-2-28]]. Check both floors so none is missed.

After you discover the boss room, Lisbeth at the Lisbeth Weapon Shop in Arc Sophia gives you Crimson Flame Stone x1, Ore x1 and Crystal x1 [[sk1-2-30]].

### Ghastly Gaze

> [!TIP]
> **Before you enter:** The boss fight opens only when the guild information in the GUILDS menu is complete [[wiki1-6-16]]. Check 1 is the Gift for the Girl quest and Check 2 is defeating Crimson Spinner [[sk1-102-6, sk1-102-7]]. Unable to verify from collected sources: the exact way to complete Check 3.

1. Back in town, talk to the PC in front of the gate to start the fight against Ghastly Gaze [[wiki1-6-16]].
2. The recommended combat power is 745 Pts [[wiki4-10-5]].
3. It is weak to thrust attacks [[sk1-1-7, wiki4-10-6]].
4. Evil-eye ailments such as stun and paralysis are the main danger. After taking some damage it also uses an all-direction attack [[sk1-1-8]].
5. Its tentacles and eyes can be destroyed, which seals the matching powerful area attacks for a while [[wiki4-10-6]].
6. Kirito gets the Last Attack Bonus, a **Rage Ring**, by landing the finishing blow [[sk1-1-9, wiki4-10-5]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365715]

Defeating Ghastly Gaze completes the Cauldron Dungeon [[sk1-1-7, wiki1-6-16]].

### After the boss

1. Leaving the inn room after the boss triggers the Silica event, per the route plan.
2. At the transfer gate, an event has Klein tell you a rumour about a fairy event in the east forest [[wiki1-6-901]].
3. Go from the Cobweb-Drifting Windy Hills into the Poison Weave Forest. Walk straight to the blue "!" to start the Leafa event, which leads directly into the Sinon event [[wiki1-6-17]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365719]

[ACHIEVEMENT:365863]

These two achievements, Lonely Fairy and Hey! Where Are You Looking!, come from this stretch of the forest [[wiki1-6-17, wiki1-6-901]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Danger Warning:** Ragneck, the giant scorpion in this forest, is very strong. Raise levels on upper floors first or your partner will die immediately [[sk1-1-6]]. Defeating it within the time limit drops Cross Boots [[sk1-2-31, wiki4-10-3]].

Poison Weave Forest chests: **Crimson Flame Ore x4**, **Sharp Edge Ore x5**, **Recovery Crystal x3**, **Diamond Ore x5**, **Running Water Ore x5**, **150000 Col**, and Weapon Chests I, J and 3 [[sk1-2-32, sk1-2-33, sk1-2-34, sk1-2-35, sk1-2-36, sk1-2-37, sk1-2-38, sk1-2-39, sk1-2-40]].

Once every chest listed above is open, including the Cauldron of Death, Greed Hole and forest chests, Treasure Valley unlocks [[sk1-2-3, sk1-2-5, sk1-2-9, sk1-2-14, sk1-2-20, sk1-2-25, sk1-2-40]].
