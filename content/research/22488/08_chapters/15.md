## Chapter 15: Floor 90: Tower Monster

[MERMAID]

Floor 90 covers three quests, one named monster (NM) fight against Impregnable, a chest sweep of the Sealed Tower of the Dim Avenger, and the floor boss, Blade Ruler. Honey is gathered on the Hill of Sky-Facing Vegetation [[sk1-47-1, sk1-95-42]]. The quest items are the Harpy Eggs from Floating Continent 1 and the Siren Wings from Floating Continent 2 [[sk1-95-43, sk1-95-44]]. NM Impregnable waits at the transfer destination of Floating Continent 3 [[sk1-46-1]]. Blade Ruler's effective attribute is blunt (打) [[sk1-47-24]].

Quests are accepted from the NPC Kirby in front of the Arc Sophia transfer gate on floor 76 [[wiki2-5-1]]. Pick up all three Floor 90 quests there before you head out:

- **Project Honey** (#42): gather 5 Honey on the Hill of Sky-Facing Vegetation on Floor 90 [[sk1-95-42]].
- **The Love Kitchen** (#43): collect 6 Harpy Eggs, dropped by the Harpies in Floating Continent 1 (after the transfer) [[sk1-95-43]].
- **Love and Ingredients** (#44): collect 4 Siren Wings, dropped by the Sirens in Floating Continent 2 (after the transfer) [[sk1-95-44]].

Where each finished quest is handed in: Unable to verify from collected sources.

### Arc Sophia

🗺️ No map links were collected for this chapter.

Go to the Arc Sophia transfer gate and take the three quests from Kirby [[wiki2-5-1, sk1-95-42, sk1-95-43, sk1-95-44]]. The Floor 90 town is Koyorunosu, and the Hill of Sky-Facing Vegetation lies beyond it [[wiki1-10-3]].

The following optional room events are listed for Floor 90:

- At night in your own room, the event where everyone sleeps side by side (kawa no ji) occurs. It is not in Kirito's memo, and it occurs whether you are alone or with someone [[wiki1-10-7]].
- Going to your room in the daytime with Lisbeth triggers "Item Storage Organizing" [[wiki1-10-8]].
- Coming to your room at night with Silica triggers "Kirito-san, hide!" [[wiki1-10-9]].
- Going to your room at night with Sinon triggers "Aromatherapy". It does not occur in the daytime [[wiki1-10-10]].
- Coming to your room at night with Leafa triggers "A pervert in Suguha's room!?" [[wiki1-10-11]].
- Taking Strea to your room at night triggers "Underwear Fashion Show" [[wiki1-10-12]].
- After the boss room has been discovered, return to town and head to your room at night for "Blackout and Equipment Display Glitch". It works alone or with someone [[wiki1-10-13]].
- If Kirito is alone on Floor 90 and enters the shopping street, the "Black Swordsman, party of one, welcome to the garden of women" event occurs [[wiki1-12-8]].

Sachi's message can be used in Kirito's room [[wiki7-13-1]]. Using a Recording Crystal while alone in Kirito's room triggers a flashback scene of Sachi [[wiki8-9-1]]. In Arc Sophia date conversations, starting the talk while seated with your partner increases how many times you can speak. Press the X button to sit on benches, fountains and similar places [[wiki7-13-6]].

> [!TIP]
> Strea's bond achievement is not part of this chapter. A community comment says she can be talked to at Agil's inn after the Floor 82 boss, and that she agrees to accompany you once you have talked to her again and again [[ra-comments-57-4, ra-comments-57-1]].

#### Hill of Sky-Facing Vegetation (Honey)

Leave Koyorunosu for the hill. Honey can be picked up on the hill, near the tree [[sk1-47-1, wiki1-10-3]]. Project Honey needs 5 of them [[sk1-95-42]].

If you cannot gather 5 in a single day, go back, rest at the inn to pass the day, and gather again [[xneo1-2-8]].

Completing Project Honey is also Floor 90 Check 1 [[sk1-102-2]].
