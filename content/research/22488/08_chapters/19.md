## Chapter 19: Floor 94: Dark Knight

[MERMAID]

Floor 94 has an east and a west treasure vault. Each vault hides a named monster at its deepest point, and defeating one releases one object of the labyrinth "Dark Knight's Bedchamber" [[sk1-64-1, sk1-64-2]]. The labyrinth's first floor also has a power device at the back that releases all objects when started, and the boss Blazing Knight waits at the end [[sk1-64-3, sk1-64-4]]. This chapter covers the three quests available on this floor (The Ant Army!, Jam SOS and the high-difficulty Perished Lives), the Strea bond deadline, and every chest on the floor for Treasure Dark [[sk1-95-49, sk1-95-50, sk1-95-51, ra-comments-57-2]].

### Preparation and Warnings

> [!CAUTION]
> **Point of No Return:** Finishing Floor 94 is the point of no return for the Strea bond achievement, so the bond must be raised beforehand [[ra-comments-57-2]]. Do not defeat the Floor 94 boss until Strea's bond is at maximum.

Strea can be talked to at Agil's inn in the city once the Floor 82 boss has been killed [[ra-comments-57-4]]. Raising the bond is tedious at first. The best method reported is to talk to her again and again until she agrees to accompany you when asked [[ra-comments-57-1]]. The sources do not say how many bond points the maximum needs. Unable to verify from collected sources.

Quests are accepted from the NPC Kirby in front of the Arc Sophia transfer gate on Floor 76 [[wiki2-5-1]]. Accept all three Floor 94 quests before heading out: The Ant Army!, Jam SOS and Perished Lives [[sk1-95-49, sk1-95-50, sk1-95-51]].

### Arc Sophia

Before you go, in town:

- The Ant Army! requires defeating 8 Murder Ant Lions found in the Majestic Waterfall Valley on Floor 94 and similar places [[sk1-95-49]].
- Jam SOS requires collecting 7 Rich Fruit from Ant Executioners' Forest East and West [[sk1-95-50]].
- Perished Lives is a high-difficulty quest: you defeat the Cellurion in the Majestic Waterfall Valley on Floor 94, and it appears only after you accept the quest [[sk1-95-51]].
- Optional town activity: Sachi's message can be used in Kirito's room [[wiki7-13-1]]. Using a Recording Crystal alone in Kirito's room triggers a flashback scene of Sachi [[wiki8-9-1]].
- Date conversations in Arc Sophia allow more lines if you begin talking while seated, using the X button on benches or fountains [[wiki7-13-6]].

### Perished Lives (Quest)

Accept the quest, then defeat the Cellurion that appears in the Majestic Waterfall Valley [[sk1-95-51]]. This is a high-difficulty fight, so prepare first.

Community advice for the fight:

- If it goes for the poison area skill, unlock your target and go around it so it cannot hit you [[ra-comments-134-1]].
- When it does the big circle skill, keep a little distance. You can cast your biggest damaging skill during that animation [[ra-comments-134-2, ra-comments-134-3]].
- If you cannot get away in time, use the battle skill "Exact Onslaught" to interrupt its skills [[ra-comments-134-4]].
- Otherwise block its normal attack and rely on luck [[ra-comments-134-5]].

Other reported methods:

- Defeat the Hellion in front of the bridge first, then pull the Cellurion to the bridge. Push it back periodically with Exact Onslaught from near its jaw [[wiki7-12-5]].
- Pulling it to the bridge to the labyrinth lets you trap it on the narrow path and kill it without damage [[wiki2-7-10]].
- Dual Blades "The Eclipse": use it, run away, and hit again, repeatedly [[wiki2-7-9]].
- During a straight-line attack, slide, shift a little further, stick to its side and use a sword skill [[wiki2-7-12]].
- During an all-direction attack, run to the edge of the range, then use a sword skill [[wiki2-7-13]].
- Block the destruction beam with Exact Guard [[wiki2-7-14]].
- If possible, parry normal attacks to raise the risk [[wiki2-7-15]].

The last several notes come from a comment about this floor's strong boss-type enemies, and whether each applies to this exact Cellurion is unconfirmed [[wiki2-7-8]].

### Dark Knight Labyrinth

On the first floor, start the power device at the back to release all objects [[sk1-64-3]]. The labyrinth's own entrance route is not described in the sources. Unable to verify from collected sources.

#### 1F

- Chests contain Flowing Water Stone x10 [[sk1-65-38]] and Earth Crust Stone x10 [[sk1-65-39]].
- Timed mission: defeat 15 Servant Knights within the time limit to obtain Shade Gloves [[sk1-65-22]].
- Start the power device at the back [[sk1-64-3]].

#### 2F

- Weapon Chest K [[sk1-65-24]]
- Grand Potion x4 [[sk1-65-25]]
- Timed mission: defeat Knight of Knights within the time limit to obtain Black Leather [[sk1-65-23]].

#### 3F

- Recovery Crystal x6 [[sk1-65-27]]
- Weapon Chest L [[sk1-65-28]]
- Weapon Chest M [[sk1-65-29]]
- Earth Crust Ore x10 [[sk1-65-40]]
- Timed mission: defeat 9 Knight of Night within the time limit to obtain Shade Boots [[sk1-65-26]].

With every chest on the floor opened (east forest, east vault, west forest, west vault and all three labyrinth floors), Treasure Dark unlocks [[sk1-65-1, sk1-65-4, sk1-65-5, sk1-65-12, sk1-65-17, sk1-65-24, sk1-65-27]].

### Blazing Knight (Boss)

Its effective attribute is blunt (strike) [[sk1-64-5]].

- When its HP drops it powers itself up, raising attack power and shortening its attack interval. Prevent this with "Phantom Break" or "Skill Ban" [[sk1-64-4]].
- Other details of its attacks are not given in the sources. Unable to verify from collected sources.
