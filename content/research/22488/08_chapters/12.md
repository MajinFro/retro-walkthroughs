## Chapter 12: Floor 87: Stronghold

[MERMAID]

Floor 87 is a long chapter on one floor. From the Shattered Cavern Plain the road branches three ways: the right and left branches hold only treasure chests, while the middle branch is the route to the labyrinth [[sk1-35-1]]. The main route runs through the three layers of the Chained Stratified Cavern, then the Ancient Underground Fortress, and finally the labyrinth "Devouring Stronghold" and its boss, the Radiance Eater [[sk1-35-2, sk1-35-3, sk1-35-4, sk1-35-6, wiki1-10-2]].

This chapter covers five achievements:
- **The Monster's Fangs** and **The Underground Crystal** (two Floor 87 quests) [[sk1-95-35, sk1-95-36]].
- **Treasure Stronghold** (every chest on Floor 87) [[sk1-36-2]].
- **The Radiance Eater** (clear the Stronghold) [[sk1-36-1]].
- **Kirito, O Rei Delas!**, which unlocks after the floor is cleared [[sk1-91-901]].

Quests are accepted from the NPC Kirby in front of the transfer gate in Arc Sophia on Floor 76 [[wiki2-5-1]]. Floor 87 has two boss-check conditions. Check 1 is the quest The Monster's Fangs. Check 2 is defeating NM Nightstalker in the Ancient Underground Fortress [[sk1-102-27, sk1-102-28]]. Complete both before you try the boss.

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Floor 87:** Open every chest listed in the table below, including the side branches and the wrong holes, and finish both quests [[sk1-36-2, sk1-36-13, sk1-36-16, sk1-95-35, sk1-95-36]].

### Arc Sophia

Before heading out, go to Kirby at the transfer gate and accept both Floor 87 quests [[wiki2-5-1]].

- **The Underground Crystal:** Collect 5 Crystal Pillars from the Chained Stratified Cavern Lower Layer [[sk1-95-35]].
- **The Monster's Fangs:** Collect 2 Orc Fangs, dropped by the Orc Generals in the Devoured Traces Cavern [[sk1-95-36]].

### Shattered Cavern Plain branches

🗺️ No map is listed for this area. At the three-way split, the **right** branch leads to Rustling Leaf Forest (chests only), the **left** branch to Devoured Traces Cavern (chests only), and the **middle** branch is the route to the labyrinth [[sk1-35-1]]. The exact layout inside each side area is not given: Unable to verify from collected sources. Sweep each one until every chest in the table is open.

#### Right branch: Rustling Leaf Forest

1. Take the right fork. Open the three weapon chests **A**, **B** and **C** [[sk1-36-2, sk1-36-3, sk1-36-4]].
2. Open the chest holding **Crimson Flame Ore x8** [[sk1-36-5]].
3. Optional mission: defeating Black Hornet within the time limit gives the body armor **Mine Cross** [[sk1-36-6]].

#### Left branch: Devoured Traces Cavern

1. Take the left fork and open the chests for **Sharp Blade Ore x8**, **Ashen White Ore x8**, **Gran Potion x5**, **Full Recovery Crystal x3**, **Weapon chest D** and **Earth Crust Ore x8** [[sk1-36-7, sk1-36-8, sk1-36-9, sk1-36-10, sk1-36-11, sk1-36-12]].
2. Orc Generals are in this cavern. Defeat them until you have 2 Orc Fangs for The Monster's Fangs [[sk1-95-36]].
3. Return to Kirby in Arc Sophia and hand in the quest.

### Chained Stratified Cavern

The route to this cavern runs through the middle branch of the plain [[sk1-35-1]].

#### Upper Layer

In the deepest room, the hole on the **left** side leads to the correct Middle Layer. The hole on the **right** leads to the wrong Middle Layer [[sk1-35-2]].

#### Middle Layer

- The hole you came down by the Upper Layer's correct route has **Weapon chest E** [[sk1-36-14]].
- The wrong hole from the Upper Layer has a chest with **Full Recovery Crystal x3** [[sk1-36-13]].
- On the map, the **center** hole leads to the correct Lower Layer. The **upper-right** and **lower-right** holes both lead to the same wrong Lower Layer section [[sk1-35-3]].

#### Lower Layer

- Pick up **Crystal Pillars** here for the quest; it needs 5 [[sk1-36-15, sk1-95-35]].
- The area reached through the wrong hole from the Middle Layer has **Weapon chest F** [[sk1-36-16]].
- In the deepest room, defeat Ant Lion Destroy to open the way to the Ancient Underground Fortress [[sk1-35-4]].

Once you have 5 Crystal Pillars, return to Kirby.

### Ancient Underground Fortress

Here you fight **NM Nightstalker**, an aura-wrapped scorpion [[sk1-35-5, wiki1-10-1]]. It is in the upper-left of the fortress after you exit the Lower Layer [[xneo1-2-1]]. Defeating it fulfils Check 2 for this floor [[sk1-102-28]].

Open the chests here for **Gran Potion x3** and **Wind Flower Ore x6** [[sk1-36-17, sk1-36-18]].

Then use the transfer device at the center to go on to the Floor 87 labyrinth, "Devouring Stronghold" [[sk1-35-6]].

### Stronghold (Labyrinth)

On floors other than 1F, crossing in front of the target monster makes a labyrinth mission fail, so the battle skill Invisible makes the missions easier [[xneo1-2-2]].

#### 1F

- Open the chests: **Flowing Water Ore x8**, **Secret Art Crystal x4**, **Teleport Crystal x3** and **Weapon chest G** [[sk1-36-20, sk1-36-21, sk1-36-22, sk1-36-23]].
- Mission: defeat 10 Ant Workers within the time limit without being found by Ant Lion to get the charm **Soul Ank** [[sk1-36-19]].

#### 2F

- Open the chests: **Weapon chest H**, **Diamond Ore x8**, **Weapon chest I** and **Recovery Crystal x3** [[sk1-36-25, sk1-36-26, sk1-36-27, sk1-36-28]].
- Mission: defeat 10 Ant Workers within the time limit without being found by Ant Lion for **Regal Gloves** [[sk1-36-24]].

#### 3F

- Open the chests: **Recovery Crystal x3**, **Earth Crust Stone x10**, **Weapon chest J** and **Wind Flower Stone x10** [[sk1-36-30, sk1-36-31, sk1-36-32, sk1-36-33]].
- Mission: defeat 10 Ant Workers within the time limit without being found by Ant Lion Destroy for **Regal Boots** [[sk1-36-29]].

With every chest from the table opened, the floor sweep is done [[sk1-36-2, sk1-36-33]].

### Boss: Radiance Eater

The boss of Floor 87 is the Radiance Eater [[wiki1-10-2]]. Its effective element is **blunt (打)** [[sk1-36-34]].

- **Wall trick:** if a large group presses it, it backs away. Push it against a wall and have everyone attack; this breaks one of its parts, and it then makes only normal attacks [[sk1-36-1]].
- **Late fight:** when its HP drops, it uses a beam attack with Bleed and Paralysis effects (rather narrow range). It fires the beam repeatedly while you are paralyzed, so use defense-lowering skills and finish it quickly [[sk1-36-1]].

Beating the boss clears the Stronghold.

After clearing Floor 87, go to Arc Sophia and talk to the depressed Asuna. Then, in the daytime, take Asuna to the Floor 83 town and choose the scenic spot to trigger the honeymoon event [[sk1-91-901]].
