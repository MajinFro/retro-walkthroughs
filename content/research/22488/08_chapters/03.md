## Chapter 3: Floor 78: Demons' Cave

[MERMAID]

Floor 78 gives you five achievements besides the boss: the Flying Dragon quest, the Wood Materials quest, the Focusing on a Single Blow quest, the Flare Dragon fight, and a full chest sweep. Silica can fight alongside you as a partner from this floor on [[sk1-98-4]]. Just outside town the road forks. The right branch, Fragrant Leaf Maze Path, is the route to the labyrinth, and the left branch, Cave Harboring Evil, has many weapon chests [[sk1-5-1]]. Defeating the Flare Dragon in Forest Inhabited by Dragons is what lets you go on to the Demons' Cave labyrinth [[sk1-5-3]].

### Madness Horn (Floor 78 boss, Demons' Cave)

Unable to verify from collected sources: how the boss fight is started on this floor. On Floors 76 and 77, the floor boss fight started when you talked to the PC in front of the town gate [[wiki1-6-16, wiki1-7-9]], so try that first. The floor's Check items are the Flying Dragon quest and the Flare Dragon kill, both covered above [[sk1-102-10, sk1-102-11]].

**Boss facts:**

- Madness Horn is weak to slash attacks. Recommended combat power is 775 Pts, and the Last Attack bonus is a Luna Talisman [[sk1-5-4, wiki4-12-1]].
- Its axe skills can be avoided with a parry, and destroying its arm seals the axe skills [[wiki4-12-1]].
- It uses buff skills, so use debuff skills to lower its stats [[sk1-5-5]].
- Its strikes have long motions, so parrying and dodge skills work well, and parrying makes it easy to cause Risk Break [[sk1-5-5]].
- The axe hits a wide area, so watch the HP of your other party members as well [[sk1-5-5]].
- Early on it is not troublesome, but when its HP gets low it unleashes a surge of combos, so the late phase decides the fight [[sk1-5-5]].
- Its attack motions are slow, and Parry, Bash and Throat plus healing crystals make the fight easy. The Lumberjack skill has a fairly high chance of instant death from full HP [[wiki4-12-4]].
- Kirito gets the Last Attack Bonus by landing the finishing blow [[sk1-5-6]].
