## Chapter 16: Floor 91: Groto Enlighten

[MERMAID]

Floor 91 is the "Groto Enlighten" floor. Its route runs through a warp-teleporter labyrinth, then a wind corridor where stones are set, and finally the floor's dungeon, where the boss waits [[sk1-51-1, xneo1-2-9]]. The chapter has three achievements: the Fur Collection quest, the boss, and the floor's chest sweep [[sk1-95-45, sk1-52-10, sk1-52-1]].

Quests are accepted from the NPC Kirby in front of the Arc Sophia transfer gate on floor 76 [[wiki2-5-1]].

> [!TIP]
> Unable to verify from collected sources: which other quests must be accepted before leaving town on this floor. Only the Fur Collection is required for this chapter's achievements.

### Arc Sophia

Prepare in town before you head out.

1. Accept the quest **The Fur Collection** from Kirby in front of the transfer gate [[wiki2-5-1, sk1-95-45]]. It asks you to collect 4 "Beast Pelt" (獣の毛皮) drops from the Griffons in the Floor 91 dungeon, Comet-Eye Strange Cavern [[sk1-95-45]].
2. The Floor 91 boss check list includes this quest as "Check 1" [[sk1-102-3]].
3. Stock up on purification items. The boss inflicts stun, paralysis, bleeding and SP slip, so bring the battle skill "Light of Purification" or Purification Crystals [[sk1-52-11]].
4. Optional town extras:
   - Strea can be talked to at Agil's inn after the Floor 82 boss. Repeated conversations raise the bond until she agrees to join you [[ra-comments-57-4, ra-comments-57-1]].
   - To invite Asuna into the party, go to Agil's shop in the central plaza [[wiki1-6-1]].
   - In Arc Sophia date conversations, starting the talk while seated on a bench or fountain with the X button increases how many times you can speak [[wiki7-13-6]].
   - Sachi's message can be used in Kirito's room [[wiki7-13-1]]. Using a Recording Crystal alone in Kirito's room triggers a Sachi flashback [[wiki8-9-1]].

### Floor 91

#### Bubble Void Labyrinth (泡沫たる空虚の迷道)

This area is a warp-route maze. The directions below are given "as you face" the teleporters: front, right and left [[sk1-51-1]].

- **Correct route:** front → right → left → left → right [[sk1-51-1]].
- The wiki describes the way forward as a left, right, left, right choice: left at the entrance, right at the second, left at the third and right at the fourth [[wiki1-10-4]]. Its table also marks several other choices as leading back to the entrance, some with 4 enemies [[wiki1-10-4]].
- **Chest room 1:** front → front. It holds a chest with **Gray-White Ore ×10** [[sk1-51-2, sk1-52-1]].
- **Chest room 2:** front → right → left → front. It holds a weapon chest and a mimic. Open the chest for **Weapon Chest A** [[sk1-51-3, sk1-52-2]].
- The second guide lists the same routes in left-based terms: Left→Left gives Gray-White Ore ×10, and Left→Right→Left→Right gives a weapon and the mimic. Left→Right→Left→Left→Right meets the woman who gives you the stone to insert into the next area [[xneo1-2-9]].
- **Check 2:** the floor's second check requires defeating NM Anemoi Queen in this labyrinth [[sk1-102-34]].

#### Wind Corridor of Gathering Magic Seals (魔印の集積する風廊)

Follow the path onward to this corridor and insert the stones as you go [[xneo1-2-9]]. Chests here:

- **Weapon Chest B** [[sk1-52-3]]
- **Flowing Water Ore ×10** [[sk1-52-4]]

Unable to verify from collected sources: how you travel from the corridor to Comet-Eye Strange Cavern.

#### Comet-Eye Strange Cavern (彗眼の奇窟)

##### 1F

- Chest: **800,000 Col** [[sk1-52-5]].
- Chest: **Teleport Crystal ×3** [[sk1-52-6]].
- Timed challenge: defeat 5 Black Knights within the time limit to receive the back item **Avenger's Cloak** (復讐者のマント) [[sk1-52-12]].

##### 2F

- Chest: **Crimson Flame Ore ×10** [[sk1-52-7]].
- Timed challenge: defeat the Black Paladin within the time limit to receive the shield **Finster Glanz** [[sk1-52-13]].

##### 3F

- Chest: **Grand Potion ×6** [[sk1-52-8]].
- Chest: **Full Recovery Crystal ×4** [[sk1-52-9]].
- Timed challenge: defeat 5 Griffons within the time limit to receive the leg armor **Purple Gold Greaves** (紫金具足) [[sk1-52-14]].
- The Fur Collection quest is completed by collecting 4 Beast Pelts, the drop item of the Griffons in Comet-Eye Strange Cavern [[sk1-95-45]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:365826]

Treasure Enlighten asks for every chest on the floor. The sources list these chests: 2 in the labyrinth, 2 in the wind corridor, 2 on 1F, 1 on 2F and 2 on 3F [[sk1-52-1, sk1-52-2, sk1-52-3, sk1-52-4, sk1-52-5, sk1-52-6, sk1-52-7, sk1-52-8, sk1-52-9]]. The mimic room in the labyrinth is reached by the second chest-room route [[sk1-51-3]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Do not mix up the chest-room routes with the main route. Chest room 1 is front→front, chest room 2 is front→right→left→front, and the main route is front→right→left→left→right [[sk1-51-2, sk1-51-3, sk1-51-1]].

### Boss: Absolute Gazer (Floor 91, Groto Enlighten)

The boss's effective attribute is thrust [[sk1-52-10]]. The floor's checks are the Fur Collection (Check 1) and the Anemoi Queen (Check 2) [[sk1-102-3, sk1-102-34]]. Unable to verify from collected sources: the boss's HP and a recommended level.

- Its status attacks include stun, paralysis, bleeding and SP slip, so bring Light of Purification or Purification Crystals [[sk1-52-11]].
- Landing the killing blow with Kirito gives the Last Attack bonus, **Lambent Light** [[wiki8-16-3]].
