**Super Mario All-Stars** is a compilation of platform games for the [SNES](/wiki/SNES "SNES"), released by [Nintendo](/wiki/Nintendo "Nintendo") in [1993](/wiki/1993 "1993"). It features graphically enhanced remakes of four [Super Mario](/wiki/Category:Mario "Category:Mario") games, modified from their original [NES](/wiki/NES "NES") versions to suit the more powerful SNES, and it had some glitches for each game fixed in it. 

Represented in this compilation are: 

  * [Super Mario Bros.](/wiki/Super_Mario_Bros. "Super Mario Bros.")
  * [Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels](/wiki/Super_Mario_Bros._2_\(Japan\) "Super Mario Bros. 2 \(Japan\)") (_Super Mario Bros. 2_ in Japan)
  * [Super Mario Bros. 2](/wiki/Super_Mario_Bros._2 "Super Mario Bros. 2") (_Super Mario USA_ in Japan)
  * [Super Mario Bros. 3](/wiki/Super_Mario_Bros._3 "Super Mario Bros. 3")
  * [Mario Bros.](/wiki/Mario_Bros. "Mario Bros.") (included as _"Battle Game"_ mode within _Super Mario Bros. 3_)



  * [](/wiki/File:Super_Mario_All-Stars_SFC_box.jpg "Japanese box")

Japanese box




It was re-released in 1994 as part of [Super Mario All-Stars + Super Mario World](/wiki/Super_Mario_All-Stars_%2B_Super_Mario_World "Super Mario All-Stars + Super Mario World"), a compilation that featured All-Stars and [Super Mario World](/wiki/Super_Mario_World "Super Mario World"), with the only significant changes to SMW being a set of unique sprites for Luigi, making him taller and slimmer than Mario, and the number of save files was changed from three to four. 

**Super Mario All-Stars 25th Anniversary Edition** is a compilation having the same contents as Super Mario All-Stars developed by [Nintendo](/wiki/Nintendo "Nintendo") for the [Wii](/wiki/Wii "Wii"). It was released in Japan on October 21, 2010, in Australia on December 2, 2010, in Europe on December 3, 2010, and in North America on December 12, 2010. The game is part of the celebration of the 25th anniversary of Super Mario Bros. and includes an emulated ROM of the original Super Mario All-Stars. The game is bundled with a 32-page book of Mario's history, and a 25.24-minute soundtrack audio CD of music through the whole series.
