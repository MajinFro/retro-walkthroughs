## Chapter 1: Setup, Options and Controls

[MERMAID]

This chapter has no achievements. It covers the setup that every later chapter depends on: the right ROM, safe initials, a single account, the Options screen, the controls, team choice and password handling. Getting these right now prevents achievements from silently failing later [[ra-comments-1-1, ra-comments-1-2, ra-comments-1-3, ra-comments-1-5]].

### Game Setup and Options

#### Before you start

- Use the US ROM and play in Hardcore Mode. The achievements require the US ROM [[ra-comments-1-2]].
- Create only one account. If you make more than one, most achievements will not unlock [[ra-comments-1-3]].
- Never start your initials with DA. A name starting with DA stops the main achievements from unlocking [[ra-comments-1-1]].
- The game does not save your progress like Jam TE. Your record is lost when you exit the game unless you keep track of the password, so the password is the only way to resume [[ra-comments-1-5]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Do not enter initials beginning with DA, and do not create a second account. Either mistake can stop the main achievements from unlocking [[ra-comments-1-1, ra-comments-1-3]].

#### Initials and the record

1. Start the game and enter your own initials when prompted. Pick any initials that do not start with DA [[ra-comments-1-1]].
2. Create this one record only and keep using it for every game [[ra-comments-1-3]].

#### Options screen

Set these before your first game:

- **Difficulty:** V.Hard. This is the difficulty the later chapters work through.
- **Tag Mode:** Off. The "Not Selfish at All" achievement only triggers with Tag Mode off [[ra-comments-21-1]].
- **Clock speed:** Set the game clock to very fast so games finish quicker [[ra-comments-1-11]].
- **Computer assistance:** The Options screen also has a computer assistance setting, which is one of the things that affects your shot percentage [[gf1-18-10]].

#### Passwords

After every win the game shows a password. Write down the whole password your own game shows, both lines of it, since nothing is saved otherwise [[ra-comments-1-5]].

The community password list was made with the initials ICE. It uses a key of 1 for Detroit Pistons and 2 for Chicago Bulls as the "played as" value, and its first entry records 01-00 after beating Dallas [[gf-passwords-2-1, gf-passwords-2-2, gf-passwords-2-3]]. Treat that list only as an example of what a record looks like. Do not enter its passwords to skip games: the list belongs to a different set of initials, and it is not confirmed that loaded passwords count toward the V.Hard achievements. Play your own games and record your own passwords.

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave the setup menus, double-check that your initials do not start with DA, that Tag Mode is off, and that you are on V.Hard with a fast clock [[ra-comments-1-1, ra-comments-21-1, ra-comments-1-11]].

### Controls and Techniques

#### Offense

- **Pass:** press pass. Press turbo and pass for a quicker, safer pass [[gf1-6-1]].
- **Shoot:** hold the shoot button down [[gf1-6-2]]. The shot percentage is affected by being on fire, closeness to the basket, defenders in your face, the point difference and whether computer assistance is on [[gf1-18-10]].
- **Head fake:** tap shoot once [[gf1-6-4]].
- **Layup:** hold shoot for a short time while running [[gf1-6-7]].
- **Dunk:** hold shoot while running. Hold turbo and shoot for a super dunk [[gf1-6-6]]. You must be moving to perform a super dunk [[gf1-7-16]]. For the easy jam, be close to the basket and hold shoot [[gf1-7-17]].
- **Mid-dunk pass:** if a teammate is open during a dunk, press pass to send the ball to them for an easy three [[gf1-7-29]].
- **Threes:** shoot only open threes, and take the two-pointer otherwise [[gf4-3-6]]. If your computer teammate runs ahead of you, you can usually get a screen for the three about 3/4 down the court [[gf1-7-12]].

#### Defense

- **Steal:** tap steal to knock the ball away [[gf1-6-9]]. Tap steal and turbo to knock the opponent down [[gf1-6-10]].
- **Best steal spot:** when the computer inbounds, run between the passer and the receiver, because it always passes back to its teammate and you can intercept [[gf4-3-1]].
- **Block:** hold shoot to jump, and hold turbo with the jump to jump higher [[gf1-6-11]]. Jump at the same time you believe the shooter will jump [[gf1-7-3]].
- **Rebound:** it is automatic if you are close enough. Otherwise press shoot and point toward the ball [[gf1-6-12]]. Press shoot to jump toward the ball after it has hit the rim or board [[gf1-7-9]].

#### Getting on fire

Three made shots in a row puts a character on fire [[gf1-8-7]]. Two in a row is heating up, and a third puts you on fire [[gf1-8-10]]. While on fire you have unlimited turbo [[gf1-8-11]]. You stay on fire until the other team scores [[gf1-8-13]].

### Team Choice

Pick a team with a power forward/center pair. On V.Hard, one player had the most success playing mostly as the Hornets (Charlotte), with some games as the Bulls (Chicago), because of those combos [[ra-comments-2-4]]. In the guide's team rankings, New York is best with 33 points, and Charlotte and Chicago are tied for second with 32 points each [[gf6-8-1, gf6-8-2, gf6-8-3]].

Charlotte and Chicago both show up as opponents in the 27-team run, so when your own team comes up as the opponent, switch to the other of the two. The list's player did the same, switching to Chicago to play Detroit [[gf-passwords-2-2]]. The order of opponents in that list is only the list's own run, so your order may differ.

> Before you leave setup, confirm your initials, Tag Mode off, V.Hard and the fast clock. Then write down both lines of your password after every win [[ra-comments-1-1, ra-comments-21-1, ra-comments-1-11, ra-comments-1-5]].
