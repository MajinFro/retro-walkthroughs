## NBA Jam: Game Overview

### The Premise

NBA Jam on the SNES is a two-on-two basketball game with 27 NBA teams to beat, and once all 27 are beaten the game turns Juice on for every game [[gf2-2-7]]. Beating the teams in a row builds toward the 27-0 record that shows the ending [[gf1-17-1]].

This set has 28 achievements worth 530 points. The route is built around one long run: beat all 27 teams in a row on V.Hard difficulty with one record. That run covers the win streaks, the V.Hard milestones and the ending. A short clean-up of score and stat achievements follows it.

### Basic Controls and Moves

The moves you will use:

- **Pass:** press pass. Press turbo and pass for a quicker, safer pass [[gf1-6-1]].
- **Shoot:** hold shoot. A shot is more accurate at the peak of the jump [[gf1-6-2, gf1-6-3]].
- **Head fake:** tap shoot once. Your computer teammate will shoot if you do this while he has the ball [[gf1-6-4, gf1-6-5]].
- **Dunk:** hold shoot while running. Hold turbo and shoot for a super dunk [[gf1-6-6]]. You must be moving to dunk [[gf1-7-16]].
- **Lay-up:** hold shoot for a short time while running [[gf1-6-7]].
- **Elbows:** tap turbo twice, then tap shoot repeatedly [[gf1-6-8]].
- **Steal:** tap steal to knock the ball away. Tap steal and turbo to knock the opponent down [[gf1-6-9, gf1-6-10]].
- **Block:** hold shoot to jump, and hold turbo as well to jump higher [[gf1-6-11]].
- **Rebound:** automatic if you are close enough. Otherwise press shoot and point toward the ball [[gf1-6-12]].
- **Alley-oop:** easier with two-player teams. The player without the ball moves under the basket and holds shoot. The ball handler presses turbo and pass [[gf1-6-13]].

### Defense and Stealing

To block a shot, jump at the moment you expect the shooter to jump [[gf1-7-3]]. To reject a dunker, wait until he reaches the peak of his jump and then jump to block [[gf1-7-1]]. A low dunk such as an easy jam can instead be stopped by knocking the dunker down with turbo and steal [[gf1-7-2]]. It is possible to block the buzzer three-pointer. Right after the ball is passed inbounds, press turbo, jump and point toward the shooter [[gf1-7-5]].

There are three common ways to steal:

- Stick close and press steal a lot [[gf1-7-6]].
- Knock opponents down. Against computer opponents this can be repeated until they drop the ball [[gf1-7-7]].
- Let a player shoot while you are near his basket and jump before the shot gets there, though you may be called for goaltending [[gf1-7-8]].

A commenter adds that running in front of the passing lane while holding turbo is sometimes enough to steal the ball [[ra-comments-2-7]].

To rebound, press shoot to jump toward the ball after it has hit the rim or board [[gf1-7-9]]. You can also knock down opposing players near the ball so you can pick it up off the floor [[gf1-7-10]]. A player close enough to the ball jumps for it automatically [[gf1-7-11]].

### Scoring Tools

- **Easy jam:** be close to the basket and hold shoot [[gf1-7-17]].
- **Longer-range dunks:** hold turbo and shoot from further out. The guide gives spots for each one, such as the tomahawk jams, the cannonball slam and the 720 slam [[gf1-7-20, gf1-7-21, gf1-7-22, gf1-7-23, gf1-7-24]].
- **Pass out of a dunk:** in mid-dunk, if your teammate is open, press pass to send him the ball for an easy three [[gf1-7-29]].

### On Fire

A character who makes three shots in a row is "on fire". Two in a row is "heating up" [[gf1-8-7, gf1-8-10]]. While on fire, future shots have a much better chance of going in, though some still miss. You also get unlimited turbo [[gf1-8-8, gf1-8-11]]. You stay on fire until the other team makes a basket, and the effect usually fades after about 30 points regardless [[gf1-8-13, gf1-8-14]]. To stay on fire, play in the back of the court, goaltend layups and three-pointers, and steal and rebound when possible [[gf1-8-9]]. To take an opponent off fire, dunk the ball [[gf1-8-12]].

### Protecting a Lead

With a lead of four points or more and the ball, stay at your own end of the court and hold your position as long as you can. The computer does nothing until the shot clock drops below 5 [[gf1-8-2, gf1-8-3]]. At 6 seconds, pass to an open teammate or take a shot. After a miss, rebound, pass back to the backcourt player and milk the clock again [[gf1-8-4, gf1-8-5]].

A commenter describes the same idea for V.Hard. With roughly a 3+ point lead in the 4th quarter and the ball, wait at half court until the shot clock shows. At about 7 seconds left, drive toward the basket and weave up or down to get past the defender [[ra-comments-1-13]]. You can also stand at half court and let your computer teammate run into your defender and push him over. The guarding defender does nothing until you cross half court [[ra-comments-1-14]].

V.Hard is hard because the CPU's full-court shots always go in, which causes last-second losses [[ra-comments-1-9]]. That is why the route never relies on a narrow lead at the buzzer.

### Power-Up Codes

Power-up codes are entered on the "Tonight's match up" screen, the one with the big VS, before tip-off [[gf-codes-3-1]]. Those listed as confirmed are:

- **Fire:** press B 7 times, then hold UP + Y + B until tip-off [[gf-codes-3-3, ra-comments-2-3]].
- **Shot %:** press any button, then hold DOWN + B + A until tip-off [[gf-codes-3-2]].
- **Turbo:** press any button 5 times, then hold Y + B + A [[gf-codes-3-6]].
- **Juice:** press any button 13 times, then hold B + X [[gf-codes-3-7]].

The Fire code puts you on fire for the whole game [[gf2-2-3]]. Juice makes all players move about twice as fast, and it turns on automatically for every game once all 27 teams are beaten [[gf2-2-6, gf2-2-7]].

Whether Tonight's Match-up codes are allowed during the V.Hard run is an open question in the comments with no answer, so the route plays the V.Hard run without codes. The codes are used only in Chapter 7, where the 100- and 200-point games are played on Normal difficulty with the Fire code [[ra-comments-2-1, ra-comments-2-3]].

### Passwords, Records and Initials

The game does not save your progress the way Jam TE does. Your record is lost when you exit unless you keep track of the password [[ra-comments-1-5]]. The game has no save of its own, so the password is the only way to resume: write it down after every win. Each password is shown as two lines of seven characters, so copy both lines exactly as your own game displays them [[gf-passwords-2-3]]. The published list is for the initials ICE, playing as Detroit, and runs from record 01-00 up to 27-00 [[gf-passwords-2-1, gf-passwords-2-2, gf-passwords-2-3, gf-passwords-2-29]]. Do not load published passwords to skip games. A commenter asked whether loaded passwords count for the V.Hard achievements and got no answer, so play your own record from the start.

A few rules for your single record:

- **Use only one record.** Creating more than one account stops most achievements from unlocking [[ra-comments-1-3]].
- **Do not start your initials with DA.** Names starting with DA prevent the main achievements from unlocking [[ra-comments-1-1]].

### Special Characters

The SNES version has secret characters such as Mark Turmell (MJT), Bill Clinton (ARK) and Al Gore (NET). Enter the first two initials, place the cursor over the last initial, then hold the listed buttons and press the final letter [[gf2-1-1, gf2-1-3, gf4-2-2]]. They are not needed for the route.

### RetroAchievements and Hardcore Rules

- **Version:** the achievements require the US ROM [[ra-comments-1-2]].
- **Emulator:** a commenter reports that an achievement called Warning did not trigger in RetroArch and required one of the emulators on the site. It is not in the current set [[ra-comments-1-6]].
- **Hardcore:** emulator features such as rewinds and emulator cheats are off. The in-game power-up codes are part of the game itself, but whether they are allowed during the V.Hard run is an open question, so the route uses them only in Chapter 7 on Normal difficulty [[ra-comments-2-1, ra-comments-2-3]].
- **Tag Mode:** keep it off. The assist achievement only triggers with Tag Mode off [[ra-comments-21-1]].
- **Computer assistance:** this is an Options screen setting. It affects shot percentage along with being on fire, closeness to the basket, defenders and the point difference [[gf1-18-10]].
- **Clock speed:** a very fast game clock makes games go quicker [[ra-comments-1-11]]. Once you have beaten every team, Juice mode is on and speeds up possessions, which helps the Chapter 7 score attack [[ra-comments-1-12, gf2-2-7]].
- **Shot percentage display:** this is a code-triggered readout of your odds. The percentage does not always match results, so treat it as a guide [[gf1-10-1, gf1-10-8]].

### The 100% Plan in Plain Language

You need only one playthrough, because one record carries the whole set. The steps below follow the route's plan.

1. **Setup (Chapter 1).** Set the options, choose your single set of initials and learn the controls [[ra-comments-1-1, ra-comments-1-3, ra-comments-21-1]].
2. **V.Hard run (Chapters 2-6).** Beat all 27 teams in a row on V.Hard without losing and without any game going to overtime. This one run earns the win streaks of 5, 10 and 20, the V.Hard milestones and the ending. A commenter reads the V.Hard achievement logic as ignoring losses, because the win count only matters once you have 27 wins [[ra-comments-18-1]]. No source confirms what a loss or an overtime game does to the V.Hard milestones, so treat every V.Hard game as must-win in regulation. Write down the password after every win [[ra-comments-1-5]].
3. **Score Attack and Stat Clean-Up (Chapter 7).** The 100- and 200-point games come last, on Normal difficulty with the Fire code [[ra-comments-2-1, ra-comments-2-3]]. Playing them last means a loss there cannot break the V.Hard streak, which is the route's reasoning rather than a rule stated by the sources.

Several basic-feat achievements are folded into the early V.Hard games, so Chapter 2 holds 11 of the 28 achievements. These cover passing, dunking, stealing, blocking, rebounding and similar feats [[gf1-6-1, gf1-6-6, gf1-6-9, gf1-6-11, gf1-6-12]]. No source reports achievements that lock each other out.
