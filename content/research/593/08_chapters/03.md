Winning all five takes your record to 10-0 and unlocks **Great Start** (beat the first 10 teams on V.Hard without going into overtime) and **Bigger Streak** (win 10 in a row).

The V.Hard CPU is the danger. Its full-court shots always go in, so it can beat you on the last second [[ra-comments-1-9]]. Build a lead early, then run the clock down so the CPU gets fewer possessions [[gf1-8-2, ra-comments-1-13]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Great Start requires the first 10 teams to be beaten without going into overtime. A thin lead is not safe, because the CPU's full-court shots always go in [[ra-comments-1-9]]. Build a lead you can hold before the clock gets low [[gf1-8-2]].

> [!WARNING]

#### Strategy for All Five Games

1. **Team choice.** One player had the most success playing mostly as the Hornets (Charlotte), with some games as the Bulls (Chicago), because both have power forward/center combos [[ra-comments-2-4]]. None of this chapter's opponents (Sacramento, Denver, Milwaukee, Indiana, Atlanta) is one of those two teams, so either works [[gf-passwords-2-8, gf-passwords-2-9, gf-passwords-2-10, gf-passwords-2-11, gf-passwords-2-12]]. If a later opponent is the team you are using, switch to the other one.
2. **Opening.** Spam three-pointers to secure a two-possession lead [[ra-comments-2-5]]. After that, focus on dunks with Larry Johnson, which applies when you are playing as the Hornets [[ra-comments-2-5, ra-comments-2-4]].
3. **Steals.** On very hard the bots pass the ball much more than on other difficulties, so you can spam steals out of the baseline inbounds [[ra-comments-2-6]].
4. **Milking the clock.** With a lead of four points or more, take the ball after a basket or goaltend, stay at your end of the court and hold your position as long as you can [[gf1-8-2]].
5. **Late-game drive.** With a 3+ point lead in the 4th quarter and the ball, stand at half court and wait for the shot clock to appear. When it reaches about 7 seconds, drive toward the basket and weave up or down. The defender runs in a straight line at your initial position, so you blow by him nearly every time [[ra-comments-1-13]].
6. **Teammate push.** Alternatively, stand at half court and get your CPU teammate to run toward the defender guarding you. He pushes the defender over and you get a free run to the basket. The defender does nothing until you cross half court [[ra-comments-1-14]].
7. **Jump pass.** When your CPU teammate inbounds to you, start a shot immediately. Hold the shoot button while both defenders jump, then pass in the air to your teammate [[ra-comments-1-15]].
