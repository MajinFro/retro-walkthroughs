## Chapter 2: V.Hard Games 1-5: First Win and the Basic Feats

[MERMAID]

### Area Overview & Preparation

This chapter covers the first five V.Hard games in Regular Mode. In the password list's run, the opponents came in this order: Dallas Mavericks, Minnesota Timberwolves, Miami Heat, Washington Bullets and Philadelphia 76ers [[gf-passwords-2-3, gf-passwords-2-4, gf-passwords-2-5, gf-passwords-2-6, gf-passwords-2-7]]. No source says you choose your opponent, so your own order may differ. Along the way you pick up the single Player 1 feats (field goal, three, dunk, assist, steal, block, rebound, On Fire), then finish with A Good Start and A Win Streak.

**Preparation notes:**

- The achievements require the US ROM version [[ra-comments-1-2]].
- Do not use a player name starting with DA, or the main achievements will not unlock [[ra-comments-1-1]].
- Create no more than one account, otherwise most achievements will not unlock [[ra-comments-1-3]].
- NBA Jam does not save your progress like Jam TE. Your record is lost when you exit the game unless you keep track of the password [[ra-comments-1-5]]. After each win, write down both lines of the password your own game shows. That password is your only way to resume.
- Do not load published passwords to skip games. A commenter asked whether loaded passwords count for the V.Hard achievements and the question went unanswered, so play each game yourself.
- The game clock can be set to very fast to make games go quicker [[ra-comments-1-11]].
- Do not enter any Tonight's Match-up codes during the V.Hard games. This route plays V.Hard with no codes.

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** The V.Hard run (A Good Start through Legendary Team, achievement 23955) requires every game to be won in regulation. A single game that goes to overtime fails the "without going into overtime" condition for every V.Hard game from team 1 to team 27 [[ra-comments-1-13, ra-comments-1-14, ra-comments-1-9, gf1-8-2, ra-comments-18-1]]. Win each game before time expires and do not let it get tied.

### Regular Mode

Play Regular Mode on V.Hard. Controls to know before you start:

- **Pass:** press pass; press turbo and pass for a quicker, safer pass [[gf1-6-1]].
- **Shoot:** hold the shoot button down [[gf1-6-2]].
- **Dunk:** hold shoot while running; hold turbo and shoot for a super dunk [[gf1-6-6]].
- **Lay up:** hold shoot for a short time while running [[gf1-6-7]].
- **Steal:** tap steal to knock the ball away [[gf1-6-9]].
- **Block:** hold shoot to jump; hold turbo and jump to jump higher [[gf1-6-11]].
- **Rebound:** automatic if you are close enough, otherwise press shoot and point toward the ball [[gf1-6-12]].

One team-choice suggestion from a player who cleared V.Hard: play mostly as the Hornets, with some games as the Bulls, because their power forward/center combos worked best [[ra-comments-2-4]]. The same player's dunk advice (Larry Johnson) applies only when you play as Charlotte [[ra-comments-2-5]].

### V.Hard games 1-5 (Dallas to Philadelphia)

#### Game 1: Dallas Mavericks

The password list's run met Dallas first [[gf-passwords-2-3]]. A plan that worked for another player: spam three-pointers to secure a two-possession lead, then focus on dunks [[ra-comments-2-5]]. Winning your first game earns Beginners Luck.

[ACHIEVEMENT:23888]

On very hard the bots pass the ball much more than on other difficulties, so spamming steals out of baseline inbounds works in your favor [[ra-comments-2-6]].

#### Game 2: Minnesota Timberwolves

The list's run met Minnesota next [[gf-passwords-2-4]]. Use the same approach: build a lead, then close out the game with the late-game techniques in the rubber banding section below [[ra-comments-2-5, ra-comments-1-13]].

#### Game 3: Miami Heat

The list's run met Miami third [[gf-passwords-2-5]]. On V.Hard, CPU full-court shots always go in and can cost you the game at the last second, so do not rely on a thin lead [[ra-comments-1-9]]. Keep the lead large enough that a late CPU shot cannot force overtime.

#### Game 4: Washington Bullets

The list's run met Washington fourth [[gf-passwords-2-6]]. Expect the CPU to throw three-pointers from midcourt [[ra-comments-1-8]].

#### Game 5: Philadelphia 76ers

The list's run met Philadelphia fifth [[gf-passwords-2-7]]. Beat five V.Hard teams without going into overtime and A Good Start unlocks.

Winning five games in a row also gives A Win Streak.

#### Single Player 1 feats

Each of these must be performed by Player 1 (the achievement descriptions say so). Pick them up naturally during games 1-5.

**Not So Dunky: Score a Field Goal.** Hold shoot to shoot [[gf1-6-2]], or hold shoot briefly while running for a lay up [[gf1-6-7]]. Head fakes work well when shooting [[gf1-7-13]]. An easy jam also works: be close to the basket and hold shoot [[gf1-7-17]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:23882]

**Three Throw!: Score a 3-pointer.** Computer teammates often let you get a screen about 3/4 down the court if the teammate runs ahead of you [[gf1-7-12]]. Only shoot open threes; when the three is covered, a two-pointer is fine [[gf4-3-6]]. Head fakes sometimes help get an open three [[gf4-3-8]]. Another easy route is to dish the ball to your open teammate in the middle of a jam [[gf1-7-29, gf4-3-7]]. Spamming threes early also builds a lead [[ra-comments-2-5]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:23874]

**Dunk It: Score a dunk.** Hold shoot while running; you must be moving to dunk [[gf1-6-6, gf1-7-16]]. The easy jam is: close to the basket, hold shoot [[gf1-7-17]]. A steal right under the basket allows an immediate dunk [[ra-comments-2-8]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:23875]

**Don't Be Greedy: Score an assist.** Press pass to pass [[gf1-6-1]]. If you are doing an easy jam with an opponent nearby, pass to your teammate in mid-dunk [[gf1-7-15]]; if the teammate is open, they can take an easy three [[gf1-7-29]]. Another method: when your CPU teammate inbounds to you, immediately start a shot, hold shoot as both defenders jump, then pass in the air to your teammate. Usually the CPU goes straight to the basket, or shoots a three while you chase the rebound [[ra-comments-1-15, ra-comments-1-16]]. A jump pass also gets you out of double teams for an easy roll to the basket [[ra-comments-2-9]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:23876]

> [!NOTE]
> The related repeating assist achievement (Not Selfish at All) only triggers with Tag Mode off [[ra-comments-21-1]]. Keeping Tag Mode off is a sensible habit here too.

**Candy from a Player: Score a steal.** Tap steal to knock the ball away [[gf1-6-9]], or stick close and press steal a lot [[gf1-7-6]]. The best steal spot is running between the computer when it inbounds, since it passes back to its teammate [[gf4-3-1]]. Stand behind the receiver of the inbounds pass to intercept it [[gf4-3-2]]. On very hard, spam steals from baseline inbounds [[ra-comments-2-6]]. Running in front of the passing lane while holding turbo can also work [[ra-comments-2-7]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:23877]

**This is No Minecraft: Score a block.** Blocking is much harder here than in Jam TE [[ra-comments-1-4]]. Hold shoot to jump (hold turbo to jump higher) [[gf1-6-11]]. To block a shot, jump when you believe the shooter will jump [[gf1-7-3]]. To reject a dunker, wait until he reaches the peak of his jump, then jump [[gf1-7-1]]. You can also try to block the buzzer 3-point attempt: right after the ball is passed inbounds, press turbo, jump and point toward the shooter [[gf1-7-5]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:23878]

**You Don't Wanna Be That Guy: Score a rebound.** Rebounding is automatic if you are close enough [[gf1-6-12, gf1-7-11]]. Otherwise press shoot to jump toward the ball after it has hit the rim or board [[gf1-7-9]], or knock down opposing players near the ball and grab it off the floor [[gf1-7-10]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:23879]

### Beating the V.Hard rubber banding

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Any overtime in a V.Hard game fails the run condition [[ra-comments-1-13, gf1-8-2, ra-comments-1-9]]. Use these techniques to close games out in regulation.

1. **Milk the clock.** With a lead of four points or more and the ball from a basket or goaltend, stay at your end of the court and hold your position as long as you can [[gf1-8-2]]. A community version: with a 3+ point lead in the 4th quarter and the ball, stand at half court until the shot clock appears, and once it reaches about 7 seconds, drive toward the basket and weave up or down to blow by the defender [[ra-comments-1-13]].
2. **Let your teammate clear the defender.** Stand at half court and get your CPU teammate to run into the defender guarding you; the CPU pushes him over for a free run to the basket. The defender will not act until you cross half court [[ra-comments-1-14]].
3. **After a missed shot,** rebound, pass back to the backcourt player, and milk the clock again [[gf1-8-5]].
4. **Use the inbound trick.** When your CPU teammate inbounds to you, start a shot, hold shoot as both defenders jump, then pass in the air to your teammate [[ra-comments-1-15, ra-comments-1-16]].

### On Fire

A character who makes three consecutive shots is on fire [[gf1-8-7]]. Two made shots in a row is "heating up", and a third made shot puts you on fire [[gf1-8-10]]. While on fire, future shots have a much improved chance of going in, though some still miss [[gf1-8-8]], and you have unlimited turbo [[gf1-8-11]]. You stay on fire until the other team makes a shot in their basket [[gf1-8-13]].

Earn this achievement naturally during the V.Hard games by hitting three shots in a row, for example by chaining open threes or dunks early when you build your lead [[gf1-8-7, gf1-8-10]].

> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Do not enter any Tonight's Match-up codes, including the Fire code, during the V.Hard games. The route plays V.Hard with no codes.
