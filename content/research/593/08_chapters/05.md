### V.Hard games 16-20 (Charlotte to Detroit)

#### Area Overview & Preparation

This stretch covers V.Hard games 16 to 20. The password list's run met Charlotte, the Los Angeles Lakers, New Jersey, Cleveland and Detroit, in that order [[gf-passwords-2-18, gf-passwords-2-19, gf-passwords-2-20, gf-passwords-2-21, gf-passwords-2-22]]. No source says you choose your opponent, so your order may differ. Treat the names below as the list's run, not a guarantee. Two achievements unlock here: **Champion Team** (beat the first 20 teams on V.Hard without going into overtime) and **Unbeatable** (win 20 in a row).

**Passwords are your only way to resume.** The game does not save your record. It is lost when you exit unless you kept track of the password [[ra-comments-1-5]]. After every win, write down the full password your own game shows, including both lines. Do not load published passwords to skip games. An RA commenter asked whether loaded passwords count for the V.Hard achievements and got no answer, so play your own chain.

**Which team to use.** The route recommends Charlotte or Chicago on V.Hard. Both have power forward/center combos, and one player had the most success with that archetype [[ra-comments-2-4]]. Whenever the next opponent is the team you are using, switch to the other one. The list's player did the same: they used Detroit, then switched to Chicago to play Detroit in game 20 [[gf-passwords-2-2, gf-passwords-2-22]].

> [!TIP]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Champion Team needs every one of the first 20 V.Hard wins to come in regulation. This is a condition you can retry, not a missable. The V.Hard CPU's full court shots always go in and cause last-second losses, so do not rely on a thin lead as the clock runs out [[ra-comments-1-9]].

#### Core Strategy for V.Hard

- **Opening.** Spam three-pointers to secure a two-possession lead, then focus on dunks [[ra-comments-2-5]]. The Larry Johnson dunk tip applies only when you play as Charlotte [[ra-comments-2-4, ra-comments-2-5]].
- **Steals.** V.Hard bots pass much more than on other difficulties. Spam steals from baseline inbounds [[ra-comments-2-6]].
- **Milk the clock.** With a lead of four points or more, take the ball after a basket or goaltend, stay at your end of the court and hold your position as long as you can [[gf1-8-2]].
- **Fourth-quarter lead.** With roughly a 3+ point lead in the 4th quarter and the ball, stand at half court and wait for the shot clock to appear. At about 7 seconds left, drive toward the basket and weave up or down to get past the defender [[ra-comments-1-13]].
- **Teammate push.** Alternatively, stand at half court and let your CPU teammate run into your defender and push him over for a free run to the basket. The defender guarding you does nothing until you cross half court [[ra-comments-1-14]].

#### Game 16: Charlotte (in the list's run)

1. The list's run met the Charlotte Hornets here [[gf-passwords-2-18]]. If you have been playing as Charlotte, switch to Chicago for this game [[ra-comments-2-4]].
2. Open with three-pointers until you hold a two-possession lead, then switch to dunks [[ra-comments-2-5]].
3. Steal from the baseline inbounds when the bots pass, then dunk right under the basket [[ra-comments-2-6]].
4. Once ahead by 3+ in the 4th quarter with the ball, wait at half court until about 7 seconds remain on the shot clock, then weave past the defender to the basket [[ra-comments-1-13]].
5. After the win, write down both lines of the password your game shows [[ra-comments-1-5]].

#### Game 17: Los Angeles Lakers (in the list's run)

1. The list's run met the Los Angeles Lakers next [[gf-passwords-2-19]]. Keep your Charlotte or Chicago team unless the Lakers are somehow your own team [[ra-comments-2-4]].
2. Spam three-pointers from the start until you have a two-possession lead, then switch to dunks [[ra-comments-2-5]].
3. Hunt for steals off the Lakers' baseline inbounds. Running in front of the passing lane can be enough, and the steal happens under the basket, so you can go up for an immediate dunk [[ra-comments-2-6]].
4. Involve your teammate. Pass out of double teams, and stand at half court so your CPU teammate runs into your defender and pushes him over [[ra-comments-1-14]].
5. In the 4th quarter with a 3+ lead and the ball, wait at half court for the shot clock, then drive at about 7 seconds [[ra-comments-1-13]]. With a lead of four or more, you can instead hold position at your end of the court and burn the clock [[gf1-8-2]].
6. Do not leave a last-second CPU possession to chance, because its full court shots always go in [[ra-comments-1-9]]. Write down your password after the win [[ra-comments-1-5]].
