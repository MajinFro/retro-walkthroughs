## Chapter 7: Score Attack and Stat Clean-Up

[MERMAID]

### Area Overview & Preparation

This final chapter covers the 100-point and 200-point score achievements plus the ten-count stat feats as Player 1. The 200-point game is the toughest achievement in the set, and it requires Power-Up Fire with computer assistance turned off [[ra-comments-1-10]]. Do it after beating every team on V.Hard. One player did exactly that, so Juice Mode was already on and possessions went quicker [[ra-comments-1-12]].

Setup for the score attack game:

- Set the computer to normal difficulty and slow time [[ra-comments-2-1]].
- Turn computer assistance off in the Options screen. It affects your shot percentage, so leaving it on works against you [[gf1-18-10]].
- Activate Power-Up Fire at the Tonight's Match-up screen (details below) [[ra-comments-2-3]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave:** Some of the ten-count feats (threes, dunks, assists, steals, blocks, rebounds, field goals) may already have unlocked during your V.Hard run. The sources do not confirm whether these counts are per game or cumulative. If any are still locked, aim to get all ten of that stat within a single game. That plan covers both readings. The Fire-code Normal game is a good place for threes, dunks and field goals, since you are on fire with unlimited turbo all game [[gf1-18-6]].

### Juice Mode

Juice Mode doubles the speed of game play [[gf4-1-11]]. After you have beaten every team, it is on for your games, which is what the player who cleared the 200-point game relied on [[ra-comments-1-12]]. If it is somehow not on, you can start it manually on the Tonight's Match-up screen: press any button thirteen times, then hold B and X [[gf4-1-12]].

### Tonight's Match-up Screen

Powerups are performed on the Tonight's Match-up screen [[gf4-1-1]]. A held button may be released when the screen goes blank or when the court appears [[gf4-1-2]].

1. Press **B** seven times [[ra-comments-2-3]].
2. Hold **B + Y + Up** until tip-off [[ra-comments-2-3, gf4-1-3]].
3. Powerup Fire keeps you on fire the whole game, with unlimited turbo and goaltending [[gf1-18-6]].

Another guide lists a different Fire input (B five times, then hold Up and shoot) [[gf1-18-5]]. Use the B-seven-times input above, which the achievement's players confirmed [[ra-comments-2-3, gf2-2-3]].

Optional extras on the same screen:

- Shot % display: one guide says to hold down, tap B once, then hold A and B. The clock then shows your shot percentage, which helps find good spots for threes [[gf4-1-6, gf4-1-7]]. A second guide gives a different input: turn on turbo for all three buttons, then rotate the pad clockwise while holding all three until tip-off [[gf1-18-7]]. The two guides give different inputs, so try whichever you prefer. The displayed percentage is unreliable [[gf1-18-9]].
- Defense Power-Up: pressing B five times and holding on the fifth press lets you goaltend without penalty [[gf4-1-5]].

> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Computer assistance must be off for the 200-point game [[ra-comments-1-10]]. Check the Options screen before starting [[gf1-18-10]]. This is not missable, so you can retry.

### 200-point game (Power-Up Fire, computer assistance off)

[ACHIEVEMENT:23962]

Breaking Records: score 100 points as Player 1. While you are on fire all game with unlimited turbo [[gf1-18-6]], this comes along the way to 200. Use dunks wherever possible. Hold shoot while running to dunk, or hold turbo and shoot for a super dunk [[gf1-6-6]]. You must be moving to super dunk [[gf1-7-16]]. For an easy jam, be close to the basket and hold shoot [[gf1-7-17]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:23963]

Mastery of NBA: score 200 points in a game. Tips from players who did it:

- Use normal difficulty and slow time [[ra-comments-2-1]]. Setting the computer to very easy caused missed dunks and long rebounds that lost time [[ra-comments-2-2]].
- Steal right under the basket and dunk immediately [[ra-comments-2-8]].
- After your CPU teammate inbounds to you, start a shot, hold shoot as both defenders jump, then pass in the air to your teammate [[ra-comments-1-15]]. He usually goes straight to the basket, or you can have him shoot a three while you chase the rebound [[ra-comments-1-16]].
- Jump passes to your teammate get you out of double teams for an easy roll to the basket [[ra-comments-2-9]].

### Stat Feats (as Player 1)

Check these off if they have not already unlocked. If one is still missing, try to get all ten of that stat in one game.

#### Threes (23956)

[ACHIEVEMENT:23956]

- Only shoot open threes; two-pointers are fine [[gf4-3-6]].
- Go about 3/4 down the court and let your computer teammate run ahead of you to get a screen for the three [[gf1-7-12]].
- Dish off in the middle of a jam for an easy open three [[gf1-7-29, gf4-3-7]]. Head fakes sometimes help too [[gf4-3-8]].
- On V.Hard, one player spammed threes to secure a two-possession lead [[ra-comments-2-5]].

#### Dunks (23957)

[ACHIEVEMENT:23957]

Hold shoot while running to dunk [[gf1-6-6]]. A steal right under the basket allows an immediate dunk [[ra-comments-2-8]].

#### Assists (23958)

[ACHIEVEMENT:23958]

Assists trigger only with Tag Mode off [[ra-comments-21-1]]. Press pass to pass; pass plus turbo throws a quicker, safer pass [[gf1-6-1]]. When doing an easy jam with an opponent nearby, pass to your open teammate in mid-dunk [[gf1-7-15]]. The inbound-then-air-pass trick above also feeds your teammate [[ra-comments-1-15]].

#### Steals (23959)

[ACHIEVEMENT:23959]

- Tap steal to knock the ball away [[gf1-6-9]], or stick close and press steal a lot [[gf1-7-6]].
- Run between the computer when it inbounds; it always passes back to its teammate. Stand behind the receiver to intercept [[gf4-3-1, gf4-3-2]]. The author recorded up to 67 steals this way [[gf4-3-3]].
- Running in the passing lane while holding turbo can also steal [[ra-comments-2-7]]. On V.Hard the bots pass more, which helps [[ra-comments-2-6]].

#### Blocks (23960)

[ACHIEVEMENT:23960]

- Hold shoot to jump to block; hold turbo and jump to go higher [[gf1-6-11]].
- Jump at the same time you believe the shooter will jump [[gf1-7-3]].
- To reject a dunker, wait until he reaches the peak of his jump [[gf1-7-1]].
- You can block the buzzer three by pressing turbo and jumping toward the shooter right after the inbound pass [[gf1-7-5]].
- Blocking is much harder in this game than in Jam TE [[ra-comments-1-4]].

#### Rebounds (23961)

[ACHIEVEMENT:23961]

Rebounding is automatic if you are close enough; otherwise press shoot and point toward the ball [[gf1-6-12]]. Press shoot to jump toward the ball after it hits the rim or board [[gf1-7-9]], or knock down opposing players near the ball [[gf1-7-10]]. A close player jumps automatically [[gf1-7-11]].

#### Field Goals (23968)

[ACHIEVEMENT:23968]

Hold shoot to shoot [[gf1-6-2]]. Hold shoot briefly while running to lay up [[gf1-6-7]]. Head fakes work well [[gf1-7-13]]. The achievement's logic was updated with an in-game check and a delta check [[ra-comments-30-1]].
