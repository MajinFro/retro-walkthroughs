### V.Hard games 11-15 (Clippers to Houston)

#### Area Overview & Preparation

This chapter covers V.Hard games 11 through 15. The password list's run met the Clippers, Magic, Warriors, Celtics and Rockets in that order as records 11-00 through 15-00, each played as player 1 [[gf-passwords-2-13, gf-passwords-2-14, gf-passwords-2-15, gf-passwords-2-16, gf-passwords-2-17]]. No source says you choose the opponent, so your own order may differ. The list spells the first team "Washington Clippers", which looks like a typo for the Los Angeles Clippers [[gf-passwords-2-13]].

The fifth win here completes the first 15 teams, which is what **Amazing Start** asks for. Every game must be won in regulation with no overtime, so treat each game as one you must close out before the final buzzer.

The main danger on V.Hard is the CPU's last-second full court shot. Community players report that these shots always go in and cost them games at the buzzer [[ra-comments-1-9]]. The plan for each game is to build a lead, burn clock, and block the buzzer three.

#### Team Choice

The route recommends playing mostly as the Hornets (Charlotte), with some games as the Bulls (Chicago). Both teams have power forward/center combos, and that archetype worked best on V.Hard [[ra-comments-2-4]]. None of this chapter's opponents is Charlotte or Chicago, so you can stay with your chosen team throughout. If a later opponent is the team you are using, switch to the other one. The Larry Johnson dunk tips below apply only when you play as Charlotte [[ra-comments-2-4, ra-comments-2-5]].

#### Core Strategy for Every Game

**Opening.** Start by spamming three-pointers to secure a two-possession lead. After that, focus on dunks with Larry Johnson [[ra-comments-2-5]].

**Steals off the baseline.** V.Hard bots pass the ball much more than on other difficulties, so spamming steals on baseline inbounds works in your favor [[ra-comments-2-6]].

**Milking the clock.** With a lead of four points or more, get the ball from a basket or a goaltend. Stay at your end of the court and hold your position as long as you can [[gf1-8-2]].

A community variant: with roughly a 3+ point lead in the 4th quarter and the ball, wait at half court until the shot clock reaches about 7 seconds. Then drive toward the basket and weave up or down to get past the defender [[ra-comments-1-13]].

Another option is to stand at half court and let your CPU teammate run into your defender. The teammate pushes the defender over and frees a run to the basket. The guarding defender does nothing until you cross half court [[ra-comments-1-14]].

**The buzzer three.** Right after the ball is passed inbounds, press turbo, jump, and point toward the shooter to block the buzzer three-point attempt [[gf1-7-5]].

### Game 11: Los Angeles Clippers

The list records this win as 11-00 [[gf-passwords-2-13]].

1. Open with three-pointers until you hold a two-possession lead [[ra-comments-2-5]].
2. Switch to dunks with Larry Johnson if you are playing as Charlotte [[ra-comments-2-5]].
3. On the opponent's baseline inbounds, step into the passing lane with turbo held. The steal happens under the basket, so you can dunk right away [[ra-comments-2-6]].
4. In the 4th quarter, once ahead by four or more, take the ball to your end and burn the clock [[gf1-8-2]].
5. On the final inbounds, jump with turbo toward the shooter to stop the buzzer three [[gf1-7-5]].

### Game 12: Orlando Magic

The list records this win as 12-00 [[gf-passwords-2-14]].

1. Use the same plan: threes for a two-possession lead, then dunks [[ra-comments-2-5]].
2. Look for steals from baseline inbounds, since the bots pass a lot [[ra-comments-2-6]].
3. In the final quarter, stand at half court and let the shot clock run to about 7 before driving past the defender [[ra-comments-1-13]].
4. If your teammate is near the defender, you can wait for the CPU to push him over and then run free to the basket. The defender does not move until you cross half court [[ra-comments-1-14]].
5. Block the buzzer three after the final inbounds [[gf1-7-5]].
