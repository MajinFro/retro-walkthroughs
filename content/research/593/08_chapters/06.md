## Chapter 6: V.Hard Games 21-27 and the Ending

[MERMAID]

### Area Overview & Preparation

This chapter covers the last seven Very Hard games. The published password list's run met San Antonio, New York, Portland, Utah, Seattle, Phoenix and Chicago in that order, as records 21-00 through 27-00 [[gf-passwords-2-23, gf-passwords-2-24, gf-passwords-2-25, gf-passwords-2-26, gf-passwords-2-27, gf-passwords-2-28, gf-passwords-2-29]]. No source says you choose your opponent, so your own order may differ. Treat the order as a guide to who is left, not a script.

Beating all 27 teams shows the SNES ending and unlocks **NBA All-Star** [[gf1-17-2, gf1-17-3]]. **Unstoppable Force** (the first 25 teams on V.Hard without overtime) and **Legendary Team** (all 27 teams on V.Hard without overtime) also finish in this chapter [[ra-comments-1-9, ra-comments-18-1]].

The game has no battery save. After every win, write down both lines of the password your own game shows. That password is the only way to resume a run [[gf-passwords-2-23]]. The published passwords belong to a different player's run with the initials ICE, so do not enter them to skip ahead. An RA commenter asked whether loaded passwords count for the V.Hard achievements and got no answer, so play every V.Hard game yourself.

> [!WARNING]
> **Initials Warning:** Do not enter initials that start with DA. A commenter reports that no main achievements will unlock with them. This rules out the FAQ's "DAN" 26-0 password, so never use it [[ra-comments-1-1, gf1-17-1]].

### V.Hard games 21-27 (San Antonio to Chicago)

🗺️ No map exists for this area, because it is a sequence of games rather than a navigable level.

#### Strategy for Very Hard without overtime

The CPU's full-court shots always go in on V. Hard, which causes last-second losses. Build a cushion and do not rely on a one-point lead [[ra-comments-1-9]].

**Team choice.** One player used mostly the Hornets with some games as the Bulls, because both have power forward/center combos [[ra-comments-2-4]]. Whenever your next opponent is the team you are using, switch to the other one. Chicago is the last team in the list's run, so if you have been playing as Chicago, switch to Charlotte for that game [[gf-passwords-2-29, ra-comments-2-4]].

**Opening.** Start by spamming three-pointers to secure a two-possession lead [[ra-comments-2-5]]. When playing as Charlotte, switch to dunks with Larry Johnson after that. The tip is not stated for the Bulls [[ra-comments-2-5]].

**Steals.** On very hard the bots pass much more, so you can spam steals out of the baseline inbounds [[ra-comments-2-6]].

**Milking the clock.** With a lead of four or more, once you get the ball from a basket or goaltend, stay at your end of the court and hold position as long as you can. The FAQ says the computer stays put until the shot clock counts down below 5, then forces your move. It also says to pass to an open teammate or shoot at about 6 seconds, and that this works best in the fourth quarter [[gf1-8-2]].

**Half-court waiting trick.** With a 3+ point lead in the 4th quarter and the ball, stand at half court until the shot clock appears. At about 7 seconds left, drive toward the basket and weave up or down to get past the defender [[ra-comments-1-13]].

**Teammate trick.** Alternatively, stand at half court and get your CPU teammate to run at the defender guarding you. He pushes the defender over and gives you a free run to the basket. The defender does nothing until you cross half court [[ra-comments-1-14]].

**Jump-pass trick.** When your CPU teammate inbounds to you, start a shot immediately and hold the shoot button as both defenders jump. Then pass in the air to your teammate [[ra-comments-1-15]]. He usually goes straight to the basket, or he can shoot a three while you chase the rebound [[ra-comments-1-16]].

**Opponent note.** The stats guide ranks New York best at 33 points, with Charlotte and Chicago next at 32 [[gf6-8-1]].

In the list's run this was the 21-00 win over the Spurs [[gf-passwords-2-23]]. Use the opening and clock tactics above. Afterwards, write down both lines of your password.

#### Game 22: New York Knicks

The list's run met the Knicks next, for 22-00 [[gf-passwords-2-24]]. New York is the stats guide's top-ranked team at 33 points, so do not rush [[gf6-8-1]]. Build the lead early with threes, then drain the clock in the fourth quarter [[ra-comments-2-5, gf1-8-2]].

#### Game 23: Portland Trailblazers

The list's run met Portland for 23-00 [[gf-passwords-2-25]]. If the bots keep passing, use the steal-from-inbounds approach [[ra-comments-2-6]].

#### Game 24: Utah Jazz

The list's run met Utah for 24-00 [[gf-passwords-2-26]]. Record your password afterwards.

#### Game 25: Seattle Supersonics

The list's run met Seattle for 25-00 [[gf-passwords-2-27]]. This is the 25th win. If none of your first 25 games went to overtime, **Unstoppable Force** unlocks here [[ra-comments-1-9]].

> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Unstoppable Force requires finishing the first 25 teams without overtime. With a lead of four or more, milking the clock at your end of the court is your best protection against a late tie, because the CPU's full-court shots always go in [[gf1-8-2, ra-comments-1-9]].

#### Game 26: Phoenix Suns

The list's run met Phoenix for 26-00 [[gf-passwords-2-28]]. The game after this is the last one needed for the ending [[gf1-17-1]].

#### Game 27: Chicago Bulls

The list's run finished against Chicago for 27-00 [[gf-passwords-2-29]]. Chicago is tied for second-best in the stats ranking at 32 points [[gf6-8-1]]. If you have been playing as Chicago, switch to Charlotte for this game [[ra-comments-2-4]]. Protect your lead, since the full-court CPU shots can undo you in the last seconds [[ra-comments-1-9]].

Beating all 27 teams without overtime unlocks **Legendary Team**.

> [!NOTE]
> One commenter had Legendary Team pop up prematurely and submitted a ticket [[ra-comments-2-10]]. Another suggests the 27-win check only looks at the last game, since losses are not counted toward the 27 wins [[ra-comments-18-1]]. Finish the run normally and do not assume it unlocked early.

### SNES ending (27-0)

After the 27th win, the SNES ending plays. The FAQ calls it pretty good but not the best [[gf1-17-2]]. In version 1.00 the ending does not depend on the skill level [[gf1-17-3]].

Beating the game unlocks **NBA All-Star**.
