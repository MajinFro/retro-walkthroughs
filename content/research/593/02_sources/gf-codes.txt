Special Guest Codes:
-------------------
 
To do special guest codes, enter the first two initials, then put the cursor 
over the last initial. Then hold down L, R, and Start. While holding them, 
press the button listed under the X OR A column. It will then display the 
guests stats, and tems beaten.
 
+------------+----------+-----------------+
|  INITIALS  |  X OR A  |  NAME           |
+------------+----------+-----------------+
| ARK        |  X       | Bill Clinton    |
| NET        |  A       | Al Gore         |
| SAL        |  X       | Divita          |
| QB         |  X       | Kabuki (*)      |
| MJT        |  A       | Mark Turmell    |
| ROD        |  X       | Scruff          |
| CAR        |  X       | Chow-Chow       |
| RJR        |  X       | Rivett          |
| AIR        |  X       | Air Dog         |
+------------+----------+-----------------+
 
(*) - Kabuki's initials are weird. To do this, type Q, B, and put the cursor 
      over the square on the initials screen.
