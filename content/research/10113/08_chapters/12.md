## Chapter 12: Ice Cavern and the Zora Tunic

[MERMAID]

This chapter takes adult Link up frozen Zora's River at night for two Hookshot Skulltulas, across the iced-over Zora's Fountain to the Ice Cavern, and back out again. The Iron Boots, Serenade of Water, Compass, Map and Pieces of Heart #26 to #28 come from this route, as do the Zora Tunic and King Zora's kiss [[gf1-207-1, gf1-207-2, gf1-209-1, gf1-209-2, gf1-213-3, gf1-443-1, gf1-442-2, ra-guide-11-5, ra-guide-11-6]]. Three achievements are in play: Bring Me to the Bottom of the Oceans (Iron Boots), A Breath of Fresh Air (Zora Tunic) and Kiss That Frog (the only missable one). The order of the Zora's Domain steps matters, so read the warnings before you touch King Zora.

### Preparation

- **Blue Fire:** Blue Fire melts the Red Ice that blocks the Compass chest, a Piece of Heart, the Map chest and parts of the block room, and it is also needed on the Zora Shop door and on King Zora. Carry as many empty Bottles as you can [[gf1-211-5, gf2-69-3, gf1-212-3, gf1-215-3, gf1-215-2]]. Fill every Bottle with Blue Fire whenever you reach the blue torch [[gf2-69-3]]. Make sure you leave the Ice Cavern with at least two bottles of Blue Fire in your inventory, one for the shop door and one for King Zora [[ra-guide-11-4, gf1-215-3]].
- **Giant's Wallet:** the Zora Tunic costs 300 Rupees at the shop, so you need the Giant's Wallet [[gf1-403-2, ra-comments-37-2]]. A commenter advises defeating 30 Gold Skulltulas and getting the Giant's Wallet before this chapter so that you do not miss Kiss That Frog [[ra-comments-37-1]].
- **Weapons:** Bring the Fairy Bow for the Ice Keese and the Hookshot for the Skulltulas and the Freezzards [[gf1-211-4, gf1-496-1]]. Ice Keese freeze you on contact, so kill them from range [[gf1-211-4]].
- **Hardcore mode:** save states are off, so the tunic-first order below matters, because you cannot undo a wrong thaw.

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Kiss That Frog is missable. Thawing King Zora with Blue Fire before you have bought and equipped a Zora Tunic makes him hand you the free tunic instead of blowing a kiss, which costs you the achievement [[ra-comments-37-3, checklist-sidequests-18-12, ra-guide-11-6]]. Do not use Blue Fire on King Zora until the steps in "Zora's Domain, second visit" are complete.

### Zora's River

🗺️ **Map:** [View Zora's River/Domain Map by EverythingZelda](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/56-zoras-river-domain-map)

Enter from Hyrule Field. Both Skulltulas here appear only at night, and time passes normally in this zone, so you can play the Sun's Song here if it is day [[gf1-207-1, gf1-96-2]].

#### Skulltula on the south wall (#45)

Run forward from the entrance and jump across the river where you crossed as a kid. Run upstream to the next place where the river must be crossed, but do not jump yet. At night, face the south wall and look up to see the Skulltula, which is out of reach from here [[gf1-207-1]].

Climb up to the high ledge, face south again, and look for the cliff with a pit in it. Jump out to this ledge, avoiding the pit, and Hookshot the Skulltula for its token [[gf1-207-1, checklist-skulltulas-9-4]].

An alternative route is to ride the grown Magic Bean platform up to the high area and jump off as it passes. Then look southwest and play the Sun's Song if nothing is crawling on the wall [[gf2-68-2]].

#### Skulltula near the wooden bridge (#46)

Work your way upstream toward the Zora's Domain waterfall. Near the end of the wooden drawbridge just before the waterfall, look at the northwestern wall for the second Skulltula. You may need to stand on the fence to reach it with the Hookshot [[gf1-207-2, checklist-skulltulas-9-5, gf2-68-3]].

#### Opening the waterfall

Stand on the plaque in front of the waterfall and play Zelda's Lullaby. The waterfall retracts, and you can jump over to the doorway and enter Zora's Domain [[gf1-96-3, gf2-35-5, gf1-207-3]].

### Zora's Domain

🗺️ **Map:** [View Zora's River/Domain Map by EverythingZelda](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/56-zoras-river-domain-map)

Zora's Domain is frozen over. It does not thaw by your own actions; it begins to unfreeze only after the Water Temple [[gf1-526-1, gf2-68-4]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Climb to King Zora's throne room only to pass through. Do not use Blue Fire on him yet. This is the Kiss That Frog deadline [[ra-comments-37-3, checklist-sidequests-18-12]].

#### The waterfall-top Skulltula (later)

The Gold Skulltula at the top of the frozen waterfall (#47) is counted in Chapter 21, so the checkpoint below leaves it out. If it is night and you take it now, it simply counts early: Hookshot it from the lip at the top of the tunnel on the left side of the throne room [[gf1-472-1, gf1-208-1, gf2-97-4]].

#### On to the Fountain

Climb to the throne room and take the passage behind King Zora's throne into Zora's Fountain [[gf1-208-2, gf1-305-2]].

### Zora's Fountain

Jabu-Jabu is gone and a slab of ice has replaced him [[gf2-68-4]].

#### Piece of Heart #26 (ice platforms)

Climb the small frozen hill and jump onto the first of the frozen platforms. Jump carefully along them. At the second platform you can go north or northeast, and you should go northeast first for the Piece of Heart [[gf1-209-1]]. Another route heads for the huge iceberg and then runs east to the end of the string of icebergs [[gf1-442-1]].

The second set of platforms steadily rotates. Stay on a spinning platform until it goes around, then jump [[gf1-209-1, gf2-68-5]].

Take the **[Piece of Heart]** (#26) [[gf1-209-1, gf2-68-5]].

#### Into the cave

Head back to the giant platform and go north along the ice platforms. Jump to the northern edge of the pond and into the massive cave mouth, which leads to the Ice Cavern [[gf1-209-2]].

### Ice Cavern

🗺️ **Map:** [View Ice Cavern Map by EverythingZelda](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/53-ice-cavern-map)

The Ice Cavern holds one Piece of Heart and three Gold Skulltulas [[checklist-sidequests-27-1]].

#### Entry corridor and Freezzard room

When the passage opens into a large room with a frozen floor, three Freezzards are waiting [[gf1-211-1]]. Defeat them with sword strikes or Din's Fire. On the ice, use the Hookshot on a Freezzard to pull yourself right to it. Their ice breath freezes you [[gf1-211-1]]. Dodge the Blade Trap in the room, then enter the door that opens [[gf1-360-2]].

#### Spinning ice-blade room (Silver Rupees)

The next room has a huge rotating ice blade and Silver Rupees that you must collect to unlock the door at the north end [[gf1-211-2, gf1-360-3]]. One rupee is behind three blue stalagmites in the corner to the left as you enter, and a Gold Skulltula is on the ceiling above it [[gf1-211-3, gf2-69-1]]. The door at the top of the cliff unlocks once all are collected, and you climb up and go through it [[gf1-211-2]].

On the wall here is a Gold Skulltula, on the left wall as you enter from the southern door, also described as behind a cluster of icicles. Hookshot it for its token [[gf1-496-1, checklist-skulltulas-15-2, gf2-69-2]].

#### Ice Keese room and the Blue Fire

In the wide room beyond, Ice Keese freeze you on contact. Kill them from afar with arrows [[gf1-211-4]].

This room has a platform with a pillar of Blue Fire. Fill your empty Bottles with Blue Fire here [[gf1-211-5, gf2-69-3, gf1-360-4]].

A Jump Attack with the Biggoron Sword or Megaton Hammer kills the Freezzard that hogs the path in one hit [[gf1-212-1]]. Use Blue Fire to melt the Red Ice around the large chest and open it for the **[Dungeon Map]** [[gf1-212-2, gf2-69-3]]. The pot frozen in Red Ice contains a fairy [[gf1-212-2]]. Another pillar of Red Ice hides a pot that holds a Purple Rupee [[gf2-69-4]].

#### Compass, Piece of Heart #27 and Skulltula

Two walls with a red tinge are visible in the blade room, one on each side, and both can be melted with Blue Fire. One leads to the Compass and Heart Piece room, the other to the block-pushing room [[gf1-443-1, gf3-136-1, gf1-496-2, gf1-497-1]]. Sources differ on which side is which, so if you open the wrong wall, go back and open the other one [[gf1-212-1, gf1-496-2, gf1-497-1]].

Use Blue Fire to melt the Red Ice over the chest on the right and open it for the **[Compass]** [[gf1-212-3, gf2-70-1]]. Melt the Red Ice on the left side for **[Piece of Heart]** #27 [[gf1-212-4, gf2-70-2]]. A Gold Skulltula is on the wall beside or above the Piece of Heart, so Hookshot it [[gf1-212-5, gf2-70-3]]. Refill your Bottles with Blue Fire and go back to the blade room [[gf1-212-3]].

#### Block-pushing room

Melt the other red-tinged wall and go to the sliding-block room [[gf1-497-1]]. The Skulltula is in the corner to your right as you come in, and you can Hookshot it. It is also described as above the pit to the left [[gf1-212-6, gf1-497-1, gf2-70-4]]. Take care near the pit, because a fall can drop you back at the start of the dungeon [[gf2-70-4]].

You need all five Silver Rupees to open the exit. Pushing the ice block off a cliff makes it respawn at the room entrance [[gf1-212-7]]. One solution is as follows:

1. Grab the block from its east side and push it forward. Climb on top and use it to reach the rupee on the tall stone block [[gf1-212-7]].
2. Push the block east from its west side. Climb into the small alcove, melt its Red Ice with Blue Fire, and take the second rupee [[gf1-212-7]].
3. Push the block off a cliff so it respawns, then push it south from its north side. Jump on and take the rupee on the rock [[gf1-212-7]].
4. Push the stone block west from its east side. Climb onto the ice block and take the rupee on top of the stone [[gf1-212-7]].
5. Jump down, go to the south side of the block, and push it north. Grab the rupee on the stone where it comes to rest, and the door opens [[gf1-212-7]].

A second solution exists and uses a different push order [[gf2-70-5]].

To reach the exit ledge, push the block west, south, west, climb to the Blue Fire torch, then push it south, east, south and jump to the opened hallway [[gf2-70-6]].

If you want the bundle of Red Rupees hanging high above the ground, play the Song of Time here to make a Block of Time appear and use it to climb [[gf1-213-1]].

#### White Wolfos and the Iron Boots

The White Wolfos waits in the shrine room [[gf3-62-1]]. Block with your shield and attack only after it strikes. A jump attack after defending works well [[gf2-70-7]]. You can also let it strike, backflip away at the last moment, and jump attack as you land. Even with the Master Sword it dies in one shot [[gf1-213-2]].

A large chest appears in the middle of the room. Open it for the **[Iron Boots]** [[gf1-213-3, gf2-70-8, gf3-62-1]]. The Iron Boots are needed in the Water Temple [[gf1-404-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48254]

This achievement is story-related and not missable [[ra-comments-36-1]].

Sheik then appears and teaches you the Serenade of Water: A Button, C-Down, C-Right, C-Right, C-Left. It takes you to the Water Temple in Lake Hylia [[gf1-213-4, gf1-396-1, gf3-235-6]]. Do not use it yet, because the Tunic and a Piece of Heart come first.

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave the Ice Cavern:** check that you have both the Map and Compass, the Piece of Heart (#27), all three Skulltulas, and at least two bottles of Blue Fire [[gf1-212-2, gf1-212-3, gf1-212-4, checklist-sidequests-27-1, ra-guide-11-4]].

### Zora's Domain, second visit

Return through the west entrance of the Fountain to Zora's Domain [[gf1-117-2]]. You will do the shop and King Zora in strict order.

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Do not thaw King Zora yet. The Tunic must be bought and worn first, or he hands you a tunic and the kiss is lost [[ra-comments-37-3, ra-guide-11-6]].

#### Step 1: Buy the tunic

Use Blue Fire on the frozen Zora Shop door [[gf1-215-3, ra-guide-11-5]]. The shop sells the Zora Tunic for 300 Rupees. You need the Giant's Wallet to hold that much [[gf1-403-2, gf2-140-38]]. Buy it.

[ACHIEVEMENT:48941]

You can also get this achievement from King Zora's free tunic, but doing so costs you the kiss [[gf1-403-1, ra-comments-37-3]].

#### Step 2: Equip and save

Equip the Zora Tunic from the equipment subscreen and use the game's normal save before you continue. Whether the tunic must be worn at the exact moment of thawing is unconfirmed, as sources differ. Follow the safe order: buy, equip, save, then thaw, and talk to him while still wearing it [[ra-guide-11-6, ra-comments-92-3]].

#### Step 3: Thaw King Zora and take the kiss

Climb to the throne room and stand next to King Zora wearing the tunic. Use the Blue Fire on him [[checklist-sidequests-18-12, ra-guide-11-6]]. Then talk to him. He blows you a kiss instead of giving you a tunic [[ra-guide-11-6, ra-comments-37-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48942]

Do not show him the Prescription yet unless you intend to start the Biggoron trade chain right now. He gives the Eyeball Frog, which has a three-minute limit [[gf1-360-5, gf3-62-6]]. Do not warp with Serenade of Water or Bolero of Fire during that quest [[gf1-215-4]]. That sequence is covered in the next chapter.

### Back at the Fountain: Piece of Heart #28

Go back through the passage behind the throne to Zora's Fountain. Equip the Iron Boots, and with the Zora Tunic on, sink into the deep water to the exact center and bottom of the pool. Take the **[Piece of Heart]** (#28) [[checklist-heartpieces-4-15, gf2-97-2, gf3-136-3, gf4-16-10]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave:** confirm Pieces of Heart #26, #27 and #28, the Map, Compass, Iron Boots, Serenade of Water and the Zora Tunic. Zora's River GS #45 and #46 and the Ice Cavern Skulltulas #74 to #76 are also done [[gf1-442-1, gf1-443-1, gf1-442-2, gf1-207-1, gf1-207-2, gf1-496-1, checklist-skulltulas-15-1, checklist-skulltulas-15-3]].
