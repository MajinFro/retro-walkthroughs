## Chapter 21: Light Arrows, Gerudo Training Ground and the Big Poes

[MERMAID]

This chapter closes out the optional work before the final dungeon. You collect the Light Arrows in the Temple of Time [[gf1-325-3, gf1-391-3]], clear the Gerudo Training Ground for the Ice Arrows [[gf1-364-1]], hunt the ten Big Poes for the fourth Bottle [[gf1-374-3]], and beat the Running Man's time trial [[gf1-529-2]]. You also pick up one more Piece of Heart in Hyrule Field and the last four Gold Skulltulas, which brings the count to 100 / 100 [[gf3-86-2]].

### Before you start

Bring everything below. Each item is needed somewhere in this chapter.

- **Epona and the Fairy Bow** for Big Poes. They can only be captured as Adult Link on Epona with the Bow [[gf1-374-1]].
- **Empty Bottles.** Empty your Bottles before the Poe hunt [[gf3-152-3]].
- **Spare rupees for arrows.** Expect to spend a good amount [[ra-comments-88-10]].
- **Longshot and Silver Gauntlets** for the Zora's Domain and Zora's Fountain Skulltulas [[checklist-skulltulas-9-6, checklist-skulltulas-9-9]]. You also need the **Lens of Truth** in the Fountain passage [[gf1-306-3]].
- **Scarecrow's Song** for the Dodongo's Cavern and Fire Temple Skulltulas [[checklist-skulltulas-8-5, checklist-skulltulas-14-5]].
- **Iron Boots** for the Hyrule Field Heart Piece [[gf1-216-1]].
- **Hover Boots**, plus the items from earlier dungeons, for the Gerudo Training Ground [[ra-comments-137-12]].
- **Megaton Hammer, Song of Time and Bombs** for the Training Ground rooms [[gf1-369-2, gf1-370-4, gf1-372-3]].
- **Silver Gauntlets** are also needed to finish the Training Ground fully [[gf1-364-3]].

> [!TIP]
> Dodongo's Cavern and the Fire Temple each hold map and compass items, and the Fire Temple also holds a Boss Key. A single achievement tracks these, and revisiting Dodongo's Cavern as an adult is a common miss [[ra-comments-121-4, ra-comments-123-12, ra-comments-123-15]]. Confirm you hold the Fire Temple compass as well [[ra-comments-122-4]]. Where each one sits:
> - Dodongo's Cavern map: chest behind the wall blown open on the west side of the main room [[gf3-23-1]].
> - Dodongo's Cavern compass: chest in the Armos trap room [[gf1-73-3]].
> - Fire Temple map: chest reached by dropping over the side of the cage, or by shooting the silver eye [[checklist-sidequests-21-1]].
> - Fire Temple compass: chest at the end of the hall in the fire maze [[gf1-194-1]].
> - Fire Temple Boss Key: chest in the Goron's cell opened by the rusted switch [[gf1-202-1]].

### Temple of Time

Play the Prelude of Light to warp to the Temple of Time [[gf2-97-5, gf1-167-2]]. Enter as Adult Link after you have collected all the medallions [[gf4-33-14]]. A cutscene starts as soon as you walk in [[gf1-325-2]]. Zelda reveals herself and gives you the Light Arrows [[gf3-86-3]]. Ganondorf captures her seconds afterward, and she is revealed as the Seventh Sage, the Sage of Time [[gf1-325-2]]. The Light Arrows are the only weapon besides the Master Sword that can hurt Ganondorf, and they are also needed to break the seals in Ganondorf's Keep [[gf1-325-3, gf1-391-4]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** To the Rescue is based on a text ID and fires when a specific line is read. One player reports it popping just as Zelda was captured [[ra-comments-49-1, ra-comments-49-2]]. Do not skip or mash through the dialogue.

[ACHIEVEMENT:49286]

Zelda's capture finishes the scene [[ra-guide-18-1]]. Leave the Temple when control returns.

### Gerudo Training Ground

**Overview.** The Training Ground is a ring of obstacle courses and timed challenges around a central Treasure Room that holds the Ice Arrows [[gf1-364-1]]. The reward cannot be missed. At worst you come back later with the Silver Gauntlets [[checklist-sidequests-31-1]]. The route needs a minimum of seven Small Keys [[gf1-364-2]]. The dungeon holds nine keys in total, but only seven are needed on the correct path [[ra-comments-98-1]]. A chest cannot be permanently lost here [[ra-comments-122-8]]. Use the Small Keys at the end in the correct order, or you will need the Silver Gauntlets [[ra-comments-137-13]].

Warp songs do not work inside, so you must walk out when you finish [[gf1-373-6]]. The entrance to the Training Ground from the Fortress: Unable to verify from collected sources.

#### Entrance room

From the top of the stairs, turn around and shoot the golden eye switch above the entrance door with an arrow. A chest appears between two pillars [[gf1-365-1]]. This guide lists a bundle of arrows, while other guides list a Blue Rupee [[gf2-116-2, gf3-76-1]]. Open the chest between the opposite pair of pillars for a Blue Rupee [[gf1-365-2]].

There are three doors. The one directly opposite the entrance leads to the Treasure Chamber, which should be your last stop. The left and right doors lead into the Training Ground [[gf1-365-3]]. Take the right door first [[checklist-sidequests-31-2]].

#### Right wing: timed enemy room

Two Lizalfos and a Beamos wait here, and you are timed [[checklist-sidequests-31-2]]. One guide calls the Lizalfos "Dinolfos" and gives the limit as 1:30 [[gf1-372-2]]. Blow up the Beamos with a Bomb or Bombchus, then kill both enemies [[gf1-372-3]]. A chest with a Small Key appears [[gf1-372-4]]. If you attack a Lizalfos mid-swing it stops what it was doing, so mauling them works [[checklist-sidequests-31-2]].

#### Right wing: lava room (Sea of Fire)

This room has a huge lava pit with pillars. Falling in forces you to recollect all five Silver Rupees [[gf1-370-1]]. Two ways to handle it:

1. Jump to the front pillar, slightly to your left, and play the Song of Time. Two blue blocks appear [[checklist-sidequests-31-3]]. The same song works anywhere Navi flutters in the middle of the room, and one block is enough [[gf1-370-4]]. If you get stuck in this room, just play the Song of Time there [[ra-comments-122-10]].
2. Go left and step on the switch, which clears the circle of fire. Return to the block pillar and jump to the lowest pillar to take a key [[checklist-sidequests-31-3]]. Another account puts the key on a high ledge against the right wall, reached from the Blocks of Time [[gf1-370-5]].

Silver Rupees in this room:
- **Rupee 1:** Longshot to the target above the entrance doorway [[gf1-370-2]].
- **Rupee 2:** a platform to the left of the cliff edge. Jump down to it [[gf1-370-3]].
- **Rupees 3 and 4:** from the lower Block of Time, jump left, then jump out to the next platform [[gf1-370-6]].
- **Rupee 5:** hit the switch on the platform to the right to shut off the pillar of flames [[gf1-370-7]].

When all five are collected the left door unseals [[gf1-370-8]].

#### Right wing: underwater rupee room

Enter the side door with the torch beside it [[checklist-sidequests-31-4]]. Play the Song of Time in the middle of the room to make the floor blocks vanish. Equip the Iron Boots and Zora Tunic and sink into the pool [[gf1-371-1]]. Kill the Shell Blades with the Hookshot and watch for the Blade Trap [[gf1-371-2]]. With the Longshot you avoid swapping boots repeatedly [[checklist-sidequests-31-4]]. When all five Silver Rupees are collected, a chest with a Small Key appears above the pool [[gf1-371-3]].

#### Right wing: Hammer and fire room

Kill the Fire Slugs and Fire Keese. A door unseals and a chest with arrows appears [[gf1-369-1]]. Smash every perimeter statue with the Megaton Hammer. The two statues beside the arrow chest hide a switch and a golden eye [[gf1-369-2]]. Step on the switch to extinguish the flames around the central chest and take the Small Key [[gf1-369-2]]. Shoot the eye to make another chest drop [[checklist-sidequests-31-5]].

#### Right wing: rotating statue room

Stand on the round platform as it circles the room. Shoot every statue eye in one pass. A chest drops and the catwalk door unlocks [[checklist-sidequests-31-6]]. Use the Longshot to reach the chest, or just jump if you lack it, and take the Small Key [[gf1-368-2]].

The Totem Room has a door on its high right ledge. Its chest holds a Small Key [[gf1-368-1]].

#### Left wing: sand and rock room

Back in the entrance room, take the left door. You have one minute to kill two Stalfos. Their chest holds a key [[checklist-sidequests-31-7]].

#### Left wing: timed silver rupee room

A timer starts as you enter. A Wallmaster drops on you if you stand still too long in the middle section, and the ceiling has Hookshot targets [[checklist-sidequests-31-8]]. The rolling-boulder version of this challenge has a 1:30 limit and opens the exit door at the back when finished [[gf1-366-2]].

If your room has icicles and a Freezard, one guide gives these steps for the 1:30 timer [[gf3-108-3]]:
1. Move forward and Longshot up, fall to the rupee, then Longshot back up.
2. Take a right, then a left, and slash the icicles for rupee two.
3. Double back to the end and walk off the edge toward the rupee, grabbing the edge to get it.
4. Stand in front of the fire wall on your left and Longshot up to the rupee.
5. Take a right, then two lefts, and kill the Freezard for the last rupee.

#### Left wing: wolf room, catwalk and Like Likes

Four Wolfos attack as you enter. Kill them and a chest with arrows appears [[gf1-367-1]]. Use the Lens of Truth to see a hole in the wall above the fake door [[checklist-sidequests-31-9]]. Destroy the fake door with Bombs or the Hammer, look above it with the Lens, and Longshot to the target [[gf1-367-3]]. Step on the switch to unlock the door, then walk through the door ahead onto the catwalk above the statue room. The door there is already unlocked, so take the key from the chest [[checklist-sidequests-31-9]].

Return to the wolf room and push the silver gauntlet block with the Silver Gauntlets. Three Like Likes sit in three holes, so shoot them with arrows or sword spin [[checklist-sidequests-31-10]]. Afterward, use the Lens. The back-right hole's chest holds a key [[checklist-sidequests-31-10]]. Ignore the chest on the ledge, because it freezes you [[gf3-77-7]]. Another account adds that two chests (a Huge Rupee and a Blue Rupee) appear when the Like Likes die [[gf3-77-6]].

#### Treasure room and the Ice Arrows

Return to the entrance room and take the center door. The Treasure Room has nine locked doors, and the final key is hidden in the room itself [[gf1-373-2]]. Loot as you go [[checklist-sidequests-32-1]]:
- Chests to the right of the entrance hold Bombchus and a Bundle of Arrows [[gf1-373-4]].
- Two locked doors in on the left lead to a Purple Rupee chest [[gf2-90-6]].
- The next two doors lead to a Red Rupee chest [[gf2-90-7]].
- The next door leads to a chest of arrows [[gf2-90-8]].

For the hidden key, unlock the left door and climb the fence wall immediately to your left into the hidden room [[checklist-sidequests-32-1]]. In the first room on the left, use the Lens of Truth to spot the ceiling passage, climb the wire wall beside the door, and open the chest at the top [[gf1-373-3]]. Continue through the remaining locked doors to the center [[checklist-sidequests-32-1]]. Open the big chest at the end for the Ice Arrows [[gf4-33-11, gf1-390-4]].

The only real use of Ice Arrows noted in the sources is killing Like Likes in one hit [[checklist-sidequests-32-8]]. Walk out of the dungeon [[gf1-373-6]].

### Hyrule Field

🗺️ **Map:** [View Hyrule Field Map by thegamemaster8](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/5394-hyrule-field-map)

#### The Big Poe hunt

Big Poes only appear to Adult Link on Epona, and the hunt can be done any time once you have Epona and the Bow [[gf1-374-1, gf1-18-5]]. Regular Poes appear in the same places on foot but do not count [[gf1-374-4]]. Shoot a Big Poe with two arrows before it disappears [[gf4-22-3]]. It turns into a Wisp. Dismount, walk to it and choose to bottle it [[gf3-52-13, gf1-374-2]]. Bring empty Bottles [[gf3-152-3]].

**Tips for the shooting:**
- Steer a Poe away from walls by moving toward the direction you want it to turn away from [[checklist-sidequests-25-1]].
- Poes keep respawning along the same path while you stay put, and they respawn at the same spot [[ra-comments-88-6, ra-comments-87-2]]. A respawn can take 15 to 30 seconds [[ra-comments-87-5]].
- Make Epona walk instead of run, and approach at a good angle [[ra-comments-87-3]].
- Let her walk slowly and keep your aim pointed where the Poe will spawn [[ra-comments-88-4]].
- Time your shots to get two off before the Poe vanishes. A Poe is briefly invincible while flashing red, so you cannot fire rapidly [[ra-comments-88-7, ra-comments-88-8]].
- Shoot again the moment the first arrow lands, and try to spawn the Poes along a straight line [[ra-comments-88-9]].
- You may need to buy arrows again in Kakariko Village after every one or two Poes [[ra-comments-87-7]]. Another player finished with 40 arrows [[ra-comments-87-8]].
- Commenters call the last spawns very stubborn [[ra-comments-87-9, ra-comments-88-1, ra-comments-87-13]]. The game gives no hints about spawn spots [[ra-comments-87-12]].

The guides number the Poes differently. The list below is the Sidequest Walkthrough's order, from hardest to easiest, with other guides' notes alongside.

#### The Poe shop and the fourth Bottle

Deliver the bottled Poes to the Poe Shop in the former gatehouse at the Hyrule Castle drawbridge [[gf1-132-2, gf1-374-3]]. Each Big Poe is worth 100 points. A prize arrives at 1,000 points, which takes ten Poes [[gf2-59-6]]. The payoff is the fourth Bottle plus 500 rupees in total [[checklist-sidequests-25-12, gf2-60-5]].

#### Iron Boots grotto Piece of Heart (#29)

Sources describe the tree in different places. Heart Piece FAQ says it is to the right of the castle (to the left of the wall) and is the tree away from the others [[checklist-heartpieces-5-1]]. The Walkthroughs put the lone golden tree northwest of Lon Lon Ranch [[gf1-216-1, gf2-71-2, gf4-17-17]]. Follow whichever matches, as follows:

1. Drop a bomb at the base (the south side) of the tree to open a pit [[gf1-216-1]].
2. Drop in. A Tektite floats on the water, so kill it [[checklist-sidequests-17-5]].
3. Equip the Iron Boots and sink to the bottom. The Zora Tunic is not needed [[checklist-heartpieces-5-1]]. The Piece of Heart sits there [[gf1-216-1]].

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave the Poe hunt behind, confirm the fourth Bottle is in your inventory and the Piece of Heart is collected. Only then move on to the Running Man.

### Gerudo Valley

#### Wind Runner: the Running Man time trial

The race only unlocks once the bridge has been repaired [[ra-comments-102-1]]. As Adult Link, challenge the Running Man (who bought your Bunny Hood as a child) to a footrace from the Gerudo Valley Carpenter's Tent to the entrance to Kokiri Forest [[gf1-529-2]]. The exact challenge dialogue: Unable to verify from collected sources.

The Running Man always beats you by one second, and the race is effectively a time trial, since he keeps your best time [[gf1-529-2]]. Reach the Kokiri Forest entrance in 1:30 or less [[ra-comments-102-2]].

**Strategy:**
- Summon Epona before the challenge [[ra-guide-17-17]].
- Spend all your carrots before entering Hyrule Field, because carrots reset on a map change [[ra-comments-102-9]].
- One player finished in 1:19 on the first try using Epona and went around the fences in Hyrule Field instead of risking a failed jump [[ra-comments-102-5]].
- Another had Epona near the small fences outside the Gerudo Bridge area, close and positioned to get up quickly, and took the lower way [[ra-comments-102-7]].
- Galloping non-stop and taking the left path without fences does not work [[ra-comments-102-6]].
- Make sure Epona does not rear up [[ra-comments-102-8]].
- Backwalking or hessing also works on a controller that can do it [[ra-comments-102-3]]. Using Epona also works [[ra-comments-102-4]].
- Do not get stuck on the bridge or at the blank area at Gerudo. One player stuck on a tree root may have softlocked [[ra-comments-102-10, ra-comments-102-11]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** The time limit is 1:30 from the tent to the Kokiri Forest entrance. A slow run just means you retry [[ra-comments-102-2]].

### Zora's Domain

🗺️ **Map:** [View Zora's River/Domain Map by EverythingZelda](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/56-zoras-river-domain-map)

Enter from Zora's River, which opens off Hyrule Field at the 2:00 position [[gf1-95-1]].

**Gold Skulltula #47.** This one appears at night. Play the Sun's Song to change the time [[gf1-119-1]]. Climb the tunnel on the left of King Zora's throne room to the top of the frozen waterfall [[gf1-472-1]]. Walk to the edge of the waterfall, at the location where the Diving Game proprietor used to stand, look out and to the left, and Longshot the Skulltula on the wall [[checklist-skulltulas-9-6, gf3-62-5]].

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave Zora's Domain, confirm the night-time Skulltula is collected. The only entrance to the Fountain from here is the passage behind King Zora's throne, which opens after King Zora moves [[gf1-305-2, checklist-sidequests-12-5]].

### Zora's Fountain

Go through the opening behind King Zora's throne [[gf1-305-2]]. Wait for night. This is the final Skulltula [[gf3-86-2]].

**Gold Skulltula #50:**
1. Go to the south end of the Fountain where the Great Fairy cavern entrance is [[gf4-33-12]].
2. Lift the large gray rock in the middle of the cave mouth with the Silver Gauntlets [[gf1-306-1]].
3. Another rock sits underneath. Bomb it to open a pit [[gf1-306-2]].
4. Drop in and land at the bottom of a ramp winding up through the outer walls [[gf1-306-2]].
5. Use the Lens of Truth on the way up. Invisible Big Skulltulas line the ramp, and you can kill them with the Longshot [[gf1-306-3]].
6. At the top, kill the one visible Big Skulltula, then check the wall left of the ladder's top. The final Gold Skulltula is there [[gf1-306-4, gf4-33-12]].

That is 100 / 100 [[gf3-86-2]]. If a different Skulltula keeps you at 99, check Zora's Domain first [[ra-comments-110-1]].

### Dodongo's Cavern

**Gold Skulltula #42** (first floor). Enter as Adult Link [[checklist-skulltulas-8-5]]. From the Antechamber, ride out to the middle platform in front of the giant Dodongo Skull. Go right onto the next elevator, jump to the lower ledge without riding up, and take the southern darkened tunnel [[gf1-169-2]]. Baby Dodongos emerge from the ground in the hall [[gf1-170-1]].

When Navi flies up toward the ceiling and turns green, play the Scarecrow's Song to summon Pierre [[checklist-skulltulas-8-5, gf1-487-1]]. Hookshot to Pierre and kill the Gold Skulltula in the alcove [[gf1-170-2]]. Walk out or use Farore's Wind to return to the start [[gf1-170-2]].

### Fire Temple

**Gold Skulltula #73** (fifth-floor area). The Fire Temple is in the middle of Death Mountain Crater [[gf1-8-8]]. The Bolero of Fire warps you back [[gf2-153-2]]. Retrace the route to the boulder maze room by the same sequence you used the first time [[gf1-183-2, gf1-184-3, gf1-185-2]]. Then go to the upper part of the boulder maze room, to the northwestern exit [[checklist-skulltulas-14-5]]:
1. Play the Scarecrow's Song to make Pierre appear [[checklist-skulltulas-14-5]].
2. Longshot to Pierre, then to the nearby target [[checklist-skulltulas-14-5]].
3. Ride up and make your way to the fifth-floor area. The Skulltula is close by [[checklist-skulltulas-14-5]].

Other guides number this same climb differently and describe the setup. The scarecrow appears on a platform, and a rising platform follows [[gf1-190-4, gf1-190-5]]. A grate leads up to a Skulltula on the wall [[gf3-56-8]], and in the next room a Skulltula waits on the wall [[gf2-65-2]].

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave the Fire Temple, check the count. This is the last Skulltula, and the cumulative total should read 100 / 100 [[gf3-86-2]].
