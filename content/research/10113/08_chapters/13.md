[MERMAID]

This chapter covers the whole adult trading sequence for the Biggoron's Sword, plus the two Gerudo Valley Gold Skulltulas and the 50-token House of Skulltula reward. Once Link has Epona, the Biggoron Sword sidequest can be done at any time [[gf1-17-2]]. The sidequest also requires King Zora to be freed from the red ice and Epona to be available for the broken bridge [[ra-guide-11-7]]. You will run through Kakariko Village, the Lost Woods, Gerudo Valley, Death Mountain Trail, Zora's Domain and Lake Hylia, and finish back at the House of Skulltula [[checklist-sidequests-18-1, gf2-81-1, gf2-81-5, gf2-81-6]].

> [!TIP]
> **Check first:** Before you start, confirm that both Lost Woods Deku upgrades are done (A Horrible Face and Stocking Nuts; the Mask of Truth and Deku Nut upgrade pair) [[ra-comments-44-2, ra-comments-73-3]]. The Poacher's Saw blocks them later in this chapter [[ra-comments-44-3]]. Whether the Lost Woods Deku Nut upgrades can still be earned as an adult is unconfirmed: some players report success after the Forest Temple [[ra-comments-44-9]], while others report that the saw's flag locks them out [[ra-comments-44-3]]. Do not rely on the doubtful option. Get the upgrades before taking the saw. If either is still missing, put the Master Sword back in the Temple of Time to become Young Link and follow Chapter 6 (Stocking Nuts) or Chapter 10 (A Horrible Face) before you hand over the Odd Potion. One commenter says both Deku upgrades must be obtained before the whole succession of exchanges [[ra-comments-44-7]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Three items in this sequence are timed: the Odd Mushroom (3 minutes), the Eyeball Frog (3 minutes) and the Eye Drops (4 minutes) [[gf2-53-2, ra-guide-12-1, ra-guide-12-2]]. Do not use transportation songs such as the Serenade of Water or the Bolero of Fire while delivering the Eyeball Frog or the Eye Drops [[gf1-215-4]]. Decide your route before you accept each timed item.

### Kakariko Village (Part 1: Egg to Cojiro)

Make sure it is daytime and talk to the Cucco Lady, who stands near the pasture at the rear of town, close to the entrance to the Graveyard [[checklist-sidequests-18-1, gf1-356-1]]. As Adult Link she gives you the **[Pocket Egg]** [[gf2-160-2, gf4-16-11]]. Play the Sun's Song twice; the egg hatches into the Pocket Cucco [[gf2-51-2, gf4-16-12]]. Another guide only says to play the song until it hatches, so keep going if it has not hatched yet [[gf1-356-2]].

Check that it is daytime again. Enter the building at the bottom of the stairs near the middle of town, where Talon is asleep on the bed [[gf1-356-3]]. Stand beside him with the Pocket Cucco equipped on a C button and use it, and Talon wakes up [[gf2-51-3]].

Go back to the Cucco Lady and use the Pocket Cucco near her. She is pleased and gives you **[Cojiro]**, a blue Cucco [[gf1-356-4, gf2-51-3, checklist-sidequests-18-2]].

If you have not yet learned the Song of Storms, the windmill is close by, and one guide suggests learning it before you leave [[checklist-sidequests-18-3]]. You play A, Down, Up, A, Down, Up [[gf1-394-3]].

### Lost Woods (Part 1: Cojiro to Odd Mushroom)

🗺️ **Map:** [View Lost Woods Map by EverythingZelda](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/54-lost-woods-map)

Enter the Lost Woods and go left from the entrance to the area where the Skull Kid used to stand [[checklist-sidequests-18-4, gf4-16-13]]. A man sits beside the stump [[gf2-113-4]]. Equip Cojiro on a C button and use it while standing in front of him, then accept his offer. He gives you the **[Odd Mushroom]** [[gf2-53-2]].

The timer starts the moment you accept: you have three minutes to reach the Potion Shop in Kakariko Village [[gf2-53-2, gf1-357-2]]. Do not stop for anything else.

### Kakariko Village (Part 2: Odd Mushroom to Odd Potion)

Run to the Kakariko Potion Shop, enter it, and go through the back door, then enter the red house to find the hidden witch's shop [[gf3-44-4]]. Climb the stairs and enter the other door at the back to reach the witch [[gf2-53-3]].

Use the Odd Mushroom while standing in front of her, and she makes the **[Odd Potion]** (called the Odd Medicine in one guide) [[gf2-53-3, gf3-44-4]]. One player reports she accepted it only on the second try [[checklist-sidequests-18-5]]. From now on she also sells blue potions for 100 rupees, per the same report [[checklist-sidequests-18-5]].

### Lost Woods (Part 2: Odd Potion to Poacher's Saw)

> [!CAUTION]
> **Point of No Return:** Taking the Poacher's Saw makes the Lost Woods Deku upgrades unobtainable (A Horrible Face, Stocking Nuts) [[ra-comments-44-3, ra-comments-73-2]]. Finish both Deku upgrades before you hand over the Odd Potion.

Go back to where the man was sitting in the Lost Woods. A Kokiri girl now stands in his place [[checklist-sidequests-18-6, gf2-53-4]]. Give her the Odd Potion and she hands over the **[Poacher's Saw]** [[gf1-358-1, gf3-167-1]].

### Gerudo Valley

Ride Epona to Gerudo Valley and jump the broken bridge, then dismount and continue on foot [[checklist-sidequests-15-7]]. Alternatively the Longshot can pull you across [[gf1-278-1]]. The Master Carpenter waits on the far side [[gf1-278-2]].

Stand in front of the carpenter outside the tent, equip the Poacher's Saw on a C button and use it. He recognizes it as his own and gives you the **[Broken Goron's Sword]** [[gf1-358-2, gf2-113-7]].

While you are here, collect the two Gold Skulltulas on this side of the bridge. Make it night with the Sun's Song if needed [[gf2-87-2]].

- **Skulltula #62:** behind the tents, on the wall. Hookshot it [[checklist-skulltulas-12-3, gf1-278-3]].
- **Skulltula #63:** on the back of the stone pillar to the left. Hookshot it [[checklist-skulltulas-12-4, gf3-197-3]].

Both require Epona or the Longshot to cross the broken bridge [[checklist-skulltulas-12-3, checklist-skulltulas-12-4]].

If you want extra rupees, breaking all the rocks near the carpenter with the Megaton Hammer reveals a chest with a Purple Rupee [[gf2-87-3, gf3-75-1]].

### Death Mountain Trail (Part 1: Biggoron and the Prescription)

Make your way to Kakariko Village and through the northern gate onto Death Mountain Trail [[gf1-60-2]]. Dodge the boulders that crash down the path [[gf1-169-1]]. Near the top, the rocks fall and a climbable wall leads up to Biggoron [[gf2-97-6, gf2-114-2]].

Stand on the right side of the cliff to get his attention, then show him the Broken Goron's Sword [[gf1-359-1]]. He says he cannot repair it until he has eyedrops from King Zora, and gives you the **[Prescription]** [[gf2-81-4, gf3-61-5]].

The Piece of Heart above Dodongo's Cavern (#22) is already yours from Chapter 4, where you backflipped onto the cliff [[gf1-66-3]]. The two Death Mountain Trail Skulltulas behind red rocks need the Megaton Hammer and are collected in Chapter 15 [[gf1-206-1, gf1-206-2]].

### Zora's Domain

🗺️ **Map:** [View Zora's River/Domain Map by EverythingZelda](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/56-zoras-river-domain-map)

King Zora is already free from the Ice Cavern chapter [[ra-guide-11-7]]. Stand on the platform in front of him and use the Prescription. He gives you the **[Eyeball Frog]**, which has to be delivered to the scientist in Lakeside Laboratory within three minutes [[gf2-81-5, gf1-360-5, ra-guide-12-1]].

Route to Lake Hylia inside the three minutes, as the sidequest guide runs it [[checklist-sidequests-19-1]]:

1. Leave Zora's Domain for Zora's River and take the portal on the left into the Lost Woods.
2. In the Lost Woods go straight, left, right, straight and left. This brings you to the Lost Woods bean plant you planted two lefts from the entrance in Chapter 10.
3. Ride the plant and jump off onto the bridge, then keep rolling toward Lake Hylia. The guide arrived with about 40 seconds left.

Do not play the Serenade of Water to shortcut [[gf1-215-4]]. The frozen-waterfall Skulltula here is collected in Chapter 21 [[gf1-472-1]].

### Lake Hylia

Go to the Lakeside Laboratory and enter. Use the frog next to the old man [[gf2-81-6, gf2-114-4]]. He mistakes it for his dinner, but then gives you the **[Eye Drops]** (World's Finest Eye Drops) [[gf1-361-1, gf4-17-2]].

You now have four minutes to climb back to Biggoron [[gf1-362-1, ra-guide-12-2]]. Step outside, get on Epona, and ride across Hyrule Field to Kakariko Village. Dismount, enter the village and take Death Mountain Trail to the summit [[gf1-362-2]]. Do not play the Bolero of Fire [[gf1-215-4]].

The laboratory crate Skulltula needs the Iron Boots and comes in Chapter 16 [[checklist-skulltulas-10-5]].

### Death Mountain Trail (Part 2: Claim Check and the Sword)

Climb the wall and use the Eye Drops in front of Biggoron to get the **[Claim Check]** [[gf2-81-7, gf4-17-3]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Save the game before turning in the Claim Check [[ra-comments-96-7]]. Hardcore turns off save states, so use the game's own save. Mash A, not B, through the dialogue. One player reports that mashing B leaves a small window in which a sword slash cancels the trade [[ra-comments-96-4]], and another confirms mashing A earned the achievement in hardcore [[ra-comments-96-6]].

The sword takes three days. Play the Sun's Song six times to force three days to pass [[gf1-363-1, ra-guide-12-3]]. Another guide says about five times [[gf2-82-1]], so keep going until three days have passed [[gf4-17-4]]. Then show Biggoron the Claim Check and he gives you the **[Biggoron's Sword]** [[gf1-363-2, gf3-62-7]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48975]

The Biggoron's Sword deals twice the damage of the Master Sword, but it needs both hands, so you cannot use a shield with it equipped [[gf1-363-3, gf3-62-8]]. It does not break [[gf1-400-3]].

Some players report problems: the sword showing in the equipment screen but being unselectable [[ra-comments-96-2]], and the achievement failing after buying the knife from Midgoron [[ra-comments-96-3]]. A commenter says the trade flag can be repaired by purchasing the Giant's Knife [[ra-comments-96-5]]. The Giant's Knife is sold by a giant Goron in Goron City [[gf2-61-6]].

### House of Skulltula

With 56 tokens, claim the 50-token reward at the House of Skulltula. The cursed family's house is the first house on the right as you enter from the main gate [[checklist-skulltulas-2-1]]. The rewards come in order: 10 tokens for the Adult's Wallet, 20 for the Stone of Agony, 30 for the Giant's Wallet, 40 for Bombchus and 50 for a Piece of Heart [[gf1-451-3, checklist-skulltulas-2-2, checklist-skulltulas-2-3, checklist-skulltulas-2-4, checklist-skulltulas-2-5]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Collect the 40-token Bombchus reward before the 50-token reward. One player did not get the achievement after talking to the 50 NPC first [[ra-comments-108-3]]. Another says that, because of battery protection, the achievement only triggers if the reward event is repeated [[ra-comments-108-1]]. Save before collecting the reward [[ra-comments-108-2, ra-comments-108-4]].

Go inside and speak to the man in the upper right corner. He gives you the **[Piece of Heart]** [[gf2-60-6, gf3-134-1, gf4-17-10]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48247]

The Piece of Heart is collected as Adult Link without trouble [[gf2-60-6]]. This is Piece of Heart #32 [[checklist-sidequests-2-28]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave:** Make sure the Biggoron's Sword is in your equipment, that you have collected the Gerudo Valley tokens (#62 and #63), and that you have claimed every House of Skulltula reward up to 50 [[gf1-363-2, checklist-skulltulas-12-3, gf1-451-3]].
