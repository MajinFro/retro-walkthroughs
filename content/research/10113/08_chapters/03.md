## Chapter 3: Kakariko Village, the Graveyard and Lon Lon Ranch

[MERMAID]

This chapter clears out the first batch of side content you can reach as Young Link. Kakariko Village is in the northeast corner of Hyrule, at the foot of Death Mountain, and Lon Lon Ranch sits in the middle of Hyrule Field [[gf1-8-7, gf1-8-10]]. You will earn six achievements here: Catch Me If You Can, Cuccos' Revenge, Shield Raider, Super Cucco Catching, You Make Me Feel So Good and A Man's Best Friend. You will also pick up two Bottles, the Sun's Song, Epona's Song, the free Hylian Shield, three Pieces of Heart, the Adult's Wallet and eight Gold Skulltula tokens. There is no boss in this chapter.

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning (Shield Raider):** Do NOT buy a Hylian Shield at the Bazaar before you pull the flowered gravestone in the Graveyard. Buying it replaces the shield chest's contents with rupees, and the achievement can be permanently missed [[ra-comments-64-1, checklist-sidequests-9-3, gf1-401-2]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Warning (Dampe's Piece of Heart):** If Dampe digs up the Piece of Heart and you leave the Graveyard without picking it up, it is lost forever and you get 50 rupees instead [[ra-guide-20-1, ra-comments-101-1]]. Pick it up before you leave.

### Kakariko Village

Walk to the village in the northeast corner of Hyrule Field [[gf1-8-7]]. Make sure it is daytime before you start the Cucco hunt below [[gf2-156-16]].

#### The Cucco Hunt (Catch Me If You Can)

The Cucco Lady (Anju) wants her seven lost Cuccos back [[ra-guide-6-2]]. Pick each Cucco up and put it into her fenced pen, which is in the south of town near the arch leading to the Graveyard [[gf1-50-2, gf1-50-4, gf4-7-8]]. The seven are:

1. **Cucco 1:** Run through the arch at the front of town. A Cucco is running around right there [[gf1-50-3]].
2. **Cucco 2:** Another Cucco is very near the Cucco Lady's pasture, close to the arch to the Graveyard in the southeast corner [[gf1-50-4]].
3. **Cucco 3:** This one is inside a crate near the center of town, across from the stairs to the upper level [[gf1-50-5]].
4. **Cucco 4:** This one is on the north side of town, on the upper level, near the gate to Death Mountain [[gf1-50-6]].
5. **Cucco 5:** This Cucco is trapped on a fenced ledge near the large tree. Climb the stairs of the partially built house and jump off the edge toward it while holding another Cucco. The held Cucco floats you out to the ledge [[gf1-50-7]].
6. **Cucco 6:** Climb the long stairset east of the pasture, run to the edge and aim at the fenced-off elevated platform. A Cucco is running around in the small grotto behind the building [[gf1-50-8]].
7. **Cucco 7:** Drop back into the grotto and climb the tall ladder all the way up. Work around behind the Windmill and grab the last Cucco [[gf1-50-9]].

Three of the Cuccos can only be reached by gliding off cliffs and over fences with other Cuccos [[gf1-383-5]]. Once all seven are in the pen, talk to the Cucco Lady. She rewards you with an Empty Bottle [[gf2-22-4, gf3-20-3, gf3-117-5]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48241]

> [!TIP]
> Keep an empty Bottle on hand. The Graveyard bugs and the ranch cow's milk both need one [[gf1-52-1, ra-guide-6-8]]. You get another Bottle from Talon at Lon Lon Ranch [[gf1-383-4]].

#### Cuccos' Revenge

While you are among the Cuccos, hit one with your sword over and over until the whole flock turns on you [[ra-guide-6-3, ra-comments-66-1]]. This works only as Young Link, so do it now [[ra-comments-66-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48242]

#### The Death Mountain Gate and the Keaton Mask

Go to the northern edge of the village and talk to the guard by the gate. He refuses to let you through until you show him Zelda's Letter, which opens the way to Death Mountain Trail [[gf1-58-2, gf2-23-3]]. The guard asks you to bring him a Keaton Mask from the Hyrule Castle Market, which starts the Happy Mask trading sequence and unlocks the Happy Mask side quest [[gf1-58-3, ra-guide-6-9]].

You can do the mask trade whenever it suits you. Borrow the Keaton Mask for 10 rupees, wear it and talk to the guard, who pays 15 rupees. Then pay the shop owner the 10 rupees [[gf1-59-3, gf1-350-1, gf3-28-6]]. Do not go up Death Mountain Trail yet. The rest of this chapter stays in the village area.

#### Impa's House Piece of Heart (Optional Now)

Piece of Heart: glide with a Cucco from the narrow wooden beam of the unfinished house. When the beam bends to the right, leap off and steer right for the balcony of Impa's House. Throw the Cucco aside and enter the crack in the building [[gf1-51-1]].

If you skip it, you can come back later. Kaepora Gaebora's ride can drop you on Impa's roof, and as an adult the Hookshot reaches it [[gf1-430-1, gf1-88-2]].

### Kakariko Graveyard

Leave through the arch in the southeast corner of town [[gf1-50-4]].

#### Gold Skulltula #29: The Bug Patch

Catch a bug from under a rock with a Bottle [[gf4-8-1]]. Pass under the arch and follow the stone path to the left side of the graveyard, where there is a patch of soft earth. Stand near the patch and release the bug [[gf1-52-1]]. Quickly recapture one bug in the Bottle [[checklist-sidequests-9-10]]. After a few seconds, a Gold Skulltula appears. Kill it and take the token (**Graveyard #29**) [[gf1-52-1, checklist-skulltulas-6-8, gf1-464-1]].

#### The Royal Family's Tomb and the Sun's Song

Head to the largest tombstone. Stand on the large Triforce symbol in front of it, take out your Ocarina and play Zelda's Lullaby. The tombstone explodes and reveals a pit. Drop down [[gf1-52-2, checklist-sidequests-9-2]]. Zelda's Lullaby is Left, Up, Right, Left, Up, Right [[gf2-21-5]].

Run down the ramp and circle the large bone pit to draw all the Keese. Shoot them with the Slingshot or cut them down, and the door at the top of the stairs unlocks [[gf1-53-1]]. Go through the door, pass the Redeads, go through the next opening and read the inscription on the wall [[gf3-234-4]]. Reading it teaches the Sun's Song [[gf1-53-2]].

The Sun's Song is Right, Down, Up, Right, Down, Up. It flips the time between noon and midnight [[gf1-393-3]].

> [!TIP]
> You can only reach the next two sections at night. If it is daytime, play the Sun's Song now. Midnight is fine for the shield and the Redead grave, but not for Dampe's tour [[gf1-393-3, checklist-sidequests-9-3, gf1-56-3]].

#### The Free Hylian Shield (Shield Raider)

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Reminder: do not buy a Hylian Shield from the Bazaar before this step [[ra-comments-64-1]].

At night, go to the front row of gravestones and find the grave with flowers in front of it. Stand behind it, grab it and pull back to reveal a pit. Drop down [[gf1-54-1, ra-guide-6-1, gf3-21-3]]. Do not pull other gravestones in this row, because they hold Poes that turn invisible when Z-targeted [[gf1-54-2]].

Inside, run forward and open the chest to find the **Hylian Shield**, whose metal is impervious to flame attacks [[gf1-54-3, gf2-22-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48226]

If you already bought the shield, players report the achievement can still unlock if a Like-Like steals the shield and you recover it. This works only as child Link and was reported to fail in the Fire Temple but succeed in the Bottom of the Well [[ra-comments-64-2, ra-comments-64-3, ra-comments-64-5, ra-comments-64-6]]. One player could not make it work and used a new save file [[ra-comments-64-4]]. Treat that route as unconfirmed.

Later, with bombs, the wall inside this chamber opens to a Fairy Fountain [[gf3-27-4]].

#### The Redead Grave (Piece of Heart)

Still at night, go to the rear row of gravestones and the far right. Counting from the right, pull aside the fourth gravestone to reveal another pit. Drop in [[gf1-55-1]]. Another guide describes this grave as the second gravestone on the left when facing away from the Royal Family's Tomb [[gf3-21-4]]. The sources word the location differently, so if you uncover a Poe instead of a pit, step back and try the next candidate [[gf1-54-2]]. You can only pull graves at night [[gf3-21-4]].

Inside, attack the Redead repeatedly. The faster you hit it, the less it can attack. If it grabs you, tap buttons and rotate the stick to break free [[gf1-55-2]]. Once it is permanently defeated, stand on the steps and play the Sun's Song. A chest appears with a **Piece of Heart** [[gf1-55-3, gf2-22-1, checklist-heartpieces-3-7]]. Open it and return to the Graveyard [[gf1-55-3]].

#### Dampe's Gravedigging Tour (Piece of Heart)

Dampe's game runs only in the first part of the night, not at midnight, and the Sun's Song will not start it. Leave to Hyrule Field and wait for nightfall (the wolf's howl), then return [[gf1-56-3, gf1-432-3, checklist-sidequests-14-5]]. Bring about 100 rupees [[checklist-heartpieces-3-6]]. Your starting wallet holds only 99, so that is the most you can carry before the Adult's Wallet [[gf1-450-2]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Warning:** When Dampe digs up the Piece of Heart, pick it up before you leave the Graveyard. Otherwise it is lost forever [[ra-comments-101-1, ra-guide-20-1]].

Dampe walks a circular route along the path. If you stand directly on a dirt patch in his path, he stops in front of it. Talk to him and pay 10 rupees to dig [[gf1-56-1]]. The Piece of Heart is in one of twelve patches, and most other patches give at least some rupees [[gf1-56-2, gf1-432-2]]. Only ask him to dig on the soil patches, not the bare path [[checklist-heartpieces-6-1]].

The Piece of Heart changes patches every time you leave the zone. If you leave to restock rupees, start your search over [[gf1-56-2]]. Dampe appears at random after nightfall, so wait for him [[ra-comments-101-5]]. One player needed about 20 digs [[ra-comments-101-4]].

When it comes up, grab the Piece of Heart before you leave.

### Kakariko Village at Night (Gold Skulltulas #22-#26)

Return to the village at night. These five Skulltulas need night, and you need your Slingshot [[checklist-skulltulas-6-1, gf1-57-1]]. If it is day, play the Sun's Song [[gf1-393-3]].

1. **Skulltula #22 (tree):** Roll into the tree near the village entrance and kill the Skulltula that falls out [[checklist-skulltulas-6-1, gf1-462-1, gf3-20-4]].
2. **Skulltula #23 (House of Skulltula window):** It sits on the window of the House of Skulltula, beneath the window on its left side [[checklist-skulltulas-6-2, gf1-57-2, gf3-20-5]]. Another guide places it on the backside window [[gf1-462-2]], so check both sides.
3. **Skulltula #24 (unfinished building):** At the Unfinished House, on the pile of bricks. Kill it with the Slingshot before jumping up for the token, because the sword hurts you at close range [[checklist-skulltulas-6-3, gf1-57-1, gf1-462-3]].
4. **Skulltula #25 (north house):** At the north end of town, the house on the left of the Death Mountain gate has a Skulltula on it. Smash the pots to get at it if it is hidden [[checklist-skulltulas-6-4, gf1-57-5, gf1-463-2]].
5. **Skulltula #26 (lookout tower ladder):** On the ladder of the tall lookout tower. Shoot it with two Slingshot shots, then climb up for the token [[checklist-skulltulas-6-5, gf3-20-8, gf2-21-3]].

Together with the Graveyard token and the five you already had, that makes 11 tokens. The Skulltula on Impa's roof needs the Hookshot, so skip it for now [[checklist-skulltulas-6-6]].

### Lon Lon Ranch

Lon Lon Ranch is in the middle of Hyrule Field [[gf1-8-10]]. Enter at night first, for the Skulltulas [[checklist-sidequests-9-12]]. If it is daytime, play the Sun's Song [[gf1-393-3]].

#### Gold Skulltulas #14 and #15 (Night)

1. **Skulltula #14 (tree):** Roll into the lone tree beside the ranch house, just left of the arch leading to the pasture. Kill the Skulltula that falls and take the token [[checklist-skulltulas-5-3, gf1-44-2, gf3-18-2]].
2. **Skulltula #15 (feeding shelter):** Circle around to the back of the horse corral and kill the Skulltula on the back of the feeding shelter. Watch out for Guay birds [[checklist-skulltulas-5-4, gf1-44-3, gf3-18-5]]. If the token is out of reach, you may need to backflip into it [[gf1-461-1]].

That brings you to 13 tokens. The window Skulltula and the rock-wall Skulltula need the Boomerang, so leave them for later [[checklist-skulltulas-5-5, checklist-skulltulas-5-6]].

#### Daytime: Talon's Super Cucco Game

Play the Sun's Song to make it day [[checklist-sidequests-9-12, gf1-393-3]]. Enter the house to the left of the entrance, where Talon runs a game [[gf4-6-11]].

For 10 rupees, you must find three Super Cuccos within 30 seconds [[gf2-103-1]]. The Super Cuccos look the same as the others [[gf3-18-1]].

The easiest method is to throw all the Cuccos into the corner behind the long counter Talon sits on, then "select" Cuccos by picking them up [[gf1-42-3, ra-comments-59-1]]. A green arrow shows over a Cucco you have already picked up. You can shield-drop a Cucco to release it faster [[ra-guide-6-7]].

Find all three and Talon gives you a Bottle filled with Lon Lon Milk [[gf1-42-2, gf2-103-2]]. Once the milk is gone, you have an Empty Bottle [[gf2-17-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48113]

Players report this achievement sometimes unlocks along with the milk achievement. The cause is unconfirmed [[ra-comments-59-2, ra-comments-59-3, ra-comments-60-1]].

#### Epona's Song

Talk to Malon in the middle of the horse pen [[checklist-sidequests-9-15]]. Talk to her repeatedly. When she mentions her mother, take out the Ocarina and she teaches you Epona's Song [[gf1-43-1, gf3-234-2, gf2-17-4]]. Either Ocarina works [[gf1-353-2]].

The song is Up, Left, Right, Up, Left, Right [[gf1-392-3]]. It will later let you ride Epona and gets free milk from any cow [[gf1-43-2]].

#### The Crawlspace (Piece of Heart)

Go to the small building at the back of the ranch (the southwest corner) and enter it [[checklist-heartpieces-3-3, gf4-6-10]]. Pull the crates aside to open the crawlspace in the corner [[gf4-6-10, gf3-18-3]]. The details in one guide: pull the upper-right box, the one beside the cow, down toward the door once, then go to the other side and push it down. Then pull the box in the upper-left corner to the right [[gf2-17-3]]. Crawl through the tunnel to an empty nest with a **Piece of Heart** [[gf1-44-1, gf1-429-1]].

#### Milk from a Cow (You Make Me Feel So Good)

Make sure you have an empty Bottle. Go to a cow in the barn on the ranch [[ra-guide-6-8, gf3-18-3]]. Stand next to the cow and play Epona's Song with the empty Bottle [[gf2-17-5, ra-guide-6-8]]. It fills the Bottle with free Lon Lon Milk [[gf3-18-4, gf3-172-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48162]

The same cow trick works with any cow, including one in a hidden pit on Death Mountain Trail [[ra-comments-60-4, ra-comments-60-5, ra-comments-60-6, ra-comments-60-7, gf1-86-1]]. One player's report said that on the OoT 1.0 ROM this also unlocked Super Cucco Catching. That is unconfirmed [[ra-comments-60-1]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Lon Lon Ranch:** As an adult you will return here for Ingo's races and Epona, which requires Epona's Song [[gf1-413-3, gf1-133-3]]. Malon's obstacle course comes much later and depends on waking Talon in the Biggoron Sword sequence [[gf1-166-1, checklist-sidequests-24-8]].

### House of Skulltula

Return to Kakariko Village. The cursed family's house is the first house on the right as you enter from the main gate [[checklist-skulltulas-2-1, gf4-8-3]]. You now hold 13 tokens, more than the 10 needed. Enter, turn right and talk to the man there. He gives you the **Adult's Wallet**, which raises your capacity from 99 to 200 rupees [[gf1-58-1, gf2-23-1, gf3-20-1, gf1-450-2]].
