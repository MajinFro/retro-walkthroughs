## Chapter 22: Lon Lon Ranch, Dampe's Race and the Fishing Pond

[MERMAID]

This chapter cleans up a run of adult-Link side content: Malon's obstacle course on Epona, Dampe's second race, the three fishing-pond achievements, the Lakeside Laboratory dive, and the House of Skulltula's final reward. Hearts are tallied here too, since the Laboratory dive gives the last Piece of Heart you need for 20 hearts [[gf3-123-1, ra-guide-20-2]]. The route runs Lon Lon Ranch, then Kakariko Graveyard, then the Fishing Pond and Lake Hylia, then back to the House of Skulltula.

> [!NOTE]
> **Plenty of Hearts check:** Dampe's gravedigging Piece of Heart was a Young Link task in Chapter 3. If it was dug up and left behind, it is lost forever and turns into 50 rupees [[ra-guide-20-1, ra-comments-101-1]]. Count your Pieces of Heart against the checkpoints before you rely on this chapter's total.

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning (Mischievous Hero):** Do not save and quit, or reset, between hooking the owner's hat and sinking it in the pond. After a reload the owner stays hatless but the achievement does not count [[ra-comments-113-7, ra-comments-113-8, ra-comments-3-9]].

### Lon Lon Ranch

Malon's course needs the Biggoron Sword trading sequence's wake-up of Talon: once he is back at the Ranch, Malon lets you run a timed course on Epona [[gf1-166-1]]. Commenters note it is done after Malon takes over the Ranch [[ra-comments-85-7]].

1. Ride Epona into the Ranch (call her with Epona's Song if needed) [[gf1-354-2]].
2. Talk to Malon while mounted. Talking to her twice gets the offer of the obstacle course; the time to beat is 50 seconds [[gf2-52-5, checklist-sidequests-24-8]].

#### Course technique

- Hug the inside wall and use a carrot just before each hurdle. The pair of very low hurdles a third of the way round needs only one carrot for both [[gf1-166-3]].
- Epona clears the smallest fences without a boost, so save carrots for the big gates and straightaways [[ra-comments-85-3, checklist-sidequests-24-9]].
- Recover carrots on the long empty stretch before the last hurdle, then burn them all on the last leg of the second lap [[gf1-166-3]]. Carrots come back about every six seconds [[checklist-sidequests-24-9]].
- To jump a gate Epona must be dead center and moving fast. Boost a beat or two before the fence, not right at it. If she does not jump, you boosted too late [[ra-comments-85-1, ra-comments-85-2, ra-comments-85-4, ra-comments-85-5, ra-comments-85-6]].
- Finish in 0:49 or better; one commenter got 48.20 [[gf2-109-2, ra-comments-85-10]]. Aim for 50 seconds or less [[checklist-sidequests-24-9]].

Malon says the present is too big to carry and sends it to your house. It is a cow living in your Kokiri Forest home [[gf2-52-5, ra-comments-85-8]].

### Kakariko Graveyard

Dampe's second race pays Piece of Heart #31 [[gf1-245-1]]. The Longshot is required for the last gap [[checklist-heartpieces-5-4]].

1. Enter the Graveyard and find Dampe's grave: the only gravestone with flowers on the left aisle. Pull it backward and drop into the pit [[gf1-244-2]].
2. Dampe challenges you and starts without notice [[gf1-135-1]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** You must finish in 1:00 or less. A commenter's 59-second run is reported as winning the prize but not the achievement, and 1:00:20 was over the limit, so aim for a comfortable margin [[ra-comments-84-1, ra-comments-84-2]].

#### Race route

🗺️ **Map:** [View Dampe's Race Map by DrgNyt](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/7461-dampes-race-map)

Roll the whole way; it is slightly faster [[gf1-245-1]]. Wear the Goron Tunic, hug inside corners, and avoid fireballs [[checklist-heartpieces-6-5]]. Listen for his fire throw before rolling into a corner [[checklist-sidequests-20-3]]. Being hit once does not ruin a run [[ra-comments-84-8]]. The route [[gf1-245-2]]:

1. Run forward, dodging flames. Turn right at the hall's end, tight.
2. Turn left at the next corner, wide.
3. Run between the pair of flames along the left side of the gap.
4. Turn left, then right immediately, wide.
5. Turn right, left, then left tight. Take another tight turn and go through the door.
6. Right, right, wide left. Run along the left or right side of the tunnel, enter the wide room, and take the door on the right.
7. Left, wide right, through another door. Run to the wall, turn right, then tight left.
8. Jump onto the ledge on your left and hug the left wall. Wide left, then tight left into the tall room with the pit.
9. Longshot to a torch near the top and pull yourself up. One commenter found lining this up the hardest part; another missed the first shot and still made 58:75 [[ra-comments-84-5, ra-comments-84-10]].

Go through the door on the far side and climb the stairs to the Windmill [[gf1-245-3]]. Dampe gives the Piece of Heart if you beat the minute [[gf1-245-1]].

#### Plenty of Hearts: other Graveyard hearts

If any earlier Graveyard hearts are outstanding: the bean-platform crate [[gf1-433-1]], the Redead grave with the Sun's Song chest [[gf1-432-1]], and Dampe's digging. Dampe's piece is in one of twelve patches and moves each time you leave; stand on a patch so he stops and pay 10 rupees [[gf1-56-1, gf1-56-2]]. Bring about 100 rupees and dig only on soil patches [[checklist-heartpieces-6-1]]. Collect the piece before leaving [[ra-guide-20-1]].

### Fishing Pond

🗺️ **Map:** [View Fishing Pond Map by TLS64](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/51-fishing-pond-map)

Do these three in order: big fish first without the sinking lure, then the loach, then the hat. The pond costs 20 rupees per round [[gf2-37-2]]. The door to the pond is on the small island north of the lake shore [[gf1-98-5]].

#### 1. Expert Angler: the Golden Scale

Do not use the sinking lure for this; one source says it disqualifies you [[checklist-heartpieces-6-4]]. The sinking lure only becomes available after a 15 lb. catch [[gf3-236-1]].

- The large fish sits near the submerged log on the left and the reeds on the right [[gf1-217-1]]. The big one usually stays underwater near the big log, and you may need to catch the small fish swimming with it first [[checklist-heartpieces-6-4]].
- Walk onto the log to its halfway point, cast, and when it bites pull down to the bottom right and reel [[ra-comments-112-5]]. Z-target a fish, cast with B, and hold A while pulling back to reel [[gf1-515-1]]. Alternating back-left and back-right helps [[gf1-217-3]].
- Fish that break free vanish. If the pond runs low, leave and re-enter to respawn them [[gf1-413-2]].

> [!NOTE]
> The needed weight is unconfirmed. Sources say 15 lb. [[gf1-217-3]], but commenters report 13 lb worked and 12 lb did not, and one says it must beat your child record by 10 lb [[ra-comments-112-11, ra-comments-112-12, ra-comments-112-8]]. Keep fishing until the owner hands over the Golden Scale. A 19 lb catch from under the log worked [[ra-comments-112-3, ra-comments-112-4]].

Take the catch to the owner for the Golden Scale [[gf2-107-2]].

#### 2. Accomplished Angler: the Hylian Loach

The Hylian Loach is a rare fish that appears only on some visits [[gf3-236-3]]. One commenter says fish as an adult first [[ra-comments-3-7]].

Reported spawn method (counts are commenter reports and not fully consistent, so verify by looking):
- The spawn is tied to a counter of paid fishing rounds, not entering the area. It carries over across resets and ages [[ra-comments-117-1, ra-comments-117-4, ra-comments-117-5, ra-comments-117-6]].
- Bring at least 80 rupees. Talk to the owner, say yes, then quit. Repeat three times, leave, re-enter at once, and pay a fourth time [[ra-comments-115-1, ra-comments-115-2, ra-comments-115-3, ra-comments-115-4]]. Another says it spawns every 4th time you pay [[ra-comments-116-5, ra-comments-116-13]]. Some commenters disagree on the cycle [[ra-comments-114-3, ra-comments-114-4]], and one found the method failed for them [[ra-comments-116-11]].
- Check by looking: it is on the left, under the lily pads, shadowier than normal fish [[ra-comments-115-3, ra-comments-118-5, ra-comments-118-7]]. Use C-Up to check [[ra-comments-116-6]]. If you cannot see it, pay and leave, repeating until it appears. Once found, do not pay again [[ra-comments-117-9, ra-comments-117-10]].
- Playing the Sun's Song outside and re-entering is another reported approach [[ra-guide-17-15]].

Catching it:
- It is only catchable at or swimming to the lily pads [[ra-comments-116-8, ra-comments-118-2]]. It ignores bait until it heads there [[ra-comments-120-1]].
- Let the two nearby fish bite, then do nothing so they release and leave [[ra-comments-114-12, ra-comments-115-5, ra-comments-115-6]]. Do not hook another fish or you startle it [[ra-comments-119-13]].
- Stand on the log, cast into the lily pads, and lure with soft tugs [[ra-comments-118-3]]. A regular lure works; the sinking lure can help [[ra-comments-116-16]]. To get the sinking lure, take it and talk to the owner so he allows it [[gf3-236-2]].
- When it bites, press A at once to snag it, then hold A and the stick left or right [[ra-comments-115-12, ra-comments-115-13]]. Tilt toward the fish's head and ease off when it jumps or kicks up dust [[ra-comments-119-9]]. Keep the rod pulled left while holding the button [[ra-comments-119-14]].
- If it escapes, save inside the Water Temple beforehand so you can reset and retry nearby [[ra-comments-117-7, ra-comments-117-11, ra-comments-115-8]].

After landing it, talk to the owner; the achievement pops [[ra-comments-115-9, ra-comments-114-8]].

#### 3. Mischievous Hero: the owner's hat

As an adult the owner wears a hat you can snare with a perfect cast [[gf3-236-4]].

1. Stand close in front of the owner, face to face. Cast so the lure bypasses him and hits the wall [[ra-comments-113-1]].
2. Hold A to reel in so the line attaches to the hat [[ra-comments-113-2]]. The hitbox and invisible walls around him are odd [[ra-comments-113-10]].
3. Walk far into the pond, cast, and let the hat sink. The line does not cast far with the hat attached [[ra-comments-113-3]].
4. Throwing it back unhooks and sinks it, unlocking the dialogue and achievement [[ra-comments-113-4]]. You are fined 50 rupees [[gf3-236-4, ra-comments-113-9]].

Comments conflict on whether the sinking lure works [[ra-comments-113-5, ra-comments-113-6]]; do not use it here. Do not save or reset between hooking and sinking [[ra-comments-113-7]].

### Lake Hylia

With the Golden Scale, enter the Lakeside Laboratory, jump in the pool, and dive to the very bottom (9 meters), then talk to the scientist for Piece of Heart #30 [[gf1-218-1, gf3-42-1, checklist-sidequests-17-4]]. Iron Boots will not work for this dive [[checklist-heartpieces-5-2]]. Heart pieces now total 36 with 8 Heart Containers, making 20 hearts [[gf3-123-1, ra-guide-20-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:49287]

Remaining Lake Hylia tokens: crate at the pool's bottom (Iron Boots, then Hookshot) [[gf1-218-2]], the dead-tree top (Longshot at night) [[gf1-243-2]], and the soil mound by the Laboratory [[gf3-30-11]].

### House of Skulltula

Reaching 100 tokens requires every dungeon except Ganon's Castle [[ra-comments-109-8]]. The last one reported is in Zora's Fountain: lift the white boulder with the Silver Gauntlets at night and climb the cave [[gf3-232-1, gf4-33-12]]. Save after each token, since a crash loses unsaved ones [[ra-comments-109-2, ra-comments-109-3]]. The world map marks a finished zone with a Skulltula icon [[ra-comments-109-1, ra-comments-110-9]].

Talk to the man in the House of Skulltula for the Huge Rupee (200 rupees) [[checklist-skulltulas-2-7, gf2-97-7]]. It repeats by leaving and re-entering [[gf1-451-4]]. If the reward does not trigger, restart the emulator, make sure achievements are loaded, and repeat the event [[ra-comments-110-5]].
