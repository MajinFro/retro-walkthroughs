## Chapter 7: Zora's River and Zora's Domain

[MERMAID]

This chapter takes young Link up Zora's River and into Zora's Domain. You buy Magic Beans, collect two Pieces of Heart with the Cuccos, win two more from the Musical Frogs, light the Zora's Domain torches for a fifth, and win the Silver Scale in the Diving Game [[gf1-95-4, gf1-174-4, gf1-97-2, gf1-441-1]]. Two Gold Skulltulas fall to you here: the tree at the entrance and the ladder by the waterfall at night [[gf1-470-1, gf1-470-2]].

> [!TIP]
> **Preparation:** Bring Bombs, the Slingshot, Deku Sticks, Din's Fire if you have it, and the Ocarina with Zelda's Lullaby, Epona's Song, the Sun's Song, Saria's Song, the Song of Time and the Song of Storms [[gf1-95-3, gf1-470-2, gf1-97-1, gf1-174-2]]. Rupees matter too: the Diving Game costs rupees to enter, and every Magic Bean costs 10 more than the last [[gf2-36-1, gf1-385-2]].

### Zora's River

🗺️ **Map:** [View Zora's River/Domain Map by EverythingZelda](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/56-zoras-river-domain-map)

#### Entrance: the tree and the boulders

1. Before bombing the rocks, talk to the owl, then roll into the tree to the left of the stone wall near the entrance. A Gold Skulltula falls out and lands near you [[checklist-sidequests-10-9, gf1-470-1]].
2. Kill it and take its token [[gf1-95-2]].
3. Bomb the stones blocking the path upstream [[gf1-95-3]].

#### The Bean Man

Follow the path to the man sitting near the iron gate. He is the Magic Bean salesman [[gf1-95-4, gf2-159-8]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48346]

Buy a Magic Bean from him [[ra-guide-8-8, gf2-34-2]]. He has ten beans in total. The first costs 10 rupees and each next one costs 10 more, up to 100 for the tenth. All ten cost 550 rupees [[gf1-385-2, gf2-34-3]]. Keep buying until you run out of money, and come back later for the rest [[gf1-95-4]].

Plant a bean in the patch of soft earth next to him [[gf1-95-5, gf3-173-3]]. Beans must be planted as young Link, and they only grow fully once you are an adult [[gf3-173-2, gf1-385-1]]. This patch holds no Gold Skulltula, so there is no need to drop a bug in it [[gf1-527-2]].

> [!TIP]
> You can still collect Gold Skulltulas from soft-soil holes after planting beans in them [[checklist-sidequests-11-1]].

#### Rock circle, Cucco and secret caves

Near the entrance, in the corner where a Cucco wanders, is a circle of rocks. Play the Song of Storms near it to reveal a Secret Cave with two Business Scrubs, one selling a Green Potion and one a Red Potion, both for 40 rupees [[gf3-41-8, gf3-238-11]]. You will use this Cucco for the Pieces of Heart below [[gf4-12-1]].

On the strip of land above, climbing the ladder leads to a Secret Cave with a small chest holding a Red Rupee. A large boulder surrounded by small rocks stands nearby. Bomb it to reveal a Fairy Fountain [[gf3-29-6, gf2-34-4, gf2-34-5, gf3-29-7]].

#### Musical Frogs: rupees

Walk out on the log upstream and stand on the white circle at its end. Take out the Ocarina [[gf1-95-6, gf4-21-9]]. Play Zelda's Lullaby, Epona's Song, the Sun's Song and Saria's Song. One guide says the frogs pay 50 rupees per song [[gf1-95-6]]. Another says each song earns a Purple Rupee [[gf2-35-3, gf3-30-4]]. Unable to verify from collected sources which payout is correct. The Song of Time also pays out [[gf1-124-3, gf2-78-5]].

#### Heart Piece: the pillar halfway up the river

1. Grab the Cucco near the circle of stones, hop across to the ledge on the right, go forward and to the left, and jump up around the ledges [[gf4-12-1]].
2. Use the Cucco to float to the ledge just before the Heart Piece. Drop the Cucco and jump to the platform with the Heart Piece [[gf4-12-1]].
3. Another route is to climb the ladder, grab another Cucco at the top, and glide to the tall pillar from the cliff with the Gossip Stone [[gf3-128-2, gf1-95-7]]. The Boomerang as Young Link or the Hover Boots as Adult Link also work [[gf1-438-1]].

#### Heart Piece: the lone platform and the high ledge

- Near the entrance to Zora's Domain, go to the high ledge, turn around and look back downstream. A platform holds a Piece of Heart. Glide out to it with a Cucco [[gf1-440-1, checklist-sidequests-11-2]].
- Another Piece of Heart sits on a lone ledge sticking out of the water, near the Gossip Stone platform. Glide to it with a Cucco [[checklist-sidequests-11-3]].
- Another way is to drop to the platform below and grab a Cucco, then make your way to the end of the river. Float down to the ledge below with the Heart Piece [[gf4-12-2]].
- You can also carry a Cucco to the waterfall at the end, turn around, climb the land bridge nearest the heart, and jump to it [[checklist-heartpieces-4-2]].

Behind the waterfall a ladder leads from the water to a hidden alcove with another Piece of Heart [[gf3-38-1]]. One guide says you can reach it with the Cucco [[gf3-38-1]].

#### Gold Skulltula on the waterfall ladder (night)

If it is daytime, play the Sun's Song. Time passes normally here, so you will not warp back to the entrance [[gf1-96-2]]. Drop down near the waterfall to the ladder rising from the river bed. Shoot the Gold Skulltula on it with the Slingshot, then climb the ladder for the token [[gf1-96-2, gf4-12-3, gf3-30-3]].

#### Musical Frogs: Song of Storms and the minigame

Return to the log as young Link. Play the Song of Storms to receive a Piece of Heart [[gf1-174-1, gf2-146-29, gf2-78-4]]. Once you have played all the basic songs for them, the frogs challenge you to a fly-eating minigame [[gf1-174-2]].

The lowest frog is matched with the A Button and the other four match the C-Pad directions [[gf1-174-3]]. From left to right, the frogs are A, Left, Down, Up, Right [[gf2-112-3]]. Enter this sequence quickly [[gf1-174-4, ra-comments-94-6]]:

A, C-Left, C-Right, C-Down, C-Left, C-Right, C-Down, A, C-Down, A, C-Down, C-Right, C-Left, A


Setting the C-Buttons to the D-pad is easier than using the analog stick. Controller drift can prevent you from hitting some notes [[ra-comments-94-5, ra-comments-94-4]]. You now hold both frog Pieces of Heart [[gf1-246-1]].

#### Entering Zora's Domain

Go around the zigzagging path and stand on the plaque before the waterfall. Play Zelda's Lullaby and the waterfall retracts. Jump to the doorway and go through [[gf1-96-3, gf3-30-5]].

Gold Skulltulas that need the Hookshot appear in this river in the future timeline. Unable to verify from collected sources whether any belong to your current route.

### Zora's Domain

🗺️ **Map:** [View Zora's River/Domain Map by EverythingZelda](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/56-zoras-river-domain-map)

#### Heart Piece: the torches

Light a Deku Stick on the lit torch near the shop. Use Deku Sticks and run quickly, because the torches do not stay lit for long [[checklist-heartpieces-4-3, gf3-30-7]]. One guide gives this order [[checklist-heartpieces-6-2]]:

1. Light the Deku Stick.
2. Light the torch near the circle of stones.
3. Run back and light the torch near the shop.
4. Light the torch near the circle of stones again.
5. Light the torches in the waterfall.

Another guide says the stick can be kept lit and rolled under the waterfall to light the last two torches [[checklist-sidequests-11-5]]. Din's Fire also lights the two in the alcove behind the waterfall [[gf1-97-1]]. When every torch is lit at once, a chest appears behind the waterfall with a Piece of Heart [[gf1-441-1, gf2-36-3]].

#### Diving Game and the Silver Scale

Go through the tunnel left of King Zora's throne room to reach the Zora running the Diving Game [[gf1-412-1, ra-guide-8-9]]. The game costs 20 rupees, though one guide says 30 [[gf2-36-1, checklist-sidequests-11-6]]. He throws rupees off the waterfall. Position yourself above them and hold A to dive [[gf1-514-1]]. Collect all five Blue Rupees, then return to the Zora [[gf2-36-1, gf1-514-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48347]

He gives you the Silver Scale, which doubles your dive depth [[gf1-97-2, gf2-36-2]].

#### Other things in the Domain

- Fish swimming in the shallow water near the waterfall can be caught in an empty Bottle [[gf1-102-3]].
- Playing the Song of Storms on the flat unsubmerged rock reveals a Fairy Fountain [[gf3-238-13, gf3-52-7]].
- The dirt hole here is the only one without a Skulltula [[checklist-skulltulas-9-1]].
- Diving into the tunnel south of the waterfall warps to Lake Hylia [[gf1-97-3]].
- Swimming to the bottom of the deep pool to the right of the waterfall at the entrance leads to Lost Woods [[gf1-124-1]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Zora's Domain:** make sure you have the Magic Bean, both Cucco Hearts, both frog Hearts, the torch Heart and the Silver Scale [[gf1-246-1, gf1-97-2, gf1-441-1]]. You can leave by climbing to the path to the front entrance, returning to Zora's River and riding it downstream to Hyrule Field [[gf1-119-3]].
