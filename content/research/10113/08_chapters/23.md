## Chapter 23: The Gossip Stone Tour

[MERMAID]

This chapter is one long errand: as Young Link wearing the Mask of Truth, listen to all 32 Gossip Stones in a single play session for **Lifestyle and Stones**. [[gf1-352-1, ra-comments-80-9, ra-comments-81-26]] The stones are spread across Hyrule, and the warp songs are the quickest way between clusters. [[ra-guide-19-2]] Nothing in this chapter is permanently lost, but the achievement does not tolerate interruptions. [[ra-comments-82-3]]

### Preparation and Rules of the Tour

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Do not reset, exit the emulator or save-quit until the achievement pops. The achievement description says it has to be done in one session, and one player reports the stone progress resets every time the game is started. [[ra-comments-80-9, ra-comments-82-3, ra-comments-80-1, ra-comments-82-4]]

- **Keep the mask on its C button.** A commenter lists clicking off the game screen, hitting a stone with a sword, saving, or unequipping the item from the C button as things that may have stopped the achievement for them. [[ra-comments-3-10]]
- **Stay Young Link.** Only Young Link can wear the Mask of Truth. [[gf1-352-1]] The Master Sword in the Temple of Time returns you to adulthood, so leave it alone while you are there. [[gf1-175-4, gf1-257-4]]
- **Get the mask first.** The Mask of Truth comes from finishing the Happy Mask trading sequence, which ends by paying 50 rupees for the Bunny Hood and then borrowing the mask. [[gf1-124-2, gf1-349-2]] One commenter was unsure whether the mask must be worn, and their achievement did not unlock. [[ra-comments-80-2]] Wear it at every stone to be safe.
- **Warp songs.** You need the warp songs, and they work for Young Link: Minuet to the Sacred Forest Meadow, Prelude to the Temple of Time, Nocturne to the Graveyard ledge, Serenade to Lake Hylia, Requiem to the Desert Colossus. [[ra-guide-19-2, gf1-395-2, gf1-397-4, gf1-397-2, gf1-396-2, gf1-396-4]]
- **Items to carry.** You will want Bombs (the castle grotto, Goron City's rock maze, the stone circle near Gerudo Valley), Din's Fire, the Song of Storms, the Silver Scale and a Cucco source. [[gf1-173-3, gf1-468-2, gf1-457-2, gf1-90-2, gf1-412-2]]
- **Which stones to talk to.** Don't skip one. One player failed twice because a single stone was missed. [[ra-comments-80-6]] The underground grotto stones are identical, so only one of them needs to be heard. [[ra-comments-80-8]] Bring a guide for the hidden ones, since some are hard to find. [[ra-comments-80-10]]

### Kokiri Forest

🗺️ **Map:** [View Kokiri Forest Map by kingofthisgame](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/1099-kokiri-forest-map)

Begin in Kokiri Forest. The first two stones are by the Great Deku Tree. Mido's archway to the Deku Tree's clearing is on the east side of the forest. [[gf1-21-3, gf1-24-1, ra-comments-81-5]]

1. **Stone #1, Deku Tree's Left Stone.** Walk to the clearing and listen to the stone there. [[ra-comments-81-5]]
2. **Stone #2, Deku Tree's Right Stone.** This is the hidden one. In the original version it sits behind one of the Deku Tree's roots. Climb the root just before it and sidehop across. [[ra-comments-81-2, ra-comments-80-4]] If you struggle, head to the northwest stone and circle around. [[ra-comments-81-3]] A commenter says the full route is to climb the tree and go all the way around, and that it is easy to miss. [[ra-comments-82-1]]

Unable to verify from collected sources: the exact coordinates of either stone beyond the descriptions above.

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave: confirm the mask is still on its C button, then climb the vines behind Mido's House to the hollow log that leads to the Lost Woods. [[gf1-23-2, ra-comments-3-10]]

### Lost Woods

🗺️ **Map:** [View Lost Woods Map by EverythingZelda](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/54-lost-woods-map)

3. **Stone #3, Kokiri Clifftop Stone.** This stone is just outside the Lost Woods. [[ra-comments-81-6]] The Song of Storms near the stone outside the entrance also opens a cave with a chest holding a Red Rupee, which is optional. [[gf3-44-2]]
4. **Stone #4, Stick Scrub's Stone.** Enter the Lost Woods and go left. Take the first two lefts, drop down the cliff, and the stone sits next to the Deku Scrub who sells the Deku Stick upgrade. [[ra-comments-81-7, checklist-sidequests-7-7, ra-guide-4-1]] Defeat the Scrub by reflecting its Deku Nut or with a jump attack if you need to get close. [[gf1-23-3]]

When you are done, return to the Lost Woods entrance for the Minuet.

### Sacred Forest Meadow

🗺️ **Map:** [View Sacred Forest Meadow Map by R351D3NT3V1L4](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/5354-sacred-forest-meadow-map)

Play the Minuet of Forest. It carries Young or Adult Link to the Sacred Forest Meadow. [[gf1-395-2]] Three stones are here: Saria's Stone, Above Maze Right Stone and Above Maze Left Stone. [[ra-comments-81-8]]

5. **Stone #5, Saria's Stone.** Saria's courtyard is at the top of the stairs, past the hedge maze. [[gf1-47-3, ra-comments-81-8]]
6. **Stones #6 and #7, Above Maze Right and Left.** The hedge-maze ladder at the end puts you on top of the hedges, where the two stones are. [[gf3-44-10, ra-comments-81-8]]

Unable to verify from collected sources: which stone is on which side, and the exact ground route as Young Link from the Minuet arrival point.

### Temple of Time

Play the Prelude of Light to warp to the Temple of Time. [[gf1-397-4]] Four stones sit around here: Blinky, Pinky, Inky and Clyde. [[ra-comments-81-9]]

8. **Stones #8 to #11.** Listen to all four. Unable to verify from collected sources: which stone stands where around the plaza.

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave: do not touch the Master Sword. Drawing it returns Link to adulthood, and the mask only works for Young Link. [[gf1-175-4, gf1-352-1]]

### Hyrule Castle

🗺️ **Map:** [View Hyrule Palace Map by ThE_bLImP](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/52-hyrule-palace-map)

🗺️ **Map:** [View Path to Zelda Map by TLS64](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/55-path-to-zelda-map)

Walk to the Castle Garden. Two stones are here, and a third is in a grotto by the moat. [[ra-comments-81-10, ra-comments-81-11]]

12. **Stone #12, Stone Above The Vines.** Climb the ivy wall by Malon and run forward past the gossip stone to the top of the large marble arch. Listen to that stone. [[gf1-38-3, gf2-15-1, ra-comments-81-10]] Drop down on the far side of the arch. [[gf1-38-3]]
13. **Stone #13, Stone Above The Stone Wall.** Run up the hill at the third graphical break near its center toward the flowers, then forward and slightly left to the brick wall. Climb it. At the top, run forward past another gossip stone. [[gf1-38-4, gf1-38-3]]
14. **Stone #14, Stone Beneath The Royal Tree.** Jump off the cliff past that stone into the moat, swim to the end where it breaks left, and climb up to the stone platform. [[gf1-38-5]] Stand beneath the tree at the corner of the fence and play the Song of Storms to open a hole. [[gf1-173-3, ra-comments-81-11]] Drop in. There are three bombable walls, one in front of you, one left (slightly behind where you land) and one right. [[gf1-173-3, gf3-238-2]] The room with the bugs, pots and rocks holds a Gossip Stone. [[gf3-238-2]] Unable to verify from collected sources: which wall hides that room.

Do not let a guard spot you in the Garden. A spotted Link is thrown back outside the gate. [[gf1-90-3, gf1-173-4]]

### Kakariko Graveyard

Play the Nocturne of Shadow. It takes Young or Adult Link to the high ledge of the Graveyard. [[gf1-397-2]]

15. **Stone #15, Non-Grave Stone.** Listen to the stone on that ledge. [[ra-comments-81-12]] Unable to verify from collected sources: its position on the ledge.

Young Link can do little else here. Run west and jump the fence to the lower Graveyard and Kakariko Village. [[gf1-246-2]]

### Dodongo's Cavern

From Kakariko, take the guard's gate to Death Mountain Trail and walk to the cavern. [[gf1-58-2, gf1-62-5]] If you ever need to reopen the entrance, throw a Bomb Flower so it lands on or near the boulder, and throw one near the cracked wall inside. [[gf1-66-2, gf1-68-1]]

16. **Stone #16, Delicious Stone.** In the main chamber, go to the back right and listen to the stone. [[ra-comments-81-13]]

### Goron City

Climb the trail, keep going right at the large post with the red flag, and enter the archway into Goron City. [[gf1-62-5]]

17. **Stones #17 and #18, Unbreakable Stone and Medigoron's Stone.** Both are in Goron City. [[ra-comments-81-14]] The boulder maze on the top floor has a Gossip Stone in it. Bomb through the rocks to the end of the maze (or follow right, left, left, right). [[checklist-sidequests-14-3, gf3-186-2, gf1-468-2]] Unable to verify from collected sources: which stone is Medigoron's.

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** An earlier Goron City achievement is voided if you destroy the three entrance boulders to the left of the tightrope, so leave them alone. [[ra-comments-68-3, ra-guide-8-2]]

### Death Mountain Trail

18. **Stone #19, Biggoron's Stone.** Exit by the top of Goron City and climb the trail. [[gf1-66-1, ra-comments-81-15]] Boulders crash down the path. Dodge them and crouch under the Hylian Shield with R when lava shadows appear. [[gf1-169-1, gf1-86-2]] Unable to verify from collected sources: the stone's position on the climb.

### Death Mountain Crater

19. **Stone #20, Antisocial Stone.** Enter through the cave to the right of Kaepora Gaebora at the summit. [[gf1-87-2]] Go right inside the Crater. [[ra-comments-81-16]]

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Without the Goron Tunic, Young Link's time in the Crater is limited. He dies if time runs out, and fairies do not help. Walk straight to the stone and leave. [[gf1-175-1, gf1-435-2]]

### Lake Hylia

Play the Serenade of Water. It sends Young or Adult Link to Lake Hylia. [[gf1-396-2]] Three stones are here, easy to forget. [[ra-comments-80-11, ra-comments-81-1]]

20. **Stone #21, Southeast Drowned Stone.** Head southeast. [[ra-comments-81-17]]
21. **Stones #22 and #23, Southwest Drowned and Little Bridge.** Go clockwise from there. [[ra-comments-81-18]] The two stones are at the very far edge of the water, next to the wall. [[ra-comments-80-3]] Tip: pressing B while swimming makes you faster. [[ra-comments-80-15]]

Take the underwater tunnel in the submerged ruins near the shore, east of the laboratory, back to Zora's Domain. The Silver Scale is required. [[gf1-102-1, gf1-412-2]]

### Zora's Domain

🗺️ **Map:** [View Zora's River/Domain Map by EverythingZelda](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/56-zoras-river-domain-map)

22. **Stone #24, Mweep Stone.** Take the Zora's Domain warp. [[ra-comments-81-19]] Unable to verify from collected sources: its exact location in the Domain.

From King Zora's throne room, take the passage behind the throne to Zora's Fountain. [[gf1-102-4, gf1-305-2]]

### Zora's Fountain

23. **Stones #25 and #26, Jabu's Stone and Farore's Stone.** Both are in the Fountain. [[ra-comments-81-20]] Farore's Stone may be near the Great Fairy island behind Jabu-Jabu, where the weak wall by the white rock is bombed. [[gf3-233-3]] Unable to verify from collected sources: exact positions of either stone.

When done, return to the Domain, go up to the front entrance and out to Zora's River. [[gf1-119-3]]

### Zora's River

24. **Stone #27, Sleepless Waterfall Stone.** Go down to the river for this one. [[ra-comments-81-21]] Unable to verify from collected sources: its exact position.
25. **Stones #28 and #29, Grotto Stone and River Grottos Stone.** Grab the bottom Cucco at the start of the river and glide to the upper grottos area, following the hidden left river path near the fork. [[ra-comments-81-22, checklist-heartpieces-4-2]] Enter the generic grotto on the left for the Grotto Stone, then exit for the River Grottos Stone. [[ra-comments-81-23]] The open-air cave on the high platform holds a Gossip Stone and a Red Rupee chest, and may be the generic grotto. [[gf3-238-12, gf3-29-6]]

Follow the river downstream back to Hyrule Field. [[gf1-119-3]]

### Hyrule Field

🗺️ **Map:** [View Hyrule Field Map by thegamemaster8](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/5394-hyrule-field-map)

26. **Stone #30, Webbing Stone.** Go to the circle of rocks outside the Gerudo Valley entrance and bomb the center. [[ra-comments-81-24, checklist-skulltulas-5-2]] Drop down, burn the webs with Din's Fire, and find the stone. [[gf1-457-2, gf3-237-9]] Unable to verify from collected sources: whether the stone can be heard without burning the webs.

### Gerudo Valley

Head west through the canyon into the valley. [[gf1-100-3]]

27. **Stone #31, Valley Waterfall Stone.** Jump down to the ledge at the bottom of the valley. [[ra-comments-81-25]] Grab the Cucco near the bridge and glide down. [[gf1-101-2, gf4-9-8]]

### Desert Colossus

28. **Stone #32, Sandstone.** Play the Requiem of Spirit, then walk south from where you arrive. [[ra-comments-81-26, gf1-396-4]] The achievement should pop when you hear it. [[ra-comments-81-26]]
