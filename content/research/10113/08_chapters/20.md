## Chapter 20: Desert Colossus and the Spirit Temple

[MERMAID]

This chapter takes you across the Haunted Wasteland to the Desert Colossus and then through both halves of the Spirit Temple. Gerudo's Fortress and the Bottom of the Well must be finished first [[gf1-19-2]]. The temple is the only dungeon that needs both Young and Adult Link, with a small West Wing for the child and a much larger East Wing for the adult [[gf1-294-1]]. Along the way you learn Nayru's Love, the Requiem of Spirit (the last song), the Silver Gauntlets, the Mirror Shield, the Spirit Medallion and the final Gold Skulltulas and Heart Piece in the desert [[gf1-291-3, gf1-303-1, gf1-323-3, checklist-sidequests-2-36]].

> [!TIP]
> **Bring before you go:**
> - Longshot, to cross the sand gap and reach crates, trees and ledges [[gf1-289-1]].
> - Lens of Truth, to see the Poe guide [[gf1-289-7]].
> - Bombs, for the cracked wall [[gf1-290-2]].
> - Din's Fire, which the child half uses constantly [[gf1-298-1]].
> - An empty bottle of bugs for the soft soil patch [[gf1-292-2]].
> - A Magic Bean for that patch [[gf1-292-4]].
> - Hover Boots, Fire Arrows and the Mirror Shield's eventual partner, the Biggoron Sword, if you have it [[gf1-310-3, gf1-314-1, gf1-321-2]].

### Haunted Wasteland

You begin between a pair of flags. Run forward past the crate to a wide gap of sinking sand and Longshot the crate on the far side [[gf1-289-1]]. Then head for the next flag to the northwest and keep going from flag to flag. If you stray too far from a flag you are sent back to the start [[gf1-289-2]]. If you cannot see the next flag, play the Song of Storms and the lightning lights up the horizon briefly [[gf1-289-3]].

After the second flag a sign points to the Carpet Merchant, who only sells Bombchus [[gf1-289-4]]. The price is 200 rupees [[gf2-91-1]]. Skip him unless you need Bombchus.

The flags end at a large stone structure ringed with red flags. Enter the opening near its south side and drop down to the bottom [[gf1-289-5]]. Kill the Gold Skulltula inside and take its token with the Longshot [[gf1-289-5, checklist-skulltulas-18-3]]. This is Skulltula #92 and it sits in plain sight [[checklist-skulltulas-18-3]]. Light both torches with Din's Fire or Fire Arrows to make a chest with a Purple Rupee appear [[gf1-289-6]]. Climb out by the ladder on the south side [[gf1-289-5]].

Climb the ramp on the north side to the top of the structure. Face north, turn on the Lens of Truth and a ghost appears [[gf1-289-7]]. Keep the Lens on and follow the ghost. Leevers start surfacing when you are close, so roll out of their way. When you reach a pair of flags, run toward the single flag to the west to enter the Desert Colossus [[gf1-289-7]].

### Desert Colossus (first visit, as Adult Link)

The Colossus is crawling with Leevers. They can only appear in sand, so stand on solid ground to be safe [[gf1-290-1]].

#### Great Fairy's Fountain: Nayru's Love

Go to the pair of trees on your right as you enter from the Wasteland. Bomb the cracked wall between them and walk through the doorway [[gf1-290-2, ra-guide-16-4]]. Stand on the Triforce symbol and play Zelda's Lullaby. The Great Fairy gives you Nayru's Love [[gf1-290-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:49186]

Nayru's Love makes you invulnerable, costs a quarter of a full magic bar and is very useful against the temple's Iron Knuckles [[gf1-290-4, gf1-391-1]]. Leave the fountain.

#### The oasis and Skulltula #94

Head to the dried-up oasis in the south. Stand on the pile of flat rocks and play the Song of Storms to fill it and make a Fairy Fountain [[gf1-291-1]]. This is optional. At night, a Gold Skulltula (#94) sits near the top of one of the palm trees, and you take its token with the Longshot [[gf1-291-2, checklist-skulltulas-18-5]]. Standing in the shallows lets you fire the Longshot undisturbed [[gf1-291-2]]. If it is not night yet, wait or come back after the bean ride below. You can also pick it up on your later adult visit.

#### Entering the temple and the Requiem of Spirit

Go to the huge statue at the west end and enter the small door at its base [[gf1-291-3]]. There is nothing to do inside yet, so turn around and walk back outside [[gf1-292-1]]. Sheik appears and teaches the Requiem of Spirit, which warps you to the Colossus [[gf1-292-1, gf3-235-8]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:49187]

The Requiem of Spirit is the sixth warp song, and it is the 12th and last song. Musician needs every other song first, so if it does not pop, one of the earlier songs is still missing [[gf1-396-3, gf2-153-11]]. Players also report odd timing on this achievement. One got it on learning the Requiem, and another had it fail until the Sun's Song was learned last, after which a developer update fixed it [[ra-comments-100-2, ra-comments-100-5]]. If it fails to award, a developer advised restarting the emulator to load the updated achievement [[ra-comments-100-6]].

### Temple of Time (switch to Young Link)

Play the Prelude of Light to warp to the Temple of Time [[gf1-292-1]]. Return the Master Sword to its pedestal to become Young Link [[gf1-172-3]]. Do nothing else here for now.

Play the Requiem of Spirit to warp back to the Colossus as a child [[gf1-292-1]]. The Gerudo will not let Young Link cross the fortress bridge, so this song is the way in [[gf1-396-4]].

### Desert Colossus (as Young Link)

Go to the entrance of the Spirit Temple. To the left of the door is the game's last patch of soft soil [[gf1-292-2]]. Drop a Bottled Bug into it and a Gold Skulltula (#93) appears. Kill it and take the token. You do not need to recapture the bugs afterwards [[gf1-292-2, checklist-skulltulas-18-4]]. If you need more bugs, look under a rock north of the oasis [[gf1-292-3]].

Plant your final Magic Bean in the soil, then go into the temple [[gf1-292-4]]. This bean is the key to the last Skulltula and Heart Piece later [[gf1-307-1]].

### Spirit Temple, West Wing (Young Link)

Fire enemies are common here, so switch back to the Hylian Shield if you have been using the Deku Shield [[gf1-295-1]].

#### Entrance and Nabooru

Run up the stairs, watching out for the pair of pots that fly at you. At the top, go left. A Gerudo Thief is trying to crawl into a hole that is too small for her. This is Nabooru [[gf1-295-1, gf1-295-2]]. Talk to her. When she asks what you are up to, say "Nothing, really." The rest of her questions do not matter. She asks for your help against Ganondorf and moves aside, and you crawl through the crawlspace [[gf1-295-3, gf1-295-4]].

#### Armos Knight Room

Run forward, dodge the Blade Trap and kill the Fire Keese. Climb the stairs and go right to find an Armos Knight circled by a Blade Trap. Drop a bomb beside it and hit it with your sword just before the bomb goes off. Bombchus also work if you mess up [[gf1-296-1]]. Then use the Boomerang on the Keese on the wall above the second crawlspace [[gf1-296-2]]. When every enemy is dead the doors unseal, so take the door to the left of the crawlspace [[gf1-296-3]].

#### Stalfos Room

A Stalfos attacks. Dodge its swings and hit it right after it attacks. Nayru's Love before the fight makes it safer [[gf1-297-1]]. Another option is to push it off the spinning platform by slashing it when it is near the edge [[gf3-109-10]].

Next, stun the Green Bubble in the pit with the Boomerang and kill it with the Slingshot. Then aim the Boomerang just left of the bridge so it curves into the Crystal Switch behind the grating. The bridge falls [[gf1-297-2, gf1-297-3]]. Cross the bridge and open the chest for a Blue Rupee [[gf2-92-1]]. Then take the door on the far side.

#### Anubis Pit Room

An Anubis flies at you here. Only fire can kill it, and since you have no Fire Arrows as a child, use Din's Fire. The fire trap from the crystal switch also works but is slow [[gf1-298-1]]. Stay away from it so it cannot shove you into the pit. When it dies, the door on the right unseals [[gf1-298-2]].

#### Lower Silver Rupee Chamber

A Wallmaster is in this room. Run in small circles until it drops and kill it, then clear the Fire Keese. You cannot collect rupees with them around [[gf1-298-2]]. Collect the five Silver Rupees from the grating. Two are near the bottom, two near the top and one is in the opposite corner [[gf1-298-3]]. Do not climb near the Gold Skulltula on the left of the grating, because it damages you even through the fence [[gf1-298-3]]. Collecting all five makes a bridge appear [[gf1-298-3]].

Kill the Keese before crossing so they cannot knock you off. On the far side, turn around. The Gold Skulltula (#96) on the right of the grating is killed and collected with the Boomerang [[gf1-298-4, checklist-skulltulas-19-1]]. Then light both torches near the wall with Din's Fire. A chest appears on the other side of the room containing a Small Key. Cross back, open it and exit through the door [[gf1-298-5]].

#### Armos Knight Room (second pass) and Small Hall

You come out of the right-hand door. To its right is a crawlspace, so go through it [[gf1-299-1]]. Open the locked door on the other side of the Small Hall [[gf1-299-1, gf1-298-5]].

#### Sun Cavern

A tiled wall is on your right. Boomerang the two Skullwalltulas, then climb to the top. Turn around and Boomerang the Gold Skulltula (#97) behind you [[gf1-299-2, checklist-skulltulas-19-2]]. Run into the large cavern, where two Lizalfos appear. Kill the Skulltulas on the wall too. Find the rock higher on the wall that has a beam of light around it and blow it open with a Bombchu. If you have none, hit the crystal switch on the wall for a chest of Bombchus [[gf1-299-3, gf3-80-10]]. The sunlight now hits the sun panel on the floor and unseals the door on the opposite wall [[gf1-299-3]].

#### Colossus Chamber and the Dungeon Map

Push the statue near the door off the cliff. It lands on a button and unseals a door on an upper level [[gf1-300-1]]. Jump down to the floor. Light the pair of torches in front of the statue with Din's Fire and open the chest for the **[Dungeon Map]** [[gf1-300-2]]. Then return to the east side, climb the tiles, go up the stairs, up another flight, and through the door on the left [[gf1-300-2]].

#### West Stairwell and Upper Silver Rupee Chamber

Run up the stairs and through the door [[gf1-301-1]]. This room has Blade Traps, low walls and a sun panel puzzle. Run through the gap on the right, rolling past the Blade Trap. Pull out the stone block with the sun panel and push it into the beam of light on the northwest side. The south door opens [[gf1-301-2]]. Kill the Beamos near the blocks if it bothers you [[gf1-301-3]].

You still need the Small Key. Collect the Silver Rupees by walking along the low walls. The platforms under the three Beamos Statues let you jump up once you destroy the statues [[gf1-301-3]]. When you have all five, the gold torch on the east side lights [[gf1-301-4]]. Light a Deku Stick on it and light the three unlit torches (near the east door, in the northeast, and near the south door) before any goes out. A chest with a Small Key appears on the low wall in the south [[gf1-301-5]]. Go through the south door.

#### Upper West Stairwell and the Iron Knuckle

Look above the door you came through for the Gold Skulltula (#98). Boomerang it, then take the door at the top of the stairs [[gf1-302-1, checklist-skulltulas-19-3]].

Run left to find a suit of armor on a throne. Strike it to start the fight [[gf1-302-2]].

**Iron Knuckle (mini-boss).** Its attacks can deal up to four hearts of damage [[gf2-93-4]]. You can dodge and strike afterwards, or cast Nayru's Love and attack while invulnerable (you can still be knocked around) [[gf1-302-3]]. A third method as Young Link is approach, backflip as it begins to slash, jump slash when it is open and backflip away again, repeating until it dies [[gf3-110-8]]. Bombs thrown at it also work [[gf3-82-1]]. Without Nayru's Love expect at least seven or eight jump attacks [[gf2-93-4]]. At half health part of its armor breaks and it moves and attacks faster [[gf1-302-4]]. Blocking does little [[gf1-302-4]]. Pillars in the room hold health and items, so get it to knock them over if you are low [[gf1-302-3]].

When it dies, the door behind its throne opens. Walk through the tunnel and you are back outside, high on one of the Colossus's arms [[gf1-302-5]].

### Silver Gauntlets

Kaepora Gaebora gives a speech. After that, open the chest in front of you for the **[Silver Gauntlets]** [[gf1-303-1, gf3-82-2]]. A cutscene shows Koume and Kotake capturing Nabooru [[gf1-303-1]]. Careful, the ledge is narrow and a fall costs you the trip back [[gf2-93-5]]. Sources disagree on which arm the chest is on, so take whichever chest the tunnel leads you to [[gf1-303-1, gf3-158-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:49189]

Young Link is finished here. Play the Prelude of Light to return to the Temple of Time [[gf1-303-2]].

### Temple of Time (back to Adult Link)

1. After the Prelude of Light drops you in the Temple of Time, walk through the Door of Time to the Master Sword pedestal [[gf1-303-2, gf1-397-4]].
2. Retake the Master Sword to become Adult Link [[gf1-305-1]].
3. Play the Requiem of Spirit to warp back to the Desert Colossus. It works for either age, so you skip the Haunted Wasteland [[gf1-396-4]].

### Desert Colossus (second visit, as Adult Link)

Make it night with the Sun's Song if necessary [[gf1-307-1, gf1-393-3]]. Because you planted the bean as a child, a Magic Leaf platform now floats to the left of the temple door [[gf1-307-1]]. Ride it. It flies around the Colossus, passing two rocks and finally the arch near the entrance. At night, jump off after the second rock to find a Gold Skulltula (#95) on top of it [[gf1-307-2, checklist-skulltulas-18-6, gf1-482-1]].

Run back to the leaf and ride it again until it passes the arch east of the temple entrance. Jump onto the arch for the final **Piece of Heart** (#36) [[gf1-307-3, gf1-448-1, checklist-sidequests-33-7]]. A player reported that this heart stayed put after saving and resetting, so you cannot lose it by leaving [[ra-comments-101-7]].

A white rock to the left of the temple hides a Secret Cave with Business Scrubs selling Green and Red Potions [[gf3-86-1]]. They cost 40 rupees a bottle each, and the Silver Gauntlets are required to lift the boulder [[gf3-239-6]]. This is optional.

### Spirit Temple, East Wing (Adult Link)

#### Entrance and Rolling Boulder Room

Run up the stairs, avoiding the flying pots. At the top, go right to a doorway blocked by a giant blue block. Grab it and push it forward until it falls into a pit [[gf1-310-1]]. The chest up the stairs contains Bombchus [[gf3-109-2]]. Continue up the stairs. Bomb the Beamos and hit the Crystal Switch on the ceiling to open the left and right doors. Take the right door [[gf1-310-2]].

In the Rolling Boulder Room, collect five Silver Rupees around the pit. One hangs above the floor, so use the Hover Boots to run out to it and switch back to the Kokiri Boots afterwards. The far door unseals when all five are collected [[gf1-310-3, gf1-310-4]].

Stand near the Block of Time on the left and play the Song of Time. It moves into an adjacent alcove and the Gold Skulltula (#99) is behind its original spot [[gf1-310-5, gf1-507-2, checklist-skulltulas-19-4]]. Go through the door on the north side.

#### Small Chest Room

Step in and a Like-Like drops from the ceiling. Kill it and open the chest for a Small Key. Return through the boulder room to the entrance [[gf1-311-1]].

#### Triforce/Compass Room

Take the unlocked door on the west side of the entrance [[gf1-311-2]]. Step toward the Triforce symbol and a Wolfos appears. Backflip when it attacks and Jump Attack its back. Then stand on the symbol and play Zelda's Lullaby. A chest appears on the opposite ledge, so Longshot to it and open it for the **[Compass]** [[gf1-311-3]]. Drop down, climb the tiled wall on the opposite ledge, and return to the entrance through the door at the top [[gf1-311-3]].

#### Lower Mirror Chamber

Take the locked door on the north side [[gf1-312-1]]. Move slowly because a Like-Like falls from the ceiling and pots on the right throw themselves at you. Climb the tiled wall on the east side [[gf1-312-2]]. At the top, an invisible Floor Master hides behind the mirror. Z-Target it, Jump Attack it once, then kill its offspring with Din's Fire [[gf1-312-2]].

Stand behind the mirror, grab the left handle and push it forward so it shines sunlight onto each sun panel. The first two panels make chests appear (a recovery heart and a frost trap), the third unseals the west door, and the last summons a Wallmaster [[gf1-312-3]]. Do not open the frost trap chest [[gf3-82-8]]. Go through the west door.

#### Colossus Chamber (adult)

You are back in the Colossus Chamber. Climb the stairs in front of you, up two flights, and look northwest at the statue's left hand, which has a Triforce symbol on it. Equip the Hover Boots and run out to it. If you miss, return to the east side and Longshot the target on the ceiling [[gf1-313-1]]. Play Zelda's Lullaby on the symbol and a chest appears on the right hand. Re-equip the Kokiri Boots, Longshot to the chest and open it for a Small Key. Drop to the bottom floor [[gf1-313-1]]. Back near the hands, a chest behind some crates holds a Blue Rupee [[gf3-111-4]].

Climb the tiled wall on the west side and both flights of stairs. At the top, look north and Navi flutters to the other side of the pit. Play the Scarecrow's Song and Pierre appears. Longshot over to him and kill the Gold Skulltula (#100) behind him. This is your hundredth token [[gf1-313-2, gf1-313-3, checklist-skulltulas-19-5]].

Return to the west side and use the Longshot target on the ceiling to climb up to the next level. Go up both flights again and through the locked door in the southeast corner [[gf1-313-4]].

#### Lower East Stairwell and Treble Anubis Room

Bomb the Beamos and go up the stairs and through the door [[gf1-314-1]]. Three Anubis are here. Fire Arrows kill them easily. Bomb the Beamos statue on the north side to open the southeast door [[gf1-314-1, gf1-314-2]].

#### Quad Armos Room

A blue switch is in front of you. To open the sealed south door, coax an Armos into standing on it. Stand near the door and shoot the Armos in the northeast corner with an arrow. It hops toward you and steps on the switch, then you go through the door at once [[gf1-314-3]].

#### Upper East Stairwell and East Iron Knuckle Room

Two chests here hold recovery hearts and are visible with the Lens of Truth [[gf1-315-1]]. Take the south door. Run in and right, then slash the armor on the throne to start the fight [[gf1-315-1, gf1-315-2]].

**Iron Knuckle (adult).** The shield is useless against it, so use the Biggoron Sword. With it, four jump attacks are enough [[gf2-95-2]]. Use Nayru's Love if things get hot [[gf1-315-2]]. A reliable tactic is to wait for it to attack, backflip out of range, Jump Attack as you land, then backflip again, repeating until it dies [[gf1-517-5]]. Defeating it opens the door behind the throne and a tunnel [[gf1-315-3]].

#### Mirror Shield

On the Colossus statue's outstretched hand, a chest appears. Open it for the **[Mirror Shield]**, which reflects light, absorbs certain magic in one boss fight and cannot be stolen by Like-Likes [[gf1-316-1, gf1-316-2]]. Be careful not to fall, or you must run back through the dungeon [[gf1-316-2]]. Equip the shield and return through the door [[gf1-316-2]].

#### Small Key Room

Run through the door in front, then through the northeast door [[gf1-317-1]]. Go down the stairs and through the north door to the Quad Armos Room [[gf1-317-2]]. Run to the north end past the Blade Trap, stand in the beam of light and hold R to aim the Mirror Shield at the sun panel on the wall. The north door unseals [[gf1-317-3]]. Open the chest in the Small Key Room for a Small Key and go back out [[gf1-317-4]].

#### Sliding Panels Room

Take the west door of the Quad Armos Room into the Treble Anubis Room, then go through the locked door in the southwest corner [[gf1-318-1, gf1-318-2]]. Throw bombs at the two Beamos on the pillars. The wall of sliding panels can be climbed as they align, or you can Longshot the top panel. At the top, destroy both Beamos and take the north door [[gf1-318-3, gf1-318-4]].

#### Triforce Door Room and Boss Key Room

Go up the stairs, stand on the Triforce symbol and play Zelda's Lullaby. The door before it unseals [[gf1-319-1]]. In the Boss Key Room, kill the Fire Slugs, then use Bombs and the Megaton Hammer to destroy all the false doors. The false door left of the flame pillar hides a golden eye. Shoot it with an arrow, and a second platform appears high above. Longshot the target above that platform, jump to the platform above the flame pillar and step on the switch. The pillar vanishes and you can open the ornate chest for the **[Boss Key]** [[gf1-319-2]]. Another route is to Longshot to the ceiling panel, step on the button and drop down to the chest [[gf2-95-5]]. Return to the previous room.

#### Upper Sun Chamber

Go right and through the west door [[gf1-320-1]]. Block the two pots with your shield, then hit the crystal switch behind the bars on the left with a vertical slash or spin attack, which opens the sealed door [[gf1-320-2]]. A Lizalfos drops in. Kill it and take the left doorway. In the mirror room, kill the White Bubbles, bomb the odd rock formation on the east side, push the mirror until it faces east through the opening, then run through [[gf1-320-3]]. Kill another Lizalfos, push that mirror until it faces north, and return the way you came [[gf1-320-3]].

Back in the first area, the two mirrors now shine sunlight on the ceiling mirror. Jump into the pit, stand in the beam and use the Mirror Shield to light the sun panel on the west wall. The floor is an elevator and lowers you [[gf1-320-3]].

#### Colossus Room and the Boss Door

Face north to see the statue's face. Use the Mirror Shield to reflect the beam onto it. It begins to disintegrate into a grating. Longshot the grating and the Boss Door is revealed. Go through [[gf1-321-1]].

#### Nabooru's Throne Room

After the cutscene, the Iron Knuckle comes to life. Use the same strategies, with the Biggoron Sword, Nayru's Love and the backflip/Jump Attack trick [[gf1-321-2]]. When it falls, the armor breaks to reveal Nabooru. Koume and Kotake brainwashed her, and they capture her again and vanish [[gf1-321-3]]. Go through the door behind her throne and down the North Hall to the boss door [[gf1-321-3, gf1-322-1]].

### Boss: Twinrova

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Twins of Evil requires beating Twinrova without falling from the platform or reviving [[gf1-322-1, ra-comments-46-1]]. Players report that if you die and save during this fight the achievement can fail [[ra-comments-46-1]]. The title says you have 12 seconds to hook to the top for attempts after the first [[ra-comments-46-3]]. Fairies may be used, but a Game Over followed by a fairy revive does not work [[ra-comments-46-5]]. Make sure you have full health, magic, the Mirror Shield, the Hover Boots and your best sword equipped before the door.

You enter on a bottom floor below tall ledges. Climb the tiled wall to the center ledge and walk to the middle of the platform. A cutscene starts the fight [[gf1-322-1]].

**Phase one.** Kotake is Ice and Koume is Fire [[gf1-521-6]]. Do not Z-Target. Run around so both sisters stay in view [[gf1-322-1, ra-guide-16-6]]. When one charges a spell, a beam appears. Use the Mirror Shield to reflect it into the other sister [[gf1-322-1]]. The beam is constant, so you can sweep it around for a couple of seconds, and each sister must take two or three hits [[gf2-96-1]]. Aiming with Z or L ruins the shot [[gf1-521-7]]. Some say to lock on and then release the lock to face the witch directly [[gf3-265-2]]. A missed beam leaves a damaging ring on the floor, and Kotake's ice also freezes you, so reflect every beam anyway [[gf1-521-7]]. Fire and Ice Arrows do nothing [[gf1-322-1]].

**Phase two.** The sisters merge into Twinrova, so you can Z-Target now. Use the Biggoron Sword (kept put away until needed) or the Master Sword [[gf1-522-8]]. You must absorb three spells of the same element in a row with the shield. Two fires then an ice cancels the fires [[gf1-522-1]]. Twinrova waves her right wand for Ice and her left for Fire. Your shield flashes red for fire and white for ice, faster as more are absorbed [[gf1-522-2]]. Release R to dodge unwanted beams, but do not dodge off the ledge [[gf1-522-1]]. After three the shield fires a massive beam, so keep it drawn to connect. Twinrova is stunned and falls to the lower platform [[gf1-322-2, gf1-323-1]].

Quickly equip the Hover Boots, draw your sword and run out. Halfway across, Jump Attack the rest of the way to hit her, then use basic sword strikes [[gf1-323-1, gf1-522-3]]. When she recovers, return to the main platform and switch back to the Kokiri Boots. It helps to release the Z-lock momentarily [[gf1-522-3]]. Repeat. With the Biggoron Sword she goes down after two knockdowns [[gf2-96-2]].

Players warn that the Hookshot must be aimed at the highest point of the platform where the fight begins. Climbing the wall counts as falling, and so does catching the ledge to pull up [[ra-comments-47-1, ra-comments-47-2, ra-comments-47-9]]. After a death, hookshot onto the platform right after entering the door [[ra-comments-46-3]]. Another player says running up counts as falling because you touch the ground around the platform [[ra-comments-46-4]]. Hover Boots help with the jumps to and from the lower platform in the final phase [[ra-comments-47-7]]. This achievement applies to the normal fight only [[ra-comments-46-6]]. Some players use the Longshot to reach her and then crouch-stab [[ra-comments-47-8]]. A speed trick is to run toward her as you absorb the third beam [[gf3-265-4]]. Do not hold R when you do not want a beam, since absorbing the wrong element resets your count [[gf1-522-1]].

When she dies, a cutscene plays and a Heart Container is left on one of the outer platforms [[gf1-323-2]]. Take it, then step into the beam of light opposite it.
