### Chapter overview

This chapter covers two linked stops: Lake Hylia as an adult, where you unlock the Scarecrow's Song, and the Water Temple beneath the lake [[gf1-8-5, gf1-9-3]]. The temple is built around raising and lowering the water so you can reach doors on the different levels of a central tall room, the "Vertical Hallway" [[gf1-9-11, gf1-220-1]]. You will earn the Longshot, the Map, the Compass and the Boss Key here, and you will finish by defeating Morpha and receiving the Water Medallion from Ruto [[gf1-389-1, gf1-241-2]].

After the temple you return to Lake Hylia to shoot the rising sun for the Fire Arrows [[gf1-243-1]]. You also collect the Longshot-only Skulltula in the lake's tree and the underwater crate Skulltula in the laboratory [[gf1-243-2, gf1-218-2]].

Four achievements are in this chapter:
- **Pierre the Wanderer**
- **Day of the Tentacles** (missable)
- **Bride to Be**
- **The Flaming Arrow**

**What you need before you start:**
- **Iron Boots** from the Ice Cavern. Sheik also teaches the Serenade of Water there, which warps you to the Water Temple in Lake Hylia [[gf1-213-3, gf1-213-4, gf1-396-2]].
- **Zora Tunic** from King Zora. It cancels the underwater time limit while you wear the Iron Boots [[gf1-403-3, gf3-62-3]].
- **Hookshot, Fairy Bow, Bombs, Ocarina, Zelda's Lullaby and the Song of Time.** The route below uses them all [[gf1-221-4, gf1-223-1, gf1-232-2]].
- **The eight-note melody you taught the scarecrow as a child.** If you forgot it or never taught one, Sheik at the Temple of Time explains how to return to childhood and teach Pierre a new song [[gf1-165-2]].

The Water Temple only needs the Ice Cavern and Forest Temple finished first, so it can be done before the Fire Temple [[gf1-18-2]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** **Day of the Tentacles** (Defeat Morpha without falling into the water or getting captured by its tentacle) can be missed during the Morpha fight [[gf1-240-3, gf1-240-4, ra-comments-38-1, ra-comments-38-2]]. Do not enter the water, and do not stand on the four inner platforms [[gf1-240-3]]. The full plan is in the Morpha section below.

### Lake Hylia

#### The shore scarecrow: Pierre the Wanderer

Go to the scarecrows on the northeastern shore of Lake Hylia. This is where you taught your song as a child [[gf1-165-1]]. As Adult Link, play the same eight-note song you taught as Young Link. The scarecrow remembers it and it becomes the Scarecrow's Song [[gf1-398-2, gf3-235-1]].

Sources disagree about which of the two scarecrows asks for the song. One says the one closest to the shore, another the one farthest from the water [[gf3-30-9, checklist-sidequests-11-8]]. Another source names the scarecrow Bonooru [[ra-guide-14-1]]. Play it to the same scarecrow you taught as a child.

The song must be exactly 8 notes [[ra-comments-86-2]]. It must also have at least two different notes and cannot be any other song in the game [[gf1-398-1]].

Once learned, playing the song at certain spots makes Pierre appear as a makeshift Hookshot target [[gf1-398-2, gf2-153-16]]. Navi flutters at those spots [[checklist-sidequests-17-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48820]

#### Entering the Water Temple

Equip the Iron Boots and the Hookshot and jump down to the gate underwater on the north side of the central island [[gf2-71-7, gf1-218-3]]. You will find a door sealed by an iron grating [[gf1-218-3]]. Shoot the diamond switch above the door with the Hookshot and the gate unseals [[gf1-218-3, gf4-26-1]]. Enter to begin the dungeon.

> [!TIP]
> Wear the Zora Tunic before you step into the water, since it cancels the Iron Boots' underwater time limit [[gf1-403-3]]. Expect to swap between the Kokiri Boots and the Iron Boots many times in this dungeon [[gf1-221-1]].

### Water Temple

Orientation: the temple has a central tall room with doors on three levels. Three Triforce plaques ("Water Switches") change the water level when you play Zelda's Lullaby in front of them [[gf1-220-1, gf1-222-1]]:
- **Switch One** lowers the water to the lowest level.
- **Switch Two** sets it to the second-highest level.
- **Switch Three** sets it to the maximum.

The Hookshot is the only weapon usable underwater [[gf1-221-2]].

#### The first hallway

Equip the Iron Boots and sink to the bottom of the right-hand hallway pool. Run down the hallway and grab the stone block set in the wall directly in front of you. Pull it back as far as it will go [[gf1-221-1]].

#### The Stinger tunnel and the Compass

Follow the underwater passage onward. Stingers appear here, and one Hookshot shot kills each [[gf1-221-2]].

At the end of the hall, look up above the spikes to the ledge and Hookshot to the target there. Turn around, look at the ceiling, and Hookshot to the furthest of the three ceiling targets. That pulls you out of the water [[gf1-221-3]].

This room holds a pillar of water and a diamond switch in the corner. Drop a bomb near the switch, then run to the pillar. When the bomb explodes the switch fires and the pillar lowers temporarily. Open the chest for the **[Compass]** [[gf1-221-4]]. An alternative is to stand beside the water spray and chest and shoot the crystal with an arrow, then open the large chest while it is available [[gf2-72-1, gf3-64-1]].

Unable to verify from collected sources how the route returns from the Compass room to the main chamber.

#### Ruto, Water Switch One and the Map

In the main room, use the Iron Boots to sink to the bottom floor. Spike enemies are here, and you kill each with two Hookshot shots, one to stun and one to kill [[gf1-221-5]]. On the east side of the room, marked by two torches, is a tunnel. Run down it and you meet Ruto again [[gf1-221-6]].

Rise to the air pocket. On the wall is a Triforce plaque. Play Zelda's Lullaby in front of it and the water drops to its lowest level. This is Water Switch One [[gf1-222-1, gf2-72-3]]. Go through the door to the left of the plaque into the Spike Room [[gf1-222-1]].

The room is full of Spikes. Hit each twice with the Hookshot and a chest appears with the **[Dungeon Map]** [[gf1-222-2, gf3-64-2]]. Another method is to run away until they turn into small rocks, then use a spin attack [[gf2-72-2]].

#### Ruto's room: torches and the first Small Key

Go to the room where you met Ruto. Stand at the entrance so the middle flame lines up with a corner torch. Shoot arrows through the flame so the arrowheads catch fire, and light the corner lanterns to unseal the door [[gf1-223-1, gf2-72-4, gf3-64-3]]. Go through the door.

In the Shell Blade room, the Shell Blades are invulnerable until they briefly open their mouths just before attacking. Fire the Hookshot into the open mouth to kill them [[gf1-223-2]]. Kill them all and a chest appears with a **[Small Key]** [[gf1-223-3]]. Return through the only door to the previous room [[gf1-223-3]].

#### South hallway: Skulltula #77

Go down the south hallway and use a Bomb on the oddly colored floor tile at its end to reveal a water-filled pit. Equip the Iron Boots and sink [[gf1-224-1]]. When you land, head west and defeat a Shell Blade with the Hookshot [[gf1-224-2]].

On the next floor, climb onto the ledge and step on the switch. The water in this room rises slightly and a Hookshot target appears on the ledge above [[gf1-224-3]]. The switch to open the grating is on the other side of it, so use a Super Spin Attack to reach it [[gf1-224-4]]. Kill the Gold Skulltula on the other side and take its token. This is **Skulltula #77** [[gf1-224-5, checklist-skulltulas-15-4]]. It requires the Iron Boots [[gf1-498-1]].

#### The west block, the pillar and the Whirlpool Room

On the west side of the chamber, jump onto the wooden platform, grab the stone block and push it forward until it falls into a water-filled pit [[gf1-224-6]]. Climb out of the water at the top of the passage and strike the diamond switch on the right. A pillar of water rises from the pit in the middle of the room. Jump onto the pillar and then across to the other side before the time limit runs out [[gf1-224-7]].

In the Whirlpool Room, look in the mouth of the dragon statue on the way down for a diamond switch. To the right of the statue is a small alcove with a Hookshot target [[gf1-225-1]]. Pull yourself to the alcove and stand on its edge as far from the statue as possible. From there you can just barely hit the corner of the switch with the Hookshot [[gf1-225-1]].

Climb out of the pool behind the grating and open the chest for another **[Small Key]** [[gf1-225-2]]. A diamond switch in this small room reopens the grating below. Strike it, then sink back down before time runs out [[gf1-225-3]]. Exit through the door in the alcove back to the Vertical Hallway [[gf1-233-1]].

#### Central Tower: Water Switch Two and another Small Key

On the south side of the tower in the middle of the Vertical Hallway is a locked door. Go through it [[gf1-226-1]].

You find Water Switch Two here. Play Zelda's Lullaby to raise the water to the second-highest level [[gf1-226-2]]. A passage appears below, so equip the Iron Boots, sink to the bottom level and go down it [[gf1-226-2]].

Hit the corner switch with the Hookshot. A grating opens in the ceiling, releasing six Spikes and three Shell Blades. Defeat them all and a grating opens across from the switch [[gf1-226-3]]. Switch to the Kokiri Boots and float up the new passage. At the top, climb out and open the chest for a **[Small Key]** [[gf1-226-4, gf3-64-8]].

#### The gash in the wall

Use Bombs to blow open the huge gash in the wall. Enter the opening and open the chest on the other side for another **[Small Key]** [[gf1-227-1, gf3-64-7]].

#### Water Pillar Room and Water Switch Three

Stand on the low water pillar and activate the diamond switch with the Hookshot or an arrow. The pillar lifts you through the shaft in the ceiling [[gf1-228-1]]. A Tektite waits at the top. Charge a Super Spin Attack as you ride up and unleash it when you see the Tektite [[gf1-228-2]].

You reach Water Switch Three, the final plaque. Play Zelda's Lullaby in front of it to raise the water to the maximum height [[gf1-228-3]].

#### Waterfall-Elevator Room

At the edge of the cliff, look to your right to see a Gold Skulltula very high on the wall. You cannot reach it yet [[gf1-229-1]].

The elevators that steadily descend in the waterfall all have Hookshot targets on their fronts. Latch onto one and pull yourself progressively higher until you reach the top of the waterfall [[gf1-229-2]]. Platforms in this room move down with the waterfall and the water knocks you off once they pass behind it, so hook one while it is moving down, then quickly hook the one above [[gf2-74-2]].

#### Dragon's Head Room

Shoot the diamond switch in the middle of the room to raise and lower the dragon heads, which reveals Hookshot targets. Climb onto a lowered head, then shoot the switch while standing on it. The head rises and lets you reach the alcove on top of the room [[gf1-230-1]].

Defeat the Like Like behind the large spikes by throwing bombs at it [[gf1-230-2]]. Use the Hookshot target on the ceiling near the door to pull yourself over the spikes blocking the path [[gf1-230-3]].

#### The Serene Pool: Shadow Link

Run to the island in the middle and keep running around it until Dark Link appears near the tree [[gf1-231-1]].

**Shadow Link (Dark Link) strategy:**
- **Do not Z-target him** with regular sword attacks. Targeting makes you clash swords endlessly, and without it he lines up his deflections less easily [[gf1-231-3, gf2-75-1, gf3-66-7]].
- **Do not use the stab attack** (forward plus attack while Z-targeted). He jumps on your sword and hits you every time [[gf1-517-2, gf2-75-2]].
- **The Megaton Hammer is the easy option.** He cannot deflect it and will not try [[gf1-231-2, gf1-517-1]].
- **Other options from the sources:** the Iron Boots slow his movements, Din's Fire is effective, and a Z-targeted thrust with the Biggoron's Sword hits him consistently [[gf3-66-3, gf3-66-1, gf3-66-4]]. A Deku Nut stuns him only once [[gf3-66-6]].
- His health equals yours, and each Master Sword hit takes one heart [[gf3-66-2]].

When he falls, the pool disappears and the north door unlocks [[gf1-231-4]].

#### Longshot Room and the river

Open the chest in front of you to get the **[Longshot]**, which has double the Hookshot's range [[gf1-232-1]]. Go around behind the chest to a Block of Time in the floor. Play the Song of Time to move it and drop down the pit beyond [[gf1-232-2, gf2-75-4]].

As you pass the second corner in the river you will see a Gold Skulltula hanging on the ceiling directly above the third whirlpool. Use the Iron Boots to come to a stop and grab the Gold Skulltula and its token from the bottom of the river. This is **Skulltula #78** [[gf1-232-3, checklist-skulltulas-15-5, gf1-498-4]].

Near the fifth whirlpool, at the final corner of the river, climb onto the small platform and shoot the golden eye switch with an arrow. The grating across from the platform opens. Use the Longshot on the chest inside to pull yourself over before the time limit runs out and the grating closes, then open it for a **[Small Key]** [[gf1-232-4, gf3-67-4]].

#### Skulltula #80 and the water reset

Float to the top floor of the main chamber and go through the west door to the Waterfall-Elevator Room [[gf1-233-2]]. Look on the wall to the right and use the Longshot to kill the Gold Skulltula and take its token. This is **Skulltula #80** [[gf1-233-3, gf1-499-5, checklist-skulltulas-15-7]]. Return to the Vertical Hallway [[gf1-233-3]].

Equip the Iron Boots, drop to the bottom level and go through the east hall back to the room where you met Ruto. Switch to the Kokiri Boots, float all the way up and play Zelda's Lullaby in front of the plaque to lower the water to its bottom level [[gf1-234-1]].

#### Central Tower again: Skulltula #79

Pull yourself up to the second level of the tower with the Longshot and play Zelda's Lullaby in front of the plaque to set the water to its middle height [[gf1-234-2]]. Climb onto the floating platform and look high on the wall near the ceiling. Longshot the Gold Skulltula and take its token. This is **Skulltula #79** [[gf1-234-3, checklist-skulltulas-15-6]]. Then go out the door to the second level of the Vertical Hallway [[gf1-234-3]].

One source says the tower door is sealed by an unlit torch. Light it with a Fire Arrow, or by shooting an arrow through the nearby lit torch into the unlit one, and the door unseals [[gf1-499-2]]. It also says that after going through you spot the Skulltula on the ceiling above and take it with the Longshot [[gf1-499-3]].

#### South side of the tower: another Small Key

Work around to the south side of the tower's exterior, where a doorway is covered by a grating. Shoot the eye in the water with an arrow to make the grating open [[gf1-235-1]]. Push the stone block forward until it stops, then take the passage that opens to the right and open the chest for a **[Small Key]** [[gf1-235-2, gf3-67-6]].

Ride the water pillar to the third floor and go through the door at the top [[gf1-236-1]]. Play Zelda's Lullaby at the plaque to raise the water back to its maximum height [[gf1-236-2]].

#### Toward the Boss Key

Equip the Iron Boots, sink all the way to the bottom level and head for the north end of the room to find a doorway with a deep pit. Sink down it, go down a short hallway, then change back to the Kokiri Boots to float to the surface [[gf1-236-3]].

In the next pond, equip the Iron Boots and walk along the bottom, watching for boulders. Run to the northeast corner, switch back to the Kokiri Boots, and as you float up grab the ledge on the north corner of the room and pull yourself up [[gf1-237-1]].

In the Stone Block Room, bomb the walls in the northwest and northeast corners to reveal a hidden tunnel with a stone block. Push it forward until it falls into the pool of water, pressing down on the switch and permanently activating it. The water level rises [[gf1-237-2, gf2-76-4]].

In the Water Pillar Triplets Room, step on the switch in the corner and three water pillars rise and the door across the room unlocks [[gf1-238-1]]. To cross fastest, jump only to the second pillar and then straight for the door [[gf1-238-2]].

#### Skulltula #81 and the Boss Key

Run forward into the passage of water, watching for rolling boulders. Look to the right and kill the Gold Skulltula on the wall above the water pit, then take its token [[gf1-239-1]]. Another source places it behind the waterfall in the tunnel right before the Boss Key and says to use the Longshot for both kill and token. This is **Skulltula #81** [[gf1-500-1, gf1-500-2, checklist-skulltulas-15-8]].

Use the Iron Boots to drop down the water pit beneath the Skulltula [[gf1-239-1]]. Kill the Shell Blade, then float to the top and open the ornate chest for the **[Boss Key]** [[gf3-67-10, gf1-239-2]].

#### Blade Trap Room and the boss door

Head to the top level of the Vertical Hallway and go to the north side of the tower. Use the Longshot on the dragon's head to pull yourself up and go through the door behind the statue [[gf1-240-1]].

In the Blade Trap Room, climb the steep hill past three slow Blade Traps using the Kokiri Boots, and keep pressing forward. Once at the top of the ramp, enter the Boss Door [[gf1-240-2]].

### Boss: Morpha

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Day of the Tentacles requires beating Morpha without falling into the water or being grabbed [[gf1-240-3, gf1-240-4, ra-guide-14-2]]. Follow the corner plan below.

**What it does:**
- Morpha spins in the water to generate tentacle arms, which grab you and hurl you into the wall [[gf1-520-7]].
- Damage figures differ by source: four to six hearts, about one heart for a hit and up to three for a grab, or three hearts for a grab [[gf1-520-5, gf2-77-1, gf3-68-1]].
- Its nucleus (the large white brain/eye) usually enters one of the arms [[gf1-520-7]].
- It spins rapidly in the water when it is preparing a new arm [[gf1-520-7]].

**The plan:**
1. **Stay out of the water and off the four inner platforms.** The water gives Morpha an easy shot at you, and standing on the platforms lets it attack from several sides [[gf1-240-3, gf1-520-6]]. A commenter notes that jumping in the water and swimming to a platform before Morpha spawns did not disqualify them, as long as they got back to a corner without falling in [[ra-comments-38-5]]. Do not rely on that, and stay on the perimeter.
2. **Go to a corner as soon as the fight starts.** The guide says Morpha cannot reach you in a corner, and a commenter had the same result [[ra-guide-14-2, ra-comments-38-2]].
3. **Camp with the Longshot.** The Longshot outranges Morpha's arms by a considerable distance [[gf1-520-7, ra-comments-38-1]].
4. **When the nucleus enters an arm, Longshot it.** This stuns it long enough for one or two hits [[gf1-240-4, gf1-521-1]]. Aim near the end of the tentacle [[gf2-77-2]].
5. **Hit it hard.** Use a Jump Attack with the Biggoron's Sword, or the Megaton Hammer if you lack it [[gf1-240-4]]. A spin attack is another option [[gf2-126-3]]. One commenter pulled with the Longshot and finished with the Megaton Hammer, repeating until it died [[ra-comments-38-9]].
6. **Longshot between slashes** so the brain does not escape [[gf3-68-2]].
7. **Repeat.** After a hit, Morpha bounces around the room, then returns to the water and spawns more arms [[gf1-521-1]].

**Extra tips from commenters:**
- A faster but less safe method is to Longshot Morpha, go around it, and trap it against a wall. A second tentacle may spawn and put you in grab range [[ra-comments-38-3, ra-comments-38-4]].
- Another commenter pulled Morpha into a wall corner and trapped it there [[ra-comments-38-6]].
- When fighting normally, keep your distance and watch for two tentacles spawning close together, which is the one dangerous moment [[ra-comments-38-7]].
- Only two tentacles exist, so circle the edges and do not jump onto the platforms [[ra-comments-38-10]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48946]

After the fight, take the Heart Container left in the dried-up pool [[gf1-241-1, gf3-68-3]].

Enter the beam of light to reach the Chamber of the Sages. Ruto reveals she is the Sage of Water and gives you the Water Medallion [[gf1-241-2, gf3-68-4, gf1-422-3]]. Lake Hylia fills back up with water [[gf1-241-3]].

### Lake Hylia after the Water Temple

#### The Fire Arrows

After the cutscene you are in Lake Hylia. Stand on the stone at the plaque set into the ground on the east side of the island [[gf1-243-1, ra-guide-14-3]]. Aim at the sun and fire an arrow into it as it rises [[gf3-70-2, ra-guide-14-3]]. The Fire Arrows fall onto the smaller island east of the larger one. Swim over and take them [[gf1-243-1, gf1-389-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48949]

If you miss, play the Sun's Song twice to retry [[ra-guide-14-3]]. Another source says the Sun's Song will not work and you must wait for time to pass normally [[gf1-389-2]]. These sources conflict, so start with the first and wait for sunrise if it fails. Playing the Sun's Song on the Fire Arrow platform spawns a fairy that restores health and magic, but it cannot be bottled [[gf3-70-3]].

#### Skulltula #54: the dead tree (night)

Return to the larger island and Longshot to the top of the dead tree. Aim for the bit of the trunk that sticks up over the large branch [[gf1-243-2, gf1-476-2]]. Make it night and a Gold Skulltula appears on top of the trunk. Do not jump up to kill it. Stand on the branch and use the Longshot [[gf1-243-2, checklist-sidequests-29-2]]. This is **Skulltula #54** [[checklist-skulltulas-10-4]].

#### Skulltula #55: the laboratory crate

Enter the laboratory and equip the Iron Boots. Sink to the bottom of the pool and roll into the crate. This reveals the game's only underwater Gold Skulltula. You cannot use a sword underwater, so kill it with the Hookshot and collect the token [[gf1-218-2, gf3-62-10, gf4-17-15]]. This is **Skulltula #55** [[checklist-skulltulas-10-5]].

#### Laboratory roof Piece of Heart

Ride the grown Magic Bean plant beside the laboratory to its roof. This works only if you planted a bean in the soft soil as a child [[gf1-445-1, gf2-71-3, gf3-174-6]]. Jump onto the roof, then climb the ladder to the high tower. The Guays can harass you on the way up [[gf1-216-2, gf3-41-12]]. The Piece of Heart is on top [[gf1-216-2]].

Alternatively, reach the roof using the Scarecrow's Song and Hookshot [[gf1-445-1, checklist-heartpieces-4-8]]. Unable to verify from collected sources exactly where on the shore to play the song for this.

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave Lake Hylia, check that you have the Fire Arrows, Skulltulas #54 and #55, and the roof Piece of Heart. The sources show a Prelude of Light warp to the Temple of Time as the next step after the tree Skulltula [[gf1-243-3]].
