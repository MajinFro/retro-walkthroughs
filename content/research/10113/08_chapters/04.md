## Chapter 4: Lost Woods, Sacred Forest Meadow and Goron City

[MERMAID]

This chapter takes you through the Lost Woods, where you earn the Bullet Bag upgrade and the Skull Kid Piece of Hearts, then to the Sacred Forest Meadow for Saria's Song, and finally up Death Mountain Trail to Goron City, where Darunia gives you the Goron's Bracelet. The Lost Woods is entered from Kokiri Forest [[gf1-23-2]]. The Sacred Forest Meadow lies in the midst of the Lost Woods [[gf1-8-2]]. Death Mountain Trail leads from Kakariko Village to Goron City [[gf1-62-5]].

Three achievements are placed here: Bullseye!! (Bullet Bag), You Can Be a Star (Ocarina minigame) and Dance Into the Fire (Darunia's gift) [[gf1-46-1, gf1-46-2, gf1-63-3]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Heads-Up for Later Deku Upgrades (A Horrible Face, Stocking Nuts):** The Deku Nut upgrade from the Mask of Truth on the Forest Stage cannot be obtained once Link has become an adult [[gf1-525-1, gf1-418-1]]. One source also says receiving the Poacher's Saw sets a flag that makes it unobtainable [[ra-comments-44-3]]. The Deku Nut upgrade under the boulder near the Sacred Forest Meadow entrance is also flagged as missable if you take the Poacher's Saw before any Lost Woods upgrades [[ra-comments-73-3]]. This chapter's route does not need the Mask of Truth yet. Do not trade ahead in the Biggoron's Sword chain before collecting these upgrades [[ra-guide-10-2]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Do Not Break These Boulders (Walking on a Tightrope, Chapter 6):** In Goron City, do NOT destroy the three boulders to the left of the entrance on the top floor. Their destruction is permanent and voids the achievement in chapter 6 [[ra-comments-68-1, ra-comments-68-3, ra-guide-8-2]]. Open the other, non-entrance rocks only where this guide tells you to.

### Kokiri Forest

🗺️ **Map:** [View Kokiri Forest Map by kingofthisgame](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/1099-kokiri-forest-map)

Return to Kokiri Forest as a child. The way into the Lost Woods is behind Mido's House: climb up on the ledge behind it, climb the vine wall, work around the ledge and enter the large hollow log [[gf1-23-2]].

Collect both Kokiri Forest Gold Skulltulas now (bring a Bottle with bugs, found under rocks). At night, one sits behind the Know-It-All Brothers' House (the house to the right as you enter from Hyrule Field). Kill it with the Slingshot, then backflip into the token while facing away from it [[gf1-452-1, gf1-45-2, checklist-skulltulas-3-1]]. The second is under the patch of soft earth by the Kokiri Shop; drop a Bottled Bug on it and kill the Skulltula [[gf1-452-2, checklist-skulltulas-3-2]]. A Magic Bean can be planted there too [[gf3-37-4]].

You need the Fairy Ocarina and Slingshot for this chapter. Saria already gave you the Ocarina when you left the forest [[gf1-34-3]].

### Lost Woods

🗺️ **Map:** [View Lost Woods Map by EverythingZelda](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/54-lost-woods-map)

#### Bullet Bag target

Enter via the log behind Mido's house and go right through the hollowed log. Run forward to the edge of the cliff and take out the Slingshot. Shoot the drum hanging from the tree limb at its exact center. A hit gives 100 points and a small victory sound. Three successive center shots make a Deku Scrub appear and give you the Bullet Bag x50 [[gf1-46-1]]. Another source describes it as upgrading seed capacity from 40 to 50 [[gf2-18-1]]. A "100" appears when you hit the right place [[gf1-406-1]]. Aim the center of the Slingshot's rubber band at the center of the drum before you fire [[gf1-46-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48224]

#### Skull Kid memory game

Drop down the cliff to the small valley below and go all the way to the right. Stand on the low trunk and two Skull Kids appear. Take out the Ocarina and play the memory game: three rounds, each longer and harder [[gf1-46-2]]. They play each addition twice, so watch the first Skull Kid and play along with the second to learn the new part fastest [[gf1-46-2]]. Another source says you may want pen and paper ready [[checklist-heartpieces-3-2]]. The notes are five, six and eight in length, with a Green Rupee and a Blue Rupee as the earlier prizes and the Piece of Heart for the last [[gf2-20-1]]. Completing the game awards the Piece of Heart [[gf1-425-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48225]

#### Gold Skulltulas #4 and #5 (bugs)

You already own the Deku Stick upgrade from Chapter 1. Bring a Bottle with bugs for these two soft-soil Skulltulas, and recapture a bug each time.

1. **#4:** Take two lefts from the entrance, drop into the valley and find the patch of soft earth past the Business Scrub. Drop the bugs on it and kill the Gold Skulltula that appears [[checklist-skulltulas-3-4, gf1-454-1, gf1-125-1]].
2. **#5:** From the entrance go right, left, right, left and left to reach a large grassy clearing near the Forest Stage. Drop the bugs on its soft earth and kill the Skulltula [[checklist-skulltulas-3-5, gf1-454-2]].

#### Onward to the Sacred Forest Meadow

Climb the ladder back up, then take the hollowed log to the right of the ladder. Kaepora Gaebora appears; the stone arch here leads to Goron City but you can't use it yet [[gf1-46-3]]. Go through the log tunnel he stood on. On the other side is a pool that leads to Zora's Domain, also not usable yet [[gf1-46-4]]. Go left through a tunnel, straight through another, left through another, and right at the next crossroads to enter the Sacred Forest Meadow [[gf1-46-5]].

Optionally, bomb the rock near the entrance to the Sacred Forest Meadow and drop into the pit for a pair of Business Scrubs. One sells the Deku Nut upgrade for 40 rupees [[gf1-418-2, gf1-86-5]]. This is the upgrade the Stocking Nuts achievement refers to, not the Mask of Truth one [[ra-comments-73-1]]. The scrub on the left is the one that sells it [[ra-guide-8-7]]. You need Bombs, which you get in Dodongo's Cavern, so this is for the return trip.

### Sacred Forest Meadow

🗺️ **Map:** [View Sacred Forest Meadow Map by R351D3NT3V1L4](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/5354-sacred-forest-meadow-map)

Run forward a short way and a Gray Wolfos ambushes you. Stand still, wait for it to move close, backflip (Back + A while Z-targeted) as it attacks, then immediately Jump Attack (A while Z-targeted). That kills it in one shot [[gf1-47-1]]. Its death makes the gate blocking the meadow disappear [[gf1-47-2]].

Turn right and follow the path around the corner. Kill or run past the Deku Scrub and go around another corner. Kill or dodge another Scrub, round another corner, take a left down a grassy detour, then another left back onto the main road [[gf1-47-3]]. Jump into the pool at the end of the road, then jump back onto the road with the Deku Scrub in front of you. Kill or dodge it, go around the corner, take a left and go up the stairs. Run down the long corridor, killing or dodging two more Deku Scrubs, then run up the stairs. At the top Saria sits in a small overgrown courtyard and teaches you Saria's Song [[gf1-47-3]].

The song is C-Down, C-Right, C-Left, C-Down, C-Right, C-Left [[gf1-393-1]].

Head back down the stairs, down the corridor and down the second flight. Climb the ladder directly in front and jump along the cliffs to leave faster. The pit in the middle of the upper cliffs holds a Fairy Fountain [[gf1-47-3, gf1-47-4]]. Run back to the entrance and return to the Lost Woods.

> [!TIP]
> RetroAchievements commenters note that entering Goron City from the Lost Woods triggers Saria to appear in the Sacred Forest Meadow, so if Saria is missing, try that [[ra-comments-13-2]].

### Lost Woods (return)

Kaepora Gaebora ambushes you again. Take a right through the hollowed log to return to Kokiri Forest, then turn right back around and re-enter the Lost Woods [[gf1-49-1]].

Go left through the hollowed log and jump onto the small trunk by the Skull Kid. Play Saria's Song for him and he gives you a Piece of Heart [[gf1-49-1, checklist-heartpieces-3-1]]. This gives you another Heart Container [[gf1-49-1]].

### Death Mountain Trail

Get to Kakariko Village and show Zelda's Letter to the guard at the northern gate to be let through [[gf1-58-2]]. Equip the Hylian Shield before you go [[gf1-62-1]].

Go forward and kill the Tektite. If it tries to jump on you, hold R for the Turtle Defense. Around the corner defeat two more Tektites, then another that jumps down from above [[gf1-62-1, gf1-62-2]]. Watch for rolling boulders as you climb [[gf1-169-1]]. Note the Piece of Heart high above the giant boulder; you get it shortly [[gf1-62-4]].

Follow the path to the large wooden post with a red flag. Go right, past the large stone pedestal with the ring of rocks, and enter the archway into Goron City [[gf1-62-5]].

### Goron City

#### Darunia

Jump down to the lowest level. Find the sealed door with the carpet in front of it, stand on the carpet and play Zelda's Lullaby to open it [[gf1-63-1]]. Enter and find Darunia. Play Saria's Song standing directly in front of him to cheer him up [[gf1-63-2]]. He gives you the Goron's Bracelet, which lets you pick up and use Bomb Flowers [[gf1-63-3, gf3-21-5]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48249]

Before leaving the room, light a Deku Stick on the torch in his room. Run out and light the empty sconce outside. Put the stick away by swinging your sword, take out another and light it again. Light all the braziers on the bottom level; once all are lit the giant pot starts spinning [[gf1-63-4]]. You can also use the lit stick on the Bomb Flowers near the sealed room to blow the wall open and reach the Goron City Shop [[gf1-63-5]].

#### Second floor

Take the stairs between the yellow-striped posts to the second level. Go right to the two unlit torches and a Goron sitting in front of a tunnel. In the tunnel, pick up a Bomb Flower and drop it near the boulders to clear the path [[gf1-63-6]]. Lighting the two torches and the Bomb Flowers opens a shortcut tunnel to the Lost Woods [[gf2-24-3]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Do NOT destroy the three boulders blocking the entrance to the boulder room on the top floor. Leave them intact so Walking on a Tightrope in chapter 6 remains available [[ra-comments-68-3, ra-guide-8-2]].

#### Deku Nut and Mask trips

The Skull Mask Forest Stage trip and the Mask of Truth upgrade belong to the Happy Mask sidequest, which you may do later. The Deku Stick upgrade (30) comes with the Skull Mask and the Deku Nut upgrade (40) with the Mask of Truth [[gf1-349-3]]. Unable to verify from collected sources whether this chapter's Slingshot and Ocarina progress is affected by either.

#### Dodongo's Cavern entrance and the Piece of Heart

From the second level, go to the far left and up the stairs between the red-striped posts. When the stairs split, go right. At the top, go right from the stairwell exit, then right again to return to Death Mountain Trail [[gf1-66-1]].

Go around the corner to the right to a small alcove with a Bomb Flower. Pick it up and throw it off the cliff so it lands on or near the boulder blocking Dodongo's Cavern [[gf1-66-2]]. Next, face away from the edge, hold Z without targeting anything, run backward toward the fence and backflip over it. If done right, you land on the cliff above Dodongo's Cavern and get the Piece of Heart [[gf1-66-3]]. This completes a Heart Container [[gf1-66-3]]. Drop down, and drop a bug into the soft earth at the cavern entrance to release a Gold Skulltula (recapture one bug) [[gf1-66-4, gf1-465-2]]. You can also plant a Magic Bean in this soil [[gf1-121-3]].

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave: you have Dodongo's Cavern next, which gives you Bombs. After you return you can finish the spinning pot (Piece of Heart), the rolling Goron Bomb Bag, the boulder maze Skulltula and other bomb-gated spots in Goron City [[gf1-85-3, gf1-85-4, gf1-468-3]].

### Bomb-gated content (Chapter 6)

The Death Mountain Trail wall Skulltula, the rolling Goron Bomb Bag, the spinning-pot Piece of Heart, the Goron City boulder maze, the Deku Nut upgrade grotto and the Great Fairy all need Bombs. Chapter 6 does them in order, once you have Bombs [[gf1-85-3, gf1-85-4, gf1-468-3, gf1-86-5]].
