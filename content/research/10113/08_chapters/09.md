[MERMAID]

This chapter takes Young Link from King Zora's throne room into Zora's Fountain, then through Jabu-Jabu's Belly to the third Spiritual Stone. You learn Farore's Wind, get the Boomerang, Map and Compass, collect four dungeon Gold Skulltulas and two Fountain ones, and fight Barinade. Barinade carries the chapter's only missable achievement, Rolling Thunder, which asks for a clean kill with no hits [[gf1-102-2, gf1-103-3, gf1-109-2, gf1-110-3, gf1-111-3, gf1-115-3, ra-comments-17-1]].

### Preparation

> [!TIP]
> **Before you start:** You need an empty Bottle, Bombs, the Slingshot with seeds, Deku Nuts, Deku Sticks, the Deku Shield and the Ocarina with Zelda's Lullaby. The Slingshot, Deku Nuts and Bombs are used throughout the dungeon, and the Deku Shield is used for crouch-stab and deflect tricks [[gf1-105-2, gf1-107-2, gf2-40-1, gf2-40-2, ra-comments-19-10]].

The Boomerang does not arrive until the middle of the dungeon. Until then, jellyfish can be handled with Deku Sticks or by throwing Princess Ruto at them [[checklist-sidequests-13-1]]. The Great Fairy gives Farore's Wind, and you will want it before the boss [[gf1-103-3, ra-comments-18-1]].

### Zora's Domain

🗺️ **Map:** [View Zora's River/Domain Map by EverythingZelda](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/56-zoras-river-domain-map)

Make your way to King Zora's Throne Room and show him the Bottled Letter. He moves aside and begs Link to save his daughter from Jabu-Jabu's Belly [[gf1-102-2, checklist-sidequests-12-5]]. Using the Letter also leaves you with the Empty Bottle that held it [[gf2-38-4]].

If you did not already empty the torches' Piece of Heart chest earlier, it is available here for Young Link. Light a Deku Stick at the torch on the throne room stairs and relight at the shop torch. Then light the burned-out brazier by the cave mouth, the one in the shallow water by the stones, and the two behind the waterfall. If every torch is lit before any burns out, a chest holding a Piece of Heart appears behind the waterfall [[gf1-441-1, checklist-heartpieces-6-2, gf2-36-3]]. Sources also say the lit-torch run can be done with Din's Fire for the two waterfall torches [[gf1-97-1]].

Now catch a fish. Go back to the bottom level and wade into the shallow water near the waterfall. Use the empty Bottle on one of the swimming fish [[gf1-102-3]].

Climb back to the throne room and take the narrow path on the right side to the throne. Go through the tunnel behind the throne to reach Zora's Fountain [[gf1-102-3, gf1-102-4]].

### Zora's Fountain

Your first stop is the tree. Swim to the island in the very southeast of the Fountain and roll into the lone tree. A Gold Skulltula falls out. Kill it and take the token (Skulltula #48, Bombs required for the area) [[gf1-103-1, checklist-sidequests-12-6, checklist-skulltulas-9-7]].

Next, the Great Fairy. Drop a bomb between the two boulders against the wall, near the silver rock that cannot be bombed. The wall blows open and reveals a large cave [[gf1-103-2, gf3-31-4, ra-guide-8-10]]. Go through the cave to the Great Fairy's Fountain [[gf1-103-2]]. Stand on the Triforce emblem and play Zelda's Lullaby. The Great Fairy appears and teaches you Farore's Wind, which sets a checkpoint in a dungeon that you can warp back to [[gf1-103-3, gf2-39-2, gf2-154-6]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48416]

Leave the fountain. Climb up onto the altar on the west side in front of Jabu-Jabu's mouth. Stand directly in front of the mouth and use the bottled fish. Jabu-Jabu inhales you along with the fish [[gf1-104-1, checklist-sidequests-12-8, gf3-31-2]].

### Inside Jabu-Jabu's Belly

#### Entrance room

Walk forward a few feet and a pair of Octoroks appear. Kill them with the Slingshot, or deflect their shots back with the Deku Shield [[gf1-105-2, gf2-40-1]]. Kill the Shaboms with Deku Nuts [[gf1-105-2]]. Run to the north end and shoot the large yellow bump on the ceiling with the Slingshot, which unseals the door at the end of the hall [[gf1-105-3, gf2-40-2]].

#### Elevator Room (first pass)

The floating jellyfish here are Biri, and you cannot hurt them yet; touching them hurts you. If one gets close, stun it with a Deku Nut [[gf1-106-1, gf2-40-3]]. Run around the left side along the platform and take the door on the other side [[gf1-106-1]].

#### Pit Room (first pass)

Step in and you meet Princess Ruto. She runs away and falls through a pit. Follow her down. Talk to her when you land, and talk to her again; she agrees to come with you if you help her find her stone. From now on you carry Ruto [[gf1-106-2, gf1-106-3]]. Pick her up and leave through the east door, which is behind you as you land [[gf1-106-2]].

#### Water Room

Drop Ruto and kill the Shaboms in the hallway with Deku Nuts. Pick her up again and run to the hall's end [[gf1-107-1]]. Jump into the pit, but do not step on the switch in the middle of the room yet. If you do, the water rises and Ruto returns to the Pit Room [[gf1-107-1]]. Throw Ruto up onto the ledge on the north side, then kill the enemies in the pit with you [[gf1-107-1]].

Use Deku Nuts to make the submerged Stingers jump, then shoot them with the Slingshot [[gf1-107-2]]. Kill the Gold Skulltula on the west ivy wall with the Slingshot (Skulltula #56). Then step on the switch to raise the water and climb the ivy into the alcove for the token [[gf1-107-3, checklist-skulltulas-11-1, gf2-40-4]].

Smash the pots in the alcove for a Fairy and Slingshot seeds. Capture the Fairy if you have an empty bottle [[gf1-107-4]]. Jump back into the pit, step on the switch again, and get onto the northern ledge where you left Ruto [[gf1-107-4]]. Pick her up and run into the hall. Kill more Shaboms with Deku Nuts, then hit the ceiling switch with the Slingshot. You can also throw Ruto at it. The door at the end unlocks [[gf1-107-5, gf2-40-5]].

#### Elevator Room (second pass)

Wait for the elevator to come down and kill the Octorok if you have to. Ride up carrying Ruto [[gf1-108-1]]. A submerged tunnel in this room leads to a Business Scrub selling Deku Nuts [[gf1-108-2]]. At the top, take the door on the right back into the Pit Room [[gf1-108-1]].

#### Pit Room (second pass)

Cross straight to the opposite door, avoiding Biri and the floor pits. If you fall you must go back through the Water Room and Elevator Room. Note the pit covered by the giant green tentacle; you will drop down it later [[gf1-108-3]]. Watch for long creatures that pop out of the ground next. Charge a Spin Attack until it glows orange to kill them in one hit [[gf2-40-6]].

#### Crawling Hallway and Stinger Room

Run past enemies in the hall. Go right and step onto the yellow switch while carrying Ruto. Her weight lets the switch activate, and the door in front of it unlocks [[gf1-109-1, gf2-40-6]]. Enter the Stinger Room.

The room is full of Stingers. Use Deku Nuts or run in front of their paths to reveal them, then shoot them with the Slingshot. If any are underground, bombs kill them in one hit. When all four are dead, a chest appears holding the **Boomerang** [[gf1-109-2, gf2-41-1, gf3-33-2]]. The Boomerang is the dungeon item of Jabu-Jabu's Belly and lets you grab Skulltula tokens from range [[gf1-386-3, gf1-109-3]]. Pick up Ruto and go back.

#### Orange Tentacle Room (Dungeon Map)

> [!WARNING]
> **Map Warning:** Get the Dungeon Map now while Ruto is still with you. Commenters report the Map cannot be reached without her unless you use a glitch, and one reports being locked out of it after skipping it [[ra-comments-121-6, ra-comments-3-3, ra-comments-122-12, ra-comments-124-7]].

At the far end of the Crawling Hallway is a blue switch in front of a door. Jump onto it while carrying Ruto, then put her down on it with A before stepping off. The switch stays down [[gf1-110-1, gf2-41-2]]. Go through the door without her.

Z-target the long Orange Tentacle and hit the narrow segment near its base with the Boomerang. Move forward until it stretches and makes a swishing noise, then throw the Boomerang while backing away. You need four hits in total. Killing it removes the other orange tentacles in the dungeon [[gf1-110-2, gf2-41-3, gf3-33-3]]. A chest appears holding the **Dungeon Map** [[gf1-110-3, gf4-13-6]].

#### Shabom Room (Compass)

Back in the hall, Ruto waits on the other side of the door and scolds you. Pick her up. Go left from the blue switch through the now-unblocked door [[gf1-111-1, gf1-111-2]]. You have forty seconds to pop every Shabom. Deku Nuts and the Slingshot both work, and a sword also works. A chest appears with the **Compass** [[gf1-111-3, gf2-41-4]].

#### Blue Tentacle Room

Return to the Crawling Hallway. Go down the hall, take a left, go straight, then left again, and enter the door [[gf1-111-4]]. Hit the Blue Tentacle with the Boomerang, step forward to bait its strike, and hit it again as you dodge. Four hits kill it and clear all Blue Tentacles in the dungeon [[gf1-112-1]]. Pick up Ruto and return [[gf1-112-1]].

#### Green Tentacle Room

From the hall, go right down the hall and take another right to the door at the end [[gf1-112-2]]. Kill the Biri first, then use the same hit-and-dodge pattern on the Green Tentacle. Four Boomerang hits remove every Green Tentacle [[gf1-112-3]]. Pick up Ruto and return to the hallway [[gf1-112-3]].

#### Pit Room: Skulltulas #57 and #58

Run straight across the hallway to the Pit Room [[gf1-113-1]]. Drop down the pit closest to the west wall, to your right as you enter from the hallway; the green tentacle used to cover it [[gf1-113-2, gf1-108-3]]. When you land, set Ruto down and face north. Two Gold Skulltulas hang on the wall. Kill each with the Boomerang, then throw the Boomerang again to collect both tokens (Skulltulas #57 and #58) [[gf1-113-3, checklist-skulltulas-11-2, checklist-skulltulas-11-3, gf2-41-5, gf2-41-6]]. When aiming manually, use Link's head as a targeting reticle [[gf1-113-3]]. Pick up Ruto and go through the west door [[gf1-113-3]].

#### Bigocto (mini-boss)

Throw Ruto up onto the ledge so she can claim her stone. The ledge rises, and when it comes back down she is gone and Bigocto has arrived [[gf1-113-3, gf2-42-1]]. Its weak point is the green eye on its backside. The Boomerang stuns it and locks it in place, so stun it and circle behind it, hugging the inner wall. Another stun, then a Jump Attack on the eye. Two Jump Attacks defeat it [[gf1-113-4, gf1-113-5, gf1-516-1, gf1-516-2]].

Two easier methods exist. You can let Bigocto run into you, which turns it around and lets you stun and hit it quickly [[gf1-516-3]]. Or Z-target it and throw the Boomerang as you head to its back [[gf3-34-1]]. One guide says a Deku Stick jump attack after a stun kills it in one hit [[checklist-sidequests-13-2]]. Another says about three sword hits on the green spot are needed [[gf2-42-1, gf2-121-1]]. Its contact does half a heart and the spiked middle does a quarter heart [[gf2-120-2]]. Stay locked on during the fight [[gf2-42-1]].

After it dies, one of the pots behind you holds a fairy [[gf3-34-2]]. Step onto the middle platform and the elevator rises. Run south down the hall and through the door, killing any Biri in the way [[gf1-113-6]].

#### Pink Thing Room and Elevator Room

Two Pink Things block the way. Stun the first with the Boomerang and jump onto it, then stun the second and jump to the door. Kill the Octorok if it gets in the way [[gf1-114-1, gf2-42-1]]. In the Elevator Room, jump across to the platform on the opposite wall. The ledge lowers and you end up next to the dungeon entrance. Run around to the door by the Pit Room, grab one of the boxes, and step on the blue button. Drop the box so it sits on the button, then go through the door [[gf1-114-2, gf2-42-2]].

#### Biri Room (Skulltula #59)

Kill all the Biri. Go to the southeast side and kill the Gold Skulltula on the ivy wall, halfway up the left side, with the Slingshot or the Boomerang. Climb up for the token (Skulltula #59) [[gf1-115-1, gf1-489-2, checklist-skulltulas-11-4, gf3-34-3]]. Continue to the top of the ivy wall. Look right to see a switch. Z-target it and hit it with the Boomerang to unlock the door at the north end of the room [[gf1-115-2]].

A glass panel blocks the switch from straight shots, so the Boomerang has to curve around it. Stand back and throw, or step to the left of the glass. It can take several tries [[gf2-42-4, gf3-34-4]]. Jump down afterwards [[gf1-115-2]].

Before opening the boss door, set Farore's Wind in this room. Commenters recommend it so you can warp straight back here after a failed attempt [[ra-comments-18-1, ra-comments-18-2]]. The Boomerang switch at the start of this run still has to be hit again after a reset [[ra-comments-18-3]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Rolling Thunder (Defeat Barinade without getting hit) can be missed. A hit fails it, so set Farore's Wind outside the boss room first. Then fight from a distance and reset on failure. In the Options menu, set Z-targeting to **Hold**; a guide strongly suggests this to make the fight much easier [[ra-guide-9-1, ra-comments-17-1, ra-comments-18-2, ra-comments-20-3]].

#### Barinade (boss)

Barinade is the first boss with a real chance of killing you [[gf1-115-3]]. All pots around the room contain Recovery Hearts [[gf2-43-1, gf2-121-2]]. Keep moving the whole time; you can walk and throw the Boomerang at once [[gf2-43-2, ra-comments-19-11]].

**Opening (all phases):** Sources agree that players are often hit by the jellyfish as soon as a phase starts, and the jellyfish eject in an unpredictable way [[ra-comments-20-8, ra-comments-17-13]]. A speedrun-style opening for the first phase: as soon as the boss starts, roll forward twice and take a couple of steps forward. Target the three tentacles up top with the Boomerang. Hit one, side-hop twice, hit the next, then side-hop back and hit the third [[ra-guide-9-2]]. Another player says to lock on, keep sidestepping and hit the three tentacles up top [[ra-comments-20-10]].

**Phase 1 (tentacles):** Z-target the tentacles attached to the ceiling and circle the boss. The direction does not matter, but stick with it, since changing course gets you electrocuted [[gf1-518-5]]. Sever all three with the Boomerang [[gf1-115-3, gf2-43-2, gf3-35-1]]. Wait until you see Navi floating over a target before pressing the lock-on button [[ra-comments-20-4]].

**Phase 2 (carousel of jellyfish):** The jellyfish detach and circle the boss. The rotating jellyfish cannot hit you if your back is against the wall. Wait there, Z-locked on Barinade, and look for an opening [[ra-comments-17-2, ra-comments-18-5]]. Stun the base with the Boomerang, which releases the jellyfish [[gf1-115-4, gf1-518-6]]. Stay to the side, since there is a counter attack at the end of the phase [[ra-comments-18-11]]. If you move in, hit once with the Boomerang, then slash five times and dodge back immediately [[ra-comments-17-3]].

Deku Nuts take out the loose jellyfish instantly if they are not tethered to the main body. Used well, they remove two or sometimes three at a time [[ra-comments-17-12, ra-comments-18-6, ra-comments-19-5]]. A stun and then Din's Fire wipes out most of them [[ra-comments-17-11]]. Throwing the Boomerang at the boss again resets the stun timer while you clear the loose ones [[ra-comments-21-11]]. Kill one or two jellyfish at a time and do not rush [[ra-comments-20-1, ra-comments-17-6]].

**Phase 3 (smaller, faster carousel):** This is the dangerous phase. The ring of jellyfish is smaller and faster, and Barinade moves around the room [[gf1-519-1]]. Keep throwing the Boomerang until it penetrates the ring and stuns him, then destroy the jellyfish. The satellite dishes resume attacking while you work [[gf1-519-1, gf1-519-2]]. Z-lock on early and keep moving sideways and back while throwing [[ra-comments-17-4, ra-comments-18-7]]. It can corner you against the wall, so back away in a curve rather than a straight line [[ra-comments-20-6, ra-comments-21-5]]. When he is stunned, lock on to a single jellyfish. With luck you may kill two or three in one stun [[ra-comments-17-5]]. A missed Boomerang throw can take away camera control, so some players use the Slingshot on the jellyfish instead. It is quicker and does not take the camera if you miss [[ra-comments-19-12, ra-comments-19-13]].

**Final phase (core exposed):** Stay at a distance. If you are too close, Barinade zaps you [[ra-comments-17-7]]. Stun him with the Boomerang, then run in and jump-attack the base. After two Jump Attacks he retreats into the ground briefly; keep dodging until he re-emerges [[gf1-519-3, gf1-519-4]]. Five Jump Attacks in total defeat him [[gf1-115-5, gf1-519-4]]. Deku Stick jump slashes after a Boomerang hit work for the damage, and a shield stab after a stun also kills him faster [[ra-guide-9-3, gf3-35-2, gf3-260-4]]. Another player says the stick method takes about three hits [[ra-comments-18-9]]. In the final phase, sidestep his beams and try not to get trapped against the wall [[ra-comments-18-10]]. Keep moving and avoid staying near him too long after you down him [[ra-comments-20-14]].

If you are retrying, use your first try to learn the pattern and see the whole fight through a few times rather than restarting at the first mistake [[ra-comments-21-1, ra-comments-18-12]]. Commenters say patience and safe play matter more than speed [[ra-comments-18-13, ra-comments-19-6]]. One player says binding the C buttons to the D-pad avoids misclicking Navi [[ra-comments-19-4]].

When Barinade falls with no hits taken:

[ACHIEVEMENT:48417]

Take the **Heart Container** he leaves, which brings you to nine hearts [[gf1-115-6, gf2-43-3, gf3-36-1]]. Then step into the ring of blue light. It teleports you and Ruto outside. Talk to her, say you want the Spiritual Stone, and agree to marry her; she gives you the Zora's Sapphire [[gf1-115-7, gf2-43-4, gf3-36-2, ra-guide-9-4]].

### Back in Zora's Fountain

Before leaving, grab the second Fountain Skulltula. It only appears at night, so play the Sun's Song if it is daytime. Climb onto the log sticking out of the water and look at the wall across from you. Shoot it with the Slingshot and fetch the token with the Boomerang (Skulltula #49) [[checklist-skulltulas-9-8, gf1-117-1, gf3-37-1, gf2-46-10, gf4-14-1]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Zora's Fountain:** Make sure you have Farore's Wind, the Map, the Compass, the Boomerang, the Heart Container, the Zora's Sapphire, and Skulltulas #48, #49 and #56 to #59. Then head back through the cave entrance on the west side of the area to return to Zora's Domain [[gf1-117-2, gf1-115-6, gf1-110-3, gf1-111-3]].
