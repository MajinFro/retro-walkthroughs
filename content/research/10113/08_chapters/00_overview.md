### What this game is

*The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* is a Nintendo 64 action-adventure game released on 1998-11-21. The achievement set has 97 achievements worth 690 points. Link can travel between childhood and adulthood by visiting the Temple of Time with the Master Sword and the Ocarina of Time [[gf1-10-2]]. When young Link opens the Door of Time, the Sacred Realm is unsealed and the three Triforce pieces go to Ganondorf (Power), Link (Courage) and Zelda (Wisdom) [[gf1-525-2]]. Things you do as a child, such as planting a magic bean, uncovering an item or befriending someone, directly affect the future [[gf1-10-3]].

The achievement set is built for the Ocarina of Time (U) (V1.0) ROM, MD5 5bd1fe107bf8106b2ab6650abecd54d6. A commenter also tells players to use the 1.0 U ROM [[ra-comments-1-2, ra-comments-4-7, ra-comments-3-1]]. Do not use any other version.

### Hardcore Mode and how the walkthrough is organised

This guide assumes Hardcore Mode, which turns off the emulator's save states, rewinds and cheats, so every attempt must be won with the game's own save system. Difficulty does not affect the set. Set Z-targeting to **Hold** for the boss challenges. Save before every missable boss and reset on failure. Saving inside a dungeon restarts you at its entrance.

You need **two separate save files**:

- **File 1 (Chapters 1-24)** is the 100% run. It collects everything, including every Map, Compass and Boss Key for the Cartographer achievement, and earns every achievement except the 3-heart ones.
- **File 2 (Chapters 25-27)** is the 3-heart run. It earns the nine 3-heart boss achievements and Blind Explorer. Blind Explorer forbids Maps, Compasses and any heart upgrade.

Achievements marked with "3 hearts" in parentheses need a 3-heart run [[ra-comments-3-2]]. One commenter describes the 3-heart run as limiting you to a maximum of 3 hearts, so Heart Containers cannot be collected [[ra-comments-1-1]]. A boss achievement reads your health bar, and any change to it fails the check [[ra-comments-132-1, ra-comments-132-2]].

Cartographer and Blind Explorer are mutually exclusive on one file. One commenter says you must choose between them at the start of the game, and that getting a heart, map or compass by accident forces a restart from the beginning [[ra-comments-141-6, ra-comments-141-7]]. Plenty of Hearts needs all 20 hearts, so it cannot share a file with the 3-heart run.

### Controls and combat basics

- **Z-targeting:** Navi's most important job is Z-Targeting, which locks Link's view onto one foe and changes his controls for battle [[gf1-13-1]].
- **A while targeted:** A strafes left or right, does a backflip when you move backward, and does the Jump Attack when you tilt forward. The Jump Attack is the most powerful attack in Link's repertoire [[gf1-13-2]].
- **R while targeted:** Holding R raises your shield while you keep moving [[gf1-13-3]].
- **B while targeted:** Tapping B gives a downward plunge, and forward plus B gives a stab [[gf1-13-4]].
- **Shield stab without lock-on:** Holding R without lock-on and pressing B stabs repeatedly, faster than a normal slash. It is recommended for killing bosses quickly [[gf3-9-1]].
- **Crouch stab:** This is done by holding R (not locked on) and pressing the attack button [[ra-comments-7-3]]. Commenters report that it copies the damage of your previous attack, so a hard hit followed by crouch stabs is very strong [[ra-comments-6-2, ra-comments-8-11]]. That stored damage resets when you enter a different room [[ra-comments-8-12]].
- **Spin attack:** The Advanced Spin Slash is done by holding B until the sword glows yellow, then releasing [[gf3-39-1]].
- **Deku Sticks:** A burning Deku Stick can be saved by drawing your sword, which puts it away before it burns out [[checklist-sidequests-6-3]].
- **Adult item limits:** Adult Link can only use the Deku Nuts, Ocarina of Time, Bombchus and Bombs among the items he got as a child. Bottles and magic powers work for either Link [[gf3-40-1]].

### Core systems you must understand

**The Ocarina.** Songs are learned from NPCs and Sheik, and the warp songs matter for travel. The Prelude of Light warps to the Temple of Time [[gf1-397-4]]. The Minuet of Forest warps to the Sacred Forest Meadow [[gf1-395-2]]. The Bolero of Fire warps to Death Mountain Crater [[gf1-395-4]]. The Serenade of Water warps to Lake Hylia [[gf1-396-2]]. The Nocturne of Shadow warps to the high ledge in Kakariko Graveyard [[gf1-397-2]]. The Requiem of Spirit warps to the Desert Colossus [[gf1-396-4]]. Playing Zelda's Lullaby in front of a Gossip Stone grants a health-restoring fairy [[gf3-19-2]]. Epona's Song gives free Lon Lon Milk from any cow in the game once you have an empty bottle [[gf1-43-2]].

**The Scarecrow's Song.** As a child, play any eight-note song of your own to the scarecrow at Lake Hylia. It cannot be one of the game's songs and must contain at least two different notes. Write it down, because you replay it as an adult to unlock the Scarecrow's Song [[gf1-98-2, gf1-165-1]]. If you forget it, Sheik at the Temple of Time teaches you how to return to childhood so you can teach Pierre a new one [[gf1-165-2]].

**Magic beans.** Beans are planted in soft soil as young Link. They only grow fully once you are an adult, and then become Magic Leaves that carry you to out-of-reach places [[gf3-173-2, gf1-385-1]]. Plan your bean planting as a child.

**Bottles.** Bottles hold fairies and Lon Lon Milk, and can capture bugs and Blue Fire [[gf1-383-3]].

**Heart Pieces.** There are 36 Pieces of Heart, and four make a Heart Container. Together with boss Heart Containers they give a maximum of 20 hearts [[gf3-123-1, ra-guide-20-2, gf2-159-17]].

**Gold Skulltulas.** Defeating Gold Skulltulas and taking their tokens frees members of the cursed family in Kakariko Village, and the House of Skulltula pays out prizes [[gf1-14-2]]. For skulltulas in soft soil, catch bugs from under rocks and drop them on the soil [[gf4-3-1]]. The Boomerang and later the Hookshot can collect tokens from a distance [[gf1-386-4, gf1-387-4]]. Take the token after killing the Skulltula, because the kill alone is not enough [[checklist-skulltulas-2-9]]. Always keep one bug when you empty a bottle, since you will need bugs again.

**Rupees.** Green rupees are worth 1, blue 5, red 20, purple 50 and huge yellow ones 200 [[gf3-168-3]]. You will need money for beans, Blue Fire, minigames and shop items.

**Silver Rupees.** Silver Rupees are puzzle rupees. They are worth five rupees each and open locked doors [[gf2-160-9]].

### Dungeon order logic

The first three dungeons are always the same. When you first become an adult you must obtain the Hookshot before anything else [[gf1-17-1]]. After that the order is flexible within limits:

- The Fire Temple and Ice Cavern can be completed without the Fairy Bow from the Forest Temple [[gf1-18-1]].
- With the Ice Cavern and Forest Temple done, you can start the Water Temple before the Fire Temple is finished [[gf1-18-2]].
- Once the Forest Temple is complete you can return to childhood at any time, and the Bottom of the Well becomes available [[gf1-18-4]].
- The Bottom of the Well must be completed before the Shadow Temple can be finished [[gf1-19-1]].
- Both the Bottom of the Well and Gerudo's Fortress must be done before the Spirit Temple can be attempted [[gf1-19-2]].

The chapters in this guide already follow a safe order that respects all of this. Follow them in sequence.

### The 100% plan in plain language

File 1 is one long pass through the game, split into 24 chapters. The first ten chapters take you through childhood: Kokiri Forest, the Deku Tree, Hyrule Castle Town, Kakariko, Lon Lon Ranch, the Goron and Zora areas, and the three child dungeons. They end with the Ocarina of Time. Chapters 11 to 21 cover adulthood: the temples, the Biggoron's Sword trading quest and the Gerudo areas. The remaining chapters mop up Lon Lon Ranch, the fishing pond and the Gossip Stone tour, then finish with Ganon's Castle.

Doing the child cleanup chapters fully before drawing the Master Sword matters because several things cannot be done later. Do not skip ahead.

File 2 is short. It covers only the child dungeons, the adult temples and Ganon's Castle, because the nine 3-heart boss achievements and Blind Explorer are the only things it needs. On that file you collect nothing that raises health or reveals a dungeon.

### Missables to keep in mind early

Each chapter warns you right before a trap, but these are worth knowing from the start, and the sources below are the reason for each:

- **Deku Nut upgrades.** Wearing the Mask of Truth on the Lost Woods forest stage and speaking to the Scrub Chief raises your Deku Nut capacity [[gf1-125-3]]. Commenters say this is missable because a flag set by the Poacher's Saw makes the second upgrade unobtainable [[ra-comments-44-1, ra-comments-44-2, ra-comments-44-3]]. A separate upgrade is sold by the Deku Scrub in a hidden grotto under a rock before the Sacred Forest Meadow entrance, and that one is the Stocking Nuts target [[ra-guide-8-7, ra-comments-73-1]]. It is **unconfirmed** whether the Lost Woods upgrades can still be earned as an adult before the Poacher's Saw. Get A Pretty Face, A Horrible Face and Stocking Nuts as Young Link before drawing the Master Sword.
- **The Hylian Shield chest.** A commenter warns that buying the shield from the Bazaar replaces the chest that gives the achievement with a rupee chest [[ra-comments-64-1]]. Do not buy the shield.
- **The dying soldier.** The guard in the Castle Town back alley is missable. He disappears for good after the Temple of Time cutscenes, so talk to him after Zelda gives you the Ocarina and before you go there [[ra-guide-10-4]]. One commenter says he appears after Zelda and Impa escape [[ra-comments-78-2]].
- **Zelda's window.** Shooting the window on the left with the slingshot right before speaking to Zelda angers a guard and triggers a dialogue. This is missable because you cannot return after collecting all three Spiritual Stones [[ra-guide-5-4, gf1-39-1]].
- **The gate guard's bribe.** It is **unconfirmed** exactly what triggers the bribe offer for Money Solves Everything. Comments mention a hatched egg from Malon and talking to the guard twice [[ra-comments-56-1]]. Have Malon's egg, be caught once and return, doing all three before meeting Zelda and no other story step in between.
- **Goron City rocks.** The entrance rocks in Goron City carry permanent flags, so destroying them can ruin the related achievement [[ra-comments-68-1]]. A commenter says you can instead jump to the side of them [[ra-comments-68-2]].
- **Dampe's heart piece.** If you dig up the heart piece in the gravedigging minigame and leave without collecting it, it is lost forever [[ra-comments-101-1]].
- **King Zora and the tunic.** Buy the Zora Tunic before you thaw King Zora with Blue Fire. Then he offers a kiss instead of the free Zora Tunic [[ra-comments-37-3, ra-guide-11-6]]. It is **unconfirmed** whether the Zora Tunic must be worn while thawing King Zora for Kiss That Frog. Buy the tunic, equip it, save, then thaw King Zora and talk to him while still wearing it. A commenter also suggests killing 30 Gold Skulltulas and getting the Giant's Wallet first [[ra-comments-37-1]].
- **Twinrova.** Saving after dying on this boss fight makes the achievement fail, and a soft reset and reload is needed [[ra-comments-46-1, ra-comments-46-2]].
- **Fishing.** Catching a fish of 10 lbs or more and then doing the early Golden Scale glitch locks you out of the heart piece [[ra-comments-112-2]]. It is **unconfirmed** how heavy the adult fish must be for Expert Angler, since commenters disagree between 12 and 13 lb. Keep fishing until the owner hands over the Golden Scale. The Sinking Lure is not allowed for the heart piece [[checklist-heartpieces-6-1]].

### Cartographer and the Jabu-Jabu map

Cartographer needs every Map, Compass and Boss Key from every dungeon [[ra-comments-3-4, ra-comments-121-2]]. A Boss Key on the way up Ganon's Castle should bring the counter to 26/26 [[ra-comments-121-3]]. Commenters report that the Jabu-Jabu's Belly map room is troublesome without Ruto. The reported method is to target the monster just outside the map room and press C-Up as you land on the switch, so the door opens even though the switch stays unpressed [[ra-comments-3-5, ra-comments-121-6]]. Treat this as a missable until the Jabu-Jabu chapter walks through it.

### Three-heart and special fight notes

- **My Last Breath (Ganon).** It is **unconfirmed** whether Bombchus count as bombs for My Last Breath. Use neither bombs nor Bombchus against Ganon.
- **Dangerously Delicious (King Dodongo).** It is **unconfirmed** whether Deku Stick hits stop it from triggering, since commenters disagree. Use the Bomb Flower and the Kokiri Sword only for King Dodongo on the 3-heart file.
- **Pieces of Heart on file 2.** One source says up to three Pieces of Heart are fine, and another says no heart upgrades are allowed [[ra-comments-103-5, ra-comments-132-1]]. This is **unconfirmed**. Pick up no Pieces of Heart and no Heart Containers on the 3-heart file.

### Practical advice before you start

Save often, but follow the per-chapter warnings about when saving is dangerous. Keep a bottle free for bugs, a spare for Blue Fire, and enough rupees on hand before each shop or minigame. Use the Song of Time, Zelda's Lullaby and the Sun's Song as the game's own tools. Write your eight-note scarecrow song on paper.

When you are ready, begin Chapter 1 on a fresh save file for the 100% run.
