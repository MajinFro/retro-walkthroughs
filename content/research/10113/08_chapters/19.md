## Chapter 19: Gerudo Valley and Gerudo's Fortress

[MERMAID]

This chapter has two jobs. Cross the broken bridge in Gerudo Valley, then free the four captured carpenters in Gerudo's Fortress to earn the Gerudo's Membership Card [[gf1-278-1, gf1-420-1]]. With the card you can play the Horseback Archery range, which gives a Piece of Heart at 1,000 points and the Biggest Quiver at 1,500 points [[gf1-285-3, gf1-286-7, gf1-286-8]]. Two achievements are placed here: Gerudo's Fan Club (the card) and Ultimate Horse Master (the top archery reward). The new collectibles are the roof-chest Piece of Heart, the archery Piece of Heart, and two night-only Gold Skulltulas at the fortress [[checklist-heartpieces-5-7, gf1-286-7, checklist-skulltulas-18-1, checklist-skulltulas-18-2]].

> [!TIP]
> **Preparation:** Bring Epona or the Longshot for the bridge [[gf1-278-1]]. Bring the Bow, because arrows stun patrolling guards [[gf1-279-3]]. Bring Deku Nuts for the sword-wielding Gerudo fights [[checklist-sidequests-16-2]]. The Hover Boots help in the kitchen room [[gf1-282-1]]. The Longshot reaches the roof chest [[gf1-447-1]]. Have 20 Rupees ready for each archery round [[gf2-88-7]].

### Gerudo Valley

Go up the log bridge near the valley entrance. At the fallen bridge spanning the canyon, either jump the gap on Epona or pull yourself across with the Longshot [[gf1-278-1]]. If you ride, charge straight at the gap at full speed [[gf1-358-2]]. The valley's Skulltulas and Pieces of Heart were already collected in Chapters 8, 10 and 13, so this section is only a crossing [[gf1-278-1]].

Once across, speak with the Master Carpenter. He explains that he came to repair the bridge, but his assistants ran off to join the Gerudo Thieves and were captured [[gf1-278-2]].

> [!TIP]
> If you still have the Poacher's Saw from the Biggoron sword trading sequence, show it to this carpenter. He gives you the Broken Goron's Sword in exchange [[gf1-358-2, gf2-81-1]].

Rocks near the bridge hide a chest with a Purple Rupee, which you can break with the Megaton Hammer [[gf3-75-1, gf2-87-3]]. If you play the Song of Storms behind the carpenter's tent, a secret cave opens. Inside, two Business Scrubs sell Green and Red Potions for 40 Rupees each [[gf3-239-1, gf3-52-11]].

Then continue to the fortress [[gf1-278-2]].

### Gerudo's Fortress

#### Getting caught

The Gerudo Thieves throw you in prison if they see you [[gf1-279-1]]. Run north from the entrance until you reach a stairset on the right, climb it, and let a purple-suited patroller see you [[gf1-279-1]]. After that, any time you are spotted in the fortress you are sent back to the cell [[gf1-279-1]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** If you are caught without the Hookshot or Longshot, you are thrown out in front of the carpenter's tent instead of into the cell [[checklist-sidequests-16-1]].

In the cell, look south and high on the wall for a window. Shoot the wooden awning with the Longshot to pull yourself up [[gf1-279-2]]. From the sill you can see the patrolling Gerudos below. Stun the one closest to the door on the left with an arrow, then jump down and enter before she recovers [[gf1-279-3]]. The Longshot also stuns, but not for nearly as long [[gf1-279-3]].

#### Carpenter 1: Ichiro

Run around the corner to find the first carpenter, Ichiro. Speak with him through the bars and a Gerudo Thief attacks [[gf1-280-1]]. She fights somewhat like a Stalfos but faster. Attack right after she attacks, and watch for her stun attack [[gf1-280-1]]. She telegraphs it by crouching slightly and holding her swords far apart [[gf1-280-1]]. If it hits, you lose the fight and go back to the cell [[gf1-280-1, gf3-247-2]].

Deku Nuts can stun her in the middle of any attack, including that special one [[checklist-sidequests-16-2]]. When she is stunned, a jump attack knocks her down and you can follow with another [[checklist-sidequests-16-2]]. If she blocks the nut, follow with a jump attack as she lowers her guard [[checklist-sidequests-16-2]]. Arrows also work well, though it takes many of them [[gf1-280-1]]. Without nuts, dodge her special attack by jumping aside once or twice [[checklist-sidequests-16-2]].

Defeat her, take the Small Key she leaves, and open Ichiro's cell [[gf1-280-2]]. Speak with him to free him. The carpenters will not leave until you talk to them [[gf1-280-3, checklist-sidequests-16-3]]. Then leave by the door on the left [[gf1-280-3]].

A fairy fountain is hidden near the crates across from the exit. You need the Song of Storms to open it [[checklist-sidequests-16-4]]. Stand in the small enclosed area on the left and play it [[gf2-88-1]].

#### Carpenter 2: Sabooro

The hallway route from Ichiro's exit to the roof corner is not covered in detail here. Unable to verify from collected sources.

When you come out on the roof, run toward the corner and stop before you go around it. Peek around using Z-Focus and you will see a patrolling Gerudo Guard. Wait until she faces away, then shoot her in the back with an arrow [[gf1-281-1]]. Run around the corner, jump up the ledge, and take the door on the right [[gf1-281-1]].

Run to the top of the ramp and speak with Sabooro in his cell at the far end of the room. The closer cell holds only pots with junk [[gf1-281-2]]. Defeat the Gerudo Thief who appears, take her Small Key, and open Sabooro's cell. Speak with him again to free him [[gf1-281-2, gf1-281-3]]. Head right and up around the corner, then through the doorway [[gf1-281-3]].

#### The kitchen and the roof chest

The next door on the left leads back into the kitchen. From the doorway, you can see two guards down the ramp to the right [[gf1-282-1]]. Shoot both with arrows to stun them. Then equip the Hover Boots, run out to the ledge directly ahead, and run across to the ramp on the other side. Swap back to the Kokiri Boots and go through the doorway [[gf1-282-1]].

> [!TIP]
> Reaching the platform in this room requires the Hover Boots or the Hookshot/Longshot. You cannot simply jump to it [[checklist-sidequests-17-1]].

Outside, it needs to be night for the Skulltula. Play the Sun's Song if it is not [[gf1-283-1]]. Jump up the ledge to the south and run along the roof to its end. Face west and jump the gap to the adjacent roof. Go around the corner to a wall covered in ivy and climb it to reach the top level of the fortress [[gf1-283-1]].

Follow the wall so you are running north. At the north end, an adjacent roof holds a chest. At night a Gold Skulltula clings to its east wall. Kill it and take the token with the Longshot; this is Gold Skulltula #91 [[gf1-283-2, checklist-skulltulas-18-2]]. The gap is too far to jump. Longshot the chest to pull yourself across; the Longshot range only just reaches it [[gf1-283-3]]. If you cannot reach it, play the Scarecrow's Song to make Pierre appear as a closer target [[gf1-283-3, checklist-sidequests-24-5]]. Open the chest for a **Piece of Heart** [[gf1-283-3, checklist-heartpieces-5-7, gf3-75-2]]. You can also smash the crate near here for a Purple Rupee [[gf2-87-5]].

Then play the Sun's Song to warp back to the doorway from the kitchen [[gf1-283-3]].

#### Carpenter 3: Jiro

From the doorway, look north. A small jump down leads to a ledge with a single door. Drop down and enter [[gf1-283-3]]. As soon as you are in, pull out the Bow and stand still. When the guard comes around the corner, shoot her before she sees you [[gf1-284-1]]. Run around the corner to the right, left at the next corner, down the hall and around another corner to a cell at a dead end [[gf1-284-1]].

Speak with Jiro and defeat the next Gerudo Thief. Take her Small Key and open Jiro's cell [[gf1-284-1]]. Speak with him and he escapes [[gf1-284-2]]. Return the way you came, watching for the thief near the entrance, who may no longer be stunned [[gf1-284-2]].

#### Carpenter 4: Shiro, and the card

Outside the door from Jiro's cell, look west and drop off the cliff to the roof below. The door on the right as you land leads on [[gf1-284-2]]. Run down the hall and around the corner to the last carpenter, Shiro, locked in his cell [[gf1-285-1]].

Speak with him and defeat the Gerudo Thief who appears. Use the Small Key she drops to open the cell, then run inside and speak with Shiro [[gf1-285-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48896]

After Shiro is freed, a Gerudo Lieutenant appears. She is impressed by your sneaking and makes you an honorary Gerudo Thief with the **Gerudo's Membership Card** [[gf1-285-2, gf2-88-3, gf3-75-6]]. That is the condition for Gerudo's Fan Club [[ra-guide-16-1]].

The card does the following:
- It lets you roam the fortress without being harassed or thrown in jail [[gf1-285-3, gf1-420-2]].
- It opens the Gerudo Training Ground and the Horseback Archery Range [[gf1-285-3, gf1-420-2]].
- It lets you open the northwest gate to the Haunted Wasteland [[gf1-285-3]].
- The bridge back in Gerudo Valley is now repaired [[gf1-285-3]].

If you want the Ice Arrows, the Training Ground entrance is in the southeast corner. Speak to the Gerudo by the grating and pay the fee [[gf1-286-1, gf2-116-1]]. The guide quotes a 10 Rupee fee [[gf2-89-2]]. Finishing every room later needs the Silver Gauntlets from the Spirit Temple, but the Ice Arrows can be taken now [[gf1-286-2]]. This is optional for this chapter.

### Horseback Archery Range

Make sure it is daytime for the minigame; the Gerudo who runs it appears only then, so use the Sun's Song if needed [[gf1-286-5]]. Epona's Song summons Epona if she is not nearby, and she appears near the south entrance [[gf1-286-5]]. If you lack Epona, the Gerudos lend you a horse [[gf1-286-6]]. Ride east up the passage on Epona to reach the range [[gf1-286-3]].

For Gold Skulltula #90, go to the range at night first. It is high on the lone target on the north side. Kill it with the Longshot and take the token [[checklist-skulltulas-18-1, gf1-286-4, gf1-479-2]]. Then return to the awning on the south side [[gf1-286-4]].

Speak to the Gerudo dressed in white beneath the awning. You must be riding Epona when you talk to her [[gf1-286-5]]. One round costs 20 Rupees and you get 20 arrows [[gf2-88-7]]. You do not steer Epona; she runs automatically and you only fire [[gf2-115-2]].

Scoring:
- Pots are worth 100 points each [[gf1-512-1]].
- Targets are worth 100 at the exact center, 60 on the outer circle and 30 on the edge [[gf2-88-4]].

Epona runs up and down the course once in each direction before the game ends [[gf1-512-2]].

Strategy:
- On the first pass, shoot every pot as you pass. There are eight pots on the way down [[gf1-512-2, checklist-sidequests-24-4]].
- After the last pot, shoot the big target at the end as Epona loops around it [[checklist-sidequests-24-4]].
- On the way back, put one in each of the three bull's-eyes, then spend remaining arrows on the last target [[checklist-sidequests-24-4]].
- Another version of the plan is to shoot the east targets until you are near enough to hit the south target accurately [[gf1-512-2]].
- Move your aim right to collect all the pots on the first pass, then spend most of your remaining arrows on the target at the end of the track [[ra-comments-97-6]].
- Aim for the pots before the bull's-eyes [[ra-comments-97-3]].
- Another guide suggests shooting all the pots at the bottom, then hitting the first target on the middle left as much as you can while turning around [[ra-guide-16-3]].

One player lowered analog stick sensitivity to about 0.8 and cleared it on the next try after 15 failed attempts [[ra-comments-97-7, ra-comments-97-8]]. Another needed around 100 to 150 tries and finished at 1,510 [[ra-comments-97-5]]. Expect a grind.

Score at least 1,000 points to win the **Piece of Heart** [[gf1-286-7, checklist-heartpieces-5-8]]. Then play again and score at least 1,500 to win the **Biggest Quiver**, which raises your arrow capacity to 50 [[gf1-286-8, gf2-88-6, gf2-155-7]]. If you reach 1,500 on your very first attempt, you must play again and repeat it to earn the Quiver [[gf1-512-3, gf1-286-8]]. Scoring a perfect 2,000 gives nothing extra [[gf1-286-8]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** A commenter says you must already own the big quiver from the shooting gallery, or the achievement will not unlock [[ra-comments-97-1]]. The achievement is tied to the Biggest Quiver reward for 1,500 points [[ra-comments-97-2, ra-guide-16-2]]. Treat the Quiver as the goal and do not leave the range until you have it.

### Before you leave

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Gerudo's Fortress:** Confirm you have the Gerudo's Membership Card and the Biggest Quiver [[gf1-285-3, gf1-286-8]]. Collect the roof-chest Piece of Heart and the archery Piece of Heart [[checklist-heartpieces-5-7, gf1-286-7]]. Pick up Gold Skulltulas #90 and #91 now, since the Longshot is already in your hands [[checklist-skulltulas-18-1, checklist-skulltulas-18-2]]. When ready, head for the northwest corner of the fortress, climb the tower, and speak to the Gerudo at the top. She opens the gate to the Haunted Wasteland [[gf1-287-1, gf1-277-1]].
