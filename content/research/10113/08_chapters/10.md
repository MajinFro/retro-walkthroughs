## Chapter 10: Young Link Cleanup and the Ocarina of Time

[MERMAID]

This chapter closes out Young Link's story. You collect the Ocarina of Time after the drawbridge cutscene and learn the Song of Time. Then you finish the child-only tasks before opening the Door of Time: the dying guard, the Happy Mask Salesman quest, the Mask of Truth Deku Nut upgrade and the Gold Skulltula rewards. After you draw the Master Sword you cannot return to childhood until after the Forest Temple [[gf1-10-1, gf1-129-2, ra-guide-10-6]]. The Master Sword comes last in this chapter for that reason.

> [!TIP]
> **Before you start:** The Boomerang is needed for most of the Skulltula and Heart Piece tasks below. Bring bombs and a bottle of bugs, and have Din's Fire ready for Skulltula #13 [[gf1-457-1, gf1-457-2, checklist-skulltulas-5-2]].

### Hyrule Field

🗺️ **Map:** [View Hyrule Field Map by thegamemaster8](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/5394-hyrule-field-map)

#### Zelda's escape and the Ocarina of Time

1. With all three Spiritual Stones, approach the closed gates of Hyrule Castle. A cutscene plays: Zelda and Impa race out and Zelda throws the Ocarina of Time into the moat [[gf1-123-4, ra-guide-10-3]].
2. When the scene ends, dive into the moat water to pick up the Ocarina of Time. Zelda then contacts you telepathically and teaches the Song of Time [[gf1-123-5, gf2-45-2, gf3-37-13]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48512]

The Song of Time is Right C, A, Down C, Right C, A, Down C [[gf1-394-1, gf3-234-5]]. The Ocarina of Time replaces the Fairy Ocarina [[gf3-147-2]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** The next stop is the dying guard in the Market back alley. Do this BEFORE the Temple of Time cutscenes, because the guard never appears again afterward [[ra-guide-10-4, checklist-sidequests-15-1]].

#### Hyrule Field tasks (Boomerang)

- **Skulltula #12 (lone tree near Kakariko):** Between Hyrule Castle and Kakariko is a lone tree. Place a bomb at its base to open a hole. Inside, kill the Skulltula with the Slingshot and take the token with the Boomerang [[checklist-skulltulas-5-1, gf1-457-1, gf1-119-4, gf1-119-5]].
- **Skulltula #13 (needs Din's Fire):** Outside the Gerudo Valley entrance is a circle of rocks. Bomb the center and drop down. Burn the webs with Din's Fire, then collect the Skulltula in the nook with the cow using the Boomerang [[checklist-skulltulas-5-2, gf1-457-2, gf2-45-1]]. If you lack Din's Fire, see the Hyrule Castle section below.
- **Fenced grotto by Lake Hylia:** its Business Scrub Piece of Heart (#12) was bought in Chapter 8, so there is nothing left to do there [[gf1-426-1, gf1-100-2, checklist-heartpieces-3-12]].

### Hyrule Market

#### The dying guard (Last Breath)

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Talk to the dying guard NOW, after Zelda's escape and before the Temple of Time. He exists only after you have the three Spiritual Stones and before you go to the Temple of Time [[ra-comments-78-6, ra-guide-10-4]].

1. Go to the back alleys of the Castle Market [[ra-comments-78-3]].
2. Find the guard and talk to him to hear his final words. He appears after Zelda and Impa escape [[ra-comments-78-2]].
3. One commenter found that searching at night failed and the guard had to be found during the day. Another says the achievement now triggers by day or night [[ra-comments-78-5, ra-comments-78-4]]. If it does not pop, try the other time of day.

[ACHIEVEMENT:48513]

One commenter reported that speaking to him after getting the Ocarina did not activate the achievement. The cause is unconfirmed [[ra-comments-78-1]].

#### Other Market tasks

- **Rupees:** Breaking the pots in the room at the Market entrance earns about 100 rupees as Young Link [[gf4-3-2]].
- **Market guardhouse crate Skulltula (#18):** the crate beside the guard in the room with the pots was already taken in Chapter 2, so skip it [[gf1-35-1, gf2-23-2, checklist-sidequests-8-2]].
- **Happy Mask Shop:** It is the small building near the Temple of Time, right of the Hyrule Castle Garden entrance. It is used in the quest below [[gf1-59-1]].

### Hyrule Castle

🗺️ **Map:** [View Hyrule Palace Map by ThE_bLImP](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/52-hyrule-palace-map)

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave:** The courtyard events (the window shot and the guard bribe) cannot be redone once the Master Sword is drawn, and the courtyard cannot be re-entered after all three stones [[ra-guide-5-4, ra-comments-58-3]]. These castle tasks remain available.

- **Din's Fire:** Go through the Hyrule Castle Garden, bomb the boulder at the dead end past the gate and crawl through to the Great Fairy's Fountain. Stand on the Triforce emblem and play Zelda's Lullaby to learn Din's Fire [[gf1-90-1, gf1-90-2, gf3-233-2]].
- **Skulltula (Song of Storms tree):** Near the moat is a lone tree. Play the Song of Storms beside it to open a hole, bomb the walls inside and take the token with the Boomerang [[checklist-skulltulas-5-9, gf1-459-2, gf1-173-3]].
- **Skulltula (castle grounds tree):** Roll into the lone tree outside the outer gate to knock a Skulltula loose [[checklist-skulltulas-5-8, gf3-179-3]].

### Lost Woods

🗺️ **Map:** [View Lost Woods Map by EverythingZelda](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/54-lost-woods-map)

#### Mask of Truth on the Forest Stage (A Horrible Face)

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** The Forest Stage Deku Nut upgrade (A Horrible Face) becomes unobtainable once you get the Poacher's Saw in the adult trading quest, and one source says it is also lost after you pull the Master Sword [[ra-guide-10-2, gf3-38-4, ra-comments-44-2]]. Whether it can still be earned as an adult before the Poacher's Saw is unconfirmed, because players disagree [[ra-comments-44-9, ra-comments-44-1]]. Get it as Young Link before drawing the Master Sword, along with A Pretty Face and Stocking Nuts [[gf1-418-1, ra-comments-73-3]].

1. Finish the Happy Mask quest (section below) until you hold the Mask of Truth [[gf1-124-2, gf4-14-14]].
2. Enter the Lost Woods and follow right, left, right, left, left to the grassy area with the Forest Stage [[gf3-142-1, gf3-199-3]].
3. Near the large tree, find the cluster of butterflies in the tall grass and drop into the hidden hole [[gf1-125-2, ra-comments-44-8]].
4. Put on the Mask of Truth and step forward. Speak to the Chief (the one with the mustache) on the right side of the room to receive the Deku Nut upgrade [[gf1-125-3, gf1-418-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:49188]

One player reports that doing the Mask of Truth upgrade before the boulder scrubs stopped Stocking Nuts from unlocking for them, so get the boulder-scrub upgrade first [[ra-comments-44-6]].

#### Skulltulas and beans in the Lost Woods

- **Forest Stage bean spot:** In the grassy Forest Stage area is the soft-soil patch whose bug Skulltula (#5) you took in Chapter 4. Plant a Magic Bean there. Adult Link can ride the plant at night to another Skulltula [[gf1-125-1, checklist-skulltulas-3-6, gf1-455-1, gf3-37-6]].
- **Bean spot (two lefts):** Plant a bean in the soft soil reached by going left twice from the entrance [[gf3-37-5, checklist-sidequests-13-22]].

### Kakariko Village

#### The windmill Piece of Heart (#5)

With the Boomerang, enter the windmill and look at the Heart Piece high on the left ledge. When the moving platform carries you away from the piece on the left side, throw the Boomerang at the top of the heart to pull it down [[gf1-121-2, checklist-sidequests-13-7, checklist-heartpieces-3-5]].

#### Kakariko Skulltulas

Several Skulltulas appear here at night: the tree near the entrance, the Skulltula House window, the unfinished house, and the lookout tower ladder [[gf3-20-4, gf3-20-5, gf3-20-6, gf3-20-8]].

- **Skulltula #17:** High on the wall to the right of the silo behind the corral [[checklist-skulltulas-5-6, gf3-37-15]].

- **Skulltula #53 (night):** On the back of the Lakeside Laboratory. Stand on the bridge and use the Boomerang [[checklist-skulltulas-10-3, checklist-sidequests-13-18]].

- **Skulltula #61 (night):** Walking up the plank to the big bridge, turn right and use the Boomerang [[checklist-skulltulas-12-2, gf3-37-18]].

### Inside the Deku Tree

**Skulltula #11 (bombable wall):** Drop through the hole in the middle of the first floor, climb the block, crawl through the crawl hole on the far wall, bomb the weak wall, kill the Skulltula and take the token with the Boomerang [[checklist-skulltulas-4-4, gf1-484-2, checklist-sidequests-13-20]].

### Happy Mask Salesman quest

For each mask: borrow it at the shop, sell it, then return and pay the fee.

| Mask | Sell to | Notes | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Keaton Mask | Death Mountain gate guard, Kakariko | Show Zelda's Letter first; sells for 15 | [[gf1-58-3, gf1-59-3, gf1-350-1]] |
| Skull Mask | Lone Skull Kid, Lost Woods | Play Saria's Song first for the Heart Piece; sells for 10 | [[gf1-65-3, gf1-527-3, ra-comments-79-3]] |
| Spooky Mask | Graveyard kid (daytime) | Sells for 30 | [[gf1-92-2, gf1-351-1]] |
| Bunny Hood | Running Man in Hyrule Field | Wait for him to sit; appears after Zelda flees | [[gf1-351-2, ra-comments-79-5, ra-comments-79-7]] |

1. Show Zelda's Letter to the guard, bring him the Keaton Mask from the shop and sell it for 15 rupees [[gf1-59-3, gf2-47-2]].
2. Return and pay the 10-rupee fee, then borrow the Skull Mask [[gf1-60-1]].
3. Sell the Skull Mask to the Lone Skull Kid in the Lost Woods [[gf1-65-3, gf2-160-13]].
4. Pay the shop, then borrow the Spooky Mask and sell it to the Graveyard kid in the daytime [[gf1-92-1, gf1-92-2]].
5. Pay for the Spooky Mask and borrow the Bunny Hood [[gf1-93-1]]. The Running Man runs counter-clockwise around Lon Lon Ranch and appears after Zelda flees [[ra-comments-79-5, ra-comments-79-6]].
6. Sell the Bunny Hood to the Running Man. Without the Giant's Wallet you receive only 99 or 200 of the 500 rupees, so claim the Giant's Wallet first [[gf1-351-3, checklist-sidequests-13-8]].
7. Return to the shop, pay the fee and borrow the Mask of Truth [[gf1-124-2, checklist-sidequests-13-9]].

### Temple of Time

> [!CAUTION]
> **Point of No Return:** After the Temple of Time cutscene the dying guard (Last Breath) is gone, and with three Spiritual Stones Zelda's courtyard is closed. Finish both, the Happy Mask quest and the Mask of Truth Deku upgrade before drawing the Master Sword [[ra-guide-10-4, ra-guide-5-4, ra-comments-58-3]].

1. Enter the Temple of Time from the Market. It stands to the right of the Happy Mask Shop [[gf1-128-4]].
2. Stand on the red carpet before the Door of Time and play the Song of Time to open it [[gf1-129-1, gf4-15-1, ra-guide-10-7]].
3. Walk through and draw the Master Sword from its pedestal [[gf1-129-2]].
4. Link is sealed away for seven years and wakes up as Adult Link, and Rauru gives you the Light Medallion [[gf3-38-5, gf1-421-4]].
