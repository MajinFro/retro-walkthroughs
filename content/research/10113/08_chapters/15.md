[MERMAID]

Sniper Elite is available as soon as you have the Fairy Bow and arrive in Kakariko Village as an adult. After that, head up Death Mountain to learn the Bolero of Fire, collect two crater Pieces of Heart, and clear the Fire Temple. Hot Hot Hot is the one achievement here that can be lost: you must beat Volvagia without wearing the Goron Tunic. The Fire Medallion (Eternal Brothers) follows the boss. [[gf1-134-2, gf2-60-7, gf1-172-2, ra-guide-13-3]]

### Preparation and Overview

The Fire Temple does not strictly need the Fairy Bow from the Forest Temple, but you have it, and the map chest is easiest with it. [[gf1-18-1, gf1-187-1]]

- **Goron Tunic:** Wear it in the Crater and the Fire Temple, because it removes the heat time limit. Without it, Young Link dies when the heat timer runs out in the Crater, and fairies do not save him. [[gf1-171-4, gf1-435-2]]
- **Tunic caution:** One commenter confirms that if you get the Longshot and beat the Fire Temple before getting the Goron Tunic, the tunic becomes completely unobtainable. Collect it from Link the Goron before finishing the temple. [[ra-comments-91-2]]
- **Magic:** Bring at least one or two Green Potions, since Din's Fire eats a small magic bar quickly. [[gf3-98-10]]
- **Fairies:** Bottle some for the boss. Two sit in the containers on the right side of the room before the boss room, and two more are in the pots near the platform by the boss door. [[ra-comments-32-21, ra-comments-32-22]]
- **Dungeon structure:** The boss chamber is next door to the entrance. The challenge is reaching the top floor to activate the route to its door. Keys come from rescuing the trapped Gorons, and even the Boss Key is held by one of them. [[gf1-178-1, gf1-178-2]]

### Kakariko Village

#### Sniper Elite: the adult Shooting Gallery

Head for the building across from the Windmill. It was the unfinished structure in your childhood and is now the Shooting Gallery. [[gf1-168-2, gf1-134-2, ra-guide-13-1]] Pay 20 Rupees to play with the Fairy Bow. [[gf3-55-1]]

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Only play for the reward once you hold the Fairy Bow. One commenter reports that winning before getting arrows paid 50 rupees but did not unlock the achievement. Another had the bigger quiver yet no achievement after winning at the wrong stage. [[ra-comments-90-2, ra-comments-90-3]]

Your goal is to hit all ten Rupees in a single run. The prize is the Big Quiver, which raises arrow capacity from 30 to 40. [[gf2-60-7, gf2-102-3, gf1-407-1]]

- Hitting at least eight of ten earns a free replay. [[gf2-60-8]]
- The targets follow these patterns: [[gf1-168-3]]
  1. In the middle. [[gf1-168-3]]
  2. From the left, then from the right. [[gf1-168-3]]
  3. Jumps from the middle. [[gf1-168-3]]
  4. From the left and right at once. [[gf1-168-3]]
  5. Two rupees from the right, going left. [[gf1-168-3]]
  6. Two rupees from the left, going right. [[gf1-168-3]]
- If a single blue rupee comes from the left, the next one always comes from the right. [[gf1-168-4]]
- Memorize the patterns to predict what comes next. You can also tell what the last pattern will be from what has already appeared. [[gf1-511-2]]
- On RetroArch, a stick sensitivity of 0.9 or 0.8 is recommended. [[ra-comments-90-4]]
- Arrows are sold at the Kakariko Bazaar: 10 for 20 Rupees, 30 for 60, 50 for 90. [[gf2-140-43, gf2-140-40, gf2-140-41]]

When you are finished, leave by the northern gate to Death Mountain Trail. You already showed Zelda's Letter to the gate guard as a child. [[gf1-58-2, gf1-60-2]]

### Death Mountain Trail

Climb the trail and take the right fork at the large wooden post with the red flag to reach Goron City. [[gf1-62-5]]

If you have not yet received the Goron Tunic, bomb the small rolling Goron on the floor beneath Goron City's top level to stop him. Talk to him and ask about both the Dragon and the Gorons. He then gives you the Goron Tunic and opens Darunia's Chamber. [[gf2-61-5, gf1-171-3]]

Put the tunic on to remove the time limit in the Crater and the Fire Temple. [[gf1-171-4]]

Drop to the bottom floor and enter Darunia's Chamber. Grab the statue at the top of the room and drag it backward to reveal the secret path into the Crater. [[gf2-62-1, checklist-sidequests-20-6]]

### Death Mountain Crater

#### The Bolero of Fire

Go left as you enter the Crater to find a broken bridge. Stand at the edge and Hookshot the wooden beam hanging above the far side to pull yourself across. [[gf1-172-1, gf1-204-1]]

Walk a short way down the bridge and Sheik appears to teach you the Bolero of Fire, which warps you to the Fire Temple at any time. [[gf1-172-2, gf3-55-3]]

The notes are Down C, A, Down C, A, Right C, Down C, Right C, Down C. [[gf1-395-3]]

#### Piece of Heart (#24): the cliff-face ledge

If you planted a bean in the soft soil beside the warp-in point as a child, it has grown into a platform. Ride it up until it passes close to the high cliff near the south entrance. At that point, jump out to the rock beside the cliff. [[gf1-176-2, gf1-436-1]]

From there, walk slowly off the edge to grab the cliff wall and climb down a short way to an alcove halfway down the cliff face. [[gf1-176-2, gf3-26-9]]

Do not drop into the alcove from above, because you will fall into the lava. Climb below it, then climb up into it. Take the Piece of Heart. [[gf1-176-2, gf1-435-1]]

Play the Bolero of Fire to warp back to the pedestal, which is faster than climbing back up. [[gf1-176-2]]

#### Piece of Heart (#25): riding the bean plant

Step onto the grown plant and ride it up. Near the start of its path it loops around a tall pile of rubble with a Piece of Heart on top. Jump off onto the rubble to collect it. [[gf1-176-1, checklist-sidequests-33-3, gf2-62-4]]

Drop back to the Triforce pedestal afterward. [[gf1-176-1]]

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave the Crater, check that you wear the Goron Tunic as you head to the temple, and that you carry fairies and Green Potions. [[gf1-171-4, gf3-98-10, ra-comments-32-4]]

From the pedestal, cross the bridge north into the large stone structure. Climb down the ladder into the pit and take the doorway beside it into the Fire Temple. [[gf1-176-3]]

### Fire Temple

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Hot Hot Hot requires defeating Volvagia without the Goron Tunic. Wear the tunic while crossing the temple, but take it off before the boss fight, not after. Fairies are allowed for this challenge. [[ra-comments-33-7, gf3-59-1, gf4-23-3, ra-comments-32-1]]
>
> Timing the swap: take it off in the boss room just before you jump to the arena platform, and equip the green tunic only when you are very close to the platform. [[ra-comments-32-5, ra-comments-33-16]]

#### Entrance Hall

Run up the stairs, watching for Fire Keese, and take the door on the left at the top. [[gf1-179-1]]

#### Pillar Room (first visit)

Darunia appears here, runs off to fight Volvagia, and begs you to rescue the imprisoned Gorons. [[gf1-179-2]]

Look left after the scene. Jump across the lava pillars to the platform in the left corner, then step on the switch to open the cell. Talk to the Goron and open the chest for a **Small Key**. [[gf1-179-3, gf3-55-6]]

If you need fairies, two sit on the right side of the room before the boss room. [[ra-comments-32-21]]

Return to the Entrance Hall.

#### Entrance Hall to Lava Cavern

Run straight across the hall and enter the door opposite. [[gf1-180-1]]

In the Lava Cavern, work left across the platforms in the middle of the room, along the border, or straight through the lava. [[gf1-180-1]]

#### Prison Corridor 1

Run to the end of the hall, step on the switch, talk to the Goron, and open his chest for a **Small Key**. Return to the Lava Cavern. [[gf1-180-2]]

#### Block of Time, Killer Tile Room (Skulltula #69)

Play the Song of Time near the Block of Time to move it. Climb onto it and take the door on top of the sandstone platform. [[gf1-181-1, gf2-63-4]]

The room beyond has flying floor tiles. Shield yourself against them (hold R). [[gf3-58-4]]

A Like-Like can swallow you and steal your Goron Tunic and Hylian Shield. Kill it from a distance, and if it swallows you, kill it to get both back. [[gf1-181-2, gf3-99-1, gf3-55-7]]

Kill the Gold Skulltula on the wall (#69) and take the token, then return to the previous room. [[gf1-181-3, checklist-skulltulas-14-1]]

#### Prison Corridor 2

Jump onto the sandstone platform across the cavern and bomb the oddly colored wall to reveal a door. Go through it. [[gf1-182-1]]

Step on the switch to open the prison cell directly in front of you, talk to the Goron, and open the chest for a **Small Key**. [[gf1-182-2, gf1-182-4]]

Leave by the north door of the cavern, which is the door to your right as you come out of the corridor. [[gf1-182-3]]

#### Lava Column Room

Run forward and slide down the steep log bridge into the cage. Grab the cage wall and climb to its top level. [[gf1-183-1]]

On the east sandstone pillar, push the massive block off the ledge onto the lava column that periodically bursts in the middle of the room. Jump down, stand on the block, and ride it up as an elevator the next time the column erupts to reach the upper level door. [[gf1-183-2]]

#### Three-Tiered Room

Fight the Fire Slug aggressively. Raise your shield if it expands into a column shape, because it is about to attack. [[gf1-184-1]]

Now solve the block puzzle:

1. Push the stone block off the ledge so it lands in the stone track below. It can only be pushed one way. [[gf1-184-2]]
2. Jump down, get in front of the block, and pull it backward onto the stone face carved into the floor. [[gf1-184-2]]
3. Drop a bomb off the cliff so it lands near the Diamond Switch, then quickly climb onto the ledge behind you. [[gf1-184-3]]
4. The explosion activates the switch and temporarily removes the pillar of flames around the wire wall. Climb the wire wall to the top level and go through the door at the top. [[gf1-184-3]]

#### Boulder Maze (3F, Skulltula #70)

Use a bomb on the left side of the wall in the hollow alcove. It isn't discolored, but it sounds hollow when struck with your sword. [[gf1-185-1, gf1-493-3]]

Kill the Gold Skulltula on the other side (#70) and take the token. [[gf1-185-1, checklist-skulltulas-14-2]]

Keeping your right side to the wall, follow the perimeter of the room to another small alcove with a door inside it. Go through. [[gf1-185-2]]

#### Prison Corridor 3

Run down the hall, step on the switch, talk to the Goron, and open his chest for a **Small Key**. [[gf1-186-1]]

Go around to the far left side of the maze, where a Goron's prison cell sits at a dead end. Step on the switch in front of it, speak to the Goron, and take his **Small Key**. [[gf1-186-2]]

Return to the locked door you passed earlier and go through it. [[gf1-186-3]]

#### Narrow Span Room and the Dungeon Map

Shoot the Silver Eye on the wall with an arrow to unlock the sealed door on the right from where you enter. [[gf1-187-1]]

In the Lava Curtain Room beyond it, look right as you enter to find a chest containing the **Dungeon Map**. [[gf1-187-2]]

If you prefer not to use the bow, you can climb over the side of the cage and drop directly onto the map. [[checklist-sidequests-21-1]]

When you come out of the Lava Curtain Room, enter the locked door on your right. [[gf1-187-3]]

#### Lava Curtain Room

Run all the way along the wire platform; the curtain moves slowly and is easy to dodge. Jump across the gap to the next wire platform, keep running, jump onto a third wire platform, and then jump onto the large sandstone steps. Climb to the top of the stairs and go through the door on the left. [[gf1-188-1]]

#### Top of the Boulder Maze

1. Look left to a small platform with a switch. Jump out and step on it. A cell door on the far side of the room opens, revealing another Goron. [[gf1-189-1]]
2. Speak with that Goron and open his chest for a **Small Key**. [[gf1-189-2]]
3. Drop a bomb onto the huge gash in the floor to reveal a massive pit, then drop to the bottom. [[gf1-189-3]]

At the bottom, step on the switch in the hall and the Goron's double-sided cell doors open. Speak with him and open his chest for a **Small Key**. [[gf1-190-1, gf1-190-2]]

Turn around, go back down the hall, and climb the wire wall back up to the Boulder Maze. [[gf1-190-3]]

> [!TIP]
> The Scarecrow's Song route here (play it to summon Pierre on the wall platform, then Hookshot to the circular target and ride the rising platform) leads to extra Skulltulas. Per the planned route, Skulltula #73 in this area is saved for chapter 21, when you have the Longshot. [[gf1-190-4, gf1-190-5, checklist-skulltulas-14-5]]

#### Two-Tiered Room and Time Trial Room

The room reached by the rising platform has a Gold Skulltula in the corner wire mesh of its upper level. Beyond it, the Time Trial Room holds another on the cliff wall above the pit, plus a chest with 200 rupees behind a flame pillar that a switch removes only temporarily. These are optional extras. [[gf1-191-1, gf1-191-2, gf1-191-3]]

Dropping through the pit in the center of the Time Trial Room returns you to the Narrow Span room. [[gf1-191-3]]

#### Back to the Lava Curtain Room, West Corridor

From the Narrow Span Room, take the south door, which is to the right of the sealed door and closest to the silver eye target, to return to the Lava Curtain Room. [[gf1-192-1]]

Jump from the jutting platform up to the west-center sandstone ledge. It looks like a jump you can't make, but you can. Go through the locked door. [[gf1-192-2]]

#### Fire Trap Room and the Compass

Keeping the wall on your right, move slowly forward until a wall of flames appears in your path. [[gf1-193-1]]

1. Go to the left, watching out for a roving boulder. [[gf1-193-1]]
2. Take a right immediately after passing the first post on your right. [[gf1-193-1]]
3. Go right again and head back toward the wall to find a door. [[gf1-193-1]]

Run to the end of the hall and open the chest for the **Compass**. [[gf1-194-1]]

Back in the Fire Trap Room, watch out for the giant totem pole whose faces spit fire. Hug the wall and go around, keeping your left side facing the wall. When another wall of flames appears, go right and pass between the pair of poles directly in front of you. Take a left immediately on the other side to reach the door. [[gf1-194-2]]

#### Fire maze and the dummy door

Go left and pass between the pair of posts just to the left of the fire wall to reach the switch. The giant curtain of flames on the north side of the room disappears. [[gf1-195-1]]

> [!TIP]
> The door here is a dummy that smashes down on you if you try to open it. Blow it up with a bomb, which reveals the real door behind it. [[gf1-195-2, gf3-57-2]]

Unable to verify from collected sources: the exact route between the Compass Corridor and this fire maze beyond what is described above.

#### Upper Flare Dancer Room (mini-boss)

To damage the Flare Dancer, first use the Hookshot to separate its heart from its flaming body. The heart then runs around the room very quickly. [[gf1-196-1, gf1-516-5]]

Circle the room in the opposite direction so you intercept the heart halfway around, and hit it with a jump attack. Use a regular strike if you have difficulty timing the jump. [[gf1-196-2, gf1-516-5]]

The Biggoron Sword gives a faster victory. [[gf1-516-6]]

Alternatively, charge an Advanced Spin Slash and release it as the exposed heart gets near you. Three or four hits are enough to defeat it. [[gf3-58-1, gf2-65-7, gf2-124-1]]

Once it dies, the pillar of flames in the middle of the room disappears. Climb onto the middle platform, which begins to rise, and take the door at the top of the shaft. [[gf1-196-3]]

#### Diamond Switch Room and Tippy-Top Room (5F, Megaton Hammer, Skulltula #71)

Once up on the second floor, drop a bomb down to the bottom floor, right next to the diamond switch. The explosion removes the pillar of flames surrounding the mesh ladder leading to the next level. [[gf1-197-1]]

In the Tippy-Top Room, a Gold Skulltula (#71) sits near the entrance. [[checklist-skulltulas-14-3]]

Step on the switch to make the pillar of flames around a chest near the top of the room disappear. Run up the narrow staircase on the inside of the giant ring to reach the chest before time runs out. [[gf1-197-2]]

Open the large chest for the **Megaton Hammer**. [[gf1-197-2, gf2-66-1]]

Stand on the large stone face coming out of the floor and smash it with the Hammer. The block falls to the room below. [[gf1-197-3]]

Smash both pieces of the corner totem pole from the left or right until they are gone to reveal a door. [[gf1-197-4]]

#### Hidden Staircase Room, Fire Trap Room (revisit)

Smash the stone block that rises out of the pit with the Hammer, and a stairwell appears inside the pit. [[gf1-198-1]]

Drop the wooden box onto the blue switch at the bottom of the stairs to unseal the door. Go through it. [[gf1-198-2]]

Stand on the stone block in the corner of the chamber above the Fire Trap Room and smash it with the Hammer to fall to the floor below. The block creates a booster that lets you reach doors in this room that were previously out of reach. [[gf1-198-3]]

Smash the rusted switch beside the sealed door on the higher ledge to unseal the door, then go through it. [[gf1-198-4]]

#### Two-Floored Prison (revisit)

Play the Song of Time to transport the Block of Time down to the floor. [[gf1-199-1]]

Jump across to the opposite ledge and smash the rusty switch with the Hammer to open the cell below. Drop down, speak with the Goron, and take the **Small Key** from the chest in his cell. [[gf1-199-2, gf1-199-3]]

Jump out to the large stone block held by scaffolding, stand on top of it, and smash it with the Hammer. Ride the block all the way down. [[gf1-199-4]]

#### Pillar Room, Entrance Hall, Room of Fiery Enemies

On the east side of the Entrance Hall, smash the totem pole pieces with the Hammer, standing on the left or right side of the totem, to reveal a door. Go through it. [[gf1-200-1]]

The next room is full of Fire Keese and Fire Slugs. Kill every enemy and the sealed door on the other end opens. [[gf1-200-2]]

#### Deja Vu Room (Skulltula #72)

Flying floor tiles, a Like-Like and a Gold Skulltula are here. Kill the Like-Like, then kill the Gold Skulltula on the rear wall (#72) and take its token. [[gf1-201-1, checklist-skulltulas-14-4, gf1-495-1]]

#### Lower Flare Dancer Room

Fight another Flare Dancer the same way. Use the Biggoron Sword if you have it, and don't bother with the Megaton Hammer, which attacks too slowly. [[gf1-201-2]]

When it dies, its pillar of flames disappears and reveals a chest with **Bombs**. [[gf1-201-3, gf2-66-5]]

#### Boss Key Prison Room

Smash the rusted switch with the Hammer to open the double-sided cell. Talk to the Goron and open the ornate chest for the **Boss Key**. [[gf1-202-1, gf2-66-6]]

Back in the Pillar Room, the knocked-down pillar now lets you jump across to the Boss Key door that you couldn't reach before. Jump across and go through it. [[gf1-202-2]]

### Boss: Volvagia

Before you jump onto the slab, double-check your tunic: you must not be wearing the Goron Tunic. Jump to the large slab in the middle of the cavern and run to the middle of the platform to begin. [[gf1-519-11, ra-comments-32-5]]

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** If you are wearing the Goron Tunic during the Volvagia fight, Hot Hot Hot is lost. Remember to put the Goron Tunic back on afterward. [[gf3-59-1, ra-comments-32-12]]

**Heat timer and preparation**

- Without the tunic, a countdown applies. One player saw 24 seconds after changing armor. Each fairy revival adds roughly 24 seconds. [[ra-comments-32-3, ra-comments-32-17]]
- The timer is tied to your current heart count, so more hearts means more time. 12 hearts gave 1:44, and 18 hearts gave 2:14. [[ra-comments-33-4, ra-comments-34-11, ra-comments-33-5]]
- Bring fairies. Players report that having three bottles with fairies is enough regardless of heart count. [[ra-comments-32-4, ra-comments-32-23]]
- Din's Fire stalls the clock while Volvagia is airborne. Magic Potions refill the bar. [[ra-comments-32-18, ra-comments-33-19]]
- The emulator's Full speed setting makes the timer run faster. [[ra-comments-32-8]]

**Phase 1: pits and Hammer**

Volvagia emerges at random from one of nine pits, which you can identify by smoke and cinders around them. Run toward that pit and keep clear of her fiery hair sweep. [[gf1-202-3, gf3-262-1, ra-comments-134-15]]

Smash her head with a Megaton Hammer jump attack to stun her. Then land one more hit with a jump attack from the Hammer, or from the Biggoron Sword. [[gf1-520-1, gf1-520-2]]

With the Hammer drawn, press A while Z-targeting her to jump attack. Landing all of these jump attacks kills her in four hits. [[ra-comments-34-3, ra-comments-33-17]]

As she takes damage, she may fake a pit with fire and surface in another. [[gf3-262-2]]

**Phase 2: flying**

When she flies and breathes fire, run continuously in a small circle near the center. [[gf1-202-4, gf1-520-3]]

The speedy alternative is to drop off the arena edge and hang from it. This makes her lose interest and go back underground immediately. [[ra-guide-13-3, gf3-59-2]]

**Phase 3: falling boulders**

When she flies to the ceiling and the roof starts raining boulders, run to the edge and hang off. Some rocks can still go over the edge, so there is a little randomness. [[gf1-202-5, ra-comments-134-13]]

Reported runs used about four to eight Hammer jump attacks in total. [[ra-comments-33-10, ra-comments-32-11]]

Commenters also hang from the edge while she flies, then Hammer her when she surfaces and follow up with a two-handed jump slash for double damage [[ra-comments-34-4, ra-comments-34-5]]. Use the Biggoron's Sword you got in Chapter 13 for that follow-up. Do not buy the Giant's Knife: it breaks easily [[gf2-61-6]], and one player's Biggoron's Sword achievement failed after buying it [[ra-comments-96-3]].

### Death Mountain Crater (after the temple)

Use the Hookshot on the planks hanging off the cliff to cross the shattered bridge again. [[gf1-204-1]]

Two rocks cover a partially visible doorway on the other side of the drawbridge. Smash them with the Hammer, possibly more than once, to open a Great Fairy Fountain. [[gf1-204-2, checklist-sidequests-22-1]]

Stand on the Triforce emblem and play Zelda's Lullaby. The Great Fairy doubles your magic meter. [[gf3-61-2, gf1-416-1]]

Outside, smash the gray rock you are thrown into to open a path to upper Crater. Jump to the ladder and climb to its top. [[gf1-205-2]]

### Death Mountain Trail (night Skulltulas #32 and #33)

Make it night with the Sun's Song if needed. These Skulltulas only appear at night. [[gf1-206-1, gf3-61-3]]

- **#33:** On the long path up to the cliff face, where rocks used to rain on you, smash the red rock that sits flush against the wall with the Hammer. [[gf1-206-1, gf3-61-4, checklist-skulltulas-7-4]]
- **#32:** At the cliff above Dodongo's Cavern, where the Bomb Flower used to be, smash the red rock to reveal a Gold Skulltula. [[gf1-206-2, gf3-61-3, checklist-skulltulas-7-3]]
