## Chapter 8: Lake Hylia, the Fishing Pond and Gerudo Valley

[MERMAID]

This chapter starts at the bottom of the underwater tunnel from Zora's Domain. You pick up the third Bottle (it already holds Ruto's Letter) and then work through Lake Hylia, its Fishing Pond, Gerudo Valley and the fenced grotto in Hyrule Field as Young Link. The prize is four Pieces of Heart (#13, #11, #14 and #12), three Gold Skulltula tokens (#51, #52 and #60), the Scarecrow's song, a planted Magic Bean, and the Fishing Lessons achievement [[gf1-98-1, checklist-heartpieces-3-13, checklist-heartpieces-3-11, checklist-heartpieces-3-14, checklist-heartpieces-3-12, checklist-skulltulas-10-1, checklist-skulltulas-10-2, checklist-skulltulas-12-1]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you start:** Bring the following. You also need to be able to switch between day and night with the Sun's Song, and be a Young Link who can dive to the lake floor [[gf2-37-5, gf1-383-6]].
> - Bombs, for the Hyrule Field grotto [[gf1-426-1]].
> - At least 20 rupees for the fishing fee, and 10 more for the Business Scrub [[gf1-444-1, gf1-100-2]].
> - An empty Bottle you can fill with Bugs. The Ruto's Letter Bottle cannot be used until the letter is delivered [[gf3-152-2]].
> - One or more Magic Beans from the Bean Man in Zora's River [[gf1-385-1]].
> - The Silver Scale, to reach the lake floor [[gf1-383-6]].

Two mistakes cost time here, and both are recoverable. A fish that breaks free is gone, but leaving and re-entering the pond respawns the big fish [[gf1-413-2]]. A lost Cucco in Gerudo Valley is replaced by riding the river to Lake Hylia and walking back [[gf1-119-2]].

### Lake Hylia

#### The Bottle with Ruto's Letter

Surface from the tunnel out of Zora's Domain and swim a short way south. On the floor, near a ruined pillar, lies a Bottle. Dive down and take it [[gf1-98-1]]. Another guide says it is right in front of you when you arrive, so look around the lake floor in front of the tunnel exit [[gf2-37-1]]. Reaching it needs the Silver Scale, which is why the Zora's Domain diving game comes first [[gf3-152-2]].

The Bottle already contains Ruto's Letter, so you cannot use it as a Bottle yet. This is your third Bottle [[gf3-152-2, gf3-30-8]]. Do not hurry back to King Zora, because there is still plenty to do here [[gf1-98-1]]. Carry the bottled Letter with you for now.

#### Teach the Scarecrow your song

Swim north to the shore and head to the east coast. There are two scarecrows [[gf1-98-2]]. The sources disagree on which scarecrow to use. One calls it the "lower" garden, one says farthest from the water, and one says closest to shore [[gf1-98-2, checklist-sidequests-11-8, gf3-30-9]]. The scarecrow you want is the dancing one named Bonooru, in the patch between the lab and the Fishing Pond [[gf4-9-3, ra-guide-14-1]]. Stand in front of him with your Ocarina out and he asks for a song [[gf1-98-2]].

Compose an eight-note song. It must have at least two different notes and must not be any song you already know or will learn later [[gf1-98-2, gf1-398-1]]. Play it for him and **write the notes down on paper**. You must play the exact same song as an adult, and Pierre the Wanderer in chapter 16 needs it too [[gf1-98-2, gf1-398-2, ra-guide-14-1]].

> [!TIP]
> If you forget the song, Sheik at the Temple of Time can send you back to childhood to teach Pierre a new one, but it is far less work to write it down now [[gf1-165-2]].

#### Gold Skulltula #52 and the Magic Bean

The tall building is the Lakeside Laboratory, which is to the right of the entrance from Hyrule Field [[gf4-9-2]]. A patch of soft earth sits beside it [[gf1-98-3, gf3-174-6]]. Release a Bug from your Bottle near the patch (recapture one afterwards) and kill the Gold Skulltula that comes out. Take its token for **Gold Skulltula #52** (✅ token 1/3 this chapter) [[gf1-98-3, checklist-skulltulas-10-2]].

Then plant one of your Magic Beans in the same patch. As an adult you will ride this plant to the roof of the laboratory, where a Piece of Heart waits [[gf1-98-3, checklist-sidequests-12-4, checklist-heartpieces-4-8]].

Where to catch Bugs for this chapter: Unable to verify from collected sources.

#### Gold Skulltula #51 (night)

From the laboratory, go around to its west side and cross the long wooden bridge over the lake. The bridge leads to the large island with the ancient dead tree [[gf1-98-3]]. Look east from the tree to see the small island with two pillars [[gf1-98-4]].

Swim to that island. If it is daytime, play the Sun's Song to bring on night [[gf2-37-5]]. A Gold Skulltula appears on one of the pillars. Kill it and take its token for **Gold Skulltula #51** (✅ token 2/3 this chapter) [[gf1-98-4, gf2-37-5, checklist-skulltulas-10-1]].

#### Optional extras at the lake

- A grave on one of the Lake Hylia islands can be pushed to reveal a Secret Cave with three Business Scrubs selling general pick-up items [[gf3-238-14]].
- Which island holds the grave: Unable to verify from collected sources.
- The Lake Hylia gossip stones are at the very far edge of the water, next to the wall, and one commenter counts three of them [[ra-comments-80-3, ra-comments-80-11]].

### Fishing Pond

🗺️ **Map:** [View Fishing Pond Map by TLS64](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/51-fishing-pond-map) [[map-fishing-1-1]]

Look to the north for a small island and swim to it. Enter the door built into the wall of the island to reach the Fishing Pond [[gf1-98-5]]. Speak to the clerk and pay 20 rupees to fish [[gf1-444-1, gf2-37-2]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Fish as **Young Link**. The achievement needs a fish of at least 10 pounds [[ra-comments-111-1, ra-comments-111-2]]. One guide says nine pounds earns the Piece of Heart [[gf1-99-1]], but the achievement asks for ten, so do not stop at nine. A caught Hylian Loach reportedly does not trigger this achievement [[ra-comments-111-3, ra-comments-111-4]]. Do not use the sinking lure, because one source says doing so disqualifies the catch [[checklist-heartpieces-6-4]].

**The controls.** Z-target a fish, press B to cast, and use B, A and the stick to wiggle the lure and attract fish. When a fish is on the line, hold A and pull back on the control stick to reel it in [[gf1-515-1]]. Once the fish bites, keep the reel pulling against the fish's direction, which usually means holding left or right all the way [[ra-guide-10-1]]. Alternating between holding back-left and back-right every few seconds while reeling also helps [[gf1-413-2]].

**Where the big fish is.** The 10-pound fish is the one that stands out from the others and sits in the middle of the pond [[ra-comments-111-5, ra-comments-111-6]]. Several sources agree on the area around the log:
- The biggest fish hang near the submerged log on the left and the reeds on the right [[gf1-515-2]].
- One guide says the big fish lurks between the log and three sticks nearby [[gf4-9-1]].
- Another says to stand near the lilypads at the rear, where the fish stays underwater by the big log. You may have to catch the small fish that swims with it first [[checklist-heartpieces-6-4]].
- You can also stand on the log on the left side after entering and look for the big fish from there [[ra-guide-10-1]].

If a fish breaks free it is gone for good. If the pond runs low on big fish, leave and re-enter and they respawn [[gf1-413-2, gf1-217-2]].

When you land a fish of 10 pounds or more, the achievement unlocks:

[ACHIEVEMENT:48348]

Now carry the fish to the owner at the counter. He gives you the Piece of Heart (**Piece of Heart #13**, ✅ 1/4 this chapter) [[gf2-37-3, gf1-444-2, gf3-31-1, checklist-sidequests-12-1]]. Leave the pond when you have it.

> [!TIP]
> The 15-pound fish that wins the Gold Scale is a task for adult Link later. Do not chase it now [[gf1-445-2, gf2-107-2]].

### Gerudo Valley

From Lake Hylia, take the north exit to Hyrule Field. Head northwest until you hit the road, then go west into the rocky canyon area. Keep going west until you reach the zone break to Gerudo Valley [[gf1-100-3, gf1-118-4]]. The fenced corral you pass on the way is covered in the Hyrule Field section below.

Run forward a short way and cross the log bridge. Grab the Cucco on the other side [[gf1-101-1]]. In this valley a Cucco is a glider, and all three pickups here depend on it. Hold on to it carefully, and toss it against a wall whenever you set it down [[gf1-101-1, gf2-38-1]].

> [!TIP]
> **If you lose the Cucco:** Jump into the river and ride it to Lake Hylia, then walk back through Hyrule Field to the valley for a fresh Cucco [[gf1-101-1, gf1-119-2]].

#### Piece of Heart #14 (crate on the far ledge)

Run to the edge of the canyon and look across to the opposite side. A small platform with a wooden crate sits on the far wall [[gf1-101-1]]. A second guide says to jump off, force the stick forward and left, and aim for the small cliff on the other side of the valley [[checklist-heartpieces-3-14]]. Glide down to it, then roll into the crate [[gf1-101-1, gf1-446-1]]. Take the Piece of Heart that was in the crate (**Piece of Heart #14**, ✅ 2/4 this chapter), which makes you drop the Cucco, then grab the Cucco again as fast as you can [[gf1-101-1, checklist-sidequests-13-15]].

#### Gold Skulltula #60 (soft earth at the bottom)

From the crate ledge, glide down to the long strip of land at the bottom of the canyon. It holds a Gerudo, a cow and a small patch of dirt [[checklist-skulltulas-12-1, gf3-195-3]]. Head to the southern edge of the strip, where the patch of soft earth sits near the cow [[gf1-101-2]].

Release Bugs near the patch. Three bugs come out, so recapture one. Kill the Gold Skulltula that appears and take its token for **Gold Skulltula #60** (✅ token 3/3 this chapter) [[gf1-101-2, checklist-skulltulas-12-1, checklist-sidequests-13-17]]. Some guides also plant a Magic Bean in this soil [[gf3-37-21]]. This chapter only needs the Lakeside Laboratory bean; the Gerudo Valley bean is planted in Chapter 10.

#### Piece of Heart #11 (behind the waterfall)

Make sure you hold the Cucco again. Run to the northern edge of the strip and look behind the waterfall, where you will see a ladder [[gf1-101-3]]. Jump out toward the ladder and do not drop the Cucco until you are right beside it. There is no current immediately behind the waterfall, so you can climb out if you land in the water [[gf1-101-3, gf1-446-2]]. Climb to the top of the ladder to find **Piece of Heart #11** (✅ 3/4 this chapter) [[gf1-101-3, checklist-sidequests-13-16, checklist-sidequests-2-19]].

You can also reach this heart from the bridge. Take the Cucco from before the bridge, jump off to the right and float through the giant waterfall into the tiny nook in the wall [[gf4-9-7, checklist-heartpieces-3-11]].

> [!NOTE]
> The area across the broken bridge, with the Carpenter's tent and more tokens, is an adult-Link job. You cross it later with Epona or the Longshot, so leave it for now [[gf1-278-1, gf1-278-3, checklist-skulltulas-12-3]].

Jump into the river and ride it downstream to return to Lake Hylia [[gf1-101-3, gf1-119-2]].

### Hyrule Field

🗺️ **Map:** [View Hyrule Field Map by thegamemaster8](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/5394-hyrule-field-map) [[map-hyrule-field-1-1]]

Leave Lake Hylia through the north exit onto Hyrule Field. Jump into the middle of the small square of fences, which is the fenced corral outside the lake [[checklist-sidequests-13-19, gf1-100-1, gf1-426-1]].

Stand in the exact center of the corral and drop a Bomb to reveal a pit [[gf1-100-1, gf1-426-1]]. If you have the Stone of Agony, it makes the spot easier to find [[gf1-426-1]]. One guide places the bomb in the upper-right quadrant instead, so try there if the center does nothing [[checklist-heartpieces-3-12]]. Drop into the hole and defeat the Business Scrub with your Boomerang, then speak to it [[gf2-44-8]]. The Scrub sells a Piece of Heart for just 10 rupees, so buy it. That is **Piece of Heart #12** (✅ 4/4 this chapter) [[gf1-100-2, checklist-heartpieces-3-12, checklist-sidequests-2-20]].

**Optional rupees nearby:**
- A hole out in the open near the fence square holds a small chest with a Blue Rupee [[gf3-237-11]].
- On the way to the lake, a rock in the trees west of the corral hides a small chest with a Red Rupee [[gf2-44-7]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave:** Check that you have the four Pieces of Heart (#13, #11, #14, #12) and the three tokens (#51, #52, #60). Also check that you still carry the Bottle with Ruto's Letter [[gf3-152-2]]. The route back is the submerged ruins near the shore, east of the laboratory and west of the scarecrows. Dive in and swim into the tunnel to Zora's Domain [[gf1-102-1]]. Other tokens in these areas, such as the back of the Lakeside Laboratory, need the Boomerang and are not counted in this chapter's total [[checklist-skulltulas-10-3, checklist-skulltulas-12-2]].
