## Chapter 6: Death Mountain Summit, Goron City and Castle Town with Bombs

[MERMAID]

With Bombs in your bag, this chapter goes back through the places that were closed to you. You bomb the summit wall of Death Mountain Trail for the Great Fairy, ride the owl down to Kakariko, work through Goron City, play Bombchu Bowling in the Market, learn Din's Fire at Hyrule Castle, and finish the Happy Mask chain and the Lost Woods upgrades [[gf1-415-3, gf1-88-1, gf1-85-3, gf1-91-2, gf1-90-2, gf1-65-1, gf1-418-2]]. Two achievements here are tied to deadlines, so read the warnings before you act in Goron City and before you touch the Poacher's Saw sidequest [[ra-guide-8-2, ra-comments-73-3]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you start:** Bring plenty of rupees, because Bombchu Bowling costs 30 rupees per game and the Lost Woods upgrades cost 40 rupees each [[gf1-91-1, gf1-23-4, gf1-418-2]]. Smashing the pots in the room at the entrance of the Market is a source of rupees [[gf4-3-2]]. Bring Bottled Bugs for the soft-soil Gold Skulltulas, and a Deku Stick for the Goron City torches [[gf1-66-4, gf1-63-4]].

### Death Mountain Trail

Enter Death Mountain Trail by the northern gate of Kakariko Village. The guard lets you through when you show him Zelda's Letter [[gf1-58-2]]. Opening that gate also makes the Happy Mask Salesman sidequest available at the Happy Mask Shop in the Market [[gf1-349-1]].

#### Lower trail

On the right side after the first corner, an oddly colored wall hides a Gold Skulltula. Blast it with a Bomb, shoot the Skulltula inside with the Slingshot, then climb the wall to take the token (Gold Skulltula #31) [[gf1-84-1, gf1-465-1]].

Past the red flag on the way to Goron City, another odd wall holds a chest with a 50 rupee piece. Open it only if you need rupees [[gf1-84-2]].

### Goron City

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Do NOT destroy the three boulders at the entrance of the top-floor boulder room before you claim **Walking on a Tightrope**. Their flags are permanent and they do not respawn. You may jump to the side of the rocks instead [[ra-guide-8-2, ra-comments-68-1, ra-comments-68-2]].

#### Top floor boulder room

Walk onto the tightrope at the top of Goron City and make your way around to the room full of rocks. Keep the three boulders on the left of the entrance intact, cross the tightrope to the left, enter the boulder room and open a treasure chest there [[ra-comments-68-3, ra-guide-8-2]]. Rocks inside the chest room may be destroyed freely [[ra-comments-68-4]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48279]

Work through the maze with bombs. From the entrance go left to the barrier of boulders and blow a path through [[gf1-468-1]]. Follow the route: destroy the brown rock to your right on entering, then the one behind it, go through the gap, destroy the brown rock on the left, take the new gap, go right and destroy the rock directly ahead [[gf1-468-2]]. The guide's shorthand for the route is right, left, left, right [[checklist-sidequests-14-3]].

At the end, two chests each hold a Purple Rupee [[gf1-85-1, gf2-29-3]]. Roll into the crate in the corner to reveal a Gold Skulltula, then kill it and take the token (Gold Skulltula #34) [[gf1-468-3, checklist-skulltulas-7-5]].

#### Rolling Goron (Bomb Bag upgrade)

Drop to the third floor, the level directly below the top floor. A large Goron rolls around in a loop. Place a bomb so that it explodes as he passes over it inside the northern tunnel, then talk to him [[gf1-85-3]]. The bomb must go off while he is inside the tunnel. Bombing him outside the tunnel did not work for one player [[ra-guide-8-3, ra-comments-67-1, ra-comments-67-2]]. It can take a few tries, so carry spare bombs [[checklist-sidequests-14-4, ra-comments-67-3]]. This upgrades you to the 30-bomb bag [[gf2-29-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48336]

#### Spinning pot (Piece of Heart)

Go to Darunia's room on the bottom floor. Stand on the carpet in front of the sealed door and play Zelda's Lullaby to open it [[gf1-63-1]]. Light a Deku Stick on the torch in Darunia's room, then light every brazier on the bottom level of the city; when they are all lit the giant pot starts to spin [[checklist-heartpieces-3-10, gf1-63-4]].

From the second level, throw a bomb into the pot. If it stops with the happy face forward, a Piece of Heart appears [[gf1-437-1, gf1-85-4]]. This is Piece of Heart #10.

If you have not yet met Darunia, play Saria's Song in front of him for the Goron's Bracelet [[gf1-63-2, gf1-63-3]].

### Death Mountain Crater (child visit)

🗺️ **Map:** [View Path to Zelda Map by TLS64](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/55-path-to-zelda-map)

Head back to the trail and climb the steeper path beside the red flag. The last boulder at the top of the ledges hides a pit with a cow, which refills milk when you play Epona's Song [[gf1-86-1, ra-comments-60-4, ra-comments-60-7]]. When the volcano erupts, hold R under the Hylian Shield when you see a shadow below you [[gf1-86-2]].

Climb to the summit. Kaepora Gaebora waits there. Do not speak to him yet [[gf1-86-3]].

#### Great Fairy Fountain at the summit

Bomb the oddly colored wall to the left of the owl and enter the cave [[gf1-86-4, gf1-415-3]]. Stand on the Triforce emblem and play Zelda's Lullaby. The Great Fairy teaches the Super Spin Attack and grants a Magic Meter [[gf1-87-1, gf2-30-4]]. Hold B until the sword glows, then release [[gf2-154-10]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48341]

#### Crater crate (Gold Skulltula)

Enter the cave to the right of the owl [[gf1-87-2]]. Immediately inside, roll into the crate on the left to reveal a Gold Skulltula (#36). Kill it, take the token and leave, because the heat kills child Link if time runs out [[gf1-87-3, checklist-skulltulas-7-7, gf1-435-2]].

### Kakariko Village

Now speak to Kaepora Gaebora. He carries you down to the roof of Impa's House [[gf1-88-1]].

#### Cow house Piece of Heart

Walk slowly off the edge of the roof and drop onto the small roof cover of the chicken coop. A secret entrance leads into the house, where the Piece of Heart is next to the cow [[gf2-31-3, checklist-heartpieces-3-8]]. If you miss the drop, play the Sun's Song to warp back to the roof [[gf1-88-3]]. This is Piece of Heart #8 in the roadmap order [[gf3-27-1]].

#### House of Skulltula

With 20 tokens, enter the House of Skulltula and talk to the freed man on the left for the Stone of Agony [[gf1-89-1, gf1-420-3]]. Your count at this point is the checkpoint total below.

[ACHIEVEMENT:48244]

### Hyrule Castle

🗺️ **Map:** [View Hyrule Palace Map by ThE_bLImP](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/52-hyrule-palace-map)

Din's Fire is found at the Hyrule Castle Garden, reached from the Market [[gf1-384-2]].

#### Din's Fire

Climb the ivy on the right to the cliff above the gate. Drop onto the other side of the marble gate and walk to the dead end marked with a sign. Bomb the boulder, crawl through the tunnel and stand on the Triforce emblem in the Great Fairy Fountain. Play Zelda's Lullaby to receive Din's Fire [[gf1-90-1, gf1-90-2]]. Din's Fire is later used to light many torches at once [[gf1-90-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48344]

Let a guard spot you and throw you out the gate, then return to the Market [[gf1-90-3]].

### Hyrule Market

#### Bombchu Bowling

Enter the Bombchu Bowling Alley in the Market; each game costs 30 rupees [[gf1-91-1]]. The clerk shows a prize first; completing all three goals wins that prize [[gf1-91-3]]. The prizes include Bombs, a Purple Rupee, Bombchus, a Piece of Heart, and the Bomb Bag upgrade [[gf2-105-2, gf1-410-1]].

Tips for the three goals:

- Stand in the exact middle of the field, slightly to the right of the center arrow, and time the shot to avoid the Blade Trap [[gf1-410-3]].
- The second target can appear at the top, left or right, and a small Cucco must be dodged. If it is on the right, adjust your position slightly [[gf1-410-4, gf1-91-5]].
- If the hole is on the left, angle the Bombchu along the floor and then the wall [[gf1-410-6]].
- The third target is in the center with a Fat Cucco, and you can sneak a Bombchu past it [[gf1-410-5]]. Another guide says to stand on the third black mark left of the middle arrow and aim straight ahead, so a ledge turns the Bombchu toward the hole [[gf3-28-3]].

Collecting any Bombchus completes the Bombchus achievement.

[ACHIEVEMENT:48343]

Keep playing until the clerk offers the Bomb Bag as the prize and win it [[gf1-410-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48342]

Also win the Piece of Heart prize (#9) [[gf1-427-2, gf4-11-5]]. The Bomb Bag achievement has two requirements, the screen ID and the upgrade flag, so collect it inside the alley [[ra-comments-70-4]].

### Lost Woods

🗺️ **Map:** [View Lost Woods Map by EverythingZelda](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/54-lost-woods-map)

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Buy the Deku Nut upgrade for **Stocking Nuts** before you obtain the adult Poacher's Saw (chapter 13) [[ra-guide-8-7, ra-comments-73-3]]. Whether the Lost Woods Deku Nut upgrades can still be earned as an adult before the Poacher's Saw is unconfirmed, since some players report it worked after the Forest Temple and others report the trade flag locks it [[ra-comments-44-9, ra-comments-44-3]]. Get A Pretty Face, the Mask of Truth upgrade and Stocking Nuts as child Link before you draw the Master Sword [[gf1-525-1, ra-guide-10-2]].

#### Happy Mask chain

Show Zelda's Letter to the Kakariko gate guard and he asks for a Keaton Mask [[gf1-58-3]]. Borrow the Keaton Mask from the Happy Mask Shop near the Temple of Time, and sell it to the guard for 15 rupees [[gf1-59-1, gf1-59-2, gf1-59-3]]. Pay the 10-rupee fee at the shop, then borrow the Skull Mask [[gf1-60-1]].

#### Forest Stage

From the Lost Woods entrance, go right, left, right, left, left to reach a grassy screen with a large tree [[checklist-sidequests-13-23, gf3-237-4]]. Drop into the invisible pit marked by butterflies in the tall grass [[gf1-64-3]]. Put on the Skull Mask and step forward. Walk to the right side and speak to the Deku Scrub Chief to raise your Deku Stick capacity to 30 [[gf1-65-1, gf2-47-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48423]

#### Selling the Skull Mask

Return to the lone Skull Kid. He will only buy the mask if you have already played Saria's Song for him and earned the Piece of Heart [[gf1-527-3]]. Wear the Skull Mask and speak to him to sell it [[gf1-65-3, gf2-47-4]].

#### Deku Nut upgrade

Go to the screen below Sacred Forest Meadow. Bomb the boulder near the entrance and drop into the hole. Two Business Scrubs are inside; defeat them with the Slingshot, and the one on the left sells the Deku Nut upgrade for 40 rupees [[gf1-86-5, gf2-33-3, ra-guide-8-7]]. This is the achievement item, not the Mask of Truth one [[ra-comments-73-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48345]

Network issues while you get the Deku Nut and save can stop this achievement from triggering [[ra-comments-73-5]].
