### Overview and preparation

This chapter takes you from the Deku Tree's final wish to your first meeting with Zelda: Hyrule Field, the Market, then the castle grounds and courtyard. The Great Deku Tree sent Link to Hyrule Castle to find the Princess of Destiny [[gf1-32-6]]. Six achievements are earned here: Sniper Fury, Rise and Shine!!, Money Solves Everything, Troublemaker, A Royal Autograph and Who Let the Dogs Out. Two of them are missable and both have deadlines before you speak to Zelda.

The order that keeps every achievement available:

1. Play the Shooting Gallery in the Market as Young Link (Sniper Fury).
2. Talk to Malon in the Market, then get the Weird Egg from her at the castle grounds (Rise and Shine!!).
3. Talk to the gate guard, get caught in the courtyard, leave, come back and pay the bribe (Money Solves Everything).
4. Shoot the left courtyard window with the Slingshot, then talk to Zelda (Troublemaker, then A Royal Autograph).
5. Learn Zelda's Lullaby from Impa, then return to the Market at night for the white dog (Who Let the Dogs Out).

Keep your Slingshot and Deku Seeds ready for the Shooting Gallery and the courtyard windows, and carry at least 20 Rupees for the gallery. The Shooting Gallery costs 20 Rupees per game [[gf2-13-3]]. The 10-Rupee bribe also needs cash [[gf2-32-3]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Money Solves Everything (bribe the castle guard) must be done before you meet Zelda. A player reports it cannot be completed after Zelda gets taken [[gf2-32-3, ra-comments-56-1, ra-comments-56-2]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Troublemaker (shoot the left courtyard window) must be done before Zelda leaves the courtyard, which happens after the third Spiritual Stone [[gf1-173-4, ra-guide-5-4, ra-comments-58-1, ra-comments-58-2]].

> [!TIP]
> **Shield warning:** Do NOT buy the Hylian Shield at the Bazaar (80 Rupees). You get the same shield soon enough for free, and a later chapter's achievement depends on it [[gf2-13-1, gf2-140-15, gf4-6-3]].

### Hyrule Field

🗺️ **Map:** [View Hyrule Field Map by thegamemaster8](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/5394-hyrule-field-map)

When you step into Hyrule Field, Kaepora Gaebora stops you to explain how to reach Hyrule Castle and how the map works [[gf1-34-4]]. Answer "Yes" if you do not want him to repeat the speech.

Once he flies off, run between the trees where a road begins and follow it to its end at Hyrule Castle. Roll all the way by pressing A continually as you run. Otherwise night will fall before you arrive and the gates will be closed [[gf1-34-5]].

> [!TIP]
> Do not stray far from the road on this first trip. Everything you need in this chapter is in the Market and the castle grounds.

### Hyrule Market

🗺️ **Map:** [View Hyrule Palace Map by ThE_bLImP](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/52-hyrule-palace-map)

#### The Gatehouse (guardhouse by the drawbridge)

Enter the gatehouse to your left as you come in [[gf1-35-1]]. Smash all the pots with your sword. They are an easy source of Rupees, and you can leave and re-enter to refill them as often as you like [[gf2-13-2, gf4-3-2]]. Collect about 100 Rupees so you can afford repeat attempts at the Shooting Gallery [[gf4-3-2]].

Roll into the crates in the rear of the room, next to the guard. One holds a Gold Skulltula, which is Gold Skulltula #18 [[gf1-35-1, checklist-skulltulas-5-7, gf1-458-1]]. Kill it and take the token [[gf1-35-1]].

#### The Market square

Go southwest from the drawbridge to the Market. Walk around to the opposite side of the fountain and speak with the little girl in the white dress. This is Malon [[gf1-35-2]]. Talk to her now. If you skip this, she will not appear at the castle later, and you would have to wait through another day/night cycle [[gf1-38-2]].

The Bazaar is the building with the scary face above it, and the Potion Shop is next door. You need nothing from either now [[gf2-13-1]].

#### Shooting Gallery

Enter the building behind Malon with the large target over the door [[gf1-35-2]]. Pay 20 Rupees for fifteen shots to hit ten Rupees. Hitting eight of ten earns a free game. Hitting all ten earns a Bullet Bag that raises your Slingshot capacity from 30 to 40 [[gf2-13-3, gf2-102-2]]. You must hit every target without a miss [[gf1-36-1]]. If you miss two or fewer, the clerk lets you try again free. If you miss more than two, you pay again [[gf1-510-1]].

The targets appear in the same order each time [[gf2-13-2]]:

| Target | Behavior | Citation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Green Rupee, stationary in the middle | [[gf2-13-2]] |
| 2 | Blue Rupee from the left, stopping a bit left of center | [[gf2-13-2]] |
| 3 | Blue Rupee from the right, stopping a bit right of center | [[gf2-13-2]] |
| 4 | Green Rupee that pops up from the middle; hit it at the top of its jump | [[gf2-13-2]] |
| 5 and 6 | Blue Rupees from left and right at the same time; hit both before they leave | [[gf2-13-2]] |
| 7 and 8 | Red Rupees moving right to left at moderate speed | [[gf2-13-2]] |
| 9 and 10 | Red Rupees moving left to right, quickly | [[gf2-13-2]] |

Tips from other players:

- Lower your control sensitivity to about 0.6 or 0.7 for this challenge [[ra-comments-54-1]].
- Keep the Slingshot aimed at the middle except for the two side shots [[ra-comments-54-2]].
- On the round where the blue Rupees move toward the right, fire before you think you need to [[ra-comments-54-3]].

If you run out of Rupees, go back to the gatehouse and smash pots to refill [[gf1-36-4]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48106]

Keep playing until the clerk gives you the Bullet Bag [[gf1-36-3, gf1-405-2]].

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave the Market: the Bombchu Bowling Alley and Treasure Chest Game here are not open to you yet. Do not spend money on them now [[gf1-410-1, gf1-428-1]]. A Bazaar purchase of the Hylian Shield is still off the list [[gf2-13-1]].

### Hyrule Castle

🗺️ **Map:** [View Path to Zelda Map by TLS64](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/55-path-to-zelda-map)

#### Castle grounds: Gold Skulltula #19 and Malon's egg

Leave the Market through the passage directly behind Malon [[gf1-37-1]]. Kaepora Gaebora appears again and rants about guards. When he flies away, roll into his tree to make a Gold Skulltula appear [[gf1-37-1, gf2-15-1]]. Kill it and take the token. This is Gold Skulltula #19 [[checklist-skulltulas-5-8, gf1-458-2]].

Malon now stands beside the long vine on the wall to your right [[gf1-38-2]]. She explains her father Talon went to the castle with milk and has not come back, and she asks you to find him [[gf1-38-2]]. Speak to her, twice if need be, and she gives you the Weird Egg [[gf2-15-2, gf1-38-2]]. A source says she hands it over at night [[gf3-165-1]]. If she is not there, you skipped talking to her in the Market, so return there first [[gf1-38-2]].

Equip the egg to a C button. Stand at the top of the vines and let the game pass through the night. In the morning, when you hear the rooster, the egg hatches into a Chicken [[gf2-15-3, gf2-156-5]].

#### Sneaking to Talon

Climb the ivy wall to the top. It has rough spots, so if you start in the wrong place you will fall off [[gf1-38-3]]. At the top, run forward past the gossip stone to the top of the large marble arch. Drop down on the far side, which puts you behind the gate that was blocking you and past the first two guards [[gf1-38-3, gf1-173-1]].

The next stretch is the hardest. Run up the hill on the left at an exact spot: it is the third graphical break in the hill, near its center. Go straight up toward the cluster of flowers at the top, then run forward and slightly left until you reach a wall with tiled bricks, and climb the bricks [[gf1-38-4, gf1-173-2]].

At the top, run forward past another gossip stone and jump off the cliff into the moat. Swim to the end of the moat where it breaks left, climb onto the small patch of dirt, then up to the stone platform. Run left from the platform to find Talon asleep [[gf1-38-5]].

If you are spotted by a guard, you are thrown back outside the gate [[gf1-90-3]]. A player's note says you have to sneak through the castle to reach the sleeping Talon, so be patient [[ra-comments-11-2]].

#### Wake Talon

Talking to Talon does not wake him. Stand next to him, where Navi flutters near him, and equip the Chicken. Press its C button [[gf2-15-4, gf1-38-6]]. If the egg has not hatched, wait until dawn [[gf1-38-6]]. Speak with Talon. He remembers he left Malon behind and runs off, clearing the path [[gf1-38-6, gf4-6-6]].

#### The bribe: Money Solves Everything

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Do this before you meet Zelda, and do not complete any other story step in between. Reports say you only need the egg from Malon, not necessarily a hatched one [[ra-comments-57-3, ra-comments-56-1]]. You must get caught once, leave, and come back [[ra-comments-56-3, ra-guide-5-2]]. One player did all of this after going to Kakariko Village and back, as long as no other main quest was done [[ra-comments-56-6, ra-comments-57-1]].

What exactly triggers the bribe dialogue is unconfirmed by any collected source. Comments mention having Malon's egg, being caught once and returning. Do all three before meeting Zelda and do no other story step in between.

The sequence that players report:

1. Have Malon's egg and talk to the gate guard on the left upon entering the courtyard area [[ra-guide-5-2, ra-comments-56-9]]. One player talked to him twice at the gate [[ra-comments-56-1, ra-comments-56-9]].
2. While you are still on the castle grounds (climb back over the gate if you left), deliberately get spotted by a guard on the castle grounds (the hill below the moat, not Zelda's courtyard, which you have not entered yet). The guard throws you outside the gate [[gf1-90-3, gf1-173-4]].
3. Talk to the guard you see immediately after being thrown out. He offers to open the gate for 10 Rupees [[ra-comments-57-2]].
4. Pay the 10 Rupees. He lets you through [[gf2-32-3]].

Players report that having been caught once and then returning is what makes the offer appear [[ra-comments-56-7, ra-comments-56-8, ra-guide-5-2]]. If you are caught and the bribe option does not show up, leave the area and come back [[ra-comments-56-3, ra-guide-5-2]]. One player reports the achievement did not pop after waking Malon's father, which supports doing the full sequence above rather than just waking Talon [[ra-comments-56-4]].

#### Into the courtyard

Daytime is required to enter the next area, because the guard is increased at night [[gf1-38-7]]. Push both milk crates through the gap Talon left, climb onto them, and jump to the drainage pipe that feeds the moat. If it is night, loiter until day, then climb through the pipe [[gf1-38-7]]. Another source describes the same step as pushing the two bull-head blocks off the ledge so the second stacks on the first, then climbing up and jumping to the crawlspace beside the waterfall, again only in daytime [[gf2-15-5, gf3-17-6]].

Inside the courtyard, the guards patrol the hedged areas. If you are spotted you are thrown back out [[gf1-90-3]]. Work your way to the northern edge of the topiary and run through the arch to reach the center of the courtyard, where Zelda waits [[gf1-39-3]]. Exact patrol timings: Unable to verify from collected sources.

#### Troublemaker: shoot the left window

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Do this before speaking to Zelda. You cannot come back to this area after collecting all three Spiritual Stones [[ra-guide-5-4, ra-comments-58-3]].

Before you talk to Zelda, equip the Slingshot. Shoot the window on the LEFT side of the courtyard. A guard throws a bomb at you, so be ready to move [[gf1-39-1, ra-comments-58-6]]. One shot should hit the window [[ra-comments-58-2]]. Shooting the left window is the reported trigger, and the goal is not simply to be caught [[ra-comments-58-7, ra-comments-58-8]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48161]

If you miss, you can try again as long as Zelda is still in the courtyard, which also means you do not need to shoot on the first visit [[ra-comments-58-5]]. One player reports a second shot is possible after the volcano dungeon [[ra-comments-58-4]].

Now shoot the window on the RIGHT, which shows Mario characters, for a Red Rupee [[gf1-39-2, gf2-16-1]]. A Deku Seed into this window gives a Red Rupee worth 20 Rupees [[gf3-17-3]]. Do not confuse the two windows: the right one only pays a Rupee and does not count for the achievement [[gf1-39-1, gf1-39-2]].

#### Meet Zelda

Speak with Zelda. She asks if you have the Spiritual Stone of the Forest, and it is fine to say yes. Promise not to tell, listen to her dream, and say you understand when asked. Agree to look through the window when Zelda offers, then agree to help her [[gf1-39-3]]. Pick the top option when given one [[gf3-17-4]]. She gives you Zelda's Letter with her signature [[gf1-39-4, gf3-17-4, gf2-161-1]]. The letter opens the gate to Death Mountain in Kakariko Village later [[gf1-39-4]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48109]

#### Impa and Zelda's Lullaby

Run toward the courtyard exit. Impa blocks it and teaches you Zelda's Lullaby, then leads you out to Hyrule Field and points you toward Kakariko Village [[gf1-40-1, gf4-6-8]]. The song is C-Left, C-Up, C-Right, C-Left, C-Up, C-Right [[gf1-392-1]]. You are back in Hyrule Field with the letter and the song [[gf2-133-7]].

### Back to the Market at night: Who Let the Dogs Out

Return to the Market and wait for night. If you lack the Sun's Song, wait around the castle garden until nightfall and then re-enter the Market [[gf1-427-1]]. One comment says to play the Sun's Song to make the dog follow you [[ra-comments-55-1]]. You do not have that song yet, so use the waiting method [[gf1-427-1]].

At night the Market is mostly empty and many dogs run around. Find the small white dog behind the stall to the left of the Market entrance from the drawbridge [[gf1-37-2, ra-comments-55-2, ra-guide-8-5]]. Walk up to it and press A so it follows you [[ra-comments-55-2, gf2-14-1]].

Lead it to the back alley in the corner and into the door on the left. Sources disagree on which one: the second door on the left [[gf1-38-1, gf1-427-1]], or the first door [[gf2-14-1, ra-guide-8-5]]. The correct house holds a woman who recognizes the dog as hers. Talk to her [[ra-comments-55-3, ra-comments-55-4]]. She gives a Piece of Heart [[checklist-sidequests-2-1, gf1-38-1]].
