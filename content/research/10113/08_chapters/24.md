## Chapter 24: Ganon's Castle and the Final Battle

[MERMAID]

This is the end of the first file. You clear the six trials in Ganon's Castle, take the Golden Gauntlets, visit the Great Fairy outside for the defense upgrade, climb the tower for the last Boss Key, beat Ganondorf, escape, and beat Ganon. Four achievements land here: The Rock (49289), King of Thieves (49290), My Last Breath (49291) and Home Sweet Home (48275). Cartographer (49294) is checked when the game is completed. [[gf1-327-1, gf1-332-5, gf1-334-1, gf1-342-2, ra-comments-122-1]]

The Light Arrows come from the Temple of Time, and the road to the castle appears once you hold both the Shadow and Spirit Medallions. [[checklist-sidequests-35-2, gf1-423-3, gf1-391-4]]

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Cartographer (49294) needs every Map, Compass and Boss Key. The Boss Key inside Ganon's Tower is the 26th item, and the achievement is checked when you complete the game, not when you pick up the last item. Check the counter in your achievements menu before you fight Ganon. [[ra-comments-3-4, ra-comments-121-2, ra-comments-121-3, ra-comments-122-1, ra-comments-123-10]]
>
> If you finish the game while an item is missing, you must go back for it and beat Ganondorf and Ganon again. [[ra-comments-124-5, ra-comments-122-3]]
>
> Only boss keys count, not small keys. [[ra-comments-122-11]]

> [!CAUTION]
> **Point of No Return:** Beating Ganon ends the first file. Cartographer is checked on completion, so every Map, Compass and Boss Key must already be in hand. Finish every other first-file achievement before climbing the tower. [[ra-comments-121-3, gf1-345-1]]

### Before you enter: preparation

- Check that every earlier dungeon Map, Compass and Boss Key is collected. The count is 25 of 26 before the tower. [[ra-comments-123-22, ra-comments-121-3]]
- Bring at least two empty Bottles for Blue Fire, and also Din's Fire, Fire Arrows, Light Arrows, Ice Arrows, the Lens of Truth, the Longshot, the Hover Boots, Iron Boots, Goron Tunic, Megaton Hammer, Mirror Shield, the Ocarina (Song of Time, Zelda's Lullaby) and bombs. These are used in the trials below. [[gf1-330-5, gf1-331-3, gf1-328-5, gf1-329-1, gf1-335-1, gf1-339-5, checklist-sidequests-35-4]]
- Bombchus are not needed in your pack at the start; one trial supplies them. [[gf1-339-2]]
- **Gold Skulltulas outside the castle:** Hookshot the Gold Skulltula behind the fallen gate and shattered arch. It is only there at night as Adult Link. [[gf1-137-1, gf1-459-1, checklist-sidequests-29-4]]

### Ganon's Castle

#### Entrance and the Keep

Run down the stairs, go right between the two Beamos statues, and go through the door into the Keep. The Keep is a hub with six hallways, one per Sage. Each hallway holds a seal, and destroying all six removes the barrier around the tower. [[gf1-328-1, gf1-327-1, gf1-330-1]]

You can do the trials in any order, but the Shadow trial must be done before the Fire and Light ones can be completed. Do Shadow first. [[checklist-sidequests-35-3]]

#### Shadow trial (do this first)

Go right up the stairs and through the door with the Shadow Medallion. [[gf1-332-2]]

1. Reach the chest on the left with the Longshot (or use the Song of Time to make three Blocks of Time and cross with the Hover Boots). It holds a **Blue Rupee**. [[gf1-332-3, gf2-98-3, gf3-86-4]]
2. Turn around and light the torch with a Fire Arrow. Temporary platforms appear toward the back. [[gf1-332-4]]
3. Run along them to the larger platform. A Like Like waits on it, and you can kill it with an Ice Arrow. Relight the same torch to renew the platforms. [[gf1-332-7, checklist-sidequests-35-4]]
4. On the next platform, run down the opaque platforms to the right and step on the switch before time runs out. A chest drops: open it for the **Golden Gauntlets**. [[gf1-332-5, gf3-86-5, gf4-34-3]]
5. Use the Lens of Truth on the left to find an invisible path to a rusty switch and smash it with the Megaton Hammer. Then follow another invisible path back toward the unsealed door. [[gf1-332-7, gf2-98-4]]
6. The Shadow trial also has an eye switch on the invisible path that makes a chest with a Small Key appear, and a bomb flower on the far right that, when shot, makes a chest with arrows appear (Longshot to it). [[gf3-114-3, gf3-114-4]]
7. In the final room, shoot the seal with a Light Arrow. Impa returns you to the Keep. [[gf1-333-1]]

#### The Rock: Great Fairy outside the castle

> [!TIP]
> Do this now. The Gold Gauntlets let you toss the black obelisks inside and outside the castle, and they appear nowhere else in the game. [[gf1-332-6]]

Leave the castle, cross the bridge of light, and go left under the arch. Climb over the rubble to the end of the small canyon and pick up the black obelisk with the Golden Gauntlets, then throw it aside. A Great Fairy Fountain is revealed. [[gf1-333-2, gf1-411-4, gf4-34-4, ra-comments-103-2]]

Stand on the Triforce symbol and play Zelda's Lullaby. The Great Fairy of Courage gives you the defense upgrade, which doubles your health in effect and puts a white border on your hearts. [[gf1-334-1, gf1-416-2, gf2-154-2, gf3-87-1, gf3-233-6]]

[ACHIEVEMENT:49289]

Head back into the castle and return to the Keep. [[gf1-334-1]]

#### Forest trial

Go right from the Keep to the door with the Forest Medallion. [[gf1-328-2]]

- **Room one:** Step into the middle and a Gray Wolfos appears. Backflip away from its attack and jump-attack its back. A chest then appears with a **Blue Rupee**. Stand on the carpet and use Din's Fire to light the four torches, then light the torch above the sealed door with a Fire Arrow. The door unseals. [[gf1-328-3, gf1-328-4, gf1-328-5, gf2-98-1, gf2-98-2, gf4-34-5]]
- **Room two (Silver Rupees, fans and a pit):** If you fall in the pit you must collect the Silver Rupees again. [[gf1-329-1]]
  1. Play the Song of Time when Navi flutters under the first Silver Rupee above the pit. A Block of Time appears. [[gf1-329-1]]
  2. Equip the Iron Boots so the fan doesn't blow you, and walk onto the block for the rupee. [[gf1-329-2]]
  3. When the fan stops, switch to the Hover Boots, wait for it to start again, and run toward the left ledge. The wind carries you across. [[gf1-329-3]]
  4. Switch back to the Kokiri Boots and bomb the Beamos statue. Jump to its platform and go right for another rupee. [[gf1-329-4, gf1-329-5]]
  5. Climb to the front of the sealed door, and when the right fan stops, jump out to grab the next rupee. [[gf1-329-6]]
  6. Wait for the fan on the left of the door to stop, then drop to the switch on the platform beneath it. A Longshot statue appears under the last high rupee. Longshot to it and grab the rupee. The door on the other side unseals; cross via the Beamos platform. [[gf1-329-7, gf1-329-8]]
  7. Room two also has a chest with a Small Key above the door, reached by Longshot to the torch, and chests behind eye switches in the fans: arrows (shoot the eye in the right fan) and bombs (Fire Arrow the frozen eye). [[gf3-113-3, gf3-113-4, gf3-113-5]]
  8. Playing the Song of Time twice with the Iron Boots on drops an Armos onto a blue switch and opens the door ahead. [[gf3-113-6]]
- **Room three:** Shoot the green seal with a Light Arrow. Saria sends you back. [[gf1-330-2]]

#### Water trial

Head right out of the Forest hallway and down the stairs to the door with the Water Medallion. [[gf1-330-3]]

- **Room one:** Stalactites fall and two Freezzards appear. Fire Arrows work well. The door behind the ice unseals once both are dead. Catch Blue Fire from the center in an empty bottle and melt the ice over the door. Fill a second bottle too. Do not bother with the other chests, because one freezes you. The chest on the right has a **Recovery Heart**. [[gf1-330-4, gf1-330-5, gf2-99-1]]
- **Room two (two-minute limit):** Push the rear ice block to the right so it hits a rock, then forward into the pit. Push the other block forward across the filled pit, forward again from the door side, then forward to the far wall. Climb up, melt the red ice with Blue Fire, and hit the rusted switch with the Megaton Hammer. [[gf1-331-1, gf1-331-2, gf1-331-3, gf2-99-2]]
- **Room three:** Shoot the orb with a Light Arrow. Ruto returns you to the Keep. [[gf1-332-1]]
- A red-ice-covered chest in the Water trial holds a **Red Rupee** if you want it. [[gf3-114-1]]

#### Fire trial

Past the Shadow seal, enter the door with the Fire Medallion, wearing the Goron Tunic. You need the Golden Gauntlets here. [[gf1-334-2, gf1-335-1, gf3-113-7]]

1. Jump onto the sinking platform, go right, and grab the first Silver Rupee. [[gf1-335-2]]
2. Return, go right, left at the corner, and straight at the branch to the ledge with rotating flames. Hang on the edge until it is safe. Take the second rupee. [[gf1-335-2]]
3. Follow the path to the black obelisk, throw it aside with the Gauntlets, and take the third rupee under it. [[gf1-335-2]]
4. Go straight at the split to the Fire Slug ledge, kill it, and take the fourth rupee. [[gf1-335-2]]
5. Cross the thrown pillar like a bridge to the last rupee. The rear door unseals. [[gf1-335-2]]
6. Return to the sinking platform, Longshot the target to the right of the door and go through. [[gf1-335-3]]
7. Shoot the seal with a Light Arrow. Darunia sends you back. [[gf1-336-1]]

#### Light trial

At the bottom of the stairs to the right of the Fire door, throw the black obelisk aside with the Gauntlets. It shatters against the shield. The door behind it bears the Light Medallion. [[gf1-336-2, gf3-114-5]]

- **Room one:** Use the Lens of Truth to kill an invisible Big Skulltula and four invisible Keese. A chest in the middle appears with a **Small Key**. Go through the locked door. Other chests are junk and traps. [[gf1-336-3, gf3-87-3]]
- **Room two:** Stand on the Triforce symbol and play Zelda's Lullaby. A chest appears with another **Small Key**. [[gf1-337-1, gf3-87-4]]
- **Room three (one-minute limit):** Longshot to the ceiling target above the central pillar for the first Silver Rupee, then collect the other four from the alcoves and pillar area as the boulders pass. [[gf1-337-2]]
- **Final room:** The Lens of Truth shows the far wall is fake. Walk through it, shoot the orb with a Light Arrow, and Rauru returns you. [[gf1-338-1, gf1-338-2]]

#### Spirit trial

Go right from the Light door and up the stairs to the Spirit Medallion door. [[gf1-338-3]]

- **Room one:** Bomb the Beamos, Longshot to the ceiling target for the rupee above it, then take the second behind it. Gather the other three near the Blade Traps in the corners. The door unseals. [[gf1-338-4, gf1-338-5, gf1-338-6]]
- **Room two:** Kill the two Fire Slugs, then hit the crystal switch beyond the bars with a Spin Attack. A chest appears with **Bombchus**. Send a Bombchu through the gap near the top of the bars to the second crystal between the torches. [[gf1-339-1, gf1-339-2, gf1-339-3, gf3-87-2]]
- **Room three:** Burn the ceiling web with a Fire Arrow, then reflect the sunlight with the Mirror Shield onto the sun panel to the right of the tunnel you came through. Other panels summon Wall Masters. [[gf1-339-4, gf1-339-5]]
- **Final room:** Shoot the orb with a Light Arrow. Nabooru returns you to the Keep and the barrier around the tower disappears. [[gf1-340-1]]

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave the Keep, confirm the counter reads 25 / 26 for Cartographer. The Boss Key in the tower will make it 26 / 26. Then finish every other first-file achievement and save, because the next fights end the file. [[ra-comments-121-3, ra-comments-123-10]]

### Ganon's Tower

Head right in the Keep until you see a bridge on the left. Cross it and enter the tower. [[gf1-340-2]]

#### Tower floors

- **Floor one:** Kill the four Fire Keese, climb the spiral staircase on the left (watch for more Keese), and take the door on the right. [[gf1-340-3, gf1-340-4]]
- **Floor two:** Both doors seal. Defeat both Dinolfos to unseal them and go through the far door. Climb the stairs and take the door on the left. [[gf1-341-1, gf1-341-2]]
- **Floor three:** Two Stalfos appear around a pillar of flames. Kill both and the flames die, revealing an ornate chest with the **Boss Key**. This makes 26 / 26. Go through the door behind it and up the stairs, then take the door on the left. [[gf1-342-1, gf1-342-2, gf2-100-1, gf3-88-1, gf4-35-3, ra-comments-121-3]]
- **Floor four:** Two Iron Knuckles. Shooting one with an arrow wakes it alone, so you can use the backflip-then-jump-attack pattern. If both wake, Nayru's Love is a good idea. Defeating both unseals the doors. Take the door the camera focuses on, climb the stairs, and take the boss door on the left. [[gf1-343-1, gf1-343-2, gf1-343-3]]
- **Floor five:** Pots hold Magic Power and Recovery Hearts. Take the door to the right of where you came in, climb the stairs, and go through the boss door on the left. [[gf1-344-1, gf1-344-2]]

> [!TIP]
> Smash the pots on Floor five to refill magic and hearts before the boss door. You need magic for Light Arrows. [[gf1-344-1, gf1-344-6]]

#### Boss: Ganondorf (King of Thieves)

> [!NOTE]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** King of Thieves needs Ganondorf beaten without falling off or climbing down the platform and without using Fairies. Equip the Hover Boots as soon as the fight starts. A commenter reports it also counted after going down to pick up magic on a 3-heart run, but that is not confirmed by the achievement text. [[gf4-34-1, ra-guide-18-3, ra-comments-50-5]]

Navi cannot Z-Target or advise until a Light Arrow stuns him. [[gf1-344-3, gf1-522-4, gf3-88-2]]

1. When Ganondorf pounds the ground at the start, roll to the outer rim so you do not fall. [[gf3-89-1]]
2. Stand on the perimeter and reflect his lightning balls back with sword swings (or empty Bottles) until one hits him. [[gf1-344-4, gf3-89-2, gf4-34-1]]
3. Once he is struck, shoot him with a Light Arrow before he recovers. He drops to the ground. [[gf3-89-5, gf1-523-1]]
4. Hover across the pit with the Hover Boots and jump-attack when halfway across, then slash. When he recovers, run back to the perimeter and switch back to the Kokiri Boots. [[gf1-344-5, gf1-523-2]]
5. Do not go near him before he is stunned: he uses a Shockwave that collapses floor pieces into pits. A missed ball destroys the panel you stand on and drops you a floor, and you have to climb back up the pillar tiles. Avoid this for the achievement. [[gf1-522-5, gf1-522-6, gf1-522-7]]
6. If he raises both arms to charge a big spell, shoot him in the face with a Light Arrow to cancel it and stun him. If you cannot, deflect the volley with an Advanced Spin Slash. [[gf1-344-6, gf3-89-3, gf3-89-4, gf4-34-2]]
7. Three knockdowns with the Biggoron's Sword are enough. His cape tears as he weakens. [[gf2-100-2, gf2-131-2, gf1-523-3]]

[ACHIEVEMENT:49290]

Commenters suggest memorising the long staircase to the boss if you need to retry. [[ra-comments-50-4]]

#### Escape

Follow Zelda down the tower. You have three minutes and only she can open the sealed doors, so stay near her. [[gf1-345-1, gf1-345-2]]

- On Floor four, go around the burning rubble and through the door she opens. [[gf1-346-1]]
- On Floor three she is trapped in flame and two Stalfos appear. Use Nayru's Love and the Biggoron Sword to kill them quickly. [[gf1-346-3, gf1-346-4, gf3-90-2]]
- On Floor two, follow her through the burning rubble and down the stairs. Take the left door at the bottom. [[gf1-347-1]]
- In the Keep, run along the bridge, past or through the Redead. At the far end she opens the door. Go up the stairs and wait for her to open the last door. [[gf1-347-2, gf1-347-3]]

> [!TIP]
> My Last Breath tips: a commenter says falling debris or fire stones during the escape can drop you to one heart before the fight starts. Another says to avoid walking into the burning rocks if you don't want to risk it. Pick your method before this point. [[ra-comments-52-8, ra-comments-52-9, ra-comments-51-8]]

#### Boss: Ganon (My Last Breath and Home Sweet Home)

> [!NOTE]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** My Last Breath needs Ganon beaten with 1 heart or less, without Fairies or Bombs. Whether Bombchus count as bombs is unconfirmed by any source. Use neither bombs nor Bombchus against Ganon. Commenters say a fairy picked up from debris still counted, but that is not confirmed by the achievement text, so do not rely on it. [[ra-comments-51-2, ra-guide-18-4]]

Navi can Z-Target Ganon this time, but you start without the Master Sword. Shields are useless. [[gf1-347-4, gf1-523-5]]

**Reaching one heart:** Commenters say to get an odd number of hearts, then let Ganon take you down to one heart. Milk can change an even total to odd. A health total of one heart is the goal. The defense upgrade is allowed, and one report says it is probably needed. [[ra-comments-51-1, ra-comments-51-3, ra-comments-51-4, ra-comments-52-13, ra-comments-52-14]]

Other ways commenters used: after the first knockdown and getting the Master Sword back, let him hit you, or walk into the ring of fire around the arena until you reach one heart. Then cast Nayru's Love for the rest of the fight. [[ra-comments-51-10, ra-comments-51-11, ra-comments-52-10]]

**Phase one (no Master Sword):**
1. Z-Target Ganon and side dodge (hold left or right and press A) behind him. [[gf1-347-5, gf1-523-4]]
2. Jump-attack his tail with the Biggoron Sword (or Megaton Hammer). He raises the tail afterward, so strafe back to the front, then around to the tail again. [[gf1-347-5, gf1-523-5]]
3. Alternatively, shoot his face with a Light Arrow to stun him, or roll between his legs, then hit the tail. [[gf2-100-3, gf2-132-1, gf3-91-1, gf4-35-4]]
4. Repeat until a cutscene lowers the flames. Run to Zelda and retake the Master Sword. [[gf1-347-6, gf3-91-2]]

**Phase two:** Light Arrow to the face or roll between his legs, then Z-Target and jump-attack the tail. Commenters note only the Master Sword does real damage here, and Nayru's Love can expire if you swap swords, leaving you open. Light Arrows cannot be used while Nayru's Love is active. [[gf1-347-7, ra-comments-52-1, ra-comments-52-2, ra-comments-52-16]]

If Nayru's Love starts flashing, spam its C-button to recast it. After the Master Sword is back, a commenter says Ganon can take at least five more hits. [[ra-comments-51-15, ra-comments-51-16]]

When Ganon falls, Zelda holds him down. Strike the final blow with the Master Sword while the Sages power it. [[gf1-523-7, gf2-100-4, gf2-132-2, gf3-91-3, gf3-267-4]]
