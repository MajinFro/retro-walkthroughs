## Chapter 26: 3-Heart Run: The Adult Temples

[MERMAID]

This chapter covers the five adult temples on your **second save file**, the 3-heart run: Forest, Fire, Water, Shadow and Spirit. Each temple ends with a boss that carries a 3-heart achievement: Dodgeball (Phantom Ganon), Whac-A-Mole (Volvagia), Tentacles of the Deep (Morpha), Invisible Stalker (Bongo Bongo) and Double Trouble (Twinrova). Each is worth 10 points and can be missed. The counters in the checkpoint at the end belong to your first file and do not change here. Every temple must still be finished, with no medallion skipped.

> [!WARNING]
> **Open question, unconfirmed:** The RA guide says a few Pieces of Heart are fine, but one commenter says no heart upgrades are allowed [[ra-guide-21-1, ra-comments-132-1]]. A commenter who held a single Piece of Heart from the masks sidequest, and still showed three hearts, reported the achievement did not unlock [[ra-comments-132-15]]. Nothing collected settles this, so pick up **no** Piece of Heart and **no** Heart Container on this file. After every boss, leave the Heart Container on the floor and walk straight into the light. Do not pick up a heart upgrade at all.

### Preparation for the whole chapter

- **Gear from the first-file route:** you need the Hookshot from Dampe's race [[gf1-135-3]]. You need the Iron Boots from the Ice Cavern [[gf1-213-3]]. You need the Zora Tunic, which you get by thawing King Zora with Blue Fire [[gf3-62-3, checklist-sidequests-18-12]]. The Zora Tunic lets you breathe underwater while wearing the Iron Boots [[gf1-403-3]].
- **Goron Tunic:** get it before you finish the Fire Temple. One commenter confirms it becomes completely unobtainable if you get the Longshot and beat the Fire Temple first [[ra-comments-91-2]].
- **Din's Fire:** it comes from the Great Fairy in the Hyrule Castle garden, reached by bombing the boulder at the dead end [[gf1-90-2, gf4-11-4]]. You will use it in the Fire, Water, Shadow and Spirit Temples.
- **Biggoron's Sword:** it helps in most of these fights. Two bosses restrict it (Whac-A-Mole and Double Trouble), covered at each boss.
- **Maps and Compasses:** do not open any chest that holds a Dungeon Map or a Compass on this file. Each temple has one of each; the route below tells you which chests to ignore. The Ice Cavern and Bottom of the Well Maps and Compasses must also stay in their chests. Save before any chest you are unsure of.
- **Gold Skulltulas:** they are not tracked on this file. The route ignores them except where a key or an item depends on the room.
- **Hardcore:** save states are off. Warp songs, Farore's Wind and normal in-game saves are the only tools you have.

#### Story steps between the temples

The overworld steps are the same as on the first file. Follow these first-file chapters for them, but skip every Piece of Heart, Gold Skulltula and side quest, and leave every Map and Compass chest closed:

1. **Before the Forest Temple:** Chapter 10 (Ocarina of Time, Master Sword) and Chapter 11 (Hookshot from Dampe, Epona, Goron Tunic) [[gf1-135-3]].
2. **Before the Fire Temple:** the Bolero of Fire from Chapter 15.
3. **Before the Water Temple:** Chapter 12 for the Ice Cavern, the Iron Boots and the Zora Tunic. The Ice Cavern Map and Compass stay in their chests [[gf1-213-3, gf3-62-3]].
4. **Before the Shadow Temple:** Chapter 17 for the Song of Storms and the Bottom of the Well (Lens of Truth). Its Map and Compass stay in their chests too [[gf1-19-1]].
5. **Before the Spirit Temple:** Chapter 19 for the Gerudo's Membership Card, then the Haunted Wasteland and the Requiem of Spirit from Chapter 20 [[gf1-19-2]].
6. **After the Spirit Temple:** the Light Arrows from Zelda in Chapter 21, then Chapter 27 for Ganon's Castle.

### Forest Temple

Reach the entrance by Hookshotting the large tree branch above the shattered staircase, then going through the doorway [[gf1-141-6]]. The temple sits in the Sacred Forest Meadow [[gf1-8-3]]. The central goal is to kill the four Poe Sisters and return their stolen flames, which reactivates the central elevator [[gf1-143-1]]. If you got the Biggoron's Sword, keep the Master Sword and Hylian Shield for most of the dungeon [[gf1-144-1]].

#### Entrance and Torch Room

1. Kill the two Gray Wolfos by waiting for their attack, backflipping out of range, and landing a Jump Attack on their turned backs [[gf1-144-1]].
2. Climb the ivy wall to the treetops, jump to the adjacent branch, and open the chest at the end for a **Small Key** [[gf1-144-3]]. Drop down and take the door beneath the stone archway.
3. In the South Passage, one Hookshot shot kills the Big Skulltula. Go through the far door [[gf1-145-1]].
4. In the Torch Room the four Poe Sisters steal the flames and the central elevator lowers [[gf1-145-2]]. Run across to the opposite end, climb the ledge and stairs, and take the door at the top [[gf1-145-3]].
5. In the North Passage, hold your shield up while Z-Targeting the Blue Bubble and let it touch you, which snuffs its flame [[gf1-146-3]].
6. In the Lower Stalfos Chamber, Z-Target one Stalfos so neither attacks, and strike from the side or back right after their attacks [[gf1-146-1]]. Killing both makes a chest appear with a **Small Key** [[gf1-146-2]]. Deku Nuts also stun them mid-attack [[checklist-sidequests-23-5]].

#### Courtyards and the Well

1. Back in the Torch Room, go to the small hallway on the right and play the Song of Time at the blue block to reveal a door [[gf1-147-1]].
2. In the West Courtyard, climb the ivy wall on the far right to the alcove door [[gf1-147-2]].
3. In the Balcony Hallway, kill the Blue Bubble to open the far door. A chest appears with the Dungeon Map. **Do not open it** on this file [[gf1-148-1]]. Take the opened door.
4. In the East Courtyard, Hookshot the white circle above the left balcony and step on the switch to drain the well [[gf1-148-2]]. Drop down, then jump into the well [[gf1-148-3]].
5. At the end of the well passage, open the chest for a **Small Key** [[gf1-149-1]]. Climb the ivy wall back to the West Courtyard and return to the Torch Room [[gf1-149-2]].
6. Climb the ledge on the right of the West Courtyard door, go up the stairs, and unlock the door. Hookshot the Big Skulltula in the West Passage and continue [[gf1-149-3]].

#### Stone Block Room, Bow and Poes

1. Kill the Blue Bubble, climb the ladders and enter the room with the giant stone block [[gf1-150-1]]. The block must be pushed along the painted arrows [[gf1-150-2]].
2. Push the block forward until a passage opens on the left. Slip through behind it and push it back until the gap closes [[gf1-150-3]]. Push it forward until it stops, loop around taking three rights, and push it into the small alcove to make a step to the third level [[gf1-150-4]].
3. Climb the ladder, turn right and right again, and push the upper block until it stops. Drop back down [[gf1-150-5]]. Climb the lower block, run to the third-level block and push it into the pit to reach the top level [[gf1-150-6]]. Dodge or kill the two Blue Bubbles and take the far door [[gf1-150-7]].
4. In the West Twisted Hallway, watch for Wallmasters. Run in large circles when you see a shadow and kill it when it lands, or enter First Person Mode to make it vanish [[gf1-151-1, gf1-151-2]]. Jump to the stone pillar, then right to the door [[gf1-151-3]].
5. Run to the bottom of the West Stairwell and through the door [[gf1-152-1]]. In the Upper Stalfos Room, defeat the Stalfos that walks over the pit. An elevator then brings two more, which you must kill quickly or they regenerate [[gf1-152-2, gf1-152-3]]. The chest holds the **Bow and Arrows** [[gf1-152-4]].
6. Return to the West Stairwell, whose Poe is red and whose stairs are on the left [[gf1-153-1]]. Shoot each of the three portraits from far away, then shoot the Poe repeatedly without Z-Targeting [[gf1-153-2, gf1-153-3]]. Her chest holds a **Small Key** [[gf1-153-4]].
7. Return to the Stone Block Room and shoot the silver eye above the door to straighten the West Twisted Hallway [[gf1-154-1]]. Run down the hall, drop in, and open the blue chest for the **Boss Key**. Then drop through the floor pit [[gf1-154-2]]. Kill both Blue Bubbles to open the south door [[gf1-154-3]].
8. On the upper West Courtyard ledge, kill the Big Deku Baba and take the far alcove door [[gf1-155-1, gf1-155-3]]. In the Floormaster room, avoid it while it glows green. When it splits into three small hands, shoot them quickly. The chest holds a **Small Key** [[gf1-156-1, gf1-156-2, gf1-156-3]].

#### East side, Amy and the Spinner Room

1. Go back through the right-hand door into the Stone Block Room [[gf1-157-1]]. The golden eye on the third level gives a chest of arrows [[gf1-157-2]]. Shoot the silver eye to re-twist the hallway and return to the Upper Stalfos Room [[gf1-157-3]]. Take the opposite door [[gf1-158-1]].
2. In the East Stairwell, destroy the three portraits and kill the Poe. Her chest holds the Compass. **Do not open it.** Climb to the top and go through the locked door [[gf1-158-2, gf1-158-3]].
3. In the East Twisted Hallway, kill the Wallmaster. Then climb the tall ladder on the right and pass the two Green Bubbles to the door [[gf1-158-4, gf1-158-5, gf1-158-6]].
4. In the Flaming Arrow Room, ride the rotating platforms and shoot an arrow through the center flame onto the frozen eye switch. This twists the previous hallway [[gf1-159-1]]. Cross it, kill the Wallmaster and drop down the corner pit [[gf1-159-2]].
5. Run south through the Crushing Ceiling Room, stopping at each gap. Step on the switch halfway down on the right to open the exit [[gf1-160-1]].
6. In the Puzzle Block Room, shoot the portrait, push the non-matching block aside, and assemble the other four. Then kill the yellow Poe [[gf1-160-2]]. Climb the ledge near the torch to the door [[gf1-160-3]].
7. Kill the Big Skulltula in the East Hallway [[gf1-161-1]]. In the Torch Room, Amy splits into four, and the real one spins on splitting. Shoot that one with an arrow and repeat [[gf1-161-2]]. Ride the elevator down [[gf1-161-3]].
8. In the Spinner Room, push the left handle's north side south and step on the northwest switch. Push the right handle's north side south and step on the east alcove switch [[gf1-162-1, gf1-162-2]]. Push the handle's east side west and go to the southwest room; the Big Skulltula guards a chest of arrows [[gf1-162-3]]. Push the handle's south side north and step on the south switch to open the north grating [[gf1-162-5]]. Go through the north doorway and the door beyond.

#### Boss: Phantom Ganon

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Dodgeball (3 hearts only, no healing) can only be earned during this fight [[ra-comments-132-1, ra-comments-132-2]]. The achievement watches any change in your health bar, so do not drink potions, take fairies or pick up recovery hearts while you fight [[ra-comments-132-2]]. Commenters report finishing it with no damage at all [[ra-comments-132-3]].

Run into the middle of the stage and turn to leave; spears trap you and Phantom Ganon appears [[gf1-162-6]]. In phase one, two Ganons ride toward you from two paintings. The fake turns back at the last minute and the real one emerges and scatters electricity [[gf1-519-6]]. Stand in the middle of the floor lines that lead to the corners, so a missed shot does not hit you [[ra-guide-12-4, ra-comments-132-11]]. Listen for the louder footsteps, or look for the darker Ganon with whiter whites, as the real one [[ra-guide-12-4]]. A commenter says the horse sound comes from the right one [[ra-comments-132-4]]. When the portal appears, shoot an arrow into it. Three hits end phase one [[ra-guide-12-4, gf2-58-1, gf2-58-2]].

In phase two, deflect the lightning balls back with sword strikes; the ball speeds up with each hit [[gf1-162-7, gf1-519-8]]. Use the sword for this fight [[gf3-261-3]]. Two hits is all your hearts can take, and the camera can snag on the railings [[ra-comments-132-12]]. Staying close and swinging as soon as the orb leaves him is easier [[ra-comments-132-8, gf3-48-3]]. A distant position gives more reaction time instead [[ra-comments-132-18]]. If he laughs and flies higher, just dodge the orb [[ra-comments-132-10]]. If he fails to deflect, you get time for only one attack, so use a jump attack [[ra-comments-132-5]]. If he deflects several times, he stays stunned longer, so jump attack and mash the sword [[ra-comments-132-6]]. When he drops stunned, do one Jump Attack, then regular strikes [[gf1-163-1]]. The Biggoron's Sword can finish the second phase in three jump attacks [[ra-comments-132-14]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48917]

Leave the Heart Container on the floor [[gf1-163-2]]. Walk into the beam of light to receive the Forest Medallion from Saria [[gf1-163-3]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave the Forest Temple:** you have the Bow and the Boss Key route behind you, and the Map and Compass chests should still be unopened [[gf1-148-1, gf1-158-2]]. The Heart Container is on the floor [[gf1-163-2]].

### Fire Temple

The temple sits in the middle of Death Mountain's crater [[gf1-8-8]]. The boss door is next door to the entrance, but you must climb to the top floor to make the bridge [[gf1-178-1, gf1-9-10]]. Keys and the Boss Key come from rescuing trapped Gorons [[gf1-178-2]]. Wear the Goron Tunic. Bring a Green Potion or two for the magic you will spend on Din's Fire. This is a magic potion, not a healing item [[gf3-98-10]].

#### Entrance, Lava Cavern and the first prisons

1. Run up the stairs past the Fire Keese and take the left door at the top [[gf1-179-1]]. In the Pillar Room, Darunia leaves to fight Volvagia. Jump across the lava pillars to the left corner platform and step on the switch. Talk to the Goron and open the chest for a **Small Key** [[gf1-179-2, gf1-179-3]].
2. Go back through the Entrance Hall straight across to the Lava Cavern [[gf1-180-1]]. Run to the left ledge with the sandstone platform and Block of Time. Go through the door under the block to Prison Corridor #1 and step on the switch. The Goron's chest holds a **Small Key** [[gf1-180-2]].
3. Back at the platform, play the Song of Time to shift the block. Climb it and take the door on top [[gf1-181-1]].
4. In the Killer Tile Room, put your back to a wall and block the tiles with your shield until they stop. Kill the Like-Like; it can eat your shield, and you recover it when it dies [[gf1-181-2]].
5. Cross the cavern to the opposite sandstone platform and bomb the oddly colored wall to reveal a door [[gf1-182-1]]. In Prison Corridor #2 step on the switch and open the chest for a **Small Key** [[gf1-182-2, gf1-182-4]]. Take the north door, to the right as you exit [[gf1-182-3]].

#### Lava Column to the top of the maze

1. Slide down the log bridge into the cage and climb its wire wall to the top. Walk the platforms out and drop to a sandstone platform [[gf1-183-1]]. Push the massive block on the east pillar onto the erupting lava column and ride it up as an elevator [[gf1-183-2]].
2. In the Three-Tiered Room, kill the Fire Slugs, putting your shield up when one expands into a column [[gf1-184-1]]. Push the stone block into the track, jump down and pull it onto the carved stone face [[gf1-184-2]]. Climb the left wire wall, drop a bomb by the Diamond Switch to remove the flame pillar, then climb the new wire wall and go through the top door [[gf1-184-3]].
3. In the Boulder Maze, hug the outside wall with your right side to it. Take the first right to an alcove with a door [[gf1-185-2]]. Prison Corridor #3 holds a Goron whose chest has a **Small Key** [[gf1-186-1]]. Continue around to the far-left dead end, step on the switch, and take the Goron's **Small Key** [[gf1-186-2]]. Return to the locked door and go through [[gf1-186-3]].
4. In the Narrow Span Room, shoot the Silver Eye to unseal the right-hand door [[gf1-187-1]]. The Lava Curtain Room has the Dungeon Map. **Do not open it** [[gf1-187-2]]. Return and unlock the door on your right [[gf1-187-3]].
5. On the wire platforms, dodge the slow lava curtain, jump across the gap, and climb the sandstone steps to the upper-left door [[gf1-188-1]].
6. On top of the Boulder Maze, step on the small platform switch to open the far Goron cell. Kill the Lava Slugs and take the Goron's chest **Small Key** [[gf1-189-1, gf1-189-2]]. Drop a bomb on the floor gash and drop into the pit [[gf1-189-3]].
7. Step on the switch around the corner and open the Goron's chest for a **Small Key** [[gf1-190-1, gf1-190-2]]. Climb the wire wall back up [[gf1-190-3]]. Play the Scarecrow's Song near the odd platform to call Pierre. Hookshot to him and to the circular target, then ride the elevator up and take the adjacent door [[gf1-190-4, gf1-190-5]].
8. The Two-Tiered Room leads to the Time Trial Room. Drop down to the Narrow Span Room, landing on a bridge so you don't fall to the cavern. The 200-rupee chest is optional [[gf1-191-1, gf1-191-3]]. Go through the south door to the Lava Curtain Room and jump to the west-center sandstone ledge for the locked door [[gf1-192-1, gf1-192-2]].

#### Fire Trap Room to the Hammer

1. Go down the West Corridor [[gf1-192-2]]. In the Fire Trap Room, keep the wall on your right until a flame wall appears. Go left, take a right after the first post on your right, then right again to the door [[gf1-193-1]]. The Compass corridor is a dead end with the Compass chest. **Skip it** [[gf1-194-1]]. Return out and cross to the other door, hugging the left wall, passing between the two poles [[gf1-194-2]].
2. In the Two-Floored Prison, go straight across. Return to the Fire Trap Room and reach the switch behind the fire wall. Run up the aisle to the alcove and bomb the dummy door [[gf1-195-1, gf1-195-2]].
3. In the Upper Flare Dancer Room, Hookshot the Flare Dancer's heart free, circle in the opposite direction and Jump Attack it. Use a regular strike if the jump is hard to time [[gf1-196-1, gf1-196-2, gf1-516-5]]. Climb the platform and ride it up [[gf1-196-3]].
4. In the Diamond Switch Room, climb to the second floor and drop a bomb beside the diamond switch, then climb the ladder [[gf1-197-1]]. In the Tippy-Top Room, step on the switch and run the narrow staircase to the chest for the **Megaton Hammer** [[gf1-197-2]]. Smash the floor face and both parts of the corner totem to reveal a door [[gf1-197-3, gf1-197-4]].
5. Smash the stone face, take a box down the new stairs and drop it on the blue switch [[gf1-198-1, gf1-198-2]]. Smash the corner block, jump to the higher ledge and smash the rusted switch beside the sealed door [[gf1-198-3, gf1-198-4]].
6. Back in the Two-Floored Prison, play the Song of Time on the Block of Time, smash the rusty switch, drop into the cell and take the Goron's chest **Small Key**. Then smash and ride the scaffolded block down [[gf1-199-1, gf1-199-2, gf1-199-3, gf1-199-4]].
7. Leave through the Pillar Room east door. In the Entrance Hall, smash the totem pole pieces on the east side to reveal a door [[gf1-200-1]]. Kill the Fire Keese and Fire Slugs to unseal the far door [[gf1-200-2]]. Block the tiles in the Deja Vu Room, kill the Like-Like and leave west [[gf1-201-1]].
8. In the Lower Flare Dancer Room, fight it the same way. The Megaton Hammer is too slow here [[gf1-201-2]]. Its pillar vanishes and a chest of bombs appears [[gf1-201-3]]. Smash the rusted switch in the Boss Key Prison Room, speak to the Goron and open the ornate chest for the **Boss Key** [[gf1-202-1]].
9. Return to the Pillar Room via the Entrance Hall west door and jump to the Boss Key door on the knocked-down pillar [[gf1-202-2]].

#### Boss: Volvagia

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Whac-A-Mole (Hammer only, no healing, 3 hearts) can only be earned during this fight [[gf3-59-1, gf4-23-3, ra-comments-133-1, ra-comments-133-2]]. Do not bring fairies into the room and do not heal [[ra-comments-133-3, ra-comments-133-6]]. Do not use the bow or Hookshot while Volvagia is in the air [[ra-comments-133-2]]. Do not even draw the sword, since one commenter was forced to redo the fight after a stray draw [[ra-comments-133-12]]. There is no in-game tracker, so you cannot see the moment you fail [[ra-comments-134-12]].

The Goron Tunic or Red Tunic may be worn [[ra-comments-133-4, ra-comments-133-5, ra-comments-134-1]]. A commenter kept the Biggoron's Sword equipped but only used the Hammer [[ra-comments-133-9, ra-comments-134-11]]; another saw it seem to break the achievement, so consider unequipping it [[ra-comments-133-8]]. Nayru's Love was confirmed as allowed by two commenters [[ra-comments-134-8, ra-comments-134-9]]. Keep the Hammer out for the whole fight [[ra-comments-133-10]].

Jump to the central slab and run to its middle to begin [[gf1-519-11]]. In phase one, Volvagia pops from one of nine pits, marked by smoke and cinders. Run to the pit, keep your distance until the hair sweep ends, then Hammer her head with a Jump Attack to stun her. A second hit follows [[gf1-202-3, gf2-67-1]]. Give her a little distance before she emerges, because a hit can take a huge chunk [[ra-comments-134-15, ra-comments-133-14]]. She can instantly kill a no-fairy 3-heart Link [[ra-comments-133-13]]. She will also fake you out by erupting fire from one hole and appearing in another [[gf3-262-2]].

In phase two, when she flies breathing fire, run in small circles near the center [[gf1-202-4]]. A flamethrower hit is an instant kill, so keep moving [[ra-comments-134-16]]. The easier answer is to jump off the edge and hang on; she loses interest and goes back underground [[ra-guide-13-3, gf3-59-2, ra-comments-134-14]]. When boulders fall, hang off the edge or stay at the platform's edge [[gf1-202-5, ra-comments-134-17]]. Some rocks still drift off the side at random [[ra-comments-134-13]], and one commenter says falling boulders can still hit you there [[ra-comments-134-2]]. A commenter's rhythm: jump attack twice, run the edge while she flies, repeat three more times [[ra-comments-133-11]]. The achievement only unlocks once she is dead and touching the ground [[ra-comments-133-7]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48913]

Leave the Heart Container [[gf1-202-6]] and enter the ring of light for the Fire Medallion from Darunia [[gf1-202-7]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave the Fire Temple:** the Map and Compass chests should still be sealed and the Heart Container on the floor [[gf1-187-2, gf1-194-1, gf1-202-6]].

### Water Temple

The temple lies deep beneath Lake Hylia [[gf1-8-5]]. The central Vertical Hallway has doors on three levels, and you raise and lower the water to reach them [[gf1-220-1, gf1-9-11]]. This is the temple that traps careless players, so one commenter went without a map and found it much harder than expected [[ra-comments-141-12]]. Consider placing Farore's Wind in the Vertical Hallway after the water is set to full [[gf1-236-2]]. Equip the Zora Tunic and take off the Iron Boots on arrival [[gf1-221-1]].

Warp in with the Serenade of Water [[gf1-213-4]].

#### Vertical Hallway: lowest water

1. Climb out, kill the Tektite, swim to the right passage and into the hallway pool. Equip the Iron Boots, sink, and pull the stone block in the wall back as far as it goes. Float back up [[gf1-221-1]]. The Compass chest on the stinger tunnel route holds the Compass. **Skip the tunnel and its chest** [[gf1-221-3, gf1-221-4]].
2. Sink to the bottom floor, kill any Spikes with two Hookshot shots each, and take the east tunnel marked by two torches to Princess Ruto [[gf1-221-5, gf1-221-6]]. Float up two floors to the air pocket and play Zelda's Lullaby at the Triforce plaque. This is Water Switch One and lowers the water to the bottom [[gf1-222-1]]. The Spike Room to the left of the plaque holds the Dungeon Map. **Do not go for it** [[gf1-222-2]].
3. Drop to the Ruto room and shoot arrows through the lit torch onto the corner lanterns [[gf1-223-1]]. In the Shell Blade Room, Hookshot each blade in its open mouth. The chest holds a **Small Key** [[gf1-223-2, gf1-223-3]].
4. Back in the main area, go to the south doorway. Bomb the odd floor tile, sink with the Iron Boots, Hookshot the Shell Blade, float up, step on the switch, and Hookshot up to the ledge [[gf1-224-1, gf1-224-2, gf1-224-3]]. On the west side, push the stone block down the water pit, sink after it, float up the right passage, and strike the diamond switch. Cross the water pillar to the far door [[gf1-224-6, gf1-224-7]].
5. In the Whirlpool Room, sink with the Iron Boots, Hookshot to the alcove target and hit the diamond switch in the dragon's mouth from the alcove edge [[gf1-225-1]]. Climb out and open the chest for a **Small Key** [[gf1-225-2]]. Strike the diamond switch to reopen the grating and sink back before time runs out [[gf1-225-3]].

#### Central Tower and the highest water

1. Pull yourself back across with the Hookshot target and return to the main room [[gf1-226-1]]. Take the locked south tower door. Hookshot to the ledge and play Zelda's Lullaby at Water Switch Two for the middle water level. Sink with the Iron Boots and go down the new passage [[gf1-226-1, gf1-226-2]].
2. Hookshot the corner switch; defeat the six Spikes and three Shell Blades. Float up the opened passage for a chest **Small Key** [[gf1-226-3, gf1-226-4]].
3. Sink to the bottom and take the east hallway to the room with three unlit torches. Float up one level and bomb the gash in the wall. The chest holds a **Small Key** [[gf1-227-1]].
4. Unlock the second-floor west door. Stand on the water pillar, hit the diamond switch, and ride it up. Kill the Tektite with a Super Spin Attack [[gf1-228-1, gf1-228-2]]. Play Zelda's Lullaby at Water Switch Three for the highest water [[gf1-228-3]]. Take the west locked door from the main room.
5. In the Waterfall-Elevator Room, Hookshot up the descending elevators one after another to the top and take the locked door [[gf1-229-2]].
6. In the Dragon's Head Room, cycle the diamond switch to raise and lower the heads, using their Hookshot targets. Finish by shooting the switch while standing on a lowered head to be lifted to the alcove [[gf1-230-1]]. Bomb the Like Like behind the spikes and Hookshot the ceiling target over them [[gf1-230-2, gf1-230-3]].

#### Shadow Link and the Longshot

Run around the island in the Serene Pool until Dark Link appears [[gf1-231-1]]. The Hammer is the answer, because he cannot deflect it [[gf1-231-2, gf1-517-1]]. Do not Z-Target him if you use the sword, and avoid the stab [[gf1-231-3, gf1-517-2]]. Stay in the room until the pool disappears and the north door unlocks [[gf1-231-4]]. The large chest holds the **Longshot** [[gf1-232-1]]. Play the Song of Time on the block behind the chest and drop down [[gf1-232-2]].

In the underground river, take it slowly and switch between boots to avoid whirlpools [[gf1-232-3]]. Near the fifth whirlpool, climb the small platform and shoot the golden eye. Longshot to the chest inside for a **Small Key** before the gate shuts [[gf1-232-4]]. Jump off the cliff to the Whirlpool Room, leave through the alcove door to the Vertical Hallway [[gf1-233-1]].

Float to the top floor and use the west door to the Waterfall-Elevator Room [[gf1-233-2]]. Then you need the water at its lowest again: go to the Ruto room and play Zelda's Lullaby at the plaque [[gf1-234-1]]. Enter the tower, Longshot to the second level and play Zelda's Lullaby for the middle level [[gf1-234-2]]. Leave to the second level of the Vertical Hallway and walk to the south side of the tower. Shoot the eye in the water to open the grating and Longshot the target behind it [[gf1-235-1]]. Push the block forward until it stops, turn right and open the chest for a **Small Key** [[gf1-235-2]]. Take the opposite passage, Longshot up and return to the main room [[gf1-235-1]].

Ride the water pillar in the west-door room to the third floor and raise the water to maximum with Zelda's Lullaby [[gf1-236-1, gf1-236-2]]. This is the moment to place Farore's Wind if you want a shortcut back [[gf1-236-2]].

#### Boss Key and the boss door

1. Sink to the bottom floor, enter the north doorway pit, and float up. Longshot across the spikes and take the door [[gf1-236-3]]. In the next room, walk along the pond floor in Iron Boots to the northeast corner, float up, and grab the ledge [[gf1-237-1]].
2. In the Stone Block Room, drop into the water, kill the Stingers with the Longshot, and bomb the northwest and northeast walls. Push the hidden block into the pool to press the switch [[gf1-237-2]]. Take the west door.
3. In the Water Pillar Triplets Room, step on the switch and run across the pillars. Jump to the second pillar and then straight to the door [[gf1-238-1, gf1-238-2]].
4. Pass the rolling boulders, use the Iron Boots to drop down the pit under the Skulltula and float to the locked door [[gf1-239-1]]. Open the ornate chest for the **Boss Key** [[gf1-239-2]].
5. Return to the Vertical Hallway, go to the top level, Longshot the dragon's head on the north side and take the door [[gf1-240-1]].
6. In the Blade Trap Room, climb the steep hill in the Kokiri Boots without stopping. If you get hit or stop, you slide back to the start. Go through the Boss Door [[gf1-240-2]].

A commenter notes the hardest part is reaching the boss room without touching traps [[ra-comments-135-8]]. It is unconfirmed whether damage before the boss counts, so avoid it anyway.

#### Boss: Morpha

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Tentacles of the Deep requires beating Morpha with 3 hearts, without falling into the water and without taking any damage [[gf1-240-3, gf1-240-4, ra-comments-135-1, ra-comments-135-2]]. One hit from Morpha does four to six hearts of damage per the guide, which means an instant loss on 3 hearts [[gf1-520-5]].

Stay out of the water and off the four inner platforms [[gf1-240-3, gf1-520-6]]. Go to a corner of the room at the start; Morpha cannot reach you there [[ra-guide-14-2, ra-comments-135-2]]. It spins in the water before it makes an arm, which is your cue [[gf1-520-7]]. Your Longshot outranges its arms [[gf1-520-7]]. When the nucleus enters an arm, Longshot it and Jump Attack with the Biggoron's Sword or Hammer [[gf1-240-4, gf1-521-1]]. Longshot between slashes to stop it escaping [[gf3-68-2]]. One commenter got enough with one Biggoron's Sword swing per cycle [[ra-comments-135-5]]. Another says pulling it into the corner locks it in place for quick spin attacks [[ra-comments-135-1, ra-comments-135-6]]. Din's Fire instantly kills a tentacle, according to a commenter [[ra-comments-135-4]].

The hardest part is not picking up the Heart Container afterward [[ra-comments-135-7]]. Leave it, then enter the beam of light for Ruto's Water Medallion [[gf1-241-1, gf1-241-2]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave the Water Temple:** confirm you have not opened the Compass or Map chests and that the Heart Container is on the floor [[gf1-221-4, gf1-222-2, gf1-241-1]].

### Shadow Temple

Gather the three medallions before this temple; Forest, Fire and Water open the way [[gf1-422-4]]. The Bottom of the Well must also be done first [[gf1-19-1]]. Warp with the Nocturne of Shadow, run east down the stairs to the torch room, and use Din's Fire from the central pedestal to open the door [[gf1-258-2, gf4-29-1]]. The temple is a giant obstacle course of traps [[gf1-9-12]]. The Lens of Truth is fine to use in the dungeon; you must not use it on Bongo Bongo [[ra-comments-136-3]].

#### Raven Room, Catacombs and Hover Boots

1. Run forward, look right and Longshot across the long pit. Run through the carved face into the Raven Room [[gf1-260-1, gf1-260-2]]. With the Lens of Truth, find the skull pillar that does not vanish, and push the Raven statue until it faces that skull; the far grating opens [[gf1-260-3]]. Use the Lens on the wall opposite the pit to find the fake face on the right, and run through to the door [[gf1-260-4]].
2. In the Catacombs, fake walls have skulls with glowing blue eyes [[gf1-261-1]]. Go through the wall, right around the corner, and through the fake wall on the right to the Redead Room [[gf1-261-2]]. Killing the Redead and two Keese makes the Dungeon Map appear. **Do not take it** [[gf1-261-3]].
3. Back in the Catacombs, follow the route through the wall between two pots and the other fake walls to the Deadhand Room [[gf1-262-1]]. Let a hand grab you, struggle free, Z-Target the head and Jump Attack it before it strikes [[gf1-262-2]]. The chest holds the **Hover Boots** [[gf1-262-4]].
4. Return to the Raven Room, go through the fake wall to the cliff, equip the Hover Boots and run to the tongue-shaped ledge [[gf1-263-1]]. Bomb the Beamos, then use the right fake wall to the Gibdos Room [[gf1-263-2, gf1-263-3]]. Killing both Gibdos makes the Compass chest appear. **Leave it** [[gf1-263-4]].
5. Back through the fake wall and past the Beamos, go through the north fake wall to the Scythe Room [[gf1-264-1]]. Roll under the scythe blades if you time it, and collect all five Silver Rupees: two in the middle, one from the northwest Longshot target and wood stack, then the west and northeast alcoves [[gf1-264-2, gf1-264-3]]. The southeast chest holds a **Small Key** [[gf1-264-4]].

#### Guillotines to the Skull Room

1. Bomb the rough wall east of the Raven Room and run down the passage [[gf1-265-1]]. Longshot the Big Skulltulas on the ramps and run around or roll under each guillotine [[gf1-265-2, gf1-265-3]]. Kill the Wall Master fully, because knocking it off the cliff doesn't count [[gf1-265-4]]. After the third guillotine, fight the Stalfos with your shield up, attacking only right after it strikes [[gf1-265-5]]. Use the Lens to cross the invisible platforms east then north to the door [[gf1-265-6]]. Rapidly toggling the Lens avoids magic use [[gf1-266-1]].
2. Beyond the grating are two chests with a Blue Rupee and arrows; see the second with the Lens [[gf1-266-2]].
3. In the crushing ceilings room, use the Lens to find the hidden block, pull it out to align with the smiling face, then push it forward to block both ceilings [[gf1-267-2]]. Jump from the platform at mid-height to the narrow cliff, collect four Silver Rupees and bomb the Beamos for the fifth [[gf1-267-1]]. Pull the block back, climb to the north ledge, bridge across the ceiling to the south switch and open the northeast chest for a **Small Key** [[gf1-267-4]]. Jump the timed guillotine and moving platform to the next door [[gf1-267-5]].
4. In the Redead room, kill both Redeads, keeping the Lens on for invisible spikes. Collect the five Silver Rupees with the Lens and Longshot targets to open the west door [[gf1-268-1, gf1-268-2]].
5. In the Skull Room, kill the Keese and bomb the top of the skull for a **Small Key** [[gf1-269-1]]. Use the invisible ceiling Longshot target to reach the north platform of the Silver Rupee Room and the locked door [[gf1-269-3]].

#### Fan Corridor, Boat and the Boss Key

1. In the fan hallway, wait for the fan to stop before running past the Blade Traps in Kokiri Boots [[gf1-270-1]]. Use the Iron Boots to stabilize against the stronger fan [[gf1-270-3]]. Longshot across the pit with the Iron Boots on [[gf1-270-4]]. Hold Z with the shield up against the Silver Eye's fire [[gf1-270-5]]. At the last fan, switch to Hover Boots to be blown through the left fake wall [[gf1-270-6]].
2. Kill the Gibdos while dodging Blade Traps [[gf1-271-1]]. Bomb the southeast rubble and use the Lens to find the chest with a **Small Key** [[gf1-271-2]].
3. In the fog-river room, push the large block into the small trench near the ladder [[gf1-271-3]]. Stand on the Triforce symbol on the boat and play Zelda's Lullaby; you fight Stalfos waves as it sails west [[gf1-271-5, gf1-271-6]]. When it reaches the west side, jump off the south side immediately, because it sinks [[gf1-271-7]].
4. In the invisible maze, use the Lens to see the walls [[gf1-272-1]]. In the Crushing Walls Room, burn both wooden walls with Din's Fire [[gf1-272-2]]. Kill the east Redead and open the ornate chest for the **Boss Key** [[gf1-272-3]].
5. Go south through the maze to the invisible Floor Master. Use the Lens and Din's Fire on its three spawns. The chest holds a **Small Key** [[gf1-273-1, gf1-273-2, gf1-273-3]]. The west door has the Triple Skull Room and the east door leads on [[gf1-274-1, gf1-274-3]].
6. Shoot a Fire Arrow at a bomb flower around the tall pillar to make a bridge [[gf1-275-1]]. Equip the Hover Boots, use the Lens, and cross the invisible platforms to the Boss Door [[gf1-275-2]].

#### Before the fight

Farore's Wind helps here. Commenters recommend placing it near the boss room, in the invisible platform room, and carrying 40 or more arrows [[ra-comments-136-1, ra-comments-136-2, ra-comments-137-4]]. Nayru's Love lasts long, so some commenters like it [[ra-comments-137-10]]. Unequip the Lens of Truth and keep it out of your C buttons; one commenter thinks having it there blocked their first success [[ra-comments-136-18, ra-comments-40-13]]. Only activating it fails the achievement per one commenter, but carry it nowhere you might tap it [[ra-comments-40-1]]. Equip Biggoron's Sword and do a jump attack before dropping in, per a commenter [[ra-comments-136-19]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Invisible Stalker (no Lens of Truth, no healing, 3 hearts) can only be earned during this fight [[gf4-29-2, gf3-73-1, ra-comments-136-1, ra-comments-136-2]]. The ordinary guide tells you to use the Lens to find the eye; for this achievement do not [[gf4-29-2, ra-comments-136-3]]. Each of Bongo Bongo's attacks deals at least two hearts [[gf2-86-1]]. Do not heal.

#### Boss: Bongo Bongo

Drop into the middle pit; the fight is on a giant drum [[gf1-275-3]]. Wear the Kokiri Boots [[gf1-275-4]]. On Hover Boots, the guide says to target a hand then unequip; a commenter says they failed every attempt with Hover Boots, and another says not to use them because dodging is too slow [[ra-guide-15-2, ra-comments-40-5, ra-comments-137-20]]. Use regular boots.

1. Z-Target one hand, back up until both are visible, shoot it with an arrow, and switch targets to the other [[ra-comments-136-7]]. Taking the left hand first is easier because you get bounced less [[ra-comments-137-1]].
2. If the second hand readies a slap or chop, shoot it. If it readies a fist, dodge, then shoot as it regroups [[ra-comments-136-8]]. Stunned hands drop arrows or magic [[gf1-275-7, gf2-86-2]].
3. Once both are stunned, wait: Bongo Bongo charges [[ra-comments-137-15]]. As the hands line up, aim your bow straight between them at about Link's height and shoot where the invisible eye is [[ra-comments-136-9, ra-comments-137-18, ra-comments-136-20]].
4. When it falls, a charged spin attack released as the hands pass deals heavy damage [[ra-comments-137-16]]. Or use jump attacks, about five with the Biggoron's Sword [[ra-comments-137-2]]. Another commenter crouch-stabs without locking on [[ra-comments-136-21]]. Several commenters needed only two to three cycles [[ra-comments-136-16, ra-comments-41-5]].

Din's Fire makes you ungrabbable but doesn't pause Bongo Bongo, and one commenter says it didn't help [[ra-comments-40-6, ra-comments-40-7]]. If you die, a commenter advises restarting quickly and using the Farore's Wind return [[ra-comments-137-5, ra-comments-137-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:49020]

Leave the Heart Container and enter the beam of light to get the Shadow Medallion from Impa [[gf1-275-8, gf1-275-9]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave the Shadow Temple:** confirm the Map and Compass chests are unopened and the Heart Container is on the floor [[gf1-261-3, gf1-263-4, gf1-275-8]].

### Spirit Temple

You must have finished the Bottom of the Well and the Gerudo Fortress first [[gf1-19-2]]. The temple needs both Young and Adult Link, in two wings [[gf1-294-1, gf1-309-1]].

#### Child wing

1. Swap back to the Hylian Shield for the fire enemies [[gf1-295-1]]. At the top of the entrance stairs, go left. Nabooru is stuck at a small hole; tell her "Nothing, really." Then crawl through once she moves [[gf1-295-2, gf1-295-3, gf1-295-4]].
2. In the Armos Knight Room, drop a bomb by the Knight and hit it just before the blast, or use Bombchus. Use the Boomerang on the Keese, then take the door left of the crawlspace [[gf1-296-1, gf1-296-2, gf1-296-3]].
3. Fight the Stalfos, dodging and striking right after its attacks. Stun the Green Bubble with the Boomerang and finish it with the Slingshot. Aim the Boomerang left of the bridge to hit the crystal switch and drop the bridge [[gf1-297-1, gf1-297-2, gf1-297-3]].
4. Anubis dies only to fire: use Din's Fire. This unseals the right door [[gf1-298-1, gf1-298-2]]. Collect the five Silver Rupees to bring the bridge. Light the two torches with Din's Fire for a chest with a **Small Key** [[gf1-298-3, gf1-298-5]].
5. Use the crawlspace to the right of the Armos Knight Room door to the Small Hall [[gf1-299-1]]. Hit the rock with a Bombchu; sunlight opens the opposite door [[gf1-299-3]].
6. Push the statue off the cliff onto the button. Lighting the torches by the Colossus statue with Din's Fire spawns the Dungeon Map. **Do not open it** [[gf1-300-1, gf1-300-2]].
7. Up the West Stairwell, push the sun-panel block into the light on the northwest side to open the south door [[gf1-301-1, gf1-301-2]]. Collecting the Silver Rupees lights a torch. Use a Deku Stick to light the three other torches before any go out; the chest has a **Small Key** [[gf1-301-4, gf1-301-5]].
8. Hit the Iron Knuckle suit to start the mini-boss [[gf1-302-2]]. Nayru's Love makes it easy [[gf1-302-3, gf3-82-1]]. Once half-health, its armor breaks and it speeds up [[gf1-302-4]]. Back-flip, Jump Attack and back-flip [[gf3-110-8]]. Go through its door and tunnel to the Colossus arm [[gf1-302-5]].
9. The chest outside holds the **Silver Gauntlets** [[gf3-82-2, gf4-31-9]]. Exit and come back as an adult; the Requiem of Spirit learned after leaving warps you back [[gf3-112-1, gf2-153-11]].

#### Adult wing

1. Push the huge blue block with the Silver Gauntlets [[gf1-310-1]]. Bomb the Beamos and hit the ceiling crystal to open both side doors [[gf1-310-2]]. In the Rolling Boulder Room, use the Hover Boots for the suspended Silver Rupee. All five open the far door [[gf1-310-3, gf1-310-4]].
2. The Like-Like chest holds a **Small Key** [[gf1-311-1]]. The Triforce/Compass room's chest holds the Compass. **Do not open it** [[gf1-311-3]].
3. Go through the north locked door. In the mirror room, kill the invisible Floor Master with a Jump Attack and its offspring with Din's Fire [[gf1-312-1, gf1-312-2]]. Push the mirror to light the panels; the third unseals the west door [[gf1-312-3]].
4. In the statue room, play Zelda's Lullaby on the Triforce symbol on the left hand; the right-hand chest holds a **Small Key** [[gf1-313-1]]. Play the Scarecrow's Song at the top and Longshot across to Pierre [[gf1-313-2]]. Take the SE locked door [[gf1-313-4]].
5. The Treble Anubis Room: Fire Arrows kill the three Anubis. Bomb the north Beamos to open the SE door [[gf1-314-1, gf1-314-2]]. Shoot an Armos to step on the blue switch [[gf1-314-3]]. Fight the Iron Knuckle with backflip and jump attacks [[gf1-315-2]]. The outside chest holds the **Mirror Shield** [[gf1-316-1]].
6. Stand in the beam and aim the Mirror Shield at the wall sun panel; the Small Key Room's chest holds a **Small Key** [[gf1-317-3, gf1-317-4]]. Pass the sliding panels with the Longshot [[gf1-318-3, gf1-318-4]]. Play Zelda's Lullaby on the Triforce symbol atop the stairs [[gf1-319-1]]. Shoot the golden eye behind the false door, Longshot up, step on the switch, and open the ornate chest for the **Boss Key** [[gf1-319-2]].
7. In the Upper Sun Chamber, hit the crystal behind the bars. Bomb the rock, rotate mirrors east and north, then use the Mirror Shield to activate the sun panel [[gf1-320-2, gf1-320-3]]. Reflect the beam onto the statue's face and Longshot the grating to reach the Boss Door [[gf1-321-1]].
8. In Nabooru's Throne Room, fight the Iron Knuckle with backflips and Nayru's Love [[gf1-321-2]]. Koume and Kotake then capture Nabooru [[gf1-321-3]].

#### Boss: Twinrova

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Double Trouble allows only the Master Sword and Mirror Shield, no fairies, 3 hearts [[gf2-96-1, gf2-96-2, ra-comments-138-1]]. Nayru's Love is allowed [[ra-comments-138-2]]. Do not use the Hookshot, despite the generic guide advice. Swap the Biggoron's Sword for the Master Sword; it deals half damage and is effectively out [[ra-comments-138-4]]. Whether Hover Boots count is unconfirmed, so decide before the fight [[ra-comments-138-1]]. One commenter says red potions don't break it, but avoid healing anyway [[ra-comments-138-6]].

Kotake is Ice and Koume is Fire [[gf1-521-6]]. Phase one: do not Z-Target. Keep both in view, block one sister's beam and reflect it into the other. A miss makes a damaging ring (ice freezes) [[gf1-322-1, gf1-521-7]]. Z-targeting then releasing the lock lines you up [[gf3-265-2]]. Each sister must take two or three hits [[gf2-96-1]]. Hang to one side of the platform so you can side-hop away from unwanted spells [[ra-comments-138-5]].

Phase two: absorb three same-element spells in a row. Opposing ones cancel. Right wand is Ice, left is Fire; the shield flashes red or white [[gf1-522-1, gf1-522-2]]. Keep the shield drawn so the beam connects and she falls [[gf1-322-2, gf1-522-3]]. Then Jump Attack across the gap and use basic Master Sword strikes. Return to the main platform for the next cycle [[gf1-323-1]]. A one-cycle kill is still possible, but timing is tight [[ra-comments-138-3]]. Two knockdowns are needed with the Biggoron's Sword; without it, expect more cycles. The exact count is unverified from collected sources [[gf2-96-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:49262]

Leave the Heart Container [[gf1-323-2]]. Enter the opposite beam of light for the Spirit Medallion [[gf1-323-3]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave:** the Map and Compass chests stay closed, and the Heart Container stays on the floor [[gf1-300-2, gf1-311-3, gf1-323-2]].
