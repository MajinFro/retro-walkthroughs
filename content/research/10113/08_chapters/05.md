[MERMAID]

Dodongo's Cavern is the second dungeon. It opens when you throw a Bomb Flower at the sealed entrance, and it ends with King Dodongo and the Goron's Ruby. The dungeon's "Big Puzzle" is climbing to the catwalk above the giant Dodongo skull and forcing its mouth open to reach the boss [[gf1-9-7, gf1-68-1]]. Bombs, the Bomb Bag and the Bomb Flowers are the dungeon's main tools [[gf1-383-8]]. Two achievements belong here: **Bombalicious** (missable, a boss challenge) and **Soul Brothers** (story progression). You also collect the Map, the Compass, the Bomb Bag, a Heart Container and four Gold Skulltulas.

### Preparation

Darunia gives you the Goron's Bracelet in Goron City. You need it to pick up and throw Bomb Flowers, and nearly every puzzle here depends on that [[gf1-63-3, checklist-sidequests-9-25]].

Equip the Hylian Shield if you have it. Keese that fly through a torch flame catch fire, and the Hylian Shield blocks them and douses the flame. A Deku Shield is destroyed by them [[gf1-69-2]].

Bring a few Deku Sticks. You need a lit one to open a door later, and a Business Scrub inside the dungeon sells them for 15 rupees each [[gf1-71-2, gf1-71-3]].

> [!TIP]
> **Bomb Flowers vs. bag bombs:** Your Bomb Bag arrives mid-dungeon [[gf1-78-2]]. For the missable achievement, you must beat King Dodongo without using bombs from that bag. The fight takes place in a room with its own Bomb Flowers [[ra-guide-7-2, ra-comments-14-22]].

### Dodongo's Cavern

#### Entrance (Death Mountain Trail)

Stand at the sealed entrance and grab one of the Bomb Flowers to your right. Throw it near the cracked wall. The explosion destroys the wall and opens the cavern [[gf1-68-1]]. A second guide says to throw the bomb from a Bomb Flower at the sturdy door [[gf2-25-1]].

#### 1F: Antechamber and Main Chamber

Run into the large antechamber along the wooden flooring and jump out to the island in the middle. Dodge the Beamos and head to the right side of the room [[gf1-68-1]]. Grab the Bomb Flower in front of the large window. Z-target the Beamos beside it, run at it and throw the flower so it hits the Beamos on the head. The Beamos explodes [[gf1-68-2, gf4-10-1]]. Grab another Bomb Flower and blow open the sealed wall to the right of the Beamos, then go through the dark tunnel [[gf1-68-3]].

A second route description says to blast the door on the platform's right. The one on the left only reveals a Gossip Stone [[gf2-25-1]]. Unable to verify from collected sources whether the Gossip Stone matters beyond a free fairy when you play Zelda's Lullaby near it [[gf3-93-7]].

#### 1F: Lower Southeast Hall (Skulltula #38)

Run forward and kill the Baby Dodongos that burst from the ground. They explode when they die, so back away fast [[gf1-69-1, gf1-170-1]]. Further along, another Baby Dodongo appears next to a sealed wall. Stand next to the wall and kill the Baby Dodongo as it passes the wall, so its explosion destroys the barrier. If you botch it, leave the dungeon and re-enter to reset the Baby Dodongos [[gf1-69-1]]. A bombable wall on the left, before the ground becomes elevated, is the same wall. You can also blow it up with a Bomb Flower [[checklist-sidequests-10-1, checklist-skulltulas-8-1]].

Go through the door behind the wall into the Torch Room. Kill the Keese on the opposite wall. If they fly through the torch flame they catch fire, so use the Hylian Shield to absorb their attacks and douse them [[gf1-69-2, gf3-23-2]]. Kill the Gold Skulltula on the wall and take the token. You may need a backflip to reach it. This is **Skulltula #38** [[gf1-69-3, checklist-skulltulas-8-1, checklist-sidequests-3-17]]. Return to the Lower Southeast Hall.

Turn right and kill the respawned Baby Dodongos. Climb the ledge and run to the far end. Grab an Armos Statue and push it onto the gray switch to unlock the door. Climb the ledge and enter the door [[gf1-70-1]]. One guide notes the statues can be pushed onto a blue switch that does not stay pressed on its own, which is why you must hold it down with a statue [[gf2-25-3]].

#### 1F: Southeast Passage and Lizalfos Nest

In the Southeast Passage, Z-target the Keese on either side and kill them with the Slingshot. Break the pots and take their contents. Go through the door at the far end [[gf1-70-2]].

The lower Lizalfos Nest holds two Lizalfos. Don't chase them. Wait for them to come to you. Block each attack with the shield, then counter with the sword [[gf1-70-3]]. A jump attack while Z-targeting is also effective [[gf2-25-4]]. Holding the shield up until one attacks, then jump attacking twice with the Kokiri Sword, will make it flee [[checklist-sidequests-7-2]]. A Deku Nut stun followed by one Deku Stick jump attack also sends one running [[checklist-sidequests-7-1]]. When both are dead, the west door unlocks [[gf1-70-4]].

#### 1F: Dodongo Breeding Ground

Run to the end of the passage. A sealed wall on the right hides a room with a Business Scrub who sells Deku Sticks for 15 rupees each. Only visit him if you need sticks [[gf1-71-3, gf2-26-1]]. Another guide says to use a Bomb Flower on that wall [[gf3-23-3]].

In the long room, kill the pair of Dodongos by running behind them and hitting their tail. They explode when they die, so stay away afterward [[gf1-71-1]]. Return to the entrance of the room and light a Deku Stick on the torch. Use the flaming stick to light the three empty sconces along the room, each near where a Dodongo stood. When all three are lit, the door at the west end opens [[gf1-71-2, gf2-26-2]].

#### 1F: Back to the Antechamber (Dungeon Map)

You emerge inside the "window" in the Antechamber. Run forward and step on the switch to open the door on the far side. Then drop out of the window. If you miss the switch, you must go back around and hit it again [[gf1-72-1]].

Run across the platforms and cliffs to the west side of the room. Before using the unlocked door, grab the Bomb Flower near it and blow open the sealed wall to its left. Open the chest for the **Dungeon Map** (Map 1 of 2 for this dungeon) [[gf1-72-2, gf2-26-3]]. Another guide places this chest on the west side of the room [[gf3-23-1]]. Then take the door you unlocked in the northwest corner [[gf1-72-3]].

#### 1F: Bomb Flower Room (Compass, Skulltula #39)

The room is crowded with Bomb Flowers around an iron pillar [[gf1-73-1]]. First go to the south end and use a Bomb Flower against the oddly colored wall. Go through the opening and the door beyond [[gf1-73-1]].

In the Armos Trap Room, three Armos statues surround a chest. The one in front comes alive when touched. Grab a Bomb Flower and drop it near the Armos Knight. Don't throw it. Just before it explodes, strike the Armos with your sword to wake it. The explosion kills the Knight, which then spins and flashes and explodes after a few seconds, so stay clear [[gf1-73-2, gf2-26-4]]. Open the chest for the **Compass** (Compass 1 of 1) [[gf1-73-3]]. Return to the Bomb Flower Room.

Now lower the iron pillar. Grab the Bomb Flower to your right as you leave the door and throw it directly between the two long lines of Bomb Flowers. They all ignite at once and the pillar drops to form stairs. If your throw isn't in the exact center, it won't ignite properly [[gf1-74-1]]. Another guide describes the same puzzle: take the lone flower and set its bomb in the gap in the ring [[gf2-26-5, gf4-10-5]].

Climb the stairs and go left or right around the path to the top of the room. There is a Skulltula on the ivy plus a Gold Skulltula above it. Kill the normal Skulltula with a jump attack, then shoot the Gold Skulltula with the Slingshot. Climb the ivy and take the token. This is **Skulltula #39** [[gf1-74-2, gf2-26-6, checklist-skulltulas-8-2, checklist-sidequests-3-18]]. Go through the door by the ivy.

#### 2F: Pillar Room

A pair of Fire Keese fly around this room. Circle the room and take them out with the Slingshot, or block them with the shield [[gf1-75-1]]. Go to the south side of the pillar. An Armos Statue covers a ladder. Pull it away from the ladder, and don't touch any other statues. They are Armos Knights in disguise [[gf1-75-4]]. Another guide says to place a bomb first, as you did before, and kill the three disguised statues [[gf2-27-1]]. Climb the ladder, step on the pillar switch to unlock the east door, and go east [[gf1-75-2]].

You are now high on the rear catwalk of the Antechamber. Run along it, past or through the Fire Keese, leap the gap in the catwalk and run through the tunnel [[gf1-75-3]].

#### 2F: Blade Trap Room (Red Rupee)

This room has pillars and blade traps. Run between the pillars to the rear, where a stone block sits beneath a ladder. Climb the block, then the ladder. Stand on the narrow ledge, carefully turn to face the small platform and jump to it. You will just barely make it. Open the chest for a **Red Rupee** [[gf1-76-1]]. Another guide places the chest on a pillar with a Bomb Flower [[gf2-27-1]].

Next, grab the Bomb Flower and hold it until it is almost ready to explode. Throw it at the wall above the ladder to blast the seal open, jump to the opened tunnel and run through it [[gf1-76-2]]. Timing matters: wait until the flashing is extremely fast, then wait about one more second before throwing [[gf2-27-2]].

#### 2F: Solo Eye Room and Upper Lizalfos Nest

Shoot the golden eye above the circle of flames with the Slingshot. The ring of fire disappears. Jump across the platform and through the tunnel. Baby Dodongos unearth themselves along the way. Go through the door at the end of the hall [[gf1-77-1]].

This is the upper level of the Lizalfos Nest. Fight two more Lizalfos the same way as before: wait for them, block with the shield, and answer with jump attacks. When both are dead the west door opens [[gf1-77-2]].

#### 2F: Double Eye Room and the Bomb Bag

Shoot the eye on the high wall above the flames. The first ring disappears. Jump to the platform, look left, and shoot the second golden eye in the alcove. The second ring disappears and you can jump the rest of the way. Run through the dark tunnel [[gf1-78-1, gf4-10-7]].

You come out in the upper area of the Blade Trap Room. Jump the gap, turn right and see a chest on an upper ledge. Jump up and open it for the **Bomb Bag** with 20 Bombs. Turn around and take the tunnel to the right [[gf1-78-2, gf2-27-4, gf1-409-2, gf3-157-1]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** From now on, the Bomb Bag is yours. **Do not use bombs from the bag in the King Dodongo fight.** Bombalicious (48277) requires you to win before he breathes fire without using bombs from your bag, and it is missable [[gf1-82-4, ra-guide-7-2, ra-comments-14-22]]. Spend the bag bombs on the walls and skull eyes below, not on the boss.

#### 2F: Antechamber, Skull Eyes and the Mouth

You are back in the Antechamber on the upper level near the front. Step on the switch directly ahead. A floating pillar rises to give you a way back up should you fall [[gf1-79-1]].

Run right and across the catwalk. Jump the first gap so you stand between the two gaps. Using bombs from your new bag, drop bombs through each gap so they land in the skull's eye sockets. Press the C button assigned to Bombs twice to drop a bomb. Blasts in both sockets make the skull open its mouth [[gf1-79-2, gf2-27-6, gf3-24-3, gf4-10-10]].

Before dropping down, run to the end of the catwalk and blow open the sealed wall. Open the chest for a replacement **Deku Shield**, or a **Blue Rupee** if you still have yours [[gf1-79-3, gf2-27-5, gf3-24-4]]. Then drop to the central platform and enter the Dodongo's mouth. Go through the door inside [[gf1-79-4]].

#### 1F: Back Room, Goron Graveyard and Skulltula #40

Drop into the large pit and kill the Fire Keese. Climb the ledge to the east and run through the dark tunnel [[gf1-80-1, gf1-80-2]]. Follow the tunnel as it curves left, then jump into the pit. Two Fire Keese swoop in. Go left around the barrier, then right on its other side. Run to the far wall and jump up over the small ledge. Go down into the next pit. A sealed wall is here. Climb up next to it, drop a bomb beside it and go through the door [[gf1-80-3, gf1-80-4]].

This room has a Gold Skulltula behind an Armos Knight statue. Shoot the Gold Skulltula with the Slingshot, then carefully step forward to grab the token without touching the Knight. This is **Skulltula #40** [[gf1-81-1, checklist-skulltulas-8-3, checklist-sidequests-3-19]]. If you do brush the Knight, defeat it with bombs [[gf1-81-2]]. Another guide says to blow open the wall at the end of the passage after entering the skull and turning right, which agrees with this room [[checklist-skulltulas-8-3, gf1-486-1]].

Return to the previous room. Drop from the doorway and climb the ledge to the left. The Fire Keese have respawned. Climb the brown block, then the next ledge, run to the end and jump into the pit. Break the pots for recovery items and a Fairy. Put the Fairy in a bottle if you have a spare. Then enter the dark tunnel [[gf1-81-3]].

#### Back Room Puzzle and the Pit

Run to the end of the tunnel to a brown stone block on a track. Push it off the edge to the floor below. Kill the Fire Keese, pull the block away from the wall, move around to its other side and push it into the opening in the floor's center. The western door unlocks. Go through it [[gf1-82-1]].

Open the chest on the far end of the room for **Bombs**. Then drop a Bomb onto the iron plate in the middle of the room. After the explosion the plate disintegrates and you can drop through the pit into the boss room [[gf1-82-2, gf1-82-3]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before the boss door:** Make sure you are healthy and carry Deku Sticks and the Slingshot. Once you drop into the pit the fight begins. If you fail, reset the fight. Sources disagree on whether you must reset the game or can just die and walk back in [[ra-comments-14-4, ra-comments-14-14]]. Whatever you do, do not use a save state, as Hardcore Mode disables them.

### Boss: King Dodongo

Take your Slingshot, a Deku Stick and, if you have them, spare Deku Sticks. The fight has two phases. In phase one he lumbers around the room and breathes fire. In phase two he rolls into a ball and ricochets around the room. The cycle repeats [[gf1-82-4]].

**Standard method (all sources agree on the basics):** Wait for him to begin inhaling and throw a bomb into his open mouth. The bomb explodes in his stomach and stuns him for a few seconds. You get one sword swing, so make it a Jump Attack [[gf1-82-4, gf2-28-4]]. After the stun he rolls. Stand on the edge of the lava or in a corner to avoid the roll [[gf1-82-5]]. Three Jump Attacks, or six normal sword attacks, kill him [[gf1-82-6]]. One guide says four normal hits [[gf2-119-4]]. Another says faster results come from Deku Stick jump strikes and shield stabs once he is stunned [[gf3-25-3]].

**Safe spot:** A little 45-degree slope between the path and the central lava makes you immune to all his attacks while you stand on it [[gf2-28-3, gf2-119-1]]. The roll can also be dodged by standing on that slope [[gf2-119-3]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Bombalicious (48277) is missable. You must kill King Dodongo **before he breathes fire at all**, using only Bomb Flowers from the arena, not bombs from your Bomb Bag [[ra-guide-7-2, ra-comments-14-1, ra-comments-14-2, ra-comments-14-22]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48277]

**Bombalicious plan.** The RetroAchievements guide says: as soon as the fight starts, turn around, grab a Bomb Flower and run toward the boss to time your throw. Jump slash with the Deku Stick, then repeat once more and he dies. Don't worry about taking hits. If he is about to breathe fire, shoot him with the Slingshot to cancel it [[ra-guide-7-2]].

Other players give this advice, and it does not all agree:

- Several say not to rush the Bomb Flower in the corner at the start [[ra-comments-14-1, ra-comments-14-11]]. Instead, run around him so he rolls, grab a Bomb Flower while he rolls, and get to him quickly to throw it into his mouth [[ra-comments-14-2, ra-comments-14-13]]. Running to Dodongo at the start triggers a roll instead of fire [[ra-comments-14-18]]. One player said running to the corner flower at the start did not work in time for them [[ra-comments-15-6]].
- Running behind him and chasing him also worked: throw the flower while he is charging, then jump slash with a Deku Stick [[ra-comments-15-5, ra-comments-15-7]].
- Throw the flower as the flame graphic appears. Dodongo must have fire in his mouth before the bomb goes in, or the achievement won't trigger [[ra-comments-14-5, ra-comments-14-20]]. Do not throw it as he is about to spew flames, because that does not work [[ra-comments-14-21]].
- Throw one flower, then jump slash with a Deku Stick for double damage. Repeat once and he is done [[ra-comments-14-19, ra-comments-14-23, ra-comments-14-3]].
- Shooting him with the Slingshot cancels the fire-breath animation and gives you time to get a Bomb Flower. Players called this the best method alongside patience [[ra-comments-14-17, ra-comments-15-2, ra-comments-15-3]].
- Staying out of his front makes fire breath less likely, and he stops at the second wall hit after rolling [[ra-comments-15-8]]. He rolls until he hits a corner [[ra-comments-14-7]].
- The challenge is tracked during the fight [[ra-comments-14-12]]. One commenter reports the achievement didn't trigger until their bomb bag was full [[ra-comments-14-9]]. Another says to have 20 bombs and just use Deku Sticks and jump strikes [[ra-comments-14-10]]. Others say a full bag and a C-button assignment are not needed under current logic [[ra-comments-14-15, ra-comments-14-16]], and one player did it without a full bag after dying to reset [[ra-comments-15-1]]. Whether a full bag matters is unconfirmed, so keep the bag topped off if you can. Picking up the flowers is the hardest part for some players [[ra-comments-15-4]].

When he dies, he rolls into the lava and the lava solidifies [[gf2-28-5, gf3-25-4]]. Grab the **Heart Container** from the middle of the room (Heart Container 2 of 8) [[gf1-82-7, gf2-145-2]]. Then enter the ring of light to warp outside, where Darunia gives you the Goron's Ruby and makes you a Blood Brother [[gf1-82-8, gf3-25-5]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48278]

The Goron's Ruby is the second of the three Spiritual Stones [[gf1-421-2, ra-guide-7-3, gf3-160-2, gf2-158-10]].

### Optional: Skulltula #41 (after the boss)

Skulltula #41 is on the second floor near the staircase room. You don't need the stone to get it, but the guide says to take care of it after beating King Dodongo. Re-enter the dungeon and ride the fast-moving pillar up to the second floor. Make your way around to the staircase room, where the stairs are raised again. Climb up the left side of the staircase to reach the Skulltula [[checklist-skulltulas-8-4, gf3-37-12, gf4-14-8, checklist-sidequests-22-5]]. The Gold Skulltula sits in an alcove at the top of the vine on the left [[gf2-46-4, checklist-sidequests-23-1]]. This approach avoids setting off the Bomb Flower chain reaction a second time [[gf1-486-2]]. One guide reaches the alcove using the Boomerang, and the checklist says a vine climb is enough. Unable to verify from collected sources whether you will already own the Boomerang at this point.

On the way, a guide says to use the elevator in the main chamber: go to the middle platform in front of the giant skull, then use the moving platform on the right to reach the second floor and the tunnel opposite the plaque [[gf1-121-4]]. Dropping to the bottom of the Blade Trap Room, you can climb the southwest ladder and tunnel back to the Antechamber [[gf1-122-1, gf1-122-2, gf1-122-3]].

### Skulltula #42 (later)

Skulltula #42 needs adult Link with the Hookshot and the Scarecrow's Song. Do not attempt it now [[checklist-skulltulas-8-5]]. In the Baby Dodongo hall, walk in until Navi flutters up toward the ceiling, play the Scarecrow's Song, Hookshot to Pierre, and kill the Skulltula in the alcove [[gf1-487-1, gf3-42-4, gf1-170-2, checklist-sidequests-22-4, gf4-17-16]]. This is revisited in chapter 21.

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Dodongo's Cavern:** Make sure you have the Map, the Compass, the Bomb Bag and four Gold Skulltulas (#38 to #41). A commenter notes that the Map and Compass are two achievement items here and that one player missed items by not revisiting as adult [[ra-comments-123-12, ra-comments-121-4]]. Skulltula #42 waits for later [[checklist-skulltulas-8-5]]. A commenter places the Delicious Stone at the back right of the main room [[ra-comments-81-13]].
