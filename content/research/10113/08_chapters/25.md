[MERMAID]

This chapter covers the first three dungeons on a **second save file**, the 3-heart run. In Inside the Deku Tree, Dodongo's Cavern and Inside Jabu-Jabu's Belly you take every dungeon item the route needs. You never pick up a Heart Container, a Piece of Heart, a Map or a Compass. Two timed or no-damage boss achievements are earned here: **Dangerously Delicious** (King Dodongo) and **Race Against the Clock** (Barinade). The 3-heart run limits you to a maximum of 3 hearts, so heart upgrades are off the table [[ra-comments-1-1]]. The cumulative counters in the checkpoint belong to your first save file and do not change on this one.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Point of No Return:** Starting the 3-heart file is a commitment. If you get a heart, map or compass by accident, you have to start from the beginning [[ra-comments-141-7]]. At the start of the game you must choose between this run and the Cartographer achievement, which is also missable [[ra-comments-141-6]]. Create the new file only when you are ready to leave every Map, Compass and heart upgrade untouched.

### Preparation and Ground Rules

Replay the child route from the earlier chapters (Kokiri Forest through Hyrule Field and Death Mountain) on this new file. Pick up Bottles early so you have somewhere to keep fairies. Mido will not let you into the Deku Tree until you carry a Sword and a Shield [[gf1-21-3]]. The Kokiri Sword is in a chest behind the Kokiri Forest crawlspace [[gf2-133-1, gf2-159-2]].

Two sources disagree about whether a few Pieces of Heart break the 3-heart achievements. The RA guide says a Piece of Heart is okay as long as you never take the fourth [[ra-guide-21-1]]. One commenter says you may keep a few small parts as long as they do not complete a whole heart [[ra-comments-126-10]], while another says you may not upgrade hearts at all [[ra-comments-126-9]]. This question is **unconfirmed**. Pick up no Pieces of Heart and no Heart Containers on this file.

The same unconfirmed state applies to Deku Sticks and Dangerously Delicious. One commenter says the achievement triggered with two Deku Stick jump attacks [[ra-comments-125-5]], and another says it did not pop with Deku Sticks [[ra-comments-125-6]]. A third says a first hit with a stick failed to trigger it [[ra-comments-125-7]]. Use only the Bomb Flower and the Kokiri Sword against King Dodongo.

### Inside the Deku Tree

#### The Trunk (entry floor)

Kill the Deku Babas in the corners of the room. Hold the shield up with R while Z-targeting to absorb their attacks, and collect the Deku Sticks and Deku Nuts they drop [[gf1-26-2]]. Slash the bushes along the northeastern wall and climb the ivy wall in the north. At the top, run right along the ledge [[gf1-26-1]].

Partway along the ledge is a chest holding the Dungeon Map. **Do not open it** [[gf1-26-3]]. Keep going right and enter the door [[gf1-26-4]].

#### Scrub Room #1

Z-target the Deku Scrub and reflect his Deku Nut back into his face. He then runs around the room. Do not chase him; stand on his nest, and when he tries to return he walks into you [[gf1-27-1]]. He opens both doors and tells you to press forward on the joystick as you land to avoid fall damage [[gf1-27-2]]. Go through the northeast door [[gf1-27-3]].

#### Slingshot Room

Jump to the gray platform, but quickly hop to the next ledge because the platform starts to fall. Open the chest to get the **Fairy Slingshot** (with a Bullet Bag holding 30) [[gf1-27-4]]. Climb the ivy wall to the right and open the chest for a **Recovery Heart** [[gf1-27-5]]. Back at the floor, shoot down the ladder hanging above the entrance door with the Slingshot, climb it and go back through the door to Scrub Room #1 and the Trunk [[gf1-27-6]].

#### The Trunk (upper level)

Return along the ledge to the Map chest, which you leave closed. Shoot the three Skullwalltullas off the ivy wall with the Slingshot [[gf1-28-1]]. Climb the wall to the top, then go left to the highest level [[gf1-28-1]]. Ignore the Big Skulltulas that drop and keep going left to a door [[gf1-28-1]].

#### Compass Room

Step on the switch next to the lantern and the three platforms in the middle rise. The far chest holds the Compass; **do not open it** [[gf1-28-2]]. For a safe pickup, step on the switch again and use the platforms to reach the small northern alcove. Jump past the Big Skulltula and open that chest for a **Recovery Heart** [[gf1-28-3]]. The Gold Skulltula in the back of the alcove drops the game's first token. It is optional here, because the Skulltula counter belongs to your first file [[gf1-28-4]].

To leave, light a Deku Stick on the lit lantern and use it on the burned-out lantern. The door unlocks [[gf1-28-5]].

#### The Trunk (webbing drop)

Run right past the first Big Skulltula. Kill the second by Z-targeting it, waiting for it to spin around, and using a Jump Attack [[gf1-29-1]]. Jump off the edge and land on the webbing on the first floor to break through to the sub-basement [[gf1-29-2]].

#### Sub-Basement (the pool)

You land in a pool. Climb onto the southeast ledge and step on the switch to activate the lantern [[gf1-29-5]]. The chest behind the lantern holds a **Recovery Heart** [[gf1-29-5]]. Two Gold Skulltulas here, on the eastern grating and on the long ivy wall, are optional for this file [[gf1-29-3, gf1-29-4]].

Light a Deku Stick on the lantern and jump onto the shallow part of the pond, where Link can stand without swimming. Jump to the southwest ledge, run past the Deku Baba and burn the webs on the wall to reveal a door [[gf1-29-6]].

#### Scrub Room #2

Deflect the Scrub's Deku Nuts back with your Deku Shield; the Slingshot does not work on him. Stand on his nest when he runs. He says "23 is number 1", a hint for the last puzzle before the boss [[gf1-30-1]]. Shoot the Silver Eye above the west door with the Slingshot to unlock it [[gf1-30-2]].

#### Trap Room

Jump in the pool and swim to the button on the left wall, directly beneath the rotating spikes. Hold A to dive and touch it, and the water drops considerably [[gf1-30-3]]. Swim back to the entrance and jump onto the floating platform. Hold R to crouch as you pass under the spikes, then hop to the opposite ledge [[gf1-30-4]]. Kill the Big Skulltula, push the large stone block all the way to the right, climb onto it and jump to the door [[gf1-30-5]].

#### Torch Room and Small Nest

Kill the Deku Baba in the center. Light a Deku Stick on the lit torch and use it on the two unlit sconces to open the north door [[gf1-31-1]]. In the Small Nest, run circles in the center to drop the Gohma Larva and kill each with one Jump Attack. Their eye turns red just before they charge [[gf1-31-2]]. Light a Deku Stick on the northern torches, burn the southern webbing and crawl through the tunnel back to the Trunk [[gf1-31-3]].

#### The Trunk (final descent)

Push the stone block along its carved path off the cliff. Jump down, light a Deku Stick on the southeast torch, then climb back to the upper level [[gf1-32-1]]. Roll over the floor webbing with the lit stick to burn it, then drop to the lowest level [[gf1-32-2]].

Swim north to the shore where three Deku Scrubs attack. Hit them in the order middle, right, then left, which matches the "23 is number 1" hint [[gf1-32-7]]. A second source gives the order as 2, 3, 1 from left to right [[gf2-11-1]]. The door to the boss then unlocks [[gf1-32-7]].

#### Boss: Queen Gohma

Run to the center, press C-Up and look at the ceiling to start the fight [[gf1-32-3]]. Z-target Gohma immediately and shoot her in the eye with the Slingshot while it glows red. This stuns her, so follow with Jump Attacks [[gf1-32-3]]. After one or two attacks she climbs the ceiling and spawns Gohma Larva. If you shoot her eye before she spawns them she falls and stays stunned a long time, which gives you three or four more Jump Attacks [[gf1-32-4]]. Strike her eggs once each before they hatch [[gf2-11-3]]. The pattern repeats if you miss [[gf1-518-2]].

The Heart Container appears after the kill. **Leave it on the floor** [[gf1-32-5]]. Walk around it into the blue light, then listen to the Great Deku Tree, who gives you the Kokiri Emerald [[gf1-32-6]].

### Dodongo's Cavern

Dodongo's Cavern needs the Goron's Bracelet to pick up Bomb Flowers. Darunia gives it after you play Zelda's Lullaby to open his door and then Saria's Song for him [[checklist-sidequests-9-25, gf1-63-3]]. Outside the cave, grab a Bomb Flower and throw it at the cracked wall [[gf1-68-1]].

#### Antechamber

Run across the wooden flooring onto the island in the middle of the embers and dodge the Beamos. Grab the Bomb Flower in front of the window, Z-target the Beamos to its right and throw the flower at its head to destroy it [[gf1-68-2]]. Use another Bomb Flower on the sealed wall to the right of the Beamos and go through the dark tunnel [[gf1-68-3]].

#### Lower Southeast Hall

Kill the Baby Dodongos that burst from the ground and move away from their explosions [[gf1-170-1]]. At the sealed wall, stand beside it and kill a Baby Dodongo as it passes so the explosion opens the barrier [[gf1-69-1]].

The Torch Room beyond has three Keese. If they fly through the torch flame they catch fire and deal extra damage, so block with your shield [[gf1-69-2]]. A Gold Skulltula on the wall is optional here [[gf1-69-3]].

Back in the hall, push an Armos Statue onto the gray switch to unlock the door [[gf1-70-1]]. Shoot the Keese in the Southeast Passage with the Slingshot [[gf1-70-2]].

#### Lizalfos Nest (lower)

Block each Lizalfos attack with the shield and counter with the sword [[gf1-70-3]]. After two Kokiri Sword jump attacks, the one you are fighting runs away [[checklist-sidequests-7-2]]. A Deku Nut stun followed by a Jump Attack also sends one running [[checklist-sidequests-7-1]]. Kill both and the west door unlocks [[gf1-70-4]].

#### Dodongo Breeding Ground

Run behind the pair of Dodongos and hit their tails; they explode when defeated [[gf1-71-1]]. Light a Deku Stick on the torch and light the three empty sconces. The door at the west end then opens [[gf1-71-2]].

A sealed wall in the passage leads to a Scrub who sells Deku Sticks for fifteen rupees [[gf1-71-3]].

#### Antechamber (the window)

Step on the switch inside the window to open the far door [[gf1-72-1]]. Drop out of the window. A sealed wall near the unlocked door hides a Dungeon Map chest, which you **do not open** [[gf1-72-2]]. Take the door in the northwest corner [[gf1-72-3]].

#### Bomb Flower Room

An oddly colored wall at the south end is sealed. Break it with a Bomb Flower [[gf1-73-1]]. The room behind it holds only the Compass chest, guarded by an Armos Knight, and you skip it [[gf1-73-3]].

Throw a Bomb Flower exactly between the two long lines of Bomb Flowers around the Iron Pillar. They ignite together and lower the pillar into stairs [[gf1-74-1]]. Climb the stairs and follow the path to the ivy. A Gold Skulltula token there is optional [[gf1-74-2]].

#### Pillar Room

Shoot the pair of Fire Keese with the Slingshot, or use the shield [[gf1-75-1]]. Pull the Armos Statue off the ladder on the south side without touching the other statues, because they are Armos Knights in disguise [[gf1-75-4]]. Climb the ladder and step on the switch to unlock the east door [[gf1-75-2]]. From the upper catwalk leap the gap and run through the tunnel [[gf1-75-3]].

#### Blade Trap Room

Climb the stone block and ladder, then jump to the small platform for a chest with a red rupee. Rupees are safe to take [[gf1-76-1]]. Hold a Bomb Flower until it is nearly exploding and throw it at the wall above the ladder to open the tunnel [[gf1-76-2]].

#### Solo Eye, Lizalfos Nest (upper), Double Eye

Shoot the golden eye with the Slingshot to extinguish the ring of fire [[gf1-77-1]]. Defeat the two Lizalfos in the upper nest the same way as before to open the west door [[gf1-77-2]]. In the Double Eye Room, shoot the high eye, jump to the platform, then shoot the second eye in the alcove on your left [[gf1-78-1]].

In the upper Blade Trap Room, jump the gap, turn right, jump up and open the chest. It holds the **Bomb Bag** with 20 bombs, which you want [[gf1-78-2]].

#### Antechamber (upper level)

Step on the switch to raise a pillar [[gf1-79-1]]. Cross the catwalk to between the two gaps. Drop bombs through each gap into the skull's eye sockets, and the mouth opens [[gf1-79-2]]. A sealed wall at the catwalk's end hides a chest with a Deku Shield or blue rupee, which is safe to take [[gf1-79-3]]. Drop to the central platform and enter the mouth [[gf1-79-4]].

#### Back Room and Goron Graveyard

Kill the Fire Keese and climb east through the tunnel [[gf1-80-1, gf1-80-2]]. In the Graveyard pit, watch for two swooping Fire Keese. Bomb the sealed wall there to open the door [[gf1-80-3, gf1-80-4]]. The Armos Knight room beyond holds one optional token behind a statue [[gf1-81-1]]. Pots in the pit after it contain recovery items and a Fairy to bottle [[gf1-81-3]].

#### Backroom and The Pit

Push the brown stone block into the opening in the floor's center to unlock the western door [[gf1-82-1]]. In the pit room, open the chest for Bombs [[gf1-82-2]]. Drop a bomb on the iron plate to dissolve it and drop to the boss [[gf1-82-3]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Dangerously Delicious is missable on this file. The block puzzle before the boss must be redone every time you retry the fight [[ra-comments-126-1]]. The checks are: no Bombs from your bag, no damage and 3 hearts only [[gf1-82-4, ra-comments-125-1]]. Do not open the bag; enter the boss room with the Kokiri Sword equipped and make no hit with Deku Sticks [[ra-comments-125-7]].

#### Boss: King Dodongo (Dangerously Delicious)

King Dodongo lumbers in a circle and inhales before his fire breath. A bomb in his mouth stuns him for one sword swing, and the second phase has him roll around the room [[gf1-82-4, gf1-82-5]]. Standing on the edge of the lava or in a corner avoids the roll [[gf1-82-5]]. The 45 degree slope between the path and the central lava makes you immune to his attacks [[gf2-28-3]]. Three Jump Attacks or six normal sword hits kill him [[gf1-82-6]].

For this achievement the bomb must be a Bomb Flower, not one from your bag. Community routing:

1. Do not run to the Bomb Flower at the start. The key is making the boss roll [[ra-comments-125-1]].
2. Run behind him at the start so he rolls around the arena [[ra-comments-125-2]]. Running into him while he is not rolling does not hurt, so run closer to him than the lava [[ra-comments-126-5]]. You can also roll under his left side without damage [[ra-comments-126-6]].
3. After you pass him he starts rolling. Then run toward him carrying a Bomb Flower in front of him [[ra-comments-126-7]].
4. Step near the lava to dodge his roll, then run at him again [[ra-comments-126-8]]. Standing nearly beside him by the lava lets him roll right past you [[ra-comments-125-3]].
5. Wait for him to come around, put the Bomb Flower in his mouth, then run in and attack [[ra-comments-125-4]]. The hardest part is getting that first bomb in quickly enough [[ra-comments-126-12]].

Common mistakes are grabbing the bomb too early or not throwing it into his mouth [[ra-comments-125-13]]. A commenter reports the Slingshot can make him cancel his fire breath and move closer [[ra-comments-126-14]]. Whether a cancelled breath satisfies the achievement is **unconfirmed**. If you fail you must reset and redo the puzzle, and dying does not unlock it [[ra-comments-125-8]]. The achievement now checks for the victory music [[ra-comments-125-10]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48918]

Do not touch the Heart Container he leaves behind [[gf1-82-7, gf2-28-5]]. Enter the ring of light and Darunia gives you the Goron's Ruby [[gf1-82-8]].

### Inside Jabu-Jabu's Belly

Empty a fish from a bottle in front of Lord Jabu-Jabu and he sucks you in [[checklist-sidequests-12-8, gf3-171-2]].

#### Entrance and Elevator Room

Two Octoroks appear on the left and right. Kill them with the Slingshot, then use Deku Nuts on the Shaboms [[gf1-105-2]]. Shoot the large yellow bump on the ceiling at the north end to unseal the door [[gf1-105-3]]. A Biri in the Elevator Room can be stunned with a Deku Nut [[gf1-106-1]].

#### Pit Room and Water Room

In the Pit Room, Ruto runs off and falls down a pit. Follow her, talk to her twice and carry her out the east door [[gf1-106-3, gf1-106-2]]. Do not step on the middle switch while Ruto is in the pit; the water rises and she returns to the Pit Room [[gf1-107-1]].

Use Deku Nuts to make Stingers jump and kill them with the Slingshot [[gf1-107-2]]. A Gold Skulltula on the west ivy wall is optional [[gf1-107-3]]. Pots in the alcove hold a Fairy and Seeds [[gf1-107-4]]. Shoot the ceiling switch in the north hall to unlock its door [[gf1-107-5]].

#### Elevator Room and Crawling Hallway

Ride the elevator up while carrying Ruto [[gf1-108-1]]. Cross the Pit Room avoiding Biri and floor pits [[gf1-108-3]]. In the Crawling Hallway, step on the yellow switch while carrying Ruto and go through the door [[gf1-109-1]].

#### Stinger Room

Kill all the Stingers with Deku Nuts and the Slingshot. A chest appears with the **Boomerang**, which you take [[gf1-109-2]].

#### Orange Tentacle Room (skip the Map)

Place Ruto on the blue switch at the hallway's end by stepping down and pressing A. Go through the door [[gf1-110-1]]. Strike the narrow segment of the Orange Tentacle four times with the Boomerang. This removes the other orange tentacles [[gf1-110-2]]. A chest with the Dungeon Map appears; **leave it closed** [[gf1-110-3]].

#### Hallway rooms

Ruto waits beyond the door and you pick her up [[gf1-111-1]]. The left door leads to the Shabom Room, whose chest holds the Compass and which you skip [[gf1-111-2, gf1-111-3]]. Take the route to the Blue Tentacle Room and hit it four times with the Boomerang while dodging [[gf1-111-4, gf1-112-1]]. For the Green Tentacle Room, go right down the hall, take another right and kill the Biri first, then the tentacle [[gf1-112-2, gf1-112-3]].

#### Pit Room (lower drop) and Bigocto

Run straight across the hallway to the Pit Room [[gf1-113-1]]. Drop down the pit closest to the west wall [[gf1-113-2]]. Two optional Gold Skulltulas hang on the north wall [[gf1-113-3]].

Bigocto's weak point is the green eye on his backside. Stun him with the Boomerang and use Jump Attacks; two kill him [[gf1-113-4, gf1-113-5]]. Letting him run into you turns him around, which is quicker [[gf1-516-3]]. One of the pots behind you holds a fairy [[gf3-34-2]]. Step onto the middle platform to raise the elevator [[gf1-113-6]].

#### Pink Things, Elevator Room, Biri Room

Stun each Pink Thing with the Boomerang and hop across [[gf1-114-1]]. In the Elevator Room, set a box on the blue switch to keep it pressed [[gf1-114-2]]. In the Biri Room, kill the Biri, then climb the ivy wall on the southeast side [[gf1-115-1]]. At the top, hit the switch to the right with the Boomerang to unlock the north door [[gf1-115-2]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Race Against the Clock is missable on this file. You need a sub-1:51 kill at 3 hearts [[gf1-115-3, ra-comments-4-2]]. The timer starts when you enter the room and runs through both parts of the cutscene (about 1 minute 10 seconds) [[ra-comments-128-1, ra-comments-127-2]]. It stops a few seconds after the boss is beaten, not on the last hit [[ra-comments-128-1, ra-comments-130-7]].

#### Boss: Barinade (Race Against the Clock)

Prepare before the door. Set Z-targeting to Hold to make the fight easier [[ra-guide-9-1]]. Put the Boomerang, Deku Nuts and Deku Sticks on your C buttons [[ra-comments-131-7]]. Another commenter also brought Din's Fire [[ra-comments-128-5]], but another says Deku Nuts are faster than Din's [[ra-comments-130-2]]. Stand in the room with a Heart Container in mind: it spawns two steps ahead of the death point, and one player ran straight into it [[ra-comments-129-10]]. Remember that no pot tricks are required; pots around the room each hold a Recovery Heart [[gf2-43-1]].

To shorten the intro, face the boss, save the game and reset [[ra-comments-127-9, ra-comments-131-3]]. After that the intro is shorter and wastes less time [[ra-comments-127-9]]. A commenter also found that playing from a continue skips the opening [[ra-comments-131-16]]. Another reported that letting the boss kill you first makes the cutscene shorter [[ra-comments-130-11]].

1. **Phase 1:** roll forward twice and take a couple steps [[ra-guide-9-2]]. Target the three top tentacles with the Boomerang, circling Barinade and hitting each one [[ra-comments-130-1, ra-guide-9-2]]. Pick a direction and stick to it, because changing course gets you electrocuted [[gf1-518-5]]. Use Link's yelp to know when he has caught the Boomerang so you can target and throw again quickly [[ra-comments-131-8]].
2. **Phase 2:** let go of the target and immediately throw the Boomerang at the center [[ra-guide-9-2]]. Throwing without Z-target at center mass can detach the jellyfish quickly. Then kill them all in one hit with Deku Nuts [[ra-comments-130-2]]. Attack Barinade directly rather than the jellyfish [[ra-comments-128-2]]. Try to stand between the cracks in the jellyfish armor so they miss you [[ra-comments-131-9]].
3. **Phase 3:** throw the Boomerang again before the jellyfish hitboxes become active so they disconnect, then use one or two Deku Nuts [[ra-comments-130-3]]. Throw it as the boss rises out of the floor; the jellyfish bunch under him and one nut kills them [[ra-comments-131-12]].
4. **Final phase:** chase Barinade, hit him with the Boomerang, then jump-slash with a Deku Stick [[ra-comments-130-4]]. When he buries into the ground, roll into him and take the minimal damage so he surfaces sooner [[ra-comments-130-5, ra-comments-129-8]]. The stick breaks after the jump attack, so you can roll right away [[ra-comments-131-13]]. Repeat twice more to end the fight [[ra-comments-130-6]].

The guide says five Jump Attacks defeat Barinade [[gf1-115-5]]. One commenter says three Deku Stick jump attacks were enough [[ra-comments-130-14]]. Be ready for either count. Keep dodging the satellite dishes, since they fire the whole time [[gf1-519-3]].

Do **not** pick up the Heart Container [[gf1-115-6]]. Step into the ring of blue light. Ruto gives you the Zora Sapphire if you agree to marry her [[gf1-115-7]].
