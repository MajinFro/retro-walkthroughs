### Chapter Overview

This chapter covers the stretch between the Water Temple and the Shadow Temple. Walking into Kakariko Village after the Water Temple starts a cutscene, and Sheik then teaches you the Nocturne of Shadow [[gf1-244-1, gf4-28-6]]. You then learn the Song of Storms from the windmill as an adult [[gf1-136-2]]. Next you return to childhood and play that song in the windmill to drain the well, which opens the Bottom of the Well [[gf1-246-3, gf1-246-4]]. The Lens of Truth is waiting there [[gf1-390-1]].

The main targets are:

- **Seeing Is Believing** (the Lens of Truth, in the Bottom of the Well).
- **The Fabulous Froggish Tenors** (the Zora's River frog minigame).
- Three Gold Skulltulas in the Well.
- Gold Skulltula #20, from the tree by the castle moat.
- Gold Skulltula #37, in Death Mountain Crater.
- Three Pieces of Heart: #23 and #18 from the frogs, and #33 from the Market Treasure Chest Game.

Once you have done the Forest Temple you can return to childhood at any time, and that is what makes the Well available [[gf1-18-4]]. The Well can only be entered as Young Link, and it needs both the Ocarina of Time and the Song of Storms [[gf3-204-4, gf1-394-4]].

### Kakariko Village (Adult Link)

#### Nocturne of Shadow

When you enter Kakariko Village after clearing the Water Temple, a cutscene plays and the village is on fire. Watch it, and Sheik will teach you the Nocturne of Shadow [[gf3-70-4, checklist-sidequests-29-3]]. This song is the only way to reach the Shadow Temple [[gf1-244-1]].

The notes are C-Left, C-Right, C-Right, A, C-Left, C-Right, C-Down [[gf1-397-1]].

#### The windmill: Song of Storms

1. From Kakariko Graveyard, take a right up the long staircase.
2. Enter the door on the right at the top of the stairs, behind the crate [[gf1-121-1]].
3. If you skipped the Piece of Heart in the windmill as Young Link, you can still take it now from the top of the staircase [[gf1-136-1]].
4. Drop down to the main floor and stand in front of the Organ Grinder.
5. Take out the Ocarina of Time, and he teaches you the Song of Storms [[gf1-136-2]].

The song is A, C-Down, C-Up, A, C-Down, C-Up [[gf1-394-3, gf2-51-1]]. Besides opening the Well later, it is used for the frog heart piece and the castle Skulltula in this chapter.

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave Kakariko as an adult:** Impa's roof Skulltula (#27) should already be yours from Chapter 11. If you skipped it, Hookshot from the House of Skulltula roof onto Impa's house roof at night and kill the Gold Skulltula on the wall [[gf1-136-4, checklist-skulltulas-6-6]].

### Hyrule Castle Ruins (Adult Link)

Head back to the Market and the former castle grounds. The Market is full of Redeads, and they drop Magic Pots if you need to refill [[gf1-132-1]].

The Gold Skulltula on the back of the fallen outer gate (#21) was already collected in Chapter 11, so there is nothing to pick up here [[checklist-skulltulas-5-10, checklist-sidequests-5-31]].

### Temple of Time

The Temple of Time is to the right of the Happy Mask Shop [[gf1-128-4]]. Run into the Master Sword chamber and replace the Master Sword on its pedestal to return to childhood [[gf1-172-3, gf3-50-2]].

The Prelude of Light also warps you to the Temple of Time from anywhere, so you can switch ages quickly [[gf1-167-2, gf1-397-4]]. You will pull the Master Sword again later to become an adult [[gf1-175-4]].

### Hyrule Castle (Young Link)

🗺️ **Map:** [View Hyrule Palace Map by ThE_bLImP](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/52-hyrule-palace-map)

🗺️ **Map:** [View Path to Zelda Map by TLS64](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/55-path-to-zelda-map)

Do the castle errands now, while you are a child and have Bombs and the Boomerang. If you want Gold Skulltula #20 (the tree by the moat), you have to sneak through the Garden again.

#### Sneaking to the moat tree

1. From the Market, enter the Garden and climb the ivy wall at the gate.
2. At the top, run forward past the gossip stone to the top of the large marble arch.
3. Drop down on the other side of the arch. This puts you past the locked gate and the first two guards [[gf1-173-1]].
4. Run up the hill at the third graphical break, near its center, toward the cluster of flowers at the top.
5. Run straight at the flowers, then forward and slightly left to a wall with brick tiling, and climb it.
6. At the top, run forward past another gossip stone and jump off the cliff into the moat.
7. Swim to the end of the moat where it breaks left, climb onto the small patch of dirt, then climb up to the stone platform [[gf1-173-2]].

If a guard spots you at any point here, you are thrown back outside the gate. Redo the route from the start.

#### Gold Skulltula #20 (Song of Storms)

1. As you climb out of the moat, find the tree to your right at the corner of the fence.
2. Stand beneath it and play the Song of Storms. A pit opens.
3. Drop in. There are three bombable walls: one in front, one to the left, one to the right.
4. Bomb the wall on the left, slightly behind you from where you land, to reveal a Gold Skulltula [[gf1-173-3]].
5. Kill it and take the token with the Boomerang, since Young Link has no Hookshot [[gf1-459-2, gf3-50-3]].

To leave, run back the way you came and let a guard spot you. He throws you out of the gate [[gf1-173-4]].

#### Din's Fire (optional)

If you have not yet taken Din's Fire, climb the ivy on the right to the cliff above the gate and drop down past the marble gate. Run to the "Dead End" sign and bomb the boulder to reveal a tunnel, then crawl through to the Great Fairy's Fountain. Stand on the Triforce emblem and play Zelda's Lullaby [[gf1-90-1, gf1-90-2]].

### Kakariko Village (Young Link): Drain the Well

Get to Kakariko as a child. Go to the windmill and play the Song of Storms to the Organ Grinder, and the well outside drains [[gf1-246-3, gf3-50-4]]. If it does nothing, you are still Adult Link [[gf1-246-3]].

Once the well is drained, go outside and drop to its bottom. Go through the passage you find there [[gf1-246-4]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave the Well entrance:** you need the Boomerang for the Skulltulas, Bombs for several rocks and walls, and Deku Sticks for the torches [[gf1-252-1, gf1-255-4, gf1-253-2]]. Fewer Magic Meter problems will come up if you also carry a Hylian Shield, since Fire Keese burn Deku Shields [[gf1-254-3]].

### Bottom of the Well

This dungeon is easy, and gets easier once you have the Lens of Truth. The Lens sees through the large number of fake walls and floors, and the only other reason to stay is the three Gold Skulltulas [[gf1-248-1, checklist-sidequests-30-1]].

#### Entrance and Reservoir

1. Run forward from the entrance and crawl through the small tunnel [[gf1-248-2]].
2. Drop down the cliff on the other side and kill the Big Skulltula hanging from the ceiling [[gf1-249-1]].
3. At the apparent dead end with a skeleton, walk forward. You pass through the fake wall [[gf1-249-2, gf3-50-5]].
4. Stay in the shallow water that circles the room. No pits appear there [[gf1-249-3]].
5. If you fall down a pit, go to the southwest corner of the room beneath the Reservoir and climb the ladder. Collect the Silver Rupees around the cesspool at the bottom of the ladder, which opens the door back up [[gf1-249-4]].

#### Trench: drain the water

1. Jump across the pool in front of you into the narrow water-filled trench. Remember this pool; you return to it in a moment.
2. Follow the trench around two corners, watching for the giant green bubble that patrols it.
3. Halfway down the hall after the second corner, stand on the Triforce symbol painted on the trench floor and play Zelda's Lullaby. The water drains [[gf1-249-5, gf3-51-1]].
4. Return to the pool near the entrance, which is now an empty pit. Drop in and open the chest for Bombs, then go through the crawlspace [[gf1-249-6, gf3-51-2]].

#### Dead Hand room

1. Kill the Big Skulltula that comes down, climb the ivy wall, and go through the door at the far end [[gf1-250-1]].
2. Four giant hands stick out of the floor. Run near one and it grabs you, which summons Dead Hand [[gf1-250-2]].

**Dead Hand strategy**

Unable to verify from collected sources: Dead Hand's HP and elemental weaknesses.

- Struggle free by rotating the control stick and pressing buttons. Then Z-target Dead Hand's head [[gf1-250-2, gf1-517-3]].
- Stand still, out of the hands' reach, and wait for Dead Hand to approach. Its head lowers to attack [[gf1-250-2]].
- Press A for a Jump Attack as the head lowers. It then runs away and sinks into the ground [[gf1-517-3, gf2-127-1]].
- Let another hand grab you to lure it out again, and repeat until it dies [[gf1-250-2, gf3-244-2]].

When Dead Hand dies, a chest appears. Open it for the Lens of Truth [[gf1-250-3, gf3-51-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48951]

Seeing Is Believing unlocks when you obtain the Lens of Truth. One commenter says it can be earned the moment you become an adult [[ra-comments-95-1]]. The collected sources place the Lens in this dungeon for Young Link [[gf1-390-1, ra-guide-15-1, gf3-149-2]], so get it here.

Two more details about the Lens:

- It uses magic while active, but rapidly tapping the assigned C-button to toggle it on and off avoids using Magic Power [[gf1-250-4]].
- Use the Lens to look behind the Lens chest. An invisible chest there holds a Huge Rupee [[gf1-250-5, gf3-51-4]].

The Lens is required to beat the Shadow Temple boss and to reach the Spirit Temple entrance [[gf1-390-2]].

#### Trench: keys and Compass

1. Climb out of the pit with the ivy on the opposite wall and run into the drained trench. Run left toward the corner and use the Lens to see an opening on the right. Go through and open the chest for a Small Key [[gf1-251-1]].
2. Go back out and around the corner. Use the Lens to spot another opening on the right, leading to a room with a large pit. Watch the pit with the Lens, then open the Big Chest for the **Compass** [[gf1-251-2]].
3. Go back down the trench in the opposite direction, around the corner to the end of the hall, and take a left. Use the Lens to find an opening on the left wall and open the chest for another Small Key [[gf1-251-3]].
4. Further down the hall, use the Lens to see an opening on the left. Immediately inside there is a locked door on the right [[gf1-251-4]].

Other guides list additional chests and small keys in this area. Examples are a Small Key in a hidden chest behind a fake wall to the east, and a Small Key under a cracked dirt mound that you bomb [[gf3-51-10, gf3-104-6]]. Unable to verify from collected sources: the exact total number of Small Keys in the dungeon.

#### Locked rooms (Gold Skulltulas)

- **Right (east) locked room:** Kill all four Keese, then activate the Lens. A narrow ledge on the right goes around the center pit. Walk it to the Gold Skulltula, kill it, and take the token with the Boomerang [[gf1-252-1, checklist-skulltulas-16-3]].
- **Left (west) locked room:** Kill the Deku Baba and avoid the flying pots, which hurl themselves at you. Kill the Gold Skulltula on the wall and take the token with the Boomerang [[gf1-252-3, checklist-skulltulas-16-2]].

After the east room, reactivate the Lens in the small prison and go right to the other locked door, which leads to the west room [[gf1-252-2]]. Return to the Reservoir afterward.

#### Hall chest, Coffin Room and the Like Like room

1. Run down the hall directly across from the opening in the wall and drop down the cliff at the end. Open the chest at the bottom for a Recovery Heart, climb the ivy wall, and go through the door at the top [[gf1-253-1]].
2. **Coffin Room:** Kill the Gibdo on the right. Light a Deku Stick at the nearby torch, then run to the southwest corner and light the torch next to the coffin there. The coffin opens and reveals a Small Key [[gf1-253-2]]. Other coffins hold enemies; put the stick away after each torch [[gf3-51-7]].
3. Run to the northeast corner of the Reservoir area. Use the Lens to dodge the pit near the wall, then crawl through the crawlspace in the alcove [[gf1-254-1]].
4. Go right out of the crawlspace into the Tiny Room and through the locked door [[gf1-254-2]].
5. **Pit Room:** Use the Lens to see the pits. Kill the enemies without falling, using the Boomerang on the Fire Keese and a Bomb on the Beamos [[gf1-254-4]]. The chest in the corner holds a replacement Deku Shield if yours burns [[gf1-254-5]].
6. Go through the next door to the Like Like room. Kill the Like Like at close quarters, then kill the Gold Skulltula on the wall and take its token [[gf1-255-1, gf1-255-2, checklist-skulltulas-16-1]].

A Like Like can swallow your shield. Kill it quickly to get it back [[gf3-52-2]].

#### Cess Pool and Dungeon Map

1. Fall through one of the pits in the Pit Room to reach the Cess Pool [[gf1-255-3]].
2. Run south from where you land. Use the Bomb Flowers to blow up the rocks blocking your path [[gf1-255-4]].
3. Bomb the rocks in the torch-lit hall, run to the end, and kill the Redead. Open the chest for the **Dungeon Map** [[gf1-255-5, gf3-50-7]].

If a Redead freezes you, the Sun's Song can stun the Redeads [[gf3-105-1]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave the Well:** you should have the Lens of Truth, the Compass, the Dungeon Map and all three Gold Skulltulas (the Like Like room and both locked rooms). Some guides also list smaller chests with Deku Nuts, Bombchus and rupees, and these are optional [[gf2-80-3, gf3-51-5, gf3-52-3]]. Unable to verify from collected sources: the full list of small chests in the dungeon.

### Zora's River (Young Link): the Frogs

🗺️ **Map:** [View Zora's River/Domain Map by EverythingZelda](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/56-zoras-river-domain-map)

Go upstream to the Musical Frogs [[gf1-174-1]]. Walk out onto the log and stand on the white circle at its end before pulling out the Ocarina [[gf1-95-6]]. This has to be done as Young Link [[gf2-146-29]].

#### Piece of Heart #23

Play the Song of Storms. The frogs give you a Piece of Heart [[gf1-174-1, checklist-heartpieces-4-10, gf3-52-5]]. Also play any other songs they have not yet heard.

The songs to play for the frogs are Zelda's Lullaby, Epona's Song, the Sun's Song, Saria's Song, the Song of Storms and the Song of Time [[gf1-174-2]].

- Each new song earns rupees: 50 rupees each in one guide, Purple Rupees in others [[gf1-95-6, gf2-35-3]].
- Playing the Song of Time earns a reward as well [[gf1-124-3, gf2-78-5]].
- Another source says that playing five of the upper-row songs, excluding the Song of Storms, is what starts the minigame [[checklist-heartpieces-4-4]]. Playing all six covers either version.

#### The Frog minigame

After all the songs, the frogs challenge you to another minigame [[gf1-174-2]]. Stand on the log and take out the Ocarina [[gf1-174-2]].

Flies appear above the frogs' heads and you must press the button for that frog. The lowest frog is the A Button. The other four frogs, in a cross shape, match the C-buttons [[gf1-174-3]]. One guide lists the frog buttons from left to right as A, Left, Down, Up, Right [[gf2-112-3]]. Either way, simply follow the button sequence below.

The sequence is [[gf1-174-4, gf1-439-1, ra-comments-94-1, ra-comments-94-3]]:

**A, C-Left, C-Right, C-Down, C-Left, C-Right, C-Down, A, C-Down, A, C-Down, C-Right, C-Left, A**

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** You must play the sequence very fast or it fails [[gf3-52-6, ra-comments-94-6]]. Setting the C-buttons to the D-pad is reported to be easier than the analog stick, and even slight stick drift can stop notes from registering [[ra-comments-94-5, ra-comments-94-4]]. Unable to verify from collected sources: whether you can retry freely after a failure.

Finish it and you receive Piece of Heart #18 [[checklist-sidequests-2-34, checklist-sidequests-30-3, gf2-146-30, gf3-134-3]]. This is Piece of Heart #30 in another guide's numbering [[gf2-78-6, gf2-113-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48950]

Together, the song heart and the minigame heart give you the two Pieces of Heart from the frogs [[gf1-246-1, gf3-120-1]].

### Hyrule Market (Young Link): Treasure Chest Game

This minigame is only open at night. Play the Sun's Song to make it night if you have to, then head for the door in the southeast corner of the Market, to the left of the Bombchu Bowling Alley and near the entrance to the back alley [[gf1-257-1]]. One other guide places it in the southwest corner instead [[gf4-21-11]]. Look for the one lit building [[checklist-sidequests-8-6]].

1. Pay 10 rupees to play [[gf3-52-8, gf2-80-9]].
2. In each room there are two chests, one with a Small Key and one with a rupee booby prize. The key lets you progress [[gf1-428-2]].
3. Use the Lens of Truth to see inside each chest, and take the Small Key in each room [[gf1-257-2, checklist-heartpieces-5-6]].
4. Repeat for every room. In the last room a lone chest holds a Piece of Heart (#33) [[gf1-257-3, gf1-428-1]].

If you run short of rupees, break pots in the room at the entrance to the Market [[gf4-3-2]].

### Death Mountain Crater (Young Link): Gold Skulltula #37

The Bolero of Fire warps you to Death Mountain Crater even as Young Link [[gf1-395-4]]. Without the Goron Tunic your time is limited, and if time runs out you die. Fairies do not help [[gf1-175-1, gf1-435-2]]. Move fast.

1. Warp in. Look left of the warp point for a patch of soft earth.
2. Drop a Bottled Bug into it. Three bugs come out, so recapture one.
3. Kill the Gold Skulltula and take its token [[gf1-175-2, checklist-skulltulas-7-8]].
4. If you still have a Magic Bean, plant one in the patch [[gf1-175-2]]. When you return as an adult, the bean has grown into a platform [[gf1-176-1]].
5. Play the Prelude of Light to return to the Temple of Time [[gf1-175-3]].

Bugs are sold at the Kakariko Potion Shop for 50 rupees [[gf2-140-49]].

### Back to Adulthood

In the Temple of Time, take the Master Sword to become an adult again [[gf1-175-4, gf1-257-4]]. From here, the Nocturne of Shadow warps you to the outside of the Shadow Temple [[gf1-258-1]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave:** confirm you have the Song of Storms and Nocturne of Shadow, the Lens of Truth, the Compass and Map, the three Well Skulltulas, castle Skulltula #20, Crater Skulltula #37, and Pieces of Heart #23, #18 and #33.
