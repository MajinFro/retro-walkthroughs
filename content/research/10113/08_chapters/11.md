## Chapter 11: Seven Years Later: Hookshot, Epona and the Goron Tunic

[MERMAID]

This chapter starts the moment Link wakes as an adult. Opening the Door of Time and drawing the Master Sword sends Young Link seven years ahead as an Adult [[gf1-10-1, gf1-129-2]]. Rauru gives you the Light Medallion and sends you back to the Temple of Time, where Sheik meets you and advises a trip to Kakariko Village [[gf1-131-1, gf1-131-2]]. The guide also states that on first reaching adulthood you must obtain the Hookshot before anything else [[gf1-17-1]].

Three achievements are earned here: **Hook Me Away** (Hookshot), **The Great Escape** (ride Epona on Hyrule Field) and **Heat Resistant Tunic** (Goron Tunic). Along the way you collect two Pieces of Heart in Kakariko Village and the Graveyard, plus a handful of Gold Skulltulas.

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave the Lost Woods and Kokiri Forest for good:** a commenter reports the Goron Tunic can become completely unobtainable if you get the Longshot and beat the Fire Temple before getting it [[ra-comments-91-2]]. Another commenter says the achievement is story-related and not missable [[ra-comments-36-2]]. These comments conflict, so treat the tunic as urgent and take it in this chapter.

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Do not skip the Goron Tunic. One commenter confirms that getting the Longshot and beating the Fire Temple before the tunic makes it completely unobtainable [[ra-comments-91-2]]. Another commenter says it is story related and not missable [[ra-comments-36-2]]. This conflict is unconfirmed, so get the tunic now.

### Temple of Time

Drawing the Master Sword turns you into Adult Link, and Rauru gives you the Light Medallion [[gf1-131-1, gf1-421-4]]. Sheik meets you back at the Temple of Time and tells you to visit Kakariko Village before the Forest Temple [[gf1-131-2]]. Head to Hyrule Field and then to Kakariko Village.

### Kakariko Village

Entering Kakariko Village as an adult triggers a scene in which you learn the Nocturne of Shadow [[gf1-244-1]]. Another source places that scene after the Water Temple, so the song's timing is unconfirmed here [[gf4-28-6]].

#### Hookshot onto the roof (Piece of Heart #19)

Do this after you have the Hookshot. Jump onto the fence in front of the Windmill door, look right to the tall building with the blue roof, and Hookshot onto it when the red reticule shows [[gf1-136-3]]. Speak to the man gazing up at Death Mountain for a Piece of Heart [[gf1-136-3, checklist-heartpieces-4-5]].

#### Gold Skulltula #27 (night, Hookshot)

At night, stand in front of Impa's house door and Hookshot to the roof of the House of Skulltula, then Hookshot again onto the roof of Impa's house. The Skulltula is on the wall there [[checklist-skulltulas-6-6, gf1-463-3]]. Play the Sun's Song to make it night if needed [[gf1-136-4]].

### Kakariko Graveyard

🗺️ **Map:** [View Path to Zelda Map by TLS64](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/55-path-to-zelda-map)

#### Bean platform crate (Piece of Heart #20)

A Magic Leaf now stands where you planted a bean as Young Link on the left side of the Graveyard. Step on it to ride up to the high cliff, then roll into the crate for a Piece of Heart [[gf1-134-3, checklist-heartpieces-4-7]].

#### Dampe's race (Hookshot)

🗺️ **Map:** [View Dampe's Race Map by DrgNyt](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/7461-dampes-race-map)

Right next to the Magic Leaf is a gravestone with flowers. Grab it from behind and pull it away to reveal a pit, then drop in [[gf1-134-4, checklist-sidequests-20-2]]. Dampe's ghost challenges you to a race and starts without notice [[gf1-135-1]]. You only need to follow him, and when you reach the end he gives you the Hookshot [[checklist-sidequests-20-2, gf1-135-3]]. The route is: dodge flames, turn right at the end of the hall, take the next left wide, and so on through the course [[gf1-135-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48583]

After the race you exit into the top of the Windmill, after playing the Song of Time to move the blue block [[checklist-sidequests-20-2, gf1-135-4]].

### Lon Lon Ranch

Go to the pasture at the rear of the ranch and speak to Ingo. He offers a horse ride for ten rupees [[gf1-353-3]]. Ignore the horse he offers, play Epona's Song, and Epona runs to you [[gf1-133-1, gf1-353-4]]. Jumping both fences earns back the 10 rupees [[gf1-133-5]]. You need at least 50 rupees to race him [[checklist-sidequests-15-4]].

Speak with Ingo while riding Epona and accept the 50-rupee race [[gf1-133-2]]. Ingo takes corners wide. Cut the inside rail, burn a few carrots to pass, then block him [[gf1-353-5]]. After you win, he bets Epona on a rematch and cheats at the start. Burn carrots at the first turn to pass him again [[gf1-353-6]]. After the second win he locks the gate. Run counter-clockwise around the paddock and burn all your carrots at the last turn to jump the gate [[gf1-133-3, gf1-354-1]].

For clearing fences, commenters advise lining up dead center and boosting a beat or two before the fence, not right at it [[ra-comments-85-1, ra-comments-85-6]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** Some players report the achievement firing at the starting screen, or Epona not responding to Epona's Song [[ra-comments-25-1, ra-comments-25-3]]. If it does not unlock, the reporter asks for a save made just before the escape attempt [[ra-comments-25-4]]. Do not use save states in Hardcore.

### Goron City

Stop the small rolling Goron on the third floor with a well-timed bomb, then talk to him [[gf1-171-2, ra-comments-91-5]]. Ask about the Dragon and the Gorons, and he gives you the Goron Tunic and opens Darunia's Chamber [[gf1-171-3]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48912]

Equipping it removes the time limit in the Crater and the Fire Temple [[gf1-171-4]]. Do not buy the Giant's Knife from the big Goron here [[gf1-400-1]].

#### Gold Skulltula #35

Walk out on a rope in view of the pedestal where the Spiritual Stone of Fire rested and Hookshot the Skulltula on its back [[checklist-skulltulas-7-6, gf1-469-1]].

### Kokiri Forest

At night, a Skulltula is on the roof of the house with two pillars [[checklist-sidequests-24-1, gf2-59-2]]. This is Gold Skulltula #3 in the guide's list, on the back of the House of Twins, reached with the Hookshot [[checklist-skulltulas-3-3]].

### Lost Woods

🗺️ **Map:** [View Lost Woods Map by EverythingZelda](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/54-lost-woods-map)

At night, ride the bean plant on the Forest Stage screen up to the ledge and slash the Gold Skulltula [[checklist-sidequests-23-3, gf3-44-7]]. Mido may block the path; play Saria's Song for him [[checklist-sidequests-23-2]].

### Hyrule Castle

🗺️ **Map:** [View Hyrule Palace Map by ThE_bLImP](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/52-hyrule-palace-map)

On the far side of the ruined outer gate of the former castle grounds, Hookshot the Gold Skulltula [[checklist-skulltulas-5-10, checklist-sidequests-5-31]].
