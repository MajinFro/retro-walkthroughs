[MERMAID]

This chapter belongs to your **second save file**, the 3-heart run. Ganon's Castle and Ganon's Tower are the last two areas, and both missable 3-heart achievements finish here: **Dark Lord** (Ganon without being hit) and **Blind Explorer** (game completed with no Map or Compass and only 3 hearts) [[gf3-91-1, ra-comments-141-1, ra-comments-141-2]]. The six Light Arrow seals open the tower, the tower leads to Ganondorf, and the escape leads to Ganon [[gf1-327-1, gf1-340-1, gf1-344-2, gf1-347-4]]. The checkpoint counters at the end belong to your first file and do not change here.

### Before you start: rules for the 3-heart file

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning (Blind Explorer, 49293):** One Map, Compass or heart upgrade anywhere on this file ruins the achievement. The achievement checks that no Map or Compass is held, that you have the medallions, and that your total HP is 3 full hearts [[ra-comments-142-1, ra-comments-142-2, ra-comments-142-3]]. Commenters disagree on whether a reset or older-save reload undoes an accidental pickup [[ra-comments-141-8, ra-comments-143-3, ra-comments-144-7]]. That is unconfirmed, so prevent the pickup instead. Save with the game's own save before every chest you are unsure of [[ra-guide-21-2, ra-comments-145-5]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning (Dark Lord, 49292):** You must defeat Ganon without being hit while you still have only 3 hearts [[gf3-91-1]]. A commenter says this applies to the last phase of the Ganon fight, not the whole fight [[ra-comments-139-2]]. Failing means redoing the first fight and running away from the tower to retry [[ra-comments-140-11]].

**Open question (unconfirmed):** Do Pieces of Heart, fewer than four, void the 3-heart achievements? The RA guide says up to three are fine, but a commenter says no heart upgrades are allowed [[ra-guide-21-1, ra-comments-143-10]]. No source settles this, so pick up no Pieces of Heart and no Heart Containers on this file.

Preparation notes from the collected comments:
- Bring fairies in bottles [[ra-comments-145-7]].
- Bring at least one green potion for the Ganon fight [[ra-comments-140-13]].
- A commenter suggests 40 or more arrows and 2 or more green potions [[ra-comments-139-6]].
- Green potions refill magic for Light Arrows, and Deku Nuts can stun Ganon's face [[ra-comments-139-4]].
- The Light Arrows are given at the Temple of Time [[checklist-sidequests-35-2]]. Obtaining the Shadow and Spirit Medallions creates the road to Ganon's Castle [[gf1-423-3]].

### Ganon's Castle

#### Outside and entrance

Cross the bridge of light to the castle [[gf1-333-2]]. Run down the stairs when you enter. At the bottom, run right between the pair of Beamos statues and through the door into the Keep [[gf1-328-1]].

The Keep is a large hub with six hallways, one per Sage: Forest, Fire, Water, Shadow, Spirit and Light [[gf1-327-1]]. Each hallway ends in a seal you destroy with a Light Arrow. When all six are gone, the barrier around Ganon's Tower drops [[gf1-330-1]].

The Shadow barrier must be done first, because it must be finished before the Fire or Light barriers can be completed. After that the order is flexible [[checklist-sidequests-35-3]]. This route does Shadow first, then Forest and Water, then Fire, Light and Spirit.

#### Shadow Seal (first)

Walk right along the Keep, past the Forest door and the Water stairs, to the stairs up to the Shadow door [[gf1-334-2, gf1-332-2]]. Enter the door emblazoned with the Shadow Medallion [[gf1-332-2]].

1. **Blue Rupee chest.** Look left as you enter and Longshot over to the chest, which holds a **Blue Rupee** [[gf1-332-3, gf3-86-4]]. This chest is not a Map or Compass, so it is safe. The alternative is the Song of Time to make three Blocks of Time and then the Hover Boots [[gf1-332-3]].
2. **Temporary platforms.** Turn around from the chest and light the torch with a Fire Arrow. A series of platforms appears temporarily [[gf1-332-4]]. Run along them to the larger platform at the back and defeat the Like-Like on it [[gf1-332-7]]. A Like-Like can swallow your shield, so kill it quickly [[gf3-52-9]].
3. **Relight the torch.** Turn around and relight the same torch, then keep heading to the back of the room on the renewed platforms [[gf1-332-7]]. If the platforms vanish, relight the torch [[gf3-86-4]].
4. **Gold Gauntlets.** On the next platform, look right at the opaque platforms. Run down them and step on the switch before time runs out. If you have to, wait for the torch to go out and relight it [[gf1-332-5]]. A chest then appears on the previous ledge. Open it for the **Gold Gauntlets** [[gf1-332-5, gf3-86-5]]. Longshot to the chest if the platforms are gone [[gf3-86-5]].
5. **Rusty switch.** From this platform, look to the left side of the room and use the Lens of Truth to see an invisible path. Follow it to the rusty switch and smash it with the Megaton Hammer. The door in the back unseals [[gf1-332-7]]. Head back to the Gauntlets platform and use the Lens of Truth to find another invisible path to the unsealed door. Go through [[gf1-332-7]].
6. **Seal.** Destroy the seal with a Light Arrow. Impa appears and returns you to the Keep [[gf1-333-1]].

#### Optional: Great Fairy of Courage (defense upgrade)

Leave the castle, cross the bridge of light, and go left under the arch. Climb over the rubble to the end of the small canyon. With the Gold Gauntlets, pick up the black obelisk and hurl it aside to reveal a Fairy Fountain [[gf1-333-2]]. Stand on the Triforce symbol and play Zelda's Lullaby to wake the Great Fairy [[gf1-334-1]]. She doubles your defense and puts a white border around your hearts [[gf1-334-1, gf2-154-2]].

Commenters report that the improved defense did not stop either achievement from unlocking [[ra-comments-140-4, ra-comments-144-15]]. It is optional but useful, since one commenter found escaping the castle at 3 hearts harder than the boss [[ra-comments-140-1]]. Return to the Keep afterwards [[gf1-334-1]].

#### Forest Seal

Go right as you enter the Keep to reach the door with the Forest Medallion above it, and go through [[gf1-328-2]].

**Forest Seal Room One.** Step into the middle of the room to make a Gray Wolfos appear. Backflip away from its attack, then Jump Attack its back [[gf1-328-3]]. A chest appears holding a **Blue Rupee** [[gf1-328-4]]. Stand on the carpet and use Din's Fire to light the four surrounding torches. Then light the torch above the sealed door with a Fire Arrow, and the door unseals [[gf1-328-5]].

**Forest Seal Room Two** (a Silver Rupee room). Falling into a pit means collecting the Silver Rupees over again [[gf1-329-1]].
1. Look left on entering to see a Silver Rupee over a pit. Play the Song of Time when Navi flutters beneath it so a Block of Time appears [[gf1-329-1]].
2. Equip the Iron Boots so the fan does not blow you around, then walk onto the block for the rupee [[gf1-329-2]].
3. Wait for the fan to stop, switch to the Hover Boots, and stand still until it blows again. Run toward the left ledge and the wind carries you there [[gf1-329-3]].
4. Switch back to the Kokiri Boots and toss a bomb at the Beamos to destroy it [[gf1-329-4]]. Jump to its vacated platform and go right for another Silver Rupee [[gf1-329-5]].
5. Jump to the sealed door's front and look right for a Silver Rupee in front of a fan. Wait for the fan to stop, jump out, and jump back before it blows again [[gf1-329-6]].
6. Look left of the sealed door. Beneath the fan is a platform with a switch. Wait for the fan to stop and jump down to it. A Longshot statue appears beneath the last Silver Rupee [[gf1-329-7]].
7. Longshot up and grab the rupee. The door on the other side unseals, so jump back via the Beamos platform and go through [[gf1-329-8]].

**Forest Seal Room Three.** Shoot the green sphere with a Light Arrow. Saria sends you back to the Keep [[gf1-330-2]].

#### Water Seal

Head right out of the Forest hallway and go down the stairs to the door with the Water Medallion [[gf1-330-3]].

**Water Seal Room One.** Run in and stalactites fall, and two Freezzards appear. Kill both, and Fire Arrows work well. The door behind the ice unseals [[gf1-330-4]]. Capture Blue Fire from the center in an empty bottle and melt the ice blocking the door. Fill a second bottle with Blue Fire, then go through [[gf1-330-5]]. Do not open the other chests here. In another room, the right chest has a Recovery Heart and the left one freezes you, so skipping them is safest [[gf1-330-5, gf2-99-1]].

**Water Seal Room Two** (two-minute limit) [[gf1-331-1]].
1. Go to the left side of the rear block and push it right into a rock. Then from its left side push it forward into the pit at the front [[gf1-331-2]].
2. Go to the remaining ice block's right side and push it forward across the filled pit. Go to its right side again (nearest the door) and push forward. Then push it from the side nearest the right wall to the opposite wall [[gf1-331-2]].
3. Jump onto the block, then up to the ledge. Melt the ice with Blue Fire and pound the rusted switch with the Megaton Hammer. The rear door unseals [[gf1-331-3]].

**Water Seal Room Three.** Shoot the orb with a Light Arrow. Ruto transports you to the Keep [[gf1-332-1]].

#### Fire Seal

The Fire door is in the Keep past the Shadow door [[gf1-334-2]]. You cannot finish this seal without the Gold Gauntlets, and you need the Goron Tunic equipped on entering [[gf1-335-1]].

**Fire Seal Room One.** Collect all five Silver Rupees and the rear door unseals [[gf1-335-2]].
1. Jump onto the platform in front of you. It starts sinking. Run to the branch and go right for the first Silver Rupee, which sinks almost immediately [[gf1-335-1, gf1-335-2]].
2. Hang on the edge of the rotating-flames ledge until it is safe to climb, or you will be knocked into the lava. Follow the flame path to the rear for the second Silver Rupee [[gf1-335-2]].
3. Climb to the black obelisk and throw it aside with the Gold Gauntlets. Take the Silver Rupee underneath it [[gf1-335-2]].
4. Go straight at the split to the Fire Slug ledge. Kill the Fire Slug and take its rupee. Cross the tossed pillar for the last Silver Rupee [[gf1-335-2]].
5. Return to the sinking platform and Longshot the target right of the unsealed door [[gf1-335-3]].

**Fire Seal Room Two.** Destroy the seal with a Light Arrow. Darunia returns you to the Keep [[gf1-336-1]].

#### Light Seal

Go right and down the stairs from the Fire door. Throw aside the black obelisk, which shatters against the tower shield, and enter the door behind it with the Light Medallion [[gf1-336-2]].

**Light Seal Room One.** Use the Lens of Truth to find and kill an invisible Big Skulltula and four invisible Keese. A chest then appears in the middle holding a **Small Key**. Take it through the locked rear door [[gf1-336-3]]. Ignore the other chests, as they are trapped [[gf3-87-3]].

**Light Seal Room Two.** Stand on the Triforce symbol and play Zelda's Lullaby. Open the chest that appears for a **Small Key**, then go through the unlocked door [[gf1-337-1]].

**Light Seal Room Three** (one-minute limit) [[gf1-337-2]]. Longshot to the ceiling target above the central pillar for the first Silver Rupee. Take the second from the alcove left of the sealed door, after the boulders pass. Run back toward the pillar for the next, and the opposite side holds two more [[gf1-337-2]]. The door unseals, so go fast [[gf1-337-2]].

**Light Seal Room Four.** The orb is missing at first. Use the Lens of Truth to see the opposite wall is fake. Walk through it and shoot the orb with a Light Arrow. Rauru returns you to the Keep [[gf1-338-1, gf1-338-2]].

#### Spirit Seal

Go right from the Light door and up the stairs to the Spirit Medallion door [[gf1-338-3]].

**Spirit Seal Room One.** Leave most Armos Knights alone, since they block the Blade Traps [[gf1-338-4]]. Destroy the Beamos with a Bomb or Bombchu. Longshot the ceiling target for the rupee above it, then take the second from behind it [[gf1-338-4]]. Collect the three remaining Silver Rupees near the corner Blade Traps and the rear door unseals [[gf1-338-5, gf1-338-6]].

**Spirit Seal Room Two.**
1. Destroy both Fire Slugs. Hit the Crystal Switch beyond the left bars with a Spin Attack to make a chest with **Bombchus** appear [[gf1-339-1, gf1-339-2]].
2. Line up with the Crystal Switch between the two torches and send a Bombchu through the gap near the top of the bars. The door unseals [[gf1-339-3]].

**Spirit Seal Room Three.** Burn the spider web on the ceiling with a Fire Arrow to let sunlight in. Use the Mirror Shield on the sun panel to the right of the tunnel you came through, which is directly behind and to the right when facing the sealed door. The other sun panels summon Wall Masters [[gf1-339-4, gf1-339-5]]. Then shoot the orb with a Light Arrow. Nabooru sends you back, and the barrier around Ganon's Tower disappears [[gf1-340-1]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave the Keep:** Check that all six seals are destroyed. The tower barrier only drops once the last one is gone [[gf1-330-1, gf1-340-1]]. Save now, ideally in a slot you do not mind keeping, and keep fairies and a green potion ready.

### Ganon's Tower

Head right until you see a bridge on the left. Cross it and go through the door into the tower [[gf1-340-2]].

#### Floors One to Five

1. **Floor One.** Kill the four Fire Keese, then climb the spiral staircase on the left, watching for more Fire Keese. Take the door on the right at the top [[gf1-340-3, gf1-340-4]].
2. **Floor Two.** The doors seal and both Dinolfos must be defeated. Then go through the far door and up the stairs, and take the door on the left [[gf1-341-1, gf1-341-2]].
3. **Floor Three.** Two Stalfos appear around a flame pillar. Defeat both and the pillar dies away, revealing a chest with the **Boss Key** [[gf1-342-1, gf1-342-2]]. The Boss Key is not a Map or Compass, so take it. Go through the door behind the chest and up to the door on the left [[gf1-342-3]].
4. **Floor Four.** Two Iron Knuckles wait here. Shooting one with an arrow wakes it alone, which lets you use the backflip then Jump Attack pattern [[gf1-343-1]]. If both wake, Nayru's Love is recommended [[gf1-343-2]]. At 3 hearts, wake them one at a time. Then take the door the camera focuses on [[gf1-343-3]].
5. **Floor Five.** Pots here give Magic Power and Recovery Hearts [[gf1-344-1]]. Take the door on the right, climb the stairs and enter the boss door on the left [[gf1-344-2]].

#### Boss: Ganondorf

Navi cannot Z-Target or advise you until Ganondorf is stunned with a Light Arrow, and she becomes unavailable again when he recovers [[gf1-344-3]].
- The fight begins with a ground pound, so roll to the outer rim quickly so you do not fall [[gf3-89-1]].
- Stay away from Ganondorf until he is stunned. He stays in the center and his Shockwave collapses floor pieces into pits [[gf1-522-5]].
- Reflect his lightning balls with sword strikes, or Empty Bottles. A missed ball destroys the panel you stand on and drops you down [[gf1-522-6]]. Climb back up using the tiles on the central pillar [[gf1-522-7]].
- When a reflected ball stuns him, shoot him with a Light Arrow, and he lowers to the ground [[gf1-523-1, gf3-89-5]].
- Equip the Hover Boots to cross the center pit, Jump Attack when halfway across, then use normal strikes. Run back to the rim and switch boots when he recovers [[gf1-344-5]].
- When he raises both arms to charge a big spell, shoot him with a Light Arrow to cancel it and stun him [[gf1-344-6, gf3-89-4]]. You can also deflect the multi-spell volley with an Advanced Spin Slash [[gf3-89-3]].
- A Biggoron's Sword jump attack combo works, and about three knockdowns finish him [[gf2-100-2]]. Ganondorf's cape acts as his life meter [[gf1-344-4]].

#### The escape

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning (Dark Lord, 49292):** Hits taken in the next fight are what matter. The escape comes first and the Ganon fight follows straight away. Go in with full hearts, fairies and a green potion [[ra-comments-140-13, ra-comments-145-7]].

After Ganondorf falls, you have three minutes to escape. Stay close to Zelda, since only she can open the sealed doors [[gf1-345-1, gf1-345-2]].
1. On the tower exterior, follow Zelda down. Through Floor Five, go left, then jump to the ledge she stands on and keep following [[gf1-345-2]].
2. Floor Four: follow her right, around the burning rubble [[gf1-346-1]]. Continue down the exterior [[gf1-346-2]].
3. Floor Three: Zelda is trapped behind flames and two Stalfos appear. Defeat them to free her [[gf1-346-3]]. Nayru's Love plus the Biggoron Sword makes this quick [[gf1-346-4]].
4. Floor Two: follow her through the burning rubble, down the stairs and through the left door [[gf1-347-1]].
5. Keep bridge: run to the end. Run past the Redead or kill it with the Biggoron Sword. Then go through the door, up the stairs and wait for Zelda to open the final door [[gf1-347-2, gf1-347-3]].

Nayru's Love can help on the way down, though sources disagree on how it interacts with the Ganon achievement [[ra-comments-140-12, ra-comments-139-1, ra-comments-140-2]].

#### Boss: Ganon

After the cutscene, run toward the ruins where Ganondorf becomes Ganon [[gf1-347-4]]. You start without the Master Sword, and Navi can Z-Target him this time [[gf1-347-4]].

**Dark Lord rules.** Being hit by the flames around Ganon or by his attacks fails the achievement, and it applies to the last phase [[ra-comments-139-1, ra-comments-139-2]]. A commenter says a "phantom hit", where Link flashes and grunts but takes no damage, did not break it [[ra-comments-140-8]]. Sources disagree on Nayru's Love: one says it does not help, and several report hits while it was active did not matter [[ra-comments-139-1, ra-comments-140-2, ra-comments-140-3, ra-comments-140-9, ra-comments-140-20]]. Do not rely on it. Rely on clean dodging.

**Phase one (no Master Sword).** Ganon's weak spot is his tail [[gf1-347-5]]. Equip the Biggoron's Sword, or the Megaton Hammer if you lack it [[gf3-91-1]].
- Z-Target him and side dodge (hold left or right and press A) around behind him. Then Jump Attack his tail [[gf1-347-5]].
- After a hit he raises the tail. Strafe back to the front, then around again to hit the tail again [[gf1-523-4]].
- Alternatively, roll between his legs just before he swings, then hit the tail [[ra-guide-18-4]]. A commenter backs away first, rolls under as he walks toward you, then Z-targets and jump-attacks [[ra-comments-139-18]].
- The hammer has short reach, so you must be fairly close [[ra-comments-139-11]].
- Running away, turning, adjusting the camera and then rolling is easier than rolling immediately [[ra-comments-139-16]].
- You can also stun his face with a Light Arrow. The roll method is faster and uses less magic [[gf2-132-1]].

When the flames around the arena lower, run to Zelda and take back the Master Sword [[gf1-347-6]].

**Phase two (Master Sword).** If you used the hammer, switch back to the Master Sword. If you have the Biggoron Sword, you can keep using it for now [[gf1-347-7]].
- Get behind him with a Light Arrow to the face or by rolling between his legs, then Z-Target and Jump Attack his tail [[gf1-347-7]]. He is faster in this half [[gf3-91-1]].
- A commenter keeps the Master Sword out and does not Z-target. After rolling under, they do an immediate quick spin (rotate the stick and press attack), which damages and stuns him and leaves time to back away [[ra-comments-139-19, ra-comments-139-20, ra-comments-139-21]].
- Another approach: roll under his legs, slash without Z-targeting, run forward a bit, quick-turn and repeat. Not Z-targeting leaves time to avoid his second slash [[ra-comments-139-13, ra-comments-139-14]].
- Another commenter stood still Z-targeted, let him walk up, then blocked or backed away so the attack missed [[ra-comments-140-14]].
- Only the Master Sword can defeat him, so use it for the final hit [[gf1-347-7, gf2-100-4]]. Once he collapses, Zelda holds him down and the sages power your sword. Go to his head and slash [[gf3-91-3]].

Dark Lord can take a minute to pop, usually during the sky scene, so don't panic if nothing appears immediately [[ra-comments-140-10]].

#### Ending: Blind Explorer

Keep playing through the ending. Blind Explorer pops later, after the conversation with Zelda near the end, around when she plays the Song of Time or you give her the ocarina, and not at the same time as the boss achievement [[ra-comments-142-5, ra-comments-144-1, ra-comments-144-14, ra-comments-143-4]]. The check happens at the very end, right before the credits [[ra-comments-144-6]].
