## Chapter 14: Forest Temple

[MERMAID]

This chapter takes Adult Link through the Sacred Forest Meadow, where Sheik teaches the Minuet of Forest, and then through the Forest Temple, the first Adult Link dungeon. You collect the Fairy Bow, the Map, the Compass and the Boss Key, beat the four Poe Sisters, ride the elevator down and fight Phantom Ganon. Saria then gives you the Forest Medallion, and you return to the Temple of Time for the Prelude of Light [[gf1-141-5, gf1-143-1, gf1-163-3, gf1-167-1]].

Two achievements are in this chapter: **Drunken Master** (missable, earned against Phantom Ganon) and **Awakening** (Forest Medallion) [[ra-guide-12-4, ra-guide-12-6]].

> [!TIP]
> **Preparation:** The Hookshot is required for most of the Gold Skulltulas here [[checklist-skulltulas-13-1]]. Before the boss, have 30 arrows and full health, and set the Bow and two empty Bottles on your C buttons so you cannot grab another item by mistake [[ra-comments-29-1, ra-comments-29-4, ra-comments-30-10]]. One commenter suggests leaving the temple after getting the bow, winning the quiver upgrade in Kakariko and entering the fight with 40 arrows [[ra-comments-26-9, ra-comments-30-9]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Drunken Master (10 pts) is lost for the attempt if you use the sword, the Hookshot or any other item before Phantom Ganon is at 1 HP. Do not press the sword button at all, and do not switch to the Hookshot on the way to the boss [[ra-guide-12-4, ra-guide-12-5, ra-comments-26-1, ra-comments-26-2, ra-comments-26-12, ra-comments-28-1, ra-comments-28-9]]. One player used the Hookshot on a Skulltula along the way and that cancelled the achievement [[ra-comments-28-2]]. Plan the order: collect the Hookshot Gold Skulltulas first, then follow the commenters' advice to leave the temple and re-enter it before the boss, opening only the Bow or a Bottle [[ra-comments-28-10, ra-comments-29-2]].

### Sacred Forest Meadow

🗺️ **Map:** [View Sacred Forest Meadow Map by R351D3NT3V1L4](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/5354-sacred-forest-meadow-map)

The Sacred Forest Meadow lies in the midst of the Lost Woods, and the Forest Temple is in the midst of the meadow [[gf1-8-2, gf1-8-3]].

**Optional side stops (Stocking Nuts).** The Deku Nut upgrade is sold in a secret grotto revealed by bombing the large boulder just outside the Sacred Forest Meadow entrance. Two Business Scrubs are there, and one sells the upgrade for 40 Rupees [[ra-comments-73-4, gf3-141-1]]. The Stocking Nuts achievement refers to this one, not the Mask of Truth one on the forest stage [[ra-comments-73-1]]. One guide warns that this upgrade is permanently lost if you become adult before showing the Mask of Truth on the forest stage [[checklist-sidequests-14-2]]. A bomb placed in the meadow's first area also opens a cave where defeating two Wolfos produces a chest with a Purple Rupee [[gf3-44-8]].

#### The hedge maze

1. Enter the maze. The Scrubs are gone, replaced by Moriblins that charge if you enter their sight. Wait for each to face away and shoot it in the back with the Hookshot. Move slowly so it does not hear you [[gf1-141-1]].
2. Use Z to look around the first corner, kill the first Moriblin, go right and kill the second. At the end of that passage go right, peek around the next corner and kill the third. Turn left at the flowery passage and kill the fourth, then go right, then left at the end and into the pool of water [[gf1-141-1]].
3. Climb out of the pool and up the ladder on the left. If a Moriblin is there, wait until it turns away before climbing out [[gf1-141-2]].
4. **Gold Skulltula (Sacred Forest Meadow).** This one appears at night only, as adult, and needs the Hookshot. At the top of the ladder go left a short way, face left and Hookshot it [[checklist-skulltulas-3-7, gf1-456-1, gf1-141-2]].
5. A pit in the center of the maze holds a Fairy Fountain, useful before the dungeon [[gf1-141-3]]. The Minuet of Forest also brings you back to it later [[gf1-395-2]].

#### Sheik and the Minuet of Forest

1. Climb back down the ladder and go up the stairs opposite it. At the top a club-wielding Moblin sends shockwaves down the passage [[gf1-141-4]].
2. Dodge the shockwaves by running left and right between each one, run around behind him and hit his back with two Jump Attacks. He drops a large number of Rupees [[gf1-141-4]]. Another source says his club pattern is left, right, left and that it takes three sword slashes [[gf2-53-7, gf3-251-3]].
3. Go up the stairs behind him and step onto the Triforce pedestal. Sheik appears and teaches the **Minuet of Forest** (A, C-Up, C-Left, C-Right, C-Left, C-Right) [[gf1-141-5, gf1-395-1]]. Playing it warps you here from anywhere, giving access to the Forest Temple and a Fairy Fountain [[gf1-395-2]].
4. Hookshot the large tree branch above the shattered staircase and go through the doorway into the temple [[gf1-141-6]].

### Forest Temple

The central goal is to kill the four Poe Sisters who steal flames from the torches, which retracts the central elevator. The dungeon loops around that central chamber [[gf1-143-1]]. Killing the four Poes opens the way to the boss [[gf1-9-9]]. Wallmasters drag you back to the dungeon entrance if they land on you [[gf1-151-1]].

#### Entrance courtyard

1. Step forward and kill the two Gray Wolfos by backflipping out of range when they attack and immediately landing a Jump Attack while their backs are turned [[gf1-144-1]].
2. Turn right and climb the vines on the wall up to the trees, then go left a little [[gf3-45-2, gf2-54-2]].
3. **Gold Skulltula (Forest Temple, first room).** From the top of the tree, look to the top-right of the ivy wall and Hookshot the Skulltula [[gf1-144-2, checklist-skulltulas-13-1]].
4. Edge along the branch, jump to the adjacent branch and open the chest at its end for a **Small Key** [[gf1-144-3]]. Drop down and go through the door under the stone archway [[gf1-144-3]].

#### South Passage and Torch Room

1. A Big Skulltula is in the hall. A single Hookshot hit kills it instantly [[gf1-145-1]]. If you are attempting Drunken Master on this pass, kill it with the bow instead [[ra-comments-28-4]]. Go through the door at the far end [[gf1-145-1]].
2. In the Torch Room, the four Poe Sisters appear and steal the four flames, lowering the central elevator [[gf1-145-2]].
3. Run across to the opposite end, jump onto the ledge and go up the stairs. **Gold Skulltula:** look right at the top and Hookshot it [[gf1-145-3, checklist-skulltulas-13-2]].

#### North Passage and Lower Stalfos Chamber

1. The door at the top of the stairs leads to a Blue Bubble. Hold up your shield while Z-targeting it and let it touch you to put out its flame, then kill it [[gf1-146-3, gf2-55-4]].
2. In the Lower Stalfos Chamber, Z-target one Stalfos (they will not attack while you target one), circle, keep your shield up and attack from the side or behind right after they attack [[gf1-146-1]]. Deku Nuts can interrupt their attacks [[checklist-sidequests-7-3]].
3. When both die, a chest in the center holds a **Small Key**. Smash the pots at the rear for a Recovery Heart and a Fairy [[gf1-146-2]]. Return to the Torch Room [[gf1-146-2]].

#### Song of Time block and the West Courtyard

1. Run down the stairs and go right to the small hallway. Stand by the blue block and play the Song of Time (right, A, down, right, A, down) to reveal a door [[gf1-147-1, gf2-54-7]].
2. In the West Courtyard, kill the Big Deku Baba and the Octorok. Hookshot the first two Skullwalltulas on the ivy wall on the right (the third is out of reach), then climb as far right as possible to avoid it and enter the alcove door at the top [[gf1-147-1, gf1-147-2]].

#### Balcony Hallway and East Courtyard

1. Kill the Blue Bubble. The far door opens and a chest appears with the **Dungeon Map** [[gf1-148-1]].
2. In the East Courtyard, kill the Deku Baba and Hookshot the Skullwalltula on the ivy wall. Hookshot the white circle above the left balcony and step on the switch to drain the well [[gf1-148-1, gf1-148-2]].
3. Jump down. Kill the Octorok and Big Deku Baba. From the right side of the dead-end dirt path, Hookshot the chest on the cliff. Aim at the part of the chest closer to the wall so you stay up. The chest holds a Recovery Heart [[gf1-148-3, gf2-55-2, gf3-45-10]].
4. **Gold Skulltula:** it clings to the wall around the corner from the chest [[gf1-148-3, gf1-491-2]].
5. Jump back down, go to the right side and drop into the well [[gf1-148-3]].

#### The Well

Run to the end collecting the Recovery Hearts. Open the chest for a **Small Key**, then climb the ivy wall back to the West Courtyard [[gf1-149-1, gf1-149-2, gf2-55-1]]. Return through the archway door to the Torch Room, climb the ledge on the right, go up the stairs and use a Small Key on the locked door [[gf1-149-3]].

#### West Passage and Stone Block Room

1. Kill the Big Skulltula and go through the far door [[gf1-149-3]].
2. Defeat the Blue Bubble, climb the ladder, then the next on the right, go left and left again through the doorway. A stone block with floor arrows is to the left [[gf1-150-1, gf1-150-2]].
3. Go forward through the doorway, left down the hall, left, and left through another doorway to reach the block's far side. Push it forward until a ladder appears on the right (ignore it), keep pushing until a gap opens on your left, slip through behind the block and push it back until the gap closes [[gf1-150-3]].
4. Push it forward until it stops, return down the passage, go around the ledge taking three rights, and push it again until it falls into a small alcove. Climb onto it for the third level [[gf1-150-4]].
5. Climb the ladder, turn right, right again through the doorway, and push the block in front of you until it stops. Drop back down [[gf1-150-5]].
6. Climb on the placed block, run down the passage and push the third-level block into the pit, then climb to the top [[gf1-150-6]].
7. Go right after two ledges, up the ladder, right around the corner, and kill or dodge the two Blue Bubbles before the door [[gf1-150-7]].

#### West Twisted Hallway to the Bow

1. Run to the end of the hall. Navi warns of Wallmasters. Run in large circles to dodge and kill them when they land, or enter First Person Mode to make them vanish [[gf1-151-1, gf1-151-2]].
2. Jump to the pillar ahead, then to the right, and enter the door [[gf1-151-3]].
3. This is the West Stairwell (stairs on the left, red Poe Sister in the portraits). It is a series of stairs with three portraits. Run down both stairsets and through the door at the bottom of the second [[gf1-152-1, gf1-153-1]].
4. In the Upper Stalfos Room, beware the pit. A Stalfos walks over it. Beat it and an elevator drops with two more Stalfos. Beat both quickly or they regenerate. A chest then appears with the **Fairy Bow** [[gf1-152-2, gf1-152-3, gf1-152-4]]. The quiver holds 30 arrows [[gf1-406-2]]. A trick for the pair: hit each twice with Jump Attacks, then quickly finish both [[gf2-55-7]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Achievement Trigger Warning:** The Hookshot Gold Skulltulas still ahead (and the Hookshot-only route steps) conflict with Drunken Master. Finish collecting every token you want first. Then leave the temple, re-enter, and go straight to the boss using only the Bow or a Bottle [[ra-comments-28-9, ra-comments-28-10, ra-comments-29-2]].

#### West Stairwell Poe (red)

1. Return to the West Stairwell through the Upper Stalfos Room door. Shoot the Poe's portrait with the Bow from as far away as possible, at the bottom or top of the stairwell. If you get close, she moves. Burn all three portraits and she appears at the bottom [[gf1-153-1, gf1-153-2]].
2. Shoot her with arrows. Do not Z-target her, because she vanishes. If you get too close she spins her torch [[gf1-153-3]].
3. The chest that appears has a **Small Key**. Her flame returns to the torch here and in the Torch Room [[gf1-153-4]].

#### Back to the Stone Block Room

Climb back up and return through the West Twisted Hallway to the Stone Block Room. Turn around, look above the door and shoot the silver eye to straighten the hallway on the other side, then go back through [[gf1-153-4, gf1-154-1]]. On the third level, a golden eye shot gives a chest with arrows [[gf1-157-2, gf3-46-7]].

#### Boss Key

Run down the straightened hall and drop into the room. Watch for Wallmasters. Open the blue chest for the **Boss Key**, then drop through the pit [[gf1-154-2]]. Kill both Blue Bubbles to open the south door [[gf1-154-3]].

**Gold Skulltula (Second floor):** you arrive on the upper West Courtyard ledge. Kill the Big Deku Baba, jump to the broken column platform with two Recovery Hearts, walk slowly to the far edge, look up and Hookshot the Skulltula [[gf1-155-1, gf1-155-2, checklist-skulltulas-13-4]]. Take the alcove door at the far end of the ledge [[gf1-155-3]].

#### Floormaster Room

Attack the Floormaster when it is not glowing green. It splits into three small ones; kill them quickly with the Bow. The chest has a **Small Key** [[gf1-156-1, gf1-156-2, gf1-156-3]]. Return to the previous room, turn right on the ledge and go through the door [[gf1-157-1]]. Drop down in the Stone Block Room, then shoot the silver eye above the doorway to re-twist the hallway and return to the West Stairwell and Upper Stalfos Room [[gf1-157-3]].

#### East Stairwell (blue Poe) and Compass

Take the opposite door in the Upper Stalfos Room [[gf1-158-1]]. Shoot all three portraits as before and kill the Poe. The chest has the **Compass** [[gf1-158-2]]. Climb to the top and go through the locked door [[gf1-158-3]].

#### East Twisted Hallway and Flaming Arrow Room

1. Drop to the floor, wait for the Wallmaster, dodge and kill it [[gf1-158-4]].
2. Climb the tall ladder to the right of the entrance door and go through the hallway with two Green Bubbles. Shoot them when their flame turns off, or ignore them, then enter the end door [[gf1-158-5, gf1-158-6]].
3. In the Flaming Arrow Room, ride the rotating platforms and shoot an arrow through the center torch flame onto the frozen eyeball switch. The previous room twists [[gf1-159-1, gf2-57-1]].
4. Return, run to the end of the twisted hall, drop down, kill the Wallmaster and drop down the pit in the corner to the right [[gf1-159-2]].

#### Crushing Ceiling Room and Puzzle Block Room

1. Run south through the gaps as the ceiling falls. Kill Big Skulltulas with arrows. Halfway down on the right, step on the switch to open the exit [[gf1-160-1]]. A chest here has a bundle of arrows [[gf2-57-2]].
2. In the Puzzle Block Room, shoot the portrait, push the mismatched fifth block aside and assemble the other four into the yellow Poe Sister within a minute. Kill her [[gf1-160-2, gf2-57-3]].
3. Climb the ledge near the torch and exit [[gf1-160-3]].

#### East Hallway and Amy

Kill the Big Skulltula on the ceiling and go through the far door [[gf1-161-1]]. Jump into the Torch Room to meet Amy, who splits into four. The real Amy spins when she splits. Shoot her with an arrow and repeat. Z-targeting is fine [[gf1-161-2, gf1-516-4]]. When Amy dies, the last flame returns and the elevator rises. Go down it [[gf1-161-3]].

#### Basement 1: the rotating room

Push the wall handles to rotate the room. One guide's route: grab the left handle's north side and push it south, then step on the switch in the northwest room. Push the right handle's north side south and step on the switch in the eastern alcove. Push the eastern side of the handle west, enter the southwest room, kill the Big Skulltula and open the chest for arrows. Finally push the south side of the handle north and step on the south switch to open the grating over the north doorway [[gf1-162-1, gf1-162-2, gf1-162-3, gf1-162-5]]. Another guide describes the same room as needing three switches, pressed by rotating the room clockwise in turns [[gf2-57-5, gf2-57-7]].

**Gold Skulltula (Basement 1):** in the southwest alcove, on the wall to the left; Hookshot it [[gf1-162-4, checklist-skulltulas-13-5]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave the basement:** Take the Basement 1 token now if you want it. After this point, the Drunken Master rules mean no Hookshot until the achievement pops [[ra-comments-28-2, ra-comments-28-9]].

### Phantom Ganon

Go to the right and up the stairs into the large stage. When you turn to leave, spears trap you and Phantom Ganon appears [[gf1-162-6]]. Biggoron Sword owners are advised to stay with the Master Sword for the dungeon, though that sword is not used until the achievement pops [[gf1-144-1, ra-comments-26-6]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Save before this fight. If you break the rules, Drunken Master (10 pts) is lost for that attempt. Never press the sword button. If you are aiming with the bow, press Z to stop, not B, and do not switch to the Hookshot [[ra-comments-26-12, ra-comments-29-3, ra-comments-30-2, ra-comments-30-4]]. Pressing the sword button in phase 1 cancels the bow and can make the achievement fail [[ra-comments-26-13]]. If you fail, one player reset the emulator without saving and retried the fight [[ra-comments-26-14]].

**Phase 1 (bow only).**
1. He rides a flying horse into a painting and comes out of two paintings. The fake turns back at the last minute; the real one emerges and scatters electricity [[gf1-519-6]].
2. Shoot him with an arrow as he emerges from the brighter painting [[gf1-519-5]]. The loudest footsteps and the darker Ganon with whiter whites are the real one. Stand in the middle of the corner floor lines so you are not hit if you miss [[ra-guide-12-4]]. Wait for the portal, then shoot into it [[gf2-58-1]]. The corner spots let you take careful shots and save arrows for phase 2 [[ra-comments-26-10, ra-comments-27-7]].
3. Three hits start phase 2 [[gf1-519-7]]. Phase 1 needs 3 arrows and phase 2 about 11 [[ra-comments-27-4]].

**Phase 2 (bottle, then bow).**
1. Have an empty bottle equipped. It is easier to hit the ball than you expect, and the bottle lingers longer than you would expect, so swing a little early [[ra-guide-12-5, ra-comments-28-5]].
2. Reflect his lightning balls to stun him. Standing at the far end is easier [[gf1-519-8, gf1-519-9]]. Stand back to give yourself time to judge the swing [[ra-comments-30-8]].
3. When he falls, shoot arrows while he is down (about four per stun). Do not hit Navi, or he gets up before you can shoot [[ra-comments-29-14, ra-comments-30-13]].
4. Repeat until he reaches 1 HP. Do not waste arrows [[ra-guide-12-5, ra-comments-29-7]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48818]

The achievement triggers at 1 HP. Only then may you use the sword for the final blow [[ra-comments-26-2, ra-comments-28-3, ra-comments-29-16]]. The challenge indicator may not appear, but the achievement still works [[ra-comments-27-12, ra-comments-28-12]]. If your arrows run out without it popping, restart and try again [[ra-comments-28-11]].

**Finishing.** Once he is stunned, use one Jump Attack and then regular strikes. Repeat the reflect-and-attack cycle until he dies [[gf1-519-10, gf1-163-1]]. Take the Heart Container that appears and step into the beam of light [[gf1-163-2, gf3-49-1]].

### Chamber of Sages

1. Step into the beam of light after taking the Heart Container [[gf1-163-2, gf3-49-1]].
2. You arrive in the Chamber of the Sages, where Saria appears and reveals that she is the Sage of the Forest Temple [[gf1-163-3, gf3-49-2]].
3. Saria gives you her power in the form of the **Forest Medallion**. It is the reward for breaking the curse of the temple [[gf1-163-3, gf3-161-2, gf1-422-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48819]

4. Afterwards you are returned outside to Kokiri Forest, where a new Deku Tree sprouts [[gf1-163-3]].

### Temple of Time

Return to the Temple of Time and run into the Master Sword chamber. Sheik teaches the **Prelude of Light** (C-Up, C-Right, C-Up, C-Right, C-Left, C-Up), which warps you to the Temple of Time [[gf1-167-1, gf1-397-3]].

> [!TIP]
> **Before you leave:** Confirm you picked up the Map, Compass and Boss Key [[ra-comments-123-14]], and that you have the Gold Skulltulas listed above (Forest Temple tokens #64-#68 and the meadow token #7) [[checklist-skulltulas-13-1, checklist-skulltulas-13-2, checklist-skulltulas-13-3, checklist-skulltulas-13-4, checklist-skulltulas-13-5, checklist-skulltulas-3-7]]. The Skulltula in the northeast room can be reached by Hookshotting the island chest, or more easily from the nearby room you fall into from the second twisted room [[checklist-skulltulas-13-3]].
