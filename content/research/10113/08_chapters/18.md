## Chapter 18: Shadow Temple

[MERMAID]

This chapter takes you from the Kakariko Graveyard ledge into the Shadow Temple, through its Lens of Truth puzzles, the Dead Hand miniboss, the invisible-spike rooms, the cursed boat and the fire walls, and finally to Bongo Bongo. You collect the Map, Compass and Boss Key, five Gold Skulltulas, the Hover Boots and a Heart Container, and Impa gives you the Shadow Medallion [[gf1-260-1, gf1-261-3, gf1-262-4, gf1-263-4, gf1-272-3, gf1-275-8, gf1-275-9]].

The Shadow Temple is reached only by the Nocturne of Shadow, which takes Young or Adult Link to the high ledge in Kakariko Graveyard [[gf1-397-2]]. Access depends on having the Forest, Fire and Water Medallions [[gf1-422-4]]. The Bottom of the Well must be finished before the Shadow Temple can be completed [[gf1-19-1]]. That is also where the Lens of Truth comes from [[gf1-390-1]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Invisible One (**Invisible One** below) can be lost during the Bongo Bongo fight. It requires beating Bongo Bongo without using the Lens of Truth and without being trapped by his hands [[gf4-29-2, ra-guide-15-2, ra-comments-40-1, ra-comments-40-2]]. Read the boss section before dropping into the pit.

### What to bring

- **Din's Fire** is needed at the entrance and again in the Shadow Temple. It comes from the Great Fairy in the Hyrule Castle garden [[gf1-90-2, gf1-258-2]].
- **Lens of Truth, Longshot, Hover Boots (found here), Iron Boots and Kokiri Boots, bombs and the Fairy Bow with Fire Arrows** are all used during the temple [[gf1-260-1, gf1-270-3, gf1-275-1]].
- **Biggoron's Sword** is recommended for the miniboss and the boss [[gf2-83-2, gf1-275-6]].
- **Arrows and magic.** Bongo Bongo's hands drop more when stunned if you run low [[gf1-275-7]].
- **Farore's Wind.** Set it before the boss room. If you fail, reset the game and use it to teleport back near the boss's room [[ra-comments-40-8]].
- The Lens of Truth is only said to fail the achievement if you activate it. Carrying it is fine [[ra-comments-40-1]].

### Kakariko Graveyard

Play the Nocturne of Shadow (C-Left, C-Right, C-Right, A, C-Left, C-Right, C-Down) to warp to the Graveyard ledge [[gf1-397-2, gf2-82-2]]. Run east down the stairs near the warp point to reach a large room of torches [[gf1-258-2]]. Stand on the pedestal in the middle of the torches and cast Din's Fire. All the torches light and the Shadow Temple door opens [[gf1-258-2, gf2-82-3]]. Go through it.

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave the Graveyard for good, note that its Pieces of Heart and Skulltulas are covered by their own routes. Dampe's race (Hookshot, then under one minute with the Longshot) rewards a Piece of Heart [[gf1-245-1]]. The Heart Piece from the grave-digging tour is lost forever if you dig it up and leave without collecting it [[ra-guide-20-1]].

### Shadow Temple

#### Entrance and the Raven Room

Run forward and look right around the corner. A long pit has a Longshot target on the far end; pull yourself across [[gf1-260-1]]. Run straight through the face carved into the opposite wall to reach the Raven Room [[gf1-260-2]].

Use the Lens of Truth on the skull posts around the Raven statue. Find the skull that does not disappear. Push the handle on the statue's front until the Raven faces that real skull, and do not stop pushing until it does, or the floor disappears. The grating across the room then opens [[gf1-260-3, gf3-70-8]]. A large pit blocks the grating for now [[gf1-260-3]].

Turn to the wall opposite the pit. With the Lens, find the fake face on the right side, run through it, down the hall and through the door [[gf1-260-4]].

#### Catacombs and the Map

The skull on the next wall has glowing blue eyes; every fake wall in this area has glowing blue eyes, while flashing blue eyes mark secret messages [[gf1-261-1]]. Go through the wall, right around the corner, use the Lens to see a fake wall on the right, and go through it and the door beyond [[gf1-261-2]].

In the Redead Room, kill the Redead and the two Keese high above. The Dungeon Map appears. Take the **Dungeon Map** (✅ Map), then return to the Catacombs [[gf1-261-3, gf2-83-1]].

#### Dead Hand and the Hover Boots

Back through the fake wall, go right, then left around the next corner. Use the Lens to pass through the wall flanked by two pots, go down the passage and through another fake wall, go right then left, and use the Lens to see through the wall at the hall's end. Go through to the door [[gf1-262-1]].

Dead Hand is the miniboss. Let one hand grab you so his body and head appear, struggle free by rotating the stick and mashing buttons, Z-target the head, stand still out of the hands' reach, and execute a Jump Attack as the head lowers [[gf1-262-2]]. Repeat until he dies. The Biggoron Sword kills him in a few hits, though Jump Attacks tend to fly past him [[gf1-262-3]]; two jump attacks are enough with it [[gf2-83-2]].

Open the chest that appears for the **Hover Boots** [[gf1-262-4, gf2-83-3]]. Return to the Raven Room.

#### Compass and the Beamos

In the Raven Room, run through the fake wall to the cliff, equip the Hover Boots and run off the cliff onto the tongue-shaped ledge. Switch back to Kokiri Boots and go through the doorway and down the tunnel [[gf1-263-1]].

Throw a bomb or two into the Beamos's head, then go to the wall on the right, which is fake, and through the door [[gf1-263-2, gf1-263-3]].

In the Gibdos Room, kill both Gibdos. A chest appears with the **Compass** [[gf1-263-4, gf2-83-5]].

#### Scythe Room (Small Key)

Return through the fake wall, kill or dodge the Beamos, and go north through the fake wall north of it [[gf1-264-1]]. In the Scythe Room, grab the two Silver Rupees in the middle in the scythe's path; you can roll under the blades if timed well [[gf1-264-2, gf1-264-3]]. Then Longshot to the northwest target and jump off the wood stack for the third, take the fourth in the west alcove and the fifth in the northeast alcove [[gf1-264-3]]. Do not enter the small enclosed area with the creepy face panel; there is a hole in the ground [[gf2-83-6]].

The southeast grating opens on a chest with a **Small Key**. Leave through the south door [[gf1-264-4]].

#### Guillotine Chamber

Return to the Raven Room and bomb the rough wall to the east. Go down the passage and through the door [[gf1-265-1]]. Move slowly down the ramps and kill the hanging Big Skulltulas with the Longshot, then jump off the cliff at the bottom [[gf1-265-2]].

Run around each rising guillotine or roll under them, watching for the Big Skulltula between the first and second blades [[gf1-265-3]]. Around the corner, Navi warns of Wall Masters; wait for it to drop and kill it, because knocking it off the cliff does not count [[gf1-265-4]]. Jump the three guillotines, and on the small platform fight the Stalfos with your shield up, attacking only right after it attacks [[gf1-265-5]].

With the Lens, jump across the invisible platforms east, then to a corner platform, then north to a door [[gf1-265-6]].

#### Invisible Scythe Room (Skulltula)

Keep the Lens on here; rapidly toggling it uses no magic [[gf1-266-1]]. Kill the Like-Like and the Keese; the rear grating opens [[gf1-266-2, gf2-84-1]]. Go through, kill the Gold Skulltula and take the token (**Skulltula #85**) with the Longshot, and open the two chests, a Blue Rupee and Arrows, the second visible only with the Lens [[gf1-266-2, gf1-266-3, gf1-503-2, checklist-skulltulas-17-1]]. Return to the Guillotine Chamber.

#### Guillotine Chamber, second half

Jump back across the invisible platforms to the Stalfos platform; it appears again. Fight it, then jump west to the rising and falling platforms [[gf1-267-1]]. When the platform is halfway to its apex, jump to the narrow cliff on the right and run southwest to the wide platform with blade traps and five Silver Rupees. Take four by running around, standing safely in the large gap between blades, and bomb the Beamos to reach the fifth beneath it [[gf1-267-1, gf2-84-3]]. The west grating opens.

Follow the twisting hall to the crushing-ceilings room. With the Lens, find the stone block in the right wall, pull it out until it lines up with the smiling face on the floor, then push it forward so both spiked ceilings are blocked [[gf1-267-2, gf2-84-4]]. Enter the cell to the south, kill the Gold Skulltula and Longshot the token (**Skulltula #86**) [[gf1-267-3, gf1-503-4, checklist-skulltulas-17-2]]. The cell across holds a chest with Arrows [[gf2-84-5]].

Pull the block back, climb it to the north platform, then use a crushing ceiling as a bridge to the south ledge. Hop the spike to the opposite side for a chest with a Blue Rupee [[gf1-267-4, gf2-84-7]]. Step on the south switch to make a chest appear in the northeast, cross to it and open it for a **Small Key** [[gf1-267-4, gf2-84-8]].

Go back east past the blade traps to the narrow ledge, head north, use the Lens to see the platform past the guillotine, jump as it rises, then jump across the horizontally moving platform to the door [[gf1-267-5]].

#### Silver Rupee Room (invisible spikes)

Kill both Redeads; a chest appears with a Blue Rupee. Keep the Lens on to avoid the invisible spikes [[gf1-268-1]]. Collect all five Silver Rupees using the Longshot targets: the first in the middle behind the nearest spikes, the second by Longshotting the east target, the third at the west target, the fourth via the invisible ceiling target above the north ledge, and the fifth on the invisible northeast ledge via its wall target [[gf1-268-2]]. The west door opens.

#### Skull Room

Climb either staircase to above the large skull. Kill the four Keese, then toss a bomb into the hollow top; the skull explodes and reveals a **Small Key** [[gf1-269-1]]. Kill the Gold Skulltula behind the skull for its token (**Skulltula #87**) [[gf1-269-2, gf1-504-2, checklist-skulltulas-17-3]]. All Keese must die for the door to unseal [[gf1-269-1]].

Return to the Silver Rupee Room, Longshot the invisible ceiling target to the north platform, and go through the locked door [[gf1-269-3]].

#### The Fan Room

Wait for the first fan to stop before running past the blade traps in Kokiri Boots [[gf1-270-1]]. Around the corner, kill the Big Skulltula, then equip Iron Boots against the stronger fan and walk slowly [[gf1-270-2, gf1-270-3]]. At the pit, Longshot the opposite ledge while wearing Iron Boots, and drop off the far side [[gf1-270-4]]. Walk across the hall in Iron Boots, holding Z with your shield up to block the Silver Eye's flames [[gf1-270-5]]. At the last fan on the right wall, switch to Hover Boots; the fan blows you through a fake wall onto a ledge. Take the door [[gf1-270-6]].

#### The Other Gibdos Room and the Cursed Boat

Return to Kokiri Boots and kill the Gibdos while dodging blade traps. A chest gives a Blue Rupee [[gf1-271-1]]. Bomb the rubble in the southeast corner, use the Lens, and open the hidden chest for a **Small Key**. Use it on the north door [[gf1-271-2]].

In the boat room, push or pull the large block along its track into the trench near the ladder [[gf1-271-3]]. Climb the block and ladder to the Triforce symbol. From the boat's front, look southwest to the alcove with a Gold Skulltula. Longshot it directly, or play the Scarecrow's Song to bring Pierre to the alcove (**Skulltula #88**) [[gf1-271-4, gf1-504-4, checklist-skulltulas-17-4]]. The skulltula can also be reached by calling the scarecrow to the platform above where the block started and Longshotting it [[checklist-sidequests-34-3]].

Stand on the boat's Triforce symbol and play Zelda's Lullaby; the boat moves west [[gf1-271-5]]. Several waves of Stalfos attack; block their attacks or fight [[gf1-271-6]]. When the boat reaches the west side, jump off the south side immediately before it sinks [[gf1-271-7]]. Go through the west door.

#### Floor Master Room (invisible maze)

The room is a maze with invisible walls; use the Lens, and kill or avoid the Floor Masters [[gf1-272-1]]. Reach each door in turn.

- **North door, Crushing Walls Room:** Burn both spiked wooden walls with Din's Fire. Kill the Redead on the east and open the ornate chest for the **Boss Key**. The west chest holds a Blue Rupee [[gf1-272-2, gf1-272-3, gf1-272-4]].
- **South door, Invisible Floor Master Room:** Use the Lens to see the Floor Master. When it splits into three invisible small ones, Din's Fire kills all three. A chest appears with a **Small Key** [[gf1-273-1, gf1-273-2, gf1-273-3]].
- **West door, Triple Skull Room:** You need not destroy the spinning skulls (they give rupees). Shoot the Gold Skulltula on the wall behind them (**Skulltula #89**) [[gf1-274-1, gf1-274-2, gf1-505-2, checklist-skulltulas-17-5]].
- **East door:** Leads back toward the boat room [[gf1-274-3]].

#### The bridge and Geometric Shape Room

In the boat room's south section, a pit is too wide even for the Hover Boots. Shoot a Fire Arrow at a bomb flower around the tall pillar on the left of the opposite ledge; the pillar falls and forms a bridge. Cross and enter the south door [[gf1-275-1, gf3-72-5]].

In the next room, equip Hover Boots and use the Lens. Run to the T-shaped platform in front of the door, go left to the straight platform, then right to the next, then to the ledge with the Boss Door [[gf1-275-2]].

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave this room, set Farore's Wind and make sure you have plenty of arrows and magic; the Lens of Truth should be unequipped and out of use for the boss [[ra-comments-40-8, ra-comments-40-13]].

### Boss: Bongo Bongo

Drop down the pit in the middle of the room. The entire fight takes place on a giant drum [[gf1-275-3]]. Bongo Bongo attacks with his hands or tries to knock you off by pounding the drum [[gf1-521-3]]. Each attack deals at least two hearts [[gf2-86-1]].

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Do not use the Lens of Truth in this fight, and do not let his hands grab and trap you, or Invisible One will be missed [[ra-comments-40-1, ra-comments-40-2, gf4-29-2]]. If it fails, reset and use Farore's Wind [[ra-comments-40-8]].

**Standard strategy:** Z-target and shoot both hands with arrows to stun them. They recover quickly, so shoot fast. A struck hand turns blue and drops an arrow or magic [[gf1-275-5, gf2-86-2]]. Normally you would use the Lens between the hands to see the eye, but for the achievement you cannot [[gf1-275-6, ra-comments-40-1]].

**Achievement approach (no Lens):**
- After both hands are stunned, Bongo Bongo charges at you. Aim down the middle between the stunned hands and shoot, which hits his eye [[ra-comments-40-3, ra-comments-40-10, ra-guide-15-2]].
- Equip Hover Boots at the start to Z-target a hand, then unequip them to dodge the second hand if it becomes a fist [[ra-guide-15-2]]. One player failed every attempt with Hover Boots and succeeded first try with normal boots [[ra-comments-40-5]]. Choose what suits you, and Kokiri Boots are also recommended by the walkthrough [[gf1-275-4]].
- Taking the left hand first (on Link's right side) means you are bounced around less [[ra-comments-41-4]].
- Hold the bow after stunning both hands until they form fists, then fire straight ahead at the eye [[ra-comments-41-7]].
- Once he drops, Jump Attack with the Biggoron's Sword then crouch-stab to finish him in one cycle [[ra-guide-15-2, gf3-73-2]]. Two sets of swings with Biggoron's Sword kill him [[gf2-86-3]].
- Alternatives reported by players: charged spin attack when he rushes you [[ra-comments-41-5]]; uncharged spin attacks until he runs into you [[ra-comments-40-16]]; Din's Fire makes you ungrabbable but does not pause time for Bongo Bongo, and one player found it did not help [[ra-comments-40-6, ra-comments-40-7]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:49018]

After he dies, take the **Heart Container** [[gf1-275-8, gf3-73-3]]. Enter the beam of light to meet Impa, the Sage of Shadow, and receive the Shadow Medallion [[gf1-275-9, gf3-73-4]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:49019]

If the medallion achievement fails to trigger, a manual unlock can be requested on the RetroAchievements Discord or by DM to RAdmin [[ra-comments-42-1]]. You return to the Graveyard afterward [[gf3-73-4]].

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave, confirm you have the Map, Compass, Boss Key, Hover Boots and Gold Skulltulas #85 to #89. The route onward leads from the Graveyard via Hyrule Field to Gerudo Valley [[gf1-277-2]].
