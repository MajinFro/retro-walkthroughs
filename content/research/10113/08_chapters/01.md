## Chapter 1: Kokiri Forest and Inside the Deku Tree

[MERMAID]

This chapter covers the whole opening of the game. You gather the Kokiri Sword and Deku Shield so Mido lets you pass [[gf1-21-3, gf1-24-1]], buy the first Deku Stick upgrade in the Lost Woods [[gf1-417-1]], clear Inside the Deku Tree, and defeat Queen Gohma for the Heart Container and the Kokiri Emerald [[gf1-32-5, gf1-32-6]]. Three achievements belong here: Bunches of Twigs, Spider Net (missable) and Entrusted Mission.

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Spider Net asks you to defeat Queen Gohma before she can retreat to the ceiling [[gf1-32-3, gf3-14-3, ra-comments-5-1, ra-comments-5-2]]. Prepare before you open the boss door: have the Slingshot and several Deku Sticks ready. The recommended plan is in the Queen Gohma section below [[ra-guide-4-2]].

### Preparation

- Mido will not let you pass unless both the Kokiri Sword and the Deku Shield are **equipped** in the Equipment subscreen, not just owned [[gf1-524-1]].
- The sword is free, the shield costs 40 rupees, and the Deku Stick upgrade costs another 40 rupees, so plan on collecting 80 rupees [[gf1-21-4, gf1-21-5, gf1-417-1]].
- Deku Sticks matter for both the dungeon puzzles and the Spider Net fight [[ra-guide-4-2, ra-comments-5-4]].
- Switching Z-targeting from Toggle to Hold (option menu on the file select screen) makes the Gohma combo easier [[ra-comments-5-3, ra-comments-7-1]].

### Kokiri Forest

🗺️ **Map:** [View Kokiri Forest Map by kingofthisgame](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/1099-kokiri-forest-map)

#### Leaving the house and meeting Mido

1. Leave Link's tree house by the door in the northwest corner [[gf1-21-1]]. Saria approaches you on the porch [[gf1-21-2]].
2. Jump across the small pool of water to the large arch and speak with Mido. He refuses to let you pass until you have a sword and shield [[gf1-21-3]].

#### Getting the Kokiri Sword

1. Head to the crawl tunnel in the southwest corner of the forest, enter it by standing in front of it and pressing A, and crawl to the other side [[gf1-21-4, gf4-4-1]].
2. Go left and follow the path to the treasure chest while avoiding the rolling boulders [[gf4-4-1]]. Open the chest on the tree trunk for the **Kokiri Sword** and equip it from the Equipment submenu [[gf1-21-4, gf1-524-1]].

> [!TIP]
> Hiding in the small alcoves along the boulder path is the way to stay clear of the boulder [[gf1-21-6]].

#### Collecting rupees

You need 80 rupees in total: 40 for the shield and 40 for the Deku Stick upgrade [[gf1-21-5, gf1-417-1]]. Blue rupees are worth five rupees each [[gf2-6-5]].

- Along the boulder path, run left into the small alcove, take the blue rupee and slash the bush. Around the next corners take the blue rupee in the far alcove and slash its bush [[gf1-21-6]].
- Outside the tunnel, Z-target one of the rocks on the left and backflip (hold back and press A) repeatedly to the edge of the training ground, then repeat until you are awarded a blue rupee and a green rupee [[gf1-21-7]].
- Mido's House: open all his chests for 11 rupees and a Recovery Heart [[gf1-22-1]].
- Jump up to the ledge behind Mido's House for a blue rupee [[gf1-22-2]].
- Run across the platforms in the water for a blue rupee on reaching the opposite shore, then run back for another [[gf1-22-3]].
- The house in the southeast corner holds six rupees in pots, which respawn if you leave and re-enter [[gf1-22-4]].

#### Buying the Deku Shield

1. Enter the item shop in the northeast corner of the forest [[gf4-4-2]].
2. Speak to the clerk and buy the **Deku Shield** for 40 rupees. It is on the lower shelf on the right [[gf1-23-1, gf2-7-1, gf1-401-1]]. Equip it on the Equipment submenu [[gf1-524-1]].

> [!TIP]
> The Deku Shield is wooden and burns in fire, so avoid flames while holding it [[gf3-11-3]].

### Lost Woods

🗺️ **Map:** [View Lost Woods Map by EverythingZelda](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/n64/197771-the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time/map/54-lost-woods-map)

#### Bunches of Twigs

1. Head back to Mido's House in the northwest, climb onto the ledge behind it, climb the vine-covered wall, work around the ledge and enter the large hollow log into the Lost Woods [[gf1-23-2]].
2. From the first Lost Woods area, take the log on the left, then the hollow log in the left corner of the next area (two lefts). Ignore the two elevated logs. Jump off the ledge into the valley below and go to the Business Scrub at the back, next to the Gossip Stone [[checklist-sidequests-7-7, gf1-417-1]].
3. Defeat him: reflect his Deku Nut back with the Deku Shield, or use a jump attack [[gf1-23-3, gf2-20-3]].
4. Speak to him and pay 40 rupees. This raises your Deku Stick capacity to 20 and fills your sticks [[gf1-23-4, gf1-417-1]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48255]

5. Climb the ladder back to the top, go through the hollowed log, take another hollowed log to the right, then either log to return to Kokiri Forest [[gf1-23-5]].

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave the Lost Woods, note that the Skull Kid Heart Pieces and the Bullet Bag target are here too, but they need the Ocarina and songs and are not required for the Deku Tree [[checklist-heartpieces-3-1, gf1-46-1]].

### Passing Mido and entering the tree

1. Go to the east side of the forest, back across the pond, and speak with Mido again. With sword and shield equipped, he lets you pass [[gf1-24-1, gf1-524-1]].
2. Halfway along the passage, Deku Babas appear. They die in one sword hit and leave Deku Sticks [[gf1-24-2]].
3. Enter the mouth of the Great Deku Tree to begin the dungeon [[gf1-24-3]].

### Inside the Deku Tree

The dungeon centers on the Trunk, with four levels: Top Floor, Main Floor, Basement and Sub-Basement [[gf1-26-1]].

#### 1F: the Trunk (Dungeon Map)

1. Kill the Deku Babas in the corners. Hold R while Z-targeting to block their attacks, and take the Deku Sticks and Nuts they drop [[gf1-26-2]].
2. Reach the upper ledge by the ladder or the vines [[gf3-12-1]].
3. Open the chest partway along the ledge for the **Dungeon Map** [[gf1-26-3]].
4. Keep going right and enter the door [[gf1-26-4]].

#### Scrub Room #1

1. Z-target the Deku Scrub and reflect his Deku Nut back at him. When he runs, stand on his nest so he bumps into you [[gf1-27-1]].
2. He opens both doors and hints to press forward on the joystick when landing to avoid fall damage [[gf1-27-2]].
3. Go through the northeast door [[gf1-27-3]].

#### Slingshot Room

1. Jump onto the gray platform and quickly to the next ledge, since it falls. Open the chest for the **Fairy Slingshot** and Bullet Bag x30 [[gf1-27-4]].
2. Climb the ivy wall on the right and open the chest for a Recovery Heart [[gf1-27-5]].
3. Shoot the ladder hanging above the entrance door with the Slingshot, climb it, and go back through the door to Scrub Room #1 [[gf1-27-6]].

#### The Trunk: climbing to the Compass Room

1. Go back along the ledge. Shoot the three Skullwalltullas on the ivy wall with the Slingshot (one seed each) [[gf1-28-1, gf3-12-5]].
2. Climb the wall to the top, then left to the highest level, and keep going left to a door [[gf1-28-1]].

#### Compass Room (Gold Skulltula #8)

1. Step on the switch next to the lantern; three platforms rise. Jump along them to the back ledge and open the chest for the **Compass** [[gf1-28-2]].
2. Press the switch again and use the platforms to reach the small northern alcove. Jump past the Big Skulltula and open the chest for a Recovery Heart [[gf1-28-3]]. Big Skulltulas die when struck in the stomach [[gf3-12-6]].
3. Kill the Gold Skulltula in the back of the alcove for token **#8** [[gf1-28-4, checklist-skulltulas-4-1]].
4. Light a Deku Stick on the lantern and use it to light the burned-out lantern. The door unlocks [[gf1-28-5]]. Press A to put the stick away and save it once done [[gf3-13-2]].

#### The Trunk: breaking the web

1. Run right past the first Big Skulltula. Beat the second by Z-targeting it, waiting for it to spin, then using a jump attack [[gf1-29-1]].
2. Jump off the edge and land directly on the webbing on the first floor to break through to the sub-basement [[gf1-29-2]].

#### Basement wet room (Gold Skulltulas #9 and #10)

1. Shoot the Gold Skulltula on the eastern grating with the Slingshot, jump out to take the token, and climb back. This is token **#9** [[gf1-29-3, checklist-skulltulas-4-2]].
2. Face the long ivy wall on the south side, kill the second Gold Skulltula, and climb up to collect token **#10** [[gf1-29-4, checklist-skulltulas-4-3]].
3. Climb back to the southeast ledge and open the chest behind the lantern for a Recovery Heart [[gf1-29-5]].
4. Light a Deku Stick on the lantern, jump onto the shallow part of the pond, then to the southwest ledge. Run past the Deku Baba and burn the webs on the wall to reveal a door [[gf1-29-6]].

> [!NOTE]
> A fourth Gold Skulltula (#11) sits behind a bombable wall and needs Bombs and the Boomerang, so it is left for later [[checklist-skulltulas-4-4, gf1-484-2]].

#### Scrub Room #2

1. Deflect the Scrub's nuts back with the Deku Shield (the Slingshot does not work). Stand on his nest when he runs. He says "23 is number 1", a hint for the last puzzle [[gf1-30-1]].
2. Shoot the Silver Eye above the west door with the Slingshot to unlock it [[gf1-30-2]].

#### Trap Room

1. Swim to the left wall directly beneath the rotating spikes and hold A to dive onto the button. The water lowers [[gf1-30-3]].
2. Swim back, jump onto the floating platform and hold R to crouch under the spikes, then hop to the far ledge [[gf1-30-4]].
3. Kill the Big Skulltula, push the large stone block all the way right, and climb it to the door [[gf1-30-5]].

#### Torch Room and Small Nest

1. Kill the Deku Baba in the center, light a Deku Stick on the lit torch, and light the two unlit sconces. The north door opens [[gf1-31-1]].
2. Run circles in the center so the Gohma Larvae drop. Jump-attack each once, avoiding their charge when their eye turns red [[gf1-31-2]].
3. Light a stick at the north torches, burn the webbing in the south, and crawl through the tunnel back to the Trunk [[gf1-31-3]].

#### The Trunk: down to the boss

1. Run left and push the stone block along its path off the cliff. Jump down, relight a Deku Stick at the southeast torch, and climb back up via the block [[gf1-32-1]].
2. Roll over the floor webbing with the lit stick (run at it and press A). It burns, opening a hole to the lowest level [[gf1-32-2]].
3. Hit the three Deku Scrubs middle first, far right second, left last [[gf1-32-7, gf2-11-1]].
4. The door to the boss unlocks once all three vanish [[gf1-32-7]].

### Queen Gohma

> [!WARNING]
> **Missable Achievement Warning:** Spider Net is missable. The boss cannot be refought, so you must defeat Queen Gohma before she retreats to the ceiling [[gf1-32-3, gf3-14-3, ra-comments-5-1]]. Practice the combo on regular enemies earlier in the dungeon [[ra-comments-7-4]]. Carry spare Deku Sticks (commenters used between 3 and 5) [[ra-comments-5-4, ra-comments-7-7, ra-comments-8-2]].

**Setup.** One commenter suggests simply entering and looking up so she notices you [[ra-comments-7-5]]. Stored crouch-stab damage resets when you enter a different room, so a jump attack beforehand will not carry into the boss [[ra-comments-8-12]].

**One-rotation plan** (for **Spider Net**):

1. Z-target Gohma and shoot her in the eye with the Slingshot while her eye glows red. This stuns her [[gf1-32-3, gf2-118-3]]. The Slingshot stun lasts longer than a Deku Nut stun, so do not re-stun with nuts [[ra-comments-5-2, ra-comments-6-8]].
2. Switch to a Deku Stick and do a jump slash while still targeted. A Deku Stick jump attack deals 4 damage [[ra-comments-6-3, ra-comments-7-2]].
3. Release the target, hold R without locking on and press the attack button repeatedly to crouch-stab. The stab copies the previous attack's damage [[ra-comments-7-3, ra-comments-6-2]].
4. Alternatives reported by commenters: Slingshot then five Deku Sticks attacked quickly with no jump slash [[ra-comments-8-6, ra-comments-8-8]]; or Slingshot, stick jump slash, then mash the stick button three times [[ra-guide-4-2]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48057]

**If she escapes.** If Gohma reaches the ceiling and lays eggs, break each egg with one hit before it hatches [[gf2-11-3, gf3-14-2]]. Shooting her red eye on the ceiling drops her to the floor, stunned for a long time [[gf1-32-4, gf3-14-4]]. Spider Net asks for the kill before she retreats, so this fallback only finishes the boss [[gf1-32-3, ra-guide-4-2]].

### After the boss

1. Take the **Heart Container** and step into the blue light [[gf1-32-5, gf3-15-1]].
2. Outside, listen to the Great Deku Tree's story. He gives you the Kokiri Emerald and asks you to seek the Princess of Destiny at Hyrule Castle [[gf1-32-6]].

[ACHIEVEMENT:48074]

3. Back in the forest, Mido blames you for the Deku Tree's death and leaves [[gf1-34-1]]. Cross the lake and head to the western edge; exit through the hollow log [[gf1-34-2]].
4. On the bridge to Hyrule Field, Saria gives you the Fairy Ocarina [[gf1-34-3]].

> [!TIP]
> Before you leave the forest: Gold Skulltulas #8, #9 and #10 are in your pocket [[checklist-skulltulas-4-1, checklist-skulltulas-4-2, checklist-skulltulas-4-3]]. Others in Kokiri Forest need night or a bottle, which come later [[checklist-skulltulas-3-1, checklist-skulltulas-3-2]].
