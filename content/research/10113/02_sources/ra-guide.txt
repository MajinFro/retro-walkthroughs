# Introduction

Welcome to grand journey of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time RA guide, if you are a first time player wanting to learn the ropes without  ease or long time veteran seeking out forgotten relics of your past this guide should help you with your mastery! This guide will focus on obtaining every achievement, when to center your attention on missables and a 3 heart guide for new players worried about it for their mastery! 

This guide will be in split up into three sections:
1. 100% run
2. Collectable Roundup 
3. 3 Heart Challenge 

The guide will be geared for more the causal player experince, for the golden skullulas and heart pieces a checklist will added with a simple note how where/how to obtain the collectable. Those items you can find at your own pace and wouldn't stress on the 100% run. The guide will not help the players through puzzles or dungeons unless an achievement asks for it.  Follow this guide and you will be throwing Deku nuts at Ganon in no time with this guide! 

# Getting Started

It's recommended to do two playthroughs for the mastery, the first playthrough being a full 100% run focusing on collectables: Heart Pieces, Golden Skullulas, Dungeon Chests, etc. The second playthrough would be the 3 heart run, if a veteran you could do the whole game in one single playthrough. It is not recommended for causal players due to the pure backtracking you would have to do, More information about the 3 heart run below. 

Few helpful tips below: 
1. Make sure to use the saving to your advantage, any time you save in the main menu it will save any item you collect or key you grab in a dungeon. If you are dealing with a hard missable you can copy your save file and move it over to the two other slots and move on with your save file to come back. One you exit the game after saving the game will either put you at the start of the dungeon or:
- Young link: Link's House[Kokiri Forest] 
- Adult Link: Temple of Time 
2. If you are struggling with the game, the guide will help you become better equipped with items. Don't be afraid to use fairy's as an extra life system, using a fairy doesn't count towards the death count so the more bottles you have the more extra lives you are holding!  
3. Before going to a boss, save before you go into the boss room and use Farore's Wind to shorten runbacks.. If you failed the missable, reset the game and go back to the save to try again. Use the three save files to your advantage if you are stuck on a boss to take a break and come back to the missable if needed.

Rolling Thunder (10) [achievement 48417]
_Defeat Barinade without getting hit_

_Strong suggestion to turn your Z target lock in the main menu to "Hold", this will make this fight much easier. This one is going to be pretty tough, this achievement will be training for the 3 heart challenge going forward._

Stock up on Deku nuts and sticks, you can use Farore's Wind to start in the room before the boss fight to save you time if you are struggling. 

1. As soon as the Boss starts, roll forward twice and take a couple steps forward. Target the three tentacles up top with your boomerang hit one, side hop twice, hit the next one, then side hop back to your original position and hit the third. Let go of the Z target and immediately throw your boomerang to hit the boss in the center. If timed right the boss will be stunned, throw deku nuts to kill all the jellyfish. If you were too late, throw again and slash the boss's body to kill the jellyfish, throwing the boomerang every 3-4 hits to renew the stun.
2. As soon as you kill the last jellyfish whip out your boomerang and wait until just before the jellyfish are all out of the ground to throw it. If done correctly, the jellyfish will be vulnerable to kill, too early and the boss will go underground, giving you another chance. Too late and you'll need to run around the room hoping you don't get cornered. Throw the deku nuts to kill all the jellyfish, Congrats the hard part is over.
3.  Walk diagonally to keep close enough to the boss to hit it with boomerang. After hitting the boss, jump slash with deku sticks. The boss will burrow down into the ground keep hold on that target and walk around in a circle, or roll into it to make it get back up (this does not count as damage). Once the boss gets up try to hit it with a boomerang while still going in a circle. Rinse and repeat to win the fight. 

 - Why [M]? All bosses are Missable after defeated.

Biggoron's Sword (10) [achievement 48975]
_Obtain the Biggoron's Sword_

This is going to be a massive side quest, but the end results will be worth it especially for how early you are in the game. 

Requirements

- Free King Zora from the Red Ice. 
- Acquire Epona. [Need to jump over bridge] 

1. Talk to the Cucco Lady in Kakariko Village, she will give you a Pocket Egg. After one night passes, the egg will hatch. Inside the Kakariko Village is Malon's Father, use the Cucco to walk up Talon. Go back to Cucco Lady to receive a special blue Cucco

2. Head to Lost woods, go where you gave the skull mask to lost kid to find the Craftsman's son. He will give you an Odd mushroom, you then have three minutes to travel it the Potion Shop in Kakariko Village. 

3. The fastest way back is the go through the shortcut in Lost Woods that leads to Goron City. From there you can climb the stairs or hookshot the wooden beams going up to exit down Death Mountain Trail. Once you are all the way down to Kakariko Village, enter the shop to the left and go around the back. You will be in that hidden area where the cuccos will be, go to the other door around the corner to talk to show Granny the Odd Mushroom

4. Head back to the Lost Woods where the Carftsman's son was to find Fado, trade the Old Poultice to receive the Poacher's Saw.

5. Use Epona to travel to Gerudo Valley you will need to jump over the broken bridge. You can Talk to the Master Craftsman to trade your Poacher's Saw for Broken Goron's Sword

6. With the Broken Goron's Sword travel all the up Death Mountain to talk to the massive Goron. His eyes are irritated by the last eruption so he cannot fix your sword until he gets some Eye Drops from King Zora himself. Once you trade the broken sword, you will acquire the prescription to head over to Zora's Domain. 

7. Once you talked to King Zora and presented the prescription to make the Wolrd's Finest Eye Drops, the king will give you Eyeball Frog. You will have three minutes to deliver the Eyeball frog to the scientist in Lakeside Laboratory in Lake Hylia.

8. From King Zora, you want to travel to the shortcut at Zora's River that leads to Lost Woods. From there you can exit to Hyrule Castle grabbing epona, heading straight to Hyrule Lake. The laboratory is the first building you see, the scientist will then hand you the Eye Drops. Once you received the eye drops you have four minutes to climb back up to reach the big goron on Death Mountain. 

9. From Hyrule Lake, call epona to head back to Kokiri Forest and head to Lost Woods. Use the shortcut, heading to Goron City. You can either climb Death Mountain, or go through inside Death Mountain using your Goron Tunic. Once up top you will receive the claim check that makes you wait three games to get the Biggoron sword. If you go inside Death Mountain, play the song of sun six different times. It will fast forward three days, making easy claim for sword. 

Congratulations you have the best sword in the game, early game!    
   

Drunken Master (10) [achievement 48818]
_Lower Phantom Ganon to 1 HP by using only the Bow and Bottles_

[Missable] This challenge may seem difficult, but have no fear it really isn't bad once you realize you can get hurt, you will need the practice with the bottle for the actual fight later! 

1. Look on the floor you will see lines leading the corners, stand in the middle of the those corners by doing so you won't get hit by his attack if you miss the shot. Turn up the volume, you want will see two ganon's inside the painting coming towards you. Once you hear the footsteps coming closer to you, you know that is the real painting what ever is the loudest. You can also tell which is the fake Ganon by the color, the darker Ganon with the whiter whites is the real one. Wait until the portal appears then shoot your arrow, rinse and repeat three times. 
2. Make sure you have an empty bottle equipped and it's easier to hit the ball than you realize. May take a little practice to understand the timing, the bottle has a shorter swing and reach than the master sword, but lingers longer than you would expect. Keep on hitting the boss with the empty bottle until  the boss reaches 1 hp to get your achievement.

 - Why [M]? All bosses are Missable after defeated.

4. The third phase you have to wait for the rocks to stop falling, watch the shadows on the ground and run the opposite direction. Hanging off the edge does not skip this attack.
5. Should be the final stage, same as step 1 and 3.

Invisible One (10) [achievement 49018]
_Defeat Bongo Bongo without using the Lens of Truth or getting trapped by his hands_

[Missable] Set up Farore's Wind to save massive time on the walk back if you fail. The Target to stun Bongo Bongo is in between the hands after shooting both.  

1. Wear the Hover Boots as soon as the boss starts Z target on the hand from left or right side. Once one hand is down, unequip hover boots to be able to dodge the second hand if it turns into a fist. If it's not a fist you can interrupt its attack by shooting it. Now line up your shot and get ready for the boss. 
2. The boss will slam itself on the ground charge forward, I normally shoot just as the hands are about to reach me/when they are right next to me. 
3. Jump slash with biggoron sword then crouch stab it to death to one-cycle the boss. If you missed the one cycle, just repeat the same actions again until the boss is dead. 

 - Why [M]? All bosses are Missable after defeated.

Poe Collector (10) [achievement 48821]
_Catch all 10 Big Poes and obtain your reward_

Big Poes spawn day/night and are easier to find if you are on epona. Use this map as a guide to mark off the big poes, you need to turn in all ten to get the fourth bottle. You can stand in the same spawn spot and wait for them to respawn over and over if you miss the kill.

1. At the entrance of Hyrule Castle, at the Y split in the road. 
2. Next to Lon Lon Ranch entrance, right side next to to tree. 
3. Left side of Hyrule Castle next to river, by a tree. 
4. Next to Gerudo Valley Entrance, ground level. Left side, start of road leading to Lon Lon Ranch.  
5. Next to Gerudo Valley Entrance, Ground level. Right side, Start of road leading to Lake Hylia
6. At the end of the road, south side Lake Hylia. In between two trees leading to the Castle. 
7. Next to #6 in the tree's south side, pass by the tree on left side. 
8. Next to Lon Lon Ranch on right side, at the Y split in the road. 
9. In between # 1 and # 8, on the ground level near the cliff.
10. Near the entrance of Kakariko Village, ground level walk near water to trigger.

Accomplished Angler (10) [achievement 48945]
_Catch the rare Hyrule Loach_

The rare Hyrule Loach is tricky, but you can do a major trick to make this very easy. 

1. Enter the pond and check the lily pads to the left if Loach is there. [Long big fish]
2. If fish is not there go outside, play song of sun re enter. The Loach appears every 4th time. 
3. Once you fish in lily pads, go to Water Temple and save the game. 
4. Keep on trying to catch the Hyrule Loach near the lily pads, if you you fail first try rest save. 
   
Notes: 
- You have to pay to find out if the loach is near the lily pads so save before to not waste rupees
- If the fish bits then gets off the hook, you won't be able to try again for 15 mins. Reset to save time.

Equip the hover boots as soon as possible and walk over to the corner. You can get hit, just make sure you always have the boots equipped so you don't fall down the ledge. It's really not too hard just make sure you hit him with a light arrow before you walk over to the other side when attacking ganon.

    

My Last Breath (10) [achievement 49291]
_Defeat Ganon with 1 heart or less remaining without using Fairies or Bombs_

At this point you should have more than enough hearts, maybe even maxed out twenty hearts. You should have the amour defense boost and be well equipped to where you don't even need fairies. It's okay to get hurt on the fight. This is the easiest fight known to zelda, all you do is roll right before ganon is about to swing in between his legs. Attack his tail , rinse and repeat. If you wanted to only use deku nuts as flex you could. The biggest take away is rolling right before ganon swings.

# 3 Heart Challenge 

The main advice that you really can take is after you 100% the game, you should gained a knowledge of where to go or what to be aware of. You need bottles as soon as possible to stock up on fairies. Really the biggest take away from this challenge is go at your own pace. Save often and don't be afraid to get as many bottles or upgrades as soon as possible to help you along your three heart challenge. If you pick up a heart piece it's okay, just don't pick up the 4th heart piece to increase your health further.
