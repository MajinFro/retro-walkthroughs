Intro [0001]
This is a condensed walkthrough that allows the reader to see, at a glance, 
all the items he is able to get at any time.  It also shows the general 
locations for each and every item, then specific instructions for exactly how 
to get them.  Also included in this walkthrough are the specific methods for 
getting through the dungeons as fast as possible.  If you're starting to read
this in the middle of your game, be sure to skim the parts of the guide that 
follow the earlier parts of the game to make sure you didn't miss any items 
so far.  The walkthrough follows this general outline:  

a. (Items available before first dungeon)
- Get <first item> <location>
Instructions of exactly how to get the item
- Get <next item> <location>
...
1. (first dungeon)
Dungeon notes: (General things to know about the dungeon)
Step-by-step dungeon walkthrough: 
To the compass
To the map
To the secret item
To the boss
b. (Items available after the first dungeon)
etc. 



TIMELINE [0002]
Early in the timeline, supposedly soon after The Minish Cap.  At 
the end of the game the series is split into two timelines, one following the 
Link sent back in time and the other following the original timeline.  

STORYLINE [0003]
A LONG TIME AGO...

Before life began, before the world had formed, three golden goddesses 
descended upon the chaotic land of Hyrule; they were Din, the goddess of 
power, Nayru, the goddess of wisdom, and Farore, the goddess of courage.

Din, with her strong flaming arms, cultivated the land to create the earth.  
Nayru poured her wisdom onto the earth to give the spirit of law to the 
world.  Farore's rich soul created all life forms who would uphold the law.

These three great goddesses returned to the Heavens, leaving behind the 
Golden Sacred Triforce.  Since then, the Triforce has become the basis for 
Hyrule's providence; where the Triforce stood became sacred land.  

In the vast, deep forest of Hyrule, the Great Deku Tree served as the 
guardian spirit.  The children of the forest, the Kokiri, lived with the 
Great Deku Tree.  Each Kokiri had his or her own guardian fairy, except one.  
His name was Link.

Early one morning, Link was having a nightmare.  It was the same 
nightmare he had every night.  During a storm, Link would find himself 
standing in front of a mysterious castle.  A rider on horseback, carrying a
girl, would race by.  The girl would look at Link as if to say something.  
Then, another rider would appear.  This big man clad in black would look down 
menacingly at Link.  Link would then awaken.

"Link!  Hey, get up, Link!  The Great Deku Tree wants to talk to you!"

As Link opened his eyes, he saw a fairy floating in front of him.  This 
fairy's name was Navi.  Navi was sent to summon Link to the Great Deku Tree.

"Oh Navi, thou hast returned!" said the Deku Tree.  "Thank you, Link, 
for coming.  Thy slumber these past moons must have been restless, and full 
of nightmares.  A vile climate pervades this world.  Verily, ye have felt it.  
The time has come to test thine courage.  I have been cursed.  I need you to 
dispel the curse with your wisdom and courage.  Art thou prepared?"

Link entered the Great Deku Tree and broke the curse.

"Well done, Link!  I knew that ye were worthy of carrying out my 
wishes.  A wicked man of the desert cast this dreadful curse on me.  
Employing his vile, sorcerous energies, the evil one is searching for the 
Sacred Realm connected to Hyrule.  For it is there that one will find the 
divine relic.  The Triforce, that contains the essence of the gods.  Whoever 
holds the Triforce can make their wishes come true.  Thou must never allow 
the desert man to lay his hands on the Sacred Triforce.  Thou must never 
suffer that man to enter the Sacred Realm of Legend.  Link, go now to Hyrule
Castle.  There, ye will surely meet the Princess of Destiny.  Present this 
stone to the Princess.  I have foreseen that she will understand everything."

The Great Deku Tree gave Link the Spiritual Stone of the Forest.  Before 
dying, the Deku Tree's last words were, "The future depends on thee, Link.  
Thou art courageous."

COMMENTS [0004]
Ocarina of Time is the quintessential Zelda game, the one that all 
other Zelda games are compared to.  It took the series to a new dimension of 
3D and pulled it off brilliantly.  It gained a reputation of having an 
unlimited number of secrets and collectable items in a vast world.  It 
introduced the famous Zelda "tri-race" that was to occupy succeeding games: 
The forest Deku, the mountainous Gorons, and the river-dwelling Zora.  It's 
a game divided into two parts: A Young Link and an Adult Link.  But most 
importantly, it contains the classic Zelda story: Link, the child, grows up 
in the process of going through his adventures, collects the Master Sword, 
defeats the evil Ganon, and saves the princess, helping the other various 
races as he goes along.

Again, the items are arranged in the order of the soonest that you can 
get them.  In this game's case, this often means having to trudge long 
distances over the Hyrule terrain from place to place, often revisiting areas
a lot.  If you want, you can collect items in chunks by waiting until you've 
advanced further to collect more items at once, thereby saving time.  It's 
usually best to get the items as early as possible, however.

The biggest feature of OoT is the time transfer from Young Link to 
Adult Link.   After you beat the Forest Temple, the first dungeon of the 
Adult Link Quest, you have free access to switch between Young and Adult Link 
as much as you like.  There are many environmental changes between the Young 
and Adult Hyrules; in the Young Link time frame, the Market is open and 
active, and there are generally fewer problems with the environment because 
Ganondorf has not come to power yet.  In the Adult Link time frame, however, 
the Market is destroyed, Zora's Domain is frozen over, and a sense of doom 
ravages the land.  Sometimes you may have to travel between times to 
accomplish certain puzzles, like in laying Magic Beans as Young Link that 
don't form into plants until Adult Link's time.

The most difficult and time-consuming part of doing a 100% run is 
collecting the 100 Skulltula Tokens.  Skulltulas are found virtually 
everywhere, and besides the occasional secret rooms in the dungeons, aren't 
very well hidden.  Nevertheless, the last 20-25 Skulltula Tokens are really 
tough to hunt down without some help.  Skulltulas are usually only found 
crawling around at night, although you can get the ones in the soil, in 
dungeons and in buildings, at any time.  To find the Skulltulas in the 
patches of soil, catch some bugs from under the rocks and plop them on the 
soil.  You'll hear the sound of their rattling skin as you get close to them, 
tipping you off to their location.  Most of the tokens require the use of the 
Boomerang or Hookshot to snag; you get the Boomerang after the third dungeon 
of the Young Link Quest.  Once you gain the Stone of Agony, it gives you 
clues as to when you've come across a secret; open up the secrets to find 
Skulltulas, Heart Pieces, and more.  Fortunately, there is no point at which 
certain items become unavailable; you can wait until right before you beat 
Ganon to collect all the items if you wish!  

In OoT and every Zelda game after it, Nintendo has included lots of 
minigames that must be done in order to have 100% completion.  These 
minigames are mostly just tests of your skills with the Bow in OoT, so you'll 
have to work on your reflexes and aiming more than in any previous Zelda 
game.  Often you get free retries if you get close to perfect scores, so 
that's a bonus.  The easiest way to get rupees to play the minigames and 
purchase items is to break all the pots in the room at the entrance to the 
Market as Young Link; you can rack up 100 rupees pretty easily that way.

The dungeons are an interesting mix; the first two dungeons aren't too 
difficult, but in Jabu Jabu's Belly you have to escort a Zora Princess 
through the dungeon to help you.  Some dungeons, especially the Water Temple, 
are very confusing.  Make notes on which door leads where if you need to.  
Fortunately, you can never get stuck by opening the wrong door with a key, 
so don't worry about that.  

In the Master Quest version of Ocarina of Time for the NGC, everything 
is the same except for the dungeons.  The only difference between it and the 
normal OoT is that the dungeons are more difficult; there are no extra items 
or upgrades to get.

- Get Kokiri Sword (Kokiri Forest)
Exit Link's house, and hop down.  Go up the ledge to the left, to the 
southwest corner of the Kokiri Forest.  Enter the tiny hole here and crawl
through it.  Go left, and follow the path to the treasure chest while avoiding
the boulders.  It contains the Kokiri Sword.

- Purchase Deku Shield (40 rupees) (Kokiri Shop)

until you have 40.  Then, enter the item shop in the northeast corner of this

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~Deku Tree [0102]~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~




Get Deku Sticks
Get Deku Nuts
Get Deku Seeds
Get Fairy Slingshot
Get 3 Skulltula Tokens (#1-#3)
Defeat Gohma
Get Kokiri Emerald and Heart

To the dungeon: On the east side of the area of the forest, you can 
get past the Kokiri blocking your path if you equip your sword and shield.  
Go past him to the east, talk to the Deku Tree, and enter the first dungeon.

Dungeon notes: The first dungeon of the game is straightforward enough.  
The main trunk takes up the top three floors, and single rooms branch off
of them.  Eventually you'll get to the bottom floors past the web, where 
it's a simple way through to the boss.  Nevertheless, this dungeon 
introduces standard puzzles that will become commonplace later on.  Link gets
the Fairy Slingshot in this dungeon, which is the most basic projectile 
weapon in the game.  To defeat the Gohma boss, first locate it on the 
ceiling.  Shoot at its eye with the Slingshot to stun it, then slice it up
with your sword.  Repeat until it dies.

To the map:
- Go forward and climb up the ladder to the left to the 2nd floor.
- Go around the perimeter of the ledge and get the map from the chest.

To the secret item:
- Continue around the ledges and go to the next room.
- Jump across the platform and get the Fairy Slingshot from the chest.

To the compass: 
- Shoot the ladder overhanging from the ceiling with the slingshot, then
  go back to the previous room.
- Shoot down the Skulltulas, then climb up the vines to the 3rd floor.
- Go around the ledge and through the door.
- Step on the switch, jump to the first platform, then jump left to the 
  opening in the wall, kill the Gold Skulltula (#1) and get the token.
- Step on the switch and jump to the ledge on the opposite side, then get
  the compass from the chest.

To the boss:
- Light the torch with a deku stick to go back to the previous room.
- Jump off one of the ledges on the 3rd floor and break through the web on
  the bottom to land in the B1 floor.
- Use the Slingshot to kill the Gold Skulltula (#2) on the vines, then 
  climb up to get the token.
- Find the Gold Skulltula (#3) on the metal grating, kill it, then jump 
  from the nearby ledge to get the token.
- Light the torch with a Deku Stick, jump into the shallow water, then go
  south and burn up the web with the flame, and go to the next room.
- Defeat the Deku Scrub, shoot the eye with the Slingshot and go through.
- Get in the water and dive down to press the switch, get onto the platform 
  and ride it to the other side, push the block, climb up on it and go to
  the next room.
- Light the torches and continue on, then burn up the eastern web and crawl
  through it to a previous room.
- Push the block into the water, light a deku stick from the opposite 
  platform, jump back across the block and roll on the web to burn it up, 
  then drop down it to the B2 floor.
- Hit the middle Deku, then the right-hand one, then the left-hand one, and
  go to the next room to get to the boss.

- Get Fairy's Ocarina (Kokiri Forest)
When you leave Kokiri Forest to the west, Saria will give this to you.

- Get Skulltula Token #4 (Market)
When you reach Hyrule Field, go all the way to the north end of it and go
across the drawbridge to the town area.  Enter the first door to your left
to reach a pot storehouse.  There are many rupees in the pots here.  Roll
into the box at the far end to find a Gold Skulltula.

- Purchase Hylian Shield (80 rupees) (Market)
Enter the Bazaar at the southeast corner of the market, and pay the money
to get this upgraded shield.

- Get Weird Egg (Hyrule Castle)
Exit the market to the north to reach the Hyrule Castle area.  Talk to the
girl called Malon next to the vines, and she will give you the egg.  This
will hatch into a chicken soon.

- Get Skulltula Token #5 (Hyrule Castle)
There is a lone tree right nearby Malon; roll into it and a Skulltula will
pop out for you to kill.

- Get Zelda's Letter (Hyrule Castle)
Climb the vines nearby Malon, go forward and jump down past the large gates.
Go up the slope to your left, being careful to avoid all the guards.  When you
get to the northwest corner of the field, climb the ladder, and hop down 
north into the castle moat.  Swim along it a little bit and get out at the 
shallow part, and you will find Malon's father at the end of the path.  Wake
him up with the chicken, and he will run off.  Push the two large crates into
place, on top of each other, to make the little crawlspace on the castle
wall accessible.  Jump to it and crawl through to enter the castle area 
itself.  Make your way west, past all the patrolling guards, until you get to
Zelda in the courtyard.  Talk to her and she'll give you this letter.

- Learn "Zelda's Lullaby" (Hyrule Castle)
When you talk to Impa as you leave, she will teach you this song.

- Learn "Epona's Song" (Lon Lon Ranch)
Enter Lon Lon Ranch, which is near the center of Hyrule Field.  Go to the 
central ringed area, where you will find Malon singing with her horses.  Talk
to her, then pull out your Fairy Ocarina and she will teach you this song.

- Get Heart Piece #1 (Lon Lon Ranch) 
In the southwest corner of the ranch, there is a small building you can 
enter.  Pull the crates aside to reveal a crawlspace in the northwest corner.  
Go through it to get the Heart Piece on the other side.

- Get Bottle #1 (Lon Lon Ranch)
Enter the house to the left of the entrance to find Talon again.  Talk to him
to play a Super Cucco hunting game for 10 rupees.  Locate the three Super 
Cuccos in the room within the time limit to get your first Bottle.

- Get Skulltula Token #6 (Lon Lon Ranch)
There is a lone tree in-between the house and the corral area.  Roll into it
to make a Skulltula pop out.

- Get Skulltula Token #7 (Lon Lon Ranch) 
On the far south side of the corral, this Skulltula comes out at night on the
wooded post.  Kill it and get the token.

- Get Skulltula Token #8 (Kokiri Forest)
Go back to Kokiri Forest.  On the back of the Know-it-all Brother's house, 
which is next to the Kokiri Sword maze entrance, is a Skulltula that appears
at night.  

- Learn "Saria's Song" (Lost Woods)
Enter the Lost Woods, which is up the ledges on the north side of Kokiri 
Forest.  From the entrance, go east, then west, then east, then west, then 
forward, then west, then east to get to the Sacred Forest Meadow.  Kill the
wolf to unblock the rest of the meadow.  Make your way through the linear 
maze, avoiding the Deku Scrubs, and up through the narrow path until you 
reach a clearing.  Saria is waiting here, and she teaches you her song.

- Get Heart Piece #2 (Lost Woods)
From the entrance to the Lost Woods, go west.  Step on the stump and play 
Saria's Song, and the Skull Kid gives you a Heart Piece.

- Get 20 Capacity Deku Stick Holder (40 rupees) (Lost Woods)
From the last Heart Piece, go south.  Drop down and go past the bridge, then
defeat the Deku Scrub.  He sells you the Deku Stick upgrade for 40 rupees.

- Get 40 Capacity Deku Seed Holder (Lost Woods)
From the Lost Woods entrance, go east.  Shoot the bulls-eye plate three times
with the Slingshot to get this Deku Seed upgrade.

- Get Heart Piece #3 (Lost Woods)
Drop down the ledge here, then go south.  Stand on the stump, and play the 
Ocarina for the Skull Kids.  Follow the tunes they play, and do it three 
times correctly to get a Heart Piece.

- Get Bottle #2 (Kakariko Village)
To reach Kakariko Village from Hyrule Field, it's in the northeast corner.  
Go across the bridge east from the market, and up the long stairway to enter 
the village.  Talk to the Cucco Lady in the south portion of the village- you 
need to find all seven of her chickens and dump them into her fenced area.  
The first chicken is right nearby, near the Graveyard entrance.  The second
chicken is next to the exit back to Hyrule Field.  The third chicken is 
hiding in a box next to one of the stairways- roll into it to get it out.  The
fourth chicken is up the stairs, near the exit to Death Moutain.  Grab that
chicken and jump off the red roof nearby, to float down to the fifth chicken,
on a ledge next to the Skulltula House.  To get the sixth chicken, grab one 
and go east up the stairs on the east side of the village, throw the chicken 
on the box, climb up and grab it, then jump to the fence.  Carefully jump off 
the end of the fence with the chicken so you can float over the huge brown 
gate.  Throw both chickens back over the gate.  Now in this gated-off area, 
climb up the ladder to find the final chicken.  When all the chickens are 
returned, the lady will give you a Bottle as a reward.

- Get Heart Piece #4 (Graveyard)
At night, go to the Graveyard, southeast from Kakariko Village.  Talk to Dampe
and play his digging game.  Pay him 10 rupees at any spot and he will dig a 
hole.  There is a Heart Piece in one of the spots he can dig.

- Learn "Sun's Song" (Graveyard)
Go to the far east, top-most ledge of the Graveyard, and play Zelda's Lullaby
next to the huge gravestone.  It will disappear and you can jump in the hole.
Kill the bats and go to the next room, then carefully go to the next room,
avoiding the zombies.  Examine the plaque at the end of the path to learn this
song; you can now switch between day and night at any time.

- Get Heart Piece #5 (Graveyard)
On the ledge just south of where the huge gravestone was, pull aside the small
gravestones on the left-hand side.  One of them hides a secret hole; jump 
inside it.  Kill the mummy inside, then play the Song of Soul and get the 
Heart Piece from the chest that appears.

- Get Skulltula Token #9 (Graveyard)
Catch a bug from under a rock with a Bottle, and go up on the ledge to the 
northwest corner of the Graveyard.  Lay a bug on the soft soil, and a Gold 
Skulltula pops out.

- Get Skulltula Token #10 (Kakariko Village)
Roll into the tree just past the entrance from Hyrule Field to make this Gold
Skulltula fall out.

- Get Adult Wallet (House of Skulltula)
Once you have 10 Skulltula Tokens, enter the House of Skulltula just right of
the entrance from Hyrule Field.  The man inside will give you the Adult 
Wallet, which lets you hold up to 200 rupees.

- Get Skulltula Token #11 (Kakariko Village)
At night, go up to the tall watchtower in the village.  Look up and shoot the
Gold Skulltula, then climb up to get the token.

- Get Skulltula Token #12 (Kakariko Village)
This Skulltula is on the back of the house just to the left of the exit that
leads to Death Mountain.

- Get Skulltula Token #13 (Kakariko Village)
Examine the building under construction in the center of the village.  A 
Skulltula is behind one of the brick walls.

- Get Skulltula Token #14 (Kakariko Village) 
Look at the back of the House of Skulltula to find this one.

- Get 50 Capacity Deku Seed Holder (Market)	
Leave Kakariko Village and go to the Market area.  In the northwest corner of
the market, enter the Archery Game building.  Win the game to get this 
upgrade for the Slingshot.

- Get Heart Piece #6 (Market)		
Inside the market, play the Sun Song to make it nighttime.  In the main area,
there are a lot of dogs hopping around; approach the white-colored dog so
that it starts to follow you.  Now enter the back alley of the market to the
southwest, and enter the second door.  The woman inside will give you the 
Heart Piece for finding her dog.    			  
  
- Get Keaton Mask (Market)
The Happy Mask Shop in the market opens up after you have spoken to the man
guarding the gate to Death Mountain and given him Zelda's letter.  Inside, 
talk to the store owner to get the Keaton Mask, the first mask.  To sell the
mask, give it to the man guarding the gate in Kakariko Village. 

- Get Skull Mask (Market)
After selling the Keaton Mask, the Skull Mask is available in the Happy Mask
Shop.  To sell it, enter the Lost Woods from Kokiri Forest.  Go west from the
entrance and talk to the Skull Kid with the mask on.  The Spooky Mask is now
available from the Happy Mask Shop.

- Get 30 Capacity Deku Stick Holder (Lost Woods)
From the Lost Woods entrance, go east, west, east, west, then west again.  
Drop into the hole in the tall grass near the butterflies.  Wear the Skull 
Mask, then step forward in this secret forest stage.  The Deku Scrubs will 
sprout out of the ground and give you this Deku Stick upgrade.

- Get Skulltula Token #15 (Kokiri Forest)
Get a bug from under a rock and catch it with your bottle, then go over behind
the item shop in the Kokiri Forest.  Lay the bug onto the soft soil here to
make a Skulltula pop out.

- Get Skulltula Token #16 (Lost Woods)
From the Lost Woods entrance, go west, and west again to the bridge area.  Lay
a bug at the soft soil at the ledge to reveal a Skulltula.

- Get Skulltula Token #17 (Lost Woods)
At the Lost Woods screen with the two Deku Scrubs shooting at you, there is a
soft soil spot.  Use another bug here to fetch out a Skulltula.

- Get Spooky Mask (Market)
Get the Spooky Mask from the Happy Mask Shop.  To sell it, go to the Graveyard
from Kakariko Village during the day, and give this to the boy there.

- Get Bunny Hood (Market)
You can get the Bunny Hood from the Happy Mask Shop now, but you can't sell it
until later on.

- Get Heart Piece #7 (Fishing Pond)
In Hyrule Field, go all the way to the southwest corner.  Climb the ladder on
the stone wall and go past the fence to get to Lake Hylia.  Go left from the 
entrance and across the water, then enter the house to get to the Fishing Pond
area.  If you can catch a big fish (about 10 pounds), the owner will give you
a Heart Piece.  The big fish usually lurks between the log poking out in the
middle of the pond and the three sticks nearby.  Stand on that log to have the
best spot to cast and reel in the fishes.

- Get Skulltula Token #18 (Lake Hylia)
The Scientist's Lab is to the right of the entrance from Hyrule Field.  Use a
bug on the soft soil next to the lab.  While you are in this area, there is a
scarecrow patch in between the lab and the fishing pond.  Play a song with 
your Ocarina in front of the dancing scarecrow named Banooru, and remember 
the notes you used; it will become important later.

- Get Skulltula Token #19 (Lake Hylia)
Go all the way across both bridges, then swim to the small platform out deep
in the lake.  Kill the Gold Skulltula there.

- Get Skulltula Token #20 (Zora's River)
In Hyrule Field, go south from the entrance to Kakariko Village, along the 
river, until you can go east to a new area, which is Zora's River.  Roll into
the tree near the entrance to make a Gold Skulltula fall out.

- Get Stone of Agony (House of Skulltula)
Enter the House of Skulltula in Kakariko Village again once you have 20 
Skulltula Tokens, and the newly uncursed man inside gives you this item, 
which alerts you when a secret is nearby.

- Get Heart Piece #8 (Gerudo Valley)
Enter Gerudo Valley, which is west from Hyrule Field.  Near the impassable
bridge, grab the chicken.  Jump off to the right and float down, through the
giant waterfall, into a tiny nook in the wall.  Get the Heart Piece here.

- Get Skulltula Token #21 (Gerudo Valley)
Jump into the water from the last Heart Piece, then climb up on the bottom
ledge of the valley.  Use a bottled bug on the soft soil spot here to dig up
a Gold Skulltula.

- Get Heart Piece #9 (Gerudo Valley)
Grab the chicken again, and jump off the cliff to the left.  Float to the 
tiny ledge with the crate on the opposite side of the valley.  Roll into the 
crate and get the Heart Piece.

- Get Power Bracelet (Goron City)
Go past the Kakariko gates to enter Death Mountain.  Go all the way up the
ledges, past the big boulder blocking Dodongo's Cavern.  Follow the path to
the right of the area with all the smaller boulders, and go forward until 
you enter Goron City.  Drop down to the bottom level of the city, and play
Zelda's Lullaby in front of the big door slab.  Go inside to find Darunia,
leader of the Gorons.  Play Saria's Song for him, and he will give you the
Power Bracelet.

- Get Skulltula Token #22 (Death Mountain)
Go back outside Goron City from the entrance you came in, and go through the

To the dungeon: Play the Serenade of Water to warp to Lake Hylia, and drop

Dungeon notes: The OoT Water Temple has a solid reputation of being one of the
most confusing and difficult dungeons in the entire franchise.  The key to
getting through it, however, is simple: remember where all the water-level
stations are, and figure out where all the hidden keys are.  The first station
you find lowers the water level to the bottom.  The station in the middle 
column of the big room raises the water level to the middle.  The station 
accessible from the west door of the second floor raises the water level to
the top.  The big room in the middle is connected to many rooms, most of 
which lead off to a key.  Make sure you explore every corridor of every floor,
and remember that changing the water level can give access to many new areas.
The mini-boss guarding the dungeon item is Dark Link, and he is easily
defeatable with the sword.  The dungeon item is the Longshot, which is simply
a hookshot that reaches farther.  To defeat the boss, Morpha, stand on the
edges and target the pink orb, avoid the arm that thrusts out of the water
and hookshot the orb, then slice it with your sword.
NOTE: PLEASE keep in mind that it is impossible to get permanently stuck in
this dungeon!  If you get stuck and the water level needs to be lowered or 
raised, just go back to where the water-leveling Triforce stations are- I've
explained their locations above.

Longshot over to the man sitting on the roof of one of the houses in the 
village, and he will give you a Heart Piece.

- Get Heart Piece #35 (Graveyard)
Enter the graveyard and race Dampe's Spirit again inside one of the headstone
holes.  At the end, use the Longshot to get across the long gap and reach the
finish in under a minute to get a Heart Piece.



~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~Shadow Temple [0208]~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~




Get Hover Boots
Get 5 Skulltula Tokens (#86-#90)
Defeat Bongo Bongo 
Get Shadow Medallion and Heart

To the dungeon: Play the Nocturne of Shadow, then go down the stairs to a 
small chamber.  Use Din's Fire to light all the torches and unlock the
entrance.

Dungeon notes: The Shadow Temple is a fairly linear dungeon, unlike the tricky
and winding mazes of the Forest Temple and the Water Temple.  You will get the
Hover Boots in this dungeon, although the main item of importance here is the
Lens of Truth, which you receive in the Bottom of the Well.  Use the Lens of
Truth to reveal many invisible platforms, doorways, and other objects as you
make your way through.  With the Hover Boots, Link can hover in the air for a
couple seconds to reach otherwise unreachable platforms across from him.  Link
will use the Iron Boots and the Hover Boots in tandem through several puzzles
in this dungeon.  Most of the enemies are ones that you've faced already: 
Skulltulas, bats, Wall Masters, Like-Likes, and so on- and you're stronger 
than you were before.  When you reach the boss, first shoot the hands with 
arrows to stun them, then use the Lens of Truth to find the central eye in the
head.  Shoot that with an arrow, then slice it up with your sword.

To the map:
- Go forward and hookshot across, then go through the fake wall.
- Use the Lens of Truth and go through the fake door to the northwest.
- Use the Lens of Truth to find a passage to the right; enter and get the map
  from the chest.

To the secret item:
- Go back and go through another fake wall to the west.
- Find the door to the west and go through, kill the Dead Hand and get the
  Hover Boots from the chest.

To the compass:
- Go back through the fake walls to the large room.
- Push the bird statue so that it faces the southwest fake door (the door that
  doesn't disappear when you use the eye of truth), then use the Hover Boots to
  run across to the gate and go through.
- Go through the left-hand door.
- Get all the silver rupees and get the key, then go back to the previous 
  room.
- Go through the fake wall on the opposite side, kill the enemies and get the
  compass from the chest.

To the boss: 
- Go back, then go east through the locked door.
- Go all the way down the passage, past the guillotines, to the platform with
  a Stalfo, then turn left and jump across the invisible platforms and 
  through the door at the end.
- Kill all the enemies, then kill the Gold Skulltula (#1) in the room beyond.
- Go back to the previous room, to the platform with the Stalfo.
- Use the Hover Boots to get to the falling platform and jump off, kill the 
  Beamos and collect the silver rupees, then go through the unlocked door.
- Use the Lens of Truth to spot a crate, then pull it out.  Push and pull it
  across the falling spike traps to the end of the room.  Enter the cage on
  the right, and kill the Gold Skulltula (#2).
- Climb up the block at the end to the top, jump to the right and step on the
  switch, then jump back over to the chest and get the key from it.
- Go back to the previous room; on this platform, go forward and turn left,
  then jump across the invisible platforms and go through the locked door.
- Collect all the silver rupees using the Lens of Truth and the Longshot, 
  then go through the unlocked door.
- Kill the Gold Skulltula (#3) behind the giant statue.
- Climb up and throw a bomb into the statue to get a key, then go back to the
  previous room.
- Hookshot up and go through the locked door.
- Use the Iron Boots to deter the fans and the Hover Boots to cross the pit
  along the passage, then drop down to a narrow corridor.
- Use the Lens of Truth to find a fake door, then use the Hover Boots to reach
  it.
- Bomb the debris in the southeast corner and use the Lens of Truth to get a
  key from the hidden chest, then go through the locked door.
- Pull the block in order to reach the ladder on the wall, then climb up onto
  the ship.
- Shoot the Gold Skulltula (#4) on the far wall and get the token with the
  Longshot.
- Play Zelda's Lullaby on the ship and ride it along until it starts to sink,
  then jump off to the platform on the left.
- Go west through the door.
- Use the Lens of Truth to spot the invisible walls, and make your way to the
  north door and enter it.
- Use Din's Fire to burn the walls and get the Boss Key from the chest.
- Go back, then make your way to the west door and go through it.
- Kill the Gold Skulltula (#5) behind the giant rotating statues.
- Go back, then make your way to the south door and go through it.
- Kill the enemies and get the key from the chest.
- Go back, then back through the east door.
- Shoot the bomb flowers next to the giant pillar on the other side of the 
  room to make it fall, then cross it and go through the locked door.
- Use the Hover Boots and the Lens of Truth to cross to the big door to get
  to the boss.



~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~Before Dungeon 5 [0209]~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~




New Items: 
Nayru's Love
"Requiem of Spirit" 
4 Skulltula Tokens (#91-#94)
1 Heart Piece (#36)

- Get Skulltula Token #91 (Haunted Wasteland)
Go back to the Gerudo Fortress, and go to the large gate near the entrance.  
Climb up the ladder and speak to the Gerudo to get him to open the gate, and
you can enter the Haunted Wasteland.  Cross the quicksand pit ahead with the
Hover Boots, then follow the single red flags across the desert until you
reach a tiny stone building.  Enter the building and drop down, then kill
the Gold Skulltula inside.

- Learn "Requiem of Spirit" (Desert Colossus)
Go back outside and climb to the top of the building, then read the altar.
Equip the Lens of Truth, and follow the Poe.  He will lead you across the
invisible path through the desert until you reach the Desert Colossus, where
the entrance to the Spirit Temple is located.  Enter the dungeon, then exit,
and Sheik will appear and teach you this song.

- Get Skulltula Token #92 (Desert Colossus)
Look at the palm trees on the south side of the area, and kill the Gold
Skulltula on one of them.

- Get Nayru's Love (Desert Colossus)
Near the palm trees on the north side of the area, there is a cracked wall.
Bomb it and enter; the Great Fairy inside teaches you this spell.

- Get Skulltula Token #93 (Desert Colossus)
Change to Young Link and warp to this area.  Use a bottled bug on the soft
soil spot right in front of the dungeon entrance to get this Gold Skulltula.

- Get Skulltula Token #94 (Desert Colossus)
Plant a Magic Bean at that soft soil spot, then return here as Adult Link.
Ride the bean platform over to a rock platform on the north side, where 
there is another Gold Skulltula.

- Get Heart Piece #36 (Desert Colossus)
Ride this same bean platform again, and jump off on top of the stone 
structure in front of the dungeon entrance to get the Heart Piece there.




~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~Spirit Temple [0210]~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~





Get Silver Gauntlets
Get Mirror Shield 
Get 5 Skulltula Tokens (#95-#99)
Defeat Twinrova
Get Spirit Medallion and Heart

Dungeon notes: The Spirit Temple is a large dungeon separated into two parts:
The Young Link section and the Adult Link section.  You will first enter the
dungeon as Young Link in order to get the Silver Gauntlets.  Then, you use
the Silver Gauntlets as Adult Link to get past the giant block obstructing
the way to the next section of the dungeon.  There are many Beamos, Fire Bat,
Armos Statues, and Lizalfos in the dungeon, as well as a wooden type of enemy 
Link can only defeat with the Fire Arrows or Din's Fire- be sure to bring 
fairies!  In the Adult Link section, Link will eventually find the Mirror 
Shield.  This shield reflects light coming from a skylight above onto other 
objects- usually the sun symbols that appear on the wall.  Several Iron 
Knuckles guard the major items of the dungeon; to defeat them, jump in and
slice with the sword, then quickly jump away before getting hammered.  The 
bosses of the dungeon are the evil Twinrova sisters.  To defeat them, first
target one of the sisters and block the fire or ice ray she shoots, then 
reflect that ray onto the other sister to damage her.  After doing that 
several times, they will merge into one.  Absorb a fire or ice shot three 
times in a row and you will reflect a beam at her.  When she's stunned, slice
her up with your sword until she dies.

To the map:
- Enter the dungeon as Young Link, go forward and left to meet Nabooru.
- Crawl through the hole, kill the enemies, and go through the left door.
- Use the Boomerang to hit the crystal, then go through the next door.
- Kill the enemies and go through the next door.
- Collect the silver rupees to make the cage door fall, then light the torches
  and get the key from the chest that appears.
- Kill the Gold Skulltula (#1) on the other side of this cage, then go through
  the door to a previous room.
- Go through the crawlspace, then through the locked door.
- Climb up the wall and shoot the Gold Skulltula (#2) at the top.
- Hit the crystal to get Bombchus from the chest, then use a Bombchu to hit 
  the rock protruding from the wall, and go through the unlocked door.
- Drop down to the left, light the torches in front of the giant statue and
  get the map from the chest.

To the compass:
- Climb back up the wall you dropped down, push the statue onto the switch,
  then go up the stairs and through the unlocked door.
- Collect all the silver rupees to light a torch, then light all the torches
  in the room with a Deku Stick, then get the key from the chest that drops.
  Pull the blocks aside and pull the block with the sun symbol into the light,
  then go through the unlocked door.
- Turn around and kill the Gold Skulltula (#3) above the door.
- Go up the steps through the door, kill the Iron Knuckle, and go through the
  next door.
- Get the Silver Gauntlets from the chest.
- Warp back to the dungeon as Adult Link, and enter.
- Go right from the entrance and push the huge block aside. 
- Go through and shoot the diamond on the ceiling and go through the left 
  door.
- Play the Zelda's Lullaby on the Triforce symbol and get the compass from the
  chest.

To the secret item:
- Go back to the previous room, then go through the right door.
- Collect the silver rupees, then play the Song of Time in front of the blue
  block and kill the Gold Skulltula (#4).
- Go to the next room and get the key, then go back two rooms, and go through
  the locked door.
- Climb the wall, then push the mirror statue so that it reflects light on the
  sun symbols- one of the symbols opens the next door for you to go through.
- Get to the top platform, then use the Hover Boots to get to the statue hand
  with the Triforce symbol.  Play Zelda's Lullaby, then hookshot to the chest
  and get the key from it.
- Climb up the stairs on the west side of the room to get to the top, play the
  Scarecrow's Song, then hookshot over to him and kill the Gold Skulltula 
  (#5).
- Go to the east side of the room and hookshot to the target, then climb the
  stairs to the top and go through the door.
- Kill the enemies and go forward through the next door.
- Touch an Armos Statue, then make it follow you onto the switch, and quickly 
  go through the door when it unlocks.
- Go up the stairs, kill the Iron Knuckle, and go through the next door.
- Get the Mirror Shield from the chest.

To the boss: 
- Go back to the Armos statue room, then reflect the light onto the sun 
  symbol with the Mirror Shield and go through the unlocked door.
- Get the key, then go back to the previous room, and west, then go through
  the locked door.
- Hookshot up to the top of the wall, then go through the door.
- Play Zelda's Lullaby on the symbol and go through the door.
- Shoot the door to the left of the flames, then shoot the golden eye behind
  it.  Hookshot to the target and step on the switch, then get the Boss Key
  from the chest.
- Go back to the previous room, then go up the stairs through the next door.
- Hit the crystal through the bars, then go through the passage until you 
  reach a mirror statue.  Bomb the cracked wall, then push that mirror 
  statue so that it points to the statue through the wall, then push the 
  second mirror statue so it reflects light through the bars.
- Go back around and reflect the light with your Mirror Shield onto the sun
  symbol to lower the platform.
- Reflect light onto the statue face, then hookshot to the big door and go
  through.
- Kill the Iron Knuckle and go through the next door to get to the boss.




~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~Before Final Dungeon [0211]~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~




New Items:
Ice Arrows
Light Arrows
1 Skulltula Token (#100)

- Get Ice Arrows (Gerudo Training Grounds)
You can now begin the Gerudo Training Ground quest for the Ice Arrows.  Enter
the training grounds from the Gerudo Fortress, on the south side of the 
hideout.  Take the door to the left.  Kill the enemies and get the key (#1),
then go through the next door.  Go up the slopes and collect the silver 
rupees within the time limit, using the hookshot to get over the flames, then
go through the next door afterwards.  Defeat the enemies, then use the Lens 
of Truth to spot a passage over the fake door to the right.  Hookshot up to
it and press the switch, then drop back down.  Use the Silver Gauntlets to
push the huge block out of the way, and go through the unlocked door.  Kill
the enemies, then use the Lens of Truth to spot an invisible chest and open 
it for the key (#2). Go back to the previous room, hookshot through the fake 
wall and go through the door.  Now drop down in the next room, and shoot the 
statue eyes from the rotating platform.  Hookshot to the chest and get the 
key (#3).  Now, play the Scarecrow's Song to hookshot back up to the top
ledge, and go through the newly unlocked door.  Get the key (#4), then go
back to the previous room, drop down and go through the door under the 
hookshot target.  Pound the totem poles with the Megaton Hammer to find a 
switch that deflames the chest that holds the key (#5).  Behind another pole
is a golden eye- shoot it to unlock the doors, then go through the door 
leading to the next area.

Use the hover boots and the switch to collect the 5 silver rupees here, then
go through the door it unlocked.  Play the Song of Time, equip the Iron Boots
and drop down into the water, then use the Longshot to collect the silver 
rupees.  Swim up and get the key (#6) from the chest, then go back to the 
previous room.  Hop to one of the center platforms and play the Song of Time 
creating stepping blocks to key (#7).  Now enter the room opposite the one with
the Hookshot target.  Kill the enemies here and get key (#8).  Now take the 
exit bringing you back into the first room.  Take the center door to enter the 
key maze.  Go through the first locked door to the left, then use the Lens of 
Truth to spot a passage in the ceiling; climb up and get the key from the chest
there.  Then, just go through all the locked doors in this room and get the Ice
Arrows from the big chest at the end.  

- Get Skulltula Token #100 (Zora's Fountain)
Go all the way back to Zora's Fountain, then go to the south end of it where
the entrance to the Great Fairy cavern is.  Lift up the boulder here with the
Silver Gauntlets and drop inside the hole.  Go up the slope and up the 
ladder, and the final Gold Skulltula is on the wall at the top, to the left.

- Get Gold Rupee (House of Skulltula)
After you get all 100 Skulltula tokens, you can go back to the House of
Skulltula and get infinite rupees from the man inside.

- Get Light Arrows (Temple of Time)
Enter the Temple of Time as Adult Link after you have all the medallions. 
Zelda gives you the final arrow upgrade, but gets captured in the process.




~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~Ganon's Castle [0212]~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~




Get Golden Gauntlets
Get Double Defense (Ganon's Castle)
Defeat Ganondorf

To the dungeon: Go north from the Market, and a rainbow bridge will have been
built that leads to the entrance of the final dungeon.

Dungeon notes: The final dungeon consists of a central room, and six passages,
which represent the six medallions, branching off from it.  Each passage has
a themed puzzle that corresponds to the dungeon you got the medallions from.
You will use a combination of practically all your skills, so come prepared!
At the end of each passage, you will use a Light Arrow to dispell the barrier
protecting the central tower of the castle.  You can break the barriers in
any order, but the Shadow Barrier is done here first because you get the 
important Golden Gauntlets inside.  At the top of the central tower, you will
face Ganondorf, the King of Evil!  To defeat him, stand on the perimeter of
the room, and deflect his energy balls back at him until he gets hit.  Then,
fire a Light Arrow at him, and jump over and slice him up with your sword.
If he starts to charge a gigantic orb and the room darkens, power up your spin
attack and unleash it when the orb is launched at you.

Shadow Barrier:
- Shoot the torch to the right with a Fire Arrow, then jump across the ice 
  blocks to the Like-Like platform, then jump to the platform after that.
- Use the Lens of Truth to find an ice path to the left, follow it to a 
  switch, and hit it with the Megaton Hammer to unlock the door.
- Go back across, shoot the torch again with a Fire Arrow, then jump across
  the ice platforms to the right and step on the switch there.
- Get the Golden Gauntlets from the chest.
- Use the Lens of Truth to find the central path, cross it to the next door 
  and go through.
- Shoot a Light Arrow at the energy orb to destroy the Shadow Barrier.
- With the Golden Gauntlets, exit Ganon's Castle, then go under the big arch
  to the left and go down the valley.  Lift up the giant pillar, then go 
  inside the cave and receive the Double Defense from the Great Fairy.

Forest Barrier:
- Use Din's Fire to light the four center torches and a Fire Arrow on the 
  torch above the door, then go through the door.
- Equip the Hover Boots and let the fan to the left push you to a couple of
  silver rupees.  Step on the switch to make a hookshot target appear.
- Collect the rest of the silver rupees, using the Hover Boots and avoiding
  the fans, then go through the next door.
- Shoot a Light Arrow at the energy orb to destroy the Forest Barrier.

Water Barrier:
- Catch some blue fire in a couple bottles, kill the enemies, drop some 
  blue fire on the red ice, and go through the door.
- Push the far block into the hole, then push the other block below the 
  ledge with the red ice.
- Jump up and melt the red ice, then go through the door.
- Shoot a Light Arrow at the energy orb to destroy the Water Barrier.

Fire Barrier:
- Equip your Goron Tunic, and collect all the silver rupees in the room.  
  Pick up the giant pillar with the Golden Gauntlets to reveal one silver 
  rupee and grant access to another one.
- Afterwards, hookshot to the next door and go through.
- Shoot a Light Arrow at the energy orb to destroy the Fire Barrier.

Light Barrier:
- Use the Golden Gauntlets to dispose of the giant pillar in front of the 
  passage to the Light Barrier.
- Kill all the invisible enemies and get the key, then go through the locked
  door.
- Play Zelda's Lullaby on the Triforce symbol to get the key, and go through.
- Collect all the silver rupees and hookshot to the last one on top, then go
  through the next door.
- Shoot a Light Arrow at the energy orb to destroy the Light Barrier.

Spirit Barrier:
- Collect all the silver rupees and go to the next room.
- Hit the crystal behind the cage to get Bombchus, then lay a Bombchu so it
  goes over the cage and hits the far crystal, and go through the next door.
- Shoot the ceiling with a Fire Arrow and reflect the light on the sun 
  symbols with the Mirror Shield to unlock the next door.
- Shoot a Light Arrow at the energy orb to destroy the Spirit Barrier.

To the boss:
- Enter the central tower after all the barriers are destroyed.
- Make your way up the tower, killing the enemies, then get the Boss Key 
  after killing the Stalfos.
- After killing the Iron Knuckles, climb up the final part of the tower and
  open the door to get to Ganondorf.

- Defeat Ganon
After you defeat Ganondorf, Zelda will instruct you to follow her in order to 
escape the castle.  The way back through the castle is linear; you have time
to defeat the enemies as you go, and Zelda will open the caged doors.  After
you escape, you will begin the final fight, against Ganon!  To damage Ganon,
shoot its head with a Light Arrow to stun it- or simply roll under its legs-
and pound the tail with the Megaton Hammer or slice it with the Biggoron's 
Sword.  Do that until it collapses, and get your Master Sword back from near
Zelda.  Now, battle it the same way as before, but use the Master Sword to
damage it this time.  When it collapses again, Zelda will use her power and
you can finish him off with your sword.
