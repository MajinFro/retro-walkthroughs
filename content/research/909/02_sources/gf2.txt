          --- 1. Introduction ---                                      [1000]
~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~

Final Fantasy Mystic Quest is like the idiot's Final Fantasy. 

Even so, that doesn't make this game bad. Sure, a blindfolded monkey could 
beat it with one hand (exaggeration alert!), but that's not the point. It's a 
good game in general that differentiates from the "real" Final Fantasy. I 
like its style. Being a very "simple" RPG, it's a great game to play when I'm 
too lazy to use my brain and such. 

This guide will walk you through the game (if you need it of course), as 
well as provide appendices and the like to help you master the game. Enjoy.

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+

        [Q] What are battlefields?

        [A] Battlefields are places on the world map in which you can fight
            monsters. Beat 10 rounds of them, and you can win prizes or EXP
            and GP. 

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+

        [Q] How many battlefields are there?

        [A] There are 20 in all. You can check the walkthrough and seek out
            the one you're looking for. 

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+

        [Q] What exactly does "Auto" or "Manual" mean?

        [A] If you're talking about this game, "Auto" and "Manual" has either
            the computer control your ally or not. Auto will allow an ally to
            automatically attack while Manual forces you to make moves. 

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+

        [Q] How come my ally doesn't level up?

        [A] Your allies have a set level when they join your party and when
            they leave. I don't think it's possible to have them level up 
            without the use of a cheat device. 

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+

        [Q] How do I use Refreshers and what do they do?

        [A] Refreshers will regain any stats lost in-battle. For example, if
            a monster lowers your attack stats, then Refresher will regain 
            them. To use them, just select them from the "Item" bar.

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+

        [Q] I heard there was a really quick way of defeating the Dark King.
            How so?

        [A] Yeah, you can easily deal 30000 HP damage by just casting Cure on
            him. It's still cheap though. Besides, he's easy anyway. 8)

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+

              4.1. The Knight                                          [4100]
-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~--~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~

The first task is naming your character. Choose whatever suits your fancy. I
personally don't care what you decide to name him. I'll refer to him as
Benjamin for the most part. When you're finished, select the End option, 
orjust press the Start Button...

                         __________________________
                    ---=|      Hill Of Destiny     |=---
                         ¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯    

An earthquake has driven Benjamin from his home up to the Hill Of Destiny 
(nice name). Coming across some random man, he demands to know what's going
on. The two will climb up to the top of the hill. A gap lies between Benjamin
and the man. Jump across with the B Button to get to the other side. The 
strange man will show you the Focus Tower, once the heart of the world. He'll
speak a bit about the situation at hand. 

[Insert Name Here], only you can save the Crystals and the World.

Obviously, Benjamin's a bit confused. Suddenly, a Behemoth will appear out of
nowhere. This is your first fight. Luckily, it's nothing too difficult. Since
regular attacks are really your only option at this point, just pound it with
that. It is possible to lose here, but you can always restart the battle. If
you manage to get a critical hit, you've basically killed the thing. 

The man will notice that the place is becoming dangerous (oh really?). Before
he flies away, he'll mention the Level Forest. Well, that's the only place
you can go...

                         __________________________
                    ---=|       Level Forest       |=---
                         ¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯    

The man appears just as he promised. He'll tell you your first objective; save
the Crystal of Earth. And with that, he runs/flies off. That helped. You can
talk to the blue guy a bit to the north. That boulder is blocking his way.
He asks if you could shove it aside, but considering that he himself is kind 
of blocking your way, that might be a bit difficult. 

So what do you do? Use the B Button to jump over him. Simply push it up, and
he'll thank you. He will also tell you to bring the Tree Wither to Kaeli in
Foresta. Push the boulder up one more tile, and open the nearby chest for a 
few Cure Potions. Don't use them yet though. Walk east to leave the Level 
Forest. 

See the flashing arrow? Walk in the direction that its point to reach Foresta.
Let's deliver that Tree Wither to Kaeli now. 

                         __________________________
                    ---=|         Foresta          |=---
                         ¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯    

Houses here are made out of tree trunks. You can talk to some of the people
hanging around to learn some nifty information. An old lady will tell you that
the Crystal of Earth can be found to the north in the Bone Dungeon. Kaeli's
house was the first house you saw (when you entered Foresta). It's in the
lower-left corner of town. Enter her house and walk up to the two beds. Open
the chest for some more Cure Potions. Yeah, let's walk into more strangers'
houses and steal their drugs!

Talk to Kaeli and she'll mention a tree that is blocking the north exit of
the Level Forest. You agree to help out, and Kaeli decides to tag along. Her
mother gets a bit anxious though. But hey, who doesn't like some random 
stranger taking out their daughter? Before you joins, Kaeli picks up her axe. 
Obviously, she's a woman with attitude. 

Leave Kaeli's house and head north. At the northeast corner is a house. Inside
is that man you helped out. He'll allow you take the contents of the chest 
above him. However, the barrels are kind of blocking your way. Wow, what weird
people. Leave his house, and walk north over the wooden bridge. See the blocks 
of wood in the water? You can jump onto them. Use them to get to the back of 
his house and enter. Inside the chest is your first spell: Cure. It heals HP. 
Now move the barrels out of your way and leave his house. 

Before we skedaddle out of here, enter the house to your south. The bed on the
left will restore your HP. Don't forget to pick up the three Cure Potions in
the nearby chest. With that collected, you can finally leave Foresta. Head
west to return to the Level Forest. Yippee! 

As you re-enter the forest, Kaeli will appear and chop down some trees with 
her Axe. Of course, you're now obligated to fight a Brownie. You'll notice how
much more powerful Kaeli is than your main character. You have two ways to
go from this point. First, head left. Defeat the two Slimes and (soon) a 
Brownie. At the end of the path here are some more Cure Potions. Return to
the intersection and go right. See the red tree up ahead? Don't touch it just
yet. First, defeat the Slime and Brownie to the east. Pick up the Heal Potions
also. 

Heal up your team with Cure Potions. Examine the glowing tree and Kaeli will
cut it out of her way effortlessly. He'll poison Kaeli, but you two still put
up a fight. 

     Boss Fight: Minotaur ~
     =------------------=
          First, I'll tell you the cheap method of winning. Simply have Kaeli 
          cast Life on the Minotaur to immediately put him out of commission.
          If you'd rather just attack the conventional way though, you might
          have a tiny bit of trouble. Use Cure to heal up your units. The 
          Minotaur hits pretty hard, so you'll need it. Obviously, if Benjamin
          dies, you'll need Kaeli's Life spell to revive him. Just remember
          that. 

Once he's down for the count, Kaeli's mom, for some reason, appears. Kaeli
seems to be pretty confident in her survival. All she needs is Elixir... found
in the depths of the Sand Temple. Benjamin has to be a good boy, and says that
he will get it. Kaeli will hand you her Axe. Now you can chop down trees and
kick monster butt! 

Before continuing, check the southwest corner. You'll find a Brownie behind
some trees. Chop them down and fight it. Some Heal Potions can be found in the
chest behind it. If you'd like, you can exit and re-enter the Level Forest. 
The chests all respawn with the same items. It's not required, but it's always
a good idea to stock up on Cure Potions. When you're done fooling around, head
over to where you fought the Minotaur and continue onward. 

-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~--~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~
              4.2. Dungeon Of Bones                                    [4200]
-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~--~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~

Simply go north, and you'll reach your first battlefield. Since you'll probably
die without the help of another member, bypass it. Continue north to reach 
the Sand Temple. 

                         __________________________
                    ---=|       Sand Temple        |=---
                         ¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯

Simply walk up until you find a chest. Open it to find the Elixir... gone? A
dude will walk in. Apparently, he got here before you did. He'll offer to sell
it to you for 9000 GP. Deal!! You probably don't have that much money at the
moment. Anyhow, Tristam will offer another, more reasonable deal. Get him 
through the Bone Dungeon. Help him find the treasure and you get the Elixir.
It's as simple as that. 

Tristam mentioned Battlefields right? You have two that are accessible at the
moment. There's one to the south (the one that you passed), and one to the 
west. Basically, Battlefields pits you up against monsters in 10 rounds. Beat 
them all for EXP, GP and items. Start with the one just south of your current 
position. If you ever need healing, head back to Foresta to heal up. 

     Battlefield One ~
     =-------------=
          Monsters: Mad Plant, Poison Toad
          Prize: 54 EXP
           ~ Pretty simple here. Tristam kicks incredible amounts of butt with
             his shurikens. I managed to breeze through all 10 rounds without
             even breaking a sweat. Benjamin and Tristam should be able to
             kill each monster within one hit. Mad Plants are weak against
             Axes, so Benjamin should really win out. Trust me, it's THAT 
             easy. If you managed to get poisoned, return to Foresta and use
             the bed to heal up. Heal Potions work too. 

     Battlefield Two ~
     =-------------=
          Monsters: Mad Plant, Poison Toad, Basilisk
          Prize: Charm
           ~ This one is a tad bit harder than the above, but that's only
             because of the Basilisks. Also, a lot of the battles put you up
             against three monsters and not just two. Sure, it's a step up,
             but nothing you can't handle. Have Benjamin kill off the Mad
             Plants while Tristam takes care of the rest. If you managed to 
             get poisoned, return to Foresta and use the bed to heal up. Heal
             Potions work too. 

Once you finish both Battlefields, return to Foresta for healing and whatnot.
Take advantage of the re-appearing chests in the Level Forest as well. When 
you're done, proceed to the Bone Dungeon, of which is north of the Sand 
Temple. 

                         __________________________
                    ---=|      Bone Dungeon        |=---
                         ¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯

See the moving sand just ahead of you? Step onto it and you'll be carried to
a chest. Open it for some Cure Potions. Now, fight the monsters on either
side to get past them. I would suggest fighting them all for the experience.
They're not difficult either. The monsters here consists mostly of those you
already fought in the Battlefields. Anyway, the skull thingy is the door to
the next room. However, there are more items here to pick up.

Will you go left or right? Go left first because... that path's kind of what
I'll describe right now. Beat up the Sand Worm in your way along with the 
other monsters here. A Sand Worm guards the chest in the northwest corner. It
contains 10 Ninja Stars for Tristam. Now, if you head over to the right side
of the room, you can find another 10 Ninja Stars directly opposite the last
chest. Pick that up and go through the skull doorway. 

Walk up a bit to be stopped by some water. Luckily, some stones are in the 
perfect position for you to jump over them. Use them (jump to them) to get to
the other side. See the steps to your right? Have Benjamin go into the water.
Now, if he could just walk on water like (???), what was the point of using
those stones? Anyway, trudge over to the west side of the water to find a 
skeleton with an abnormally large rib-cage. 

Follow the skeleton until you reach open water again. Walk up the nearby steps
to find a door. Tristam will appear and blow the crap out of the door for you.
Afterwards, he tries to sell you some bombs for 30G. Decline the first time,
and he'll cut the price in half. 15G is the lowest you can get them at. Before
continuing, return through the skeleton back to the first part of this room.
Walk back up the steps onto dry land. A bit to your north is a skull. Blow it
out of the way with your newly acquired bomb. Past it is a Steel Shield. 
Return to the door that Tristam blew up for you and continue. 

Uh oh, more monsters. Boy, this room is loaded. Beat up the various Rocs, 
Skeletons, and Sand Worms. First, blow up the cracked door at the upper-right
corner. In the chest is another 10 Ninja Stars for Tristam. Head over to the
northeast corner and continue onwards. The staircase there will bring you down
to B2. From the staircase, head east. Defeat the Gorgon that is blocking the
chest. Inside is 10 Bombs. 

To your north is a Skeleton. Beat it up. Continue up to find a rather long
skeleton. Place a bomb at the weak part of the skeleton to blow up the entire
thing. First, head right to find a secret doorway leading to a chamber with
a chest. Use the stones to hop across the water. The chest contains 10 Ninja
Stars. Return to the shattered rib-cage (poor thing) and advance west. Walk
through the skeletal head. 

To your south are two monsters. You only need to defeat one to reach the chest 
at the south end. It contains another 10 Ninja Stars for Tristam. Geez, this 
game doesn't have much faith in you, does it? Head north to find some moving 
sand. The only part that you can cross is lined with monsters. Defeat them all 
and make your way to the other side. Don't step on the moving sand, or else
you'll go back. Instead, blow a hole in the weak part of the nearby skeleton
with a bomb. 

Kill the Minotaur Zombie that is blocking your path. Now, move south. Don't
step on the moving sand unless you want to be carried back. See the staircase?
Descend them to B2. Shove the Skeleton out of the way. To your immediate left
is a Minotaur Zombie guarding a chest containing 10 Ninja Stars. Return back
and look up. The skeletal head has its mouth closed. Of course, a little bomb
can easily blow it back up. Do just that and continue. 

You'll reach a fork while still in the rib-cage. Don't go left just yet. 
Instead, go north through the doorway. Take the three Seeds and return to
the split. Go left to find some more moving sand. The only safe passage is
lined with monsters. Clear the pathway and check the next skeleton. The mouth
is closed once more. Repeat what you did a few minutes ago to open it. Once
more, you'll reach a fork. Go up first. The Quake spell can be found in here.
You don't want to miss out.

Back at the fork, head right. Get the Minotaur Zombie out of the way and
advance. Once again, place a bomb on the skull to open up a passage. Enter the
next room. Before continuing, heal up a bit. A difficult monster is just up
ahead. See the big, red thingy? Yeah, that's it. Jump across the water (using
the stone) to face it. *battle cry*

     Boss Fight: Flamerus Rex ~
     =----------------------=
          You know, they could have at least come up with a better name. 
          Assuming you didn't already use up all your Quakes, now would be a
          great time for Benjamin to use them. The Flamerus Rex can deal 100+
          damage with most of his attacks, so make sure that Benjamin or 
          Tristam heals up occasionally. Both Life and Cure heal HP so use
          them. Because the Flamerus Rex is strong against poison, don't count
          on Tristam dealing too much damage. Have him heal and revive Benjamin
          with Life if need be. When the boss's HP gets pretty low, he can use
          Rip Earth, which deals a good amount of damage to both of them. Like
          always, heal when your HP gets too low. Have Benjamin use Quake until
          he runs out. Then throw some bombs to finish the Flamerus Rex off.

Once the freak is defeated, Tristam will immediately run to get his treasure.
A crystal rises from the corpse of the Flamerus Rex. You have already fulfilled
your first objective; to release the first crystal. Tristam will play around
with a weapon reminiscent of the Hookshot in Zelda games. He'll also give you
the Elixir he promised. For some reason, Tristam didn't take _all_ of the 
treasures. Be sure to open the last three for some Cure Potions, more 
Explosives, and the Sand Coin. Do not forget the last item!!

You will want to return to Foresta now to give Kaeli the Elixir. As you exit
the Bone Dungeon, Tristam will leave you. 

-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~--~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~
              4.3. Wakewater!                                          [4300]
-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~--~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~

By releasing the last crystal, Foresta has been restored. The grass is green
again and people are young. Head over to Kaeli's house to give her the Elixir.
For such a terminally ill person, she doesn't seem very sick... Kaeli will 
state that she will head off to Aquaria to see Spencer. Why don't we get over
there first? If you need to heal, go ahead to the house at the southeast 
corner. Sleep in the bed. 

To reach Aquaria, you'll have to go through the Focus Tower. First, head east
from the Sand Temple. You'll hit a Battlefield. If you want to fight here, go
ahead. 

     Battlefield Three ~
     =---------------=
          Monsters: Basilisk, Sand Worm, Minotaur Zombie
          Prize: 150 GP
           ~ If you chose to fight here by yourself, you might have some
             trouble. Even so, the monsters are nothing new as you've already
             battled them in the Bone Dungeon. Minotaur Zombies are the more
             dangerous foe here. They can confuse, paralyze, and blind you. It 
             really sucks when Benjamin (out of confusion) happens to cast 
             Quake on himself only to die instantly. If you can keep those
             status ailments off your back, this Battlefield should be easy. 

You will find the Focus Tower west of the Battlefield. The smart thing to do
here would would be to enter. 

                         __________________________
                    ---=|  Focus Tower to Aquaria  |=---
                         ¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯

Simply follow the path until you reach the strange man. Yes, he's here waiting
for you. He'll mention Captain Mac and a girl named Phoebe. Suddenly, the
freak flies off again without clarifying what the hell he just said. Nice...

Going any further south leads to nothing. Just enter the nearby opening. Pick
up the 10 bombs and check the gold seal on the wall. Because you have the Sand
Coin, you can open it. The other seals cannot be opened because you (duh!) do
not have the Coin for their respective doors. The path will lead you to a 
staircase. Ascend them to 2F. Turn to your left and head south to reach a 
chest. Yay, you've just picked up your second Black Magic spell.  

Although there appears to be a bunch of other staircases around and such, those

     =------------------------=

some Heal Potions and bombs. There's one more house you should visit before
heading on out. The house below the inn (with the weathervane on it) is the
residence of Otto. He'll state that his daughter had went up north using the
Rainbow Road. Norma was blown off by the wind and is stuck down there. If the
wind doesn't die down, she'll be stuck there for all eternity. You can go
upstairs for some Heal Potions. 

Before going, you might want to stop at the inn. Upstairs, where the beds are,
are two chests. Inside are some Refreshers and Heal Potions. Upon leaving 
Windia, head south and east. The Windhole Temple is up ahead. 

                         __________________________
                    ---=|     Windhole Temple      |=---
                         ¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯

There's really nothing interesting here. In fact, none of the temples 
throughout this world serve that much of a purpose. Get the Heal Potions in the 
middle before heading out. North of the Windhole Temple is Mount Gale. 

                         __________________________
                    ---=|        Mount Gale        |=---
                         ¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯

Ignore the first staircase you see. Continue along and ignore the second 
staircase unless you want a chest of Heal Potions. Ascend and beat up the
Water Hag. Climb the multiple stairsets until you reach a Skuldier blocking
your way. Defeat it and advance. At the gap, use the Dragon Claw to grapple
yourself across. 

Jump the next gap (there's no point in using the Dragon Claw for that). Head
south and defeat another Water Hag. You'll now be on the opposite side of the
rocks. Continue descending the staircase and follow the path to come across a 
Vampire. Kill it and take the steps down. If you'd like, you can go north to
find a chest of Refreshers across the gap. Keep going south and climb down
another staircase. Go northeast and up the stairs. 

Kill off another Skuldier and proceed. Head up the next long staircase you see.
To your west is a messed-up bridge reminiscent of the Rope Bridge you crossed
before. Walk across to the opposite side and defeat the monster blocking your
way. Look north and grapple yourself across the chasm. Here, you'll find the
Apollo Helm; the strongest helmet you'll pick up. Defeat the Vampire, and take 
your prize. Well, you can't just go back south because Benjamin can't jump 
that far AND there is no grappling hook. Instead, look to your west. Get the
Water Hag out of the way and jump across that gap. 

Now just use the Dragon Claw and circle around. Proceed south and get back on
the other half of the bridge. Use it to get to Vampire just below you. Ben
will automatically assume that he holds the fourth crystal... for some
reason... Nah, it's Pazuzu. 

     Boss Fight: Dullahan ~
     =------------------=
          So this Dullahan is some headless knight in shining armor, eh? 
          First thing's first; get rid of the two Vampires he has at his side. 
          That should be instinct by now. Aero will get rid of them easily.
          The best way to defeat Dullahan is through pounding him with magic.
          That means casting Meteor, White, etc. Kaeli won't be too useful in
          this fight, but she's a good healer. Use her to heal Benjamin if he
          loses a lot of HP. She can also heal the various status ailments
          that Dullahan might cast upon you. The one move you want to watch 
          out for is Doom Dance. It is capable of automatically killing one 
          of your units regardless of their HP. Needless to say, it can get
          pretty annoying. All you basically need to do is use Meteor until
          that ugly Dullahan dies. 

Well, this dungeon was kind of pointless. At least we got the Apollo Helm out
of it. Pazuzu holds the last crystal. He can be found in Pazuzu's Tower, named
after him of course. Use the Exit spell and return to Windia. 

Hmm, it seems that the wind has stopped. You can have some fun by getting on
top of the inn's roof using the Dragon Claw but there's no point in doing 
that. Give Otto a visit and he'll power up the rainbow road for you. Before 
you go though, he'll tell you to hit the switches with your axe to work the
elevator. I wonder how he knows that...

                         __________________________
                    ---=|      Pazuzu's Tower      |=---
                         ¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯

Now inside Pazuzu's Tower, you should see a little girl to the right. Walk up
the steps and talk to her. Well, now that you've rescued her, you might as
well continue. 

With that, begin walking into the middle area of the room. You'll have to
fight through some monsters obviously. You don't have to beat them all up. 
Just clear a path to the big bird up ahead. Yep, that's Pazuzu. Before you
can kick his butt, he'll fly off. Damn. Return to the entrance and head right
past the area where you found Norma. Go through the opening and defeat the 
Beholder blocking the doorway. Continue along. 

Get rid of the monster blocking the staircase up ahead. Proceed and kill off
another annoying Garuda and then another one at the end. Continue west and 
you will see the elevator of Pazuzu's Tower. Defeat the Beholder and head 
south. There's a staircase to your left. Don't take it just yet. Kill off the 
Manticor followed by a Chimera. Beware of Chimeras as they are capable of 
petrifying you. That would suck. Last, but not least, get rid of the Beholder 
blocking the doorway. A line of four monsters block two chests. You will find 
20 bombs for the picking. With those collected, return to the staircase I told
you to ignore previously. Walk up the following stairs all the way to 5F. 

A Garuda up ahead is just asking to be vanquished. Do just that and continue.
Walk through the doorway and you'll reach another set of stairs and a door.
Go through. Once more, climb all the stairs to the very end. Monsters guard
each stop so clear them out of your way. Once you finally reach the top, you
will appear on 7F. Head west and use your Dragon Claw to get to the platform
to your north. Get rid of the Thanatos and step onto the ledge. Advance this
way until you see a staircase leading down. This will bring you to 6F.

Proceed south and get rid of the Sorcerer that is blocking your path. Walk a 
tiny bit to the right and kill the Naga after the steps. Be careful of
their Sleep Lure attack. They can also confuse Kaeli, but not Benjamin due to
his Apollo Helm. Go south and through the doorway. Here, you have a staircase,
a chest containing Cure Potions, and a cracked door. Pick up the Cure Potions
and continue left. Pop open the chest for the Flare spell!

Return to the cracked wall and throw a Mega Grenade to blast it out of the 
way. Continue and defeat the two monsters that are blocking your way. At the
north end, you'll find Pazuzu YET AGAIN! Once more, he flies off. Pazuzu sure
is good at running away. Backtrack and hit the purple switch on the wall with
your Giant's Axe. Head back south and move through the opening by the Flare
chest. Kill the Naga just ahead and follow the path to a staircase. 

You should be on 5F upon descending the stairs. Begin by getting rid of the
Thanatos. Head south and east. Slay the monsters ahead and jump the gap. Go
north from here and kill another Thanatos. Ahead is a staircase leading down.
The smart thing to do would be to take them. The new monster you see is a 
Gargoyle. In my book, monsters that are *pink* are weaklings. Defeat it and
continue south. 

After killing off a Chimera, you will appear at a staircase along with two 
doorways to your left and right. First, defeat the Sorcerer blocking the one
on the right. Inside is a chest containing the... Excalibur! Now that's a
friggin' powerful weapon!! With that collected, go through the opening on the
left. Blow a hole in the cracked wall and proceed through it. A line of 
Gargoyles have to be defeated before you get anywhere. Get the Heal Potions
and Cure Potions. Talk to Pazuzu and he'll fly off. Typical...

Return through the door you bombed open and head west a little bit. After
defeating the monster, go north through the doorway. A Chimera blocks the next
switch that you have to hit. Use that axe and smack it. Walk down the nearby
steps (to your immediate left). A Thanatos will pop up right in front of your
face. Slay it and continue all the way down the stairs. At the end, you should
appear on 2F. 

A staircase is nearby. There's no point in descending though. Defeat the two
monsters and proceed south through the doorway. Ignore the cracked door and 
defeat the next line of monsters. It's a lot, but shouldn't take that much
time. After killing the last Naga, go through the doorway and through the 
other opening to the right. Ignore the staircase here as well. Steal some Heal
Potions and Cure Potions and leave the room. Head north past another line of
monsters. At the end, a switch can be found. Once more, hit it with your axe.

The elevator now has Pazuzu on 5F. Head back south and descend the stairs to
the first floor. Use the Dragon Claw to grapple to the opposite hook. You
should know how to get to 5F from here. Head east and through the doorway. 
Walk up the steps and repeat this process on 3F. You will find Pazuzu waiting
at 5F. Playtime's over, pal. 

     Boss Fight: Pazuzu ~
     =----------------=
          The birdman is weak against wind attacks, so Aero works wonders.
          Flare deals more damage though. Those two spells should deal the
          most damage against Pazuzu. A lot of his attacks deal 500+ damage.
          Keep Kaeli's HP high as she seems to die very often. Psychshield
          makes it so that if you attack Pazuzu while it is up, you will eat
          a nasty counter-attack. Do not attack with his shield up for that
          very reason. When the Psychshield is down, it is safe to kick his
          butt. Take caution with that one move and you shouldn't die THAT
          often. If you send a constant barrage of Flare and Aero, the battle
          will go much quicker. Aero (assuming Kaeli casts it) deals 1500+ 
          damage while Flare can deal 2000+ damage. Those are some pretty 
          figures if you ask me. 

Afterwards, Benjamin will take the elevator and ride it up to the top of 
Pazuzu's Tower. The screen will darken and the final crystal will appear in
all of its glory. Of course, you can't forget the Sky Coin in the chest. That
is all four crystals. However, there is one more task we must accomplish 
before we can call it quits. Cast Exit and leave the tower. 

-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~--~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~
              4.6. D-D-Doom Castle                                     [4600]
-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~--~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~-~

Return back to Windia and get your butt to Otto's house. Talk to him to hear
that Captain Mac is still stuck. Reuben manages to barge in. Looks like
he's back just as he said he would. He'll explain his finding on a stairwell
to the top of the Focus Tower. In order to save Captain Mac, you'll have to
blast Spencer's Place to drain the water into the lake. However, you'll need
a Thunder Rock so Otto can build the rainbow road. Reuben's dad knows about
this. 

Kaeli leaves the party to take care of Norma and Reuben joins! Arion lives in
Fireburg, remember? Head over to Reuben's house and talk to Arion, who is 
currently resting in bed. Upon asking him about Thunder Rock, he hands it 
over to you. Now you can head back to Windia and talk to Otto again. He'll
go upstairs with the Thunder Rock and power up his machine. With the new
rainbow road, you can now get over to Spencer's Place. Do just that. 

Use the new rainbow road past Pazuzu's Tower to get to Spencer's Place. You
might notice a ship to your left. You'll reach that soon enough. Enter 
Spencer's Place and Reuben will use a Mega Grenade and blow up the area. The
water will flow free and form a gigantic lake outside. Captain Mac's ship will
float toward the landmass. Before you go though, head north and climb into
the water. Keep going and climb back onto the ground. Throw a Mega Grenade to
blow a hole in the wall...

Now, return to the entrance and hop on the platform to your left and proceed. 
You'll see a white tile on the ground. Bomb the nearby wall with a Mega 
Grenade. Take the warp with the Gemini Crest and you'll teleport just before a
cave. Follow it through the hole you blew in the wall. At the end is the 
Mobius Crest. With it, you can warp on the respective tile. Try it out on the
mobius tile nearby. You'll teleport near the entrance of Spencer's Place. Now,
return to Windia. 

Head to Otto's House and talk to Kaeli, who is currently tending to Norma. She
will hand you the Captain's Cap to show Captain Mac. Okay, so we have all of
that done. Now, how do we get to his ship? The key is the house at the 
northeast corner. Proceed to its basement and step on the tile. With the 
Mobius Crest, you will warp to the Ship Dock. 

                         __________________________
                    ---=|        Ship Dock         |=---
                         ¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯

Although it doesn't really look like much of a dock, Captain Mac's ship is
up ahead anyway. Step onto the boardwalk to his ship. Notice the gigantic size
difference between you and his ship. (Benjamin is bigger than the entire 
ship!!)

                         __________________________
                    ---=|           Deck           |=---
                         ¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯

Well, here we are. Now to look for Captain Mac. There are a bunch of stairs
on the deck. Ignore them for now. Begin by heading to the middle of the deck;
where the steering wheel is located. Go north from there and go right. See the
barrel that's blocking the upper-right corner of the tiny center room? Push
it down and get the Heal Potions. Then climb up the big pole. See the 
intersection of the two masts? Move a bit to the right and up the abnormally
thick piece of rope. 

At the top of the rope, head left and down the middle mast. You will drop in-
between two Thanatos. Defeat either one (or both) and open the chest for some
more Heal Potions. With that, head over to the stairs in the upper-left 
corner. 

                         __________________________
                    ---=|        Mac's Ship        |=---
                         ¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯

Obviously, you should take the next stairs leading down. You will reach B2.
Simply follow the path, defeating any monsters that get in your way. You 
will reach another staircase at the end. Here, you'll have two ways to go;
left or right? The right path has a chest containing Refreshers. Just make
sure that you DO NOT fall down the hole. The left path will let you proceed.
Take the stairs at the end of that. 

Once again, you appear on B2. Advance southbound and clear out that ugly 
Gargoyle. The stairs to Mac's floor awaits. Take it up. Be sure to take the
Gaia's Armor before getting to Captain Mac. You can tell that he's pretty
nervous. Before Kaeli takes her dad back to Windia, Captain Mac will mention
the Prophecy... 

Suddenly, Reuben will faint. You are going to have to take him back to Windia.
You will come to the Ship Dock. Spencer will appear along with Tristam. Now
isn't this a happy reunion? It seems that Phoebe is looking for you. The two
will skedaddle and you're free to step on the tile to get back to Windia. Get
your butt to the inn and you'll find Phoebe there. Reuben will limp his way to
bed and Phoebe joins. 

Walk upstairs and check on Captain Mac. Apparently, he has discovered a lost
part of the Prophecy. "The one behind the 4 is darker than the night, and 
rises midst the land." The Dark King must be defeated, or else the world still
will not be safe. Mac will lend you his ship. Use it to get to the Focus Tower
and finish this fight. 

Get back to Mac's Ship and step up to the steering wheel. Sail to the Focus
Tower. This is the entrance to Doom Castle. Before entering, make sure that
you are PREPARED. You can't be more safe with 99 Cure Potions, Seeds, and
Heal Potions. This is the final dungeon after all. 

                         __________________________
                    ---=|        Doom Castle       |=---
                         ¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯

Ignore the steps leading into the moving sand. Defeat the Shadow and proceed.
There are a bunch of steps leading into the sand here. You will need to go 
down a particular one. You are looking for the last one (the one on the far
east). Take them down and you'll be stopped by some bones. Walk a bit to the
left until you are moving along the wall. You will stop by a skull and a 
staircase. Bomb the former and defeat the Gorgon. You will find the Aegis
Shield on this platform. Wield it with pride. 

It's easy to return to the entrance from there. Simply walk west along the
moving sand to reach the first steps you saw. Repeat the process of walking
to the far-east staircase. Let yourself go down and stop at the bones. Use the
Dragon Claw to grapple onto the hook to your east. Climb the stairs and defeat
the Gorgon. Use the Dragon Claw again to get yourself to the next platform (to
the west). Jump over the hook and grapple onto the next platform to the left.

Clear out the Minotaur Zombie and climb down the wall. You will automatically
move to another skull and a chest containing Cure Potions. Now, make sure that
you are one tile above the chest (and not the skull). Look to your left and
throw a Mega Grenade to blow up the bones blocking the hook. Then use the 
Dragon Claw to get to the stairs. Upon getting across, climb up the stairs.
Oh noes! It's the Skullrus Rex!!

     Boss Fight: Skullrus Rex ~
     =----------------------=
          Of course, begin by getting rid of the two Shadows. They won't be
          tough to take down. I'd suggest casting Flare or something to 
          damage both at the same time. Upon taking down the Shadows, begin
          pounding the Skullrus Rex with Flare (Benjamin) and White (Phoebe).
          If you can deal 2000+ damage with each move, the Skullrus Rex will
          die within 2.5 turns. The only move you will want to watch out for
          is Sleep Powder. Luckily, Benjamin can't be put to sleep. The 
          Skullrus Rex is easily defeated. 

Once that thing is out of the way, climb the stairs that was behind it and 
proceed. Follow the path to another staircase. This one will bring you right
before the green door in the Focus Tower. Open it and enter the depths of the
Doom Castle. 

The first room is icy. You will also see the statues that were originally 
found in the Ice Pyramid. Defeat the Shadow on the left and stab the statue
with your Excalibur. The door will thus open. Go through and move left. Hop
over the narrow gap and continue. Once again, poke the statue and proceed.
Get the Phanquid out of the way and stab the statue that it was originally
blocking. This will pop open a door on the opposite side. It will prove to
be very useful later. Return to the entrance and head east. 

Jump over the hook and use the Dragon Claw to get to the eastern platform. 
Simply go through the door and open the chest for some Heal Potions. Continue
through the opening. You will appear at a four-way intersection separated by
a hole in the middle. Jump over it and keep going. When you see the hook to
your south, grapple to it and hit the nearby switch (stab it). Head east and
jump the hole. 

There are two paths here. Take the path that doesn't have the Phanquid 
blocking it. Stab the statue and return to the Phanquid. Defeat it and walk
past it. Pick up the Arrows from the chest and move through the door you 
opened previously. A Freezer Crab blocks yet another statue to poke. Do just
that and backtrack to the four-way intersection (hole in middle). A bit to 
the west of that is the hook you used before. Use the Dragon Claw to grapple
onto the hook by the entrance. 

Jump over the hook you just used. Now, grapple onto the platform to the east.
Basically, all we're doing is just circling around. When you reach the open
door, go through. Poke the statue in the next room to open the door on the 
right. Ignore that door for now. Instead, head left. Stab the statue you 
find. Accomplishing that, head south and jump left over the hole. Follow the
path east to a four-way split again. Once more, hop over and circle around.
You'll come to a chest containing Refreshers on your way. 

Keep going forward and you'll enter the room that you opened. Pick up the 
Arrows and defeat the Freezer Crab. Keep moving and use the Dragon Claw to 
get across the next chasm. Heal up and prepare to fight the Stone Golem. 

     Boss Fight: Stone Golem ~
     =---------------------=
          Two Cerberus's accompany the Stone Golem in all of his... well...
          stoniness! The faster you take care of them, the better. You don't
          want Doom Gaze (instant KO) to get the best of you before you even
          begin to fight the Stone Golem. Don't use Flare or Meteor or the 
          Stone Golem is strong against those two spells. Beware of any status
          ailments he might inflict upon you. Use Heal to dispatch any problems
          away. As a side note, how does a "Stone" Golem melt?

Once the Stone Golem is down for the count, backtrack to the entrance area. 
You will have to leave the icy area altogether to get to the next floor. Once
you're out of it, you will notice that the next two stairs have been unlocked.
Ascend them to the next portion of Doom Castle. 

You should see a pattern going on here. The first area was ground-ish to 
resemble the style of the Bone Dungeon. The second was icy to resemble the
Ice Pyramid. Now the third style is that of the Lava Dome. Interesting... 

Start off by heading east until you reach the lower-right corner. Jump onto
the nearby block and follow the blocks (because you can't jump diagonally, you
only have one path to take). Soon, you will jump to some stairs. Follow them
up the ledge and proceed to defeat the Iflyte. Go down the stairs and then
jump onto the block to the west. Kill the Stheno and open both chests for some
Arrows and Cure Potions. Hop on the blocks to your south and defeat the 
Cerberus. The two chests contain Arrows and Heal Potions. 

Use the Dragon Claw to grapple onto the stub to the left. Once again, jump
across the blocks in the lava to reach a staircase. Defeat the Iflyte and
continue. At the north-south fork, go down. Ignore the Cerberus and walk up
to the open wall. Grapple onto the stub and jump over it. Kill the Stheno
and advance. Hop over the block and battle the Twinhead Wyvern! 

     Boss Fight: Twinhead Wyvern ~
     =-------------------------=
          The Twinhead Wyvern enters the fight with a Stheno and Iflyte as
          allies. Do what you have to do first obviously. The Iflyte and 
          Stheno are easily defeated with magic or plain physical strikes.
          Do not use Flare or Fire in this fight. The Twinhead Wyvern is 
          strong against flame-based spells. Instead, use Meteor or White. I
          find it best for Benjamin to cast Meteor while Phoebe casts White.
          The battle should be over within a few turns. You shouldn't have 
          much trouble with status problems, but use Heal if needed. 

Now that that's over with, you can return to the entrance. Like before, the
next stairset will now be available. 6F is the second to last floor. However,
it will prove to be pretty long (and maybe a little bit confusing). Needless 
to say, it isn't over just yet. 

Note that you can easily fall off the side here down into the abyss. You will
land in the previous floor if you happen to do that. Begin by heading north.
Soon, you will reach a fork. Head right and go down at the next split. Walk 
up the steps and get rid of the Cerberus. Begin moving east once more and 
follow the pathway, making sure that you don't fall off, until you reach a 
monster. 

Dispose of the Chimera and continue west. Ignore the path leading north. 
Eventually, you will reach a Thanatos blocking some steps. Defeat the monster
and head down the steps and through the doorway. A Cerberus blocks your path
further ahead. Kill it as well. Go through the opening and proceed east. You
should be able to see yourself through the glass (HI!). Of course, you'll be
fighting various monsters while in that tunnel. 

Soon enough, you will reach an opening. Head south and kill the Chimera in 
your path. When you reach the next opening, turn right and enter the tunnel
path (you should see yourself in the glass again). Walk a tiny bit to the next
doorway. Then head north and through the opening. Kill the Cerberus and step
up. Simply follow this path to the end. A Pazuzu lookalike can be found on 
the middle platform. This is Zuh!

     Boss Fight: Zuh ~
     =-------------=
          This is basically Pazuzu all over again. Begin the fight by killing
          off his Chimera and Thanatos allies. Luckily for you, all three of
          your enemies are weak to Aero. Start off the battle by casting that
          and repeat until both Thanatos and Chimera are dead. For the
          remainder of the battle, you can easily win by casting Flare over
          and over again. Be wary of the Psychshield. When it's up, DO NOT 
          attack unless you want to die young. To win, all you really have to
          do is repeatedly use Flare and White. Use Cure when necessary and
          Life if someone happens to fall in battle. 

With Zuh defeated, you'll have to leave this area. I found it faster to just
fall off the edge of a platform and leave from fiery floor. But whichever way
you choose, simply make your way to 7F. Walk up to the statue and the crystals
shall speak to you. The walls surrounding the nearby chests will go down for
your access. They contain 25 of whatever they are holding. You probably won't
even need it at this point. When you're done looting, go through the final
door to meet the Dark King. Oh man, what a cool place! 

     Boss Fight: Dark King ~
     =-------------------=
          The Dark King begins in his regular form; lookin' all cool in his
          attire. Begin by using White and Flare and other powerful spells.
          This part of the fight is the easiest, so don't use Cure just yet. 
          When he starts to mock you (he'll actually say something), the Dark
          King will grow some new arms. Each of them is armed with a dangerous
          weapon. Now this is where it starts to get a little bit tough. Watch
          out for his stronger attacks that can deal a heavy amount of damage.
          I would just give Phoebe the role of healer now while Benjamin
          blasts away with Flare. Be patient and concentrate more on keeping
          Phoebe and Benjamin alive than actually damaging him. If you are
          diligent, you should deal about 2000 HP damage every turn at the
          least. Repeat this process for a bunch of turns until the Dark King
          speaks again. Yeah, he turns into a spider. Use the same routine
          with Phoebe curing and Benjamin using Flare. At this point, begin to
          watch out for Mega Flare and Laser. Trust me, it's not fun getting 
          hit by those moves. At long lasts, he'll finally transform into his
          final form; an octopussy-looking thing. Have Phoebe continue to heal
          the two. A few more Flares will finally put the Dark King in his
          place. 

Congratulations, you have defeated the Dark King and saved the world from a 
certain demise. Enjoy your ending!

          --- 8. Boss Listing ---                                      [8000]
~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~=~

The bosses of Final Fantasy Mystic Quest are listed below. Included are 
strategies and HP statistics. 

[-----------------------------------------------------------------------------]

     Minotaur -- 240 HP ~
     =----------------=
          First, I'll tell you the cheap method of winning. Simply have Kaeli 
          cast Life on the Minotaur to immediately put him out of commission.
          If you'd rather just attack the conventional way though, you might
          have a tiny bit of trouble. Use Cure to heal up your units. The 
          Minotaur hits pretty hard, so you'll need it. Obviously, if Benjamin
          dies, you'll need Kaeli's Life spell to revive him. Just remember
          that. 

     Flamerus Rex -- 2200 HP ~
     =---------------------=
          You know, they could have at least come up with a better name. 
          Assuming you didn't already use up all your Quakes, now would be a
          great time for Benjamin to use them. The Flamerus Rex can deal 100+
          damage with most of his attacks, so make sure that Benjamin or 
          Tristam heals up occasionally. Both Life and Cure heal HP so use
          them. Because the Flamerus Rex is strong against poison, don't count
          on Tristam dealing too much damage. Have him heal and revive Benjamin
          with Life if need be. When the boss's HP gets pretty low, he can use
          Rip Earth, which deals a good amount of damage to both of them. Like
          always, heal when your HP gets too low. Have Benjamin use Quake until
          he runs out. Then throw some bombs to finish the Flamerus Rex off.

     Squidite -- 2500 HP ~
     =-----------------=
          Yeah, it's a gigantic squid. Got a problem with that? Squidite 
          starts the battle with two Sparna allies. Phoebe can easily shoot
          them down with her Bow of Grace. Once those are taken care of, you
          can focus on Squidite itself. Use Phoebe's Thunder spell to take
          advantage of Squidite's weakness to that element. However, don't
          think it's just going to sit there. Squidite can deal some heavy
          damage as well as inflict certain status ailments upon your units.
          Needless to say, if Phoebe needs to use Heal, USE IT! Once Phoebe
          runs out of Thunder, just start pounding it with physical attacks
          to bring it down. 

     Snow Crab -- 3000 HP ~
     =------------------=
          The Snow Crap has two Desert Hags to help him out. Have Benjamin
          and Phoebe use Fire to take them down quickly. The Snow Crab has 
          some tough attacks. He can easily deal 200+ damage, so make sure
          that you heal when necessary. Thunder works the best in this battle.
          Have Phoebe cast that while Benjamin either heals or uses Quake. 
          When the Snow Crab's HP is low, he'll begin to use Tornado for very 
          heavy damage. Heal whenever necessary, and you should be fine. 
          Mmm, seafood! 

     Ice Golem -- 6500 HP ~
     =------------------=
          Oh no; a giant made of ice! So scary. The key to this fight is Fire
          magic. Luckily, both Benjamin and Phoebe should have that spell 
          available. Simply roast the Ice Golem with some of that. Be wary of
          his attacks though. They can deal a considerable bit of damage to
          your units. When necessary have either of your units heal. There's
          only one of him, so the Ice Golem can easily be overpowered. When
          your Black Magic runs dry, start pounding him with physical attacks.
          Keep your team healed up and you should be fine. 

      Jinn -- 6000 HP ~
      =-------------=
           Begin the battle by clearing out the Red Bones that Jinn has next
           to him. Even though Jinn is fire-based, Blizzard doesn't seem to be
           super-effective against him. Jinn's Flame Pillar move is the one
           you will want to watch out for. 200+ damage will be dealt to both
           of your units. Both Benjamin and Reuben should have Life, so be
           sure to use it when the other falls. Use Mega Grenade or the Wizard
           spell, Thunder, to deal heavy damage. With the Charm Claw, you can
           poison Jinn. Simply keep your units alive with Cure and Life to win 
           out. 

     Medusa -- 7000 HP ~
     =---------------=
          Begin by taking out the Were Wolves that join the fight. Benjamin
          should use White while Reuben just attacks with his Morning Star.
          Try to take them out before they manage to pull off a Quake or 
          something. White deals a lot of damage. Only a few castings are
          needed to put her down. Be sure to have Reuben heal when needed.
          Keep Benjamin alive while Reuben heals and attacks. Beware of her
          Screech Voice move; it's capable of confusing a unit. Stone Voice
          can petrify and Para-snake can paralyze. Ouch. 

     Dualhead Hydra -- 14000 HP ~
     =------------------------=
          The White spell will be your salvation in this battle. Although you
          obviously can't cast it forever, it still will deal a considerable 
          amount of damage to the Dualhead Hydra. Reuben is useful for healing,
          so have him use Life to replenish your party's HP. The Dualhead 
          Hydra is capable of quite a few different attacks. Para-Breath and
          Poison Breath will paralyze and poison, respectively. Stone Gas will
          petrify. Heal Potions will clear a unit of those annoying ailments.
          Fire Breath deals heavy damage as well. Although a Moon Helm can 
          help defend against Fire Breath, Reuben (without any fire protection)
          will surely be fried. It's not imperative that he survives, but he 
          really helps out with his Life spell. 

     Gidrah -- 13000 HP ~
     =----------------=
          Get rid of the two Skuldiers by using Aero. Once they are cleared
          out, have Benjamin use Meteor or White while Kaeli continues to
          blast Gidrah with Aero. Gidrah can paralyze and petrify your team,
          so watch out for those. Remember to use Heal in order to clear
          status ailments. If both units are petrified, then you lose. Make
          sure that doesn't happen. 

     Dullahan -- 14000 HP ~
     =------------------=
          So this Dullahan is some headless knight in shining armor, eh? 
          First thing's first; get rid of the two Vampires he has at his side. 
          That should be instinct by now. Aero will get rid of them easily.
          The best way to defeat Dullahan is through pounding him with magic.
          That means casting Meteor, White, etc. Kaeli won't be too useful in
          this fight, but she's a good healer. Use her to heal Benjamin if he
          loses a lot of HP. She can also heal the various status ailments
          that Dullahan might cast upon you. The one move you want to watch 
          out for is Doom Dance. It is capable of automatically killing one 
          of your units regardless of their HP. Needless to say, it can get
          pretty annoying. All you basically need to do is use Meteor until
          that ugly Dullahan dies. 

     Pazuzu -- 25000 HP ~
     =----------------=
          The birdman is weak against wind attacks, so Aero works wonders.
          Flare deals more damage though. Those two spells should deal the
          most damage against Pazuzu. A lot of his attacks deal 500+ damage.
          Keep Kaeli's HP high as she seems to die very often. Psychshield
          makes it so that if you attack Pazuzu while it is up, you will eat
          a nasty counter-attack. Do not attack with his shield up for that
          very reason. When the Psychshield is down, it is safe to kick his
          butt. Take caution with that one move and you shouldn't die THAT
          often. If you send a constant barrage of Flare and Aero, the battle
          will go much quicker. Aero (assuming Kaeli casts it) deals 1500+ 
          damage while Flare can deal 2000+ damage. Those are some pretty 
          figures if you ask me. 

     Skullrus Rex -- 10000 HP ~
     =----------------------=
          Of course, begin by getting rid of the two Shadows. They won't be
          tough to take down. I'd suggest casting Flare or something to 
          damage both at the same time. Upon taking down the Shadows, begin
          pounding the Skullrus Rex with Flare (Benjamin) and White (Phoebe).
          If you can deal 2000+ damage with each move, the Skullrus Rex will
          die within 2.5 turns. The only move you will want to watch out for
          is Sleep Powder. Luckily, Benjamin can't be put to sleep. The 
          Skullrus Rex is easily defeated. 

     Stone Golem -- 10000 HP ~
     =---------------------=
          Two Cerberus's accompany the Stone Golem in all of his... well...
          stoniness! The faster you take care of them, the better. You don't
          want Doom Gaze (instant KO) to get the best of you before you even
          begin to fight the Stone Golem. Don't use Flare or Meteor or the 
          Stone Golem is strong against those two spells. Beware of any status
          ailments he might inflict upon you. Use Heal to dispatch any problems
          away. As a side note, how does a "Stone" Golem melt?

     Twinhead Wyvern -- 15000 HP ~
     =-------------------------=
          The Twinhead Wyvern enters the fight with a Stheno and Iflyte as
          allies. Do what you have to do first obviously. The Iflyte and 
          Stheno are easily defeated with magic or plain physical strikes.
          Do not use Flare or Fire in this fight. The Twinhead Wyvern is 
          strong against flame-based spells. Instead, use Meteor or White. I
          find it best for Benjamin to cast Meteor while Phoebe casts White.
          The battle should be over within a few turns. You shouldn't have 
          much trouble with status problems, but use Heal if needed. 

     Zuh -- 20000 HP ~
     =-------------=
          This is basically Pazuzu all over again. Begin the fight by killing
          off his Chimera and Thanatos allies. Luckily for you, all three of
          your enemies are weak to Aero. Start off the battle by casting that
          and repeat until both Thanatos and Chimera are dead. For the
          remainder of the battle, you can easily win by casting Flare over
          and over again. Be wary of the Psychshield. When it's up, DO NOT 
          attack unless you want to die young. To win, all you really have to
          do is repeatedly use Flare and White. Use Cure when necessary and
          Life if someone happens to fall in battle. 

     Dark King -- 40000 HP ~
     =-------------------=
          The Dark King begins in his regular form; lookin' all cool in his
          attire. Begin by using White and Flare and other powerful spells.
          This part of the fight is the easiest, so don't use Cure just yet. 
          When he starts to mock you (he'll actually say something), the Dark
          King will grow some new arms. Each of them is armed with a dangerous
          weapon. Now this is where it starts to get a little bit tough. Watch
          out for his stronger attacks that can deal a heavy amount of damage.
          I would just give Phoebe the role of healer now while Benjamin
          blasts away with Flare. Be patient and concentrate more on keeping
          Phoebe and Benjamin alive than actually damaging him. If you are
          diligent, you should deal about 2000 HP damage every turn at the
          least. Repeat this process for a bunch of turns until the Dark King
          speaks again. Yeah, he turns into a spider. Use the same routine
          with Phoebe curing and Benjamin using Flare. At this point, begin to
          watch out for Mega Flare and Laser. Trust me, it's not fun getting 
          hit by those moves. At long lasts, he'll finally transform into his
          final form; an octopussy-looking thing. Have Phoebe continue to heal
          the two. A few more Flares will finally put the Dark King in his
          place.
