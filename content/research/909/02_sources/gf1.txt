(                \_______       3. Story        _______/                )
 \    =======            \_____________________/            =======    /
  \___________________________________________________________________/
   ===================================================================

This world is divided into four main regions. In the center of the world,
where they meet, lies Focus Tower. Focus Tower was the connection point,
and was a symbol of unity. The world stayed in a peaceful condition, 
under this way of life, for many years.

One day though, four powerful mosnters stormed Focus Tower. Each stole
one of the coins, that held the key to a section of the world, closing
off the areas from each other. When the monsters left, the world was
in a state of chaos; weaker monsters were appearing in all sections
of the world, and there were many quakes occuring.

Benjamin (aka Hero) lived in a village near the Hill of Destiny. His 
village was destroyed by one of these Quakes, and he fled to the Hill of
Destiny, following a mysteriosu cloaked man. This is where the game 
opens up.

Out of Battle
=============

Here is a list of the controls, while you're in an area, but not in
a battle with an enemy:

(A)    - Use weapon + Open Chest + Talk to People
(B)    - Jump
(Y)    - Switch between Auto and Manual companion battle modes.
(X)    - Enter save screen
(L)    - Switch weapon
(R)    - Switch weapon
(Strt) - Pause Menu
(Slct) - Nothing
(Dpad) - Move

In Battle
=========

(A)    - Make Selection
(B)    - Previous Selection
(Y)    - Switch between Auto and Manual companion battle modes.
(X)    - Nothing
(L)    - Switch weapon
(R)    - Switch weapon
(Strt) - Nothing
(Slct) - Nothing
(Dpad) - Change Selections

And here is a brief description of what each option is:

(Battle)  - All battle maneivers are listed here.
            (Attack) - Strike with your selected weapon
            (Item) - Use on of your items.
            (Spell) - Use one of your spells.
            (Defense) - Guard for an enemy's attack.
(Run)     - Attempt to escape the battle.
(Control) - Switch between Auto and Manual companion battle modes.


Map Screen
==========

Here is a list of the controls, while you're on the Map Screen:

(A)    - Enter area
(B)    - Enter Area
(Y)    - Switch between Auto and Manual companion battle modes.
(X)    - Enter Save Screen
(L)    - Switch weapon
(R)    - Switch weapon
(Strt) - Pause Menu
(Slct) - Toggle Scrolling
(Dpad) - Move

As you move from location to location, you'll notice one of two 
things: The area has darknened arrows, or red arrows. If it's red,
you've done what you needed to to open that path, and can move on,
if it's grey, there's still something you haven't done. Additionally,
there are lots of clouds at the beginning. As you exlpore more of
the map, they'll dissapear.

Menu Screen
===========

Here is a list of the controls, while you're on the Pause Menu:

(A)    - Select Option
(B)    - Return to game
(Y)    - Switch between Auto and Manual companion battle modes.
(X)    - Nothing
(L)    - Switch weapon
(R)    - Switch weapon
(Strt) - Nothing
(Slct) - Nothing
(Dpad) - Switch highlighted option

And here is a brief description of what each option is:

(Item) - View/Use items.
(Spell) - View/Use spells.
(Armor) - View armor and their stats
(Weapons) - View Weapons and their stats
(Status) - View charcter stats, EXP, GP, and EXP need to level-up
(Customize) - There are several options here, as shown below:
              (Life Indicate) - Switch between HP bars, or numbers.
              (Control) - Switch between Auto and Manual companion. 
              (Message Speed) - Change speed of messages between 1 & 7.
              (Window Color) - Set the color of pause menu interface.
(Save) - Enter the Save Menu

Weapon(s) [Second Time]: Ninja Stars

 ________________O=====================================O________________
(________________|           Hill of Destiny           |________________)
                 O=====================================O

=================
Enemies - None
Items   - None
Boss    - Behemoth
=================

You don't even control most of this level, so it's more of an 
introduction to the game. Hero will follow a cloaked man, to the edge of
a paltform. Press B to jump across, and the first platform will sink
down. 

The cloaked figure will look to Focus Tower in the distance-, and tell
the story of how the Focus Tower connects the four sections of the world
and how monsters have closed them off, and are draining power from the
crystals that guarded each section. As he talks, a monster will appear,
and fight - Behemoth.
                         
               O-------------------------------------O
               |           Behemoth  - 80HP          |
O--------------O-------------------------------------O----------------O
| EXP: 84      | Battle Strategy: This fight isn't hard. You only have|
| GP: 3        | one weapon, and no item or spells though. Keep       |
O--------------O choosing to attack, and he should die. Although, it  |
| Attack List  | is possible to die, if he gets critical hits to much,|
| ===========  | you'll just start the battle over anyway, so don't   |
| Horn         | worry about it. Horn is just a physical attack.      |
O--------------O------------------------------------------------------O 

After you defeat Behemoth, another Quake will start. The cloaked figure
will tell you to meet him in Level Forest. Hero leaves, automatically,
as a quake whipes Hill of Destiny off the map. You only have one 
destination on the map - Level Forest. So, enter the forest.


 ________________O=====================================O________________
(________________|             Level Forest            |________________)
                 O=====================================O

=================
Enemies - None
Items   - Tree Wither + Cure Potion(s)
Boss    - None
=================

When you arrive, the cloaked man is waiting for you. Talk to him, and he
wil ltell you to save the Crystal of Earth first, and then leave. This
guy isn't very talkative, is he?

Well, head north, until you see a man in a blue robe. If you talk to him
you'll find out the stone is blocking his path. Jump over him, and push
the stone, and he'l lthank you. He'll also give you a Tree Wither, and
tell you to show it to a girl named Kaeli.

Head left, and you can get 3 potions from the treasure chest. Head all
the way to the right after getting the potion, and you'll leave Level 
Forest, and open the path to Foresta.


 ________________O=====================================O________________
(________________|                Foresta              |________________)
                 O=====================================O

=================
Enemies - None
Items   - Cure [Spell] + Cure Potion(s)
Boss    - None
=================

When you enter Foresta, head into the first tree home on the left. There
are two girls at the table in this house; The one on the left is Kaeli.
Talk to her, and she'll see the Tree Wither, and volunteer to help you.
Kaeli joins your party!

In the back of Kaeli's house, there is a treasure chest with 3 potions
in it. After you get it, leave the house. The house on the right side
of town (the one on the bottom) also has a treasure chest with potions,
if you'd like to get it.

What you really need to pick up in this area, is the white magic spell,
Cure. Head to the north end of the town, and cross the bridge. On the 
right, in the water, are two logs. Jump across them, to the back of a
tree home. There's a door there, so walk into the house, and there will
be a treasure chest with the Cure spell inside. Also, remember, if you
use the bed on the left side of this house, your health will be 
restored. Remember this, as you're fighting monsters later.

After you get the cure spell, exit the house, and leave Foresta. Once 
on the world map, you have to go back to Level Forest. With Kaeli's 
help, you'll be able to go deeper into the forest.


 ________________O=====================================O________________
(________________|             Level Forest            |________________)
                 O=====================================O

=================
Enemies - Brownie + Slime
Items   - Cure Potion(s) + Heal Potion(s)
Boss    - Minotaur
=================

When you enter the forest with Kaeli in your party, she'll immediately 
cut down a tree with her axe, giving you access to more of the level.
Take out the Brownie that was behind the tree she cut down, and make 
sure to grab thepotion that you got the last time you were here - Chests
that look like this one restore when you leave the level - and you're
good to go.

If you take out the two slimes on the left, you'll clear a path to a
Brownie, who is guarding a chest of potions. Take out the slime on the
right, and you'll be able to advance forward.

There's a glowing tree ahead. Don't approach it until you're at least
level 2 - 2 is fine, 3 would be better - If you take out most of the
enemies in the area, you'll be at level 3, so don't worry. Also, make
sure to heal first, and possibly take out the Brownie and the Slime on
the right, to reach a chest with 3 Heal Potions inside. When you are
ready, approach the tree, and a Minotaur will poison Kaeli. Then, you'll
enter combat!

               O-------------------------------------O
               |           Minotaur - 240HP          |
O--------------O-------------------------------------O----------------O
| EXP: 84      | Battle Strategy: If you have your partner on Manual, |
| GP: 3        | make her attack each turn, unless Hero dies; In which|
O--------------O case, she should use Life to revive him. Use a       |
| Attack List  | similair tactic with Hero. Attack every turn, but use|
| ===========  | Cure if either Kaeli or Hero get low on health. His  |
| Axe          | axe attack is just a physical attack, but his Scream |
| Scream       | ability will do damage, and also lower your defense. |
| Roundhouse   | Also, be prepared for his Roundhouse; It's also a    |
|              | physical attack, but it lays heavier damage. Just    |
|              | keep attacking, and he'll go down, though. If you    |
|              | want to be cheap, use a Life Spell, to bring him     |
|              | down in one hit.                                     |
O--------------O------------------------------------------------------O 

After the battle, Kaeli's mom will come, and take Kaeli home. Hero will
volunteer to get the elixer to save Kaili - He's told it's in the Sand
Temple that's a little north. Kaeli will give Hero her axe - You can 
cut trees down now! - and then, they'll leave.

You can access a Brownie and a chest with a Heal Potion by cutting down
trees on the south-west section of the map. You can also find a chest 
with a Potion inside it, by cutting down trees on the south-east. To
move on though, take the north exit, past where you fought Minotaur.

 ________________O=====================================O________________
(________________|              Battlefield            |________________)
                 O=====================================O

=================
Enemies - Mad Plant + Poison Toad
Reward - 54 EXP
=================

North of Level Forest, right before Sand Temple, is the first
battlefield. Like all future battelfields, it's optional. Fight 10 sets
of monsters, and earn GP and EXP, and sometimes an item. That's how 
things work here! I don't recommend fighting here, until you get Tristam
in your party (you'll get him in Sand Temple).

 ________________O=====================================O________________
(________________|              Sand Temple            |________________)
                 O=====================================O

=================
Enemies - None
Items   - None
Boss    - None
=================

This area is real small. Walk through the door in the first room, into
second room, and check the one treasure chest there. You'll find it's
empty. Soon, Tristam will come in, and offer to seel you the elixer for
9000 GP - Of course, you can't afford that! (Even if you do have more
than 9000 GP, he won't sell, despite what people say.)

Hero and Tristam will strike a deal though. Tristam will accompany you
through the Bone Dungeon, and he'll give you the Elixer afterwards. This
is a good deal, isn't it? Well, it's how things are going to turn out.
After Tristam joins the party, exit the sand temple.

 ________________O=====================================O________________
(________________|              Battlefield            |________________)
                 O=====================================O

=================
Enemies - Poison Toad + Basilisk + Mad Tree
Reward - Charm
=================

Before heading to the Bone Dungeon, you really should head left from 
sand Temple, and clear out a battlefield. When you win, you'll pick up
a Charm. The charm is an armor, that will increase your defense, so it's
good to have. When both battlefields you can reach so far, are cleaned 
out, head to the Bone Dungeon. 

 ________________O=====================================O________________
(________________|              Bone Dungeon           |________________)
                 O=====================================O

=================
Enemies - Basilisk + Sandworm + Poison Toad + Roc + Skeleton + Gorgon
          + Minotaur Zombie
Items   - Ninja Star(s) + Cure Potion(s) + Seed(s) + Quake [Spell]
           + Sand Coin + Explosive(s)
Boss    - Flamerus Rex
=================

In the beginning of this level, you have a few paths. You can just step
onto the moving sand, to be pushed across the room, which is the easiest
way, or you could choose to fight the monsters on either the left or 
right side, to go around the sand - if you do this, there's a chest with
10 Ninja Stars for Tristam, on both sides. In the other side, open the 
chest at the edge of the sand, to get 3 potions, and then go through the
set of bones that make up a door.

In the next room, jump across the rocks in the water, and go down the 
steps, into the water on the other side. On the left side of the water,
there's another passage of bones. Go through them, and head up the
stairs on the other side. Walk upward, towards the blocked door, and
Tristam will blow it open with a bomb! He'll then offer to sell you 50
bombs for 30GP. Say "Sorry" and he'll cut the price to 15GP, where you
have no choice but to pay - but that's god, you want those bombs!

Before you head to the next room, backtrack a bit. Go into the water,
and through the bones again. Climb up the stairs that are in the
previous room, and walk up, toward the wall. There's aset of bones lying
on the ground. Use a bomb to blow them up. There's a treasure chest
containing a Steel Shield behind them, and it will boost your defense by
5 points! After getting it, continue, and go through the door Tristam
blew open.

The next room is loaded with enemies. You can fight them for the EXP if
you'd like, but there are two main points of interest here. On the north
east part of the room, you can blow apart a blocked door. A Roc, and a
chest of Ninja Stars is behind it. The other, would be stairs to the 
next floor. These are blocked by a Skeleton in the north-west section of
the room.

On the right side of the next room, is a chest full of bombs, blocked by
a Gorgon. If you are out of bombs, which is unlikely, you need to pick
them up. Head north, and there is a single Skeleton, blocking a set of
bones. Defeat the skeleton, and place the bombs, by the two rib bones(?)
that you have access to. The whole set of bones will shatter! If you 
walk all the wya to the right, into the wall, you can enter a hidden
room. Jump across the rocks here, to find a chest with 10 ninja stars
inside it.

On the tight side, out of the mouth of the bones, is a Gorgon, and a 
Skeleton. If you defeat one, or both of them, you can access another
chest with 10 Ninja Stars inside. Afterwards, head to the top of the
screen. You'll find some moving sand pushing east, so you can't cross
over it. Instead, go around the top of it, taking out the Skeleton, and
the Gorgon, bombing the set of bones, when you reach them. After you 
take care of him, head south, using the stairs to reach the next room.

There's a Skeleton blocking the way, as soon as you enter. Kill him, and
move to the left. A Minotaur Zombie is blocking a treasure chest with 10
Ninja Stars in it. After picking up the items, head to the right, to the
Skull coming out of the wall. Bomb its teeth, to open a path. Go
straight, into the next room, where you can pick up 3 seeds. After, turn
around, and come back, through the set of bones. Take a left, about half
way through the bones, and you can enter, to another area of the room.

There's more moving sand, so you'll have to battle the enemies to get
around it - There are 2 Skeletons, and a Minotaur Zombie, that you have
to worry about. On the other side, is another Skull with teeth to bomb.
Head straight, down the bones, and into another room. A chest containing
the Quake spell is here, and you'll want to pick it up! After getting
it, leave the room, and fight the Minotaur Zomie thats on the right of
the set of bones, you'll have one more set of teeth to bomb.

Head into the next room, and make sure to heal, and cure any status 
ailiments, because you're about to fight a boss! Jump over the rock in
the water, and you'll enter battle with Flamerus Rex!

               O-------------------------------------O
               |         Flamerus Rex - 2,200 HP     |
O--------------O-------------------------------------O----------------O
| EXP: 120     | Battle Strategy: There's only one of him, so bombs   |
| GP: 0        | will do a good amount of damage. Have Hero bomb him  |
O--------------O every turn - Or you could use Quake, which is better |
| Attack List  | until you run out of it. Use Tristam's Ninja Stars,  |
| ===========  | using Life if either Tristam, or Hero need healing.  |
| Bone Missile | His Twin Fang and Bone Missle do decent damage on    |
| Rip Earth    | you, but you should be more concerned about Rip Earth|
| Sleep Powder | which does big damage to Hero and Tristam. So don't  |
| Twin Fang    | let both of them get low on HP at the same time. If  |
|              | he uses Sleep Powder, one character will fall asleep,|
|              | so make sure both are able to deal decent damage.    |
|              | Once he falls on the ground, he's about to die. Just |
|              | hurt him a little more. He's immune to poison and    |
|              | parylzing.                                           |
O--------------O------------------------------------------------------O 

After you beat Flamerus Rex, the Sun Crystal will be restored, and 
Tristam will show off a really cool weapon (that you'll get later in the
game.) He'll then give you the elixer, and allow you to check the other
chests, which contain Potions, bombs, and the Sand Coin, the key to a 
door in Focus Tower. Now, backtrack through the dungeon, and leave. 
You'll be doing this alone though, because Tristam takes off, at the
exit of the room you fight Flamerus Rex in. When you are back on the
world map, head back to Foresta. Kaeli still needs the elixer that you
just got.

 ________________O=====================================O________________
(________________|              Focus Tower            |________________)
                 O=====================================O

=================
Enemies - None
Items   - Fire [Spell] + Explosive(s)
Boss    - None
=================

When you enter, walk forward, until you see a 1-square gap in the all on
the left. You can walk under that spot, so do so, and then keep walking
upward. You'll meet your friend that cloaked man again, and he'll tell 
you to look for a girl named Phoebe, to join you on your quest, and as
always. He also states "Mac" will play an important role in your quest,
and he leaves without explaining it further.

There are some stairs, walk up them, to reach another floor. Check the
treasure chest, to get some more bombs, and then open the open the door
with the sword on it. Walk through the door, and forward, until you see
more stairs. Go up them, and when you come out from another set of 
stairs, go left, and walk south. You'll see another treasure chest; This
one has the Fire spell in it. After getting the spell, walk north again,
and out the door on the top-most wall.

 ________________O=====================================O________________
(________________|              Battlefield            |________________)
                 O=====================================O

=================
Enemies - Mintmint
Reward - 99 EXP
=================

I don't reccomend fighting in this area, until after you visit Libra
Temple, and you have Phoebe on your team. With two people in your party,

 ________________O=====================================O________________
(________________|              Libra Temple           |________________)
                 O=====================================O

=================
Enemies - None
Items   - Explosive(s)
Boss    - None
=================

Walk forward, and you'll see a strange crest on the ground. You can't
do anything about that yet. Check the chest to get some bombs, and then
talk to the girl standing behind it. You'll find out that Aquaria has
been frozen! More importantly though, you'll find out that this girl is
Phoebe! When Hero finds this out, he tells her, that he's going with her.
And so, Phebe joins the party! You can leave the Libra Temple, after she
joins.

 ________________O=====================================O________________
(________________|              Battlefield            |________________)
                 O=====================================O

=================
Enemies - Giant Toad + Mintmint
Reward - 300 GP
=================

There is another battlefield to the right of Libra Temple. This one may
be slightly challenging if you have a low level, but the enemies here
will pay some good EXP (for this stage of the game at least.) To top 
that off, you'll also win 300 GP!

 ________________O=====================================O________________
(________________|              Battlefield            |________________)
                 O=====================================O

=================
Enemies - Giant Toad + Scorpion + Mintmint
Reward - Magic Ring
=================

Yup, two in a row! This one is just right of the other one, and south of
Aquaria. Before you enter this battlefield, make sure Phoebe and/or Hero
have at least a few Black Magics left. You'll be facing a new type of 
enemy here, the Scorpion, and they are weak against Fire. Defeat 10 sets
of enemies, and you'll be rewarded with a Magic Ring; An item that 
raises your defense by 3!

(________________|                Aquaria              |________________)

=================
Enemies - None
Items   - Heal Potion(s) + Refresher(s) + Steel Helm + Cure Potion(s)
Boss    - None
=================

When you arrive in Aquaria, head all the way to the left, and into the
