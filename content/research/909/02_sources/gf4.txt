This is another one of those games that has been considered a flop.  It really 
wasn't a flop, it was just the first RPG Squaresoft made for the SNES.  It may 
be a bit primative compared to today's standards, but certainly not a flop.  
This is a beginner's RPG, and should be treated a such.  It may be for 
beginners, but veteran RPG players can find some enjoyment in it.  I have 
played Square games for about 7 years now, and I still liked this game.  So, 
with that said, let's get to the walkthrough.

2) Characters:
[NOTE: In ALL of my Walkthroughs, the hero is referred to as Jason (Named 
after my husband.)  I got a semi nasty E-Mail about not naming the hero in one 
of my Walkthroughs correctly.  But I assure you, my readers, that I do know 
the real names of the hero in every game I have written about.  Thank you and 
enjoy!]

*Hero (Refered to from now on as Jason)- When you first meet Jason, he is 
climbing the Hill of Destiny (well, I guess when your village is destroyed, 
you have nothing better to do...).  Jason meets a Owl Like creature that 
informs him that he is the Knight of the ancient Prophecy (you know, you'll 
save the world, bring peace, all that...).  The Prophecy states that four 
monsters have locked the four doors (wow, four monsters- four doors!) of the 
Focus Tower and fled with the four keys.  You know what you have to do, save 
the world!  What else is new?
*Kaeli- This is the first character that joins your party (Not sure you can 
really call it a party, there are only two of you at any given time).  Kaeli 
has the ability to talk to trees, a trait that comes in handy both early on in 
the game and again later on in the game.  She hails from Foresta and when she 
joins you for the first time she has 120 Hit Points, attacks with an axe, and 
knows the spell of Life.  After the fight with Minotaur in the Level Forest, 
she leaves the group (she is poisoned in the attack) and gives you her axe!  
Kaeli will join you again later on in the game, when Phoebe blows up Spencer's 
Place.  When she rejoins you she is Level 31 and has 1280 Hit Points.
*Tristam- This character joins you in the Sand Temple.  Tristam is a treasure 
hunter (Hmmm....  Or is he a Thief????? Heeee)  and forces you to accompany 
him to the Bone Dungeon.  If he gets the treasure he seeks, he will give you 
the Elixir which you need to heal Kaeli from the fight with Minotaur.  Tristam 
takes his leave of you shortly after you fight Flamerus Rex.  He is good to 
his word, he gets his treasure, you get the Elixir.  Also while you are in the 
Bone Dungeon with Tristam, he teaches you to use Bombs, and will sell you 50 
explosives for 30 GP.  Tristam gives you some useful things: He gives you the 
Multi Key when you get to Fireburg, and the Dragon Claw later on in Spencer's 
Place.  Tristam joins you again on the Rope Bridge after Reuben falls.  
Tristam is now Level 23 and has 1120 Hit Points.
*Phoebe- You first meet Phoebe un the Libra Temple.  She originally hails from 
Aquaria.  She attacks with a Cat Claw at first, but once you get in the Wintry 
Cave, she gives it to you and starts to use the Bow of Grace.  She also 
teaches you to use Jumbo Bombs in the Falls Basin.  She leaves you sometime 
soon after the Ice Pyramid.  She joins you again in Windia after completing 
Mac's Ship.  She is then Level 34 and has 1480 Hit Points, and now knows the 
Spells of Cure, Heal, Life, Aero, Fire, Thunder, and White!  She will stay 
with you for the remainder of the game.
*Reuben- You meet Reuben in Fireburg where he joins you for the first time.  
He is Level 23 and has 1040 Hit Points.   He leaves you when he falls off the 
Rope Bridge.  He will join you again in Windia after the defeat of Pazuzu.  He 
will then be leve 31 and have 1320 Hit Points.  He knows the Spells of Life 
and White on your second meeting.
*Arion- He is Reuben's father.  You rescue him by completing the Mine quest 
and moving a large rock with Mega Grenades.  You learn to use Mega Grenades 
from the man in the locked home in Fireburg.
*Spencer- Spencer is Phoebe's grandfather.  He helps out with information a 
few times in the game.  His Place is where you find the Mobius Crest late in 
the game.
*Captain Mac- Mac is Kaeli's father.  His ship was caught on a ledge when 
water drained from a lake.  You rescue him and his ship late in the game and 
reunite Kaeli and her father.  He lets you take his ship in the ending 
sequences.
*Otto- You meet Otto in Windia.  He bulids you a Rainbow Road to get to 
Pazuzu's Tower so you can rescue his daughter Norma.  And another Rainbow Road 
to get back into Spencer's Place toward the end of the game.
*Norma- Otto's daughter.  You rescue her from Pazuzu's Tower later in the 
game.

monster in a similar class will be weak against the same things, and usually 
attack you with the same things.

This is gained after the defeat of Pazuzu- climb to the top of the tower and 
it is in the red chest.
*How do you get to the top of the Focus Tower?
You need the Sky Coin to gain access to the top of the Focus Tower.

17) Odds And Ends
*There is a free bed in Foresta in the tree stump house on your right at the 
bottom. Once inside it is the left bed.
*There is a very easy way to kill the Dark King (the end boss).  Just have 
Jason cast Cure on him.  But don't have your companion do the same.  Your 
companion will only undo the damage done by the hero.  But, now real 
adventurers will have no need for this, right?
*One tip, if you get to a dead end (well, one blocked by a chest) just jump 
over the obstacle.
*There are certain spells later in the game that monsters hit you with that 
can cause instant death if they connect.  These spells are: Corrode Gas, Doom 
Dance,  and Doom Powder.
*All Inns cost the same price to stay: 95 GP.
*After clearing Mac's Ship, and preparing to fight through the Doom Castle, in 
the lower levels of Mac's Ship, you can purchase Seeds and Cure Potions!  
Seeds Cost 50 GP for 1 and Cure Potions cost 25 for 1.
