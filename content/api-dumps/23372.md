> First - shoutout to all the 90s Nickelodeon fans. Guts, Legends of the Hidden Temple, Nick Arcade, Double Dare, etc. GOATed gameshow programming.
> 
> Anyways, big thanks to solidification for the achievement set and making a few tweaks to make this set fun and challenging to go for.
> 
> As for the game itself, it definitely hits the nostalgia, but the gameplay itself certainly has some jank. The aerial events are kinda redundant, but Basic Training, Tornado Run and Aggro Crag can be fun to optimize despite some jank movement.
> 
> The toughest achievement IMO is Gotta Collect 'Em All. Knocking off all the balls in 55 seconds (35 seconds or more left on the clock) is quite the challenge. First time you do it, you might think "how is possible?" or you won't believe the hit box of your arms in collecting the balls at times. But it is doable. You really need to be efficient from start to finish.
> 
> Only other piece of advice is when you do the Aggro Crag on Advanced, take your time. This is classic NES/SNES platforming in a few spots where you can't see what is ahead of you off the screen. But falling to the bottom when you are halfway up is insanely painful. But the timer for this is pretty generous. So, just take your time, and even if you fall a couple times, you should be fine.
