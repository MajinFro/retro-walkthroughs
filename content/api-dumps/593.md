> **itsSked**: Mastered. Played NBA Jam a ton as a kid, though I mostly played the Tournament Edition. But I wanted to start with the original on SNES.
> 
> Achievement set was about what I expected. It's mostly just a grind beating each team. I turned the clock up to very fast to make the games go quicker.
> 
> The 200 points was the toughest achievement, although it took me a little bit to realize I could turn on Power-Up Fire (you're on fire the whole game no matter what) and turn off computer assistance. Took me a few tries even so, but we eventually got there. I also did this achievement after I beat each team, so juice mode was on to help the possessions go even quicker.
> 
> For people struggling with the severe rubber banding when beating each team, the best tips I can offer are:
> 
> 1. Once you build roughly a 3+ point lead in the 4th quarter and have the ball, stand at half court and wait for the shotclock to appear to give the other team fewer possessions. Once it gets down to about 7 seconds left, drive toward the basket and weave up or down and you'll blow by the defender nearly every time as the defender runs in a straight line at your initial position.
> 
> 2. Alternatively, you can stand at half court and try to get your CPU teammate to run toward the defender guarding you and your CPU will push him over so you have a free run to the basket. The defender guarding you won't do a thing unless you cross halfcourt first. Most of the time, this strat worked. Of course, with 1 and 2, you have to pray you don't miss the dunk lol.
> 
> 3. When your teammate CPU inbounds the ball to you, immediately start to shoot the ball, but hold the shoot button as both defenders jump at you, and then pass the ball while in the air to your CPU teammate. Most of the time, the CPU will just go straight to the basket, or you can have him shoot from 3 and you can outrun the defenders who jumped anyways to get a rebound just incase.
> 
> Thanks for the set!

> **31percent**: Mastered.
> 
> For the 200 pt challenge I set the computer to normal difficulty and slow time. I had a few attempts changing the computer to very easy difficulty, but they kept missing dunks that would doink off the rim. This kept creating long rebound situations and losing time. Also make sure to activate the constant on fire code. At the matchup screen press B seven times then hold down B+Y+UP until tip off.
> 
> This mastery took me much longer than the median time suggested. I started my first playthrough of beating the 27 teams on a lower difficulty to learn the mechanics because I had never played the game before.
> 
> For the very hard rank I took the same approach vs every team. Playing mostly as the Hornets and some games as the Bulls. These are two teams with power forward/center combos and I had the most success with that archetype. I started off spamming 3 pointers trying to secure a two possession lead. After that I would focus on dunks with Larry Johnson.
> 
> The very hard mode bots pass the ball much more than the other difficulties. We can use this to our advantage by trying to spam steals out of the baseline inbounds. Some of the time just running in front of the passing lane while holding turbo is enough to steal the ball. Since the ball is stolen right under the basket we can go up for an immediate dunk. It took me a while to beat the first very hard team. After you get one win, you get the hang of it quick. Also don't be afraid to get your teammate involved. One of the comments above explains the jump pass very well. This can get you out of double teams and you find an easy roll to the basket.
> 
> I did have the achievement "Legendary Team" pop up prematurely and submitted a ticket.
> 
> Overall fun game and challenging set. Go Pistons!
