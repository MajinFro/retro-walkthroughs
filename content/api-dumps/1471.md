> **JamesPlaysGames**: On your playthrough, do everything EXCEPT interact with the Princess, or the Dragon Lord. Save your game at the King, and then here's the different runs you need to do, resetting after each one to reload your save. You'll need to do 3 runs to the final boss, and then a 4th run where you just rescue the Princess and bring her to the King.
> 
> 1. Go to the Dragon Lord. When he offers to have you join him, accept, and die.
> 
> 2. Go to the Dragon Lord. Fight and kill him, then return to the King, having never rescued the Princess.
> 
> 3. Rescue the Princess, and take her to the Dragon Lord. Fight and kill him, then return to the King.
> 
> 4. Rescue the Princess and take her immediately to the King.
